Q 1 | cur | International
In March 2025, NASA astronauts Butch Wilmore and Sunita Williams returned to Earth on board SpaceX's spacecraft named :
(1) Dragon
(2) Discovery
(3) Columbia
(4) Challenger
= 1
~ Wilmore and Williams came back with NASA's Crew-9 mission aboard SpaceX's Crew Dragon capsule 'Freedom', which splashed down off Florida on 18 March 2025. They had reached the ISS in June 2024 on Boeing's Starliner, whose thruster faults stretched an 8-day trip to about nine months. Discovery, Columbia and Challenger were NASA Space Shuttles.

Q 2 | cur | Sports, Awards & Misc.
The 2024 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine was awarded to Victor Ambros and Gary Ruvkun for discovering :
(1) Ribosomal RNA
(2) Micro RNAs
(3) Transfer RNA
(4) Messenger RNA
= 2
~ Ambros and Ruvkun won the 2024 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for discovering microRNA and its role in post-transcriptional gene regulation. MicroRNAs are tiny RNA molecules (about 22 nucleotides) that bind to messenger RNA and switch genes off; they were first found in the roundworm C. elegans.

Q 8 | sci | Chemistry
“Aqua Regia” is a mixture of which of the following ?
(1) Concentrated HCl and Concentrated HNO₃
(2) Concentrated HCl and Concentrated H₂SO₄
(3) HCl and HBr
(4) HCl and HF
= 1

Q 9 | sci | Physics

(1) 1 metre = 10^19 nanometre
(2) 1 metre = 10^90 nanometre

Q 10 | sci | Physics
Which of the following is not a luminous object ?
(1) Moon
(2) Sun
(3) Electric lamp
(4) Flame of candle
= 1
~ A luminous object emits its own light. The Sun, a glowing electric lamp and a candle flame produce light, but the Moon is non-luminous — it only reflects sunlight towards the Earth.

Q 11 | sci | Physics
The type of mirror used in the headlight of a car is :
(1) Concave mirror
(2) Convex mirror
(3) Plane mirror
(4) Plane convex mirror
= 1
~ Headlights use a concave (parabolic) reflector with the bulb at its focus: light from the focus is reflected as a strong parallel beam that lights the road far ahead. Convex mirrors, which give a wide field of view, are used as rear-view mirrors.

Q 12 | sci | Physics
The distance at which the average radius of Earth's orbit subtends an angle of 1 arc second, is known as :
(1) 1 astronomical unit
(2) 1 light year
(3) 1 Parsec
(4) 1 Fermi
= 3
~ A parsec ('parallax of one arc second') is the distance at which 1 astronomical unit — the mean Earth–Sun distance, i.e. the average radius of Earth's orbit — subtends an angle of 1″. 1 parsec ≈ 3.26 light years ≈ 3.09 × 10^16 m. A fermi (10^-15 m) is a nuclear-scale unit.

Q 18 | geo | Ecology, Population & Urbanisation
The ‘hydrological cycle’ is controlled by :
(1) Epiphytes
(2) Forests
(3) Lichens
(4) Grasslands
= 2
~ Forests regulate the water cycle: their canopy intercepts rain, roots and leaf litter help water soak in and recharge groundwater, and transpiration returns large amounts of moisture to the air, aiding rainfall. Deforestation therefore increases runoff, floods and droughts.

Q 20 | geo | Ecology, Population & Urbanisation
The name of the scientist who proposed the “Ten Percent Rule” of energy is :
(1) Lindeman
(2) Odum
(3) Tansley
(4) Haeckel
= 1
~ Raymond Lindeman (1942) proposed the ten per cent law: only about 10% of the energy at one trophic level is passed on to the next, the rest being lost mostly as heat. Tansley coined the term 'ecosystem' (1935), Haeckel coined 'ecology' (1866), and Odum is known for ecosystem ecology.

Q 21 | geo | Ecology, Population & Urbanisation
Plants that tolerate a wide range of temperatures throughout the year, are called :
(1) Eurythermal
(2) Stenothermal
(3) Microthermal
(4) Hekistothermal
= 1
~ 'Eury' means wide: eurythermal organisms tolerate a wide range of temperatures, while stenothermal ('steno' = narrow) ones survive only within a narrow range. Microthermal and hekistothermal plants are those adapted to low and very low (alpine/polar) temperatures respectively.

Q 25 | ment | Reasoning

Q 26 | ment | Mathematics
A bag contains ₹ 5, ₹ 2 and ₹ 1 coins in the ratio of 3 : 4 : 5. The total value of all the coins is ₹ 2,100. How many coins of ₹ 5 are there in the bag ?
(1) 225
(2) 210
(3) 245
(4) 250
= 1
~ Let the numbers of coins be 3k, 4k and 5k. Total value = 5(3k) + 2(4k) + 1(5k) = 15k + 8k + 5k = 28k = 2,100, so k = 75. Number of ₹ 5 coins = 3 × 75 = 225.

Q 28 | ment | Reasoning
In a queue of boys, A is seventh from the left and B is fourth from the right. When A and B interchange their positions, then A becomes fifteenth from the left. Find the position of B from the right.
(1) 12th
(2) 17th
(3) 20th
(4) 19th
= 1
~ After the swap A takes B's old place, so B's old place is 15th from the left and 4th from the right: total boys = 15 + 4 − 1 = 18. B now stands at A's old place, 7th from the left, i.e. 18 − 7 + 1 = 12th from the right.

Q 31 | sci | Physics
A MAC (Media Access Control) address is ______ bits long.
(1) 48
(2) 32
(3) 64
(4) 128
= 1
~ A MAC address is the 48-bit (6-byte) hardware address burnt into a network interface card, usually written as 12 hexadecimal digits such as 00:1A:2B:3C:4D:5E. By contrast, an IPv4 address is 32 bits and an IPv6 address is 128 bits.

Q 37 | sci | Biology
Given below are two Statements, one is labelled as Assertion (A) and other is labelled as Reason (R).
Assertion (A) : In photosynthesis, the radiation energy of sunlight is converted into chemical energy and stored in food materials.
Reason (R) : The final product of photosynthesis is glucose sugar, which soon turns into starch.
Select the correct answer using the codes given below :
(1) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of the Assertion (A).
(2) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of the Assertion (A).
(3) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false.
(4) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
= 1
~ Both statements are true. In photosynthesis (6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂) chlorophyll traps light energy and locks it in the chemical bonds of glucose, which is soon converted into starch for storage. Since the energy is stored precisely in this glucose/starch, UPPSC treats R as the correct explanation of A.

Q 44 | ment | Reasoning
The year 2036 will have the same calendar as that of :
(1) 2007
(2) 2006
(3) 2008
(4) 2009
= 3
~ 2036 is a leap year, so it can share its calendar only with a leap year starting on the same weekday. From 2008 to 2035 there are 28 years including 7 leap years: 28 + 7 = 35 odd days = 0 (mod 7). So 2036 repeats the calendar of 2008 (both begin on a Tuesday).

~ One whose mind stays the same in every place, time and situation is 'sthirchitt' (steady-minded). Samdarshi sees all alike, shaantchitt is calm-minded, and alaukik means supernatural.

Q 185 | hin | One-word Substitution
'उत्तर और पूर्व दिशा के मध्यवर्ती कोण' को क्या कहते हैं ?
(1) ईशान
(2) वायव्य
(3) नैऋत्य
(4) आग्नेय
= 1
~ The north-east corner is 'Ishaan'. Vaayavya is north-west, Nairritya south-west and Aagneya south-east.

Q 186 | hin | One-word Substitution
'हरावल' शब्द निम्नलिखित में से किस वाक्यांश के लिए प्रयुक्त होता है ?
(1) सेना का वह भाग जो सबसे आगे रहता है।
(2) सेना का वह भाग जो सबसे पीछे रहता है।
(3) जो स्वेच्छा से किसी सेवा कार्य में लगता है।
(4) सेना का वह भाग जो मध्य में रहता है।
= 1
~ 'Haraaval' means the vanguard, the part of an army that marches in front. The rear part is 'chandaaval', and one who serves of his own will is a 'svayamsevak'.

Q 187 | hin | One-word Substitution
'वाक्यांश तथा उसके लिए प्रयुक्त एक शब्द' का कौन-सा युग्म सुमेलित नहीं है ?
(1) किए हुए उपकार को मानने वाला – कृतज्ञ
(2) जानने की इच्छा रखने वाला – बुद्धिमान
(3) जिसका पार न पाया जाए – अपार
(4) जो इन्द्रियों के परे हो – अतीन्द्रिय
= 2
~ One who wishes to know is 'jigyaasu', not 'buddhimaan' (intelligent). Kritagya (grateful), apaar (boundless) and ateendriya (beyond the senses) are correctly matched.

Q 188 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
अधोलिखित में से 'तत्सम' शब्द को चिह्नित कीजिए :
(1) स्वामी
(2) बच्चा
(3) आग
(4) कान
= 1
~ 'Svaami' (master) is a tatsam word, taken unchanged from Sanskrit. Bachcha (from 'vatsa'), aag (from 'agni') and kaan (from 'karn') are tadbhav.

Q 189 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा विकल्प 'तत्सम' शब्द नहीं है ?
(1) हाथ
(2) अग्नि
(3) पुत्र
(4) जल
= 1
~ 'Haath' (hand) is tadbhav; its tatsam form is 'hast'. Agni, putra and jal are tatsam words.

Q 190 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
'नैहर' शब्द का तत्सम रूप है :
(1) पीहर
(2) ज्ञातिगृह
(3) नयघर
(4) नौघर
= 2
~ 'Naihar' (a married woman's parental home) comes from the Sanskrit 'gyaatigrih' (home of one's kin). 'Peehar' is itself a tadbhav (from 'pitrigrih').

Q 191 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
तत्सम-तद्भव का कौन-सा विकल्प सही है ?
(1) मधूक – भौंरा
(2) वल्गा – बाग
(3) वृत्तिक – गोला
(4) मत्सर – मछली
= 2
~ 'Valga' (reins) gives the tadbhav 'baag', so (b) is correct. Madhook gives 'mahua' (bhaunra comes from 'bhramar'), gola comes from 'golak', and machhli from 'matsya' ('matsar' means jealousy).

Q 192 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
निम्नलिखित में से कौन-सा 'तुरंत' का तत्सम रूप है ?
(1) तुरत
(2) त्वरित
(3) जल्दी
(4) शीघ्र
= 2
~ 'Turant' (at once) is the tadbhav of the Sanskrit 'tvarit'. 'Turat' is another tadbhav form, 'jaldi' is a Persian-origin word, and 'sheeghra' is a tatsam synonym, not its source.

Q 193 | hin | One-word Substitution
'जहाँ किसी बात का डर न हो' वाक्यांश के लिए उपयुक्त शब्द है :
(1) निर्द्वन्द्व
(2) द्विवर
(3) निरापद
(4) निर्भ्रान्त
= 3
~ A place or state free from any danger or fear is 'niraapad' (nir + aapad, safe). Nirdvandva means free from conflict or doubt, and nirbhraant means free from error or confusion.

Q 194 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
नीचे दिए गए शब्दों में से तत्सम नहीं है :
(1) मुख
(2) अगणित
(3) अकेला
(4) दाध
= 3
~ 'Akela' (alone) is a tadbhav word; its tatsam form is 'ekaaki' (or 'ekal'). Mukh (face) and aganit (countless) are tatsam; option (d) 'daadh' is apparently a misprint for the tatsam 'daagh' (heat).

Q 195 | hin | Opposites
'अग्रगामी' का विलोम शब्द है :
(1) अधोगामी
(2) ऊर्ध्वगामी
(3) पश्चगामी
(4) पूर्वगामी
= 3
~ 'Agragaami' (going ahead) has the opposite 'pashchagaami' (going behind). Adhogaami (going down) is the opposite of oordhvagaami (going up).

Q 196 | hin | Opposites
'बाह्य' एवं 'ग्राह्य' शब्द के विलोम का सही विकल्प है :
(1) अभाव, लभ्य
(2) ग्रहण, त्याज्य
(3) अभ्यन्तर, त्याज्य
(4) अग्राह्य, आविर्भाव
= 3
~ 'Baahya' (outer) has the opposite 'abhyantar' (inner), and 'graahya' (acceptable) has the opposite 'tyaajya' (to be given up). So (c) is correct.

Q 197 | hin | Opposites
निम्नलिखित में से अशुद्ध विलोम-युग्म है :
(1) वैकल्पिक – अनिवार्य
(2) गौरव – लाघव
(3) अथ – श्री गणेश
(4) मृदुल – रुक्ष
= 3
~ 'Ath' (beginning) and 'Shri Ganesh' (a beginning) mean the same thing; the opposite of 'ath' is 'iti' (end). Vaikalpik–anivaarya, gaurav–laaghav and mridul–ruksh (soft–rough) are correct pairs.

Q 198 | hin | Opposites
निम्नलिखित प्रदत्त विलोम-युग्मों में से अशुद्ध का चयन कीजिए :
(1) सदाचारी – दुर्व्यवहारी
(2) विधि – निषेध
(3) आज्ञा – अवज्ञा
(4) दक्षिण – वाम
= 1
~ The opposite of 'sadaachaari' (virtuous) is 'duraachaari' (wicked), not 'durvyavahaari' (ill-behaved; its pair is 'sadvyavahaari'). Vidhi–nishedh, aagya–avagya and dakshin–vaam are correct.

Q 199 | hin | Opposites
निम्नलिखित विलोम शब्द-युग्म में एक सही नहीं है :
(1) दीर्घ – ह्रस्व
(2) ऋजु – सरल
(3) आर्द्र – अनार्द्र
(4) अवर – प्रवर
= 2
~ 'Riju' means straight/simple, the same as 'saral', so they are synonyms; the opposite of 'riju' is 'vakra' (crooked). Deergh–hrasva, aardra–anaardra and avar–pravar are correct opposites.

Q 200 | hin | Opposites
विलोम की दृष्टि से निम्नलिखित में से सही शब्द-युग्म है :
(1) हताश – निराश
(2) आकुल – व्याकुल
(3) उत्तेजन – प्रशमन
(4) कटु – तिक्त
= 3
~ 'Uttejan' (excitement, provoking) and 'prashaman' (calming, pacifying) are opposites. The other pairs are synonyms: hataash = niraash (hopeless), aakul = vyaakul (restless), katu = tikt (bitter).
