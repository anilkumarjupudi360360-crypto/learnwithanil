# UPPSC Samiksha Adhikari/Sahayak Samiksha Adhikari Etc. (Gen./Spl. Rectt.) Preliminary Examination-2021, held 5 December 2021, General Hindi (GH-62), booklet Series B (a candidate's copy); answers: UPPSC's key sheet for Series B of 8 December 2021

Q 1 | hin | Opposites
'नैसर्गिक' का विपरीतार्थक शब्द है –
(1) अनुर्वर
(2) आविर्भाव
(3) प्राकृत
(4) कृत्रिम
= 4
~ 'Naisargik' (natural, produced by nature) is the opposite of 'kritrim' (artificial, man-made). 'Prakrit' means much the same as naisargik; 'anurvar' (infertile) is the opposite of 'urvar' and 'aavirbhav' (appearance) of 'tirobhav'.

Q 2 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
एक की वर्तनी शुद्ध है –
(1) अधिकारिक
(2) आधिकारिक
(3) अधिकारक
(4) अधीकारिक
= 2
~ Adding the suffix '-ik' lengthens the first vowel of the word (vriddhi: a → aa), as in samaj → samajik. So adhikar + ik = 'aadhikarik' (आधिकारिक) is the correct spelling; 'adhikarik', 'adhikarak' and 'adheekarik' are wrong forms.

Q 3 | hin | One-word Substitution
'वह कवि जो तत्काल कविता करे' के लिए एक शब्द है –
(1) सुकवि
(2) रससिद्ध कवि
(3) महाकवि
(4) आशुकवि
= 4
~ 'Aashu' means quick or instant, so a poet who composes verse on the spot is an 'aashukavi' (extempore poet). 'Sukavi' is simply a good poet and 'rassiddh kavi' a poet perfected in rasa.

Q 4 | hin | Synonyms
इनमें से 'वसुधा' का पर्यायवाची शब्द है –
(1) विपुला
(2) शर्वरी
(3) धेनुका
(4) पयस्विनी
= 1
~ 'Vasudha' means the earth, and 'vipula' is another name for the earth (like dhara, bhoo, mahi, avani). 'Sharvari' means night, while 'dhenuka' and 'payasvini' (milch) refer to a cow.

Q 5 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
'कर्पट' का तद्भव रूप है –
(1) कपट
(2) कारपेट
(3) कपूर
(4) कपड़ा
= 4
~ The tatsam 'karpat' (cloth) becomes the tadbhav 'kapda'. 'Kapur' comes from 'karpur' (camphor), 'kapat' is itself tatsam and 'carpet' is an English word.

Q 6 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
इनमें से 'खेलना' शब्द से बना विशेषण है –
(1) दयालु
(2) कृपालु
(3) खिलाड़ी
(4) धार्मिक
= 3
~ 'Khiladi' (one who plays) is formed from the verb 'khelna' (to play) with the suffix '-aadi'. Dayalu, kripalu and dharmik are adjectives formed from the nouns daya, kripa and dharma.

Q 7 | hin | Synonyms
'चन्द्रहास' का पर्यायवाची शब्द है –
(1) तीर
(2) तलवार
(3) भाला
(4) धनुष-बाण
= 2
~ 'Chandrahas' means a sword (it was also the name of Ravana's sword); other synonyms of talwar are asi, kripan, khadg and karwal.

Q 8 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
निम्नलिखित में से एक शब्द विशेषण नहीं है –
(1) अकूत
(2) अचार
(3) अकर्ण
(4) अचंड
= 2
~ 'Achaar' (pickle) is a noun. 'Akoot' (immeasurable), 'akarn' (earless, deaf) and 'achand' (not fierce, mild) are adjectives.

Q 9 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
'सौ गुना लम्बा' में विशेषण का कौन सा भेद है?
(1) गणनावाचक
(2) क्रमवाचक
(3) आवृत्तिवाचक
(4) सम्पूर्णतावाचक
= 3
~ Numeral adjectives with 'guna' (-fold), such as duguna, tiguna and 'sau guna' (hundredfold), are aavrittivachak (multiplicative). Ek/do are counting (gananavachak) and pehla/doosra ordinal (kramvachak).

Q 10 | hin | One-word Substitution
'सब कुछ जानने वाले' के लिए एक शब्द है –
(1) ज्ञानी
(2) महाज्ञानी
(3) सर्वज्ञ
(4) जानकार
= 3
~ 'Sarvagya' = sarva (all) + gya (knower): one who knows everything. Its opposite is 'alpagya'; 'gyani' or 'jaankar' only means a knowledgeable person.

Q 11 | hin | Opposites
इनमें से 'प्रथम' का उपयुक्त विपरीतार्थक शब्द होगा –
(1) प्रधान
(2) गौण
(3) अंतिम
(4) प्रत्यक्ष
= 3
~ 'Pratham' (first) is the opposite of 'antim' (last). 'Pradhan' (main) pairs with 'gaun' (secondary) and 'pratyaksh' (direct) with 'paroksh' (indirect).

Q 12 | hin | Sentence Correction
निम्नलिखित में एक वाक्य जो शुद्ध है, वह है –
(1) उसने मुक्तकण्ठ से बड़ाई की।
(2) उसने मुक्तहस्त धन लुटाया।
(3) समस्त प्राणिमात्र का कल्याण करो।
(4) आपकी आयु चालीस वर्ष है।
= 2
~ 'Muktahast' (open-handed) correctly goes with lavishing wealth, so (b) is right. (a) should read 'muktakanth se prashansa ki'; in (c) 'samast' and 'matra' repeat the same idea; in (d) 'aayu' means the whole lifespan, so 'avastha' (present age) is correct.

Q 13 | hin | One-word Substitution
'उत्तराधिकार में प्राप्त सम्पत्ति' – इन अनेक शब्दों के लिए एक शब्द है –
(1) धरोहर
(2) उत्तराधिकृत
(3) रिक्थ
(4) परिसम्पत्ति
= 3
~ Property received by inheritance is called 'rikth'. 'Dharohar' is something left in someone's keeping (a trust or deposit) and 'parisampatti' means assets in general.

Q 14 | hin | Synonyms
'चोर' का पर्यायवाची शब्द है –
(1) खनक
(2) उदक
(3) धूसर
(4) थलचर
= 1
~ 'Khanak' (one who digs a hole through a wall to break in) is a synonym of 'chor' (thief), like taskar and dasyu. 'Udak' means water, 'dhoosar' dust-coloured and 'thalchar' a land animal.

Q 15 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
'पाण्डु' शब्द विशेषण की दृष्टि से है –
(1) केवल विशेषण
(2) केवल विशेष्य
(3) विशेषण और विशेष्य दोनों
(4) उपर्युक्त में से एक भी नहीं
= 3
~ 'Pandu' as 'pale yellowish-white' is an adjective (vishesan), but it is also a noun (vishesya) — King Pandu of the Mahabharata, or 'pandu rog' (jaundice). So it is both.

Q 16 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
निम्नलिखित में से एक वर्तनी शुद्ध है –
(1) नुपूर
(2) स्मर्ण
(3) कवयित्री
(4) परिस्थिती
= 3
~ 'Kavayitri' (poetess) is spelt correctly. The others should be 'noopur' (anklet), 'smaran' (memory) and 'paristhiti' (circumstance, with a short final i).

Q 17 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
वर्तनी की दृष्टि से शुद्ध शब्द है –
(1) परिच्छा
(2) परीच्छा
(3) परीक्षा
(4) परिक्षा
= 3
~ Pari + eeksha (i + ee = ee, deergh sandhi) gives 'pareeksha': long 'ree' and the conjunct 'ksh', not 'chchh'. So परीक्षा is correct.

Q 18 | hin | One-word Substitution
'बिना पलक झपकाए' के लिए एक शब्द होगा –
(1) चकित
(2) देखते रहना
(3) विपलक
(4) निर्निमेष
= 4
~ 'Nimesh' is the blink of an eye, so 'nirnimesh' (also 'animesh') means without blinking, staring fixedly. 'Chakit' only means astonished.

Q 19 | hin | Opposites
'अनुरक्ति' का विपरीतार्थक शब्द है –
(1) आसक्ति
(2) प्रशस्ति
(3) विरक्ति
(4) प्रकृति
= 3
~ 'Anurakti' (attachment, affection) is the opposite of 'virakti' (detachment). 'Aasakti' means much the same as anurakti, and 'prashasti' means praise.

Q 20 | hin | Sentence Correction
निम्नांकित में एक वाक्य जो शुद्ध है, वह है –
(1) मैं अनेकों बार विदेश गया।
(2) इस हीरे का मूल्य नापा नहीं जा सकता।
(3) बिना टिकट यात्रा दण्डनीय है।
(4) आप केवल इतना ही काम कर दीजिए।
= 3
~ (c) is correct. (a) 'anek' is already plural, so 'anekon' is wrong; (b) value is 'aanka' (assessed), not 'napa' (measured); (d) 'keval' and 'hi' together are redundant.

Q 21 | hin | One-word Substitution
'जो ईश्वर में विश्वास रखता हो' के लिए एक शब्द है –
(1) आस्थावान
(2) आस्तिक
(3) विश्वासी
(4) आसुरी
= 2
~ One who believes in God is 'aastik' (opposite 'naastik'). 'Aasthavan' and 'vishwasi' mean faithful or trusting in general, and 'aasuri' means demonic.

Q 22 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
निम्नलिखित में 'विशेष्य' शब्द है –
(1) विपन्न
(2) वादी
(3) विशिष्ट
(4) मानस
= 4
~ A vishesya is the noun that an adjective qualifies. 'Maanas' (mind, heart) is a noun, whereas 'vipann' (distressed), 'vaadi' (one who argues/asserts) and 'vishisht' (special) are adjectives.

Q 23 | hin | Opposites
'अनुग्रह' का उपयुक्त विपरीतार्थक शब्द होगा –
(1) विग्रह
(2) आग्रह
(3) परिग्रह
(4) संग्रह
= 1
~ 'Anugrah' (favour, kindness) is the opposite of 'vigrah' (hostility, discord). Aagrah, parigrah and sangrah have their own opposites (duragrah, aparigrah, tyag).

Q 24 | hin | Opposites
इनमें से विलोम शब्दों का एक सही युग्म है –
(1) आपत्ति – विपत्ति
(2) अवर्षण – अनावर्षण
(3) गणतन्त्र – जनतन्त्र
(4) आदृत – तिरस्कृत
= 4
~ 'Aadrit' (honoured) and 'tiraskrit' (insulted, disdained) are opposites. The other pairs mean nearly the same thing: aapatti/vipatti (trouble), avarshan/anavarshan (lack of rain), gantantra/jantantra (people's rule).

Q 25 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
निम्नलिखित में 'हस्ती' किस शब्द का तत्सम रूप है?
(1) हाथ
(2) हार
(3) हाथी
(4) हँसी
= 3
~ 'Hasti' is the tatsam form of 'haathi' (elephant). 'Haath' comes from 'hast' and 'hansi' from 'haasya'.

Q 26 | hin | Opposites
'आधुनिक' का विलोम है –
(1) समीचीन
(2) अर्वाचीन
(3) प्राचीन
(4) समसामयिक
= 3
~ 'Aadhunik' (modern) is the opposite of 'prachin' (ancient). 'Arvachin' is a synonym of aadhunik, 'samsamayik' means contemporary and 'samichin' means proper.

Q 27 | hin | One-word Substitution
'जो युद्ध में स्थिर रहता है' इन अनेक शब्दों के लिए एक शब्द है –
(1) युद्ध स्थित
(2) युद्ध स्थिरी
(3) युधिष्ठिर
(4) युद्ध प्रेमी
= 3
~ Yudhi (in battle) + sthir (steady) = 'Yudhishthir', one who stays firm in battle; in the sandhi 's' becomes 'sh' and 'th' becomes 'thh'. It is also the name of the eldest Pandava.

Q 28 | hin | Synonyms
'जीभ' का पर्यायवाची है –
(1) वचन
(2) रसना
(3) ध्वनि
(4) जीव
= 2
~ 'Rasna' (that which tastes) is a synonym of 'jeebh' (tongue), along with jihva and rasagya. 'Vachan' (speech) and 'dhvani' (sound) are only related ideas, and 'jeev' (living being) merely looks like 'jeebh'.

Q 29 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
'प्रताप सिंह का घोड़ा काला है।' – इनमें 'काला' शब्द विशेषण की दृष्टि से है –
(1) सार्वनामिक
(2) क्रमबोधक
(3) विधेय विशेषण
(4) विशेष्य विशेषण
= 3
~ An adjective placed after its noun, in the predicate before the verb, is a 'vidheya visheshan' (predicative adjective): 'ghoda kaala hai'. In 'kaala ghoda' it would be an attributive (vishesya) adjective.

Q 30 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
'प्रयागराज में दसवाँ व्यक्ति कोरोना पीड़ित है।', में 'दसवाँ' शब्द है –
(1) गणनावाचक विशेषण
(2) क्रमवाचक विशेषण
(3) आवृत्तिवाचक विशेषण
(4) समुदायवाचक विशेषण
= 2
~ 'Daswan' (tenth) gives the order of the person, so it is an ordinal (kramvachak) adjective. 'Das' (ten) would be cardinal, 'dasguna' multiplicative and 'dason' collective.

Q 31 | hin | Synonyms
'तिमिर' का पर्यायवाची है –
(1) प्रकाश
(2) रात्रि
(3) सूर्य
(4) अन्धकार
= 4
~ 'Timir' means darkness (andhkar), like tam and dhvaant. 'Prakash' (light) is its opposite, and 'ratri' (night) is a different word.

Q 32 | hin | Synonyms
'हुताशन' पर्यायवाची है –
(1) निर्धन
(2) अग्नि
(3) अगणित
(4) अखंड
= 2
~ 'Hutashan' = huta (oblation) + ashan (eater): the one who consumes offerings, i.e. fire (agni). Other synonyms are anal, pavak and vahni.

Q 33 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
निम्नलिखित में से किस शब्द की वर्तनी अशुद्ध है?
(1) याज्ञवल्क
(2) सदृश
(3) हस्तक्षेप
(4) गृहिणी
= 1
~ The sage's name is correctly spelt 'Yagyavalkya' (ending in 'kya'), so 'याज्ञवल्क' is wrong. Sadrish, hastakshep and grihini are spelt correctly.

Q 34 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
'चौथा' का तत्सम शब्द है –
(1) चतुष्पद
(2) चतुष्क
(3) चतुष्काठ
(4) चतुर्थ
= 4
~ 'Chautha' (fourth) comes from the tatsam 'chaturth'. Chatushpad gives 'chaupaya' (quadruped), chatushk gives 'chauk' and chatushkashth gives 'chaukhat' (door-frame).

Q 35 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
'घोड़ा' का उपयुक्त तत्सम शब्द है –
(1) अश्व
(2) घोटक
(3) तुरंग
(4) बाजी
= 2
~ 'Ghoda' (horse) developed from the tatsam 'ghotak' (t → d). Ashva, turang and baji are synonyms for horse, not the source of the word 'ghoda'.

Q 36 | hin | Opposites
'आवर्तक' का विपरीतार्थक शब्द है –
(1) प्रवर्तक
(2) अनावर्तक
(3) आवृत
(4) प्रकर्षक
= 2
~ 'Aavartak' (recurring) is the opposite of 'anavartak' (non-recurring), as in recurring vs non-recurring expenditure. 'Pravartak' means founder or initiator.

Q 37 | hin | One-word Substitution
'जो किसी विषय का ज्ञाता हो' के लिए एक शब्द है –
(1) अभिज्ञ
(2) चैतन्य
(3) विशिष्ट
(4) विशेषज्ञ
= 4
~ One who is an expert in a particular subject is a 'visheshagya' (vishesh + gya). 'Abhigya' means generally aware, 'chaitanya' conscious and 'vishisht' special.

Q 38 | hin | Synonyms
'बिजली' का पर्याय है –
(1) शर्वरी
(2) तनया
(3) रमणी
(4) दामिनी
= 4
~ 'Damini' means lightning (bijli), like chapla, tadit and vidyut. 'Sharvari' means night, 'tanaya' daughter and 'ramani' a beautiful woman.

Q 39 | hin | Synonyms
'अपर्णा' शब्द का पर्यावाची है –
(1) औरत
(2) देवांगना
(3) आराधना
(4) पार्वती
= 4
~ 'Aparna' is a name of Parvati, who gave up even eating leaves (parna) during her penance for Shiva. Other synonyms are Uma, Gauri, Girija and Bhavani.

Q 40 | hin | Synonyms
निम्नलिखित विकल्पों में से 'चंचरीक' शब्द का पर्यायवाची चुनिए –

(2) भ्रमर
(3) मित्र
(4) पुत्र
= 2
~ 'Chanchareek' means the black bee, so it is a synonym of 'bhramar'; others are ali, madhup, madhukar, shatpad and bhring.

Q 41 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
'गोधूम' का तद्भव शब्द है –
(1) गोत
(2) गोद
(3) गेहूँ
(4) गोह
= 3
~ The tatsam 'godhoom' becomes the tadbhav 'gehoon' (wheat). Similarly 'gotra' gives 'got' and 'krod' gives 'god' (lap).

Q 42 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
'कोई आदमी आया है' वाक्य में प्रयुक्त विशेषण है –
(1) अनिश्चयवाचक
(2) निश्चयवाचक
(3) प्रश्नवाचक
(4) संबंधवाचक
= 1
~ 'Koi' before the noun 'aadmi' is a pronominal adjective that points to no particular person, so it is indefinite (anishchayvachak). 'Yah aadmi' would be definite and 'kaun aadmi?' interrogative.

Q 43 | hin | Opposites

(2) लाघव
(3) पराभव
(4) निर्मोक
= 2
~ 'Gaurav' (from guru: weight, greatness) is the opposite of 'laaghav' (from laghu: lightness, smallness). 'Vaibhav' is splendour, 'parabhav' defeat and 'nirmok' a snake's slough.

Q 44 | hin | Opposites

(4) विष
= 4
~ 'Amrit' (nectar) is the opposite of 'vish' (poison); 'piyush' and 'sudha' are synonyms of amrit.

Q 45 | hin | Opposites

(4) अन्याय
= 2
~ 'Sadachar' (good conduct) is the opposite of 'kadachar' (misconduct), also 'durachar'. 'Aachar' just means conduct.

Q 46 | hin | Word Usage & Grammar
निम्नलिखित में से तद्भव शब्द है –

(2) गयंद

(4) वानर

Q 47 | hin | One-word Substitution
'जो तोला जा सके' के लिए एक शब्द होगा –
(1) अपरिमेय
(2) अनुमेय
(3) परिमेय

Q 58 | hin | Sentence Correction
निम्नलिखित में एक वाक्य जो शुद्ध है, वह है –
(1) उस जंगल में प्रातःकाल का दृश्य बहुत ही सुहावना होता था।
(2) बाघ और बकरी एक घाट पर पानी पीती हैं।
(3) उन्होंने इस बात पर आपत्ति प्रकट की।
(4) तमाम देश भर में यह बात फैल गयी।
= 1
~ (a) is correct: 'bahut hi suhavana' is a normal emphatic use. In (b), subjects of different genders joined by 'aur' take a masculine plural verb ('peete hain'); in (d) 'tamam' and 'bhar' say the same thing twice ('desh bhar mein'). (c) is treated as faulty because the usual idiom is simply 'aapatti ki' — 'prakat' is unnecessary.
