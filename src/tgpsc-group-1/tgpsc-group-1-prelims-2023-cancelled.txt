Q 27 | sci | Science & Technology
Integrated chip is produced on a piece of semi-conductor crystal by the process of
(1) Embedding
(2) Photolithography
(3) Tomography
(4) Cryptography
= 2
~ Integrated circuits are made on a wafer of semiconductor crystal (usually silicon) by photolithography: light passed through a patterned mask transfers the circuit pattern onto a light-sensitive photoresist, and the exposed areas are then etched and doped to build transistors and connections layer by layer. Tomography is an imaging technique and cryptography is the science of coding information; embedding is not the chip-making process.

Q 29 | sci | Biology & Health
The temperature in human body is controlled by
(1) Hypothalamus
(2) Skin
(3) Cerebellum
(4) Muscles
= 1
~ The hypothalamus, a part of the forebrain, works as the body's thermostat: it receives signals from temperature receptors and triggers sweating and widening of skin blood vessels to lose heat, or shivering and narrowing of vessels to save heat. The skin and muscles only carry out these responses, and the cerebellum controls balance and coordination of movement.

Q 30 | sci | Chemistry
The chemical used in airbags of vehicles that releases nitrogen to explode balloons in case of accidents is
(1) Sodium azide
(2) Sodium nitrite
(3) Silver nitrate
(4) Potassium nitrate
= 1
~ Air bags contain sodium azide (NaN3). In a crash a sensor fires an igniter and the azide decomposes almost instantly (2NaN3 → 2Na + 3N2), and the nitrogen gas released inflates the bag within about 30 milliseconds. Potassium nitrate in the mixture only converts the reactive sodium into harmless oxides, and sodium nitrite and silver nitrate are not used for this.

Q 33 | geo | World Geography
The sequence of geological composition from the depth to the surface of the Earth is :
(1) Crust → Mantle → Inner core → Outer core
(2) Mantle → Crust → Inner core → Outer core
(3) Outer core → Inner core → Crust → Mantle
(4) Inner core → Outer core → Mantle → Crust
= 4
~ The Earth's interior has a solid inner core (about 5,150 to 6,371 km deep), a liquid outer core (about 2,900 to 5,150 km), the mantle (from the Moho discontinuity down to about 2,900 km) and the thin crust at the surface. Going from the depth to the surface, the order is inner core, outer core, mantle and crust, which is option (4).

Q 36 | sci | Biology & Health
Arrange the following in order with respect to the urine formation in human beings :
A. Formation of concentrated urine
B. Tubular reabsorption
C. Glomerular filtration
D. Tubular secretion
Choose the correct order :
(1) A, C, B, D
(2) C, B, D, A
(3) B, A, D, C
(4) A, D, B, C
= 2
~ Urine forms in the nephron in steps: glomerular filtration in Bowman's capsule produces the filtrate; tubular reabsorption returns water, glucose, amino acids and salts to the blood; tubular secretion adds H+, K+ and ammonia to the filtrate; and finally water is withdrawn in the loop of Henle and collecting duct to give concentrated urine. The order is C, B, D, A.
