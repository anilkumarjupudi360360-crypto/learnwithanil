Choose the correct answer :
(1) A only
(2) B only

Q 17 | irl | International Relations & Organisations
Which of the following organisation has jurisdiction over the Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide, 1948, also known as Genocide Convention ?
(1) International Court of Justice (ICJ)
(2) Permanent Court of Arbitration (PCA)
(3) International Criminal Court (ICC)
(4) United Nations General Assembly (UNGA)
= 1,3
~ TGPSC accepted options (1) and (3) because both courts deal with genocide. Under Article IX of the Genocide Convention, disputes between States about it go to the ICJ (for example, The Gambia v. Myanmar and South Africa v. Israel), while the ICC, set up by the Rome Statute (1998), tries individuals for the crime of genocide. The PCA and the UN General Assembly have no such jurisdiction.

Q 19 | irl | International Relations & Organisations
Which of the following statements about the United Nations (UN) are correct ?
A. The United Nations has six principal organs.
B. The General Assembly is composed of representatives from all member states, each of which has an equal vote.
C. The International Criminal Court (ICC), being an organ of UN, settles disputes between nations.
D. The main function of UN Economic and Social Council is facilitating discussion and policy recommendations for the world’s economic and social issues.

(1) A and B only
(2) C and D only
(3) A, B and D only

= 3
~ The UN has six principal organs: General Assembly, Security Council, Economic and Social Council, Trusteeship Council, International Court of Justice and Secretariat (A correct). Q 23 | sci | Biology & Health
Which of the following hormone is insect specific and not exclusively a plant hormone ?
(1) Auxin
(2) Juvenile
(3) Gibberellin
(4) Cytokinin
= 2
~ Juvenile hormone is secreted by the corpora allata of insects; it keeps larval characters during moults and, together with ecdysone, controls metamorphosis. It is an insect hormone, not a plant hormone. Auxin, gibberellin and cytokinin are plant growth hormones that control cell elongation, stem growth and cell division.

Q 25 | sci | Science & Technology
Which one of the following is currently the leading energy storage system type for use in electric vehicles ?
(1) Lead-acid batteries
(2) Lithium-ion batteries
(3) Nickel-metal hydride batteries
(4) Ultracapacitors
= 2

Q 149 | men | Arithmetic

(1) 5
(2) 7
(3) 6
(4) 3
= 2
~ Let b students take both subjects. Only Chemistry = 2b, so Chemistry in all = 2b + b = 3b. Only Physics = 2 × 3b = 6b, so Physics in all = 6b + b = 7b, i.e. 7 times the number taking both.

Q 150 | men | Arithmetic
Consider the following addition :

+ | C | A | B | 8 | B
 | B | 2 | 9 | 2 | C
where A, B and C represent three different digits from 0 to 9.
Which of the following two inequalities is/are correct ?
I. A + B – C ≤ 7
II. 2A – 3B < 4C
(1) Only I is correct.
(2) Only II is correct.
(3) Both I and II are correct.
(4) Neither I nor II is correct.
= 3
~ Hundreds: 2B + carry ends in 9, so the carry is 1 and B is 4 or 9. Units 9 + B carries 1, so tens C + 8 + 1 = 12 gives C = 3, and then 9 + B = 13 gives B = 4.
