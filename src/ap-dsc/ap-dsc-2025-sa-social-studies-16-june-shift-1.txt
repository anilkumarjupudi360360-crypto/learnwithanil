Q 31 | psy | Learning & Learning Theories
This method is also called as ‘Saving Method’
(1) Recall Method
(2) Relearning Method
(3) Retention Method
(4) Recognition Method
= 2
~ In Ebbinghaus’s relearning method, material learnt earlier is learnt again after an interval; the trials or time saved compared with the original learning measure retention, so it is called the saving method: Savings % = (original trials − relearning trials)/original trials × 100. Recall asks for reproduction without cues and recognition asks to pick out learnt items.
