(2) a, b

Q 30 | psy | Intelligence & Individual Differences
Identify correct statement regarding Normal probability curve
a. The Skewness is equal to 263
b. This is equal to 6 standard deviations
c. This is also called Gaussian curve
d. This is symmetric in shape
(1) a, b, d
(2) a, b, c
(3) c, d
(4) b, c, d
= 3,4
~ The final key accepts both (3) and (4). The normal curve is also called the Gaussian curve and is perfectly symmetrical (skewness = 0), so c and d are correct; a is wrong, since 0.263 is its kurtosis, not skewness. Statement b is loosely true: practically the whole curve (99.73%) lies within ±3σ, a span of 6 standard deviations.

Q 39 | psy | Learning & Learning Theories
Reason for progression in positively accelerated learning curve is
(1) Increase in number of trails
(2) Increase in time
(3) Increase in number of correct responses
(4) Performance is directly proportional to number of trails
= 3,4
~ The final key accepts both (3) and (4). In a positively accelerated (concave) learning curve progress is slow at first and then rises quickly: with practice the number of correct responses keeps increasing, and performance rises along with the number of trials. Trials or time by themselves do not explain the upward progress.

Q 40 | psy | Learning & Learning Theories
A boy learnt an essay. According to Ebbinghaus, the percentage of information he forgets after 31 days.
(1) 66%
(2) 75%
(3) 80%
(4) 95%
= 3
~ Ebbinghaus's forgetting curve shows that only about 21% of learnt material is retained after 31 days, i.e. about 79% (≈ 80%) is forgotten. Most of the forgetting happens in the first hours and days.

~ In the chain of markets, goods move from producers (industries, farms) to distribution points such as company depots, agencies or market yards; the wholesaler buys in bulk there and supplies retail shops, which sell to consumers. Retail shops come after the wholesaler, not before.
