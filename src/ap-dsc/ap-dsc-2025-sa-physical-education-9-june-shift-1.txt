Q 24 | ped | Organisation, Management & Recreation
Every Team shall play Twice with every other team means
(1) Single league
(2) Round robin
(3) Double league
(4) Knock out
= 3
~ In a double league tournament every team plays every other team twice, so the number of matches is n(n−1). In a single league (round robin) each team meets every other team only once, n(n−1)/2 matches, and in a knock-out a losing team is eliminated.

Q 25 | ped | Organisation, Management & Recreation
The following method in league does not indicate number of rounds to be played
(1) Cycle method
(2) Tabular method
(3) Elimination method
(4) Stair case method
= 4
~ In the staircase method the fixtures are written as steps (1-2, 1-3, 1-4 … then 2-3, 2-4 …), which list all the matches but do not show the rounds in which they are played. The cyclic method arranges fixtures round by round, and in the tabular method the number in each square shows the round. Elimination is a knock-out method, not a league method.

Q 29 | ped | Pedagogy & Methods
Method of teaching the individual skills of a major game
(1) Whole method
(2) Command method
(3) Imitation method
(4) Part method
= 4
~ Major games involve complex skills, so each skill is broken into parts (e.g., a basketball lay-up: dribble, two steps, jump and shot) and each part is taught and practised separately before they are combined – the part method. The whole method suits simple activities that cannot be split, and the command method suits drill and marching.

= 2

Q 41 | ped | Organisation, Management & Recreation
In which quarter and which serial number “the 7th Bye” is posted in single Knock-out tournament where 19 teams are participating
(1) II quarter & serial no. 10
(2) IV quarter & serial no. 16
(3) I quarter & serial no. 1
(4) III quarter & serial no.11
= 4
~ 19 teams need 32 − 19 = 13 byes (7 in the lower half, 6 in the upper half). Teams 1–10 form the upper half (I quarter 1–5, II quarter 6–10) and teams 11–19 the lower half (III quarter 11–15, IV quarter 16–19). In the commonly taught quarter-wise order (19, 1, 11, 10, 15, 6, 16 …) team 11 receives the 3rd bye and the 7th goes to team 16 (IV quarter). The official answer places this bye at the top of the lower half – III quarter, serial no. 11. (Official key; some sources differ.)

Q 43 | ped | Organisation, Management & Recreation
The formula for find out the number of matches in a double elimination tournaments
(1) (n−1)/2 or (n−1)/1
(2) (2n−2) or (2n−1)
(3) (2n−1) or (2n−3)
(4) (n−1) or (2h−1)
= 2
~ In a double-elimination tournament a team goes out only after two defeats. Total matches = 2(n−1) = 2n−2, and one more match (2n−1) is needed if the losers'-bracket winner beats the winners'-bracket champion in the final. n−1 is the single-knockout total.

Q 44 | ped | Organisation, Management & Recreation
In which round “Bye” is given in a single knockout tournament
(1) Third round
(2) Second round
(3) Fourth round
(4) First round
= 4
~ Byes are given only in the first round, so that the number of teams entering the second round becomes a power of 2 (2, 4, 8, 16…); after that no byes are needed. Number of byes = next higher power of 2 − number of teams.

Q 53 | ped | Organisation, Management & Recreation

Q 54 | ped | Pedagogy & Methods
The change from one exercise to another exercise should be indicated by this command in calisthenics.

(2) Change-Next
(3) Change-Change
(4) Next-Change
= 4
~ In calisthenics the shift to a new exercise is signalled by 'Next – Change': 'Next' warns the group that a new exercise is coming, and on 'Change' all switch to it together without breaking the rhythm. Counts such as 'Three-Four' only keep time.

Q 55 | ped | Pedagogy & Methods
Number of parts consists in Response command
(1) 4
(2) 3
(3) 5
(4) 2
= 2
~ A response command has three parts: explanation (a short, clear description of the movement), pause (time for the class to picture the movement) and execution (a sharp word or count that starts it), e.g. 'Arms forward upward … (pause) … Raise'. A rhythmic command has five parts.

= 2
