Q 1 | gk | Polity & Economy
The first Indian Constitutional amendment was related to
(1) Tribal rights
(2) Fundamental rights
(3) Formation of Sikkim State
(4) Age limit for voting
= 2
~ The First Amendment Act, 1951 dealt with Fundamental Rights: it added reasonable restrictions on free speech (Art. 19), allowed special provisions for backward classes (Art. 15(4)) and protected land-reform laws (Arts. 31A, 31B, Ninth Schedule). Sikkim became a state by the 36th Amendment (1975); the voting age was lowered to 18 by the 61st (1988).

Q 33 | psy | Mental Health & Adjustment
A boy was scolded by his mother for no reason. Unable to react against his mother, he showed his anger toward his younger brother. Here, the defense mechanism used by the boy is
(1) Regression
(2) Repression
(3) Compensation
(4) Displacement
= 4
~ Displacement is redirecting an emotion, usually anger, from its real source to a safer, less threatening target – here from the mother to the younger brother. Regression is returning to childish behaviour, repression is pushing painful thoughts into the unconscious, and compensation is making up for a weakness by excelling elsewhere.

Q 34 | psy | Learning & Learning Theories
In the learning curve, the stage almost parallel to X-axis is
(1) Initial spurt
(2) Stage of fluctuations
(3) Plateau stage
(4) Physiological limit
= 3
~ A plateau is a stage in the learning curve where, despite practice, there is little or no improvement for some time, so the curve runs almost flat, parallel to the X-axis (time/trials). The initial spurt shows a steep rise, and the physiological limit is the final ceiling of performance.

Q 37 | psy | Intelligence & Individual Differences

Q 38 | psy | Learning & Learning Theories
A boy learned to pronounce ‘would’. Now he has the learn to pronunciation of the word ‘could’. Here, the transfer of learning will is
(1) Positive
(2) Negative
(3) Zero
(4) Bilateral
= 1
~ 'would' and 'could' share the same spelling pattern and pronunciation (silent 'l', 'ood' sound), so learning one makes learning the other easier – this is positive transfer. Negative transfer would occur if the first learning interfered, and bilateral transfer is between the two sides of the body (e.g. right hand to left hand).

Q 40 | psy | Growth & Development
