Q 2 | gk | Geography & Environment
Pacific Ocean is named by
(1) Columbus
(2) Magellan
(3) Amerigo Vespucci
(4) Leif Erikson
= 2
~ Ferdinand Magellan, crossing it in 1520–21 after the stormy strait at South America's tip, found the waters calm and called the ocean 'Mar Pacifico' (peaceful sea). Columbus reached the Americas, Amerigo Vespucci gave his name to America, and Leif Erikson reached North America around 1000 CE.

Q 35 | psy | Learning & Learning Theories

= 1

Q 51 | bio | Ecology & Environment
The effective Biological control methods adopted to control pests are
i) Introducing Natural predators
ii) Sterility of Males
iii) Crop rotation
iv) Crop holiday
(1) i, ii
(2) iv, iii
(3) i, ii, iv
(4) i, ii, iii
= 4
~ Introducing natural predators (e.g. ladybird beetles against aphids), releasing sterilised males so that pest females lay infertile eggs, and crop rotation, which breaks the pest's life cycle by changing its host plant, are biological pest-control methods. A crop holiday (leaving the land uncultivated for a season) is a farming/economic decision rather than a biological control method.

Q 52 | bio | Plant Biology
Biomass is generated during the process of
(1) Respiration
(2) Photosynthesis
(3) Transpiration
(4) Reproduction
= 2
~ In photosynthesis green plants convert light energy, CO₂ and water into glucose and other organic matter, which builds up as biomass (primary production). Respiration breaks down organic matter and reduces biomass, and transpiration is only loss of water vapour.

Q 53 | bio | Plant Biology
The tissue that increases the girth of the stem is
(1) Apical meristem
(2) Lateral meristem
(3) Intercalary meristem
(4) Parenchyma
= 2
~ Lateral meristems (vascular cambium and cork cambium) divide to form secondary tissues, so the stem and root grow in girth (thickness). Apical meristem increases length at the tips, and intercalary meristem (at internodes/leaf bases) adds length in the middle.
