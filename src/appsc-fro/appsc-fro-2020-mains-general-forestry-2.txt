Q 1 | bdc | Biomes & Population Ecology
The grasslands found in Argentina are called:
(1) savannas
(2) prairies
(3) steppes
(4) pampas
= 4
~ The pampas are the temperate grasslands of Argentina, extending into Uruguay and southern Brazil. Prairies are the North American temperate grasslands, steppes are the Eurasian ones, and savannas are tropical grasslands with scattered trees (as in Africa).

Q 2 | bdc | Biomes & Population Ecology
The various forms of a species to meet the challenges of changing environment that arise by reorganisation of their genes during sexual reproduction are called:
(1) ecads
(2) ecotypes
(3) pioneers
(4) seres
= 2
~ Ecotypes are genetically distinct local populations of a species adapted to particular habitats; their genetic variation arises through recombination of genes in sexual reproduction followed by natural selection. Ecads are environmentally induced, non-heritable forms, so they do not fit "reorganisation of genes". Pioneers and seres relate to ecological succession.

Q 3 | ecw | Ecosystem Concepts
A standing dead tree devoid of leaves and branches is called:
(1) litter
(2) down woody
(3) talus
(4) snag
= 4
~ A snag is a standing dead (or dying) tree that has lost its leaves and most branches; it is valuable habitat for cavity-nesting birds and other wildlife. Litter and down woody material lie on the ground, and talus is a slope of broken rock debris.

Q 4 | ecw | Wildlife & Protected Areas
Skinner's box is associated with which type of behaviour?
(1) Operant conditioning
(2) Trial and error learning
(3) Classical conditioning
(4) Habituation
= 1
~ B.F. Skinner's operant conditioning chamber (Skinner box) lets an animal such as a rat press a lever to obtain a reward, showing that behaviour is shaped by its consequences. Classical conditioning is associated with Pavlov, and trial and error learning with Thorndike's puzzle box.

Q 5 | bdc | Biomes & Population Ecology
In India, phytosociological approach to study plant communities in grasslands was used for the first time by:
(1) Harry George Champion
(2) Norman Loftus Bor
(3) Jagat Singh
(4) Shankar Purushottam Agharkar
= 4
~ APPSC's key is Shankar Purushottam Agharkar, the Indian botanist who is credited in this question with the first phytosociological (plant-community) approach to Indian grasslands. Champion is known for his forest-type classification and Bor for his work on grass taxonomy, so they are the tempting wrong options. (Official key; some sources differ.)

Q 6 | bdc | Biomes & Population Ecology
In age structure studies, an urn shaped figure indicates:
(1) declining population
(2) stable population
(3) young population
(4) clumped population
= 1
~ An urn-shaped age pyramid has a narrow base and a wider middle, meaning few young individuals are being added to replace the reproductive and older age classes, so the population is declining. A broad base indicates a young, expanding population, and a bell shape a stable one. "Clumped" describes spatial distribution, not age structure.

Q 7 | ens | Climate & Global Issues
Which of the following is NOT a man-made disaster in India?
(1) Boat capsizing
(2) Forest fire
(3) Building collapse
(4) Cyclones
= 4
~ Cyclones are natural, hydro-meteorological disasters caused by atmospheric and oceanic processes. In the High Powered Committee classification of disasters in India, boat capsizes, major building collapse and forest fires are listed among the accident-related (man-made) disasters.

Q 8 | fpt | Pests & Diseases
______ invades monoculture of Ailanthus excels causing defoliation.
(1) Ascotis imparta
(2) Tonica niviferana
(3) Atteva fabriciella
(4) Plecoptera reflexa
= 3
~ Atteva fabriciella (the ailanthus webworm moth) is the defoliator of Ailanthus excelsa; its larvae web and feed on the leaves, and outbreaks are common in pure plantations. Plecoptera reflexa, for example, is a known defoliator of Dalbergia sissoo, not Ailanthus.

Q 9 | ecw | Wildlife & Protected Areas
______ is an example of secondary hole nesters.
(1) Indian pangolin
(2) Rose ringed parakeet
(3) Greater yellow naped woodpecker
(4) Pygmy hog
= 2
~ Secondary hole nesters do not excavate their own cavities but use holes already made by others; the rose-ringed parakeet nests in existing tree hollows, often old woodpecker or barbet holes. The greater yellow-naped woodpecker is a primary excavator, while the pangolin and pygmy hog are mammals, not birds.

Q 10 | fim | Remote Sensing & GIS
What are the names of the satellite-based modules used for near real-time monitoring of forest fires by the Forest Survey of India?
(1) MODIS and SNPP-VIIRS
(2) MODIS and SNPV
(3) LISS III and MODIS
(4) LISS III and SNPV
= 1
~ The Forest Survey of India's Forest Fire Alert System detects active fires in near real time from the MODIS (Terra/Aqua) and SNPP-VIIRS (Suomi NPP) sensors. LISS III is a mapping sensor used for forest cover assessment, not for fire alerts, and "SNPV" is not a satellite sensor.

Q 11 | anm | Animal Health & Nutrition
Chloral hydrate used for wild animal restraint is an example of:
(1) tranquilizers
(2) neuroleptics
(3) hypnotics and sedatives
(4) dissociative anaesthetics
= 3
~ Chloral hydrate is an old hypnotic and sedative that depresses the central nervous system to induce sleep or calm. Tranquilizers and neuroleptics are drugs such as acepromazine, and dissociative anaesthetics are drugs such as ketamine.

Q 12 | pls | Genetics & Plant Diseases
Inheritance of blood group can be explained by which of the following?
(1) Quantitative inheritance
(2) Multiple alleles
(3) Incomplete dominance
(4) Gene linkage
= 2
~ ABO blood groups are controlled by a single gene locus with three alleles (IA, IB and i), which is the classic example of multiple alleles; IA and IB are also codominant. Quantitative inheritance involves many genes giving continuous variation, which does not apply to discrete blood groups.

Q 13 | pls | Genetics & Plant Diseases
A photographic image that shows the sum of all the chromosome information in an individual cell is called:
(1) Southern blot
(2) Idiotype
(3) Cartomorph
(4) Karyotype
= 4
~ A karyotype is the photograph or image of the complete chromosome set of a cell, with chromosomes arranged in pairs by size and centromere position. A Southern blot is a DNA hybridisation technique, and "cartomorph" is not a standard genetic term.

Q 15 | anm | Economic Zoology
SI = D – b (M – A). D = daughter’s average; M = dam’s average; A = herd average; b = regression coefficient of daughters’ yield on dam’s yield.
What is this index known as?
(1) Simple Daughter Average Index
(2) Krishna's Index
(3) Heizer's Index
(4) Gifford's Index
= 2
~ This is Krishna's index, also called the corrected daughter average: the daughters' average (D) is corrected for how far their dams were above or below the herd (M – A), using the regression coefficient b of daughters' yield on dam's yield. The Simple Daughter Average Index uses only D and ignores the dams' performance.

Q 16 | anm | Economic Zoology
Which of the following is a dual-purpose breed of India?
(1) Red Sindhi
(2) Sahiwal
(3) Rathi
(4) Gir
= 3
~ Rathi, from the arid tracts of Rajasthan, is listed in many textbooks as a dual-purpose breed, giving fair milk yield and useful draught bullocks. Red Sindhi, Sahiwal and Gir are classic milch (dairy) breeds of Indian zebu cattle. (Official key; some sources differ.)

Q 17 | anm | Animal Health & Nutrition
What is the percentage of crude protein in energy-rich concentrates?
(1) Less than 25%
(2) Less than 60%
(3) Less than 35%
(4) Less than 18%
= 4
~ Energy-rich concentrates (cereal grains, brans, molasses) are low in crude protein, and APPSC's key puts the limit at less than 18%. Protein-rich concentrates such as oilseed cakes are those with more than about 20% crude protein; 18% is the usual crude-fibre limit that separates concentrates from roughages. (Official key; some sources differ.)

Q 20 | anm | Animal Health & Nutrition
Parakeratosis in pigs is due to the deficiency of which mineral?
(1) Phosphorus
(2) Iron
(3) Calcium
(4) Zinc
= 4
~ Parakeratosis in pigs (scaly, crusty skin lesions with poor growth) is caused by zinc deficiency, and is aggravated by excess dietary calcium, which reduces zinc availability. Iron deficiency causes anaemia in piglets.

Q 21 | anm | Animal Health & Nutrition
Perosis in young chicks is due to the deficiency of which mineral?
(1) Magnesium
(2) Cobalt
(3) Selenium
(4) Manganese
= 4
~ Perosis (slipped tendon) is a leg deformity of young chicks caused mainly by manganese deficiency, which impairs bone and cartilage development; choline and biotin deficiencies can also contribute. Selenium deficiency causes exudative diathesis, not perosis.

Q 23 | anm | Animal Health & Nutrition
Which of the following does Prosthogonimus species infect?
(1) Bull
(2) Steer
(3) Male gallinaceous birds
(4) female gallinaceous birds
= 4
~ Prosthogonimus is the oviduct fluke of birds; it lives in the oviduct and bursa of Fabricius of hens and other female domestic fowl, causing abnormal or shell-less eggs. Snails are the first intermediate host and dragonflies (nymphs or adults) the second, and cattle are not infected.

Q 24 | anm | Animal Health & Nutrition
Which of the following agents causes snotsiekte in wild ruminants?
(1) Bacteria
(2) Fungi
(3) Phytoplasmas
(4) Viruses
= 4
~ Snotsiekte is the African name for wildebeest-associated malignant catarrhal fever, caused by a virus (alcelaphine herpesvirus 1). Wildebeest carry it without illness, and it is fatal in susceptible cattle and other ruminants.

Q 25 | anm | Animal Health & Nutrition
With which of the following is the term modified Kirby Bauer method associated?
(1) Protein analysis
(2) Assisted parturition
(3) Artificial insemination
(4) Antimicrobial susceptibility
= 4
~ The Kirby-Bauer method is the disc diffusion test: antibiotic discs are placed on a bacterial lawn on agar, and the zone of inhibition shows whether the organism is susceptible. It is the standard antimicrobial susceptibility test.

Q 26 | anm | Animal Health & Nutrition
What is the causal agent of Mediterranean fever?
(1) Entamoeba histolytica
(2) Staphylococcal infection
(3) Lyssa virus
(4) Brucella melitensis
= 4
~ Mediterranean (Malta or undulant) fever is human brucellosis caused by Brucella melitensis, acquired mainly from goats and sheep. Entamoeba causes amoebiasis and the lyssavirus causes rabies.

Q 27 | anm | Animal Health & Nutrition
Zoonotic diseases transmitted from human beings to the lower vertebrate animals are called:
(1) anthropozoonoses
(2) zooanthroponoses
(3) amphixenoses
(4) amlinxoses
= 2
~ Zooanthroponoses (reverse zoonoses) are diseases passing from humans to animals. Anthropozoonoses pass from animals to humans, and amphixenoses can pass in either direction.

Q 28 | anm | Economic Zoology
What is the scientific name of rock bee?
(1) Apis indica
(2) Apis dorsata
(3) Apis florea
(4) Apis mellifera
= 2
~ The rock bee, or giant honey bee, is Apis dorsata; it builds a single large open comb on cliffs, rock overhangs and tall trees. Apis florea is the little bee, Apis indica (cerana) the Indian hive bee, and Apis mellifera the European bee.

Q 33 | anm | Economic Zoology
Who first translated the meaning of the waggle dance of bees?
(1) Karl von Frisch
(2) Edward Lorenze
(3) Jürgen Tautz
(4) Ernst Spitzner
= 1

Q 34 | anm | Economic Zoology
Which of the following is the causal agent of milk shrimp disease?
(1) Flavobacterium sp
(2) Leucouthrix sp
(3) Thelohania sp
(4) Zoothamnium sp
= 3
~ Milk (or cotton) shrimp disease is a microsporidian infection caused by Thelohania, which turns the shrimp's muscle milky white and opaque. Leucothrix is a filamentous bacterium and Zoothamnium a protozoan, both gill or body foulers, while Flavobacterium is a bacterium.

Q 35 | fim | Sampling & Inventory
The ratio of sample size to study population size is known as:
(1) stratum
(2) sampling unit
(3) sampling fraction
(4) sampling fame
= 3
~ The sampling fraction is n/N, the sample size divided by the size of the population. A stratum is a subgroup of the population, and a sampling unit is an individual element that can be selected.

Q 36 | fim | Sampling & Inventory
Find the median of the following data set: 10, 11, 13, 11, 12, 10, 13, 11, 12, 15, 12
(1) 6
(2) 12
(3) 11
(4) 11.5
= 2
~ Arranged in order the 11 values are 10, 10, 11, 11, 11, 12, 12, 12, 13, 13, 15. The median is the (11 + 1)/2 = 6th value, which is 12. The mode is 11 and 12 (both occur three times), and 6 is the position of the median, not its value.

Q 37 | fim | Sampling & Inventory
Who coined the term null hypotheses?

(2) Cyril Harold Goulden

= 1
~ The term "null hypothesis" was introduced by R.A. Fisher in his book The Design of Experiments (1935). C.R. Rao and David Cox are well-known statisticians but are not credited with coining it.

Q 38 | fim | Sampling & Inventory
A hypothesis that specifies all the parameters of a distribution is called:
(1) composite hypothesis
(2) simple hypothesis
(3) parametric hypothesis
(4) non-parametric hypothesis
= 2
~ A simple hypothesis completely specifies the population distribution, for example H0: mean = 50 with a known standard deviation. A composite hypothesis leaves at least one parameter unspecified, for example H1: mean > 50.

Q 44 | ecw | Ecosystem Concepts
Which of the following is NOT a provisioning service obtained from forests?
(1) Fuel wood
(2) Nutrient cycling
(3) Fibre
(4) Food
= 2
~ In the Millennium Ecosystem Assessment classification, provisioning services are the products people obtain from ecosystems, such as food, fibre, fuel wood and fresh water. Nutrient cycling is a supporting service that underpins all the other services.

Q 45 | fel | Forest Economics
The method employed to estimate recreational values of a national park is:
(1) hedonic pricing
(2) market value method
(3) travel cost method
(4) benefit transfer method
= 3
~ The travel cost method infers the recreational value of a site such as a national park from what visitors spend in travel cost and time to reach it. Hedonic pricing uses property prices, and benefit transfer borrows values from other studies rather than estimating them directly.

Q 46 | fel | Forest & Environment Laws
Which section of the Indian Forest Act,1927, deals with the following: ‘Persons enjoying benefits out of forests are bound to take steps to extinguish any forest fire’?
(1) Section 33 (1) (d)
(2) Section 33 (2)
(3) Section 26 (1) (c)
(4) Section 79 (1)
= 4
~ Section 79 of the Indian Forest Act, 1927 makes persons who exercise rights or receive forest produce in a forest bound to help Forest and Police officers, including by extinguishing any forest fire and preventing its spread. Sections 26 and 33 are penal provisions on acts such as setting fire in reserved and protected forests, not the duty to extinguish fires.

Q 47 | fel | Forest & Environment Laws

(2) Section 12 A
(3) Section 36 A
(4) Section 34 A
= 3
~ Section 36A of the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972, inserted in 2002, empowers the State Government to declare areas adjoining national parks and sanctuaries, or connecting protected areas, as conservation reserves. Section 36C similarly deals with community reserves.

Q 48 | fim | Tree & Stand Measurement
In a tree, Christen hypsometer is used to measure which of the following?
(1) Taper
(2) Diameter
(3) Volume
(4) Height
= 4
~ A hypsometer is an instrument for measuring tree height, and the Christen hypsometer does so using similar triangles with a graduated staff held against the tree. Diameter is measured with a calliper or girth tape, and taper and volume are calculated from several measurements.

Q 49 | slv | Silvicultural Principles & Regeneration
The time that elapses between successive main fellings on the same area is known as:
(1) felling interval
(2) felling series
(3) felling cycle
(4) felling regime
= 3
~ The felling cycle is the planned time between two successive main fellings on the same area, as in the selection system. A felling series is a group of compartments worked as one unit for sustained yield, not a time period.

Q 50 | fim | Tree & Stand Measurement
The formula of glorious simplicity is also called:
(1) Simmon's modification

= 4

Q 52 | fel | Forest Economics
The rotation that yields the highest net returns on the invested capital is called:
(1) forest rental
(2) soil expectation value

(4) technical rotation
= 3
~ The financial rotation is the age at which the capital invested in the forest gives the highest financial return (net income on the capital). Q 53 | nrs | Forest & Water Resources

= 3
