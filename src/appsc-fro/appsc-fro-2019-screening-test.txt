Q 1 | men | Data Interpretation
The following pie diagram shows the monthly expenditure of Rakesh and Sohan. Rakesh earns Rs. 15,000 per month and Sohan Rs 18,000 per month. (The percentage shares from the diagram are given below.)
Expenditure | Rakesh | Sohan
Children Education | 12% | 8%
House | 19% | 27%
Miscellaneous | 31% | 32%
Food etc | 38% | 33%
How much more or less does Sohan spend on children’s education than Rakesh?
= Rs. 360 Less
~ Convert each share into rupees using each person's own income. Rakesh spends 12% of Rs 15,000 = Rs 1,800 on children's education; Sohan spends 8% of Rs 18,000 = Rs 1,440. So Sohan spends 1,800 − 1,440 = Rs 360 less than Rakesh.

Q 5 | sci | Chemistry
Arrange the following in order of decreasing magnetic moments:
Fe²⁺, Mn²⁺, Cu²⁺, V³⁺
= Mn²⁺ > Fe²⁺ > V³⁺ > Cu²⁺
~ The spin-only magnetic moment is μ = √[n(n + 2)] BM, where n is the number of unpaired d-electrons. Mn²⁺ (3d⁵) has 5 unpaired electrons (5.92 BM), Fe²⁺ (3d⁶) has 4 (4.90 BM), V³⁺ (3d²) has 2 (2.83 BM) and Cu²⁺ (3d⁹) has 1 (1.73 BM). Hence Mn²⁺ > Fe²⁺ > V³⁺ > Cu²⁺.

Q 6 | sci | Chemistry
Which of the following is/are correct about sigma bond?
1. Formed by axial overlapping
2. Weaker
3. Independent existence
4. Free rotation possible
= 1, 3 and 4
~ A sigma bond forms by head-on (axial) overlap of orbitals along the internuclear axis, so it can exist on its own and the bonded atoms can rotate freely about it. Statement 2 is wrong: the greater overlap makes a sigma bond stronger than a pi bond, which forms by sideways overlap, exists only alongside a sigma bond and prevents free rotation.

Q 7 | sci | Physics
The temperature at which a conductor becomes a super conductor is called:
= Transition Temperature
~ When certain materials are cooled, their electrical resistance suddenly falls to zero at a particular temperature called the transition (or critical) temperature, Tc. For mercury, in which Kamerlingh Onnes discovered superconductivity in 1911, Tc is about 4.2 K. Below Tc a superconductor also expels magnetic fields (Meissner effect).

Q 8 | men | Arithmetic
The principal amount which yields a Compound Interest of Rs. 208 in second year at 4% is:
= Rs. 5000
~ Compound interest for the second year is interest on the first year's amount, i.e. P × r × (1 + r). So P × 0.04 × 1.04 = 208, giving 0.0416P = 208 and P = Rs 5,000. Check: first-year interest = 200, amount 5,200; second-year interest = 4% of 5,200 = 208.

Q 9 | men | Arithmetic
A person sold a box of pen at a gain of 15%. Had he bought it for 25% less and sold it for Rs. 60 less he would have made profit of 32%. The cost price of the box of pen was:
= Rs. 375
~ Let the cost price be x, so the selling price is 1.15x. The new cost price is 0.75x and the new selling price, 1.15x − 60, must be 132% of 0.75x = 0.99x. So 1.15x − 60 = 0.99x, 0.16x = 60 and x = Rs 375. Check: new CP 281.25, new SP 371.25, profit 90 = 32%.

Q 10 | men | Arithmetic
There are 40 students in a hostel. If the number of students increases by 5 and the expenses of the mess increased by Rs. 45 per day while the average expenditure per head decreases by Rs. 1 then find the total original expenditure of the mess:
= Rs. 720
~ Let the original average be Rs x per head, so the original expenditure is 40x. With 45 students the average is x − 1, so 45(x − 1) = 40x + 45. Then 45x − 45 = 40x + 45, 5x = 90 and x = 18. Original expenditure = 40 × 18 = Rs 720 (new: 45 × 17 = 765 = 720 + 45).

Q 11 | men | Arithmetic
A and B can complete a work in 5 days. They start working but after 3 days B left the work. if the work is completed after 3 more days B alone can do the work in:
= 15 days
~ Together A and B do 1/5 of the work a day, so in 3 days they finish 3/5. The remaining 2/5 is finished by A alone in 3 days, so A does 2/15 of the work a day. B's rate = 1/5 − 2/15 = 1/15 a day, so B alone needs 15 days.

Q 12 | geo | Indian Geography
Production of tea requires
= moderately hot and humid climate with frequent rains
~ Tea is a plantation crop of tropical and sub-tropical areas. It needs warm temperatures (about 20–30 °C), high humidity and heavy, well-distributed rainfall (about 150–300 cm) through the year. It is grown on well-drained hill slopes because waterlogging harms its roots, as in Assam, Darjeeling and the Nilgiris.

Q 13 | men | Reasoning
A man is facing north-west. He turns 90° in the clockwise direction, then 180° in the anticlockwise direction and then another 90° in the same direction. Which direction is he facing now?
= South East
~ He starts facing north-west, and a 90° clockwise turn makes him face north-east. A 180° anticlockwise turn from north-east makes him face south-west. Another 90° anticlockwise (the same direction) turn takes him from south-west to south-east. In all he turned 90° clockwise and 270° anticlockwise, a net 180° anticlockwise, so he faces the direction opposite to north-west.

Q 14 | men | Arithmetic
Two bus tickets from city A to B and three tickets from city A to C cost Rs. 77 but three tickets from city A to B and two tickets from city A to C cost Rs. 73. What are the fares for cities B and C from A ?
= Rs. 13, Rs. 17
~ Let the fares be B and C. Then 2B + 3C = 77 and 3B + 2C = 73. Adding gives 5(B + C) = 150, so B + C = 30; subtracting the second from the first gives C − B = 4. So C = 17 and B = 13: Rs 13 to B and Rs 17 to C.

Q 15 | men | Arithmetic
A tailor had a number of shirt pieces to cut from a roll of fabric. He cut each roll of equal length into 10 pieces. He cut at the rate of 45 cuts a minute. How many rolls would be cut in 24 minutes?
= 120 rolls
~ Cutting a roll into 10 pieces needs only 9 cuts, because the last piece is left after the ninth cut. In 24 minutes he makes 45 × 24 = 1,080 cuts, so the number of rolls cut = 1,080 ÷ 9 = 120.

Q 16 | geo | Environment & Ecology
Acid rains occur when atmosphere is heavily polluted with:
= SO₂ and NO₂
~ Sulphur dioxide and nitrogen dioxide, released mainly by burning coal and petroleum in power plants, industries and vehicles, react with water vapour and oxygen in the air to form sulphuric acid and nitric acid. These come down as rain with pH below 5.6, called acid rain, which damages soils, lakes, forests and buildings.

Q 17 | geo | Environment & Ecology
Eutrophication of lakes is caused by
= Phosphates
~ Eutrophication is the nutrient enrichment of a lake, mainly by phosphates (and nitrates) from detergents, sewage and fertiliser run-off. The nutrients cause algal blooms; when the algae die, their decomposition uses up dissolved oxygen and fish die. Phosphorus is usually the limiting nutrient in fresh water, so phosphates are the key cause.

Q 19 | sci | Physics
Fresh water achieves its greatest density at :
= 4 degree C
~ Water shows anomalous expansion: on cooling it contracts only down to 4 °C and then expands until it freezes at 0 °C. So fresh water is densest (about 1,000 kg/m³) at 4 °C. This is why the bottom of a frozen lake stays at about 4 °C and aquatic life survives the winter.

Q 20 | mth | Geometry & Mensuration
A 4 cm cube is cut into 1cm cubes. What is the percentage increase in the surface area after such cutting?
= 300%
~ The original surface area = 6 × 4² = 96 cm². Cutting gives 4³ = 64 cubes of edge 1 cm, with total surface area 64 × 6 × 1² = 384 cm². The increase is 384 − 96 = 288 cm², which is 288 ÷ 96 × 100 = 300%.

Q 21 | mth | Geometry & Mensuration
The surface area of three coterminous faces a cuboid area 6,15,10 sq.cm respectively. What is the volume of the cuboid?
= 30
~ Let the edges be l, b and h, with lb = 6, bh = 15 and hl = 10. Multiplying the three, (lbh)² = 6 × 15 × 10 = 900, so the volume lbh = √900 = 30 cubic cm. The edges are 2, 3 and 5 cm.

Q 22 | men | Arithmetic
The sum of the ages of a son and his father is 56 years. After four years the age of father will be three times that of his son. Their present age respectively are:
= 12 year and 44 years
~ Let the son be S and the father F years old. S + F = 56 and F + 4 = 3(S + 4), so F = 3S + 8. Then S + 3S + 8 = 56, 4S = 48, S = 12 and F = 44. Check: after four years they will be 16 and 48, and 48 = 3 × 16.

Q 23 | geo | Environment & Ecology
Taj Mahal is threatened due to the effect of
= Sulphur dioxide
~ Sulphur dioxide from the Mathura oil refinery and industries around Agra forms sulphuric acid in moist air. This acid reacts with the marble (calcium carbonate) of the Taj Mahal, causing yellowing and pitting, called ‘marble cancer’. The Supreme Court's Taj Trapezium Zone restrictions (1996) aim to control this pollution.

Q 24 | sci | Biology & Health
Death while sleeping in closed room with burning coal furnace is caused by
= CO
~ Coal burning in a closed room with little oxygen undergoes incomplete combustion and gives off carbon monoxide, a colourless, odourless gas. CO binds haemoglobin about 200–250 times more strongly than oxygen, forming carboxyhaemoglobin, so the blood cannot carry oxygen and the sleeper dies of asphyxia without waking.

Q 27 | sci | Chemistry
Which of the following has pH value 7?
= Pure water
~ pH 7 is neutral. Pure water ionises slightly into equal amounts of H⁺ and OH⁻ ions, each 10⁻⁷ mol/L at 25 °C, so its pH = −log(10⁻⁷) = 7. Acids have pH below 7 and bases above 7; even rainwater is slightly acidic (about 5.6) because of dissolved carbon dioxide.

Q 32 | mth | Statistics & Probability
Which set of data has a mean of 15, a range of 22, a median of 14, and a mode of 14?
= 3,14,19,25,14
~ For 3, 14, 19, 25, 14: the sum is 75, so mean = 75 ÷ 5 = 15; range = 25 − 3 = 22; in order the values are 3, 14, 14, 19, 25, so the median (middle value) is 14; and 14 occurs twice, so the mode is 14. All four conditions are met.

Q 34 | mth | Statistics & Probability
A graph of a cumulative frequency distribution is called:
= Ogive
~ An ogive, or cumulative frequency curve, is drawn by plotting cumulative frequencies against upper class limits (‘less than’ ogive) or lower class limits (‘more than’ ogive) and joining the points. The two ogives cross at the median, so ogives are used to find the median, quartiles and percentiles graphically.

Q 35 | mth | Statistics & Probability
In the case of classification of data, the class having its upper limit is treated as the lower limit of its next class is called:
= Open ended class
~ APPSC's final key gives ‘open ended class’, reading each such class as open at its upper end: its upper limit is not counted in it but in the next class. Standard statistics texts, however, call classes in which the upper limit of one class is the lower limit of the next (10–20, 20–30, …) exclusive classes, and use ‘open-ended class’ for a class whose lower limit (first class) or upper limit (last class) is not stated, such as ‘below 10’ or ‘60 and above’. (Official key; some sources differ.)

Q 40 | sci | Physics
_________ is the fourth state of matter.
= Plasma
~ Besides solid, liquid and gas, plasma is called the fourth state of matter. It is a gas heated so strongly (or subjected to such strong electric fields) that its atoms are ionised into free electrons and positive ions, so it conducts electricity. The Sun and stars, lightning and neon and fluorescent tubes contain plasma.

Q 52 | geo | Disaster Management

Q 53 | ssg | Soil Properties & Formation
Which type of soil water is most useful for plant?
= Capillary water
~ Capillary water is held by surface tension in the small pores and around soil particles after gravitational water has drained away. Most of it, between field capacity (about −0.33 bar) and the permanent wilting point (about −15 bar), is available to roots. Gravitational water drains away too fast, and hygroscopic water is held too tightly for plants to use.

Casuarina and other species are planted as coastal shelterbelts along the Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu coasts.

Q 55 | sci | Biology & Health
A camel can tolerate a loss of water at about ________ of its body weight when exposed to severe desert heat.
= 30 percent
~ The camel is highly adapted to deserts: it can lose water equal to about 25–30% of its body weight without harm, whereas most mammals die after losing about 15%. It keeps its blood volume steady during dehydration, lets its body temperature rise by day to save water, and passes dry dung and concentrated urine.

Q 56 | sci | Biology & Health
Ringworm is a __________ that attacks the skin, hair and nails of both animals and humans.
= Fungus
~ Ringworm (tinea or dermatophytosis) is not caused by a worm but by dermatophyte fungi such as Trichophyton, Microsporum and Epidermophyton, which feed on keratin in skin, hair and nails. It causes ring-shaped, itchy, scaly patches, spreads by contact with infected people, animals or objects, and is treated with antifungal drugs.

= 16
~ 40% of X = Y, so Y = 0.4X. Then Y% of 40 = 0.4X × 40 ÷ 100 = 0.16X. If this equals X% of k, then Xk ÷ 100 = 0.16X, so k = 16. Check with X = 100: Y = 40, 40% of 40 = 16, and 100% of 16 = 16.

Q 62 | mth | Algebra
If x = 2+√3, then (x² + 1/x²) is equal to:
= 14
~ Since x = 2 + √3, 1/x = 1/(2 + √3) = (2 − √3)/(4 − 3) = 2 − √3. So x + 1/x = 4, and x² + 1/x² = (x + 1/x)² − 2 = 16 − 2 = 14.

If 2x+3y = 8 and 4x-y = 2, then
= x = 1, y = 2
~ From 4x − y = 2, y = 4x − 2. Substituting in 2x + 3y = 8 gives 2x + 12x − 6 = 8, so 14x = 14 and x = 1; then y = 4(1) − 2 = 2. Check: 2(1) + 3(2) = 8 and 4(1) − 2 = 2.

Q 64 | mth | Algebra
Let A and B are two sets containing 14 and 22 elements respectively. If A and B have 7 Common Elements, then the number of elements in A∪B is
= 29
~ For two sets, n(A ∪ B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A ∩ B), because the common elements are counted in both A and B. So n(A ∪ B) = 14 + 22 − 7 = 29. Equivalently, 7 elements only in A + 7 common + 15 only in B = 29.

Q 134 | pls | Classification & Morphology
A pair of appendages that present at the point of attachment of a leaf to the stem is
= Stipules
~ Stipules are a pair of small lateral outgrowths at the leaf base, where the leaf joins the stem. Leaves with stipules are called stipulate and those without are exstipulate. Stipules may be leafy (pea), tendrils (Smilax) or spines (Zizyphus, Acacia), and in some plants they protect the young bud.

Q 135 | pls | Plant Physiology
Photosynthesis is :
= Anabolic process
~ Anabolism is the building up of complex molecules from simple ones using energy. In photosynthesis green plants use light energy trapped by chlorophyll to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water (6CO2 + 6H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2). Respiration, which breaks glucose down to release energy, is the corresponding catabolic process.

Q 138 | slv | Nursery & Planting
Seeds which cannot be dried to moisture contents below 30% without injury and are unable to tolerate freezing are called
= Recalcitrant seeds
~ Recalcitrant seeds are shed with a high moisture content and lose viability if dried below a fairly high level (around 20–30%) or frozen, so they cannot be stored for long; sal (Shorea robusta), mango, jackfruit and many dipterocarps are examples. Orthodox seeds, such as those of teak, acacias and eucalyptus, can be dried to about 5% and stored for years at low temperature.

Q 142 | slv | Silvicultural Principles & Regeneration
What is the selective removal of certain parts of a plant, such as branches, buds, or roots is called?
= Pruning
~ Pruning is the selective removal of plant parts such as branches, buds or roots. In forestry, lower branches are pruned to produce a clean, knot-free bole and better timber, to reduce fire risk and to lessen shade on crops in agroforestry. Natural pruning happens when shaded lower branches die and fall in a dense crop; root pruning gives nursery seedlings a compact root system.

Q 143 | fim | Remote Sensing & GIS
GIS deals with which kind of data
= Spatial data

Q 144 | fel | Forest Economics
Which one of the following is the algebraic difference between discounted benefits and discounted costs as they occur over time?
= Net present value
~ Net present value (NPV) = sum of discounted benefits − sum of discounted costs = Σ (Bt − Ct)/(1 + r)^t, where r is the discount rate and t the year. A forestry project is worth taking up if its NPV is positive. The benefit–cost ratio divides discounted benefits by discounted costs, and the internal rate of return is the discount rate at which NPV becomes zero.

Q 145 | anm | Animal Health & Nutrition
Foot and mouth disease in animals is caused by
= Virus
~ Foot and mouth disease is a highly contagious disease of cloven-hoofed animals such as cattle, buffalo, sheep, goats and pigs, caused by an Aphthovirus (family Picornaviridae) with seven serotypes. It causes fever, blisters in the mouth and on the feet, lameness and a sharp fall in milk yield. It is controlled by vaccination, as under the National Animal Disease Control Programme (2019).

Q 146 | fim | Sampling & Inventory
Sampling method in which the sampling units are selected according to a predetermined patters is
= Systematic sampling
~ In systematic sampling the units are chosen at fixed intervals according to a set pattern, for example every 10th tree, or plots at regular spacing on a grid or along parallel strips, often after a random start. It is easy to lay out in forests and covers the area evenly. In simple random sampling every unit is picked by chance, and in stratified sampling the area is first divided into strata.

Q 147 | fse | Survey & Engineering
Type of survey in which the curvature of the earth is not taken into account is called
= Plane survey
~ In plane surveying the earth's surface is treated as a flat plane and its curvature is ignored; this is accurate enough for small areas (up to about 250 sq km), so most forest, engineering and cadastral surveys are plane surveys. Geodetic surveying takes the curvature into account and is used for large areas, such as the national triangulation of the Survey of India.

Q 148 | fse | Survey & Engineering
The vertical distance between any two consecutive contours is known as
= Contour interval
~ The contour interval is the constant vertical distance between two consecutive contour lines on a map, for example 20 m on many Survey of India 1:50,000 toposheets. The horizontal distance between two consecutive contours is the horizontal equivalent; closely spaced contours show a steep slope and widely spaced ones a gentle slope.

Q 150 | fel | Forest Policy
Maintenance of environmental stability, maintenance of ecological balance including atmosphere equilibrium are the principle objectives of____________.
= National Forest Policy, 1988
~ The National Forest Policy, 1988 states that its principal aim is to ensure environmental stability and maintenance of ecological balance, including atmospheric equilibrium, which are vital for all life; direct economic benefit must be subordinated to this aim. It also set the goal of one-third of India's area under forest or tree cover (two-thirds in the hills). The earlier 1952 policy gave more weight to revenue and industry.
