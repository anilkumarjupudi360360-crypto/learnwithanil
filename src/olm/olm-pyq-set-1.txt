# OLM PYQ Set 1: SHGs, NRLM, Mission Shakti & Livelihoods. Real questions from official exam papers on this site, compiled by Learn with Anil for the Odisha Livelihoods Mission CBT; the source exam is in brackets before each question; answers: that exam's official answer key

Q 1 | liv | SHGs & NRLM
[TS Police SI FWE 2023 Paper 4]
What is the primary objective of a Self Help Group (SHG) In India ?
(1) To Provide micro-finance to groups
(2) To improve technology of farming
(3) To provide high quality of seeds
(4) To provide education needs
= 1
~ A Self Help Group is a small group of poor people, mostly women, who save regularly and lend to members, and through the NABARD-led SHG-Bank Linkage Programme get small bank loans, so its core purpose is micro-finance. Farm technology, seeds and education are not its primary aim.

Q 2 | liv | SHGs & NRLM
[UPSC CSE Prelims 2023]
Consider the following statements:
1. The Self-Help Group (SHG) programme was originally initiated by the State Bank of India by providing microcredit to the financially deprived.
2. In an SHG, all members of a group take responsibility for a loan that an individual member takes.
3. The Regional Rural Banks and Scheduled Commercial Banks support SHGs.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(1) Only one
(2) Only two
(3) All three
(4) None
= 2
~ The SHG–Bank Linkage Programme was launched by NABARD in 1992, not by the State Bank of India, so statement 1 is wrong. SHG members share joint liability for loans taken by individual members, and Regional Rural Banks, commercial banks and cooperative banks lend to SHGs. Two: (b).

Q 3 | liv | SHGs & NRLM
[TS Police Constable FWE 2016]
Read the following statements:
A) The DWCRA aimed at providing self-employment to women of BPL families.
B) The NREP was launched in 1980.
C) The NSS organisation gave three concepts of employment to measure its status-usual status, current weekly status and current daily status
D) The PMRY aimed at providing wage employment in rural areas
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(1) A, B and C only
(2) B, C and D only
(3) A, C and D only
(4) A, B and D only
= 1
~ DWCRA (1982) was a sub-scheme of IRDP giving self-employment to rural women of BPL families; the National Rural Employment Programme was launched in 1980 and merged into the Jawahar Rozgar Yojana in 1989; and the NSSO measures employment by usual, current weekly and current daily status. D is wrong: the Prime Minister's Rozgar Yojana (1993) gave self-employment to educated unemployed youth and was merged into PMEGP in 2008.

Q 4 | liv | SHGs & NRLM
[RPF Constable CBT 2025 (2 Mar, Shift 2)]
Which of the following is a government programme meant to reduce poverty in India?
(1) Make in India
(2) NRLM
(3) Green Revolution
(4) Digital India
= 2
~ The National Rural Livelihoods Mission (NRLM, 'Aajeevika') was launched by the Ministry of Rural Development in June 2011, replacing the Swarnajayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana, to reduce rural poverty. It organises poor rural women into self-help groups and links them to credit and livelihoods; it was renamed Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana–NRLM (DAY-NRLM) in 2016. Make in India promotes manufacturing, Digital India builds digital infrastructure, and the Green Revolution of the 1960s raised food-grain output.

Q 5 | liv | SHGs & NRLM
[KSP Civil Police Constable 2026 (KK)]
Which is the web and mobile platform under the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM)?
(1) JanOS
(2) LokOS
(3) DayNOS
(4) VaOS
= 2
~ LokOS is the web and mobile application developed under DAY-NRLM of the Union Ministry of Rural Development, so option (2) is correct. It digitises the records of women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and their federations (village organisations and cluster-level federations): member profiles, savings, loans, meetings and bookkeeping. This makes SHG accounts transparent, helps in bank linkage and credit, and supports initiatives such as Lakhpati Didi, which aims to raise the annual income of SHG women to at least one lakh rupees. The name can be read as 'Lok' (people) + 'OS' (operating system), a people's operating system for the rural livelihoods network. JanOS, DayNOS and VaOS are not names of any DAY-NRLM platform; they are distractors made to sound similar. Useful facts: NRLM was launched in 2011 as 'Aajeevika' and later renamed DAY-NRLM after Pandit Deendayal Upadhyaya. In Karnataka, the mission is implemented by the Karnataka State Rural Livelihood Promotion Society under the name 'Sanjeevini'.

Q 6 | liv | SHGs & NRLM
[Karnataka PSI 2020 Paper 2]
Which of the following ministries is organised SARAS Aajeevika Mela at India Gate Lawns in October, 2019 ?
(1) Ministry of Road Transport and Highways
(2) Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs
(3) Ministry of Rural Development
(4) Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
= 3
~ The SARAS Aajeevika Mela 2019 was organised by the Ministry of Rural Development at India Gate Lawns, New Delhi, from 10 to 23 October 2019. It was an initiative of the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM) and was inaugurated by Rural Development Minister Narendra Singh Tomar. Women of self-help groups (SHGs) from across India displayed and sold handicrafts, handlooms and regional food, giving rural producers direct access to urban buyers. The Road Transport, Housing and Urban Affairs, and Health and Family Welfare ministries have no role in this mela. Hence option (3). Tip: anything named 'Aajeevika' (livelihood) is linked to the Ministry of Rural Development's NRLM; its urban counterpart DAY-NULM belongs to the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

Q 7 | liv | SHGs & NRLM
[Karnataka PSI 2021 Paper 2]
Consider the following statements regarding 'SonChiraiya' and select the correct one/s :
1. SonChiraiya was launched for marketing of urban self help group products.
2. It was launched by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 only
(3) Both 1 and 2
(4) Neither 1 nor 2
= 1
~ 'SonChiraiya' is a single brand and logo launched by the Union government in August 2021 for the marketing of products made by urban self-help groups (SHGs), so statement 1 is correct. It was launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) under the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Urban Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NULM), not by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, so statement 2 is wrong. The aim of SonChiraiya is to give urban SHG products – handicrafts, handlooms, food items and so on – better visibility and access to e-commerce platforms and wider markets, helping the women members earn more. The name recalls India's old title 'Sone ki Chidiya' (golden bird). Hence the answer is '1 only'. Exam tip: when a scheme concerns urban SHGs, think of MoHUA and DAY-NULM; rural SHGs come under the Ministry of Rural Development's DAY-NRLM. Wrong-ministry statements are a common trap in such two-statement questions.

Q 9 | liv | Mission Shakti & Odisha Schemes
[TGPSC Group-II 2024 Paper 2]
Arrange the following Women Empowerment Schemes in India in chronological order :
A. Launch of the National Rural Livelihood Mission (NRLM) with a focus on women’s self-help groups.
B. Implementation of the Mahila Samakhya Programme to empower women through education and awareness.
C. Establishment of Rashtriya Mahila Kosh (RMK) to provide microfinance support to women entrepreneurs.
D. Initiation of the Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) providing financial assistance to pregnant women.
Choose the correct answer :
(1) A, C, B, D
(2) C, A, D, B
(3) B, C, A, D
(4) D, A, C, B
= 3
~ The Mahila Samakhya Programme began in 1988 under the National Policy on Education, 1986 to empower rural women through education (B). Rashtriya Mahila Kosh was set up in 1993 to give micro-credit to poor women (C). The National Rural Livelihood Mission (Aajeevika), built around women's self-help groups, was launched in 2011 (A), and the Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana began in 2017 (D). So the order is B, C, A, D.

Q 10 | liv | Mission Shakti & Odisha Schemes
[TSPSC AMVI 2023 Paper 1]
Which of the following schemes is aimed at providing financial assistance to pregnant and lactating women in India?
(1) Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana
(2) Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana
(3) Swachh Bharat Abhiyan
(4) Digital India Programme
= 2
~ Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana, a centrally sponsored maternity benefit scheme under the National Food Security Act running nationwide from 1 January 2017, pays pregnant women and lactating mothers ₹5,000 by direct benefit transfer for the first live birth, as part wage compensation and to promote good nutrition. Under Mission Shakti it also gives ₹6,000 for a second child who is a girl (births from 1 April 2022). Jan Dhan is about financial inclusion; the others are sanitation and digital programmes.

Q 11 | liv | Mission Shakti & Odisha Schemes
[UPSC EPFO EO/AO 2023]
The beneficiaries under the Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) Scheme are children in the age group of:
(1) 0 – 6 years
(2) 0 – 3 years
(3) 3 – 6 years
(4) 6 – 15 years
= 1
~ Option 1, 0–6 years. The Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) scheme, launched on 2 October 1975 and run by the Ministry of Women and Child Development, serves children aged 0–6 years, pregnant women and lactating mothers through anganwadi centres. Its six services are supplementary nutrition, pre-school non-formal education, nutrition and health education, immunisation, health check-ups and referral services. Options 2 and 3 are only sub-groups within the 0–6 range (pre-school education, for example, is for children aged 3–6), and option 4 is school age, when children are covered by PM POSHAN (the mid-day meal scheme) in classes 1–8 rather than by ICDS. ICDS now runs as part of Mission Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0, and the Poshan Tracker app monitors anganwadi services. Anganwadi workers and helpers are honorary workers paid an honorarium, not regular government employees. Exam link: ICDS covers the early 0–6 stage, while the Right to Education under Article 21A covers children aged 6–14.

Q 12 | liv | Mission Shakti & Odisha Schemes
[UPSC CSE Prelims 2019]
Which of the following statements is/are correct regarding the Maternity Benefit (Amendment) Act, 2017?
1. Pregnant women are entitled for three months pre-delivery and three months post-delivery paid leave.
2. Enterprises with crèches must allow the mother minimum six crèche visits daily.
3. Women with two children get reduced entitlements.
Select the correct answer using the code given below.
(1) 1 and 2 only
(2) 2 only
(3) 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 3
~ The 2017 Act raised paid maternity leave to 26 weeks, of which up to 8 weeks may be before delivery, not three months each (1 is wrong). Hence 3 only, (c).

Q 16 | liv | Mission Shakti & Odisha Schemes
[Karnataka PSI Re-exam 2024 Paper 2]
“Sukanya Samruddhi” scheme is undertaken by the following agency :
(1) Life Insurance Corporation of India
(2) National Savings Institute
(3) Unit Trust of India
(4) NABARD
= 2
~ Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) is a small savings scheme for the girl child, launched by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 22 January 2015 at Panipat, Haryana, under the 'Beti Bachao Beti Padhao' campaign. Small savings schemes such as SSY, PPF, NSC and Kisan Vikas Patra belong to the Ministry of Finance, and the National Savings Institute (NSI), an attached office of the Department of Economic Affairs, assists the Ministry in mobilising and managing them. So option (B) is the keyed answer. The accounts themselves are opened at post offices and authorised banks. LIC is an insurance company, UTI is a mutual-fund institution, and NABARD is the apex bank for agriculture and rural development; none of them runs SSY. Key features: parents or guardians can open an account for a girl below 10 years; deposits of ₹250 to ₹1.5 lakh a year for 15 years; maturity 21 years from opening; partial withdrawal for higher education after she turns 18; tax benefit under Section 80C with tax-free interest and maturity. Karnataka link: in 2022 the State decided to implement its Bhagyalakshmi scheme through SSY in post offices instead of LIC.

Q 24 | liv | Microfinance & Enterprise
[UPSC EPFO APFC & EO/AO 2025]
Consider the following statements regarding Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana:
1. The average ticket size of loan disbursed has more than doubled between FY 2016 and FY 2025.
2. Since its launch in 2015, Maharashtra has recorded the highest disbursal among States.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 only
(3) Both 1 and 2
(4) Neither 1 nor 2
= 1
~ Option 1 is correct. Data released on ten years of the Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (April 2025) show the average ticket size of loans rising from about ₹38,000 in FY2016 to about ₹1.02 lakh in FY2025, roughly 2.7 times, so statement 1 is correct. Statement 2 is wrong: Tamil Nadu has the highest cumulative disbursal (about ₹3.23 lakh crore), followed by Uttar Pradesh and Karnataka, not Maharashtra. MUDRA (Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency) loans, launched on 8 April 2015, are collateral-free loans for non-corporate, non-farm small and micro enterprises, given by banks, NBFCs and MFIs in three categories: Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,000 to ₹5 lakh) and Tarun (₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh). The Union Budget 2024–25 added Tarun Plus, raising the limit to ₹20 lakh for borrowers who have repaid earlier Tarun loans. Women hold about two-thirds of MUDRA loan accounts. Remember: MUDRA itself refinances lenders; it does not lend directly to borrowers.

Q 25 | liv | Microfinance & Enterprise
[UPSC EPFO EO/AO 2017]
StandUp India Programme envisages each bank branch to give loan between ₹10 lakh to ₹100 lakh
1. to at least one SC/ST borrower
2. to at least one woman borrower
3. to at least one rural unemployed youth borrower
Select the correct answer using the code given below.
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 and 3
(3) 1 and 3
(4) 1 and 2
= 4
~ Option 4 is correct: statements 1 and 2. Under the Stand-Up India scheme, launched on 5 April 2016, every scheduled commercial bank branch is to facilitate loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore (₹100 lakh) to at least one Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe borrower and at least one woman borrower for setting up a greenfield enterprise. With about 1.25 lakh branches, this was meant to reach at least 2.5 lakh borrowers. Rural unemployed youth are not a target group of this scheme, so statement 3 is wrong and options 2 and 3 fall away; option 1 leaves out women. Such youth are served by schemes like the Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP), implemented through KVIC. Linked facts: the loan is a composite term-plus-working-capital loan, and Stand-Up India begins where MUDRA loans (then up to ₹10 lakh) end. Later changes: the Union Budget 2021-22 cut the margin money requirement from 25% to 15% and extended the scheme up to 2025.
