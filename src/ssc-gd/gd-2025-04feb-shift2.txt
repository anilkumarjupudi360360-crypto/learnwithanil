Q 1 | rea | Syllogism & Statements
Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.
Statements:
All wires are cables.
All wires are ropes.
Some ropes are steels.
Conclusions
(I) Some steels are wires.
(II) Some ropes are cables.
(1) Both conclusions (I) and (II) follow
(2) Only conclusion (II) follows
(3) Only conclusion (I) follows
(4) Neither conclusion (I) nor (II) follows
= 2
~ All wires are cables and all wires are ropes, so every wire is both a cable and a rope: some ropes are cables, and conclusion II follows. Some ropes are steels, but those ropes need not be wires, so 'some steels are wires' is only possible, not certain. Only conclusion II follows.

Q 14 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles
Eight people are sitting in two parallel rows containing 4 people each, in such a way that there is equal distance between adjacent persons.
In row 1 – I, N, K and S are seated and all of them are facing south
In row 2 – P, A, R and T are seated and all of them are facing north.
Thus, each person faces another person from the other row.
R sits to the immediate right of A. Only two people sit between K and S. A faces K. The one who faces N, sits to the immediate left of P. T doesn’t sit at any of the extreme ends.
Which of the following options represents both people facing each other?
(1) I and R
(2) N and P
(3) K and T
(4) S and R
= 1
~ Number seats 1 to 4 from west to east; row 2 faces north (its right is east). Only two people are between K and S, so they sit at the ends; A faces K and R is just right of A, so A is in seat 1, R in 2, and K in seat 1 of row 1, S in seat 4. Q 20 | rea | Blood Relations & Directions

'A + B' means 'A is the sister of B',
'A - B' means 'A is the father of B',

'A ÷ B' means 'A is the mother of B'.

(3) Daughter’s sister

Two trains, each 180 m long, are moving towards each other on parallel tracks at speeds of 50 km/hr and 70 km/hr. In how many seconds will they completely cross each other?
(1) 10.4
(2) 10.2
(3) 10.8
(4) 10.6
= 3
~ Moving towards each other, the relative speed is 50 + 70 = 120 km/h = 120 × 5/18 = 100/3 m/s. To cross completely they cover 180 + 180 = 360 m. Time = 360 ÷ (100/3) = 10.8 seconds.

Q 55 | qa | Average
The average age of 48 students of a class is 22 years. If the age of the teacher is also included, the average age of the whole group becomes 23 years. The age (in years) of the teacher is:
(1) 70
(2) 60
(3) 68
(4) 71
= 4
~ Students' total age = 48 × 22 = 1056. With the teacher, 49 people average 23: total 1127. The teacher is 1127 − 1056 = 71 years. Quick check: the teacher adds 1 year to each of 49 people over the old average, so 22 + 49 = 71.

Q 57 | qa | Simple & Compound Interest
Sarang invested some money in HDFC at 4% per annum rate of interest. What would be the corresponding simple interest (in ₹) if after 2 years, Sarang got ₹153 as compound interest, considering annual compounding?
(1) 145
(2) 165
(3) 150
(4) 160
= 3
~ Two-year compound interest at 4% = P × (1.04² − 1) = 0.0816P = 153, so P = ₹1875. Simple interest for 2 years = 1875 × 4 × 2 ÷ 100 = ₹150. (The ₹3 difference is interest on the first year's ₹75.)

Q 58 | qa | Number System & Simplification
Find the value of [(84 ÷ 6) × {91/7 + 16/2 × (8 − 6)}].
(1) 400
(2) 409
(3) 406
(4) 426
= 3

Q 59 | qa | Profit, Loss & Discount
A dishonest shopkeeper promises to sell his goods at cost price. However, he uses a weight that actually weighs 26% less than what is written on it. Find his profit percentage.
(1) 34 5/37%
(2) 36 6/37%
(3) 37 10/37%
(4) 35 5/37%
= 4
~ For goods marked 1000 g he gives 26% less, that is 740 g. He gains 260 g on 740 g, so profit = 260/740 × 100 = 35 5/37% (about 35.14%). The profit is counted on the 740 g he actually gives.

Q 60 | qa | Simple & Compound Interest
Amit invests a sum of ₹5400 and Gopal invests a sum of ₹9400 at the same rate of simple interest per annum. If, at the end of 4 years, Gopal gets ₹480 more interest than Amit, then find the rate of interest per annum (in percentage).
(1) 5
(2) 3
(3) 2
(4) 4
= 2
~ Gopal invests ₹4000 more than Amit, so the extra ₹480 is the interest on ₹4000 for 4 years: 4000 × r × 4 ÷ 100 = 480 gives 160r = 480, r = 3%.
