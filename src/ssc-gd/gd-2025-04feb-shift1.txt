Q 1 | rea | Series
What should come in place of the question mark (?) in the given series based on the English alphabetical order?
EHO HJP KLQ ? QPS
(1) NMR
(2) NNR
(3) MNR
(4) MMR
= 2
~ Take the letters position by position. First letters: E, H, K, N, Q (+3 each). Second letters: H, J, L, N, P (+2 each). Third letters: O, P, Q, R, S (+1 each). So the missing cluster is N, N, R = NNR. NMR, MNR and MMR each break at least one of the three steps.

Q 2 | rea | Series
What should come in place of the question mark (?) in the given series?
208 190 163 127 82 ?
(1) 54
(2) 45
(3) 28
(4) 12
= 3
~ The differences are 208 − 190 = 18, 190 − 163 = 27, 163 − 127 = 36, 127 − 82 = 45: multiples of 9 rising by 9 each time. The next difference is 54, so the missing number is 82 − 54 = 28.

Q 3 | rea | Coding-Decoding
In a certain code language, ‘JAMB’ is coded as ‘5139’ and ‘BALM’ is coded as ‘9521’.
What is the code for ‘L’ in the given code language?
(1) 2
(2) 5
(3) 1
(4) 9
= 1
~ JAMB = 5139 and BALM = 9521 share the letters A, B and M, and the codes share the digits 5, 1 and 9. So A, B and M take 5, 1 and 9 in some order, J is 3 (the digit left over in 5139) and L is 2 (the digit left over in 9521). The code for L is 2.

Q 4 | rea | Blood Relations & Directions
In a certain code language.
'X @ C' means 'X is the daughter of C',
'X $ C' means 'X is the husband of C',
'X = C' means 'X is the mother of C' and
'X * C' means 'X is the father of C'.
Based on the above, how is J related to N if 'N @ E $ J @ S'?
(1) Sister's daughter
(2) Sister
(3) Mother
(4) Daughter's son
= 3
~ N @ E: N is the daughter of E. E $ J: E is the husband of J, so J is E's wife. J @ S: J is the daughter of S (this only adds J's parent). N is the daughter of E, and J is E's wife, so J is N's mother.

Q 19 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles
Eight people are sitting in two parallel rows containing 4 people each in such a way that there is equal distance between adjacent persons.
In row 1, D, E, O and S are seated and all of them are facing south.
In row 2, F, A, R and M are seated and all of them are facing north.
Thus, each person faces another person from the other row.
Only D sits to the left of E. Only R sits to the left of A. Only one person sits between A and F. Only one person sits between D and O.
Which of the following represents both people facing each other?
(1) E and A
(2) E and M
(3) E and F
(4) E and R
= 2
~ Number the seats 1 to 4 from west to east.
