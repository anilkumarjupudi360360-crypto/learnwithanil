Q 2 | rea | Blood Relations & Directions
In a certain code language,
'A + B' means ‘A is the daughter of B’,
'A – B' means ‘A is the brother of B’,
'A × B' means ‘A is the wife of B’ and
'A ÷ B' means ‘ A is the father of B’.
Based on the above, how is P related to T if ‘P + Q ÷ R × S – T’?
(1) Brother's wife's sister
(2) Wife's sister
(3) Brother's wife's mother
(4) Wife's mother
= 1
~ P + Q: P is Q's daughter. Q ÷ R: Q is R's father, so P and R are sisters. R × S: R is S's wife. S − T: S is T's brother. So R is T's brother's wife, and P, R's sister, is T's brother's wife's sister.

Q 5 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles
Eight people are sitting in two parallel rows containing 4 people each in such a way that there is equal distance between adjacent persons.
In row 1 – L, M, N and O are seated and all of them are facing south.
In row 2 – P, Q, R and S are seated and all of them are facing north.
Thus, each person faces another person from the other row. Q sits to the immediate right of S. Only two people sit between L and M. S faces L. The one who faces N, sits to the immediate left of P. R does not sit at any of the extreme ends.
Who among the following faces O?
(1) P
(2) S
(3) R
(4) Q
= 4
~ Number seats 1 to 4 from west to east; row 2 faces north (its right is east). Only two people between L and M puts them at the two ends of row 1. S faces L and Q is just right of S, so S is in seat 1 (L in 1) and Q in 2. R is not at an end, so R is in 3 and P in 4. The person facing N is just left of P, that is R in seat 3, so N is in 3, M in 4 and O in 2. O faces Q.

Q 14 | rea | Syllogism & Statements

Statements:

All rabbits are bananas.
All bananas are lamps.
Conclusions:
(I): All cars are lamps.
(II): Some lamps are rabbits.
