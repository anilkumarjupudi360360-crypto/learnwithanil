Q 16 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles

Q 29 | sci | Physics
Two objects A and B of mass 2 kg and 3 kg, respectively, were dropped from different heights. A took 5 second and B took 3 seconds to touch the ground. What is the ratio of their kinetic energy with which they hit the ground?
(1) 10/9
(2) 2/3
(3) 50/27
(4) 5/3
= 3
