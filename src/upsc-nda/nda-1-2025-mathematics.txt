Q 1 | alg | Sequences & Series
The sum of the first k terms of a series S is 3k² + 5k. Which one of the following is correct?
(1) The terms of S form an arithmetic progression with common difference 14.
(2) The terms of S form an arithmetic progression with common difference 6.
(3) The terms of S form a geometric progression with common ratio 10/7.
(4) The terms of S form a geometric progression with common ratio 11/4.
= 2
~ The k-th term is S_k − S_(k−1) = 6k + 2 (and T₁ = 8 = S₁), giving 8, 14, 20, … – an AP with common difference 6.

Q 2 | alg | Sequences & Series

(1) One
(2) Two
(3) Three
(4) More than three

Q 3 | alg | Quadratic Equations & Inequalities

Q 5 | alg | Sequences & Series
If 5th, 7th and 13th terms of an AP are in GP, then what is the ratio of its first term to its common difference?
(1) −3
(2) −2

(4) 3
= 1

Q 6 | alg | Sequences & Series
If p, 1, q are in AP and p, 2, q are in GP, then which of the following statements is/are correct?
I. p, 4, q are in HP.
II. (1/p), 1/4, (1/q) are in AP.
Select the answer using the code given below.
(1) I only
(2) II only
(3) Both I and II
(4) Neither I nor II
= 3
~ p + q = 2 and pq = 4. The HM of p and q is 2pq/(p + q) = 8/2 = 4, so p, 4, q are in HP (I); equivalently 1/p + 1/q = (p + q)/pq = 1/2 = 2 × 1/4, so II holds too.

Q 8 | mat | Determinants

Q 9 | mat | Determinants
Consider the following statements in respect of the determinant
Δ = |[k(k + 2), 2k + 1, 1], [2k + 1, k + 2, 1], [3, 3, 1]|
I. Δ is positive if k > 0.
II. Δ is negative if k < 0.
III. Δ is zero if k = 0.
How many of the statements given above are correct?
(1) None
(2) One
(3) Two
(4) All three
= 2
~ Expanding gives Δ = (k − 1)³ (checked in python: k = 0 gives −1, k = 1 gives 0, k = 2 gives 1). So Δ < 0 for all k < 0 (II true), but Δ ≤ 0 for 0 < k ≤ 1 (I false) and Δ = −1 at k = 0 (III false). Only one is correct.
