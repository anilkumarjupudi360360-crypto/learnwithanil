Q 1 | eng | Synonyms & Antonyms
Select the option that is opposite in meaning to the underlined word.
All human beings are fallible (underlined: fallible)
(1) frail
(2) erring
(3) mortal
(4) perfect
= 4
~ "Fallible" means capable of making mistakes, so its opposite is "perfect" (free from error). Frail, erring and mortal all describe human weakness and are close to fallible.

Q 145 | cur | National
Consider the following statements :
1. First Indian Military Heritage festival was inaugurated in October 2023 in Pune
2. First Green Hydrogen Fuel Cell Bus was flagged off in India in Bangalore
3. South Africa chaired the 15th BRICS Summit held in 2023
4. Viksit Bharat Sankalp Yatra was flagged off in 2023 from Jharkhand
How many of the above statements is/are correct ?
(1) 1
(2) 2
(3) 3
(4) 4
= 2
~ The first Indian Military Heritage Festival was held in New Delhi in October 2023, and India's first green hydrogen fuel cell bus was flagged off in New Delhi in September 2023, so statements 1 and 2 are wrong. South Africa hosted and chaired the 15th BRICS Summit in Johannesburg (August 2023), and the Viksit Bharat Sankalp Yatra was flagged off from Khunti, Jharkhand on 15 November 2023. Two statements are correct.

Q 146 | cur | National
Which of the following statements about 'ULLAS' scheme is/are NOT correct ?
1. It's a Centrally Sponsored scheme which aims at securing childhood for street children
2. It's a scheme aligned with National Education Policy-2020 which aims at empowering adults aged 15 or above who could not get due schooling
Select the answer using the code given below :
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 only
(3) Both 1 and 2
(4) Neither 1 nor 2
= 1
~ ULLAS (Understanding Lifelong Learning for All in Society) is the New India Literacy Programme. It is aligned with NEP 2020 and targets adults aged 15 and above who missed schooling. It has nothing to do with street children, so only statement 1 is incorrect.

Q 147 | cur | International
Which one of the following statements is NOT correct about NATO ?
(1) It was formed out of the signing of the Washington Treaty
(2) Russia is not a member of this organization
(3) It derives its authority from the UN Charter
(4) Finland is its latest member
= 4
~ NATO was created by the North Atlantic (Washington) Treaty of 1949, which rests on the right of collective self-defence under Article 51 of the UN Charter. Russia is not a member. Finland joined in April 2023, but Sweden joined later, in March 2024, so Sweden (not Finland) is the latest member.

Q 149 | cur | Defence & Security
Which one among the following Joint Military Exercises was NOT conducted between India and USA ?
(1) Yudhabhyas
(2) Vajra Prahar
(3) Malabar
(4) Varuna
= 4
~ Varuna is the India–France naval exercise. Yudh Abhyas (Army) and Vajra Prahar (Special Forces) are India–USA exercises, and the US Navy also takes part in the Malabar exercise.

2. Global Maritime India Summit-2023 was held in Vishakhapatnam
3. 'Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047' was launched during Global Maritime India Summit-2023
How many of the above statements is/are correct ?
(1) None
(2) 1
(3) 2
(4) 3
= 2
