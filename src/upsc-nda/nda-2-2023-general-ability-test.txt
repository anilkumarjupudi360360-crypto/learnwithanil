Q 1 | eng | Fill in the Blanks
As the Southwest monsoon ________ through most of July, flash floods caused by the waters of the Narmada hit Jabalpur town.
(1) lashed
(2) flourished
(3) occupied
(4) dried
= 1
~ Heavy rain is said to "lash" a place; a monsoon that lashed (struck violently) through July explains the flash floods. "Flourished", "occupied" and "dried" do not fit rain causing floods.

Q 2 | eng | Fill in the Blanks
Given the scale of the challenges that ________ us today, our company proposes more viable plans for sustainability.
(1) supports
(2) conflict
(3) confronts
(4) convince
= 3
~ Challenges "confront" (stand before and face) us, so "confronts" is the only word with the right meaning. "Supports" and "convince" make no sense here, and "conflict" would need "with". (Strictly, the plural "challenges" takes "confront", but the meaning is what is being tested.)

Q 10 | eng | Fill in the Blanks

Q 51 | phy | Light & Sound
The streaming of light beams coming from the Sun through trees is said to have suggested that light travels in straight line. The particles on the path of light beams are visible to us because
(1) dust particles in the air reflect light into our eyes
(2) dust particles in the air scatter light into our eyes

Q 52 | phy | Light & Sound
The flash of lightning is seen before the thunderstorm is heard. It verifies that
(1) sound travels much faster than light
(2) light travels much faster than sound
(3) light and sound both travel with same speed
(4) intensity of flash of lightning is very high during thunderstorm
= 2

Q 59 | phy | Electricity & Magnetism
Which one of the following graphs correctly represents the current (I) – voltage (V) variation for a rectangular piece of a semiconductor wafer?
[Each option is a graph with I on the vertical axis and V on the horizontal axis, with origin 0.]
(1) [A straight line through the origin, rising with positive slope]
(2) [A straight line with negative slope: I decreases as V increases, reaching zero on the V-axis]
(3) [A curve starting at the origin, flat at first and then rising steeply (exponential-like, as for a diode)]
(4) [A curve that starts high on the I-axis and falls towards the V-axis as V increases (like a hyperbola)]
= 1
~ A uniform rectangular piece of semiconductor has no p–n junction, so at a fixed temperature it acts like an ordinary resistor and obeys Ohm's law. Its I–V graph is a straight line through the origin. The exponential curve in (3) is the graph for a p–n junction diode.

Q 60 | bio | Plants & Animals
The green plants in a terrestrial ecosystem absorbs sunlight that falls on their leaves and convert it into food energy. The percentage of solar radiation absorbed by all green plants for the process of photosynthesis is about
(1) 1%
(2) 5%
(3) 8%
(4) 10%
= 1
~ Green plants capture only about 1% of the sunlight that falls on them and store it as food energy through photosynthesis. The 10% figure in (4) is the ten per cent law for energy passed from one trophic level to the next.

Q 61 | chm | Physical & Chemical Changes
Consider the following reaction:
Fe₂O₃(s) + 2Al(s) → 2Fe(s) + Al₂O₃(s)
Which of the following statements about the given reaction is NOT correct?
(1) It is an example of displacement reaction
(2) It is highly endothermic reaction
(3) Fe₂O₃ acts as oxidizing agent
(4) This reaction is applicable to joining of railway tracks
= 2
~ This is the thermite reaction. Aluminium displaces iron from its oxide, and Fe₂O₃ oxidises the aluminium. The reaction is highly exothermic, not endothermic: it releases enough heat to give molten iron, which is used to weld railway tracks.
