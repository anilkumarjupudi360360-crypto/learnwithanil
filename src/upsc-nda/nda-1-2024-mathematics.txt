Q 1 | mat | Determinants
Let A and B be matrices of order 3×3. If |A| = 1/(2√2) and |B| = 1/729, then what is the value of |2B(adj(3A))| ?
(1) 27
(2) 27/(2√2)
(3) 27/2
(4) 1
= 4
~ For order 3, |adj(3A)| = |3A|² = (27|A|)² = (27/(2√2))² = 729/8. Then |2B·adj(3A)| = 2³ × |B| × 729/8 = 8 × (1/729) × (729/8) = 1.

Q 2 | alg | Complex Numbers
If z is any complex number and iz³ + z² − z + i = 0, where i = √(−1), then what is the value of (|z| + 1)²?
(1) 1
(2) 4
(3) 81
(4) 121
= 2
~ Multiplying by −i gives z³ − iz² + iz + 1 = (z − i)(z² + i) = 0, so z = i or z² = −i. In every case |z| = 1, so (|z| + 1)² = 4.

Q 3 | alg | Permutations & Combinations
What is the sum of all four digit numbers formed by using all digits 0, 1, 4, 5 without repetition of digits?
(1) 44440
(2) 46460
(3) 46440
(4) 64440
= 4
~ Sum over all 24 arrangements = 6 × 10 × 1111 = 66660; subtract those starting with 0 (3-digit numbers): 2 × 10 × 111 = 2220. 66660 − 2220 = 64440 (confirmed by listing all 18 numbers in python).

Q 4 | alg | Complex Numbers
If x, y and z are the cube roots of unity, then what is the value of xy + yz + zx ?
(1) 0
(2) 1
(3) 2
(4) 3
= 1
~ With x, y, z = 1, ω, ω²: xy + yz + zx = ω + ω³ + ω² = ω + 1 + ω² = 0.

Q 5 | alg | Permutations & Combinations
A man has 7 relatives (4 women and 3 men). His wife also has 7 relatives (3 women and 4 men). In how many ways can they invite 3 women and 3 men so that 3 of them are man's relatives and 3 of them are his wife's relatives?
(1) 340
(2) 484
(3) 485
(4) 469
= 3
~ If the man invites k women and 3 − k men, the wife invites 3 − k women and k men: Σ [C(4,k)C(3,3−k)]·[C(3,3−k)C(4,k)] for k = 0..3 = 1 + 144 + 324 + 16 = 485.

Q 6 | alg | Permutations & Combinations

(2) 206
(3) 215
(4) 220

Q 8 | alg | Quadratic Equations & Inequalities
If −√2 and √3 are roots of the equation a₀ + a₁x + a₂x² + a₃x³ + x⁴ = 0 where a₀, a₁, a₂, a₃ are integers, then which one of the following is correct?
(1) a₂ = a₃ = 0
(2) a₂ = 0 and a₃ = −5
(3) a₀ = 6, a₃ = 0
(4) a₁ = 0 and a₂ = 5
= 3
~ With integer coefficients, irrational roots come in conjugate pairs, so ±√2 and ±√3 are the roots: (x² − 2)(x² − 3) = x⁴ − 5x² + 6. Hence a₀ = 6, a₁ = 0, a₂ = −5, a₃ = 0.

Q 9 | alg | Complex Numbers

Q 11 | mat | Matrices
Consider the following statements in respect of two non-singular matrices A and B of the same order n:
1. adj(AB) = (adjA)(adjB)
2. adj(AB) = adj(BA)
3. (AB)adj(AB) − |AB|Iₙ is a null matrix of order n
How many of the above statements are correct?
(1) None
(2) Only one statement
(3) Only two statements
(4) All three statements
= 2
~ In general adj(AB) = (adjB)(adjA), not (adjA)(adjB), and adj(AB) ≠ adj(BA) since AB ≠ BA (random matrices confirm both fail). Statement 3 is the identity M·adj(M) = |M|I, so only one is correct.

Q 12 | mat | Matrices
Consider the following statements in respect of a non-singular matrix A of order n:
1. A(adjAᵀ) = A(adjA)ᵀ
2. If A² = A, then A is identity matrix of order n
3. If A³ = A, then A is identity matrix of order n
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(1) 1 and 2 only
(2) 2 and 3 only
(3) 1 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 1
~ adj(Aᵀ) = (adjA)ᵀ, so 1 is true. If A² = A and A is invertible, multiplying by A⁻¹ gives A = I, so 2 is true. A³ = A only gives A² = I (e.g. A = −I), so 3 is false.

Q 16 | mat | Matrices

Q 22 | alg | Sets, Relations & Functions
Let A = {x ∈ R: −1 < x < 1}. Which of the following is/are bijective functions from A to itself?
1. f(x) = x|x|
2. g(x) = cos(πx)
Select the correct answer using the code given below:
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 only
(3) Both 1 and 2
(4) Neither 1 nor 2
= 1

Q 23 | alg | Sets, Relations & Functions
Let R be a relation on the open interval (−1, 1) and is given by R = {(x, y) : |x + y| < 2}. Then which one of the following is correct?
(1) R is reflexive but neither symmetric nor transitive
(2) R is reflexive and symmetric but not transitive
(3) R is reflexive and transitive but not symmetric
(4) R is an equivalence relation
= 4
~ For x, y ∈ (−1, 1), |x + y| < 2 always holds, so R is the universal relation on the interval. The universal relation is reflexive, symmetric and transitive, i.e. an equivalence relation.

Q 24 | alg | Sets, Relations & Functions

(4) (A ∪ B) − (A ∩ B)

Q 28 | alg | Binary Numbers & Logarithms

(1) −1
(2) −1/2

(4) 1
= 4

Q 32 | alg | Quadratic Equations & Inequalities
If a, b and c (a > 0, c > 0) are in GP, then consider the following in respect of the equation ax² + bx + c = 0:
1. The equation has imaginary roots.
2. The ratio of the roots of the equation is 1 : ω where ω is a cube root of unity.
3. The product of roots of the equation is (b²/a²).
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(1) 1 and 2 only
(2) 2 and 3 only
(3) 1 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 4
~ b² = ac, so the discriminant b² − 4ac = −3ac < 0 (imaginary roots), and the product c/a = b²/a². Also (α + β)²/(αβ) = b²/(ac) = 1 gives r + 1/r = −1 for r = α/β, so r = ω. All three are correct (e.g. x² + 2x + 4 has roots −1 ± √3i with ratio ω).

Q 34 | alg | Binomial Theorem
In a binomial expansion of (x + y)²ⁿ⁺¹(x − y)²ⁿ⁺¹, the sum of middle terms is zero. What is the value of (x²/y²)?
(1) 1
(2) 2
(3) 4
(4) 8
= 1

Q 38 | mat | Determinants
If in a triangle ABC, sin³A + sin³B + sin³C = 3sinA sinB sinC, then what is the value of the determinant [[a, b, c], [b, c, a], [c, a, b]]; where a, b, c are sides of the triangle?

Q 45 | alg | Quadratic Equations & Inequalities
Under which one of the following conditions does the equation (cosβ − 1)x² + (cosβ)x + sinβ = 0 in x have a real root for β ∈ [0, π]?
