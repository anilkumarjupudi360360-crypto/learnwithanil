Q 1 | alg | Equations & Expressions
If p + q + r = 0, then what is z^(p²/qr) × z^(q²/rp) × z^(r²/pq) equal to?
(1) 1
(2) z
(3) z²
(4) z³
= 4
~ The product is z^((p³ + q³ + r³)/pqr). Since p + q + r = 0, p³ + q³ + r³ = 3pqr, so the exponent is 3 and the value is z³.

Q 2 | alg | Polynomials & Quadratics
What is the value of k for which (k² − 5k + 4)x² + (k² − 3k − 4)x + (k² − 4k) = 0 is an identity?
(1) −1
(2) 0
(3) 1
(4) 4
= 4
~ For an identity every coefficient must be zero: k² − 5k + 4 = 0 gives k = 1, 4; k² − 3k − 4 = 0 gives k = 4, −1; k² − 4k = 0 gives k = 0, 4. The only common value is k = 4.

Q 3 | alg | Equations & Expressions
If a²/(b² + c²) = b²/(c² + a²) = c²/(a² + b²), then what is the value of a⁴ + b⁴ + c⁴ equal to?
(1) a²b² + b²c² + c²a²
(2) 2(a²b² + b²c² + c²a²)
(3) 3(a²b² + b²c² + c²a²)
(4) 4(a²b² + b²c² + c²a²)
= 1
~ Adding numerators and denominators, each ratio equals (a² + b² + c²)/2(a² + b² + c²) = 1/2, so 2a² = b² + c², 2b² = c² + a², which give a² = b² = c². Then a⁴ + b⁴ + c⁴ = 3a⁴ = a²b² + b²c² + c²a².

Q 4 | alg | Equations & Expressions
If 1/x = 1/p + 1/q, then what is pq/(p² − q²) × ((x + p)/(x − p) − (x + q)/(x − q)) equal to?
(1) −2
(2) −1
(3) 1
(4) 2
= 4
~ x = pq/(p + q), so (x + p)/(x − p) = −(2p + q)/q and (x + q)/(x − q) = −(p + 2q)/p. Their difference is 2(p² − q²)/pq, so the whole expression is 2 (checked with p = 2, q = 3).

Q 5 | ari | Number System
Let P = 5⁵ × 15¹⁵ × 25²⁵ × 35³⁵ and Q = 10¹⁰ × 20²⁰ × 30³⁰ × 40⁴⁰. What is the number of consecutive zeros at the end of the sum (P + Q)?
(1) 100
(2) 65
(3) 50
(4) Zero
= 4
~ P has no factor 2, so P is odd, while Q is even. Hence P + Q is odd and cannot end in 0: the number of trailing zeros is zero.

Q 6 | alg | Polynomials & Quadratics
If (x − 5) is the HCF of x² − x − p and x² − qx − 10, then what is the value of (p + q)?
(1) −23
(2) −17
(3) 17
(4) 23
= 4
~ x = 5 must be a root of both: 25 − 5 − p = 0 gives p = 20 and 25 − 5q − 10 = 0 gives q = 3. So p + q = 23 (the polynomials are (x − 5)(x + 4) and (x − 5)(x + 2)).

Q 7 | alg | Polynomials & Quadratics
The LCM and HCF of two polynomials p(x) and q(x) are (x + a)(x³ − a³) and (x² − ax + a²), respectively. If p(x) = x⁴ + a²x² + a⁴, then what is q(x) equal to?
(1) x⁴ − 2a²x² + a⁴
(2) x⁴ + 2a²x² + a⁴
(3) x² − a²
(4) x⁴ − a²x² + a⁴
= 3
~ p(x) × q(x) = LCM × HCF. Since x⁴ + a²x² + a⁴ = (x² + ax + a²)(x² − ax + a²) and (x + a)(x³ − a³) = (x + a)(x − a)(x² + ax + a²), q(x) = (x + a)(x − a) = x² − a².

Q 8 | alg | Polynomials & Quadratics
Which of the following expressions can divide both the polynomials x³ + 2x² − 5x + 2 and x³ + 4x² + x − 6 exactly?
I. x − 1
II. x + 1
III. x + 2
Select the correct answer using the code given below:
(1) I and II only
(2) II and III only
(3) I and III only
(4) I only
= 4
~ At x = 1 both polynomials give 0. At x = −1 they give 8 and −4, and at x = −2 the first gives 12, so only (x − 1) divides both.

Q 9 | ari | Ratio, Time & Work
Eight men and 24 women can finish a piece of work in one day while 12 men and 18 women can also finish it in one day. What is the time taken by 24 men and 72 women to finish the work?
(1) 1/3 of a day
(2) 2/3 of a day
(3) 3 days
(4) 9/2 days
= 1
~ 8M + 24W = 12M + 18W gives 4M = 6W, so 1 man = 1.5 women and the work = 36 woman-days. 24 men + 72 women = 108 women, so time = 36/108 = 1/3 of a day.

Q 10 | alg | Equations & Expressions
For what integral value of k, the system of equations kx − 5y + 6 = 0 and 4(k − 1)y − 12x + 3 = 0 has no solution?
(1) 4
(2) 3
(3) 1
(4) No such value exists
= 4
~ No solution needs k/(−12) = −5/(4(k − 1)) ≠ 6/3, i.e. 4k(k − 1) = 60, or k² − k − 15 = 0, whose roots (1 ± √61)/2 are not integers. So no integral k works.

Q 11 | ari | Number System
Seven times a two-digit positive number is equal to four times the number obtained by reversing the order of the digits. How many such two-digit numbers are there?
(1) 1
(2) 4
(3) 6
(4) 8
= 2
~ 7(10a + b) = 4(10b + a) gives 66a = 33b, so b = 2a. The numbers are 12, 24, 36, 48: four of them.

Q 12 | ari | Percentage, Profit & Interest

Q 13 | alg | Equations & Expressions
If x varies directly as y, then which of the following is/are correct?
I. (x² + y²) varies directly as y².
II. (x³/y⁴) varies directly as y.
Select the answer using the code given below:
(1) I only
(2) II only
(3) Both I and II
(4) Neither I nor II
= 1
~ With x = cy, x² + y² = (c² + 1)y², so I is correct. But x³/y⁴ = c³/y, which varies inversely as y, so II is wrong.

Q 14 | ari | Ratio, Time & Work
X can do a piece of work in 4 days and Y can do the same piece of work in 6 days. Which of the following statements is/are correct?
I. If X and Y work alternately starting with X, then the piece of work will be finished on the fifth day.
II. If X and Y work alternately starting with Y, then the piece of work will be finished in less than 5 days.
Select the answer using the code given below:
(1) I only
(2) II only
(3) Both I and II
(4) Neither I nor II
= 1
~ Every 2 days they do 1/4 + 1/6 = 5/12. Starting with X: after 4 days 10/12 is done, X finishes the remaining 1/6 in 2/3 of the fifth day (total 4⅔ days), so I is correct. Starting with Y: after 4 days 10/12, and Y needs the whole fifth day for 1/6, so it takes exactly 5 days and II is wrong.

Q 15 | ari | Ratio, Time & Work
12 men working 8 hours a day can completely build a wall of length 100 m, breadth 20 cm and height 5 m in 10 days. How many days will 16 men working 10 hours a day require to build a wall of length 200 m, breadth 60 cm and height 5 m?
(1) 18
(2) 24
(3) 27
(4) 36
= 4
~ The second wall has 6 times the volume (200 × 0.6 vs 100 × 0.2). Days = 10 × (12 × 8)/(16 × 10) × 6 = 36.

Q 16 | ari | Speed & Distance
A train P starts from station X for station Y and another train Q starts from station Y for station X at the same time. After passing each other, P takes 4 hours to reach Y and Q takes 1 hour to reach X. If the average speed of P is 60 km/h, then what is the average speed of Q?
(1) 72 km/h
(2) 80 km/h
(3) 96 km/h
(4) 120 km/h
= 4
~ For trains starting together, (speed of P)/(speed of Q) = √(time of Q after meeting/time of P after meeting) = √(1/4) = 1/2. So Q's speed = 2 × 60 = 120 km/h.

Q 17 | ari | Speed & Distance
Without stoppages, a train travels at an average speed of 60 km/h and with stoppages it covers the same distance at an average speed of 48 km/h. How many minutes per hour does the train stop?
(1) 10 minutes
(2) 12 minutes
(3) 15 minutes
(4) 20 minutes
= 2
~ In each hour the train loses 60 − 48 = 12 km, which at 60 km/h takes 12/60 h = 12 minutes.

Q 18 | ari | Speed & Distance
A person goes downstream at x km/h and upstream at y km/h by the same boat. What is the ratio of speed of boat in still water to speed of water?
(1) x/y
(2) y/x
(3) (x + y)/(x − y)
(4) (x − y)/(x + y)
= 3
~ Boat speed = (x + y)/2 and stream speed = (x − y)/2, so their ratio is (x + y)/(x − y).

Q 19 | alg | Polynomials & Quadratics
Let Q = x² + bx + c. If the sum of the roots is equal to product of the roots of the equation Q = 0, then which of the following statements is/are correct?
I. Q can be a perfect square.
II. Q is positive for all real values of x.
Select the answer using the code given below:
(1) I only
(2) II only
(3) Both I and II
(4) Neither I nor II
= 1
~ −b = c, so Q = x² + bx − b. With b = −4, Q = x² − 4x + 4 = (x − 2)², so I is correct. Q > 0 for all x only when b² + 4b < 0 (−4 < b < 0); e.g. b = 0 gives Q = x², which is 0 at x = 0, so II is not always true.

Q 20 | ari | Ratio, Time & Work
The ratio of the present age of X to that of Y is 4 : 5. k (less than 10) years ago, this ratio was 1 : 2. How many values of k are possible if the ages are expressed in complete years?
(1) 1
(2) 2
(3) 3
(4) More than 3
= 3
~ Let the ages be 4t and 5t. (4t − k)/(5t − k) = 1/2 gives k = 3t, so t must be a whole number with 3t < 10: t = 1, 2, 3, giving k = 3, 6, 9. Three values.

Q 21 | ari | Number System
27²⁷ − 9⁴⁰ − 3⁷⁹ is divisible by how many natural numbers less than 10?
(1) 1
(2) 2
(3) 3
(4) More than 3
= 4
~ 27²⁷ − 9⁴⁰ − 3⁷⁹ = 3⁸¹ − 3⁸⁰ − 3⁷⁹ = 3⁷⁹(9 − 3 − 1) = 5 × 3⁷⁹. It is odd, so the divisors below 10 are 1, 3, 5, 9: four numbers.

Q 22 | ari | Number System
If the HCF of p and q (p > q) is G, then which of the following statements is/are correct?
I. HCF of p and (p + q) is G
II. HCF of p, (p − q) is G
Select the answer using the code given below:
(1) I only
(2) II only
(3) Both I and II
(4) Neither I nor II
= 3
~ Any common divisor of p and p ± q also divides q, and conversely, so HCF(p, p + q) = HCF(p, p − q) = HCF(p, q) = G. Both are correct.

Q 33 | ari | Percentage, Profit & Interest

Q 34 | ari | Ratio, Time & Work
A locomotive engine can go 40 km/hr. Its speed gets reduced by a quantity that varies directly as the square root of the number of wagons attached. It is known that its speed becomes 16 km/hr if 36 wagons are attached. What is the smallest number of wagons with which the engine is unable to move?

(2) 81
(3) 64
(4) 49
= 1
~ Speed = 40 − k√n; 16 = 40 − 6k gives k = 4. The speed becomes 0 when 4√n = 40, i.e. n = 100 (with 81 wagons it still moves at 4 km/hr).

Q 35 | ari | Ratio, Time & Work
Two numbers P and Q are such that P : Q = x : y. If 1 is added to both the numbers, the ratio becomes 7 : 10. If 3 is added to both the numbers, the ratio becomes 3 : 4. What is (x + y) : (x − y) equal to?

(2) −3
(3) 3
(4) Cannot be determined due to insufficient data
= 1
~ 10(P + 1) = 7(Q + 1) and 4(P + 3) = 3(Q + 3) give P = 6, Q = 9. So (x + y)/(x − y) = 15/(−3) = −5.

Q 36 | gm | Lines, Angles & Triangles

Q 39 | sts | Statistics & Data
The sum of the deviations of a set of n numbers x₁, x₂, x₃, …, xₙ measured from 15 is −90 and the sum of the deviations of the same numbers measured from −3 is 54. What is the arithmetic mean?

Q 40 | sts | Statistics & Data

(2) 4 only
(3) −12, 4

Q 41 | trg | Ratios & Identities
If cosθ/(1 − sinθ) + cosθ/(1 + sinθ) = 4, then which one of the following is a value of (tan²θ + cot²θ)?

Q 42 | trg | Ratios & Identities
For 0 < θ < π/2, consider the following:
I. (tan⁴θ + tan⁶θ)(cot⁴θ + cot⁶θ) = sec²θ cosec²θ
II. (tanθ + sinθ)/(tanθ − sinθ) = cot²θ(secθ + 1)²
Which of the above is/are identities?
(1) I only
(2) II only
(3) Both I and II
(4) Neither I nor II
= 3
~ I: the left side is tan⁴θ(1 + tan²θ) × cot⁴θ(1 + cot²θ) = sec²θ × cosec²θ, since tan⁴θ cot⁴θ = 1. II: (tanθ + sinθ)/(tanθ − sinθ) = (1 + cosθ)/(1 − cosθ) = (1 + cosθ)²/sin²θ = cot²θ(secθ + 1)². Both checked numerically at several angles.

Q 46 | gm | Circles & Quadrilaterals
In a cyclic quadrilateral ABCD, the diagonal AC bisects the angle C. Which of the following statements is/are correct?
I. ∠ABD = ∠ADB
II. The diagonal BD is parallel to the tangent of the circle at A
Select the answer using the code given below:
(1) I only
(2) II only
(3) Both I and II
(4) Neither I nor II
= 3
~ ∠ACB = ∠ACD means chords AB and AD subtend equal angles, so AB = AD and ∠ABD = ∠ADB. Also, by the alternate segment theorem the tangent at A makes ∠ADB with AB, which equals ∠ABD, so BD is parallel to the tangent. Both are correct.

Q 47 | gm | Mensuration
Let x be the length of a diagonal of a face of a cube and y be the length of a diagonal of the cube. If x + y = (5 + 2√6) units, then what is the total surface area of the cube?
(1) 6(x + y)
(2) 6xy
(3) 3(x + y)
(4) 3xy

~ With side a, x + y = a(√2 + √3) = 5 + 2√6 = (√2 + √3)², so a = √2 + √3. Total surface area = 6a² = 6(5 + 2√6) = 6(x + y).

Q 48 | gm | Lines, Angles & Triangles
ABC is a triangle in which AB = AC and D is any point on BC. Which of the following statements is/are correct?
I. AB² − AD² = CD × BD
II. BC² + BD² − DC² = 2BD × BC
Select the answer using the code given below:
(1) I only
(2) II only
(3) Both I and II
(4) Neither I nor II
= 3
~ I: drop AM ⊥ BC (M the midpoint); AB² − AD² = BM² − DM² = (BM − DM)(BM + DM) = BD × DC. II: with BC = a and BD = d, a² + d² − (a − d)² = 2ad = 2BD × BC. Both hold (checked with coordinates).

Q 50 | gm | Lines, Angles & Triangles
ABC is an equilateral triangle. Let PQRS be a square inscribed in it such that P is on AB and Q is on AC. Which of the following is/are correct?

Q 51 | gm | Lines, Angles & Triangles
The perimeter of a right-angled isosceles triangle is 20 units. If α is the area of the triangle, then
(1) 15 < α < 16
(2) 16 < α < 17
(3) 17 < α < 18
(4) 18 < α < 19
= 3
