Q 1 | eng | Synonyms & Antonyms
Directions: In the following items, a pair of words is provided. You are required to select the option that most appropriately describes the meaning of both the words and mark your response on the Answer Sheet accordingly.
Presumptive and Presumptuous
(1) Presumptive means something likely to be true, and Presumptuous means to be disrespectfully bold
(2) Presumptive means to be disrespectfully bold, and Presumptuous means something likely to be true
(3) Presumptive means to exist, and Presumptuous means something very important
(4) Presumptive means something very important, and Presumptuous means to exist

~ "Presumptive" means based on a reasonable assumption, i.e. probably true (e.g. heir presumptive); "presumptuous" means overstepping limits, impertinently bold.

An 1860 report states that by 1780, potatoes, peas and beans were in high repute as foods in Calcutta, and that the Dutch are said to have been the first to introduce the culture of potatoes, which was received from their settlement of the Cape of Good Hope. From there the British received annually the seeds of every kind of vegetable useful at the table, as well as several plants of which there appears to be much need, especially various kinds of pot herbs.

According to this passage, how long ago was the potato domesticated in South America?
(1) Two thousand years ago
(2) Two to five thousand years ago
(3) Four to seven thousand years ago
(4) Ten thousand years ago
= 3
~ Domestication was "sometime between 5000 and 2000 BC"; adding about 2,000 years of the Common Era gives roughly 4,000 to 7,000 years ago.

Q 118 | eng | Reading Comprehension
Passage: Christopher Columbus of Spain set foot in the New World in 1492. Six years later, Vasco da Gama of Portugal reached India. During the next two centuries the Spaniards and the Portuguese actively transferred, not only to Europe but to many parts of Asia as well, a number of plant species which they encountered in South and central America, in expectation of their potential either as food or as medicine. These plants had been developed to a high degree by the Maya and Inca civilizations of these regions. Of these, the potato (Solanum tuberosum) is believed to have been domesticated in terraced slopes on the high plateau of Bolivia-Peru in South America, sometime between 5000 and 2000 BC. In this passage the word "culture" refers to
(1) The arts, music, literature and other intellectual achievements
(2) All the knowledge and values shared by a society
(3) The practices of the Maya and Inca civilisations
(4) Certain techniques of raising plants for propagation
= 4
~ In "introduce the culture of potatoes", culture means cultivation, i.e. the growing of plants (as in agriculture, horticulture), not arts or social values.

Which form of the word "settlement" can be used both as a verb and as an adjective?
(1) Settled
(2) Settling
(3) Settler
(4) Settle
= 1
~ "Settled" is the past tense/past participle of the verb (They settled at the Cape) and also an adjective (a settled life, settled weather). "Settler" is a noun and "settle" only a verb. Note: Doon Defence Dreamers' key gives (2); "settling" is chiefly a verb form or noun, and is much less established as an adjective; SSBCrack's key gives (1).

The word "terraced" has been formed by changing
(1) A noun to a verb
(2) A verb to a noun
(3) An adjective to a noun
(4) A noun to an adjective
= 4
~ "Terrace" is a noun; "terraced" in "terraced slopes" is an adjective describing the slopes, so a noun has been changed into an adjective. Note: Doon Defence Dreamers' key gives (1); "terraced" here qualifies "slopes" and is not used as a verb; SSBCrack's key gives (4).
