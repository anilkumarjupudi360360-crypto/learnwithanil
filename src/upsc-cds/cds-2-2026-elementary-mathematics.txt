Q 1 | ari | Number System
Two positive integers x and y are in the ratio 17 : 19. If the LCM of the two numbers is 1615, then what is (x + y) equal to?
(1) 36
(2) 180
(3) 360
(4) Cannot be determined due to insufficient data
= 2
~ Let x = 17k and y = 19k; since 17 and 19 are co-prime, LCM = 323k = 1615, so k = 5. Then x + y = 36k = 180.

Q 2 | alg | Equations & Expressions
If p/q = q/r = r/s = k, then what is (p³ + q³ + r³)/(q³ + r³ + s³) equal to?
(1) p/s
(2) r/q
(3) s/p
(4) q/r
= 1
~ Each term of the numerator is k times the matching term of the denominator (p = kq, q = kr, r = ks), so the ratio is k³. Also p/s = (p/q)(q/r)(r/s) = k³, so the answer is p/s.

Q 3 | alg | Polynomials & Quadratics
If x² − 4x + 3 is a factor of x⁴ + px² + q, then what is (p − q) equal to?
(1) −19
(2) −17
(3) 17
(4) 19
= 1
~ x² − 4x + 3 = (x − 1)(x − 3), so x = 1 and x = 3 are zeros: 1 + p + q = 0 and 81 + 9p + q = 0. Subtracting gives 8p = −80, so p = −10, q = 9 and p − q = −19.

Q 4 | alg | Polynomials & Quadratics
Suppose p and q are positive integers where q is not a perfect square. If 1/(p + √q) is a root of a quadratic equation having integer coefficients, then which of the following statements is/are correct?
I. The sum of the roots is 2p times the product of the roots.
II. The sum of the reciprocals of the roots is equal to 2p.
Select the answer using the code given below:
(1) I only
(2) II only
(3) Both I and II
(4) Neither I nor II
= 3
~ The roots are (p − √q)/(p² − q) and its conjugate (p + √q)/(p² − q), so sum = 2p/(p² − q) and product = 1/(p² − q); sum = 2p × product (I true). The reciprocals of the roots are p + √q and p − √q, whose sum is 2p (II true).

Q 5 | alg | Equations & Expressions
If 1/(2b + 2c) + 1/(2c + 2a) = 1/(a + b), then what is a² + b² + c² equal to?
(1) 9c²
(2) 4c²
(3) 3c²
(4) 2c²
= 3
~ Clearing denominators: (a + b)(c + a) + (a + b)(b + c) = 2(b + c)(c + a), i.e. (a + b)² + 2c(a + b) = 2c² + 2ab + 2c(a + b), which gives a² + b² = 2c². Hence a² + b² + c² = 3c².

Q 6 | ari | Speed & Distance
A train completely crossed two persons X and Y moving in the same direction as the train in 8 seconds and 8·1 seconds respectively. X is walking at a uniform speed of 3 km/hr and Y is walking at a uniform speed of 4 km/hr. What is the speed of the train?
(1) 81 km/hr
(2) 82 km/hr
(3) 83 km/hr
(4) 84 km/hr
= 4
~ The train's length is the same in both cases: (v − 3) × 8 = (v − 4) × 8·1, so 8v − 24 = 8·1v − 32·4, giving 0·1v = 8·4 and v = 84 km/hr.

Q 7 | ari | Number System
A number is formed by three digits, each less by unity than the digit that follows it. If 15 is added to the number, then the sum is 30 times the sum of the digits of the number. What is the product of the digits of the number?
(1) 6
(2) 24
(3) 60
(4) 120
= 3

Q 12 | alg | Polynomials & Quadratics
Consider the polynomial
p(x) = x⁵ + 2x⁴ + x³ − x² − 2x − 1:
I. x² + 2x + 1 is a factor of p(x).
II. x² + x + 1 divides p(x).

(2) II only

(4) Neither I nor II

Q 14 | ari | Speed & Distance
Had I walked 0·5 km/hr faster, I would have taken 8 minutes less to walk 2 km. What is my original speed?

(2) 2·75 km/hr
(3) 3 km/hr
(4) 3·25 km/hr

Q 15 | alg | Polynomials & Quadratics
If the roots of the equation x² + mx + n = 0 are increased by the same quantity k, then they become the roots of the equation x² + nx + m = 0. What is the value of (m + n)?
(1) −4
(2) −2
(3) 2
(4) 4
= 1
~ Sum: −m + 2k = −n, so k = (m − n)/2. Product: n − mk + k² = m, i.e. k² − mk − 2k = 0, so k = m + 2 (k ≠ 0). Then (m − n)/2 = m + 2, giving m + n = −4.

Q 16 | alg | Polynomials & Quadratics
If α and β are the roots of the equation 2x² − 2(n + 1)x + (n² + n + 1) = 0, then what is (2α³ + 2β³) equal to?
(1) n³ − 1
(2) 1 − n³
(3) −(n³ + 1)
(4) −(2n³ + 1)
= 3
~ α + β = n + 1 and αβ = (n² + n + 1)/2. So 2(α³ + β³) = 2(n + 1)³ − 3(n + 1)(n² + n + 1) = (n + 1)(−n² + n − 1) = −(n³ + 1).

Q 18 | alg | Polynomials & Quadratics
What is the HCF of x³ + x²y − 10xy² + 8y³ and x³ + x²y − 4xy² − 4y³?

Q 19 | alg | Equations & Expressions

Q 20 | alg | Polynomials & Quadratics
The LCM and HCF of two polynomials p(x) and q(x) are (x + k)² (x² − 7x + 6) and (x + k) respectively. If [x³ + (2k − 1)x² + k(k − 2)x − k²] is one of the polynomials, then which of the following statements is/are correct?
I. The other polynomial is (x + k) (x − 6) where k ≠ −6.
II. (x + k) cannot be the HCF if k = −6.
Select the answer using the code given below:
(1) I only
(2) II only
(3) Both I and II
(4) Neither I nor II
= 3
~ The given polynomial is (x + k)²(x − 1), so the other = LCM × HCF ÷ it = (x + k)³(x − 1)(x − 6)/[(x + k)²(x − 1)] = (x + k)(x − 6) (I true). If k = −6 both polynomials would contain (x − 6)², so their HCF would be (x − 6)², not (x + k) (II true).

Q 24 | ari | Speed & Distance
A boatman can row to a place (Y) at a distance of 24 km from the starting point (X) and back in 14 hours. If he can row 4 km with the stream in the same time as 3 km against it, what is the speed of the stream?
(1) 0·5 km/hr
(2) 1 km/hr
(3) 2 km/hr
(4) 2·5 km/hr
= 1
~ Downstream and upstream speeds are 4t and 3t: 24/(4t) + 24/(3t) = 14 gives 14/t = 14, t = 1. So the speeds are 4 and 3 km/hr and the stream's speed = (4 − 3)/2 = 0·5 km/hr.

Q 37 | trg | Ratios & Identities
If (1 − tan x)/(1 + tan x) = 1 − 2 tan x/(1 + tan² x), 0 ≤ x < π/2, x ≠ π/4, then what is (sin x + cos x) equal to?

Q 42 | trg | Ratios & Identities
