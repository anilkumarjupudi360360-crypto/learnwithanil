Q 5 | pol | Judiciary
Which one of the following Writs literally means 'you have the body'?
(1) Habeas Corpus
(2) Mandamus
(3) Prohibition
(4) Certiorari
= 1
~ "Habeas corpus" is Latin for "you may have the body". The writ orders that a detained person be produced before the court, so the court can check whether the detention is lawful. Mandamus means "we command".

Q 10 | pol | Constitution & Rights
Which of the following statements is/are correct?
1. Using powers given under Article 342 A of the Constitution of India, the President of India may notify a Central List of socially, educationally and economically backward classes with respect to a State
2. Every State may prepare a list of socially and educationally backward classes for its own purposes different from the Central List
Select the answer using the code given below:
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 only
(3) Both 1 and 2
(4) Neither 1 nor 2
= 3
~ Article 342A(1) lets the President specify, by public notification, the backward classes in the Central List in relation to each State. Article 342A(3), added by the 105th Amendment (2021), lets every State keep its own list, which may differ from the Central List. Strictly, Article 342A speaks of "socially and educationally" backward classes; the word "economically" in statement 1 looks like loose wording, not a trap. (Answer by Learn with Anil; UPSC has not published its key.)

Q 37 | geo | Physical Geography
The Tetrahedral Hypothesis of the origin of continents and ocean basin was proposed by
(1) F B Taylor
(2) Lowthian Green
(3) Alfred Wegener
(4) W M Davis
= 2
~ William Lowthian Green proposed the tetrahedral hypothesis (1875): a cooling, shrinking Earth takes a roughly tetrahedral shape, with continents at the corners and oceans on the faces. Taylor and Wegener are linked with continental drift, and Davis with the cycle of erosion.
