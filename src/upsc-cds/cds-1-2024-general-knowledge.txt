Q 1 | his | Ancient India
Consider the following statements about the Jorwe culture of the Deccan :
1. It covers, practically, the whole of modern Maharashtra except the coastal Konkan district.
2. The Pravara-Godavari valleys seem to have been the nuclear zone.
3. The large sites of this culture yield evidence of shifting agriculture.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(1) 1 only
(2) 1 and 2 only
(3) 2 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 2
~ The Jorwe chalcolithic culture (c. 1400–700 BCE) spread over almost all of Maharashtra except the Konkan coast, with the Pravara-Godavari valleys (Jorwe, Nevasa, Daimabad, Inamgaon) as its core. Its large sites such as Inamgaon and Daimabad were settled farming villages, not shifting cultivation, so statement 3 is wrong.

Q 2 | his | Ancient India
Consider the following statements about Madurai, the capital city of the Pandya kingdom :
1. It has been described in Maduraikkanchi as a large grand city, enclosed by walls on three sides and the Vaigai river on the fourth side.
2. The Arthashastra mentions it as a centre of fine cotton textiles.
3. Other literary sources describe it as a major craft centre.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(1) 1 only
(2) 1 and 2 only
(3) 2 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 4
~ The Sangam poem Maduraikkanchi describes Madurai as a grand walled city with the Vaigai on one side, Kautilya's Arthashastra praises the fine cotton fabrics of Madhura (Madurai), and other Tamil texts describe its many artisans and craft streets. All three statements are correct.

Q 3 | his | Medieval India
Consider the following statements about Sawai Jai Singh's astronomical work :
1. He learnt of the accuracy of European observations, and obtained de La Hire's tables from which he reproduced a refraction table.
2. His astronomers also developed a telescope of their own to observe the lunar phases of Venus.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 only
(3) Both 1 and 2
(4) Neither 1 nor 2
= 1
~ Sawai Jai Singh of Amber obtained Philippe de La Hire's astronomical tables (via Jesuits) and used them, including a refraction table, in his Zij-i-Muhammadshahi. His observatories (Jantar Mantar) relied on large masonry instruments; his astronomers did not develop their own telescope, so statement 2 is wrong.

Q 6 | his | Modern India
Which one among the following was not a part of the action programme of the Non-Cooperation Movement?
(1) The Congress organisation was to reach down to the village and the Mohalla level
(2) Boycott of government affiliated schools and colleges
(3) Taking control of the law and order machinery of the State by the Congress Working Committee
(4) Surrender of titles and honours given by the government

~ The Non-Cooperation programme (Calcutta and Nagpur sessions, 1920) included surrender of titles, boycott of government schools, courts and councils, boycott of foreign cloth, and reorganising Congress down to village and mohalla committees. Seizing control of the State's law and order machinery was never part of this non-violent programme.

Q 7 | his | Ancient India
Consider the following statements about the Mauryan State and the forest people :
1. The forest people were subjected to new forms of political and economic dominance and the necessity to subordinate and assimilate them led to a change in the earlier attitude of excluding these people from imperial territory.
2. The State recognised that the forest produce was the sole monopoly of the forest people.
3. The State was concerned with the conservation of forests and to this end the burning of forests was prohibited.
4. The forest people could be harnessed to serve the State and could be used as troops, spies and assassins.
How many of the above statements is/are correct?
(1) 1
(2) 2
(3) 3
(4) 4
= 3
~ Statements 1, 3 and 4 are correct: the Mauryas sought to subordinate forest tribes (Ashoka's edicts warn them), the Arthashastra and Ashokan edicts forbid burning forests, and Kautilya recommends using forest people (atavikas) as troops and spies. Statement 2 is wrong because the State itself claimed forest produce, managed by the superintendent of forest produce (kupyadhyaksha).

Q 9 | sci | Science & Technology

(2) It orbits the Earth, about 15 lakh km away.

Q 13 | sci | Physics

(1) 0·5 MeV
(2) 900 MeV
(3) 100 GeV
(4) 1000 GeV
= 3
~ The Higgs boson found at CERN's LHC in 2012 has a mass of about 125 GeV, which is closest to 100 GeV. 0.5 MeV is roughly the electron's mass and about 900 MeV is roughly a proton's.

Q 14 | sci | Physics
Which one of the following correctly describes the principle of the working of an atomic clock?
(1) Vibration of a small quartz crystal
(2) Simple harmonic motion of atoms inside a crystal
(3) Resonant frequency in cesium (or rubidium) atom
(4) Excitation and de-excitation of hydrogen atoms
= 3
~ An atomic clock is locked to the resonant frequency of an atomic transition, usually the hyperfine transition of cesium-133 (9,192,631,770 Hz, which defines the second) or rubidium. A vibrating quartz crystal is how an ordinary quartz watch works.

Q 15 | pol | Constitution & Rights
Political analysis involves both normative and empirical approaches. Which of the following statements is/are correct about the two approaches?

2. Knowledge acquired through the normative approach is objectively neutral.
Select the correct answer using the code given below.
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 only
(3) Both 1 and 2
(4) Neither 1 nor 2

Q 16 | pol | Constitution & Rights
Which one amongst the following cannot be a feature of a 'totalitarian regime'?
(1) State control of the political and often personal realms of individual life
(2) A monopoly of the means of mass communication
(3) One-party State
(4) Autonomy of civil society

Q 21 | pol | Constitution & Rights
Which of the following statements as per the 'Universal Declaration of Human Rights' is/are correct?

Q 27 | sci | Biology
An antibacterial enzyme present in saliva and tears is called
(1) ribozyme
(2) lipase
(3) lysozyme
(4) isomerase
= 3
~ Lysozyme in tears, saliva and mucus breaks down bacterial cell walls (peptidoglycan) and so acts as part of innate immunity. A ribozyme is a catalytic RNA, and lipase digests fats.

Q 34 | sci | Chemistry

Q 47 | geo | Physical Geography
Identify the type of drainage pattern on the basis of the given characteristics :
1. It represents the most common pattern of streams and their tributaries
2. It occurs in areas of uniform rock type and regular slope
3. A map, or aerial photograph, shows a pattern like the veins on a leaf—smaller streams join the main stream at an acute angle

(2) Radial
(3) Trellis
(4) Centripetal
= 1
