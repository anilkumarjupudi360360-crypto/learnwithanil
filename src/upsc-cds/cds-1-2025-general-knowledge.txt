Q 1 | geo | Physical Geography
Consider the following statements with reference to Secondary waves (S-Waves) generated during an earthquake :
1. They create troughs and crests in the material through which they pass.
2. The direction of vibrations of S-waves is perpendicular to the wave direction in the vertical plane.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct ?
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 only
(3) Both 1 and 2
(4) Neither 1 nor 2
= 3
~ S-waves are transverse waves: they vibrate perpendicular to the direction of travel in the vertical plane and so create troughs and crests in the material they pass through. Both statements are as given in the NCERT text.

Q 2 | geo | Physical Geography
Identify the Mineral on the basis of the following characteristics relating to it :
I. It is usually a greenish crystal.
II. It is often found in basaltic rocks.
III. Magnesium, iron and silica are its major elements.
Select the answer using the code given below :
(1) Amphibolite
(2) Feldspar
(3) Pyroxene
(4) Olivine

~ Olivine is a magnesium-iron silicate that usually forms green crystals and is common in basaltic rocks. Feldspar is mainly silicon, oxygen, aluminium with sodium/potassium/calcium.

Q 4 | geo | Physical Geography

Q 6 | geo | World Geography

Select the answer using the code given below :
(1) Sub-tropical steppe climate
(2) Mid-latitude steppe

(2) 1, 3, 2

Q 21 | sci | Physics
If your image appears to be erect, no matter how far you stand from a mirror, the mirror is likely to be :
(1) Plane
(2) Concave
(3) Convex
(4) Either plane or convex
= 4
~ Both a plane mirror and a convex mirror always form an erect (virtual) image at any distance. A concave mirror gives an inverted real image when the object is beyond its focus.

Q 22 | sci | Physics
The rule that determines the direction of a magnetic field produced around a straight conductor carrying current is :
(1) Right-hand thumb rule
(2) Fleming's left-hand rule
(3) Fleming's right-hand rule
(4) Hund's rule
= 1
~ Hold the conductor in the right hand with the thumb along the current, and the curled fingers give the direction of the field lines (Maxwell's right-hand thumb rule). Fleming's rules give the force on a conductor and the induced current.

(3) greater than its weight.
(4) zero.

(1) China
(2) England
(3) USA
(4) Ireland
= 3
~ The bald eagle had been the US national emblem since 1782, but it was formally made the national bird only in December 2024, when President Biden signed the law.

Q 118 | cur | Defence & Security
Surya Kiran is a joint military exercise between India and :
(1) Bangladesh
(2) Bhutan
(3) Singapore
(4) Nepal
= 4
~ Surya Kiran is the India–Nepal joint army exercise. India–Bangladesh is Sampriti, and India–Singapore includes Agni Warrior and SIMBEX.

Q 119 | cur | National
Which of the following statements is/are correct ?
1. Moidam-The Mound-Burial system of the Ahom dynasty was inscribed in the UNESCO World Heritage list.
2. The Government of India conferred the status of classical language to Pali, Assamese, Bengali and Maithili.
3. Project PARI is an initiative of Ministry of Education.
Select the answer using the code given below :
(1) 2 only
(2) 1 only
(3) 2 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 2
~ The Moidams of Charaideo (Assam) were added to the World Heritage list in July 2024. In October 2024, classical status went to Marathi, Pali, Prakrit, Assamese and Bengali, not Maithili. Project PARI (Public Art of India) belongs to the Ministry of Culture. Only statement 1 is correct.

Q 120 | sci | Science & Technology
Which one of the following PSLVs, launched by ISRO, is not correctly matched with their Missions ?
PSLV : Mission
(1) PSLV C60 : SPADEX
(2) PSLV C59 : Proba-3
(3) PSLV C58 : XPoSat
(4) PSLV C57 : DS-SAR
= 4
~ PSLV-C57 launched Aditya-L1 (September 2023), and DS-SAR went up on PSLV-C56 (July 2023). The other pairs are correct: C58 XPoSat (January 2024), C59 Proba-3 (December 2024) and C60 SpaDeX (December 2024).
