Q 1 | sci | Biology
A person can see near objects clearly through his eyes but cannot see distant objects clearly. The possible reason(s) for this defect could be:
1. Excessive curvature of the eye lens.
2. The eye-ball has become too small.
3. The focal length of the eye lens is too large.
4. Elongation of the eye-ball.
Select the correct answer using the code given below:
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 and 4 only
(3) 1 and 4 only
(4) 1 and 3 only
= 3
~ This is myopia (short-sightedness), caused by excessive curvature of the eye lens (too short a focal length) or elongation of the eyeball, so the image of distant objects forms in front of the retina. A too-small eyeball or a too-long focal length cause hypermetropia instead.

Q 2 | sci | Physics
Consider a journey by a car represented by the graph given below in three parts A, B and C. The speed of the car in these parts is vₐ, v_b and v_c, respectively:
[Figure: distance (m) on the y-axis versus time (s) on the x-axis. The graph starts at O and has three straight segments: A, a moderately sloped segment; B, the steepest segment; C, an almost flat segment with the least slope.]
Which one of the following is correct in this case?
(1) vₐ < v_b < v_c
(2) v_b > vₐ > v_c
(3) vₐ = v_b = v_c
(4) vₐ > v_b ; vₐ > v_c
= 2
~ On a distance–time graph, the slope gives the speed. Segment B is the steepest, A has a medium slope and C is nearly flat, so v_b > vₐ > v_c.

Q 3 | sci | Physics
A concave mirror of radius of curvature 50 cm is used to form an image of an object kept at a distance of 25 cm from the mirror on its principal axis. What will be the position of the image from the mirror?
(1) At infinity
(2) At 50 cm
(3) At 25 cm
(4) At 75 cm
= 1
~ The focal length is f = R/2 = 25 cm, so the object is at the focus. The reflected rays come out parallel and the image forms at infinity (1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/25 − 1/25 = 0).

Q 4 | sci | Physics
If the Moon is brought closer to the Earth such that its distance from the Earth becomes half of the original distance, then the gravitational force of attraction between the Earth and the Moon would:
(1) reduce to half of its original value.
(2) increase to two times of its original value.
(3) remain the same as the original value.
(4) increase to four times of its original value.
= 4
~ Gravitational force follows the inverse-square law, F = GMm/r². Halving r makes the force 1/(1/2)² = 4 times as large.

Q 5 | sci | Physics
Sonic boom is produced when a source of sound travels at a speed:
(1) greater than the speed of sound.
(2) greater than the speed of light.
(3) lesser than the speed of sound.
(4) equal to the speed of sound.
= 1
~ A body moving faster than sound (supersonic) produces shock waves, which are heard as a loud sonic boom. No material source can travel faster than light.

Q 8 | sci | Chemistry
Which among the following methods is used to separate the constituents present in a compound?
(1) Electrochemical method
(2) Heating method
(3) Fractional distillation
(4) Decomposition followed by ion exchange
= 1
~ The constituents of a compound are chemically combined, so only chemical methods such as electrolysis (an electrochemical method) can separate them. An example is water splitting into hydrogen and oxygen. Physical methods such as fractional distillation separate only the components of mixtures.

Q 9 | sci | Chemistry
During white-washing of walls, slaked lime reacts slowly with carbon dioxide in air to form a thin layer of calcium carbonate on the walls. Which of the following reactions represents this correctly?
(1) CaO (s) + CO₂ (g) → CaCO₃ (s)
(2) CaO (l) + CO₂ (g) → CaCO₃ (s)
(3) Ca(OH)₂ (l) + CO₂ (l) → CaCO₃ (s) + H₂O (l)
(4) Ca(OH)₂ (aq) + CO₂ (g) → CaCO₃ (s) + H₂O (l)
= 4
~ Slaked lime is calcium hydroxide, Ca(OH)₂, applied as an aqueous suspension. It reacts with gaseous CO₂ in the air to give solid CaCO₃ and water. CaO is quicklime, not slaked lime, and CO₂ in air is a gas, not a liquid.

(2) Stack

Q 32 | eco | Agriculture, Industry & Trade
Which of the following is/are the effects of devaluation or depreciation of currency?

(2) 1 and 4 only

Q 34 | sci | Physics
The following diagram shows a pendulum at different positions. Which one of the following statement is true?
[Figure: a pendulum hanging from a fixed support, shown at five positions P, Q, R, S, T. R is the lowest point, directly below the support (mean position). Q and S are a little higher on either side, and P and T are the highest positions at the two extremes.]

Q 35 | sci | Physics
In which of the following media is the speed of sound the maximum?
(1) Glass
(2) Stainless steel
(3) Water
(4) Oxygen
= 2
~ Sound travels fastest in stiff solids. In stainless steel it travels at about 5,960 m/s, compared with roughly 5,000–5,600 m/s in glass, about 1,500 m/s in water and about 316 m/s in oxygen.

Q 36 | sci | Physics
A charged particle moves through a magnetic field B with a velocity v. Which one of the following statements is true for the force (F) experienced by the particle?
(1) F is maximum when v and B are parallel to each other.
(2) F is maximum when v and B are anti-parallel to each other.
(3) F is maximum when v and B are perpendicular to each other.
(4) The force F is independent of the angle between v and B.
= 3
~ The magnetic force is F = qvB sin θ. It is greatest (qvB) when θ = 90°, i.e. v ⊥ B, and zero when v is parallel or anti-parallel to B.

Q 37 | sci | Physics

Q 38 | sci | Physics

Q 40 | sci | Chemistry

Q 46 | sci | Biology
Water goes up through xylem vessels in tall trees. The process in plants and properties of water that help in upward movement are:
(1) Respiration and cohesion
(2) Transpiration pull, cohesion and adhesion
(3) Root pressure, cohesion and adhesion
(4) Transpiration and adhesion
= 2
~ By the cohesion–tension theory, transpiration pull from the leaves draws up a continuous column of water. Cohesion between water molecules and adhesion to the xylem walls keep the column intact. Root pressure is too weak to lift water in tall trees.

Q 47 | geo | Environment & Ecology
