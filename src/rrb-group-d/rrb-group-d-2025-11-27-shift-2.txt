Q 1 | sci | Biology
What is the location of the cell wall in an organism?
(1) Just inside the plasma membrane in an animal cell
(2) Just inside the plasma membrane in a plant cell
(3) Just outside the plasma membrane in a plant cell
(4) Just outside the plasma membrane in an animal cell
= 3
~ A cell wall is a rigid outer layer found in plant cells (and in fungi and bacteria); in plants it is made mainly of cellulose and lies just outside the plasma membrane, giving the cell shape and protection. Animal cells have no cell wall at all, so options 1 and 4 are wrong, and in a plant cell the wall is outside, not inside, the membrane.

Q 2 | sci | Physics
If the length of a wire is doubled and its cross-sectional area is halved, its resistance will become:
(1) Half the original
(2) Four times the original
(3) One-fourth the original
(4) Twice the original
= 2
~ Resistance R = ρL/A, so it is directly proportional to length and inversely proportional to area. New resistance = ρ(2L)/(A/2) = 4ρL/A = 4R. Doubling the length alone would only double R, but halving the area doubles it again, making it four times the original.

Q 4 | sci | Physics
The increase in which of the following factor(s) will increase the resistance of a conductor?
(i) Temperature of the conductor
(ii) Length of the conductor
(iii) Area of cross-section of the conductor
(1) Both (i) and (ii)
(2) Only (iii)
(3) Only (i)
(4) Both (ii) and (iii)
= 1
~ For a conductor R = ρL/A, so resistance rises when the length increases and falls when the area of cross-section increases. In metallic conductors the resistivity also rises with temperature because the vibrating ions obstruct the flow of electrons more. So an increase in temperature and in length both increase resistance, while a larger cross-section lowers it.

Q 5 | sci | Chemistry
⁴⁰₁₈Ar and ⁴⁰₂₀Ca are a pair of:
(1) isotones
(2) isobars
(3) isoelectronic
(4) isotopes
= 2
~ Isobars are atoms of different elements that have the same mass number but different atomic numbers. Argon (Z = 18) and calcium (Z = 20) both have mass number 40, so they are isobars. They are not isotopes (different atomic numbers), not isotones (Ar has 40 − 18 = 22 neutrons, Ca has 40 − 20 = 20), and not isoelectronic (18 and 20 electrons).

Q 6 | sci | Physics
A heavy wooden block is pushed across a rough floor. The applied force is gradually increased:
At first, the block does not move.
Then, at a certain force, the block just begins to slide.
Finally, when the block is sliding, it moves with constant velocity.
Which of the following correctly describes the force situation in these three stages?
(1) Stage 1 – Balanced, Stage 2 – Unbalanced, Stage 3 – Balanced
(2) Stage 1 – Unbalanced, Stage 2 – Balanced, Stage 3 – Balanced
(3) Stage 1 – Unbalanced, Stage 2 – Unbalanced, Stage 3 – Balanced
(4) Stage 1 – Balanced, Stage 2 – Balanced, Stage 3 – Unbalanced
= 1
~ In stage 1 the block stays at rest because static friction grows to equal the push, so the forces are balanced. In stage 2 the push exceeds the maximum static friction, so a net force acts and the block starts moving from rest (it accelerates), which means the forces are unbalanced. In stage 3 the block moves with constant velocity, so by Newton's first law the net force is zero and the forces are balanced again.

Which structure is used by pea plant to climb up other plants?

(2) Shoot
(3) Runner
(4) Tendrils
= 4

Q 8 | sci | Physics
According to the New Cartesian Sign Convention, if a light ray travels from the object to the lens, the object is always placed such that:
(1) The object distance (u) is always positive
(2) The object is placed to the left of the lens
(3) The object is always placed above the principal axis
(4) The light falls on the lens from the right-hand side
= 2
~ In the New Cartesian Sign Convention the object is always placed to the left of the lens, so light falls on the lens from the left. Distances are measured from the optical centre; those measured in the direction of the incident light (to the right) are positive and those against it (to the left) are negative. Hence the object distance u is always negative, not positive, which rules out option 1.

Q 10 | sci | Biology
Select the correct feature about sexual reproduction in organisms.

(2) It passes the DNA of only one parent to the offspring.
(3) It produces a large number of offspring.

Q 11 | sci | Chemistry
Concentrated sulphuric acid can be regarded as a dehydrating agent because it removes water from which of the following?
(1) Sodium hydroxide
(2) Ammonia gas
(3) Propane
(4) Glucose
= 4
~ Concentrated sulphuric acid has a strong attraction for water and can even remove the elements of water (hydrogen and oxygen in a 2:1 ratio) from compounds. When it is added to glucose or sugar it pulls out water and leaves behind black carbon: C₆H₁₂O₆ → 6C + 6H₂O, which is why it is called a dehydrating agent. Sodium hydroxide and ammonia are bases that simply react with the acid, and propane contains no oxygen, so no water can be removed from it.

Q 13 | sci | Chemistry
The processes used for removing the gangue from the ore are based on the differences between which properties of the gangue and the ore?
(1) Refrative index

(3) Only physical
(4) Only chemical
= 2

Q 14 | sci | Chemistry
Which of the following correctly matches a measurable property of matter with its SI unit?
(1) Pressure – Newton
(2) Volume – Cubic centimetre
(3) Density – Kilogram per cubic metre
(4) Temperature – Calorie
= 3
~ Density is mass per unit volume, so its SI unit is kilogram per cubic metre (kg/m³). The other pairs are wrong: the SI unit of pressure is the pascal (newton is the unit of force), of volume is the cubic metre (the cubic centimetre is a smaller CGS unit), and of temperature is the kelvin (calorie is a unit of heat energy).

Q 15 | sci | Biology
Which of the following methods is part of the fresh initiatives in irrigation to improve water availability for crops?
(1) Drip irrigation and crop rotation
(2) Manuring and chemical spraying
(3) Rainwater harvesting and watershed management
(4) Sprinkler irrigation and ploughing

Q 16 | sci | Chemistry
The characteristic feature(s) of carbon, that lead to the formation of its large number of organic compounds is:
(1) both tetravalency and catenation
(2) solubility
(3) only tetravalency
(4) only catenation
= 1
~ Carbon forms a huge number of compounds because of two properties together. Catenation is its ability to bond with other carbon atoms to form long chains, branched chains and rings, and tetravalency (valency 4) lets each carbon atom bond with four other atoms such as hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen or halogens. Either property alone would not explain the variety, so "both" is correct.

Q 17 | sci | Biology
The splitting of a unicellular organism into two equal daughter cells containing identical genetic material is termed as which of the following?
(1) Binary fission
(2) Conjugation
(3) Spore formation
(4) Budding
= 1
~ In binary fission a single-celled organism such as Amoeba or a bacterium copies its DNA and divides into two equal daughter cells with identical genetic material. In budding the new individual grows as a small outgrowth and is unequal in size, spore formation produces many spores, and conjugation is an exchange of genetic material between two cells, not splitting.

Q 18 | sci | Physics
An object is dropped from a height of 78.4 m. What is the time taken to reach the ground? (Use g = 9.8 m/s²)
(1) 5 s
(2) 6 s
(3) 2 s
(4) 4 s
= 4
~ The object is dropped, so initial velocity u = 0. Using h = ut + (1/2)gt²: 78.4 = (1/2) × 9.8 × t² = 4.9t², so t² = 78.4 ÷ 4.9 = 16 and t = 4 s.

Q 19 | sci | Physics
A convex lens is also called a converging lens because:
(1) it does not bend light rays at all
(2) it diverges rays of light away from each other
(3) it always forms virtual images
(4) it brings parallel rays to a [point]
= 4
~ A convex lens is thicker in the middle than at the edges, so it bends (refracts) rays parallel to its principal axis inwards and brings them together at a single point called the principal focus; this is why it is called a converging lens. It does bend light, it does not spread rays apart (that is a concave lens), and it forms real images for objects beyond the focus, so it does not always form virtual images.

Q 21 | sci | Chemistry
What is common between the reaction of metal sodium carbonate and sodium bicarbonate with hydrochloric acid?
(1) Hydrogen carbonate is formed
(2) Sodium chloride is formed
(3) Hydrogen gas is released
(4) Carbon monoxide is released
= 2
~ Both are metal carbonates/hydrogencarbonates, and they react with hydrochloric acid to give a salt, water and carbon dioxide: Na₂CO₃ + 2HCl → 2NaCl + H₂O + CO₂ and NaHCO₃ + HCl → NaCl + H₂O + CO₂. So sodium chloride is formed in both. The gas released is carbon dioxide, not hydrogen or carbon monoxide; hydrogen gas is given off when an acid reacts with a reactive metal, not with a carbonate.

Q 22 | sci | Biology
Which one of the following correctly represents Mendel's conclusion about inheritance?
(1) Each trait is controlled by two factors.
(2) Each traits is controlled by one factor.
(3) Each trait is controlled by one chromosome.
(4) Each trait is controlled by three genes.
= 1
~ From his pea plant crosses Mendel concluded that every trait is controlled by a pair of factors (now called genes or alleles), one inherited from each parent. This is why a recessive trait hidden in the F1 generation can reappear in the F2 generation. Mendel did not know about chromosomes, and he described two factors per trait, not one or three.

Q 24 | sci | Physics
Which of the following is NOT an application of ultrasound?
(1) Detecting cracks in metal blocks
(2) Cleaning the objects
(3) Medical imaging
(4) Regular hearing tests
= 4
~ Ultrasound is sound of frequency above 20,000 Hz, which humans cannot hear. It is used to detect hidden cracks and flaws in metal blocks, to clean hard-to-reach parts of objects, and in medical imaging such as ultrasonography and echocardiography. A regular hearing test checks how well a person hears audible sounds (20 Hz to 20,000 Hz), so ultrasound, being inaudible, is not used for it.

Q 25 | sci | Chemistry
Corrosion of metals is a process in which:
(1) metals change their colour permanently
(2) metals are converted to their oxide, sulphide or carbonate by reaction with environmental agents
(3) metals melt at low temperature
(4) metals gain weight due to reaction with acids
= 2
~ Corrosion is the slow eating away of a metal when it reacts with substances around it such as air, moisture, acids or gases, forming compounds like oxides, sulphides or carbonates. Examples are rusting of iron (hydrated iron oxide), black coating on silver (silver sulphide) and green coating on copper (basic copper carbonate). A change of colour is only a visible sign, not the process itself, and corrosion has nothing to do with melting.

Q 28 | qa | Number System & Simplification
Simplify the following:
4 − [3 − 4 ÷ 2 + {4 − (5 − 2)}] + [4 + 18 ÷ 2 × 3(4 − 2)]
(1) 60
(2) 56
(3) 58
(4) 54
= 1

Q 29 | qa | Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns

(2) 10
(3) 21
(4) 23

(3) 3
(4) 2
= 4

Q 42 | qa | Geometry & Mensuration

Q 51 | rea | Ranking & Arrangement
A, B, C, R, S, T and U are sitting around a circular table facing the centre of the table. C sits to the immediate right of R. Only three people sit between C and T when counted from the left of C. Only three people sit between R and B when counted from the left of R. S sits to the immediate right of A. How many people sit between R and A when counted from the right of A?
(1) Two
(2) Four
(3) One
(4) Three
= 3
~ Place R; C is immediately to R's right. Counting leftwards from C, three people are skipped and the fourth seat is T; counting leftwards from R, the fourth seat is B. Of the three seats left, the only pair in which S is immediately to the right of A puts A and S next to R (S between A and R), and U takes the last seat. Going round to the right the order is R, C, U, B, T, A, S. Counting to the right from A, we meet S and then R, so only one person (S) sits between R and A.

Q 52 | rea | Directions & Distance
Mr. Zzz starts from Point A and drives 5 km towards the East. He then takes a right turn, drives 14 km, turns right and drives 16 km. He then takes a right turn and drives 16 km. He takes a final right turn, drives 11 km and stops at Point P. How far (shortest distance) and towards which direction should he drive in order to reach Point A again? (All turns are 90 degrees turns only unless specified).
(1) 4 km to the North
(2) 2 km to the South
(3) 1 km to the West
(4) 3 km to the East
= 2
~ Take A as (0, 0), with East and North positive. 5 km East gives (5, 0); a right turn from East means South, 14 km gives (5, −14); a right turn from South means West, 16 km gives (−11, −14); a right turn from West means North, 16 km gives (−11, 2); a right turn from North means East, 11 km gives P = (0, 2). P is 2 km directly north of A, so he must drive 2 km to the South.

Q 53 | rea | Ranking & Arrangement

(1) Canara Bank
(2) Bank of Baroda
(3) Punjab National Bank
(4) State bank of India
= 1
~ Canara Bank, a public sector bank headquartered in Bengaluru, reported a standalone net profit of ₹5,004 crore for the January–March 2025 quarter (Q4 FY25), up 33% from a year earlier, in results announced on 8 May 2025.
