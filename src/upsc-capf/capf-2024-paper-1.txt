Q 1 | sci | Science & Technology
Which one among the following is NOT a non-procedural computer language?
(1) LISP
(2) Python
(3) Prolog
(4) ML
= 2
~ Non-procedural (declarative) languages describe what result is wanted rather than the step-by-step procedure: LISP and ML are functional languages and Prolog is a logic-programming language. Python is essentially a procedural (imperative) language in which programs are written as ordered statements, loops and functions, so it is the one that is NOT non-procedural.

Q 2 | sci | Science & Technology
Consider the following:
1. MPEG
2. P*64
3. Indeo
Which of the above can be used to compress video files?
(1) 1 and 2 only
(2) 1 and 3 only
(3) 2 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 4
~ All three are video compression schemes: MPEG is the family of audio-video compression standards of the Moving Picture Experts Group, P*64 (p×64) is the ITU-T H.261 video-conferencing codec that works at multiples of 64 kbit/s, and Indeo is Intel's video codec. Hence 1, 2 and 3.

Q 3 | sci | Physics
Which one among the following has largest energy per photon?
(1) X-ray
(2) Ultra-violet ray
(3) Visible-ray
(4) Infra-red ray
= 1
~ The energy of a photon is E = hν = hc/λ, so the radiation with the highest frequency (shortest wavelength) carries the most energy per photon. X-rays have a much shorter wavelength than ultraviolet, visible and infrared radiation, so their photons are the most energetic.

Q 4 | sci | Science & Technology
In Sun-Earth system, the distance between Lagrange points L2 and L3 is about
(1) 15 lakh kilometre
(2) 30 lakh kilometre
(3) 16 crore kilometre
(4) 32 crore kilometre
= 4
~ L2 lies about 15 lakh km beyond the Earth on the side away from the Sun (about 15.1 crore km from the Sun), while L3 lies on the far side of the Sun at roughly the Earth-Sun distance (about 15 crore km). So L2 and L3 are about 15.1 + 15 ≈ 30 crore km apart, and the closest option is 32 crore km; 15 lakh km is only the Earth-L2 distance and 16 crore km is only about one Sun-Earth distance.

Q 5 | sci | Physics
Which one of the following statements with regard to greenhouse effect is correct?
(1) It can take place inside a glass chamber where no radiation can pass into it or pass back through it
(2) It can take place inside a glass chamber where long wavelength infrared radiation can pass through into it and short wavelength infrared radiation cannot pass back through it
(3) It can take place inside a glass chamber where short wavelength infrared radiation can pass through into it and long wavelength infrared radiation cannot pass back through it
(4) It can take place inside a glass chamber where all infrared radiation can pass into it or pass back through it
= 3
~ Glass lets in the Sun's short-wavelength radiation, which warms the surfaces inside; these warm surfaces re-emit long-wavelength (thermal) infrared radiation, which glass does not let back out, so heat is trapped. Greenhouse gases act in the same way in the atmosphere, so (c) is correct and (b) has the wavelengths the wrong way round.

Q 6 | sci | Physics
A cannon shoots a ball upwards with an initial speed of 100 m/s. The total time of flight of the ball is 20 s before it hits the ground. The ball loses 70% of its speed after hitting the ground. Which among the following is the correct height that the ball will bounce up after its first bounce? (g = 10 m/s²)
(1) 100 m
(2) 70 m
(3) 50 m
(4) 45 m
= 4
~ Time of flight T = 2u/g = 2 × 100/10 = 20 s, so the ball comes back to the ground with the same speed of 100 m/s. After losing 70% of its speed it rebounds at 30 m/s, so the bounce height is h = v²/2g = 30²/(2 × 10) = 900/20 = 45 m.

Q 7 | geo | Environment & Ecology
Which one of the following statements correctly defines PM1 particles in air?
(1) These are suspended particles of diameter more than 10 micron
(2) These are the particles that are filtered by the nose during respiration
(3) These are extremely fine particles of diameter less than 1 micron
(4) These are coarse particles that penetrate directly through the lungs into the bloodstream
= 3
~ PM1 is particulate matter with a diameter of less than 1 micron (micrometre); such ultra-fine particles escape the nose's filtering, reach deep into the lungs and can even enter the bloodstream. Particles larger than 10 microns are coarse and are largely trapped in the nose and throat, so (a), (b) and (d) do not describe PM1.

Q 8 | sci | Physics
Which one of the following heat transfer mechanism does NOT require a medium?
(1) Conduction
(2) Convection
(3) Radiation
(4) Collision
= 3
~ Conduction needs particles in contact and convection needs a moving fluid, so both require a material medium. Radiation transfers heat as electromagnetic waves (mainly infrared) that can travel through a vacuum, which is how the Sun's heat reaches the Earth.

Q 9 | geo | Physical Geography
Which one of the following statements about the solstices, an event that occurs when the Sun appears to reach most northerly or southerly, is correct?
(1) The winter solstice takes place on June 21 in both the northern and southern hemispheres
(2) The winter solstice takes place on December 21 in both the northern and southern hemispheres
(3) The summer solstice occurs in northern hemisphere on June 21 and on December 21 in southern hemisphere
(4) The summer solstice occurs in northern hemisphere on December 21 and on June 21 in southern hemisphere
= 3
~ Around June 21 the Sun is overhead at the Tropic of Cancer, giving the northern hemisphere its summer solstice (longest day), and around December 21 it is overhead at the Tropic of Capricorn, giving the southern hemisphere its summer solstice. Because one hemisphere's summer solstice is the other's winter solstice, (a) and (b) are wrong and (d) reverses the dates.

Q 10 | sci | Physics
Which one among the following does NOT have any linkage with the phenomenon of electromagnetic induction?
(1) Electric transformer
(2) Induction cooker
(3) Galvanometer
(4) Electron microscope
= 4
~ A transformer works on mutual induction, an induction cooker heats the pan through eddy currents induced by a rapidly alternating magnetic field, and the galvanometer is the instrument used to detect induced currents (Faraday's experiments) and uses eddy-current damping. An electron microscope only uses electric and magnetic fields as lenses to deflect and focus a beam of electrons, which does not involve electromagnetic induction.

Q 11 | sci | Chemistry
Which one among the following statements about matter is NOT correct?
(1) On increasing the temperature of solids, the kinetic energy of particles increases
(2) The maximum temperature at which a solid melts to become a liquid at atmospheric pressure is called its melting point
(3) Particles of steam have more energy than water at 100°C
(4) Direct change of gas to solid is called deposition
= 2
~ The melting point is the minimum (fixed) temperature at which a solid melts to become a liquid at atmospheric pressure, not the "maximum" temperature, so (b) is the incorrect statement. The others are correct: heating raises the kinetic energy of particles, steam at 100°C holds extra energy equal to the latent heat of vaporisation compared with water at 100°C, and a direct gas-to-solid change is called deposition.

Q 12 | sci | Chemistry
The isotope of which one among the following elements is used in the treatment of cancer?
(1) Uranium
(2) Cobalt
(3) Iodine
(4) Fluorine
= 2
~ Cobalt-60 emits high-energy gamma rays and is the classic radioisotope used in radiotherapy ("cobalt therapy") to destroy cancer cells. Iodine-131 is used mainly for thyroid disorders (including thyroid cancer), fluorine-18 is used in PET scans for diagnosis, and uranium isotopes are nuclear fuels, so the standard answer is cobalt.

Q 13 | sci | Chemistry
Water is a good solvent for dissolving ionic compounds because
(1) It has a high specific heat
(2) It has no colour
(3) It has a high dipole moment
(4) It has a high boiling point
= 3
~ A water molecule is bent and strongly polar (dipole moment about 1.85 D), so its partially negative oxygen end attracts cations and its partially positive hydrogen ends attract anions, pulling ions out of the crystal and surrounding them (hydration). This polarity also gives water a very high dielectric constant that weakens the attraction between ions; specific heat, colour and boiling point do not explain its dissolving power.

Q 14 | sci | Chemistry
Which among the following is correct with respect to bond formation in an ethyne molecule?
(1) Carbon-Carbon single bond
(2) Carbon-Carbon double bond
(3) Carbon-Carbon triple bond
(4) Carbon-Hydrogen double bond
= 3
~ Ethyne (acetylene), C2H2, has the structure H-C≡C-H: the two carbon atoms are joined by a triple bond (one sigma and two pi bonds) and each carbon forms a single bond with a hydrogen atom. A carbon-carbon single bond is found in ethane and a double bond in ethene, and C-H bonds are always single.

Q 15 | sci | Chemistry
Which among the following are the main ingredients for manufacturing of glass?
(1) Silica, sodium carbonate, borax, alumina and cullets
(2) Iron oxide, lead, sodium bicarbonate, alumina and cullets
(3) Magnesium carbonate, alumina, silica and cullets
(4) Iron oxide, sodium, alumina, silica and cullets

The other options leave out the soda flux or list substances like sodium metal, sodium bicarbonate or iron oxide (an impurity that colours glass) that are not main ingredients.

Q 17 | sci | Chemistry
Which one among the following is an example of endothermic process?
(1) Combustion of carbon
(2) Mixing acid and alkali
(3) Photosynthesis
(4) Respiration
= 3
~ Photosynthesis absorbs light energy to build glucose from carbon dioxide and water (about +2800 kJ per mole of glucose), so it is endothermic. Combustion of carbon, neutralisation of an acid by an alkali and respiration all release heat, so they are exothermic.

Q 22 | sci | Biology
In which organism did Gregor J. Mendel conduct the experiment to explain principles of inheritance?
(1) Groundnut
(2) Garden Pea
(3) Four O'clock plant
(4) Fruit fly
= 2
~ Mendel worked out the laws of inheritance from hybridisation experiments on the garden pea (Pisum sativum), which has clear contrasting traits, a short life cycle and self-pollinating flowers. The four o'clock plant was later used by Correns to show incomplete dominance, and the fruit fly by T. H. Morgan to study linkage.

Q 28 | sci | Science & Technology
Which one of the following is the correct chronology of invention/discovery in the field of Biotechnology?
(1) Discovery of Plasmid, Double Helical structure of DNA, DNA fingerprinting, cloning of Dolly
(2) Double Helical structure of DNA, Discovery of Plasmid, DNA fingerprinting, cloning of Dolly

Q 29 | geo | Environment & Ecology
In an ecosystem why is the number of trophic levels of a food chain usually limited?
(1) Lower trophic level has lesser energy than the higher trophic level
(2) Because of the loss of energy at each trophic level
(3) Higher trophic level individual can control the lower ones more efficiently
(4) Limited trophic level makes a food chain less vulnerable to parasites
= 2
~ Only about 10% of the energy at one trophic level passes to the next (Lindeman's ten per cent law); the rest is lost, mostly as heat in respiration, so after four or five levels too little energy is left to support another level. Option (a) is reversed: lower trophic levels hold more energy, not less.

Q 30 | sci | Biology
Which one of the following structure is NOT found in prokaryotes?

(2) Cell membrane
(3) Ribosome
(4) Cell wall
= 1

Q 31 | geo | Physical Geography
Consider the following statements regarding the rotation of the Earth:
1. Since the Earth rotates by 15° each hour, time zones normally differ by one hour
2. At the International Date Line, the calendar day changes - advancing a day for eastward travel, dropping back a day for westward travel
3. Daylight saving time advances the clock by one hour
4. The Moon rotates and revolves about the Earth in the same direction that the Earth revolves around the Sun
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(1) 1 only
(2) 3 and 4 only
(3) 1, 3 and 4
(4) 2, 3 and 4
= 3
~ The Earth turns 360° in 24 hours, i.e. 15° per hour, so adjacent standard time zones normally differ by one hour; daylight saving time puts clocks forward by one hour; and the Moon spins and orbits the Earth anticlockwise (seen from above the North Pole), the same sense in which the Earth orbits the Sun. Statement 2 reverses the rule: crossing the Date Line eastward (Asia to America) you go back a day, and crossing it westward you advance a day.

Q 33 | geo | Physical Geography
Consider the following characteristics of a type of wind:
1. They occur at upper levels in the atmosphere
2. They are subjected to two forces, a pressure gradient force and the Coriolis force
3. When the forces balance, air moves at right angles to the pressure gradient, parallel to the isobars
Identify the type of wind on the basis of the given characteristics:
(1) Jet Stream
(2) Geostrophic Wind
(3) Westerlies
(4) Easterlies
= 2
~ A geostrophic wind blows in the upper atmosphere, above the layer of surface friction, where the pressure gradient force is exactly balanced by the Coriolis force, so the air flows parallel to the isobars instead of across them. Jet streams are particular fast bands of upper-air flow, while westerlies and easterlies are planetary surface wind belts affected by friction.

Q 34 | geo | Physical Geography
Where does podsolization take place?
(1) Regions in high middle latitudes
(2) Coastal regions in lower latitudes
(3) Regions with hot and humid climate
(4) Regions having arid climate
= 1
~ Podzolization occurs in the cool, humid high middle latitudes under coniferous (taiga) forests, where acidic litter and heavy leaching remove iron, aluminium and humus from the upper soil, leaving a bleached ash-grey layer. Q 35 | geo | Physical Geography
Which one of the following statements with reference to the Nebular Hypothesis for the origin of Solar System is correct?
(1) The initiation of the nebular hypothesis can be considered as a diffuse, roughly spherical, slowly rotating expanding nebula
(2) As a result of rotation and expansion, a disk is formed with matter concentrated in the centre
(3) Expansion continues with the formation of the proto-Sun and the rings of material are left behind

Q 36 | geo | Physical Geography

(2) Both have 30 December, 2023
(3) 31 December, 2023 in Alaska and 01 January, 2024 Hawaiian Islands

Q 42 | geo | Physical Geography
Consider the following statements regarding the temperature of the Earth's surface:
1. The temperature of a surface is determined by net radiation
2. Net radiation produces a radiant energy flow that can heat or cool a surface
3. Net radiation balance is usually positive during the daytime and negative during night time
4. Soil surface loses energy as temperature increases during the daytime
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(1) 1 and 2 only
(2) 3 and 4 only
(3) 1, 2 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2, 3 and 4
= 3
~ Net radiation (incoming minus outgoing radiation) governs surface temperature: when it is positive, as in the daytime, the surface gains energy and warms, and when it is negative, as at night, the surface loses energy and cools.
