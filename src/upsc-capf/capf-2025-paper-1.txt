Q 11 | eco | Growth, Planning & National Income
Which among the following regarding the nature of goods and income elasticity are correctly matched?
Nature of Goods    Income Elasticity
1. Necessity : Between 0 and 1
2. Luxury : More than 1
3. Inferior : More than 0

(1) 1 and 3 only

Q 12 | eco | Agriculture & Industry
Which of the following statements about the relationship between price elasticity of demand and type of firm is/are correct?
1. Perfectly elastic demand is associated with a competitive firm.
2. Perfectly inelastic demand is associated with a monopolistically competitive firm.
Select the answer using the code given below.
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 only
(3) Both 1 and 2
(4) Neither 1 nor 2
= 1
~ A firm in perfect competition is a price-taker and faces a horizontal, perfectly elastic demand curve, so statement 1 is correct. A monopolistically competitive firm faces a downward-sloping, fairly elastic demand curve because its product has close substitutes, not a perfectly inelastic one, so statement 2 is wrong.

Q 13 | eco | External Sector & Trade
The comparative advantage theory (CAT) is a trade theory that describes the basis of two nations engaging in trade. Which one among the following statements regarding the theory is not correct?
(1) David Ricardo was the author of the CAT.
(2) The CAT is explained in the book titled On the Principles of Political Economy and Taxation that was first published in 1817.
(3) According to the CAT, the nation which does not have absolute advantage in any goods cannot benefit from trade.
(4) The two nations that engage in trade can gain from specializing in the commodity in which they have a comparative advantage.
= 3
~ David Ricardo set out comparative advantage in On the Principles of Political Economy and Taxation (1817). The whole point of the theory is that even a nation with no absolute advantage in any good gains from trade by specialising in the good in which its relative (opportunity) cost is lowest, so the claim that such a nation cannot benefit from trade is the incorrect statement.

Q 28 | pol | Union & State Government

Q 29 | pol | Constitutional & Statutory Bodies
Consider the following statements about the powers and duties of the Comptroller and Auditor General of India:
1. He audits and reports on all expenditures from the Consolidated Fund of India.
2. He controls the issue and receipt of money from the Consolidated Fund of India.
3. He audits and reports on the expenditure of all bodies substantially financed from the Union or State revenues.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(1) 1 and 2 only
(2) 2 and 3 only
(3) 1 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 3
~ Under Article 149 and the CAG (Duties, Powers and Conditions of Service) Act, 1971, the CAG audits and reports on all expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of India (and of each State), and also on bodies and authorities substantially financed from Union or State revenues. Q 41 | ih | Ancient India

(2) Rakhigarhi

~ Facilitated transport is passive movement down a concentration gradient in which membrane proteins (carriers or channels) help the molecules across without using ATP. It is not active transport, not all passive transport is facilitated (simple diffusion needs no protein), and water crosses membranes passively by osmosis, often through aquaporins.

Q 88 | sci | Biology
Transport of Na⁺ and Ca²⁺ across the plasma membrane is an example of
(1) antiport
(2) symport
(3) uniport
(4) aquaporins
= 1
~ In the Na⁺/Ca²⁺ exchanger the same carrier moves Na⁺ into the cell while it moves Ca²⁺ out, i.e. the two ions travel in opposite directions, which is antiport. Symport moves two substances in the same direction, uniport moves a single substance, and aquaporins are water channels.

Q 89 | sci | Biology

(4) Each subunit of allosteric enzyme also contains a regulatory site in addition to the active site.

= 3

Q 93 | sci | Biology
Which one of the following classes demonstrates double circulation of blood?
(1) Invertebrates
(2) Fishes
(3) Amphibians
(4) Mammals
= 4
~ Mammals (like birds) have a four-chambered heart that keeps oxygenated and deoxygenated blood fully separate, so blood passes through the heart twice in each round, once in the pulmonary and once in the systemic circuit: complete double circulation. Fishes have single circulation, and in amphibians blood mixes in the single ventricle of the three-chambered heart, so their double circulation is only incomplete.

Q 100 | sci | Chemistry

= 3

Q 102 | sci | Chemistry

= 3

Q 106 | sci | Physics
Which one of the following principles of physics is primarily used in sonography, a medical diagnostic process?
(1) Resonance
(2) Superposition of waves
(3) Doppler effect
(4) Standing wave
= 3
~ Sonography sends high-frequency ultrasound into the body and analyses the returning echoes; the change in frequency of echoes from moving targets such as flowing blood or a beating foetal heart (the Doppler effect) is used in Doppler sonography to measure the speed and direction of flow. Q 107 | sci | Physics
Which one among the following laws states that magnetic monopoles do not exist?
(1) Gauss' law
(2) Newton's law
(3) Pascal's law
(4) Ampere's law
= 1
~ Gauss' law for magnetism says the net magnetic flux through any closed surface is zero, because magnetic field lines always form closed loops; hence an isolated north or south pole (a magnetic monopole) cannot exist. Ampere's law relates a magnetic field to the current producing it, while Newton's and Pascal's laws deal with motion and fluid pressure.

Q 108 | sci | Physics
The larger area under the hysteresis curve (B-H) of a ferromagnetic material depicts
(1) maximum magnetization
(2) extent of demagnetization
(3) extent of lagging
(4) minimum energy
= 3
~ Hysteresis means the lagging of the magnetic induction B behind the magnetising field H, and the area enclosed by the B-H loop equals the energy lost as heat per unit volume in each cycle; so a larger loop area shows a greater extent of lagging and greater hysteresis loss (as in hard steel, unlike soft iron with its narrow loop). Maximum magnetization is read from the saturation point of the loop, not from its area. One source reports UPSC's final key as (a).

Q 109 | sci | Physics
The working principle of refrigerator is based on the
(1) first law of thermodynamics
(2) second law of thermodynamics
(3) Planck's radiation law
(4) Rayleigh-Jeans law
= 2
~ A refrigerator uses work done by its compressor to move heat from the cold inside to the warmer room; by the Clausius statement of the second law of thermodynamics, heat cannot flow from a colder to a hotter body on its own without external work. The first law only states energy conservation, while Planck's and Rayleigh-Jeans laws describe black-body radiation.

Q 110 | sci | Physics

= 4
