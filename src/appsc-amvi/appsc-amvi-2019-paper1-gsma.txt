Q 1 | sci | Physics
Who among the following Indian scientists is the first scientist to get Nobel Prize?
= C. V Raman
~ Sir C. V. Raman won the 1930 Nobel Prize in Physics for discovering the Raman effect, the scattering of light announced on 28 February 1928 (now National Science Day). He was the first Indian and the first Asian to win a Nobel Prize in science; Rabindranath Tagore (Literature, 1913) was the first Indian Nobel laureate overall.

Q 6 | men | Reasoning
Complete the series 1, 2, 8, 33, 148, _?_
= 765
~ Each term is the previous term multiplied by n and then increased by n², for n = 1, 2, 3, and so on: 1×1+1 = 2, 2×2+4 = 8, 8×3+9 = 33 and 33×4+16 = 148. The multipliers rise by one at each step and the added numbers are the squares 1, 4, 9, 16, so the next term is 148×5+25 = 740+25 = 765.

Q 7 | men | Reasoning
Complete the series 18, 22, 21, 20, 24, 18, _?_
= 27
~ The series is made of two alternating sequences. The terms in the odd places, 18, 21, 24, rise by 3 each time, while the terms in the even places, 22, 20, 18, fall by 2 each time. The missing number is the seventh term, which is in an odd place, so it is 24 + 3 = 27 (the next even-place term would be 16).

Q 11 | ih | Art & Culture

= Sankhya
~ The six astika (orthodox) darshanas, which accept the authority of the Vedas, are Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Sankhya, Yoga, Mimamsa and Vedanta. Q 17 | geo | World Geography
Which of the following the second layer of atmosphere:
= Stratosphere
~ From the ground upward, the layers of the atmosphere are the troposphere, stratosphere, mesosphere, thermosphere and exosphere. The stratosphere, the second layer, extends from the tropopause (about 8 km at the poles to 18 km at the equator) up to about 50 km. It contains the ozone layer, its temperature rises with height, and its calm air suits jet aircraft.

Q 18 | geo | World Geography
Find the correct statement/s :
1. Igneous rocks have no layers.
2. Limestone is white as well as black
3. Fossils are found in the igneous rocks
Optios:
= 1 and 2 only
~ Igneous rocks form from cooled magma or lava and are massive, not layered (stratified), so statement 1 is correct. Limestone, mainly calcium carbonate, is usually white or grey, but impurities such as organic matter can make it dark grey or black, so statement 2 is accepted. Fossils occur in sedimentary rocks, not in igneous rocks, because the heat of molten rock destroys organic remains; statement 3 is wrong.

Q 33 | sci | Biology & Health

= Malaria
~ Lifestyle diseases are non-communicable diseases linked to unhealthy diet, physical inactivity, smoking, alcohol and stress, such as type 2 diabetes, hypertension, obesity, heart disease and some cancers. Q 120 | eco | Indian Economy
The green revolution in Indian agriculture was not driven by:
= Use of traditional variety of seeds
~ The Green Revolution of the mid-1960s, associated with M. S. Swaminathan and Norman Borlaug, was driven by high-yielding variety (HYV) seeds, mainly dwarf wheat and later rice, together with chemical fertilisers, assured irrigation, pesticides, farm machinery, credit and minimum support prices. Traditional seed varieties could not respond well to heavy fertiliser doses. Replacing them with HYV seeds was the core of the change, so traditional seeds were not a driver of the Green Revolution.
