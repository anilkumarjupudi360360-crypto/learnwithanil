Q 2 | cur | International
Which country has developed the world’s first deep-sea radar capable of detecting high-flying aircraft ?
(1) USA
(2) China
(3) Iraq
(4) Russia
= 2
~ In January 2025 Chinese researchers led by Zhang Bo and Peng Zhaohui of the Chinese Academy of Sciences’ Institute of Acoustics reported, in the journal Acta Acustica, a seabed-based acoustic “radar”, described as the world’s first: a sensor array about 1,000 m deep that picks up aircraft sound reflected between the seabed and the sea surface. In a 2022 trial it tracked a fixed-wing aircraft flying at 5,000 m, locating it within seconds once it came within 17 km, with an error under 1 km.

Q 6 | pol | Constitution & Rights

(1) The scheme was uphold as constitutional
(2) The scheme was struck down as unconstitutional
(3) The scheme was modified to include more transparency measures
(4) The scheme was deferred for further reviews
= 2

Q 17 | sci | Chemistry

(2) Oxygen
(3) Nitrogen
(4) Hydrogen
= 4
~ Dilute acids react with active metals to form a salt and hydrogen gas, for example Zn + H₂SO₄ → ZnSO₄ + H₂; the gas burns with a pop sound when a burning splinter is brought near it. Carbon dioxide is evolved when acids react with metal carbonates or hydrogen carbonates, not with metals.

Q 18 | sci | Physics
What is the cause of dispersion of light through a prism ?
(1) Refraction
(2) Reflection
(3) Interference
(4) Scattering
= 1
~ Dispersion is the splitting of white light into its component colours (VIBGYOR) by a prism. It happens because the refractive index of glass is different for different wavelengths, so each colour is refracted, or bent, through a different angle: violet the most and red the least. The cause is therefore refraction.

Q 19 | sci | Chemistry
What happens when a dry litmus paper is exposed to Anhydrous hydrochloric acid vapor ?
(1) Colour changes from blue to red
(2) Colour changes red to blue
(3) No change
(4) Colour changes red to yellow
= 3
~ Dry hydrogen chloride gas does not change the colour of dry litmus paper, because the hydrogen ions (H⁺) that give acidic properties are formed only when HCl dissolves in water, as H₃O⁺ ions. Only moist blue litmus paper turns red in HCl gas; acids show their acidic behaviour in aqueous solution.

Q 21 | sci | Physics
A spectral line is emitted when an electron
(1) jumps from lower orbit to higher orbit
(2) jumps from higher orbit to lower orbit
(3) rotates in a circular orbit
(4) rotates in an elliptical orbit
= 2
~ In Bohr’s model, an electron moving in a stationary orbit does not radiate energy. When it jumps from a higher energy orbit to a lower one, it emits a photon whose energy equals the energy difference (hν = E₂ − E₁), seen as a line in the emission spectrum. A jump from a lower to a higher orbit needs absorption of energy instead.

(4) Laundry soap

Q 23 | geo | Environment & Ecology
In terms of net primary production, which of the following ecosystems is the most productive ?
(1) Deserts
(2) Tropical rain forests
(3) Oceans
(4) Estuaries
= 2
~ Tropical rain forests have one of the highest net primary productivities per unit area, about 2,200 g/m² per year in Whittaker’s classic estimates, and the largest total on land. Estuaries are also productive (about 1,500 g/m² per year) but lower on average, while the open ocean (about 125) and deserts (below 100) are among the least productive ecosystems.

Q 24 | geo | Environment & Ecology
Electrostatic precipitator is used to remove
(1) Poisonous gases present in the smoke released from the industry
(2) Carbon dioxide present in the smoke released from the automobiles
(3) Particulate matter present in the smoke released from the industry
(4) Oxides of nitrogen present in the smoke released from the automobiles
= 3
~ An electrostatic precipitator removes particulate matter such as fly ash and dust from industrial exhaust, for example at thermal power plants. Electrode wires at high voltage give the particles a negative charge and they are collected on earthed plates; it can remove over 99% of particulates. Gases such as sulphur dioxide are removed by scrubbers instead, and vehicle exhaust gases by catalytic converters.

Q 25 | sci | Science & Technology
Golden rice is a genetically modified plant where the incorporated gene is meant for the biosynthesis of
(1) Vitamin A
(2) Vitamin B
(3) Vitamin C
(4) Omega 3
= 1
~ Golden rice carries added genes, phytoene synthase from daffodil (later maize) and a carotene desaturase from a soil bacterium, that make the grain produce beta-carotene, the precursor of vitamin A, in its endosperm, giving it a golden-yellow colour. It was developed by Ingo Potrykus and Peter Beyer to fight vitamin A deficiency, a major cause of childhood blindness.

Q 27 | sci | Science & Technology
The standard length of the MAC address is
(1) 8 bits
(2) 16 bits
(3) 32 bits
(4) 48 bits
= 4
~ A MAC (Media Access Control) address, the hardware address burnt into a network interface card, is 48 bits (6 bytes) long, usually written as 12 hexadecimal digits such as 00:1A:2B:3C:4D:5E. The first 24 bits identify the manufacturer (OUI). For comparison, an IPv4 address is 32 bits and an IPv6 address 128 bits.

Q 29 | sci | Physics
Hydraulic Brakes work on which principle ?
(1) Archimedes principle
(2) Pascal's principle
(3) Newton's laws
(4) Casini's laws
= 2
~ By Pascal's principle, pressure applied to a confined liquid is transmitted equally and undiminished in all directions. In hydraulic brakes the pedal pushes the master-cylinder piston, and the brake-fluid pressure is carried to the larger wheel-cylinder pistons, which press the shoes or pads with a multiplied force. Archimedes' principle concerns buoyancy; "Casini's laws" (Cassini's laws describe the Moon's rotation) have nothing to do with brakes.

Q 32 | ih | Andhra History
The official language of the Satavahanas was
(1) Sanskrit
(2) Prakrit
(3) Pali
(4) Aramic
= 2
~ The Satavahanas used Prakrit as their official language: their inscriptions, such as those at Nashik and Naneghat, and the legends on their coins are in Prakrit written in the Brahmi script. King Hala's Gatha Saptashati is a Prakrit anthology. Sanskrit became the main court language only under later dynasties, and Aramaic (printed "Aramic") was used in north-western India, not in the Deccan.

Q 33 | ih | Art & Culture

(1) Simhavarman
(2) Pulakesin II
(3) Narasimhavarman
(4) Mayuravarman
= 3

Q 34 | ih | Ancient India
The philosophy that Charvakas propounded is known as______.
(1) Mimamsa
(2) Lokayata
(3) Panchayatana
(4) Upasaka
= 2
~ The Charvaka school, traditionally traced to Brihaspati, is called Lokayata, "the view of the common people". It is a materialist philosophy that accepts only direct perception as a source of knowledge and rejects the Vedas, rebirth and the soul. Mimamsa is an orthodox Vedic school founded by Jaimini, Panchayatana is the worship of five deities, and an upasaka is a lay follower.

Q 46 | geo | Indian Geography
The boundary line between China and India is called as

(2) The Redcliff Line
(3) The Gulf of Mannar
(4) The Indira Point
= 1

Q 48 | geo | AP Geography

Q 50 | ih | Ancient India
