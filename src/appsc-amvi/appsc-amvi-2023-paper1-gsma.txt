Q 14 | sci | Biology & Health
A rare form of epilepsy, known as PSE, is triggered by visual stimuli that form patterns in time or space, such as flashing lights, bold, regular patterns or regular moving patterns. What is the full form of PSE?
(1) Photosensitive Epileptic Seizures
(2) Photosensitive Endocrinal Seizures
(3) Photosensitive Epileptic Sensitivity
(4) Photosensitive Endocrinal Sensitivity
= 1
~ PSE stands for photosensitive epileptic seizures, a form of reflex epilepsy in which seizures are set off by flashing or flickering lights or bold, regular patterns. Q 15 | sci | Biology & Health
Fasciola hepatica is the scientific name of a flat worm that invades the ______ of the host animal.
(1) brain
(2) kidney
(3) heart
(4) liver
= 4
~ Fasciola hepatica, the common liver fluke, is a parasitic flatworm (phylum Platyhelminthes, class Trematoda) that lives in the liver and bile ducts of sheep, cattle and sometimes humans, causing fascioliasis ('liver rot'). Its larvae develop in freshwater snails, and the final host is infected by eating water plants or grass carrying the encysted larvae.

Q 16 | sci | Biology & Health
Which of the following is an essential amino acid for the human body?
(1) Isoleucine
(2) Alanine
(3) Aspartic acid
(4) Cysteine
= 1
~ Essential amino acids cannot be made by the human body and must come from food. There are nine: histidine, isoleucine, leucine, lysine, methionine, phenylalanine, threonine, tryptophan and valine. Alanine and aspartic acid are non-essential, and cysteine is synthesised from methionine (conditionally essential), so isoleucine is the answer.

Q 20 | geo | Disaster Management
Which seismic zone does New Delhi belong to?
(1) Seismic Zone III
(2) Seismic Zone II
(3) Seismic Zone IV
(4) Seismic Zone I
= 3
~ On the seismic zoning map of IS 1893 (Part 1), New Delhi lies in Zone IV, the high-damage-risk zone, because of its nearness to the Himalayan collision belt and local faults. A 2025 revision of the code that added a new Zone VI was withdrawn by BIS through a gazette notification of 3 March 2026, so the 2016 code with Zones II–V applies again.

Q 21 | geo | Disaster Management
The occurrence of the Killari earthquake in 1993 resulted in further revision of the seismic zoning map, in which the low hazard zone or Seismic Zone I was merged with Seismic Zone ______.
(1) II
(2) III
(3) IV
(4) V
= 1
~ The 1993 Killari (Latur) earthquake struck an area then shown as low-hazard Zone I, exposing flaws in the old five-zone map. In the revised map of IS 1893 (Part 1): 2002, Zone I was merged with Zone II, leaving four zones (II, III, IV and V), of which Zone V is the most severe.

Q 22 | geo | Environment & Ecology

(1) 1971
(2) 1981

Q 29 | geo | Indian Geography
Which of the following is NOT a tributary of Indus river?

(2) Chenab
(3) Ravi

= 4

Q 30 | geo | Indian Geography
Where is the Luni river located?
(1) Indian desert
(2) Sahara desert
(3) Thar desert
(4) Cholistan desert
= 1,3
~ The Luni rises in the Aravallis near Ajmer and flows south-west across the Thar Desert of western Rajasthan, ending in the Rann of Kutch; it is the largest river of this arid region and becomes saline downstream. The Thar is also called the Great Indian Desert, so options (1) and (3) name the same desert, and APPSC's final key gives marks for either. Cholistan is the Thar's extension in Pakistan; the Sahara is in Africa.

Q 31 | geo | Indian Geography
The Lakshadweep islands cover an area of ______.
(1) 32 km²
(2) 42 km²
(3) 52 km²
(4) 62 km²
= 1
~ Lakshadweep, India's smallest Union Territory, is a group of about 36 coral islands, atolls and reefs in the Arabian Sea off the Kerala coast, with a total land area of only about 32 km² (32.62 km²). Kavaratti is its capital, and Minicoy in the south is separated from the main group by the Nine Degree Channel. Most of the islands are low-lying atolls.

Q 32 | geo | Environment & Ecology
Where is the Pitti Bird Sanctuary located?
(1) Rameswaram
(2) Madurai
(3) Lakshadweep
(4) Goa
= 3
~ Pitti (Pakshi Pitti, "bird island") is a tiny, uninhabited coral islet in Lakshadweep that has been declared a bird sanctuary. It is an important breeding ground for pelagic seabirds, especially the sooty tern, brown noddy and greater crested tern. It is the only bird sanctuary in Lakshadweep; Rameswaram, Madurai and Goa are on the mainland.

Q 80 | sci | Science & Technology
Which of the following can be used to capture the heat radiated by volcanic eruptions?

(2) Visible ray sensors
(3) Infrared sensitive sensors
(4) Ultraviolet ray imagery
= 3

Q 81 | sci | Science & Technology

(1) Visible spectrum camera
(2) Infrared camera
(3) X-ray camera
(4) Laser
= 4
~ A passive sensor records natural energy: sunlight reflected by the Earth (visible cameras) or heat emitted by it (thermal infrared cameras).
