Q 1 | hin | Grammar
निम्नलिखित वाक्यों में से अशुद्ध वाक्य का चयन कीजिए :
(1) उसने हाथी पर हौदा रख दिया।
(2) दासता-युक्त जीवन ठीक नहीं।
(3) चार आदमी के बैठने की व्यवस्था करो।

Q 2 | hin | Grammar
‘अध्यात्म’ शब्द का सही संधि-विच्छेद है :
(1) अध + आत्म
(2) अधि + आत्म
(3) अध्या + तम
(4) अधा + आत्म
(5) उपर्युक्त में से कोई नहीं
= 2
~ 'Adhyatma' = 'adhi' + 'aatma'. This is Yan Sandhi: when 'i' (इ) is followed by a different vowel 'aa' (आ), 'i' changes to 'y' (य्), so इ + आ = या.

Q 11 | env | Environment & Ecology
Who coined the term ‘keystone species’ ?
(1) Robert T. Paine
(2) Michael E. Soulé
(3) James A. Estes
(4) John H. Lawton
(5) None of the above
= 1
~ American ecologist Robert T. Paine coined the term ‘keystone species’ in 1969 after his experiments removing the predatory starfish Pisaster ochraceus from rocky intertidal shores, which caused the community’s diversity to collapse.

Q 12 | sci | Technology & Space
Which of the following statements about blockchain technology is most accurate ?
(1) It requires one central server to verify all data.
(2) It is a distributed ledger where records are difficult to alter retroactively.
(3) It is used only for Bitcoin and has no other applications.
(4) It stores data only in analog form.
(5) None of the above
= 2
~ A blockchain is a decentralised, distributed digital ledger: copies are kept on many nodes and each block is linked to the previous one by a cryptographic hash, so past records cannot easily be altered. It needs no central server, stores digital data, and is used well beyond Bitcoin (supply chains, land records, etc.).

Q 32 | env | Environment & Ecology
Which principle states that two closely related species competing for the same resources cannot coexist indefinitely ?
(1) Liebig’s Law of the Minimum
(2) Gause’s Rule
(3) Hardy-Weinberg Principle
(4) Allen’s Rule
(5) None of the above
= 2
~ Gause’s Rule, or the competitive exclusion principle (G.F. Gause, 1934, from experiments with Paramecium), says two species competing for the same limiting resources cannot coexist indefinitely – the weaker competitor is eliminated. Liebig’s law concerns limiting nutrients; Allen’s rule concerns shorter limbs in cold climates.

Q 41 | env | Disaster Management
Which of the following correctly represents the relationship used in disaster risk assessment according to standard frameworks of NDMA/UNDRR ?
(1) Risk = (Hazard × Vulnerability × Coping Capacity) ÷ Exposure
(2) Risk = (Hazard × Vulnerability × Exposure) ÷ Coping Capacity
