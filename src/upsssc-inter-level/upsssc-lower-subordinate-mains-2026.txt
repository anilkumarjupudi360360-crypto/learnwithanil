Q 39 | sci | Physics & Chemistry
Which effect causes the melting temperature of nanoparticles to decrease as their size decreases ?
(1) Casimir effect
(2) Gibbs-Thomson effect
(3) Seebeck effect
(4) Hall-Petch effect
(5) None of the above
= 2
~ By the Gibbs-Thomson effect, smaller particles have a larger surface-to-volume ratio and surface energy (higher curvature), so less heat is needed to melt them—their melting point falls as size decreases. Casimir is a force between close plates, Seebeck produces a thermoelectric voltage, and Hall-Petch relates metal strength to grain size.
