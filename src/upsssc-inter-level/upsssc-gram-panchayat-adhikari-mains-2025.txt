Q 1 | rur | Rural Development & Schemes
Which scheme aims to provide employment to migrant labourers returning to villages in Uttar Pradesh ?
(1) Deen Dayal Antyodaya Yojana
(2) Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)
(3) Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan
(4) Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan
(5) None of the above
= 4
~ The Garib Kalyan Rojgar Abhiyaan was launched on 20 June 2020 to give 125 days of work to migrant workers who had returned to their villages during the COVID-19 lockdown. Q 3 | rur | Panchayati Raj
The provisions for the Panchayats are included under which Part of the Constitution of India ?
(1) Part VI
(2) Part IX
(3) Part VIII
(4) Part XII
(5) None of the above
= 2
~ The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 added Part IX, 'The Panchayats' (Articles 243 to 243-O), and the Eleventh Schedule. Part VI deals with the States, Part VIII with Union Territories and Part XII with finance.

Q 4 | rur | Panchayati Raj
What was one of the major shortcomings identified in the British Era Panchayat System that led to reforms in the Post-Independence Panchayati Raj System ?
(1) Over-centralization of power with local elites
(2) Weak legal framework and non-representation of people
(3) Ineffective agricultural policies
(4) High level of corruption
(5) None of the above
= 2
~ Local bodies of the British period (Mayo's 1870 and Ripon's 1882 resolutions, 1919 Act) rested on weak laws, had little power and money, and were dominated by nominated officials with a narrow franchise, so ordinary people were not represented. Independent India answered this with Article 40, the three-tier Panchayati Raj and finally constitutional status in 1992.

Q 6 | rur | Panchayati Raj
Which Article of the Indian Constitution mandates the establishment of Panchayats at the village, intermediate and district levels ?
(1) Article 243A
(2) Article 243B
(3) Article 280
(4) Article 40
(5) None of the above
= 2
~ Article 243B says Panchayats shall be constituted in every State at the village, intermediate and district levels (the intermediate level may be skipped where the State's population is below 20 lakh). Article 243A is on the Gram Sabha, Article 40 is the Directive Principle on village panchayats and Article 280 is on the Finance Commission.

Q 24 | rur | Panchayati Raj
Under Article 243O of the Constitution, which of the following statements is correct regarding the delimitation of constituencies for Panchayat elections ?
(1) The validity of any law related to delimitation of constituencies made under Article 243K cannot be questioned in court.
(2) The Election Commission of India is responsible for delimitation in all states.
(3) The Parliament of India has exclusive authority over Panchayat elections.
(4) The Supreme Court can review delimitation disputes.
(5) None of the above
= 1
~ Article 243O bars courts from interfering in Panchayat electoral matters: the validity of a law on delimitation of constituencies or allotment of seats made under Article 243K cannot be questioned in any court, and a Panchayat election can be challenged only by an election petition. Panchayat elections are run by the State Election Commission, not the ECI.

Q 31 | rur | Panchayati Raj
What was the primary recommendation of the P.K. Thungon Committee (1989) regarding local government institutions ?
(1) Establishing a centralized governance model for rural areas
(2) Providing constitutional recognition and ensuring periodic elections
(3) Shifting all local governance responsibilities to State governments
(4) Abolishing Panchayati Raj institutions due to inefficiency
(5) None of the above
= 2
~ The P.K. Thungon Committee (a parliamentary consultative sub-committee, 1988-89) recommended that Panchayati Raj bodies be given constitutional recognition, with a three-tier structure, a fixed five-year term and regular, timely elections, and the Zila Parishad as the planning and development agency of the district. This paved the way for the 64th Amendment Bill (1989) and later the 73rd Amendment.

(3) 1, 2 and 3
