# NCERT Class 11 Fine Arts (Art & Culture), An Introduction to Indian Art Part I, Chapter 6: Temple Architecture and Sculpture
# Questions written by Learn with Anil from the chapter (current NCERT edition, Reprint 2026-27). 16 Nov 2026.

Q 1 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
In a Hindu temple, the garbhagriha is:
(1) The pillared hall where worshippers gather
(2) The gateway in the compound wall
(3) The 'womb-house', a small cubicle housing the main icon
(4) The platform for the deity's vehicle
= 3
~ The garbhagriha, literally 'womb-house', began as a small cubicle with a single entrance for the main icon. The mandapa is the entrance portico or hall for worshippers.

Q 2 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
In South India, the mountain-like pyramidal tower over the sanctum of a free-standing temple is called the:
(1) Shikhara
(2) Gopuram
(3) Amalaka
(4) Vimana
= 4
~ The tower is called the shikhara in North India and the vimana in South India. In the south, 'shikhara' means only the crowning element at the top of the vimana.

Q 3 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
The Dashavatara temple at Deogarh is in which district?
(1) Lalitpur, Uttar Pradesh
(2) Vidisha, Madhya Pradesh
(3) Jhansi, Uttar Pradesh
(4) Sagar, Madhya Pradesh
= 1
~ The Dashavatara temple at Deogarh, in Lalitpur district of Uttar Pradesh, is built in the panchayatana style, with a main shrine and four small subsidiary shrines at the corners, and faces west.

Q 4 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
The Dashavatara temple at Deogarh is built in the panchayatana style. What does this mean?
(1) The temple has five gopurams in its compound wall
(2) The main shrine stands on a rectangular plinth with four smaller subsidiary shrines at the four corners
(3) Five shrines stand in a single row
(4) The temple tower has five storeys
= 2
~ In the panchayatana plan the main shrine is surrounded by four small shrines at the corners, five in all. Deogarh's tall curvilinear shikhara and its Vishnu reliefs (Sheshashayana, Nara-Narayana, Gajendramoksha) make it an early classic Nagara temple.

Q 5 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
The huge relief at Mahabalipuram, nearly thirty metres long, is interpreted either as the Descent of the Ganga or as:
(1) Gajendramoksha
(2) Arjuna's Penance
(3) Krishna lifting Govardhana
(4) The marriage of Shiva and Parvati
= 2
~ The second reading, Arjuna's Penance, links the scene to Bharavi's Sanskrit poem Kiratarjuniya, in which Arjuna does penance to obtain a weapon from Shiva.

Q 6 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
The Shore temple at Mahabalipuram was probably built in the reign of:
(1) Mahendravarman I
(2) Narasimhavarman I (Mamalla)
(3) Pulakesin II
(4) Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha)
= 4
~ The Shore temple was probably built under Narasimhavarman II, also called Rajasimha, who reigned from 700 to 728 CE. It has two shrines to Shiva and one to Vishnu as Anantashayana.

Q 7 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
Which sub-type of the Nagara temple is a rectangular building with a roof rising into a vaulted (wagon-vaulted) chamber?
(1) Latina
(2) Phamsana
(3) Valabhi
(4) Rekha-prasada
= 3
~ Valabhi temples are rectangular with wagon-vaulted roofs, a form derived from older buildings such as Buddhist chaitya halls. Latina (rekha-prasada) has a curving spire on a square base; phamsana is broader and shorter, with slab roofs.

Q 8 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
In the temples of Odisha, the shikhara is called ______ and the mandapa is called ______.
(1) deul; jagamohana
(2) vimana; gopuram
(3) jagamohana; deul
(4) amalaka; kalasha
= 1
~ In Odisha the shikhara is the deul and the mandapa is the jagamohana. The main temple sites are Bhubaneswar, Puri and Konark in ancient Kalinga.

Q 9 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
Consider the following statements about the Sun temple at Konark:
1. It was built in stone around 1240.
2. Twelve pairs of enormous sculpted wheels show it as the Sun god's chariot.
3. The Sun god's chariot is drawn by seven horses.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(1) 1, 2 and 3
(2) 1 and 2 only
(3) 2 only
(4) 2 and 3 only
= 1
~ All three are given in the chapter. Its shikhara, said to have reached 70 metres, fell in the nineteenth century, and its jagamohana is said to be the largest enclosed space in Hindu architecture.

Q 10 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
The temples of Khajuraho, built of sandstone, were patronised by which dynasty?
(1) The Paramaras
(2) The Pratiharas
(3) The Chandelas
(4) The Kalachuris
= 3
~ The Khajuraho temples were built by the Chandela kings. Their sculptures are in almost full relief, with sharp noses, prominent chins and long slanting eyes.

Q 11 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
The Lakshmana temple at Khajuraho is dedicated to:
(1) Shiva
(2) Surya
(3) Devi
(4) Vishnu
= 4
~ The Lakshmana temple is dedicated to Vishnu and has an image of Chaturmukha Vishnu in its garbhagriha. The Kandariya Mahadeva temple is called the epitome of Central Indian temple architecture.

Q 12 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
The Sun temple at Modhera in Gujarat was built in 1026 by:
(1) Siddharaja Jayasimha
(2) Raja Bhimdev I of the Solanki dynasty
(3) Kumarapala
(4) Vimal Shah
= 2
~ Raja Bhimdev I of the Solanki dynasty built it. In front of it is the Surya Kund, a rectangular stepped tank with 108 miniature shrines between the steps; the temple faces east so that the sun shines into the shrine at the equinoxes.

Q 13 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
The Kailashnath temple at Ellora (Cave 16), a complete Dravida building carved out of the living rock, is the greatest achievement of which dynasty?
(1) The early Chalukyas
(2) The Pallavas
(3) The Vakatakas
(4) The Rashtrakutas
= 4
~ The Rashtrakutas, who took control of the Deccan by about 750 CE, made the Kailashnath temple; its vimana rises about thirty metres and its sculpture is dynamic, often larger than life.

Q 14 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
According to Xuanzang's records, the monastery at Nalanda was founded by:
(1) Kumaragupta I
(2) Harshavardhana
(3) Dharmapala
(4) Ashoka
= 1
~ Xuanzang's account says Kumaragupta I founded the monastery in the fifth century CE; later kings expanded it into a great university where Theravada, Mahayana and Vajrayana teachings were all taught.

Q 15 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
Which of the following is NOT a feature of Dravida temples as described in the chapter?
(1) A compound wall with a central gateway called the gopuram
(2) Several clustered shikharas rising together, very popular in the south
(3) Fierce dvarapalas guarding the entrance
(4) A large temple tank within the complex
= 2
~ Multiple clustered shikharas were not popular in South India. Dravida temples have a compound wall with a gopuram, dvarapalas at the entrance and often a temple tank.

Q 16 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
The Srirangam temple at Tiruchirapalli has how many concentric rectangular enclosure walls?
(1) Three
(2) Five
(3) Seven
(4) Nine
= 3
~ Srirangam has seven concentric enclosure walls, each with gopurams; the innermost is the oldest and the outermost the newest.

Q 17 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
Consider the following statements about the Rajarajeswara (Brihadeeshwarar) temple at Thanjavur:
1. It was built by Rajaraja Chola and completed around 1009.
2. Its multi-storeyed vimana rises to about 70 metres.
3. It is the first temple with two large gopuras planned together with the temple and its sculpture.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(1) 1 only
(2) 1 and 2 only
(3) 1, 2 and 3
(4) 2 and 3 only
= 3
~ All three are in the chapter. The vimana is topped by a monolithic octagonal stupika, and the main deity is Shiva as a huge lingam in a two-storeyed sanctum.

Q 18 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
The Virupaksha temple at Pattadakal was built by:
(1) Vikramaditya II himself
(2) Pulakesin II
(3) A Rashtrakuta king
(4) Lokamahadevi, the chief queen of Vikramaditya II
= 4
~ It was built by Lokamahadevi, chief queen of the Chalukya king Vikramaditya II, during his reign (733–44). The Papanatha temple at Pattadakal is another Chalukyan temple, dedicated to Shiva.

Q 19 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
The temples of the Hoysalas, such as those at Belur, Halebid and Somnathpuram, have which kind of plan?
(1) Circular
(2) Stellate (star-shaped)
(3) Apsidal
(4) Octagonal
= 2
~ Hoysala temples have a stellate plan with many projecting angles. They are made of soft soapstone, which allowed intricate carving; the Halebid frieze has hundreds of elephants, no two alike.

Q 20 | art | An Introduction to Indian Art, Part I
The statue of Gommateshwara (Bahubali) at Shravanabelagola was commissioned by:
(1) Chamundaraya, general and prime minister of the Ganga kings
(2) Kharavela of Kalinga
(3) Vimal Shah
(4) Amoghavarsha I
= 1
~ Chamundaraya commissioned the granite statue of Bahubali, about 18 metres (57 feet) high, described as the tallest monolithic free-standing structure in the world. Vimal Shah built the Jain temples at Mount Abu.
