# NCERT Class 11 Political Science, Indian Constitution at Work, Chapter 8: Local Governments
# Questions written by Learn with Anil from the chapter (current NCERT edition, Reprint 2026-27). 16 Nov 2026.

Q 1 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
Why does the chapter consider local government important for democracy?
(1) Because it removes the need for state governments
(2) Because it controls the armed forces
(3) Because it makes laws for the whole country
(4) Because it is government closest to the people, dealing with their everyday problems, and lets them take part directly in decisions
= 4
~ Local government is government at the village and town level. It solves everyday problems quickly and lets ordinary people take part, which deepens democracy.

Q 2 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
Elected local self-government bodies were first created in British India in 1882 under:
(1) Lord Curzon
(2) Lord Dalhousie
(3) Lord Ripon
(4) Lord Mountbatten
= 3
~ Lord Ripon's resolution of 1882 introduced elected local bodies, called local boards. Their powers were limited and only a few people could vote.

Q 3 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
At the time of making the Constitution, local government was placed in:
(1) The Fundamental Rights
(2) The Union List
(3) The Fundamental Duties
(4) The Directive Principles of State Policy, as a subject for the states
= 4
~ Article 40 directed the state to organise village panchayats, and local government was a State List subject. It got a full constitutional status only with the 73rd and 74th Amendments.

Q 4 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
Which committee, set up in 1957, recommended a three-tier system of Panchayati Raj?
(1) Balwant Rai Mehta Committee
(2) Ashok Mehta Committee
(3) L.M. Singhvi Committee
(4) Sarkaria Commission
= 1
~ The Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957) recommended elected bodies at the village, block and district levels. Many states then set up Panchayati Raj institutions.

Q 5 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
The 73rd and 74th Amendments, which gave constitutional status to local governments, were passed in:
(1) 1985
(2) 1989
(3) 1992
(4) 1996
= 3
~ Both were passed in 1992 and came into force in 1993. The 73rd deals with rural local bodies (panchayats) and the 74th with urban local bodies (municipalities).

Q 6 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
Under the 73rd Amendment, the three tiers of Panchayati Raj are at the level of:
(1) Village, block (intermediate) and district
(2) Village, state and union
(3) Ward, city and metropolitan area
(4) Village, tehsil and state
= 1
~ The Gram Panchayat works at the village level, the Mandal or Panchayat Samiti at the block level, and the Zilla Parishad at the district level.

Q 7 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
Which states are allowed not to have the intermediate (block) level of panchayats?
(1) States with a population below 20 lakh
(2) States with a coastline
(3) States with a High Court
(4) States in the Sixth Schedule only
= 1
~ The 73rd Amendment lets very small states, with a population below 20 lakh, skip the intermediate tier, since a two-tier system is enough there.

Q 8 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
Consider the following statements about the 73rd Amendment:
1. Elections to panchayats must be held every five years.
2. If a panchayat is dissolved early, elections must be held within six months.
3. One-third of the seats and of the posts of chairperson are reserved for women.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(1) 1 and 2 only
(2) 2 and 3 only
(3) 1 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 4
~ All three are correct. These provisions ended the practice of states postponing panchayat elections for years, and brought lakhs of women into public life.

Q 9 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
Under the 73rd Amendment, reservation for the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in panchayats is:
(1) Fixed at one-third in all states
(2) Left entirely to the panchayat
(3) In proportion to their share in the population
(4) Not provided
= 3
~ Seats for SCs and STs are reserved in proportion to their population. Reservation for Other Backward Classes is left to the states to decide.

Q 10 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
Which body conducts elections to the panchayats and municipalities?
(1) The Election Commission of India
(2) The District Collector
(3) The Governor
(4) The State Election Commission
= 4
~ Each state has a State Election Commissioner, independent of the state government, to conduct local body elections. The Election Commission of India handles elections to Parliament and state legislatures.

Q 11 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
The State Finance Commission, set up under the 73rd and 74th Amendments, is meant to:
(1) Review the finances of local bodies and recommend how revenue should be shared between the state and local governments
(2) Prepare the state budget
(3) Audit the accounts of the Centre
(4) Fix the salaries of state ministers
= 1
~ Every five years the state government must set up a State Finance Commission to examine the financial position of local bodies and recommend how funds should be shared with them.

Q 12 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
How many subjects are listed in the Eleventh Schedule for the panchayats?
(1) 18
(2) 22
(3) 29
(4) 35
= 3
~ The Eleventh Schedule lists 29 subjects, such as agriculture, minor irrigation, drinking water and primary education. The state legislature decides how many of them actually go to the panchayats.

Q 13 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
How many subjects are listed in the Twelfth Schedule for urban local bodies?
(1) 12
(2) 18
(3) 29
(4) 30
= 2
~ The Twelfth Schedule, added by the 74th Amendment, lists 18 subjects for municipalities, such as urban planning, water supply and public health.

Q 14 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
Which of the following are urban local bodies under the 74th Amendment?
(1) Gram Panchayat, Panchayat Samiti and Zilla Parishad
(2) Gram Sabha and Mandal Parishad
(3) Nagar Panchayat, Municipal Council and Municipal Corporation
(4) District Board and Taluk Board
= 3
~ The 74th Amendment provides for Nagar Panchayats for areas changing from rural to urban, Municipal Councils for smaller towns and Municipal Corporations for large cities.

Q 15 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
The Gram Sabha consists of:
(1) Only the elected members of the Gram Panchayat
(2) Government officers of the block
(3) Members of the state legislature from the area
(4) All adult voters registered in the electoral roll of the village panchayat area
= 4
~ The Gram Sabha is the assembly of all voters of the village. It is meant to discuss the panchayat's work and approve plans, making the panchayat accountable to the people.

Q 16 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
The provisions of the 73rd Amendment were extended to the Scheduled (tribal) Areas of several states in 1996 by a law known as:
(1) PESA, the Provisions of the Panchayats (Extension to the Scheduled Areas) Act
(2) The Forest Rights Act
(3) The Tribal Autonomy Act
(4) The Gram Nyayalaya Act
= 1
~ Many tribal areas were at first kept out of the 73rd Amendment. PESA (1996) extended panchayats to Fifth Schedule areas while respecting traditional customs of managing community resources.

Q 17 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
The chapter notes a major weakness in the working of local governments even after the amendments. Which one?
(1) They have too many funds and too few responsibilities
(2) They depend heavily on state and central grants and have few powers and resources of their own
(3) Their elections are held by the Supreme Court
(4) They have no elected members
= 2
~ Local bodies raise very little revenue themselves and rely on grants. Many states have also not handed over the 29 subjects and the staff to go with them, so local bodies often remain implementers of schemes.

Q 18 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
Which of the following was a direct result of the reservation of seats for women in local bodies?
(1) The abolition of the Gram Sabha
(2) A large number of women, including many from SC, ST and OBC groups, entering elected local offices
(3) The end of elections in villages
(4) A ban on men contesting
= 2
~ Reserving one-third of seats and chairperson posts for women brought lakhs of women into local politics. Many states have since raised women's reservation in local bodies to half.

Q 19 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
Which programme of the 1950s, involving people in local development, preceded the creation of Panchayati Raj institutions?
(1) Green Revolution
(2) Community Development Programme
(3) Integrated Rural Development Programme
(4) National Rural Employment Guarantee
= 2
~ The Community Development Programme (1952) sought people's participation in rural development. Its weaknesses led to the Balwant Rai Mehta Committee and elected Panchayati Raj bodies.

Q 20 | pol | Indian Constitution at Work
Which Indian leader saw village panchayats as the basis of a truly decentralised democracy, often described as "gram swaraj"?
(1) Mahatma Gandhi
(2) Dr. B.R. Ambedkar
(3) Sardar Patel
(4) Subhas Chandra Bose
= 1
~ Gandhi wanted power decentralised to self-governing villages. Ambedkar, by contrast, distrusted village politics as a site of caste domination, so the Constitution at first put panchayats only in the Directive Principles.
