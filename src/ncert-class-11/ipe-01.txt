Q 1 | geo | India: Physical Environment
The mainland of India extends between which latitudes?
(1) 6°4'N and 35°6'N
(2) 8°4'N and 37°6'N
(3) 8°4'N and 33°6'N
(4) 10°4'N and 37°6'N
= 2
~ The mainland stretches from 8°4'N to 37°6'N. The islands of Andaman and Nicobar extend further south.

Q 2 | geo | India: Physical Environment
The mainland of India extends between which longitudes?
(1) 68°7'E and 97°25'E
(2) 60°7'E and 90°25'E
(3) 72°7'E and 97°25'E
(4) 68°7'E and 102°25'E
= 1
~ The east–west extent of the mainland is from 68°7'E to 97°25'E, nearly 30 degrees of longitude.

Q 3 | geo | India: Physical Environment
Which latitude passes through the middle of India and divides the country into almost two equal parts?
(1) The Equator
(2) The Tropic of Capricorn (23°30'S)
(3) The Tropic of Cancer (23°30'N)
(4) The Arctic Circle
= 3
~ The Tropic of Cancer runs roughly through the middle of the country.

Q 4 | geo | India: Physical Environment
The Tropic of Cancer does NOT pass through which of the following states?
(1) Rajasthan
(2) Mizoram
(3) Odisha
(4) Tripura
= 3
~ The Tropic of Cancer passes through Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram. Odisha lies entirely south of it.

Q 5 | geo | India: Physical Environment
Both the latitudinal and longitudinal extent of India's mainland is about 30°, yet the north–south distance (about 3,214 km) is more than the east–west distance (about 2,933 km). Why?
(1) The distance between two longitudes decreases towards the poles, while the distance between two latitudes stays the same
(2) The distance between two latitudes decreases towards the poles
(3) India's eastern border is not measured
(4) The Himalayas add extra distance in the north
= 1
~ Lines of longitude converge towards the poles, so a degree of longitude covers fewer kilometres at India's latitudes than a degree of latitude does.

Q 6 | geo | India: Physical Environment
Why is there a time difference of nearly two hours between Gujarat in the west and Arunachal Pradesh in the east?
(1) Because of the height of the Himalayas
(2) Because India's longitudinal extent is about 30°, and the sun appears to move 1° in 4 minutes
(3) Because of the Tropic of Cancer
(4) Because Arunachal Pradesh follows a different calendar
= 2
~ About 30° of longitude × 4 minutes per degree gives roughly 2 hours. So the sun rises about two hours earlier in Arunachal Pradesh than in Gujarat.

Q 7 | geo | India: Physical Environment
The Standard Meridian of India, used for Indian Standard Time, is 82°30'E. It passes through:
(1) Allahabad (Prayagraj)
(2) Bhopal
(3) Nagpur
(4) Mirzapur in Uttar Pradesh
= 4
~ The time along 82°30'E, passing through Mirzapur, is taken as the standard time for the whole country.

Q 8 | geo | India: Physical Environment
The chapter notes that some countries have more than one standard meridian. The main reason is:
(1) They lie on the equator
(2) They have very high mountains
(3) They are islands
(4) Their vast east-to-west extent
= 4
~ Countries with a large east–west extent, such as the USA with several time zones, need more than one standard meridian.

Q 10 | geo | India: Physical Environment

Q 11 | geo | India: Physical Environment
The length of India's coastline is about 6,100 km for the mainland. Including the island groups, it is about:
(1) 5,500 km
(2) 7,517 km
(3) 15,200 km
(4) 3,214 km
= 2
~ The mainland coastline is about 6,100 km, and the coastline of the entire country including Andaman and Nicobar and Lakshadweep is about 7,517 km.

Q 12 | geo | India: Physical Environment
Consider the following statements:
1. India is bounded by old, worn-down block mountains in the north-west, north and north-east.
2. South of about 22° north latitude, India tapers and extends towards the Indian Ocean, dividing it into the Arabian Sea and the Bay of Bengal.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 only
(3) Both 1 and 2
(4) Neither 1 nor 2
= 2
~ India is bounded by young fold mountains in the north-west, north and north-east. South of about 22°N the peninsula juts into the Indian Ocean.

Q 13 | geo | India: Physical Environment
The Andaman and Nicobar Islands and the Lakshadweep Islands are located respectively in:
(1) Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal
(2) Bay of Bengal and Gulf of Mannar
(3) Indian Ocean and Red Sea
(4) Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea
= 4
~ The Andaman and Nicobar Islands lie in the Bay of Bengal and the Lakshadweep Islands in the Arabian Sea.

Q 14 | geo | India: Physical Environment
Which of the following is true about India's position in the world?
(1) It is located in the south-central part of the continent of Asia, and an ocean is named after it
(2) It is located in western Asia, with no coastline
(3) It lies entirely in the Southern Hemisphere
(4) It lies entirely within the tropics
= 1
~ India lies in the south-central part of Asia, bordering the Indian Ocean — the only ocean named after a country.

Q 15 | geo | India: Physical Environment
India's neighbours in the north-west are:
(1) Nepal and Bhutan
(2) China and Nepal
(3) Myanmar and Bangladesh
(4) Pakistan and Afghanistan
= 4
~ Pakistan and Afghanistan lie to the north-west. China (Tibet), Nepal and Bhutan are northern neighbours, and Myanmar and Bangladesh are eastern neighbours.

Q 16 | geo | India: Physical Environment

Q 18 | geo | India: Physical Environment
Sri Lanka is separated from India by a narrow channel of sea formed by:
(1) The Ten Degree Channel and the Eleven Degree Channel
(2) The Strait of Malacca
