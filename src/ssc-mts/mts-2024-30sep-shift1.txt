Q 1 | qa | Percentage, Profit & Interest
Atul sold two sacks of rice for ₹19,200 each. On one, he gained 20% and on the other, he lost 20%. Find his gain or loss percentage in the whole transaction.
(1) 7% profit
(2) 8% profit
(3) 5% loss
(4) 4% loss
= 4
~ When two articles are sold at the same price, one at x% gain and the other at x% loss, there is always a loss of x²/100 %. Here 20²/100 = 4% loss. Check: cost prices are 19,200/1.2 = ₹16,000 and 19,200/0.8 = ₹24,000, total ₹40,000; selling price ₹38,400, so the loss is ₹1,600 = 4%.

Q 2 | qa | Percentage, Profit & Interest
Riya offers 12% discount on all her goods and offers an additional discount of 6% to those customers who pay in cash. How much will a customer have to pay for a fan marked for ₹2,400 if he pays in cash?
(1) ₹2,300.50
(2) ₹2,122.50
(3) ₹1,985.28

Q 7 | qa | Time, Work, Speed & Distance
A thief saw a policeman, and he started running at a speed of 18 m/s. After 12 seconds, the policeman starts running behind the thief at a certain speed. If the policeman covered 864 meters distance to reach the thief, then the speed (in m/s) of the policeman is:
(1) 15
(2) 30
(3) 24
(4) 28
= 3
~ In 12 seconds the thief gets a lead of 18 × 12 = 216 m. When caught, the policeman has run 864 m, so the thief has run 864 − 216 = 648 m in the same time: 648 ÷ 18 = 36 s. Policeman's speed = 864 ÷ 36 = 24 m/s.

Q 9 | qa | Time, Work, Speed & Distance

(1) 10 sec
(2) 5 sec
(3) 15 sec
(4) 20 sec
= 1

Q 22 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles
Seven people A, B, C, D, E, F and G are sitting around a circular table, facing the center (but not necessarily in the same order). C is sitting to the immediate right of E. D is sitting third to the left of C. A is the immediate neighbour of B and C. F is not the immediate neighbour of E.
What is the position of G with respect to A?
(1) To the immediate left
(2) Second to the left
(3) Second to the right
(4) Third to the left
= 4
~ Facing the centre, a person's right is the next seat anticlockwise. C is right of E; A sits between C and B (on the side away from E); D is third to the left of C, which puts D two seats beyond B. The seats left are between B and D, and between D and E. F cannot sit next to E, so F is between B and D and G is between D and E. Order going right: C, A, B, F, D, G, E. Counting left from A: C, E, G, so G is third to the left of A.

Q 23 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles
Seven people A, B, C, D, E, F and G are sitting around a circular table, facing the centre (but not necessarily in the same order). A is sitting second to the right of E. Only one person is sitting between D and A. B is sitting third to the right of G. F is an immediate neighbour of B. How many people are sitting between D and E when counted from the left of E?
(1) One
(2) Two
(3) Four
(4) Three
= 2
~ Take E's seat first. A is second to E's right; D, with one person between D and A, is two further to the right. B is third to the right of G: the only placement that leaves a free seat next to B for F puts G between A and D and B to the immediate left of E, with F beside B and C between E and A. Going left from E: B, F, then D, so two people (B and F) sit between D and E.
