Q 6 | qa | Mensuration
A cylindrical tank of height 10 m and radius 5 m has a small circular cut of 0.5 m radius on its top surface. What would be the total surface area of the tank?
(1) 150π m²
(2) 599π/4 m²
(3) 156π m²
(4) 299π/2 m²
= 2
~ A closed cylinder's total surface area = 2πrh + 2πr² = 2π × 5 × 10 + 2π × 25 = 100π + 50π = 150π m². The circular cut removes π × 0.5² = π/4 m² from the top, leaving 150π − π/4 = 599π/4 m².

Q 9 | qa | Percentage, Profit & Interest
Two successive increases of 15% each, is by what percentage more than the two successive decreases of 12% each? (Rounded off to 2 decimal places)

(2) 30.56%
(3) 28.25%

Q 10 | qa | Algebra
Two mixers and one TV cost ₹7,000, while two TVs and one mixer cost ₹9,800. The value of one TV is:
(1) ₹4,200
(2) ₹3,200
(3) ₹4,600
(4) ₹3,600
= 1
~ Let a mixer cost M and a TV cost T: 2M + T = 7,000 and M + 2T = 9,800. Adding gives 3(M + T) = 16,800, so M + T = 5,600. Subtracting the first equation from the second: T − M = 2,800. So T = (5,600 + 2,800) ÷ 2 = ₹4,200.

Q 13 | qa | Geometry
Find the area of the quadrilateral ABCD, the coordinates of whose vertices are A(6, −3), B(−4, −2), C(3, 1) and D(5, 0)?
(1) 30 unit²
(2) 16 unit²
(3) 25 unit²
(4) 21 unit²
= 4

Q 20 | qa | Number System & Simplification

(1) 450
(2) 150
(3) 178
(4) 380
= 1

Q 26 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles
P, Q, R, S, T and U are sitting around a circular table facing the centre (but not necessarily in the same order). P is second to the left of T. Q is an immediate neighbour of both U and P. S and P are immediate neighbours. Who among the following has both T and U as his immediate neighbours?
(1) P
(2) R
(3) Q
(4) S
= 2
~ Take T's seat; P is second to T's left, so one seat lies between them on that side. P's two neighbours are Q and S. If Q took the seat between P and T, U would need T's seat, so S sits there and Q is on P's other side, with U next to Q. The last seat, between U and T, is R's, so R has T and U as neighbours.
