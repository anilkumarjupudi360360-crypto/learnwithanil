Q 1 | qa | Percentage, Profit & Interest
Sharad invested two equal amounts for 6 years and 3 years at the rate of 7% and 9% per annum, respectively. The sum of their simple interest is ₹1,587. The invested amount (in ₹) by Sharad is:
(1) 4,600
(2) 4,650
(3) 4,550
(4) 4,374
= 1
~ Let each equal amount be P. Interest = P × 7 × 6/100 + P × 9 × 3/100 = 0.42P + 0.27P = 0.69P = 1,587, so P = ₹2,300. Sharad invested two such amounts: 2 × 2,300 = ₹4,600.

Q 2 | qa | Mensuration
What is the number of gold coins, each having a radius of 0.875 cm and a thickness of 2 mm, that need to be melted to create a cuboid with dimensions 5.5 cm × 5 cm × 3.5 cm? (Use π = 22/7)
(1) 225
(2) 250
(3) 200
(4) 175
= 3
~ Volume of one coin (a thin cylinder) = πr²h = 22/7 × 0.875 × 0.875 × 0.2 = 0.48125 cm³. Volume of the cuboid = 5.5 × 5 × 3.5 = 96.25 cm³. Number of coins = 96.25 ÷ 0.48125 = 200.

Q 5 | qa | Time, Work, Speed & Distance
The speed of a boat in still water is 13 km/h and the speed of the stream is 4 km/h. How much more time will it take in going upstream than ingoing downstream a distance of 68 km? (Correct to the nearest minute)
(1) 3 h 33 min
(2) 3 h 30 min
(3) 2 h 55 min
(4) 3 h 50 min
= 1
~ Upstream speed = 13 − 4 = 9 km/h and downstream speed = 13 + 4 = 17 km/h. Times for 68 km: 68/9 = 7.556 h and 68/17 = 4 h. Difference = 3.556 h = 3 h 33 min (0.556 × 60 ≈ 33).

Q 7 | qa | Time, Work, Speed & Distance
In a circular race of 1500 m, P and Q start from the same point and at the same time, at speeds of 20 km/h and 35 km/h, respectively. After what time will they meet for the first time on the track if they are running in the same direction?
(1) 840 sec
(2) 640 sec
(3) 360 sec
(4) 340 sec
= 3
~ Running the same way round, they meet first when the faster runner has gained one full lap (1,500 m). Relative speed = 35 − 20 = 15 km/h = 15 × 5/18 = 25/6 m/s. Time = 1,500 ÷ 25/6 = 360 seconds.

Q 17 | qa | Mensuration

Q 32 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles
S, T, U, V, W and X are sitting around a circular table facing the centre (but not necessarily in the same order). W sits second to the right of U. T sits second to the right of W. X sits to the immediate right of T. Who sits to the immediate right of X?
(1) W
(2) U
(3) S
(4) V
= 2
~ Going to the right round the table from U: U, _, W, _, T, X (W is second right of U, T second right of W, X immediately right of T). With six seats, the seat to the immediate right of X is U's seat. So U sits to the immediate right of X.

Who is the immediate neighbour of both Dhiraj and Mohan?
(1) Param
(2) Lokesh
(3) Suresh
(4) Rohan
= 4
