Q 1 | rc | Passages
Read the following passage and answer the question that follows. Your answer should be based on the passage only.
The main threat to maintaining progress in human development comes from the increasingly evident unsustainability of production and consumption patterns. Current production models rely heavily on fossil fuels. We now know that this is unsustainable because the resources are finite. The close link between economic growth and greenhouse gas emissions needs to be severed for human development to become truly sustainable. Some developed countries have begun to alleviate the worst effects by expanding recycling and investing in public transport and infrastructure. But most developing countries are hampered by the high costs and low availability of clean energy sources. Developed countries need to support developing countries' transition to sustainable human development.
Unsustainability in production pattern is due to which of the following?
1. Heavy dependence on fossil fuels
2. Limited availability of resources
3. Expansion of recycling
Select the correct answer using the code given below.
(1) 1 and 2 only
(2) 2 only
(3) 1 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 1
~ The passage says current production relies heavily on fossil fuels, which is unsustainable because the resources are finite; so heavy dependence on fossil fuels (1) and limited resources (2) cause the unsustainability. Expanding recycling (3) is mentioned as a remedy some developed countries use, not a cause. Hence 1 and 2 only, (a).

Q 4 | lr | Series & Patterns
What is the value of X in the sequence 20, 10, 10, 15, 30, 75, X?
(1) 105
(2) 120
(3) 150
(4) 225
= 4
~ Each term is the previous one multiplied by 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, ... in turn: 20 × 0.5 = 10, 10 × 1 = 10, 10 × 1.5 = 15, 15 × 2 = 30, 30 × 2.5 = 75, so X = 75 × 3 = 225, (d).

Q 5 | qa | Number System
An Identity Card has the number ABCDEFG, not necessarily in that order, where each letter represents a distinct digit (1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9 only).
The number is divisible by 9. After deleting the first digit from the right, the resulting number is divisible by 6.
After deleting two digits from the right of original number, the resulting number is divisible by 5. After deleting three digits from the right of original number, the resulting number is divisible by 4.
After deleting four digits from the right of original number, the resulting number is divisible by 3. (1) 8
(2) 9
(3) 11
(4) 12

Q 6 | lr | Directions, Clocks & Calendars
Two friends X and Y start running and they run together for 50 m in the same direction and reach a point. X turns right and runs 60 m, while Y turns left and runs 40 m. Then X turns left and runs 50 m and stops, while Y turns right and runs 50 m and then stops.
How far are the two friends from each other now?
(1) 100 m
(2) 90 m
(3) 60 m
(4) 50 m
= 1
~ Let them run 50 m north. X turns right (east) 60 m, then left (north) 50 m; Y turns left (west) 40 m, then right (north) 50 m. Both end 100 m north of the start, X 60 m east and Y 40 m west, so they are 60 + 40 = 100 m apart, (a).

Q 7 | lr | Directions, Clocks & Calendars
Which date of June 2099 among the following is Sunday?
(1) 4
(2) 5
(3) 6
(4) 7
= 4
~ 1 June 2000 was a Thursday. From 1 June 2000 to 1 June 2099 is 99 years with 24 leap days (2004 to 2096), i.e. 99 + 24 = 123 odd days ≡ 4 (mod 7), so 1 June 2099 is Thursday + 4 = Monday. Then 7 June 2099 is a Sunday, (d).

Q 8 | qa | Arithmetic
A bill for ₹1,840 is paid in the denominations of ₹50, ₹20 and ₹10 notes. 50 notes in all are used. Consider the following statements:
1. 25 notes of ₹50 are used and the remaining are in the denominations of ₹20 and ₹10.
2. 35 notes of ₹20 are used and the remaining are in the denominations of ₹50 and ₹10.
3. 20 notes of ₹10 are used and the remaining are in the denominations of ₹50 and ₹20.
Which of the above statements are not correct?
(1) 1 and 2 only
(2) 2 and 3 only
(3) 1 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 4
~ Let a, b, c be the numbers of ₹50, ₹20 and ₹10 notes: a + b + c = 50 and 50a + 20b + 10c = 1840, which gives 4a + b = 134. 1: a = 25 gives b = 34, c = −9, impossible. 2: b = 35 gives a = 24.75, impossible. 3: c = 20 gives a + b = 30 and 4a + b = 134, so a = 34.67, impossible. None is correct, so all three are "not correct", (d).

Q 9 | qa | Number System
Which number amongst 2⁴⁰, 3²¹, 4¹⁸ and 8¹² is the smallest?

(2) 3²¹
(3) 4¹⁸
(4) 8¹²
= 2

Q 30 | lr | Puzzles & Arrangements
Three persons A, B and C are standing in a queue not necessarily in the same order. There are 4 persons between A and B, and 7 persons between B and C. If there are 11 persons ahead of C and 13 behind A, what could be the minimum number of persons in the queue?
(1) 22
(2) 28
(3) 32
(4) 38
= 1
~ C is 12th from the front (11 persons ahead). With 7 persons between B and C, B is 4th or 20th. With 4 persons between A and B, A is 5 places from B, and the queue length is A's position + 13. B = 4th gives A = 9th (A cannot be at −1), so the queue has 9 + 13 = 22 persons, with C (12th) inside it. B = 20th would make A 15th or 25th and the queue 28 or 38. The minimum is 22, (a).

Which of the above statements is/are correct?
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 only
(3) Both 1 and 2
(4) Neither 1 nor 2
= 3

Q 69 | qa | Arithmetic
There are two containers X and Y.
X contains 100 ml of milk and Y contains 100 ml of water. 20 ml of milk from X is transferred to Y. After mixing well, 20 ml of the mixture in Y is transferred back to X. If m denotes the proportion of milk in X and n denotes the proportion of water in Y, then which one of the following is correct?
(1) m = n
(2) m > n
(3) m < n
(4) Cannot be determined due to insufficient data
= 1
~ After the first transfer Y has 20 ml milk and 100 ml water. 20 ml of this (1/6 of it) goes back: 10/3 ml milk and 50/3 ml water. X now has 80 + 10/3 = 250/3 ml milk in 100 ml, so m = 5/6. Y keeps 100 − 50/3 = 250/3 ml water in 100 ml, so n = 5/6. Hence m = n, (a).

Q 77 | qa | Permutations, Combinations & Probability

(1) 18

(3) 54
(4) 81
= 4
~ The 9 cups form a 3 × 3 grid. Choosing positions for the 3 tea cups gives C(9, 3) = 84 arrangements. A row with no coffee would hold all 3 tea cups, which happens in 3 ways (one per row). So 84 − 3 = 81, (d).

Q 78 | qa | Algebra

(2) Only two
(3) Only three
(4) No such possibility is there
= 3
~ For n − 1, n, n + 1: the sum is 3n and the product is n(n² − 1). Equality gives n = 0 or n² = 4, i.e. n = 0, 2 or −2: the triples (−1, 0, 1), (1, 2, 3) and (−3, −2, −1). Three possibilities, (c).
