Q 1 | rc | Passages
Read the following passage and answer the question that follows. Your answer should be based on the passage only.
According to the Food and Agriculture Organization, one-third of food produced for human consumption is lost or wasted globally. Food is lost or wasted throughout the supply chain, from initial agricultural production to final household consumption. The increasing wastage also results in land degradation by about 45%, mainly due to deforestation, unsustainable agricultural practices, and excessive groundwater extraction. The energy spent over wasted food results in about 3.5 billion tonnes of carbon dioxide production every year. Decay also leads to harmful emissions of other gases in the atmosphere. Addressing the loss and wastage of food in all forms is critical to complete the cycle of food sufficiency and food sustainability.
Which of the following statements best reflect the most logical and rational inferences that can be made from the passage?
1. The current methods of food distribution are solely responsible for the loss and wastage of food.
2. Land productivity is adversely affected by the prevailing trend of food loss and wastage.
3. Reduction in the loss and wastage of food results in lesser carbon footprint.
4. Post-harvest technologies to prevent or reduce the loss and wastage of food are not available.
Select the correct answer using the code given below.
(1) 1, 2 and 3
(2) 2 and 3 only
(3) 1, 3 and 4
(4) 1, 2 and 4
= 2
~ Statement 2 follows: the passage links food waste to land degradation of about 45%, so the prevailing trend hurts land productivity. Statement 3 follows: wasted food carries the energy spent on it (about 3.5 billion tonnes of CO2 a year) and its decay emits other gases, so cutting waste shrinks the carbon footprint. Statement 1 is too strong: food is lost across the whole chain, from production to the household, not because of distribution alone. Statement 4 is never discussed. Hence 2 and 3 only, (b).

Q 5 | qa | Geometry & Mensuration
What is the least possible number of cuts required to cut a cube into 64 identical pieces?
(1) 8
(2) 9
(3) 12
(4) 16
= 2
~ 64 identical pieces means a 4 × 4 × 4 arrangement, so each of the three directions needs 3 parallel cuts. Rearranging pieces between cuts does not help, because the centre cubes of a 4 × 4 × 4 block have all six faces inside the block and each face needs its own cut. Minimum = 3 + 3 + 3 = 9 cuts: (b).

Q 6 | qa | Arithmetic
In the expression 5 * 4 * 3 * 2 * 1, * is chosen from +, −, × each at most two times. What is the smallest non-negative value of the expression?
(1) 3
(2) 2
(3) 1
(4) 0
= 4
~ Try to reach 0 using each of +, −, × at most twice in the four places: 5 − 4 − 3 + 2 × 1 = 1 − 3 + 2 = 0 (two minus signs, one plus, one multiplication). Since 0 is the smallest possible non-negative value and it can be achieved, the answer is (d).

Q 8 | qa | Arithmetic
X, Y and Z can complete a piece of work individually in 6 hours, 8 hours and 8 hours respectively. However, only one person at a time can work in each hour and nobody can work for two consecutive hours. All are engaged to finish the work. What is the minimum amount of time that they will take to finish the work?
(1) 6 hours 15 minutes
(2) 6 hours 30 minutes
(3) 6 hours 45 minutes
(4) 7 hours
= 3
~ Hourly work: X = 1/6, Y = 1/8, Z = 1/8. To finish fastest, X (the fastest) should work every other hour: X, Y, X, Z, X, Y gives 3 × 1/6 + 3 × 1/8 = 1/2 + 3/8 = 7/8 in 6 hours. The remaining 1/8 is done by X in (1/8)/(1/6) = 3/4 hour = 45 minutes. Total 6 hours 45 minutes: (c).

Q 9 | qa | Number System
How many consecutive zeros are there at the end of the integer obtained in the product 1² × 2⁴ × 3⁶ × 4⁸ × ··· × 25⁵⁰?
(1) 50
(2) 55
(3) 100
(4) 200
= 4
~ The general term is k^(2k), k = 1 to 25. Trailing zeros = number of factors 5 (factors 2 are far more). Only k = 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 contribute: 5¹⁰ gives 10, 10²⁰ gives 20, 15³⁰ gives 30, 20⁴⁰ gives 40, and 25⁵⁰ = 5¹⁰⁰ gives 100. Total = 10 + 20 + 30 + 40 + 100 = 200: (d).

Q 10 | qa | Number System
On January 1st, 2023, a person saved ₹ 1. On January 2nd, 2023, he saved ₹ 2 more than that on the previous day. On January 3rd, 2023, he saved ₹ 2 more than that on the previous day and so on. At the end of which date was his total savings a perfect square as well a perfect cube?
(1) 7th January, 2023
(2) 8th January, 2023
(3) 9th January, 2023
(4) Not possible
= 2
~ Daily savings are 1, 3, 5, 7, … (each day ₹ 2 more), and the sum of the first n odd numbers is n². For n² to be a perfect cube as well, n itself must be a perfect cube. Apart from the trivial n = 1, the next is n = 8: total 64 = 8² = 4³. So the 8th of January, 2023: (b).

Q 16 | qa | Arithmetic
What percent of water must be mixed with honey so as to gain 20% by selling the mixture at the cost price of honey?
(1) 20%
(2) 10%
(3) 5%
(4) 4%
= 1
~ Let honey cost ₹ c per litre. Mix w litres of water with 1 litre of honey and sell the mixture at ₹ c per litre: revenue = c(1 + w) against cost c. A 20% gain needs 1 + w = 1.2, so w = 0.2 litre, i.e. 20% water: (a).

Q 20 | qa | Number System
Consider the following statements in respect of the sum S = x + y + z, where x, y and z are distinct prime numbers each less than 10:
1. The unit digit of S can be 0.
2. The unit digit of S can be 9.
3. The unit digit of S can be 5.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(1) 1 and 2 only
(2) 2 and 3 only
(3) 1 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 3

Q 24 | lr | Series & Patterns
Consider the sequence A _ BCD _ BBCDABC _ DABC _ D that follows a certain pattern. Which one of the following completes the sequence?
(1) B, A, D, C
(2) B, A, C, D
(3) A, A, C, D
(4) A, A, D, C
= 3
~ Split the sequence into blocks of five: A_BCD | _BBCD | ABC_D | ABC_D. Filling A, A, C, D gives AABCD, ABBCD, ABCCD, ABCDD: in each block one more letter of ABCD is doubled, in order A, B, C, D. Hence A, A, C, D: (c).

Two persons P and Q enter into a business. P puts ₹ 14,000 more than Q, but P has invested for 8 months and Q has invested for 10 months. If P's share is ₹ 400 more than Q's share out of the total profit of ₹ 2,000, what is the capital contributed by P?

(2) ₹ 26,000
(3) ₹ 24,000
(4) ₹ 20,000
= 1

Q 29 | qa | Algebra
A father said to his son, "n years back I was as old as you are now. My present age is four times your age n years back".
If the sum of the present ages of the father and the son is 130 years, what is the difference of their ages?
(1) 30 years
(2) 32 years
(3) 34 years
(4) 36 years
= 1
~ Let the father be F and the son S. 'n years back I was as old as you are now' gives F − n = S, so n = F − S. 'My present age is four times your age n years back' gives F = 4(S − n) = 4(2S − F), so 5F = 8S. With F + S = 130: S = 50, F = 80, and the difference is 30 years: (a).
