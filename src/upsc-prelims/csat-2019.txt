Q 1 | rc | Passages
Read the following passage and answer the question that follows. Your answer should be based on the passage only.
Political theorists no doubt have to take history of injustice, for example, untouchability, seriously. The concept of historical injustice takes note of a variety of historical wrongs that continue into the present in some form or the other and tend to resist repair. Two reasons might account for resistance to repair. One, not only are the roots of injustice buried deep in history, injustice itself constitutes economic structures of exploitation, ideologies of discrimination and modes of representation. Two, the category of historical injustice generally extends across a number of wrongs such as economic deprivation, social discrimination and lack of recognition. This category is complex, not only because of the overlap between a number of wrongs, but because one or the other wrong, generally discrimination, tends to acquire partial autonomy from others. This is borne out by the history of repair in India.
What is the main idea that we can infer from the passage?
(1) Untouchability in India has not been taken seriously by political theorists.
(2) Historical injustice is inevitable in any society and is always beyond repair.
(3) Social discrimination and deprivation have their roots in bad economies.
(4) It is difficult, if not impossible, to repair every manifestation of historical injustice.
= 4
~ The passage explains why historical injustice resists repair: its roots run deep, it builds structures of exploitation and discrimination, and its wrongs overlap while one of them (usually discrimination) gains partial autonomy. The main idea is that repairing every form of historical injustice is difficult, if not impossible, (d). The passage does not say theorists ignored untouchability (a), that injustice is always beyond repair (b), or that bad economies are the root (c).

Q 9 | qa | Number System
The number of times the digit 5 will appear while writing the integers from 1 to 1000 is
(1) 269
(2) 271
(3) 300
(4) 302
= 3
~ Count from 000 to 999 as three-digit strings: each of the three places shows the digit 5 in one-tenth of the 1000 numbers, that is 100 times. That gives 3 × 100 = 300, and 1000 adds no 5. Hence (c).

Q 10 | qa | Geometry & Mensuration
A solid cube is painted yellow, blue and black such that opposite faces are of same colour. The cube is then cut into 36 cubes of two different sizes such that 32 cubes are small and the other four cubes are big. None of the faces of the bigger cubes is painted blue. How many cubes have only one face painted?
(1) 4
(2) 6
(3) 8
(4) 10
= 3
~ Take a big cube as 8 small ones: 4 big cubes plus 32 small cubes make 64 small units, so the cube is 4 × 4 × 4. The 4 big cubes (2 × 2 × 2 each) fill the middle two layers, so they touch no blue face; blue is on the top and bottom. Each of these two outer layers is 4 × 4 small cubes, and only its central 2 × 2 cubes have just one painted face (blue). So 4 + 4 = 8. Hence (c).

Q 11 | qa | Arithmetic
A and B are two heavy steel blocks. If B is placed on the top of A, the weight increases by 60%. How much weight will reduce with respect to the total weight of A and B, if B is removed from the top of A?
(1) 60%
(2) 45.5%
(3) 40%
(4) 37.5%
= 4
~ Placing B on A raises the weight by 60%, so B = 0.6A and the total is 1.6A. Removing B cuts the weight by 0.6A out of 1.6A, which is 0.6 ÷ 1.6 = 37.5%. Hence (d).

Q 12 | lr | Directions, Clocks & Calendars
Mr 'X' has three children. The birthday of the first child falls on the 5th Monday of April, that of the second one falls on the 5th Thursday of November. On which day is the birthday of his third child, which falls on 20th December?
(1) Monday
(2) Thursday
(3) Saturday
(4) Sunday
= 2
~ April has 30 days, so a 5th Monday needs 1 April to be a Saturday, Sunday or Monday. From 1 April to 1 November is 214 days (30 weeks and 4 days), so 1 November falls 4 days later in the week: Wednesday, Thursday or Friday. A 5th Thursday in November (30 days) needs 1 November to be Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday, so it is Wednesday or Thursday. 20 December is 49 days (7 weeks) after 1 November, so it falls on the same day: Wednesday or Thursday. Only Thursday is offered. Hence (b).

Q 13 | lr | Logical Reasoning
Consider the following Statements and Conclusions:
Statements: 1. Some rats are cats.
2. Some cats are dogs.
3. No dog is a cow.
Conclusions: I. No cow is a cat.
II. No dog is a rat.
III. Some cats are rats.
Which of the above conclusions is/are drawn from the statements?
(1) 1, II and III
(2) Only I and II
(3) Only III
(4) Only II and III
= 3
~ 'Some rats are cats' converts to 'some cats are rats', so III follows. Cows and cats are linked only through dogs, and 'some cats are dogs' with 'no dog is a cow' does not stop some other cats from being cows, so I does not follow. Nothing links dogs and rats definitely, so II does not follow. Hence only III, (c).

Q 18 | qa | Arithmetic
In 2002, Meenu's age was one-third of the age of Meera, whereas in 2010, Meenu's age was half the age of Meera. What is Meenu's year of birth?
(1) 1992
(2) 1994
(3) 1996
(4) 1998
= 2
~ Let Meenu be x in 2002; then Meera is 3x. In 2010, x + 8 = (3x + 8) ÷ 2, so 2x + 16 = 3x + 8 and x = 8. Meenu was 8 in 2002, so she was born in 1994. Hence (b).

Q 19 | qa | Algebra
Rakesh and Rajesh together bought 10 balls and 10 rackets. Rakesh spent ₹ 1300 and Rajesh spent ₹ 1500. If each racket costs three times a ball does, then what is the price of a racket?
(1) ₹ 70
(2) ₹ 90
(3) ₹ 210
(4) ₹ 240
= 3
~ Together they spent ₹ 1300 + ₹ 1500 = ₹ 2800 on 10 balls and 10 rackets. If a ball costs b, a racket costs 3b, so 10b + 30b = 2800 and b = 70. A racket costs 3 × 70 = ₹ 210. Hence (c).

Q 20 | qa | Arithmetic
In a conference, out of a total 100 participants, 70 are Indians. If 60 of the total participants are vegetarian, then which of the following statements is/are correct?

2. At least 10 Indian participants are non-vegetarian.
Select the correct answer using the codes given below:
(1) 1 only
(2) 2 only
(3) Both 1 and 2
(4) Neither 1 nor 2
= 3
~ There are 70 Indians and 60 vegetarians among 100 people, so at least 70 + 60 − 100 = 30 Indians must be vegetarian (1). There are 40 non-vegetarians, and the 70 Indians can include at most 60 vegetarians, so at least 70 − 60 = 10 Indians are non-vegetarian (2). Hence both, (c).
