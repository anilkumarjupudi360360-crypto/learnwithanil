Q 1 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
After interchanging which two signs and two numbers, the value of the given equation will be ‘1’?
4 × 6 ÷ 9 + 8 – 3
(1) × and ÷, 4 and 8
(2) + and –, 4 and 6
(3) ÷ and +, 6 and 9
(4) × and ÷, 8 and 3
= 4
~ Swap × with ÷ and 8 with 3: the expression becomes 4 ÷ 6 × 9 + 3 – 8. By BODMAS, 4 ÷ 6 × 9 = 6, so 6 + 3 – 8 = 1. (Option 1, for example, gives 8 ÷ 6 × 9 + 4 – 3 = 13.)

Q 2 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
If ‘I’ means ‘+’, ‘J’ means ‘×’, ‘K’ means ‘÷’ and ‘L’ means ‘–’, then which of the following equation is correct?
I. 81 J 18 K 9 I 20 L 10 = 172
II. 9 I 17 J 5 L 40 K 5 = 86
(1) Only I
(2) Only II
(3) Both I and II
(4) Neither I nor II
= 3
~ Decode and apply BODMAS. I: 81 × 18 ÷ 9 + 20 – 10 = 81 × 2 + 10 = 172 (true). II: 9 + 17 × 5 – 40 ÷ 5 = 9 + 85 – 8 = 86 (true). So both equations are correct.

Q 3 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
If 80 P 10 Q 70 R 50 = 70 and 45 P 55 Q 65 R 75 = 110, then 40 P 10 Q 18 R 28 = ?
(1) 60
(2) 80
(3) 70
(4) 90
= 1
~ Taking P = +, Q = – and R = + fits both equations: 80 + 10 – 70 + 50 = 70 and 45 + 55 – 65 + 75 = 110. Hence 40 + 10 – 18 + 28 = 60.

Q 4 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
If ‘@’ means ‘÷’, ‘#’ means ‘×’, ‘>’ means ‘+’ and ‘<’ means ‘–’, then which of the following equation is NOT correct?
(1) 18 < 24 @ 8 > 17 # 20 = 61
(2) 17 # 7 < 18 @ 3 > 19 = 132
(3) 80 > 70 < 100 @ 2 # 3 = 0
(4) 20 # 25 > 30 < 40 @ 10 = 526
= 1
~ Option 1 becomes 18 – 24 ÷ 8 + 17 × 20 = 18 – 3 + 340 = 355, not 61, so it is NOT correct. The others hold: 17 × 7 – 18 ÷ 3 + 19 = 132; 80 + 70 – 100 ÷ 2 × 3 = 0; 20 × 25 + 30 – 40 ÷ 10 = 526.

Q 5 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
If 13 * 17 # 2 = 229 and 8 * 6 # 5 = 20, then 8 * 14 # 2 = ?
(1) 100
(2) 110
(3) 70
(4) 130
= 4
~ The rule is (a² + b²) ÷ c: (13² + 17²) ÷ 2 = 458 ÷ 2 = 229 and (8² + 6²) ÷ 5 = 100 ÷ 5 = 20. So (8² + 14²) ÷ 2 = (64 + 196) ÷ 2 = 130.

Q 25 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
A + B means ‘A’ is the mother of ‘B’, A – B means ‘A’ is the father of ‘B’, A × B means ‘A’ is the sister of ‘B’, A ÷ B means ‘A’ is the daughter of ‘B’. If N × K – R ÷ S + T, then how is K related to T?

= 3
