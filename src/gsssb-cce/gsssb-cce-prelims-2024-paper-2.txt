Q 1 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
If 'A' means 'multiplied by', 'B' means 'plus', 'C' means 'minus' and 'D' means "divided by", then what will be the value of following expression?
6 A 6 C 3 B 38 D 2 = ?
(1) 40
(2) 51
(3) 52
(4) 39
= 3
~ Replace the letters: 6 × 6 − 3 + 38 ÷ 2. By BODMAS, first 38 ÷ 2 = 19 and 6 × 6 = 36; then 36 − 3 + 19 = 52.

Q 8 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
On the basis of Prime numbers and their sequence only which of the following pair is similar to the expression given below?
181 : 191
(1) 109 : 113
(2) 215 : 264
(3) 117 : 184
(4) 111 : 120
= 1
~ 181 and 191 are consecutive prime numbers (183, 185, 187 = 11 × 17 and 189 in between are composite). In the same way 109 and 113 are consecutive primes (110, 111 = 3 × 37 and 112 are composite). The other pairs contain non-primes such as 215, 117 and 111.

36 : 722

(2) 12 : 262

(4) 43 : 862

Q 11 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
Which of the following pairs is similar to the expression given below based on the sum of numbers and subtraction method only?
998 : 25
(1) 279 : 15
(2) 319 : 16
(3) 846: 17
(4) 868 : 20

Which of the following equations are correct?

(2) A^3 + b^3 + c^3– 3abc = (a + b + c)(a^2 + b^2 + c^2 – ab – bc – ca)

Q 45 | qa | Algebra, Geometry & Mensuration
The pairs of equations 4x+2y-2 = 0 and -12x-6y+6=0 have:
(1) Unique solution
(2) Exactly two solutions
(3) Infinitely many solutions
(4) No solution
= 3
~ Compare the ratios: a1/a2 = 4/(−12) = −1/3, b1/b2 = 2/(−6) = −1/3, c1/c2 = (−2)/6 = −1/3. All three ratios are equal (the second equation is −3 times the first), so the lines coincide and there are infinitely many solutions.

(4) દ્વારિકાઈશ્વર
= 1
~ Guna sandhi: aa + ee = e, so dvaraka + eesh = 'dvarakesh' (Lord of Dwarka). The other options are different words or wrong joins.

Q 94 | guj | Grammar
‘પ્રત્યક્ષ’ શબ્દનો સાચો સંધિ વિગ્રહ શોધો.

(2) પ્ર + તીઅર્ક
(3) પ + ત્યેંક
(4) પ્રતિ + અક્ષ
= 4
~ Yan sandhi: i + a = ya, so prati + aksh = 'pratyaksh' (before the eyes, i.e. direct/visible; 'aksh' = eye).

Q 95 | guj | Grammar
‘કેતુ + ઉદય’ શબ્દનું સાચું સંધિ જોડાણ શોધો

(2) કેતઓય
(3) કેતુદય

Q 100 | guj | Translation & Comprehension
વાક્યનો સાચો અંગ્રેજી અનુવાદ કરો :
આ બનાવ પછી હું વધારે અધીરો બન્યો
(1) The incident, however, made me more anxious than ever to sail.
(2) made me more anxious than ever to sail
(3) The incident, however, made me more
(4) The incident, however, made me more restless and painful all the time
= 1
~ 'Aa banav' = this incident; 'vadhare adhiro banyo' = became more anxious/eager. Option 1 is the only complete sentence with that meaning; options 2 and 3 are fragments, and option 4 adds 'painful all the time'.
