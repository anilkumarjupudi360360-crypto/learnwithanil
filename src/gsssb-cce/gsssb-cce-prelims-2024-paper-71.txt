Q 1 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
A is the wife of B. B is the brother of C. C is the sister of D. D is the father of E. Who is the sister of father of E?
(1) A
(2) D
(3) B
(4) C
= 4
~ D is the father of E, so the sister of E's father is the sister of D. C is the sister of D, so the answer is C.

Q 2 | rea | Verbal Reasoning

(1) 3
(2) 2
(3) 1
(4) 4
= 1

Q 3 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
‘P + Q’ means ‘P is the sister of Q’, ‘P – Q’ means ‘P is the daughter of Q’, ‘P × Q’ means ‘P is the brother of Q’, ‘P ÷ Q’ means ‘P is the husband of Q’. If X + Y – Z × M ÷ L, then how is M related to X?
(1) Father
(2) Father’s sister
(3) Father’s brother
(4) Mother
= 3
~ X + Y: X is Y's sister; Y – Z: Y is Z's daughter, so Z (male, since Z × M makes Z a brother) is X's father. Z × M: Z is M's brother; M ÷ L: M is L's husband, so M is male. Hence M is X's father's brother.

Q 4 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
In the following question below are given some statements followed by some conclusions based on those statements. Taking the given statements to be true even if they seem to be at variance from commonly known facts. Read all the conclusions and then decide which of the given conclusion logically follows the given statements.
Statements:
I. All I are M.
II. Some T are I.
Conclusion:
I. Some M are not I.
II. No T is M.
(1) Only conclusion I follows
(2) Only conclusion II follows
(3) Both conclusions I and II follows
(4) Neither conclusion follows
= 4
~ All I are M and some T are I, so some T are definitely M; hence 'No T is M' is false. 'Some M are not I' is only a possibility (M may be the same as I), so it does not definitely follow. Neither conclusion follows.

Q 8 | rea | Data Interpretation
The question below consists of a question and two statements numbered I and II given below it. You have to decide whether the data provided in the statements are sufficient to answer the question.
How much time train P will take to cover a distance of 532 km?
I. The ratio of speed at which train P and train Q runs is 4 : 5 respectively.
II. The average of speeds of the train P and train Q is 400/9 km/hr.
(1) If the question can be answered by using one of the statement alone but cannot be answered by using the other statement alone.
(2) If the question can be answered by using either of the statement (I) or (II) alone.
(3) If the question can be answered by using both the statements together but not by either statement alone.
(4) If the question cannot be answered even by using both the statements together.
= 3
~ Neither statement alone gives P's speed. Together: P + Q = 2 × 400/9 = 800/9 km/hr and P : Q = 4 : 5, so P = 4/9 × 800/9 = 3200/81 km/hr and time = 532 ÷ 3200/81 = 532 × 81/3200 ≈ 13.47 hours. Both statements are needed.

Q 9 | rea | Data Interpretation

Q 10 | rea | Puzzles & Arrangements
Runs of 5 boys R, B, C, L and Q are compared. Runs of no two boys are same. Runs of B is less than Q but more than C. Runs of L is more than R but less than C. Which of the following is the correct sequence?
I. Q > L > R
II. B > L > C

(2) Only II
(3) Both I and II
(4) Neither I nor II
= 1
~ Q > B > C and C > L > R give the order Q > B > C > L > R. So Q > L > R is true, but B > L > C is false because L is less than C. Only I is correct.

Q 11 | rea | Puzzles & Arrangements
Height of 6 persons J, K, L, M, N and O is compared. Height of K is more than only 3 persons. Height of no two persons is same. Height of J is more than M, N and O. Height of J is not maximum. If height of J is 165 cm, then what can be the height of M?
(1) 169 cm
(2) 160 cm
(3) 172 cm
(4) 168 cm
= 2
~ K is taller than exactly 3 persons, so K is 3rd tallest. J is taller than M, N and O and is not the tallest, so J must be 2nd: L > J > K > M, N, O. M is therefore shorter than J (165 cm); among the options only 160 cm is possible.

Q 23 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
By interchanging the given two signs and numbers (not digits) which of the following equation will be NOT correct?
× and +, 7 and 4
(1) 4 × 2 + 7 – 9 ÷ 3 = 12
(2) 7 + 8 × 6 ÷ 2 – 4 = 10
(3) 7 × 3 – 4 + 6 ÷ 2 = –14
(4) 7 + 8 ÷ 2 – 4 × 3 = 12
