# UKPSC Reviewing Officer / Assistant Reviewing Officer (Pre) Exam-2023, held 17 December 2023, General Studies & General Aptitude Test, Series A (UKPSC's own booklet); answers: UKPSC's final answer key published with the result of 29 February 2024 (dropped: Q19, 34)

Q 1 | pol | Constitution & Polity
The Preamble of the Indian Constitution is based on which of the following documents/laws ?
(1) Nehru Report
(2) Objectives Resolution
(3) Government of India Act, 1935
(4) Indian Independence Act, 1947
= 2
~ The Preamble is based on the Objectives Resolution moved by Jawaharlal Nehru in the Constituent Assembly on 13 December 1946 and adopted on 22 January 1947. Its ideals (sovereign republic, justice, liberty, equality) were later shaped into the Preamble.

Q 2 | pol | Constitution & Polity
Who is the author of the book, “The Indian Constitution : Cornerstone of a Nation” ?
(1) Granville Austin
(2) Prabhu Chawla
(3) Susanne Rudolf
(4) M.P. Singh
= 1
~ American historian Granville Austin wrote “The Indian Constitution: Cornerstone of a Nation” (1966), a classic study of the making of the Constitution; he described it as first and foremost a social document. He also wrote “Working a Democratic Constitution”.

Q 3 | pol | Constitution & Polity
Which of the following Article of the Indian Constitution deals with the procedure of amendment of the Constitution ?
(1) Article 280
(2) Article 352
(3) Article 360
(4) Article 368
= 4
~ Article 368 (Part XX) gives Parliament the power to amend the Constitution and lays down the procedure. The others: Article 280 – Finance Commission, Article 352 – National Emergency, Article 360 – Financial Emergency.

Q 4 | pol | Constitution & Polity
Which of the following Article provides for the Constitution of Finance Commission to review the financial position of Panchayats ?
(1) Article 243 A
(2) Article 243 B
(3) Article 243 D
(4) Article 243 I
= 4
~ Under Article 243-I (added by the 73rd Amendment), the Governor constitutes a State Finance Commission every five years to review the financial position of Panchayats. Article 243-A is on the Gram Sabha, 243-B on constitution of Panchayats and 243-D on reservation of seats.

Q 5 | pol | Constitution & Polity
The Right of Privacy is envisaged in which Article of the Indian Constitution ?
(1) Article 14
(2) Article 19
(3) Article 21
(4) Article 32
= 3
~ In Justice K.S. Puttaswamy v. Union of India (2017), a nine-judge bench of the Supreme Court held that the right to privacy is a fundamental right, intrinsic to the right to life and personal liberty under Article 21.

Q 9 | eco | Indian Economy
Which one of the following is not included in Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI) ?
(1) Heath
(2) Education
(3) Living Standard
(4) Per Capita Income
= 4
~ The MPI (UNDP–OPHI global index, and NITI Aayog's national index) measures deprivation in three equally weighted dimensions: health, education and standard of living. It is a non-income measure, so per capita income is not part of it.

(2) 1961-71

(4) 1981-91
