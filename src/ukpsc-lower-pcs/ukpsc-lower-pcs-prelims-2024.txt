Q 1 | pol | Constitution & Polity

I. Fundamental Duties were added in the Constitution of India.
II. Declaration of Emergency due to Chinese invasion.
III. Right to Information Act.
IV. 73rd Amendment Act for Panchayati Raj.
Select the correct answer from the code given below :
(1) IV, III, II, I
(2) II, I, IV, III

Q 2 | pol | Constitution & Polity
Consider the following statements :
1. Uniform Civil Code for the citizens is described under Article-44 of the Constitution of India.
2. Uttarakhand became the first state of India to implement the Uniform Civil Code.
Select the correct answer from the code given below :
(1) Only (1) is correct.
(2) Only (2) is correct.
(3) Both (1) and (2) are correct.
(4) Neither (1) nor (2) is correct.
= 1
~ Article 44 (a Directive Principle) says the State shall endeavour to secure a uniform civil code for citizens throughout India, so (1) is correct. Q 35 | ukg | History & Culture of Uttarakhand
The system of ‘Barah Aaana Bisi’ introduced by Commissioner Henry Ramsay was related to which one of the following ?
(1) Land settlement
(2) Judicial reforms
(3) War fund
(4) Famine fund
= 1
~ ‘Barah Aana Bisi’ means ‘twelve annas per bisi’; the bisi (20 nali, roughly an acre) was Kumaon's unit of land. Under this system of Henry Ramsay (Commissioner of Kumaon, 1856–84) land revenue was reckoned at a rate per bisi of land held, so it belongs to land settlement.
