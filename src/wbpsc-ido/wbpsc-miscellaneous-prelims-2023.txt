Q 2 | apt | Arithmetic
When will it be double if some capital was invested at the rate of 6¼%?
(1) In 12 years
(2) In 9 years
(3) In 16 years
(4) In 8 years
= 3
~ At simple interest, money doubles when the interest equals the principal, i.e. R × T = 100. So T = 100 ÷ 6¼ = 100 ÷ 25/4 = 16 years.

Q 3 | apt | Reasoning
If 12th January of 2024 was Friday, then what will be the 7th June of 2025?
(1) Monday
(2) Thursday
(3) Saturday
(4) None of the above
= 3
~ 12 Jan 2024 to 12 Jan 2025 = 366 days (2024 is a leap year) = 2 odd days, so 12 Jan 2025 is Sunday. From 12 Jan to 7 June 2025: 19 + 28 + 31 + 30 + 31 + 7 = 146 days = 20 weeks + 6 odd days. Sunday + 6 = Saturday.

Q 5 | apt | Arithmetic
The ratio of the collection of story books of Rabi and Madhu is 2 : 3 respectively. After purchasing of 6 new books by Madhu the ratio of their books would be 1 : 3. How many books did they have earlier?
(1) 2, 4
(2) 4, 6
(3) 4, 8
(4) None of the above
= 2

Q 7 | apt | Arithmetic
How many times does 7/11 occur from 1 to 7?
(1) 7
(2) 13
(3) 9
(4) 11
= 3
~ The stretch from 1 to 7 is 7 − 1 = 6 long, and 6 ÷ 7/11 = 66/7 = 9 3/7, so 7/11 fits in 9 whole times. Equivalently, the multiples of 7/11 lying between 1 and 7 are 14/11, 21/11, …, 70/11 — 9 of them (77/11 = 7 is the end point itself).

Q 15 | apt | Arithmetic
A can complete a work in 9 days when B can complete the same work in 6 days. They can complete the work in 3 days if C joins them. How many days will C take if C is told to complete the work alone?
(1) In 15 days
(2) In 12 days
(3) In 21 days
(4) In 18 days
= 4
~ One day’s work: A + B + C = 1/3, A = 1/9, B = 1/6. So C = 1/3 − 1/9 − 1/6 = (6 − 2 − 3)/18 = 1/18, i.e. C alone takes 18 days.

Q 53 | apt | Arithmetic
A rational number is
(1) expressible in a recessing decimal.
(2) not expressible in a recessing decimal.
(3) always expressible in a recessing decimal.
(4) None of the above
= 1
~ A rational number p/q has a decimal form that either terminates (1/4 = 0.25) or recurs (1/3 = 0.333…), never non-terminating and non-recurring like √2. So it is expressible as a recurring decimal (“recessing” is a misprint for “recurring”) — option A as per WBPSC's key; option C's “always” is not accepted, since terminating decimals are not normally called recurring.

Q 63 | apt | Arithmetic
Two trains start their journeys with velocities of 60 km/hr and 30 km/hr respectively in opposite directions from two different stations 120 km apart. The train of higher velocity is stopped after 40 min of its departure. When will the other meet the first one?
(1) At 160 minutes
(2) At 130 minutes
(3) At 140 minutes
(4) None of the above
= 1
~ In 40 min the fast train covers 60 × 40/60 = 40 km and the slow one 30 × 40/60 = 20 km, leaving 120 − 60 = 60 km between them. The slow train covers this alone at 30 km/hr in 2 hr = 120 min, so they meet 40 + 120 = 160 minutes after the start.
