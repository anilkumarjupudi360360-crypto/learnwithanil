Q 1 | sci | Biology & Health
Identify the mammal/mammals from the following.
(1) Cat only
(2) Bat only
(3) Rat only
(4) All of the above
= 4
~ Cats, bats and rats are all mammals: they have hair, are warm-blooded and feed their young on milk. The bat is the only mammal capable of true flight, which is why it is sometimes wrongly taken for a bird.

Q 2 | sci | Biology & Health
Which of the following vitamin/mineral deficiency causes mental disorder in children ?
(1) Vitamin B12
(2) Vitamin A
(3) Vitamin C
(4) Iron
= 1
~ Vitamin B12 (cobalamin) is needed for the formation of the myelin sheath of nerves, so its deficiency in children causes developmental delay, neurological damage and impaired mental function. Vitamin A deficiency mainly causes night blindness and vitamin C deficiency causes scurvy.

Q 10 | geo | Physical Geography
Which of the following is organically formed rock ?
(1) Lignite
(2) Gypsum
(3) Gravel
(4) Sand Stone
= 1
~ Lignite (brown coal) is formed from compressed plant remains, so it is an organically formed sedimentary rock. Gypsum is chemically formed (by evaporation), while gravel and sandstone are mechanically formed from rock fragments.

(1) Nitrous oxide
(2) Carbon dioxide
(3) Oxygen
(4) Methane
= 3
