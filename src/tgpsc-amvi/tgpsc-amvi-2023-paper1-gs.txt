Q 1 | cur | Sports & Awards
Who won the Nobel Peace Prize for their work on literature in 2022?
(1) Greta Thunberg
(2) Annie Ernaux
(3) Malala Yousafzai
(4) Al Gore
= 2
~ The French writer Annie Ernaux won the 2022 Nobel Prize in Literature, not the Peace Prize, for the courage and clinical acuity with which she uncovers the roots of personal memory; the question mixes up the two prizes. The 2022 Peace Prize went to Ales Bialiatski (Belarus), Memorial (Russia) and the Center for Civil Liberties (Ukraine). Malala Yousafzai (2014) and Al Gore (2007) are past Peace laureates; Greta Thunberg has never won.

Q 2 | cur | International
Which international organization recently recommended that the world should cut methane emissions by 45% by 2030?
(1) United Nations
(2) World Health Organization
(3) International Monetary Fund
(4) World Meteorological Organization
= 1
~ The Global Methane Assessment released in May 2021 by the UN Environment Programme (UNEP) and the Climate and Clean Air Coalition found that human-caused methane emissions can be cut by up to 45% this decade, avoiding nearly 0.3°C of warming by 2045. Methane is short-lived but a powerful greenhouse gas. The Global Methane Pledge launched at COP26 (2021) aims at a 30% cut from 2020 levels by 2030.

Q 3 | cur | International
In which year did the United Nations adopt the Global Compact for Safe, Orderly and Regular Migration, which aims to better manage international migration?
(1) 2020
(2) 2023
(3) 2018
(4) 2014
= 3
~ The Global Compact for Safe, Orderly and Regular Migration was adopted at an intergovernmental conference in Marrakech, Morocco, on 10 December 2018 and endorsed by the UN General Assembly on 19 December 2018. It is the first intergovernmentally negotiated UN agreement covering all dimensions of international migration; it is not legally binding and rests on 23 objectives.

Q 4 | cur | International
In 2020, which country withdrew from the Open Skies Treaty, which allows unarmed surveillance flights over the territories of its signatories?
(1) Russia
(2) China
(3) United States
(4) France
= 3
~ The Open Skies Treaty, signed in 1992 and in force since 2002, lets member states fly unarmed observation flights over one another's territory to build mutual confidence. The United States announced its withdrawal in May 2020, citing Russian violations, and left on 22 November 2020. Russia followed, leaving in December 2021. China was never a party, and France remains a member.

Q 17 | sci | Biology & Health
Which of the following diseases is caused by a virus and can be prevented by vaccination?
(1) Malaria
(2) Tuberculosis
(3) Hepatitis B
(4) Dengue fever
= 3
~ Hepatitis B is caused by the hepatitis B virus, which attacks the liver and spreads through blood, sexual contact and from mother to child; a safe vaccine prevents it and is given in India's Universal Immunisation Programme, starting with a birth dose. Malaria is caused by the Plasmodium protozoan and tuberculosis by a bacterium. Dengue is viral, but no dengue vaccine was in India's programme in 2023.

Q 19 | sci | Biology & Health
Which of the following is NOT an organ of the human body?
(1) Photophores
(2) Liver
(3) Esophagus
(4) Small intestine
= 1
~ Photophores are light-producing organs found in many deep-sea fishes, squids and some crustaceans, which use bioluminescence for camouflage, signalling or attracting prey; humans have no such organ. The liver (the largest gland), the oesophagus (food pipe) and the small intestine (where most digestion and absorption take place) are all organs of the human digestive system.

Q 20 | sci | Biology & Health
Which of the following is an example of a macromolecule?
(1) Water
(2) Glucose
(3) Sodium chloride
(4) Deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA)
= 4
~ Macromolecules are very large molecules, usually polymers built from smaller units; DNA is a polymer of nucleotides whose molecular mass runs into millions of daltons. Proteins, polysaccharides such as starch, and nucleic acids are the main biological macromolecules. Water (18 u), glucose (180 u) and sodium chloride (about 58.4 u, an ionic compound) are small molecules or formula units.

Q 21 | sci | Biology & Health
Which of the following is required to increase the absorption of iron in the human body?
(1) Phytate
(2) Vitamin C
(3) Calcium
(4) Polyphenols
= 2
~ Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) increases the absorption of non-haem iron from plant foods by reducing ferric iron (Fe³⁺) to the more soluble ferrous form (Fe²⁺) and keeping it in solution in the intestine. Phytates in cereals and pulses, calcium, and polyphenols such as the tannins in tea and coffee all inhibit iron absorption, which is why tea with meals can worsen iron-deficiency anaemia.

Q 22 | env | Environment & Ecology
Which of the following is a type of ecological succession that occurs in an area that was previously devoid of life?
(1) Primary succession
(2) Secondary succession
(3) Tertiary succession
(4) Pioneer succession
= 1
~ Primary succession begins where no life or soil existed before, such as bare rock, fresh lava, newly formed sand dunes or a new pond, with pioneer species like lichens colonising first; it is very slow. Secondary succession occurs where an existing community has been destroyed but soil remains, as in abandoned farmland or a burnt forest. "Pioneer" describes the first species, not a separate type of succession.

Q 23 | sci | Chemistry
Which of the following cooking methods involves a chemical reaction that results in the formation of a new substance?
(1) Boiling corn
(2) Frying a chicken
(3) Steaming vegetables
(4) Baking a cake
= 4
~ Baking a cake is a chemical change: heat makes baking soda or baking powder release carbon dioxide, egg proteins set, and sugars and proteins undergo caramelisation and Maillard browning, producing new substances that cannot be turned back into batter. Boiling corn and steaming vegetables are mainly physical changes. Frying chicken also browns by the Maillard reaction, so the options are loosely framed, but the key takes baking, the textbook example.

(1) Patna
(2) Mumbai
(3) Jhansi
(4) Indore

Q 29 | env | Environment & Ecology

(2) Only Statement 2 is true

Q 34 | geo | Indian Geography
Which of the following statements are true regarding the monsoon climate of India?
A. It brings rainfall during the summer season
B. It is caused by the differential heating of land and water
C. It affects only the coastal regions of India
D. It is responsible for 80-90% of the rainfall in India
(1) Only statement D is true
(2) Only statements A and B are true
(3) Only statements A, B and C are true
(4) Only statements A, B and D are true
= 4
~ The south-west monsoon brings rain in summer (June–September); it arises from the differential heating of the Asian landmass and the surrounding seas, which sets up the pressure gradient that draws moist winds inland. It affects almost the whole country, not just the coasts, so C is false. The south-west monsoon alone gives about three-quarters of India's annual rainfall, and with the retreating (north-east) monsoon the share exceeds 80%. Hence A, B and D.

Q 36 | geo | Indian Geography

Q 41 | eco | Indian Economy
Match the following types of unemployment in List- I with their descriptions in List- II:
Sl No. | Types of unemployment --- List I | Sl No. | Descriptions -- List II
A | Frictional unemployment | i | Unemployment due to changes in technology or demand for goods and services
B | Structural unemployment | ii | Unemployment due to lack of skills or education
C | Cyclical unemployment | iii | Unemployment due to seasonal fluctuations in industries
D | Seasonal unemployment | iv | Unemployment due to economic downturns or recessions
(1) A - i, B - ii, C - iii, D - iv
(2) A - i, B - ii, C - iv, D - iii
(3) A - ii, B - i, C - iv, D - iii
(4) A - i, B - iv, C - ii, D - iii
= 2
~ Cyclical unemployment comes from economic downturns or recessions (C-iv) and seasonal unemployment from seasonal fluctuations in work (D-iii), which leaves options 2 and 3. Frictional unemployment is the short spell of people moving between jobs or entering the labour force; structural unemployment is a mismatch between workers' skills and the jobs available, often caused by changes in technology or in demand. The key pairs frictional with i and structural with ii. (Preliminary key; some sources differ.) Descriptions i and ii both fit structural unemployment, so option 3 can also be argued.

Q 42 | geo | Indian Geography

Q 112 | rea | Logical Reasoning
Five cousins Mugdha, Lata, Kareena, Sushma, and Radha are seated around a circular table facing the centre. Mugdha is to the immediate right of Lata. Kareena does not sit adjacent to Radha but is a neighbour of Lata.
Which of the following statement(s) is/are true based on the above conditions?
A) Kareena sits to the immediate right of Sushma.
B) Radha sits second to the right of Lata.
(1) A only
(2) B only
(3) Both A and B
(4) Neither A nor B
= 3
~ All face the centre. Mugdha is on Lata's right, so Kareena, Lata's other neighbour, is on Lata's left. Kareena's other neighbour cannot be Radha, so it is Sushma, and Radha takes the remaining seat beside Mugdha. Going round to the right: Lata, Mugdha, Radha, Sushma, Kareena. Kareena is immediately right of Sushma and Radha is second to the right of Lata, so both A and B are true.

Q 113 | rea | Logical Reasoning
Six friends Mohan, Manish, Manas, Minal, Mehak and Mohit are sitting in a straight row facing North. Mehak and Mohit are in the centre. Mohan and Manish are at the corners. Manas is sitting to the immediate left of Mohan. Who is sitting to the immediate right of Manish?
(1) Minal
(2) Manas
(3) Mehak
(4) Mohit
= 1
~ Number the seats 1 to 6 from left to right (all face north). Mehak and Mohit take seats 3 and 4; Mohan and Manish take the ends. Manas is on Mohan's immediate left, so Mohan cannot be at seat 1: Mohan is at 6 and Manas at 5. Manish is at seat 1, and the only person left, Minal, sits at seat 2, immediately to Manish's right.

Q 114 | rea | Logical Reasoning
In a row of 50 students (all are facing north), Raj is to the immediate left of Kiran whose position is 21st from the left end. Find the position of Raj from the right end.
(1) 30th
(2) 32nd
(3) 31st
(4) 29th
= 3
~ When everyone faces north, a person's left is towards the left end of the row. Kiran is 21st from the left, so Raj, immediately to his left, is 20th from the left. Position from the right = total − position from the left + 1 = 50 − 20 + 1 = 31st.

Q 115 | rea | Arithmetic & Mental Ability
If the day before yesterday was Sunday, when will Thursday be?

(2) Today
(3) Tomorrow
(4) Day after tomorrow
