(3) କ୍ରିୟାପଦ କେଉଁ ଲିଙ୍ଗକୁ ସୂଚାଉଛି
(4) କ୍ରିୟାପଦ ବାକ୍ୟର କେଉଁ ପଦକୁ ବିଭକ୍ତ କରୁଛି
= 1
~ A verb's ବିଭକ୍ତି (personal ending) is the suffix added after the root and tense marker that shows the person (ପୁରୁଷ) and number (ବଚନ) of the verb, e.g. the ଇ of ଖାଉଛି. Tense is shown by other suffixes (ଉଛ୍, ଇଲ୍, ଇବ୍), and Odia verbs do not mark gender.

(2) ପୁରୁଷ ଓ ବଚନ
(3) ବଚନ

Q 145 | odi | Grammar

Q 152 | odi | Grammar

Q 154 | odi | Grammar

Q 155 | odi | Grammar

Q 156 | odi | Grammar

Q 158 | odi | Grammar
______ ଏକ ପାଦପୂରଣାର୍ଥକ ପ୍ରତ୍ୟୟ ନୁହେଁ ।

(2) ଟି
(3) ହେ
(4) ତ
= 1
~ Expletive (pada-purana) particles such as ଟି, ହେ and ତ add no new meaning and only fill out or soften the sentence (e.g. 'ଯାଅ ହେ', 'ଆସ ତ'). ଣି is not one of them: it adds a real sense to the verb, such as completion in 'ସେ ଆସିଲାଣି' (he has already come) or likelihood in future forms (see Q155).

~ The sentence is not a real question; it expresses wonder that anyone in Odisha could fail to know Madhubabu (i.e. everyone knows him). Such an emotional, rhetorical exclamation is treated as an exclamatory (ବିସ୍ମୟସୂଚକ) sentence.

ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦେଶ (ପ୍ରଶ୍ନସଂଖ୍ୟା 196 ରୁ 200) : ଅନୁଚ୍ଛେଦଟିକୁ ସାବଧାନତା ସହକାରେ ପଢ଼ି ନିମ୍ନରେ ଦିଆଯାଇଥିବା ସଠିକ୍ ଉତ୍ତରଟି ବାଛ ।
