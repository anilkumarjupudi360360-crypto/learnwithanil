Q 30 | cur | National & International
The five principles of Panchsheel are substantially a reiteration of :
(1) The UN Charter
(2) The Charter of the League of Nations
(3) The Treaty of Berlin
(4) The Protocols of Zion

Q 32 | pol | Constitution & Polity
Who / which among the following has been given the power by the Constitution of India to impose ‘Reasonable Restrictions’ on the Fundamental Rights ?
(1) President of India
(2) Parliament
(3) Supreme Court
(4) High Courts
= 2
~ Clauses (2) to (6) of Article 19 allow reasonable restrictions only by a law made by the legislature, so the power lies with Parliament (and State legislatures on their subjects). The Supreme Court and High Courts do not impose restrictions; they only judge whether a restriction is reasonable.

Q 34 | pol | Constitution & Polity
Which of the Rights deals with Right to Vote and to be elected in India ?
(1) Fundamental Rights
(2) Natural Rights
(3) Constitutional Right
(4) Legal Right
= 3
~ The right to vote is not a Fundamental Right in Part III. It rests on Article 326 (elections on the basis of adult suffrage), with qualifications for membership in Articles 84 and 173 and the details in the Representation of the People Acts. The Supreme Court has called the rights to vote and to contest constitutional rights (PUCL, 2003; Rajbala, 2015), though older rulings such as Jyoti Basu (1982) called them statutory.

Q 35 | pol | Constitution & Polity
Who / Which of the following can abolish a State Legislative Council ?
(1) Parliament
(2) Governor
(3) President
(4) State Assembly
= 1
~ Under Article 169, Parliament may by law abolish (or create) a State Legislative Council if the State Legislative Assembly passes a resolution to that effect by a majority of its total membership and two-thirds of members present and voting. Such a law is not treated as an amendment of the Constitution under Article 368.

Q 36 | pol | Constitution & Polity

Q 38 | pol | Constitution & Polity

Q 45 | pol | Constitution & Polity

The Rajya Sabha resolution only opens the way; the service itself is created by an Act of Parliament.
