Q 1 | his | Modern India & National Movement
What is the correct sequence of the following ?
i. The Hartog Committee
ii. The Hunter Education Commission
iii. The Sadler University Commission
iv. Kothari Education Commission
(1) i, ii, iii, iv
(2) iii, iv, ii, i
(3) ii, iii, iv, i
(4) ii, iii, i, iv
= 4
~ Chronological order: Hunter Education Commission (1882) → Sadler (Calcutta) University Commission (1917–19) → Hartog Committee (1929, auxiliary committee of the Simon Commission on education) → Kothari Education Commission (1964–66). Hence ii, iii, i, iv.

Q 2 | pol | Constitution & Polity
Which of the following Articles are related to the Right to Religion ?
(1) Articles 14 to 18
(2) Articles 19 to 22
(3) Articles 25 to 28
(4) Articles 29 and 30
= 3
~ Articles 25–28 form the Right to Freedom of Religion: freedom of conscience and to profess, practise and propagate religion (25), to manage religious affairs (26), freedom from taxes for promoting a religion (27) and from religious instruction in certain institutions (28). Articles 14–18 cover equality, 19–22 freedom, and 29–30 cultural and educational rights.

Q 3 | pol | Constitution & Polity
Which election method is used for Lok Sabha elections in India ?
(1) Proportional Representation
(2) First Past The Post (FPTP)
(3) Single Transferable Vote (STV)
(4) Mixed Electoral System
= 2
~ Lok Sabha members are elected from single-member territorial constituencies under the First Past The Post (simple plurality) system: the candidate with the most votes wins, even without a majority. Proportional representation by single transferable vote is used for Rajya Sabha and for Presidential and Vice-Presidential elections.

Q 4 | geo | Environment & Ecology
What is edge species ?
(1) The species which occur abundantly in the ecotone
(2) Species on the verge of extinction
(3) Rare species
(4) Endemic species
= 1
~ An ecotone is the transition zone between two ecosystems (e.g., forest and grassland). Species that occur mostly or abundantly in this junction zone are called edge species, and the greater variety and density of organisms found there is the 'edge effect'.

Q 5 | geo | Indian & World Geography
Match the following : Composition of Atmosphere.
a. Oxygen — 1. Is one of the most variable gaseous substance present in atmosphere, constituting between 0.2 to 4 percent.
b. Nitrogen — 2. Constitutes only 21 percent of the total volume of atmosphere.
c. Ozone — 3. Among gases accounts for 78 percent of total atmospheric volume.
d. Water vapour — 4. It forms less than 0.00005 percent by volume of the atmosphere and is unevenly distributed.
(1) a-1, b-2, c-3, d-4
(2) a-2, b-3, c-1, d-4
(3) a-2, b-3, c-4, d-1
(4) a-4, b-1, c-3, d-2
= 3
~ By volume, nitrogen is about 78% of dry air and oxygen about 21%. Ozone is a trace gas (less than 0.00005%), concentrated mainly in the stratosphere. Water vapour is the most variable gas: up to about 4% in warm, humid tropics and very little over deserts and polar regions. So a-2, b-3, c-4, d-1.

Q 13 | pol | Constitution & Polity
Consider the following statements about the Prime Minister and Union Council of Ministers in India :
1. The Prime Minister must always be a member of the Lok Sabha.
2. The Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha.
3. The Prime Minister can allocate portfolios to Ministers.
4. If someone becomes a Minister or Prime Minister without being an MP, such a person has to get elected to the Parliament within six months.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct ?
(1) Only 2, 3 and 4
(2) Only 1, 2 and 3
(3) Only 1, 3 and 4
(4) Only 1, 2 and 4
= 1
~ Statement 1 is wrong: the Prime Minister may be a member of either House (e.g., Indira Gandhi in 1966 and Manmohan Singh were Rajya Sabha members). The Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha (Art. 75(3)), the PM allocates portfolios, and a non-member must become an MP within six months (Art. 75(5)).

Q 14 | pol | Constitution & Polity
What happens if the Rajya Sabha suggests Amendments to a money bill ?
(1) The Lok Sabha is bound to accept them
(2) The Lok Sabha may or may not accept them
(3) The bill is sent to a Joint Session of both the Houses
(4) The bill is automatically rejected
= 2
~ Under Article 109, the Rajya Sabha must return a money bill within 14 days with or without recommendations. The Lok Sabha may accept or reject all or any of them; either way the bill is deemed passed by both Houses. There is no joint sitting for a money bill.

Q 29 | pol | Constitution & Polity
Consider the following statements with respect to the Election Commission of India :
1. Article 324 of the Indian Constitution grants the Election Commission the power to supervise and control the conduct of elections.
2. The Election Commission of India is responsible for conducting local body elections.
3. The Election Commission was a single-member body until 1993 and thereafter it became a multi-member body permanently.
4. The Chief Election Commissioner shall not be removed from his office except in like manner and on the like grounds as a judge of the Supreme Court of India.

(2) Only 1, 3 and 4
(3) Only 1 and 3
(4) Only 2 and 4
= 1

Q 100 | eco | Indian Economy
Which of the following is NOT a component of India’s fiscal deficit ?
(1) Revenue Deficit
(2) Primary Deficit
(3) Monetary Deficit
(4) Effective Revenue Deficit
= 3
~ Fiscal deficit = total expenditure − (revenue receipts + non-debt capital receipts). Revenue deficit and effective revenue deficit (revenue deficit minus grants for creating capital assets) are parts of it, and primary deficit is fiscal deficit minus interest payments. ‘Monetary deficit’ is not a budget deficit measure (the related term ‘monetised deficit’ means the increase in the RBI's net credit to the government).
