Q 1 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
Choose the most appropriate option to complete the following series.
V, R, N, ______, F, B
(1) K
(2) H
(3) J
(4) P
= 3
~ Each letter is 4 places before the previous one: V(22) → R(18) → N(14) → J(10) → F(6) → B(2). So the missing letter is J.

Q 2 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
In a certain code language, STRAIGHT is written as TSARGITH. How will THURSDAY be written as in that language?
(1) AYSDURTH
(2) HTRUDSYA
(3) UHTDRSYA
(4) HTRUDSAY
= 2
~ The word is split into pairs and the letters of each pair are swapped: ST-RA-IG-HT → TS-AR-GI-TH. Likewise TH-UR-SD-AY → HT-RU-DS-YA, i.e. HTRUDSYA.

Q 3 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
A class of boys is standing in only one row. A boy is at thirty third position from both the ends in this sequence. How many boys are there in the class?
(1) 64
(2) 65
(3) 69
(4) 66
= 2
~ Total = (position from one end) + (position from the other end) − 1 = 33 + 33 − 1 = 65, because the boy himself is counted twice.

Q 4 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
Suraj drove his bicycle towards the north. Then he turned to the right and covered a distance of 2 km. Then, he turned to the right and covered a distance of 4 km. He found himself 2 km east of his starting point. How far did he go by bicycle in the north direction?
(1) 2 km
(2) 4 km
(3) 6 km
(4) 8 km
= 2
~ After going north x km, he turns right (east) for 2 km and right again (south) for 4 km. Ending exactly 2 km east of the start means his north–south displacement is zero: x − 4 = 0, so x = 4 km.

Q 5 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
At present, A is younger than B by 8 years. If 4 years ago, their ages were in the ratio 1 : 2, then what is the present age of B (in years)?
(1) 12
(2) 20
(3) 11
(4) 18
= 2
~ Let B = A + 8. Four years ago (A − 4) : (A + 4) = 1 : 2, so 2A − 8 = A + 4 and A = 12. Hence B = 12 + 8 = 20 years.

Q 14 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
The calendar for the year 2007 will be the same for the year
(1) 2014
(2) 2016
(3) 2017
(4) 2018
= 4
~ 2007 is a non-leap year. Odd days from 2007 to 2017 = 8 ordinary years × 1 + 3 leap years (2008, 2012, 2016) × 2 = 14 = 0 (mod 7), so 1 January 2018 falls on the same day (Monday) and 2018 is also a non-leap year. Hence 2018 has the same calendar.

Q 17 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
Patro walks 10 km towards North. From there he walks 6 km towards South. Then, he walks 3 km towards East. How far and in which direction is he with reference to his starting point?
(1) 5 km West
(2) 7 km West
(3) 7 km East
(4) 5 km North-east
= 4
~ Net northward movement = 10 − 6 = 4 km, then 3 km east. Distance = √(4² + 3²) = √25 = 5 km, and since he is north and east of the start, the direction is North-east.

Q 18 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
In a certain code language, 'cool drinks available here' is written as 'bhu man juk lop', 'available lost objects here' is written as 'gan bhu nut juk', 'be cool search objects' is written as 'vax der man nut', and 'available drinks desert search' is written as 'but juk der lop'. How will 'lost drinks' be written in that language?
(1) gan lop
(2) nut gan
(3) lop bhu
(4) juk gan
= 1
~ Compare common words: 'available' (sentences 1, 2, 4) = juk; 'here' (1, 2) = bhu; 'objects' (2, 3) = nut; so in sentence 2 'lost' = gan. 'drinks' (1, 4) shares juk and lop; juk is 'available', so 'drinks' = lop. Hence 'lost drinks' = 'gan lop'.

Q 19 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
There are seven persons A, B, C, D, E, F, and G. Each of them has different heights. C is shorter than only G. The number of persons taller than B is equal to the number of persons shorter than D. Neither A nor E is shortest. Who among the following is shortest?
(1) G
(2) D
(3) B
(4) F
= 4
~ G is the tallest (C is shorter than only G). If D were shortest, nobody would be taller than B, i.e. B would be tallest — impossible. If B were shortest, 6 would be shorter than D, making D tallest — also impossible. A and E are not shortest, so F is the shortest.

Q 31 | rea | Verbal Reasoning
In a row, Aman is at the 20th position from the left and Gita is at the 14th position from the right. If they interchange their positions, Aman becomes 30th from the left. Now, what is the position of Gita from the right?

Q 51 | math | Arithmetic
A boy read 3/8th of a book on one day and 4/5th of the remainder on another day. If there were 30 pages unread, how many pages did the book contain?
(1) 240
(2) 300
(3) 600
(4) 412
= 1
~ After day 1, 5/8 of the book remains. He reads 4/5 of it, so 1/5 of 5/8 = 1/8 of the book is left unread. 1/8 of the book = 30 pages, so the book has 30 × 8 = 240 pages.

Q 52 | math | Arithmetic
One year ago, Promila was four times as old as her daughter Sakshi. Six years hence, Promila's age will exceed her daughter's age by 9 years. The ratio of the present ages of Promila and her daughter is
(1) 9 : 2
(2) 11 : 3
(3) 12 : 5
(4) 13 : 4
= 4
~ The age gap never changes, so Promila = Sakshi + 9 now. One year ago: (S + 9 − 1) = 4(S − 1) → S + 8 = 4S − 4 → S = 4, so Promila = 13. Ratio = 13 : 4.

Q 53 | math | Arithmetic
A contract is to be completed in 46 days and 117 men were set to work, each working 8 hours a day. After 33 days, 4/7 of the work is completed. How many additional men may be employed so that the work may be completed in time, each man now working 9 hours a day?
(1) 85
(2) 90
(3) 81
(4) 80
= 3
~ 117 × 33 × 8 man-hours did 4/7 of the work. The remaining 3/7 needs 117 × 33 × 8 × (3/4) = 23,166 man-hours, to be done in 46 − 33 = 13 days at 9 hours a day: men = 23,166 ÷ (13 × 9) = 198. Additional men = 198 − 117 = 81.

Q 54 | math | Arithmetic
Robert is travelling on his cycle and has calculated to reach point A at 2 P.M. if he travels at 10 kmph; he will reach there at 12 noon if he travels at 15 kmph. At what speed must he travel to reach A at 1 P.M.?
(1) 8 kmph
(2) 11 kmph
(3) 12 kmph
(4) 14 kmph
