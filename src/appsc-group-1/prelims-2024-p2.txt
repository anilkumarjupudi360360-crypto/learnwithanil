Q 1 | men | Numbers & Series
If 808 × 8008 × 80008 = A and the sum of all the digits in the A is M, what will be the value of √M?
(1) 8
(2) 12
(3) 0
(4) 4

(1) 7827
(2) 8394

Q 4 | men | Arithmetic
Amit has to travel distances of 66 km, 88 km and 110 km on three consecutive days. He travels with the greatest possible constant speed (in km/hr) on all the three days so that he covers the respective distances in an integer number of hours on each day. How many hours will it take him to complete the journey?
(1) 8 hours
(2) 12 hours
(3) 22 hours

Q 7 | men | Reasoning
Rajani has a digital watch that shows time in a 24-hour format. The time on the watch shows 23:45. After passing 'A' minutes the watch shows the time in such a way that the four whole numbers are again arranged in ascending order from left to right. Then what will be the value of A × A?
(1) 7569
(2) 8464
(3) 6724
(4) 9604
= 4
~ Here "ascending order" means four consecutive digits rising by 1, as in 23:45 (2-3-4-5); merely rising digits would allow 23:46 and give A = 1. The next such time is 01:23 (0-1-2-3), and from 23:45 to 01:23 is 15 + 83 = 98 minutes, so A × A = 98² = 9604.

Q 8 | men | Reasoning
If E * F means 'E is the son of F'
E + F means 'E is the father of F'
E > F means 'E is the daughter of F'
E < F means 'E is the wife of F'
which of the following pair of people represents first cousins with regard to the relation given in the expressions, if it is provided that H is the sister of P?
Q > R < P + S and M * H < I + J < K + L
(1) QH
(2) MS
(3) ML
(4) MK
= 2

Q 9 | men | Reasoning

Q 10 | men | Arithmetic
Consider the following statements:
I. An equilateral triangle in a circle will have the maximum area compared to other triangles inside the same circle.
II. If the height of an equilateral triangle is 10 cm, its area will be equal to 100√3/3 cm².
III. If three sides of a triangle are 5, 12 and 13 cm, then the circumradius of the triangle is 2.5 cm.
IV. Scalene triangle is a triangle with none of the sides equal to any other sides.
Which among the above given statements are correct?
(1) I, II and III only
(2) I, II and IV only
(3) II, III and IV only
(4) All the above
= 2
~ I and IV are true; II is true because area = h²/√3 = 100/√3 = 100√3/3 cm². III is false: 5-12-13 is a right triangle, so its circumradius is half the hypotenuse = 6.5 cm, not 2.5 cm.

Q 33 | men | Arithmetic

I. A Parallelogram having all the sides equal is a Rhombus.
