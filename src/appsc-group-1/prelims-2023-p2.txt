Q 1 | men | Numbers & Series
There are nine 2-digit consecutive odd numbers. If 59 is the mean of the first five such numbers, then what is the mean of all the nine numbers ?
= 63
~ The mean of five consecutive odd numbers is the middle one, so the first five are 55, 57, 59, 61, 63; all nine are 55 to 71, whose mean is the middle (5th) number, 63.

Q 2 | men | Arithmetic
Latha went to the market to buy some vegetables. While leaving her house she checked her wallet and got to know that she had some hundred-rupee notes and some twenty-rupee notes. After shopping she had the same number of hundred-rupee notes as she originally had twenty-rupee notes and the same number of twenty-rupee notes as she originally had hundred-rupee notes. If the remaining money was one-third of what she initially had while stepping out of her house, find the initial ratio of the number of hundred-rupee notes and twenty-rupee notes.
= 7 : 1
~ Let her start with h hundred-rupee and t twenty-rupee notes. Then 100t + 20h = (100h + 20t)/3, so 300t + 60h = 100h + 20t, i.e. 280t = 40h and h : t = 7 : 1.

Q 3 | men | Reasoning
In a class, Raghu got the 11th rank and he was 31st from the bottom of the list of boys passed. Three boys did not take the examination and one failed. What is the total strength of the class ?
= 45
~ Boys who passed = 11 + 31 − 1 = 41; adding 3 who did not take the exam and 1 who failed gives 41 + 3 + 1 = 45.

Q 4 | men | Numbers & Series
124 chocolates were left when the total chocolates in a box were divided equally among 403 students. How many chocolates would remain if the chocolates from the same box are distributed equally among 31 students ?
= 0
~ Total = 403k + 124. Since 403 = 13 × 31 and 124 = 4 × 31, the total is a multiple of 31, so nothing remains.

Q 5 | men | Numbers & Series
A shopkeeper has three cloth pieces of lengths 7 m, 3 m 85 cm and 12 m 95 cm respectively. He measures each of the three cloth pieces with a rod at his disposal and finds that the length of each cloth piece is an integral multiple of the length of the rod. What can be the maximum length of the rod ?
= 35 cm
~ The rod length is the HCF of 700 cm, 385 cm and 1295 cm. 700 = 2² × 5² × 7, 385 = 5 × 7 × 11 and 1295 = 5 × 7 × 37, so the HCF is 5 × 7 = 35 cm.

Q 6 | men | Arithmetic
100 students were to participate in school games. 60 students participated in game 1, while 50 students participated in game 2. 10 students did not come on time, so they were disqualified. How many students participated in only one of the games ?
= 70
~ 100 − 10 = 90 students took part, so 60 + 50 − (both) = 90 gives both = 20. Only one game = 90 − 20 = 70.

Q 7 | men | Arithmetic
In a rural market there are two types of vendors, X and Y. 70 percent of the vendors are X, and 60% of the X earn ₹50,000 or more in a month. Total number of vendors who earn ₹50,000 or more per month in the rural market is 48 percent of the total vendors. What percentage of Y earn less than ₹50,000 ?
= 80%
~ X earning ₹50,000 or more = 60% of 70% = 42% of all vendors, so Y contributes 48% − 42% = 6% of all vendors. Y is 30% of vendors, so 6/30 = 20% of Y earn ₹50,000 or more and 80% of Y earn less.

Q 8 | men | Reasoning
Select the missing numbers from the given alternatives.
17 | 12 | 11
47 | 43 | ?
4 | 5 | 6
= 55, 49
~ Top number × (3, 4, 5) minus the bottom number gives the middle number: 17 × 3 − 4 = 47 and 12 × 4 − 5 = 43, so ? = 11 × 5 − 6 = 55 − 6 = 49. The key prints both 55 (the product 11 × 5) and 49 (the final value).

Q 9 | men | Numbers & Series
If we listed all numbers from 100 to 10,000, how many times would the digit 5 be printed ?
= 3980
~ From 0 to 9999 each of the four digit places shows 5 exactly 1000 times, giving 4000. Numbers 0 to 99 contain 20 fives (10 in the units place, 10 in the tens place) and 10,000 has none, so the count is 4000 − 20 = 3980.

Q 11 | men | Numbers & Series
Find the wrong term in the given series.
7, 12, 40, 222, 1742, 17390, 208608
= 1742
~ The rule is multiply by 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 and subtract 2, 8, 18, 32, 50, 72 (2n²): 7×2−2 = 12, 12×4−8 = 40, 40×6−18 = 222, 222×8−32 = 1744, 1744×10−50 = 17390, 17390×12−72 = 208608. So 1742 should be 1744.

Q 12 | men | Arithmetic
Excluding stoppages, the speed of a bus is 84 kmph and including stoppages, it is 70 kmph. For how many minutes does the bus stop per hour ?
= 10 minutes
~ Distance lost per hour due to stops = 84 − 70 = 14 km, which takes 14/84 hour at running speed = 1/6 hour = 10 minutes.

Q 14 | men | Reasoning
A child made a big cube by joining 64 smaller red cubes. He then painted the faces of the big cube in yellow colour, except one face. His teacher came and dismantled all the cubes and asked him to count the number of cubes whose two faces are yellow. If the child answered correctly, then how many cubes did he count ?
= 20
~ The big cube is 4 × 4 × 4. The 8 edges lying between two painted faces each have 2 middle cubes with two yellow faces (16), and the 4 corners on the unpainted face each have two yellow faces (4), giving 16 + 4 = 20.

Q 15 | men | Arithmetic
In a school sports competition, 40% students participated in shooting, 35% students participated in wrestling, 32% students participated in boxing, 9% students participated in shooting and wrestling, 11% students participated in boxing and wrestling, 8% students participated in shooting and boxing and 5% students participated in all the three sports. If 64 students did not participate in any sport, then find the total number of students in the school.
= 400
~ Students in at least one sport = 40 + 35 + 32 − 9 − 11 − 8 + 5 = 84%, so 16% took part in none. 16% = 64 means the total is 64 × 100/16 = 400.

Q 17 | men | Reasoning
What can be the maximum sum of Saturdays and Sundays in a normal year ?
= 105
~ A normal year has 365 days = 52 weeks + 1 day, so there are 52 Saturdays and 52 Sundays plus at most one extra Saturday or Sunday: 52 + 52 + 1 = 105.

Q 18 | men | Reasoning
If in a particular non-leap year, the month of June had 5 Mondays, then that year must have started with which one of the following days ?
= None of the above
~ June has 30 days (4 weeks + 2 days), so 5 Mondays means 1 June is a Sunday or a Monday. From 1 January to 1 June in a non-leap year is 151 days = 21 weeks + 4 days, so 1 January was a Wednesday or a Thursday; no single day is certain.

Q 19 | men | Numbers & Series
How many pairs of positive integers m, n satisfy the following equation :
1/m + 4/n = 1/12,
where n is an odd integer less than 60 ?
= 3
~ m = 12n/(n − 48), so n must be an odd number from 49 to 59. Only n = 49 (m = 588), n = 51 (m = 204) and n = 57 (m = 76) give whole numbers, so there are 3 pairs.

Q 20 | men | Numbers & Series
If x = – 0·5, then which of the following has the smallest value ?
= 1/x
~ 1/x = 1/(−0·5) = −2, which is smaller than x itself (−0·5) and smaller than any positive expression of x such as x² (0·25) or −x (0·5).

Q 21 | men | Arithmetic
Two liquids A and B are in the ratio 5 : 1 in container 1 and 1 : 3 in container 2. In what ratio should the contents of the two containers be mixed so as to obtain a mixture of A and B in the ratio 2 : 1 ?
= 5 : 2
~ Fraction of A is 5/6 in container 1, 1/4 in container 2 and 2/3 in the mixture. By alligation the ratio is (2/3 − 1/4) : (5/6 − 2/3) = 5/12 : 2/12 = 5 : 2.

Q 22 | men | Reasoning
There are 6 members A, B, C, D, E and F in a family who are spread across 3 generations. There are two couples in the family and no one from the third generation is married. E is the wife of C. F and B are a couple and the only people belonging to their generation. There are 4 male members in the family.
Which of the following must be true ?
= There is no female in the third generation.
~ The two couples (C–E and F–B) give 2 men and 2 women, so the unmarried members A and D must both be male to make 4 males. The third generation is unmarried, so it can contain only A and D, and therefore has no female.

Q 23 | men | Numbers & Series
Let N be the greatest number that will divide 6360, 7420 and 9540, leaving the same remainder in each case. Then the sum of the digits of N is :
= 7
~ N is the HCF of the differences 7420 − 6360 = 1060, 9540 − 7420 = 2120 and 9540 − 6360 = 3180, which is 1060. Sum of digits = 1 + 0 + 6 + 0 = 7.

Q 25 | men | Arithmetic
The circumference of the front wheel of a cart is 30 feet and that of the back wheel is 36 feet. What is the distance travelled by the cart, when the front wheel has done five more revolutions than the rear wheel ?
= 900 feet
~ For distance D, D/30 − D/36 = 5, i.e. D × (6/1080) = 5, so D = 5 × 180 = 900 feet.

Q 26 | men | Numbers & Series
Ramesh wants to plant 372 tomato plants, 558 chilli plants and 1116 onion plants in equal rows. Also, he wants to make distinct rows of each plant, i.e. every row should have only one kind of plant. What are the minimum number of rows that are required ?
= 11
~ Plants per row = HCF(372, 558, 1116) = 186. Rows = 372/186 + 558/186 + 1116/186 = 2 + 3 + 6 = 11.

Q 27 | men | Numbers & Series
The total number of integers between 400 and 600, each of which, either begins with 5 or ends with 5 but not both, is
= 100
~ Begin with 5: 500 to 599 = 100 numbers; end with 5: 405, 415, ..., 595 = 20 numbers; both: 505, 515, ..., 595 = 10 numbers. Either but not both = 100 + 20 − 2 × 10 = 100.

Q 28 | men | Arithmetic
A shopkeeper is selling his goods at 40% discount, but is still able to make 20% profit on the list price. By what percentage does he mark up his goods ?
= 100%
~ Taking the 20% profit on cost: cost = 100 gives selling price 120. After a 40% discount the selling price is 60% of the marked price, so marked price = 120/0·6 = 200, a 100% mark-up over cost.

Q 29 | men | Arithmetic
Anand and Rishi are the two biggest competitors of chess. The probability of Rishi losing a game against Anand is 75%. What is the probability that Anand loses at least one out of the four games played between them ?
= 175/256
~ Anand wins a game with probability 3/4, so he wins all four with probability (3/4)⁴ = 81/256. The probability that he loses at least one is 1 − 81/256 = 175/256.

Q 30 | men | Arithmetic
A tank has eight pipes, some of which fill the tank and the rest empty the tank. Each inlet pipe fills the tank in 8 hours while each outlet pipe empties it in 6 hours. If all the pipes are kept open when the tank is full, it takes 6 hours to drain the tank. How many pipes are inlet pipes ?
= Four
~ With i inlet pipes, (8 − i)/6 − i/8 = 1/6. Multiplying by 24: 4(8 − i) − 3i = 4, so 32 − 7i = 4 and i = 4.

Q 31 | men | Arithmetic
Ajeet and Sujeet go for shopping in a mall. They use an escalator to move between various floors. The escalator moves at a constant speed. Ajeet takes nine steps for every five of Sujeet’s steps. Ajeet takes 30 steps to reach the top of the escalator, while Sujeet takes 20 steps to reach the top. If the escalator was turned off, how many steps would they have to take to walk up ?
= 80 steps
~ In one time unit Ajeet takes 9 steps and Sujeet 5, so Ajeet is on the escalator for 30/9 units and Sujeet for 20/5 = 4 units. With escalator speed e steps per unit, 30 + (10/3)e = 20 + 4e gives e = 15, so total steps = 20 + 4 × 15 = 80.

Q 32 | men | Arithmetic
A beats B by 15 seconds in a 200 m race, B beats C by 25 seconds in a 500 m race, C beats D by 32 seconds in an 800 m race and D beats E by 35 seconds in a 1 km race. What must be the speed of A in order to beat E by 800 m in a 2 km race ?
= 3·33 m/s
~ Time gaps grow in proportion to distance, so over 2 km A leads E by 150 + 100 + 80 + 70 = 400 s. Beating E by 800 m means E needs these 400 s to cover 800 m, so E runs 2 m/s and takes 1000 s for 2 km; A takes 600 s, i.e. 2000/600 = 3·33 m/s.

Q 33 | men | Arithmetic
5 persons will live in a tent. If each person requires 16 m² of floor area and 100 m³ space for air, then the height of the cone of smallest size to accommodate these persons would be
= 18·75 m
~ Base area = 5 × 16 = 80 m² and volume = 5 × 100 = 500 m³. For a cone, volume = (1/3) × base area × height, so 500 = (1/3) × 80 × h and h = 1500/80 = 18·75 m.

Q 35 | men | Arithmetic
A change-making machine contains one-rupee, two-rupee and five-rupee coins. The total number of coins is 300. The amount is ₹960. If the number of one-rupee coins and two-rupee coins are interchanged, the value comes down by ₹40. The total number of five-rupee coins is
= 140
~ With x one-rupee, y two-rupee and z five-rupee coins: x + 2y + 5z = 960 and 2x + y + 5z = 920 give y = x + 40. Using x + y + z = 300 gives z = 260 − 2x; substituting, 3x + 80 + 5(260 − 2x) = 960, so x = 60, y = 100 and z = 140.

Q 36 | men | Reasoning
P, Q, R and S are four statements. Relation between these statements is as follows :
I. If P is true, then Q must be true.
II. If Q is true, then R must be true.
III. If S is true, then either Q is false or R is false.
Which of the following must be true ?
= If P is true, then S is false
~ If P is true, then Q is true (I), so R is true (II). Since both Q and R are true, S cannot be true, because III says S being true makes Q or R false. So S is false.

Q 37 | men | Arithmetic
A decorative lamp has 2 brown, 3 green and 4 white bulbs. What is the probability of lighting three bulbs such that at least one bulb that glows is white ?
= 37/42

Q 43 | men | Reasoning
Kranthi is facing west. She then turns 45 degrees in the clockwise direction and then another 180 degrees in the same direction and then 270 degrees in the anti-clockwise direction. Which direction is she facing now ?
= South-west
~ From west, 45° clockwise gives north-west, a further 180° clockwise gives south-east, and 270° anti-clockwise from south-east (to north-east, north-west, then south-west) gives south-west.

Q 44 | men | Arithmetic
A watch loses 90 seconds in an hour and the watch owner adjusts the time lost by the watch in one day at the end of 48 hours. After how many days will the watch show the correct time before the watch owner adjusts the time ?
= 40 days
~ The watch loses 90 s × 48 = 72 min in every 48 hours and the owner then puts back one day's loss (36 min), so it falls a net 36 min behind every 2 days; the key takes 720 min (12 hours) ÷ 36 = 20 cycles = 40 days, when the watch is exactly 12 hours behind after the 20th adjustment. Read strictly as "before the owner adjusts", it is already 18 × 36 + 72 = 720 min behind just before the 19th adjustment. Official key: 40 days; the working above gives 38 days.

Q 46 | men | Arithmetic
There are 6 tasks and 6 people. Task 1 cannot be assigned either to person 1 or to person 2; task 2 must be assigned to either person 3 or person 4. Every person is to be assigned one task. In how many ways can the assignment be done ?
= 144
~ Task 2 can go to person 3 or 4 (2 ways); task 1 then goes to one of the 3 remaining people other than persons 1 and 2 (3 ways); the other 4 tasks go to the remaining 4 people in 4! = 24 ways, so 2 × 3 × 24 = 144.

Q 47 | men | Reasoning
In a row of 40 girls, when Ankitha was shifted to her left by four places, her number from the left end of the row becomes 10. What was the number of Kanchana from the right end of the row if Kanchana was three places to the right of Ankitha’s original position ?
= 24
~ After moving 4 places left Ankitha is 10th from the left, so she was originally 14th; Kanchana, 3 places to her right, is 17th from the left, i.e. 40 − 17 + 1 = 24th from the right.

Q 48 | men | Numbers & Series
Vaccination drive for Covid-19 is carried out in a village of 1000 people. After the 5th member of Group E, the pattern repeats, starting from 1 member in Group A and so on. The last beneficiary to be vaccinated belongs to
= Group D
~ One cycle of Groups A to E takes 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 15 people; 1000 = 15 × 66 + 10, and the 10th person of a cycle falls in Group D (positions 7 to 10).

Q 49 | men | Arithmetic
Ramesh said to his daughter, “I am four times as old as you now. Five years from now, I will be thrice as old as you.” His daughter asked him, “Ten years from now, how many times my age would you be ?”
What would Ramesh’s answer be ?
= 2·5 times

Q 50 | men | Arithmetic
In a badminton tournament between schools A and B, school A won 50% of the matches played in the first three weeks. At the end of the tournament, its success rate was 75%. If 6 matches were played in the first three weeks and school A won all its matches played after that, how many matches did the tournament have ?
= 12
~ School A won 3 of the first 6 matches; if it then won all n later matches, (3 + n)/(6 + n) = 3/4 gives n = 6, so the tournament had 6 + 6 = 12 matches.

Q 51 | men | Arithmetic
The ratio of jobs done by 30 girls to the job done by 25 boys, in the same time period is 5 : 6. If 9 girls and 10 boys can finish a job in 40/13 days, then how many girls are required to complete the job in 6 days ?
= 12
~ 30 girls : 25 boys = 5 : 6 means one girl : one boy = 25 : 36, so 10 boys equal 14·4 girls and 9 girls + 10 boys = 23·4 girls. The job is 23·4 × 40/13 = 72 girl-days, so 72 ÷ 6 = 12 girls are needed.

Q 53 | men | Reasoning
There are two different ways of going from city A to city B. There are 3 different ways of going from city B to city C. There are two different ways of going from city C that are not passing through city B.
How many different ways of going from city A to city C are there ?
= 6
~ Each of the 2 routes from A to B can be followed by any of the 3 routes from B to C, giving 2 × 3 = 6; the two other routes start from city C, so they add no new route from A to C.

Q 55 | men | Arithmetic
The Compound interest on a certain sum for 2 years is ₹1,512 and Simple interest is ₹1,440. If the sum is invested such that the Simple interest is ₹2,592 and the number of years is equal to the rate of interest per annum, then find the rate ?
= 6%
~ SI of ₹1,440 for 2 years is ₹720 a year; CI − SI = ₹72 = 720 × r/100, so r = 10% and the sum is ₹7,200. Then 7,200 × R × R/100 = 2,592 gives R² = 36, so the rate is 6%.
