Q 1 | ih | Medieval India
Vijnanesvara wrote Mitakshara at the court of the Chalukya emperor Vikramaditya VI. It was
= A commentary on the law book of Yajnavalkya
~ Vijnanesvara's Mitakshara, written at the court of the Kalyani Chalukya king Vikramaditya VI, is a commentary on the Yajnavalkya Smriti. It became the basis of the Mitakshara school of Hindu law (inheritance and joint family) followed in most of India except Bengal (Dayabhaga).

~ I-Tsing (Yijing) was a Chinese Buddhist pilgrim who came to India in the late 7th century CE and studied at Nalanda, nearly a thousand years after Ashoka (3rd century BCE), so this pair is wrong.

Q 34 | pol | Constitution Basics & Preamble

Q 35 | pol | Social Justice & Welfare
Social justice enables the Courts to uphold legislation, except
= To enact unquestionable laws to protect certain people of the society.
~ Social justice lets courts uphold laws that favour weaker sections (reservations, land reforms), but no law can be made immune from judicial review, which is part of the basic structure.

Q 40 | pol | State Government
The orders passed by the Governor of a State (Article 163) of a State fall in four categories. Find out the category which is not subject to Judicial Review.

Q 41 | pol | Union Executive

~ Even during a National Emergency the President must act on the advice of the Council of Ministers. The 42nd Amendment made that advice binding under Article 74(1), and the 44th Amendment only lets him return it once for reconsideration, so an emergency gives him no independent discretion.

Q 42 | pol | Constitution Basics & Preamble

= Fundamental Rights and Directive Principles
~ The Preamble cannot be enforced in court, but it is a key to interpreting the Constitution and can be used to work out the scope of Fundamental Rights and Directive Principles where the text is unclear.

Q 44 | pol | Federalism & Centre-State Relations
Which of the following are not the federal features of the Indian Constitution ?
= Office of the Governor
~ The Governor is appointed by the President and holds office at his pleasure, which gives the Centre control over the States; it is a unitary feature, while division of powers, a written constitution and an independent judiciary are federal features.

Q 45 | pol | Judiciary
What option does the government exercise and legislators have when they do not like a decision made by the Supreme Court ?
= Both are options, pass a constitutional amendment that circumvents the decision or pass a law challenging the decision
~ The legislature can amend the Constitution (e.g., the 1st Amendment after Champakam Dorairajan, the 24th after Golak Nath) or pass a law that removes the basis of a judgment, subject to judicial review.

Q 46 | pol | State Government
Regarding the disagreement between the two Houses of the State Legislature, which of the following is correct ?
= The only power of the Council is to interpose some delay in the passage of the bill
~ Under Article 197 the Legislative Council can hold an ordinary bill for at most three months the first time and one month the second time; there is no joint sitting and the Assembly's will prevails.

Q 47 | pol | Amendments & Governance
Choose the correct answer from the following :
= A member of an All India Service can be dismissed or removed only by the Union government.
~ Although All India Service officers serve under the States, the All India Services (Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 1969 let only the Central Government dismiss, remove or compulsorily retire them; the State Government can suspend an officer and impose the other penalties.

Q 48 | pol | Union Executive
Regarding the powers of the President, which of the following is correct ?
= Courts are debarred from invalidating any legislation on the ground that the President's previous sanction was not obtained.
~ Under Article 255, recommendation and previous-sanction requirements are matters of procedure only; an Act is not invalid merely because the President's previous sanction was missing if the President later gave assent.

Q 49 | pol | Union Executive
Match the following :
a. Reprieve    I. Reduce the amount of sentence without changing its character
b. Respite    II. Substitute one form of punishment for other of a lighter character
c. Remission    III. Awarding lesser sentence
d. Commutation    IV. A stay of execution of sentence
= a-IV, b-III, c-I, d-II
~ Reprieve is a stay of execution; respite is awarding a lesser sentence for special reasons (such as pregnancy); remission reduces the term without changing its nature; commutation substitutes a lighter form of punishment.

Q 50 | pol | Union Executive
Regarding the pardoning powers, choose the correct answer.
= The only authority for pardoning a sentence of death is the President.
~ Under Article 72 only the President can pardon a death sentence; the Governor (Article 161) can suspend, remit or commute it but cannot pardon it.

Q 51 | pol | Parliament

= Adjournment sine die means terminating a sitting for an indefinite period.
~ Adjournment sine die, done by the presiding officer, ends a sitting without fixing a day for the next meeting; prorogation ends a session and dissolution ends the life of the Lok Sabha.

Q 58 | pol | Federalism & Centre-State Relations

Q 60 | pol | Federalism & Centre-State Relations
