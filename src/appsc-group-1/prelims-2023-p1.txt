Q 1 | ih | Ancient India
During the Indus Valley Civilization, the beads manufacturing industry was located at
= Chanhudaro
~ Chanhudaro in Sindh was a specialised bead-making centre of the Harappan civilisation; drills, unfinished beads and furnaces for carnelian beads were found there.

Q 2 | ih | Ancient India
Select the correct statements about religious beliefs of the Rig Vedic Period :
I. Dyus was a celestial God of the Rig Vedic Aryans similar to Jupiter of the Greeks.
II. Prithvi was considered as the mother of all Gods.
III. Agni was worshipped as the intermediary of taking the offerings to the deities.
IV. Cow sacrifice was also the part of worship.
= I, III and IV only
~ Dyaus (Dyaus Pitar, the sky father) corresponds to Greek Zeus/Roman Jupiter, Agni carried the sacrificial offerings to the gods, and animal (including cattle) sacrifice formed part of Vedic ritual. The mother of the gods in the Rig Veda is Aditi, not Prithvi, so II is wrong.

Q 3 | ih | Ancient India
Match the following schools of philosophies with their theories :
a. Sankhya    I. Predetermined fate theory
b. Vaisheshika    II. World was formed by the blending of nature
c. Lokayata    III. Considered Atom as basis of the world
d. Ajivika    IV. Advocated materialistic ideology
Choose the correct answer using the codes given below :
= a-II, b-III, c-IV, d-I
~ Sankhya explains the world as evolving from Prakriti (nature) in union with Purusha; Vaisheshika (Kanada) is the atomist school; Lokayata (Charvaka) is materialist; the Ajivikas of Makkhali Gosala held that everything is fixed by fate (niyati).

Q 4 | ih | Ancient India
Hathigumpha inscription of Kharavela gave the details of irrigation facilities provided in the Kalinga Region by
= Mahapadmananda
~ The Hathigumpha inscription records that Kharavela extended into his capital a canal first dug by "Nandaraja" some three centuries earlier, a Nanda king generally identified with Mahapadma Nanda.

Q 5 | ih | Ancient India
Select the correct statements regarding the inscriptions of Ashoka :
I. They are in Brahmi, Kharosthi, Greek and Aramaic scripts.
II. The Monarch’s name was mentioned as Ashoka only in Maski and Gurjara inscriptions.
III. The XIII Rock edict mentioned the names of the five Greek Rulers as his contemporaries.
IV. The IV and V edicts mentioned the war of succession with his brothers.
= I, II and III only
~ Ashoka's edicts are in Brahmi, Kharosthi, Greek and Aramaic; most call him only "Devanampiya Piyadasi", and the personal name Ashoka appears in minor rock edicts such as Maski and Gujarra (Nittur and Udegolam, found later, also name him); Rock Edict XIII names five Greek kings including Antiochus and Ptolemy. No edict describes a war of succession with his brothers, so IV is wrong. (Official key; some sources differ.)

Q 7 | ih | Ancient India
Which of the following was not the feature of the Guilds during the Gupta Period ?
= Non-Autonomous Bodies
~ Gupta-period guilds (shrenis) were largely autonomous: they framed their own rules, had their own seals, accepted deposits and could even maintain militia, so being non-autonomous was not their feature.

III. Kalidasa – Malavikagnimitra

Q 12 | ih | Medieval India

b. Kudumba    II. Lottery system

Q 33 | pol | Union Executive
Consider the following Assertion (A) and Reason (R) and choose the correct code :

Q 34 | pol | Constitutional & Statutory Bodies

I. It is a statutory body.
II. Its members are drawn from administrative background only.

Q 35 | pol | State Government
Which of the following statements is not one of the powers of the Speaker of the Legislative Assembly ?
= He has no veto except in case of a tie & The Speaker will preside when a resolution for his removal from office is under consideration of the Assembly.
~ The Speaker has only a casting vote in case of a tie, not a veto (Article 189), and under Article 181 the Speaker cannot preside while a resolution for his removal is under consideration, so the key accepts both statements.

Q 36 | pol | Constitutional & Statutory Bodies
The powers of the Election Commission include which of the following ?
I. The Election Commission takes decisions on every aspect of conduct and control of elections from the announcement of elections to the declaration of results.
II. During the election period, the Election Commission can order the Government to dismiss some government officials.
III. When on election duty, Government officers work under the control of the Election Commission.
Choose the correct answer using the codes given below :
= I and III only
~ Under Article 324 the Commission superintends and controls every stage of the election, and officials on election duty are deemed to be on deputation to it (Section 28A, RP Act 1951) and work under its control; but it has no power to order the government to dismiss officials.

Q 37 | pol | Judiciary
What is/are the provision(s) to safeguard the autonomy of the Supreme Court of India ?
I. While appointing the Supreme Court Judges, the President of India has to consult the Chief Justice of India.
II. The Supreme Court Judges can be removed by the Chief Justice of India only.
III. The salaries of the Judges are charged on the Consolidated Fund of India on which the legislature does not have to vote.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct ?
= I and III only
~ Article 124(2) requires consultation with the Chief Justice in appointing judges, and their salaries are charged on the Consolidated Fund of India (non-votable). Judges are removed only by the President on an address of both Houses of Parliament (Article 124(4)), not by the Chief Justice.

Q 40 | pol | Parliament
Consider the following Assertion (A) and Reason (R) and choose the correct code :
Assertion (A) :
The Parliament of India consists of the President and two Houses.
Reason (R) :
The President is a part of the legislature even though he does not sit in Parliament except for the purpose of delivering his opening address.

Q 41 | pol | Fundamental Rights & Duties

I. To protect the weaker sections from social injustice.
II. To develop scientific temper and spirit of inquiry.
III. To preserve the rich heritage of our composite culture.

Q 43 | pol | Constitution Basics & Preamble

Q 45 | pol | Constitution Basics & Preamble
Consider the following statements :

II. The Preamble is a part of the Constitution but has no legal effect independently of other parts.

Q 48 | pol | Amendments & Governance
Consider the following statements regarding GST :

Q 53 | pol | Constitutional & Statutory Bodies

Q 54 | pol | Constitutional & Statutory Bodies
With reference to the Protection of Human Rights (Amendment) Act, 2019, consider the following statements about National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) :
