Q 1 | sur | Surveying
Which of the following set of terms does not relate to operation of a theodolite?
(1) Transiting and inverting
(2) Face left and face right
(3) Right swing and left swing
(4) Gauging and sounding
= 4
~ Transiting (plunging) the telescope, face left/face right observations and swinging the telescope right or left are all theodolite operations. Gauging (measuring stream discharge) and sounding (measuring water depth) belong to hydrographic surveying, not to theodolite work.

Q 3 | sur | Surveying
Error due to inclination of line of collimation in levelling across a river can be eliminated by
(1) Reversion
(2) Reciprocal ranging
(3) Reciprocal levelling
(4) Keeping level in middle
= 3
~ Across a river the level cannot be set up midway between the two points, so reciprocal levelling is used: readings are taken with the level near each bank in turn and the mean of the two apparent differences of level cancels the collimation error (and curvature and refraction).

Q 4 | sur | Surveying

(1) 845.7 m
(2) 837.3 m
(3) 841.6 m
(4) 839.4 m

Q 5 | sur | Surveying
In any closed traverse, if the survey work is error free, then
I. The algebraic sum of all the latitudes should be equal to zero
II. The algebraic sum of all the departures should be equal to zero
III. The sum of the northings should be equal to the sum of the southings
Which of the above statements are correct?
(1) I and II
(2) I and III
(3) II and III
(4) I, II and III
= 4
~ For an error-free closed traverse the closing error is zero, so ΣL = 0 and ΣD = 0. ΣL = 0 simply means the sum of northings (+ latitudes) equals the sum of southings (− latitudes), so all three statements hold.

Q 6 | sur | Surveying
Consider the following statements:
Reciprocal leveling eliminates the effect of
I. Errors due to earth's curvature
II. Errors due to atmospheric refraction
III. Mistakes in taking levelling staff readings
IV. Error due to line of collimation
Which of these statements are correct?
(1) I, II and III
(2) I, III and IV
(3) II, III and IV
(4) I, II and IV
= 4
~ In reciprocal levelling the true difference of level is the mean of the two apparent differences, so the errors of curvature, refraction (assumed equal in both observations) and collimation cancel out. Mistakes in reading the staff are blunders and are not eliminated by any method.

For the loads shown on the truss, the statements that correctly represents the nature of forces in the members of the truss is:

Q 20 | som | Strength of Materials

Q 21 | som | Strength of Materials
A circular solid shaft of span L = 5 m is fixed at one end and free at the other end. A twisting moment T = 100 kN-m is applied at the free end. The torsional rigidity GJ is 50000 kN-m²/rad. Following statements are made for this shaft:
1. The maximum rotation is 0.01 rad
2. The torsional strain energy is 1 kN-m
With reference to the above statements, which of the following applies?
(1) Both statements are true
(2) Statement 1 is true but 2 is false
(3) Statement 2 is true but 1 is false
(4) Both statements are false
= 2

Q 23 | som | Strength of Materials

Q 35 | hyd | Irrigation & Water Resources
______(i)______ evaporates water, and ______(ii)______, by causing and controlling winds, circulates the evaporated water vapour, and thus, helping in its ______(iii)______ at different places.

(2) Sun, Coriolis force, filtration
(3) Sun, cycle, precipitation

= 4
~ In the hydrologic cycle the sun's heat evaporates water; the winds, deflected and steered by the Coriolis force of the earth's rotation, circulate the vapour, which condenses and falls again as rain or snow (reprecipitation) at other places. Filtration plays no part.

Q 36 | env | Water Supply & Sanitary Engineering
In a domestic wastewater sample, COD and BOD were measured. Generally, which of the following statement is true for their relative magnitude?
(1) COD = BOD
(2) COD > BOD
(3) COD < BOD
(4) None of the above
= 2
~ COD uses a strong chemical oxidant (acid potassium dichromate) and oxidises both biodegradable and non-biodegradable organic matter, while BOD measures only the part that microbes can oxidise during the test period. Hence for domestic wastewater COD is greater than BOD.

Q 37 | env | Water Supply & Sanitary Engineering

(2) Turbid surface water - Sedimentation - Coagulation - Flocculation - Filtration - Disinfection - Storage and Supply
