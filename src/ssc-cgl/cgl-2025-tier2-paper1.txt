Q 1 | qa | Mensuration
The diagonals of three faces of a rectangular parallelepiped that meet at a single corner measure √5 cm, √10 cm, and √13 cm. What is the volume of this parallelepiped?
(1) 12 cm³
(2) 24 cm³
(3) 6 cm³
(4) 18 cm³
= 3
~ Let the edges be a, b, c. The face diagonals give a² + b² = 5, b² + c² = 10 and c² + a² = 13. Adding, 2(a² + b² + c²) = 28, so a² + b² + c² = 14. Then c² = 14 − 5 = 9, a² = 14 − 10 = 4 and b² = 14 − 13 = 1, so a = 2, b = 1, c = 3 cm. Volume = abc = 2 × 1 × 3 = 6 cm³.

Q 2 | qa | Geometry
In a triangle ΔPQR, ∠R = 62°. The perpendicular bisector of PQ at S meets QR at T. If ∠TPR = 38°, what is the measure of ∠PQR?
(1) 60°
(2) 80°
(3) 35°
(4) 40°
= 4
~ Every point on the perpendicular bisector of PQ is equidistant from P and Q, so TP = TQ and triangle TPQ is isosceles: ∠TPQ = ∠TQP = ∠PQR. Hence ∠P = ∠TPQ + ∠TPR = ∠Q + 38°. Angle sum in ΔPQR: (∠Q + 38°) + ∠Q + 62° = 180°, so 2∠Q = 80° and ∠PQR = 40°. Check: ∠P = 78°, and 78° + 40° + 62° = 180°.

Q 3 | qa | Data Interpretation & Statistics
A number is chosen at random from the set {1, 2, 3, ..., 120}. What is the probability that the number chosen is divisible by 6 or 8 but not divisible by 24?
(1) 25/120
(2) 19/120
(3) 21/120
(4) 23/120
= 4
~ From 1 to 120 there are 20 multiples of 6, 15 multiples of 8 and 5 multiples of both (that is, of LCM 24). Divisible by 6 or 8 = 20 + 15 − 5 = 30. All 5 multiples of 24 are in this group, so removing them leaves 25 numbers (6, 8, 12, 16, 18, 30, …, 114), which gives 25/120 = 5/24. SSC's key marks 23/120, which this count does not support. (SSC key; some sources differ.)

Q 4 | qa | Geometry
In an equilateral triangle, the circumcenter coincides with which of the following?
(1) A point outside the triangle
(2) The midpoint of one of its sides
(3) The centroid of the triangle
(4) One of the vertices
= 3
~ In an equilateral triangle the perpendicular bisector of each side passes through the opposite vertex, so it is also a median, an altitude and an angle bisector. Hence the circumcentre, centroid, orthocentre and incentre all fall at one point, two-thirds of the way along each median from the vertex. The circumcentre lies outside only in an obtuse triangle and at the midpoint of the hypotenuse in a right triangle.

Q 5 | qa | Trigonometry & Heights
What is the value of 3/2 (cos 39°/sin 51°) − √(sin²39° + sin²51°)?
(1) 3/2
(2) 7/8
(3) 5/6
(4) 1/2
= 4
~ The angles are complementary: 39° + 51° = 90°, so sin 51° = sin(90° − 39°) = cos 39°. Then cos 39°/sin 51° = 1 and the first term is 3/2 × 1 = 3/2. Also sin²39° + sin²51° = sin²39° + cos²39° = 1, whose square root is 1. Value = 3/2 − 1 = 1/2.

Q 6 | qa | Data Interpretation & Statistics
A set of n values x₁,x₂,...,xₙ has a standard deviation of 5. The standard deviation of n values x₁−7,x₂−7,...,xₙ−7 will be:
(1) 5
(2) 0
(3) 10
(4) 7
= 1
~ Standard deviation measures the spread of values about their mean. Subtracting the same constant 7 from every value lowers the mean by 7 as well, so each deviation (xᵢ − 7) − (x̄ − 7) = xᵢ − x̄ is unchanged. Hence the SD stays 5 (and the variance stays 25). SD changes only when every value is multiplied or divided by a constant k; it then gets multiplied by |k|.

Q 8 | qa | Mensuration
A rectangle is such that its area is 48 cm² and its perimeter is 28 cm. If a rhombus is formed by joining the midpoints of the sides of this rectangle, what is the area of the rhombus?
(1) 12 cm²
(2) 24 cm²
(3) 48 cm²
(4) 20 cm²
= 2
~ From the perimeter, l + b = 14, and lb = 48, so the sides are 8 cm and 6 cm. Joining the midpoints of a rectangle's sides gives a rhombus whose diagonals equal the rectangle's sides, 8 cm and 6 cm. Area of rhombus = ½ × d₁ × d₂ = ½ × 8 × 6 = 24 cm². In general this rhombus always has half the area of the rectangle (48/2 = 24).

Q 9 | qa | Geometry
If a chord of a circle subtends an angle of 60° at the circumference of the circle, then what is the ratio of the radius of the circle and the length of the chord respectively?
(1) 1:2
(2) 3:4
(3) 2 : √5
(4) 1 : √3
= 4
~ The angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference, so the chord subtends 120° at the centre. In the isosceles triangle formed by the centre and the chord's ends, chord = 2r sin(120°/2) = 2r sin 60° = 2r × √3/2 = r√3. So radius : chord = r : r√3 = 1 : √3. (Equivalently, chord = 2R × sine of the inscribed angle.)

Q 10 | qa | Mensuration
A hemispherical tank is full of water. It is connected to a pipe that empties it at a rate of 7 litres per second. How much approximate time (in minutes) will it take to empty half the tank if the internal radius of the tank is 2.1 meters? (Use π = 22/7 and 1 m³=1000 liters)
(1) 16 minutes
(2) 11 minutes
(3) 46 minutes
(4) 23 minutes
= 4
~ Volume of the hemisphere = (2/3)πr³ = (2/3) × (22/7) × 2.1³ = 19.404 m³ = 19,404 litres. Half the tank = 9,702 litres. At 7 litres per second this takes 9702/7 = 1386 seconds = 1386/60 = 23.1 minutes, that is about 23 minutes. Emptying the whole tank would take about 46 minutes, which is the trap option.

Q 11 | qa | Geometry
In a right-angled triangle ABC, the angle at B is 90°. If AB = 6 cm and BC = 8 cm, what is the distance between the centroid G and the orthocenter H?
(1) 5 cm
(2) 10/3 cm
(3) 8 cm
(4) 20/3 cm
= 2
~ In a right-angled triangle the altitudes meet at the right-angle vertex, so the orthocentre H is B. Hypotenuse AC = √(6² + 8²) = 10 cm, and the median from B to the midpoint M of AC equals half the hypotenuse, BM = 5 cm. The centroid lies on this median two-thirds of the way from the vertex: BG = (2/3) × 5 = 10/3 cm. So GH = 10/3 cm (5 cm is the distance to the circumcentre M).

Q 12 | qa | Algebra
If 3x − ky + 8 = 0 and 9x − 18y + 20 = 0 have no solution, find k.
(1) 9
(2) 10
(3) 2
(4) 6
= 4
~ A pair a₁x + b₁y + c₁ = 0, a₂x + b₂y + c₂ = 0 has no solution (parallel lines) when a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂. Here a₁/a₂ = 3/9 = 1/3 and b₁/b₂ = (−k)/(−18) = k/18, so k/18 = 1/3 and k = 6. Check: c₁/c₂ = 8/20 = 2/5, which is not 1/3, so the lines are parallel and distinct and the system has no solution.

Q 13 | qa | Mensuration
A sphere of radius 21 cm is cut into 8 identical pieces by making three mutually perpendicular cuts through its center (one along each axis). Find the total surface area of each piece in cm².
(1) 625.25π cm²
(2) 560π cm²
(3) 450.75π cm²
(4) 551.25π cm²
= 4
~ Each piece is one-eighth of the sphere. Its curved part = 4πr²/8 = πr²/2 = 441π/2 = 220.5π cm². Each cut also exposes flat faces: every piece has three quarter-circles of radius 21 cm, area 3 × πr²/4 = 3 × 441π/4 = 330.75π cm². Total surface area = 220.5π + 330.75π = 551.25π cm². Forgetting the flat faces gives only 220.5π.

Q 14 | qa | Algebra
If x + 1/x = 2√3, then find the value of x³ + 1/x³.
(1) 10√3
(2) 6√3
(3) 24√3
(4) 18√3
= 4
~ Use the identity x³ + 1/x³ = (x + 1/x)³ − 3(x + 1/x). Here (2√3)³ = 8 × 3√3 = 24√3 and 3 × 2√3 = 6√3. So x³ + 1/x³ = 24√3 − 6√3 = 18√3. The option 24√3 is the trap: it is only the cube of (x + 1/x), without subtracting 3(x + 1/x).

Q 15 | qa | Ratio, Average & Mixtures
The expenditure of a company on five heads A, B, C, D and E is in proportion to 3:5:7:11:? respectively. The expenditure on E is equal to the arithmetic mean of the expenditures on A and D. If the expenditure on C exceeds that on B by ₹18,000, find the total expenditure (₹) of the company.
(1) ₹2,40,000
(2) ₹2,97,000
(3) ₹1,80,000
(4) ₹2,52,000
= 2
~ E is the mean of A and D: (3 + 11)/2 = 7 parts, so the ratio is 3 : 5 : 7 : 11 : 7 and the total is 33 parts. C exceeds B by 7 − 5 = 2 parts = ₹18,000, so one part = ₹9,000. Total expenditure = 33 × 9,000 = ₹2,97,000.

Q 16 | qa | Ratio, Average & Mixtures
A, B, and C invest in a business in the ratio 2:3:5. After 4 months, A increases his capital by 50%, B decreases his capital by 1/3, and C leaves the capital unchanged. At the end of the year, if the total profit is Rs. 33,000, find the share of B.
(1) Rs. 7,700
(2) Rs. 8,000
(3) Rs. 9,000
(4) Rs. 10,500
= 1
~ Profit is shared in the ratio of capital × time. A: 2 for 4 months, then 3 (50% more) for 8 months = 8 + 24 = 32. B: 3 for 4 months, then 2 (one-third less) for 8 months = 12 + 16 = 28. C: 5 × 12 = 60. Ratio 32 : 28 : 60 = 8 : 7 : 15 (total 30). B's share = 7/30 × 33,000 = Rs. 7,700.

Q 17 | qa | Trigonometry & Heights
Simplify: (tan²θ − sin²θ)/(2 + tan²θ + cot²θ)
(1) sec⁶θ
(2) sin⁶θ
(3) cos²θ
(4) sin²θ
= 2
~ Numerator: tan²θ − sin²θ = sin²θ(1/cos²θ − 1) = sin²θ × tan²θ = sin⁴θ/cos²θ. Denominator: 2 + tan²θ + cot²θ = (tanθ + cotθ)², and tanθ + cotθ = 1/(sinθ cosθ), so it equals 1/(sin²θ cos²θ). Dividing: (sin⁴θ/cos²θ) × sin²θ cos²θ = sin⁶θ. Check with θ = 45°: (1 − ½)/(2 + 1 + 1) = 1/8 = (1/√2)⁶.

Q 19 | qa | Time, Work, Speed & Distance
P and Q can do a piece of work in 20 days and 30 days, respectively. They work together for 6 days, and then Q is replaced by R. If the rest of the work is completed by P and R in 8 more days, in how many days can R alone complete 60% of the work?
(1) 28 days
(2) 34 days
(3) 64 days
(4) 48 days
= 4
~ P does 1/20 and Q 1/30 of the work per day, together 1/12. In 6 days they finish 6/12 = 1/2. P and R finish the other 1/2 in 8 days, so P + R = 1/16 per day and R = 1/16 − 1/20 = 1/80 per day. R alone needs 80 days for the whole work, so 60% of it takes 0.6 × 80 = 48 days.

Q 20 | qa | Mensuration
The base of a right pyramid is an equilateral triangle with side 10 cm, and its vertical height is 24 cm. Find the volume (in cm³) of the pyramid.
(1) 100√3 cm³
(2) 400√3 cm³
(3) 150√3 cm³
(4) 200√3 cm³
= 4
~ Area of the equilateral-triangle base = (√3/4) × side² = (√3/4) × 100 = 25√3 cm². Volume of a pyramid = ⅓ × base area × height = ⅓ × 25√3 × 24 = 200√3 cm³ (about 346.4 cm³). Q 21 | qa | Percentage, Profit & Interest
At what rate of interest per annum will a sum of ₹15,625 amount to ₹19,683 in 1 year 6 months, if the interest is compounded half-yearly?
(1) 20%
(2) 8%
(3) 16%
(4) 18%

Q 22 | qa | Data Interpretation & Statistics
If the marks scored by the students in a class test out of 50 then, Find the Median of the following data:
Marks | Frequency
0-10 | 5

20-30 | 6

Q 25 | qa | Mensuration
The base of a right prism is an isosceles trapezium with parallel sides measuring 10 cm and 20 cm, and non-parallel sides measuring 13 cm each. The volume of the prism is 3240 cm³. What is the total surface area of the prism?
(1) 1940 cm²
(2) 1368 cm²
(3) 1620 cm²
(4) 2120 cm²
= 2
~ Each slant side overhangs by (20 − 10)/2 = 5 cm, so the trapezium's height = √(13² − 5²) = 12 cm. Base area = ½ × (10 + 20) × 12 = 180 cm², so the prism's length = 3240/180 = 18 cm. Q 26 | qa | Number System & Simplification
How many integers in the set {1, 2, 3......, 100} have exactly 3 divisors?
(1) 2
(2) 8
(3) 4
(4) 6
= 3
~ If n = p₁^a × p₂^b × …, it has (a + 1)(b + 1)… divisors. This equals 3 only when n = p² for a prime p, with divisors 1, p and p². Squares of primes up to 100 are 4, 9, 25 and 49 (the next, 11² = 121, is too large). So 4 integers have exactly 3 divisors.

Q 30 | qa | Trigonometry & Heights
If sinθ/x = cosθ/y, then sinθ − cosθ is:
(1) (x − y)/√(x² + y²)
(2) (5x − y)/(3√(x² + y²))
(3) (x − y)/(2√(x² + y²))
(4) (x − y)/√(3x² + 5y²)
= 1
~ Let sinθ/x = cosθ/y = k, so sinθ = kx and cosθ = ky. Since sin²θ + cos²θ = 1, k²(x² + y²) = 1 and k = 1/√(x² + y²) (taking θ acute, as usual). Then sinθ − cosθ = k(x − y) = (x − y)/√(x² + y²). Equivalently, tanθ = x/y gives sinθ = x/√(x² + y²) and cosθ = y/√(x² + y²).

Q 32 | rea | Coding-Decoding
In a certain language, COMPUTER is coded as OCPMTURE. How is KEYBOARD coded in that code?
(1) EKYOBARD
(2) EKBYAODR
(3) KYEOBARD
(4) KYEOBADR
= 2
~ Split the word into pairs of letters and swap the two letters of each pair: CO → OC, MP → PM, UT → TU, ER → RE, which gives OCPMTURE. Split KEYBOARD the same way into KE, YB, OA, RD and swap each pair: EK, BY, AO, DR. So KEYBOARD is coded as EKBYAODR. Q 33 | rea | Coding-Decoding

Q 37 | rea | Analogy & Classification
Select the option that is related to the fifth number in the same way as the second number is related to the first number and the fourth number is related to the third number:
4 : 49 :: 6 : 81 :: 8 : ?
(1) 187
(2) 176
(3) 145
(4) 121
= 4
~ Add 3 to the first number and square the result: (4 + 3)² = 7² = 49 and (6 + 3)² = 9² = 81. Applying the same rule to 8 gives (8 + 3)² = 11² = 121. The other options cannot be correct because 187, 176 and 145 are not even perfect squares.

Q 38 | rea | Series
In the following letter sequence, each group follows a specific pattern. Identify the group of letters that should come next in the sequence.
ZYA, WVD, TSG, QPJ, ?
(1) MNM
(2) MON
(3) NMM
(4) OMN
= 3

Q 110 | eco | Planning, Growth & Budget

(1) Revenue deficit
(2) Fiscal deficit
(3) Primary deficit
(4) Budget deficit
= 3

Q 138 | comp | Computer Basics & Hardware

(1) Lower power consumption
(2) Ease of instruction set updates
(3) Faster execution speed
(4) Reduced physical gate count
= 2
~ A microprogrammed control unit generates control signals from microinstructions stored in a control memory (ROM), so instructions can be added or changed simply by rewriting the microprogram; this flexibility suits complex (CISC) instruction sets. Q 142 | comp | Computer Basics & Hardware

(2) To encrypt data before transmission
(3) To provide error detection using parity bits
(4) To prevent data loss by managing buffer overflow
= 4
~ Hardware handshaking is flow control done over extra wires of a serial (RS-232) interface. Q 149 | comp | Software & Operating Systems
