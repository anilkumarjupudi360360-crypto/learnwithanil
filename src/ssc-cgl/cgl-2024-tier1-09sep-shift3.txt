Q 1 | rea | Analogy & Classification
Select the option that is related to the fifth letter-cluster in the same way as the second letter-cluster is related to the first letter-cluster and the fourth letter-cluster is related to the third letter-cluster.
CWE:ZFX ::PYT:MDI :: KIL:?
(1) PRO
(2) RTQ
(3) PQO
(4) STQ
= 2
~ Each letter of the second cluster is the opposite letter of the first cluster's letter (A↔Z, B↔Y …, positions adding to 27) moved 2 places forward. CWE: C → X → Z, W → D → F, E → V → X gives ZFX. PYT: P → K → M, Y → B → D, T → G → I gives MDI. Apply the same to KIL: K → P → R, I → R → T, L → O → Q. So KIL is related to RTQ.

Q 2 | rea | Series
Which of the following terms will replace the question mark (?) in the given series?
MFT, NIS, PLQ, SON, WRJ, ?
(1) BUP
(2) BVD
(3) BUE
(4) RUE
= 3
~ Take each letter position separately. First letters M, N, P, S, W move +1, +2, +3, +4, so the next move is +5: W + 5 = B (counting on past Z). Second letters F, I, L, O, R move +3 each: R + 3 = U. Third letters T, S, Q, N, J move −1, −2, −3, −4, so the next is J − 5 = E. The missing term is BUE; BUP and BVD fail in the second or third letter and RUE in the first.

Q 3 | rea | Non-verbal Reasoning
Six letters P, Q, R, S, T and U are written on different faces of a dice. Two positions of this dice are shown in the figure below. Find the letter on the face opposite to R.
(1) U
(2) Q
(3) S
(4) T
= 3
! img/c24s3-q3.png
~ R is on the front face in both positions, so every letter seen beside it is on a face adjacent to R: P and T in the first position, Q and U in the second. A face is never adjacent to its own opposite face. Of the six letters, only S has not appeared next to R, so S is on the face opposite R. U, Q and T are all adjacent to R and cannot be opposite it.

Q 4 | rea | Analogy & Classification
Three of the following numbers are alike in a certain way and one is different. Pick the odd one out.
(NOTE : Operations should be performed on the whole numbers, without breaking down the numbers into its constituent digits. E.g. 13 – Operations on 13 such as adding /deleting /multiplying etc., to 13 can be performed. Breaking down 13 into 1 and 3 and then performing mathematical operations on 1 and 3 is not allowed.)
(1) 36 – 84
(2) 33 – 72
(3) 66 – 154
(4) 72 – 168
= 2
~ In three pairs the numbers are in the ratio 3 : 7, i.e. second number = first number × 7/3. 36 = 12 × 3 and 84 = 12 × 7; 66 = 22 × 3 and 154 = 22 × 7; 72 = 24 × 3 and 168 = 24 × 7. For 33 – 72, 33 = 11 × 3 would need 11 × 7 = 77, not 72. So 33 – 72 is the odd one out.

Q 5 | rea | Non-verbal Reasoning

(1) Y
(2) M
(3) Q
(4) A
= 4
! img/c24s3-q5.png

Q 7 | rea | Mathematical Operations
Which two numbers (not digits) should be interchanged to make the given equation correct?
24 ÷ 3 − 4 × 2 + 6 × 5 + 12 = 50
(1) 4 and 3
(2) 5 and 4
(3) 3 and 2
(4) 24 and 12
= 4
~ Interchange 24 and 12: 12 ÷ 3 − 4 × 2 + 6 × 5 + 24 = 4 − 8 + 30 + 24 = 50, which is correct (division and multiplication first, by BODMAS). The given form equals 8 − 8 + 30 + 12 = 42. Q 11 | rea | Miscellaneous Reasoning
The following Venn diagram shows Men’s profession as Doctors and Chess Players.
How many men are doctors?
(1) 47
(2) 34
(3) 23
(4) 24
= 1
! img/c24s3-q11.png
~ Men who are doctors lie in the overlap of the Men and Doctors circles. This overlap has two parts: 23 (men who are doctors but not chess players) and 24 (the central region: men who are doctors and also chess players). Q 12 | rea | Miscellaneous Reasoning
The position of how many letters will remain unchanged if each of the letter in the word ‘JANITOR’ is arranged in English alphabetical order?
(1) Zero
(2) Three
(3) One
(4) Two
= 1
~ The letters of JANITOR in alphabetical order are A, I, J, N, O, R, T. Compare position by position with J, A, N, I, T, O, R: 1st J/A, 2nd A/I, 3rd N/J, 4th I/N, 5th T/O, 6th O/R and 7th R/T. No position holds the same letter before and after the arrangement, so the number of letters whose position remains unchanged is zero.

Q 13 | rea | Analogy & Classification
Four letter-clusters have been given, out of which three are alike in some manner and one is different. Select the letter-cluster that is different.
Note: The odd one out is not based on the number of consonants/vowels or their position in the letter cluster.
(1) PSUV
(2) BEHI
(3) FIKL
(4) TWYZ
= 2
~ Look at the gaps between consecutive letters. PSUV: P + 3 = S, S + 2 = U, U + 1 = V. FIKL: F + 3 = I, I + 2 = K, K + 1 = L. TWYZ: T + 3 = W, W + 2 = Y, Y + 1 = Z. These three follow the pattern +3, +2, +1. BEHI: B + 3 = E, E + 3 = H, H + 1 = I, i.e. +3, +3, +1. So BEHI is the odd one out.

Q 14 | rea | Series
Select the combination of letters that when sequentially placed in the blanks of the given series will complete the series.
P _ A A _ _ Z A _ V _ _ A _ _
(1) ZVPAPZAV
(2) ZPVVAPVZ
(3) VZAAPVPZ
(4) VPZZPVAP
= 1
~ Putting Z, V, P, A, P, Z, A, V in the blanks gives P Z A A V | P Z A A V | P Z A A V, the block PZAAV repeated three times. The printed letters fit this block: P in place 1, A A in places 3–4, Z A in places 7–8, V in place 10 and A in place 13. Any other option breaks the repetition; for example ZPVVAPVZ gives P Z A A P V Z A V …, which has no repeating block.

(1) BR 27
(2) CQ 25
(3) CR 25

Q 16 | rea | Coding-Decoding
In a certain code language, ‘SILK’ is written as ‘TIML’ and ‘INSECT’ is written as ‘IOTEDU’. How will ‘CHANGE’ be written in that language?
(1) DEIHAO
(2) DOIHAE
(3) DIAOHE
(4) DEIOHA
= 3
~ Compare letter by letter. SILK → TIML: S → T, L → M, K → L (+1 each) while I stays I. INSECT → IOTEDU: N → O, S → T, C → D, T → U (+1 each) while I and E stay unchanged. So every vowel is kept and every consonant moves one letter forward. CHANGE: C → D, H → I, A stays, N → O, G → H, E stays, giving DIAOHE.

Q 17 | rea | Mathematical Operations
What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following equation, if ‘+’ and ‘−’ are interchanged and ‘×’ and ‘÷’ are interchanged?
321 − 371 ÷ 22 + 216 × 6 = ?
(1) 7848
(2) 7884
(3) 8747
(4) 8447
= 4
~ After interchanging the signs, the expression becomes 321 + 371 × 22 − 216 ÷ 6. By BODMAS, do the multiplication and division first: 371 × 22 = 8162 and 216 ÷ 6 = 36. Then 321 + 8162 − 36 = 8483 − 36 = 8447. Hence 8447 comes in place of the question mark; the other options are look-alike numbers built from the digits 4, 7 and 8.

Q 18 | rea | Analogy & Classification
In the following number-pairs, the second number is obtained by applying certain mathematical operations to the first number. Select the set in which the numbers are related in the same way as are the numbers of the following sets.
(NOTE : Operations should be performed on the whole numbers, without breaking down the numbers into its constituent digits. Q 19 | rea | Blood Relations & Directions
In a certain code language,
A + B means ‘A is the son of B’,
A − B means ‘A is the sister of B’,
A × B means ‘A is the brother of B’ and
A ÷ B means ‘A is the mother of B’.
Based on the above, how is T related to P if ‘P ÷ Q × R − M + T’?
(1) Brother
(2) Husband
(3) Father
(4) Son
= 2
~ Decode step by step. P ÷ Q: P is the mother of Q. Q × R: Q is the brother of R. R − M: R is the sister of M. M + T: M is the son of T. So Q, R and M are siblings and P is their mother, while T is also a parent of M. Since P is the mother, T must be the father of M, which makes T the husband of P.

Select the number from the given options to complete the series.
35, 38, 43, 50, 59, ______
(1) 68
(2) 70
(3) 62
(4) 78
= 2

Q 26 | geo | Indian Geography

Q 27 | geo | World & Physical Geography
Which of the following is the largest crustal plate on Earth with an area of over 103,000,000 km²?
(1) African plate
(2) South American plate
(3) Eurasian plate
(4) Pacific plate
= 4
~ The Pacific plate is the largest of the Earth's major lithospheric plates, at about 103 million km². Q 28 | cur | Static GK & Culture
‘Nuakhai’ is the state festival of:
(1) Odisha
(2) Tamil Nadu
(3) Meghalaya
(4) Bihar
= 1
~ Nuakhai ("nua" = new, "khai" = eat) is the harvest festival of Odisha, celebrated most widely in western Odisha around Sambalpur. It falls on the fifth day of the bright fortnight of Bhadrapada (August–September), the day after Ganesh Chaturthi. Families first offer the newly harvested rice (nabanna) to the deity, Goddess Samaleswari in Sambalpur, then eat it together, and the festival ends with Nuakhai Juhar, greetings exchanged with elders and friends.

Q 29 | sci | Chemistry
Which of the following is a straight-chain alkyl carboxylic acid with the chemical formula CH₃CH₂CH₂CO₂H?
(1) Methanoic acid
(2) Ethanoic acid
(3) Propionic acid
(4) Butyric acid
= 4
~ CH₃CH₂CH₂CO₂H has four carbon atoms in a straight chain ending in a –COOH group, so it is butanoic acid, commonly called butyric acid (C₃H₇COOH). It gives rancid butter and vomit their unpleasant smell. The other acids have fewer carbons: methanoic (formic) acid is HCOOH, ethanoic (acetic) acid is CH₃COOH, and propionic (propanoic) acid is CH₃CH₂COOH. In IUPAC names the parent alkane gives the stem and the suffix is "-oic acid".

Q 31 | pol | Constitution & Rights
Which Article of the Indian Constitution prohibits providing of any religious instruction in the educational institutions maintained by the State?
(1) Article 29
(2) Article 28
(3) Article 30
(4) Article 31
= 2

Q 36 | sci | Chemistry
Which of the following statements is INCORRECT?
(1) Fats and oils get reduced over time and smell bad.
(2) Antioxidants are added to foods containing fats and oil to prevent oxidation.
(3) Chips manufacturers usually flush bags of chips with Nitrogen to prevent the chips from getting ranicid.
(4) Fats and oils are oxidised, they become rancid.
= 1
~ Fats and oils left exposed to air are oxidised, not reduced, and become rancid: their smell and taste change. This is why statement 1 is the incorrect one. As the NCERT Class 10 chapter on chemical reactions explains, antioxidants are added to foods containing fats and oils, airtight containers slow down oxidation, and chips manufacturers flush packets with an unreactive gas such as nitrogen to keep oxygen out and prevent rancidity.

Q 37 | ih | Modern India
Under which of the following Acts was the power to rule India, transferred from the English East India Company to the British Crown?
(1) Regulating Act 1773
(2) Pitt's India Act 1784
(3) Government of India Act 1858
(4) Charter Act of 1833
= 3
~ After the Revolt of 1857, the Government of India Act 1858 ended Company rule and transferred the power to govern India to the British Crown. A Secretary of State for India, assisted by a 15-member Council of India, took charge, and the Governor-General also became the Viceroy, Lord Canning being the first. The Regulating Act 1773 began parliamentary control, Pitt's India Act 1784 set up the Board of Control, and the Charter Act 1833 ended the Company's trade.
