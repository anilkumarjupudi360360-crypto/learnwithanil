Q 1 | rea | Series
Select the letter-cluster from among the given options that can replace the question mark (?) in the following series.
QAZ, RBY, SCX, TDW, ?
(1) UBS
(2) UEV
(3) JKS
(4) VSA
= 2
~ Track each position separately. First letters: Q, R, S, T move one step forward, so the next is U. Second letters: A, B, C, D move one step forward, so the next is E. Third letters: Z, Y, X, W move one step backward, so the next is V. The missing cluster is UEV. UBS has the right first letter but breaks the second and third patterns; JKS and VSA fit none of them.

Q 2 | rea | Miscellaneous Reasoning
Choose the address that is the same as the one given below.
Flat No. 12B, Sunrise Towers,Park Street, Kolkata,West Bengal - 700016
(1) Flat No. 12B, Sunrise Towers, Park Street, Kolkata, Bihar - 700016
(2) Flat No. 12B, Sunrise Towers, Park Street, Kolkata, Jharkhand - 700016
(3) Flat No. 12B, Sunrise Towers, Park Street, Kolkata, Odisha - 700016
(4) Flat No. 12B, Sunrise Towers, Park Street, Kolkata, West Bengal - 700016
= 4
~ Compare the options part by part with the given address. Flat No. 12B, Sunrise Towers, Park Street, Kolkata and the PIN code 700016 are the same in all four options, so the state decides the answer. The given address says West Bengal; options 1, 2 and 3 change it to Bihar, Jharkhand and Odisha. Only option 4 keeps every element, including West Bengal, so it is the same address.

Q 4 | rea | Syllogism & Statements
Consider the following statement and choose the option that follows.
Statement: The railway authority decided to reduce ticket prices on long-distance trains.
Assumptions:
I. Lower prices will attract more passengers.
II. The authority wants to compete with airlines.
(1) Only I is implicit
(2) Only II is implicit
(3) Both are implicit
(4) Neither is implicit
= 1
~ An assumption is what the decision-maker must take for granted. A fare cut makes sense only if the authority expects cheaper tickets to draw more passengers, so I is implicit. The statement says nothing about airlines; the cut could aim at buses, road travel or simply higher occupancy, so competing with airlines is only one possible motive and II is not implicit. Hence only I is implicit.

Q 5 | rea | Syllogism & Statements
Consider the following statement and choose the option that follows.
Statements:
I. Banks have increased staff training on cyber security.
II. There has been a surge in cyber attacks.
(1) I is the cause and II is the effect.
(2) II is the cause and I is the effect.
(3) Both are unrelated.
(4) No relation.
= 2
~ A surge in cyber attacks is an event that pushes organisations to respond, and increasing staff training on cyber security is a natural response to it. So statement II (the surge in attacks) is the cause and statement I (more training) is the effect. The reverse is illogical, because training staff cannot cause a surge in cyber attacks; and the two are clearly linked, so options 3 and 4 are wrong.

Q 6 | rea | Series
Find the WRONG term in the given series.
5, 10, 15, 26, 37, 50
(1) 50
(2) 26
(3) 15
(4) 5
= 3
~ The terms follow n² + 1: 2² + 1 = 5, 3² + 1 = 10, 4² + 1 = 17, 5² + 1 = 26, 6² + 1 = 37, 7² + 1 = 50. The third term should be 17, so 15 is wrong. Check with differences: the correct series 5, 10, 17, 26, 37, 50 has differences 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 (consecutive odd numbers), which 15 breaks.

Q 7 | rea | Mathematical Operations
Replace special characters with mathematical signs as given in options to make it meaningful equation.
16 # 4 @ 2 = 62
(1) ÷, −
(2) −, ÷
(3) ×, −
(4) −, ×
= 3
~ Put each pair in place of # and @ and apply BODMAS. Option 1: 16 ÷ 4 − 2 = 2. Option 2: 16 − 4 ÷ 2 = 14. Option 3: 16 × 4 − 2 = 64 − 2 = 62, which matches. Option 4: 16 − 4 × 2 = 8. So # = × and @ = −, giving 16 × 4 − 2 = 62.

Q 8 | rea | Mathematical Operations
Which two signs should be swapped to correct the following?
28 ÷ 4 × 3 − 5 + 7 = 19
(1) ÷ and ×
(2) × and −
(3) ÷ and +
(4) + and −
= 4
~ As given, 28 ÷ 4 × 3 − 5 + 7 = 21 − 5 + 7 = 23, not 19. Swap + and −: 28 ÷ 4 × 3 + 5 − 7 = 21 + 5 − 7 = 19, which is correct. Other swaps fail, for example × and − gives 28 ÷ 4 − 3 × 5 + 7 = 7 − 15 + 7 = −1, and ÷ and × gives 28 × 4 ÷ 3, which is not even a whole number.

Q 9 | rea | Mathematical Operations
By interchanging the given two signs which of the following equation will be NOT correct?
+ and −
I. 18 ÷ 3 + 8 × 5 − 4 = 20
II. 6 + 8 × 9 ÷ 3 − 4 = 14
(1) Neither I nor II
(2) Both I and II
(3) Only II
(4) Only I
= 2
~ Interchange + and − and solve using BODMAS. I becomes 18 ÷ 3 − 8 × 5 + 4 = 6 − 40 + 4 = −30, not 20. II becomes 6 − 8 × 9 ÷ 3 + 4 = 6 − 24 + 4 = −14, not 14 (a sign slip here is tempting). Neither equation becomes correct after the interchange, so both I and II are NOT correct.

Q 10 | rea | Blood Relations & Directions
In a family, there are 6 members P, Q, R, S, T and U.
• P and Q are a married couple
• R is the son of P
• T is the sister of R
• Q is the mother of S

Who is the father of S?
(1) P
(2) Q
(3) U
(4) R
= 1
~ Q is the mother of S, so Q is female. P and Q are a married couple, so P is Q's husband and therefore the father of S (R, T and S are the children of P and Q). U is P's brother, which makes him S's uncle, not father. Q is the mother, and R is S's brother, so neither can be the father.

Q 12 | rea | Syllogism & Statements
Read the following statement carefully and identify the conclusion that follows.
Statement: The library will remain closed on Sunday due to renovation.
I. The renovation is planned for Sunday.
II. People cannot use the library this Sunday.
(1) Only Conclusion I follows
(2) Only Conclusion II follows
(3) Both I and II follow
(4) Neither I nor II follows
= 3
~ The library is closed on Sunday because of renovation, which means renovation work is scheduled for that Sunday, so conclusion I follows. Q 13 | rea | Syllogism & Statements
Read the following statement carefully and identify the conclusion that follows.
Statement: The price of petrol has increased due to international oil prices.
Conclusions:
I. Petrol prices are linked to global markets.
II. The government has no control over fuel prices.
(1) Only Conclusion I follows
(2) Only Conclusion II follows
(3) Both I and II follow
(4) Neither I nor II follows
= 1
~ Since petrol became costlier because of international oil prices, domestic petrol prices clearly depend on global markets, so conclusion I follows. The statement does not say the government is powerless; governments can still influence fuel prices through taxes, duties or subsidies. Q 14 | rea | Analogy & Classification
Find the option that best completes the analogy. DP : XZ :: EN : ?
(1) YZ
(2) YB
(3) ZB
(4) YX
= 4
~ Use letter positions (A = 1 … Z = 26). In DP : XZ, D (4) + 20 = X (24) and P (16) + 10 = Z (26). Apply the same steps to EN: E (5) + 20 = Y (25) and N (14) + 10 = X (24). So EN : YX. YZ and YB begin with the right letter Y but do not apply +10 to N, and ZB fits neither step.

(1) SGALA

Q 19 | rea | Miscellaneous Reasoning
In a 30 L mixture, milk: water = 3:2. How much water must be added to make the ratio 1:1?
(1) 6 L
(2) 10 L
(3) 9 L
(4) 12 L
= 1
~ Milk = 3/5 × 30 = 18 L and water = 2/5 × 30 = 12 L. Adding water does not change the milk, so for a 1:1 ratio water must also become 18 L. Water to be added = 18 − 12 = 6 L. Check: 18 : 18 = 1 : 1. Adding 10 L would give 18 : 22, which is not equal.

Q 20 | rea | Miscellaneous Reasoning
Find the LCM of 12, 18, 30?
(1) 90
(2) 180
(3) 60
(4) 120
= 2
~ Write prime factors: 12 = 2² × 3, 18 = 2 × 3², 30 = 2 × 3 × 5. The LCM takes the highest power of each prime: 2² × 3² × 5 = 4 × 9 × 5 = 180. The other options fail: 90 is not divisible by 12, and 60 and 120 are not divisible by 18.

Q 21 | rea | Mathematical Operations
If 10 @ 2 = 120 and 5 @ 5 = 100, what is 8 @ 3?
(1) 80
(2) 96
(3) 108
(4) 110
= 4
~ The rule is a @ b = (a + b) × 10. Check: 10 @ 2 = (10 + 2) × 10 = 120 and 5 @ 5 = (5 + 5) × 10 = 100. So 8 @ 3 = (8 + 3) × 10 = 110. A product rule does not fit both examples (10 × 2 = 20 and 5 × 5 = 25 would need different multipliers), so the sum rule is the consistent one.

Q 22 | rea | Blood Relations & Directions

(1) Daughter

Q 24 | rea | Miscellaneous Reasoning
If a man’s present age is twice what it was 10 years ago, what is his current age?
(1) 20
(2) 30
(3) 40
(4) 25
= 1
~ Let the present age be x. Ten years ago he was x − 10. Given x = 2(x − 10), so x = 2x − 20, which gives x = 20. Check: ten years ago he was 10, and 20 is twice 10. With 30, ten years ago he would be 20, and 30 is not twice 20, so 30 is wrong.

Q 25 | rea | Series
The series given below contains a sequence of numbers. Accordingly identify the incorrect combination 6, 7, 16, 51, 210
(1) 210
(2) 16
(3) 7
(4) 51
= 1
~ The pattern is × n + n with n = 1, 2, 3, 4: 6 × 1 + 1 = 7, 7 × 2 + 2 = 16, 16 × 3 + 3 = 51, and 51 × 4 + 4 = 208. The last term should be 208, so 210 is the incorrect term. The first four terms fit the rule exactly, so 7, 16 and 51 are correct.

Q 26 | cur | Static GK & Culture
The Nabanna (new rice) during the Nuakhai festival in Sambalpur, Odisha, is first offered to which deity?
(1) Maa Samaleswari
(2) Maa Mangala
(3) Maa Bhubaneswari
(4) Maa Budhi Thakurani
= 1
~ Nuakhai is the harvest festival of western Odisha, celebrated on Bhadrava Shukla Panchami, the day after Ganesh Chaturthi. In Sambalpur the nabanna (first new rice of the season) is first offered to Maa Samaleswari, the presiding deity of the town, at the auspicious moment (lagna) fixed by the temple priests, amid hulahuli and drums. Q 27 | ih | Art & Culture
Which of the following pairs of temple style & region are correctly matched?
1. Vesara – Deccan
2. Rekha-Deul – Odisha
3. Bhumija – Malwa
4. Kalinga – Tamil Nadu
(1) 1 & 2 only
(2) 1, 2 & 3 are correct
(3) 2 & 4 only
(4) only 1

Q 28 | ih | Ancient India

Q 29 | cur | Static GK & Culture

(1) Lavani
(2) Domkach
(3) Bhavai
(4) Baul

(4) 12.00 meters
= 3

Q 32 | ih | Modern India
Read the following statements:
Statement I: The Act of 1793 followed the tradition of personal rule without bringing in any legal reforms.
Statement II: Judges were assigned to interpret laws rather than depend on the ruler's personal judgment.
Which of the above statements is/are true?
(1) Only 1 is true
(2) Only 2 is true
(3) Both Statement 1 and 2 are true
(4) Neither 1 nor 2 is true
= 2
~ The Act of 1793 refers to Lord Cornwallis's Regulations (the Cornwallis Code), issued along with the Permanent Settlement of Bengal. It did bring legal reforms: revenue collection was separated from the administration of justice, a graded system of civil and criminal courts was set up, and judges were to decide cases according to written regulations. This introduced the rule of law in place of the personal rule of earlier rulers. So Statement I is false and Statement II is true.

Q 33 | cur | Sports

Q 34 | eco | Agriculture, Industry & Trade
Which of the following best reflects the strategic impact of the India–Australia Economic Cooperation and Trade Agreement (ECTA) after three years of implementation?
(1) ECTA removed all tariffs on Australian farm goods unilaterally.
(2) ECTA mainly benefitted old sectors like cotton, with little new growth.

= 3
~ The India–Australia ECTA was signed on 2 April 2022 and came into force on 29 December 2022; the question's "three years" count from the signing, whose third anniversary fell in April 2025. Q 38 | pol | Government & Parliament
Fill in the blanks:
The Union Home Minister chairs a Zonal Council meeting as ______ chairman.
(1) ex-officio
(2) rotating
(3) ceremonial
(4) honorary
= 1
~ Zonal Councils are statutory bodies set up under the States Reorganisation Act, 1956, to promote Centre–State and inter-State cooperation. There are five of them (Northern, Central, Eastern, Western and Southern), and the Union Home Minister is the chairman of each by virtue of his office, that is, ex officio. Q 39 | sci | Biology
Which of the following statements regarding human antibodies is/are accurate?
1. IgG constitutes the largest proportion of antibodies in human blood.
2. IgE plays a key role in allergic responses.
3. IgA is primarily located in bodily secretions such as saliva and breast milk.
(1) Only 1 and 2 are correct
(2) Only 2 and 3 are correct
(3) Only 1 and 3 are correct
(4) 1, 2 and 3 all are correct
= 4
~ All three statements are correct. IgG is the most abundant antibody, about 75–80% of the antibodies in blood serum, and the only one that crosses the placenta. IgE binds to mast cells and basophils and triggers histamine release in allergic reactions; it also acts against parasitic worms. IgA is the main antibody of secretions such as saliva, tears, mucus and breast milk (especially colostrum), protecting mucous surfaces. IgM, a large pentamer, is the first antibody made in an infection.

= 2

(2) Many houses had been damaged by the storm.
(3) Many houses were damaged by the storm.

= 3
~ In the active sentence 'The storm' is the subject, 'damaged' is simple past and 'many houses' is the object. Q 90 | eng | Error Spotting & Sentence Improvement
Choose the most suitable option to replace the highlighted part of the sentence:
I "am liking this movie" a lot.
(1) have liked
(2) like
(3) liked
(4) am enjoy this movie
= 2
~ 'Like' is a stative verb: it expresses a state of mind or feeling rather than an action. Stative verbs such as like, love, hate, know, believe and prefer are normally not used in the continuous tenses, so 'I am liking this movie a lot' should be 'I like this movie a lot' (simple present).
