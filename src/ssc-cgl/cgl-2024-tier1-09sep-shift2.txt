Q 6 | rea | Coding-Decoding
In a certain code language, ‘let it go’ is coded as ‘vc nt ap’ and ‘this is it’ is coded as ‘mq vc lh’. What is the code for ‘it’ in that language?
(1) nt
(2) lh
(3) vc
(4) mq
= 3
~ Compare the two sentences. The only word common to 'let it go' and 'this is it' is 'it', and the only code common to 'vc nt ap' and 'mq vc lh' is 'vc'. So 'it' is coded as 'vc'; the position of a code need not match the position of its word. The codes 'nt' and 'ap' stand for 'let' and 'go', while 'mq' and 'lh' stand for 'this' and 'is'.

Q 7 | rea | Syllogism & Statements
In this question, three statements are given, followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.
Statements:
All fans are brown.
Some brown are chairs.
Some chairs are plastic.
Conclusions:
I. Some brown are fans.
II. Some chairs are brown.
III. No chair is plastic.
(1) Only conclusions II and III follow.
(2) Only conclusion III follows.
(3) None of the conclusions follows.
(4) Only conclusions I and II follow.
= 4
~ Conclusion I: "All fans are brown" can be converted to "Some brown are fans", so I follows. Conclusion II: "Some brown are chairs" converts directly to "Some chairs are brown", so II follows. Conclusion III, "No chair is plastic", contradicts the statement "Some chairs are plastic", so it cannot follow. Hence only conclusions I and II follow.

Q 9 | rea | Blood Relations & Directions
In a certain code language,
R + T means ‘R is the sister of T’.
R − T means ‘R is the father of T’.
R * T means ‘R is the wife of T’.
R ÷ T means ‘R is the mother of T’.
Based on the above, how is F related to C if ‘A + B − C ÷ D * E − F ÷ G’?
(1) Wife
(2) Daughter
(3) Daughter’s daughter
(4) Mother
= 3
~ Decode step by step: B is the father of C; C is the mother of D; D is the wife of E; E is the father of F; F is the mother of G. Since D is E's wife and E is F's father, D is F's mother. F is the mother of G, so F is female, a daughter of D. D (a wife, hence female) is C's daughter. So F is C's daughter's daughter, i.e. C's granddaughter.

Q 11 | rea | Coding-Decoding

(2) Bo
(3) Ao

Q 25 | rea | Mathematical Operations
What will come in the place of ‘?’ in the following equation, if ‘+’ and ‘−’ are interchanged and ‘×’ and ‘÷’ are interchanged?

= 3

Q 26 | geo | Indian Geography
In which part of India does the hot wind ‘Loo’ blow?
(1) South and Southwestern
(2) East and Northeastern
(3) West and Southwestern
(4) North and Northwestern
= 4
~ The loo is a strong, gusty, hot and dry wind that blows during the day over north and north-western India (Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, Delhi, Uttar Pradesh and Bihar) in May and June, before the monsoon arrives. It raises day temperatures to 45°C or more and can cause heat stroke. Evening dust storms in the north-west and the kalbaisakhi thunderstorms of West Bengal are other features of India's hot weather season, while the south and east stay more humid.

Q 28 | geo | Environment & Ecology
Which of the following can be represented as a functional unit of nature?
(1) Ecosystem
(2) Humans
(3) Vehicles
(4) Plants
= 1
~ NCERT describes an ecosystem as a functional unit of nature, where living organisms interact among themselves and with the surrounding physical environment. Its biotic parts (producers, consumers, decomposers) and abiotic parts work together through productivity, decomposition, energy flow and nutrient cycling. The term was coined by A.G. Q 29 | geo | Indian Geography
At what latitude does the easterly jet stream blow over peninsular India during the summer months?
(1) 30°N
(2) 24°N
(3) 28°N
(4) 14°N
= 4
~ In summer the subtropical westerly jet stream shifts north of the Himalayas with the apparent movement of the sun. Q 31 | sci | Chemistry

= 1

Q 36 | sci | Biology
Which condition, also known as icterus, causes a yellowing of your skin and the whites of your eyes?
(1) Pemphigus
(2) Ichthyosis
(3) Eczema
(4) Jaundice
= 4
~ Jaundice (icterus) is the yellow colour of the skin, the whites of the eyes and mucous membranes caused by excess bilirubin, a yellow pigment formed when old red blood cells are broken down. It appears when the liver cannot process or excrete bilirubin (as in hepatitis), when bile flow is blocked, or when red cells break down too fast. Pemphigus is an autoimmune blistering disease, ichthyosis causes dry scaly skin, and eczema is itchy, inflamed skin.

Q 37 | ih | Ancient India

(2) Prayag
(3) Chirand
(4) Girnar
= 4

Q 45 | eco | Money, Banking & Finance
What is the relationship between interest rate and demand for money?
(1) Inverse
(2) Direct
(3) No relationship exists
(4) Proportionate
= 1
~ The rate of interest is the opportunity cost of holding money, because cash earns nothing while bonds and deposits earn interest. When the interest rate rises, people hold less money and more interest-earning assets, so the (speculative) demand for money falls; when it falls, they hold more money.
