Select the option that represents the correct order of the given words as they would appear in an English dictionary.
1. Warriors
2. Warehouse

6. Wardenship

Q 3 | rea | Syllogism & Statements
Three statements are given, followed by four conclusions numbered I, II, III and IV. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.
Statements:
Some desks are trays.
Some trays are plates.
Some plates are desks.
Conclusions:
I. All desks are plates.
II. All plates are desks.
III. Some plates are trays.
IV. All trays are desks.
(1) Only conclusion III follows
(2) Only conclusion IV follows
(3) Only conclusion II follows
(4) Only conclusion I follows
= 1
~ A "some" statement can be reversed: "Some trays are plates" gives "Some plates are trays", so conclusion III follows. All three statements are particular ("some"), and particular statements can never prove a universal ("all") conclusion. So "All desks are plates", "All plates are desks" and "All trays are desks" are only possibilities, not certainties. Hence only conclusion III follows.

Q 4 | rea | Analogy & Classification
Select the option that is related to the third word in the same way as the second word is related to the first word.
(The words must be considered as meaningful English words and must not be related to each other based on the number of letters/number of consonants/vowels in the word.)
Skin : Touch :: Nose : ?
(1) Smell
(2) Taste
(3) Nose ring
(4) Sweat
= 1
~ The skin is the sense organ through which we feel touch. In the same way, the nose is the sense organ through which we smell, so Nose : Smell. Taste is the sense of the tongue, not the nose. Q 5 | rea | Blood Relations & Directions
Ganesh was taking a walk with his mother’s brother’s father’s granddaughter. Who was he walking with?
(1) Daughter
(2) Mother
(3) Cousin
(4) Granddaughter
= 3
~ Ganesh's mother's brother's father is his mother's father, that is, Ganesh's maternal grandfather. A granddaughter of this grandfather is a daughter of one of his children, for example of Ganesh's maternal uncle, which makes her Ganesh's cousin. (She could also be Ganesh's own sister, but Sister is not an option.) Daughter and Granddaughter belong to younger generations than Ganesh, and Mother is the grandfather's daughter, not his granddaughter, so Cousin is the answer.

Q 7 | rea | Series
Which of the following letter-clusters will replace the question mark (?) in the given series?
UV, OY, IB, EE, ?
(1) ZK
(2) AH
(3) BI
(4) BH
= 2
~ First letters: U, O, I, E are the vowels taken in reverse order (U, O, I, E, A), so the next first letter is A. Second letters move 3 places forward each time: V + 3 = Y, Y + 3 = B (counting on past Z), B + 3 = E, so the next is E + 3 = H. The missing cluster is AH. Q 16 | rea | Analogy & Classification
Select the option that is related to the fifth number in the same way as the second number is related to the first number and the fourth number is related to the third number.
5 : 45::3 : 3::6 : ?
(1) 90
(2) 106
(3) 110
(4) 96
= 4
~ The second number = first number × (first number − 2)². Check: 5 × (5 − 2)² = 5 × 9 = 45 and 3 × (3 − 2)² = 3 × 1 = 3. So for 6: 6 × (6 − 2)² = 6 × 16 = 96. Simple rules such as multiplying by a fixed number fail, since 5 gives 45 (× 9) while 3 gives 3 (× 1); 90, 106 and 110 do not fit the rule.

Q 17 | rea | Series
Which of the following numbers will replace the question mark (?) in the given series?
8, 15, 26, ? , 56, 75
(1) 39
(2) 41
(3) 35
(4) 43
= 1
~ The differences between terms are consecutive prime numbers: 15 − 8 = 7 and 26 − 15 = 11, so the next differences are 13, 17 and 19. Then 26 + 13 = 39, 39 + 17 = 56 and 56 + 19 = 75, which matches the last two terms. With 41 the differences would be 15 and 15, which breaks the pattern.

Q 21 | rea | Coding-Decoding
In a certain code language, ‘ASK’ is written as ‘62’ and ‘BYE’ is written as ‘64’. How will ‘CRY’ be written in that language?
(1) 68
(2) 72
(3) 86
(4) 92
= 4
~ The code is twice the sum of the letter positions. ASK: A (1) + S (19) + K (11) = 31, and 31 × 2 = 62. BYE: B (2) + Y (25) + E (5) = 32, and 32 × 2 = 64. CRY: C (3) + R (18) + Y (25) = 46, and 46 × 2 = 92. So CRY is written as 92.

Q 24 | rea | Coding-Decoding
In a certain code language, ‘TABLE’ is coded as ELEAT and ‘SWING’ is coded as GNLWS. How will ‘FRAME’ be coded in the same language?
(1) EMERF
(2) EMDRF
(3) ERMDF
(4) MEDFR
= 2
~ The word is written in reverse order and then the middle letter is moved 3 places forward. TABLE reversed is ELBAT, and B + 3 = E gives ELEAT. SWING reversed is GNIWS, and I + 3 = L gives GNLWS. Q 25 | rea | Mathematical Operations
Which two signs should be interchanged to make the given equation correct?
588 ÷ 28 × 32 + 72 − 160 = 760
(1) − and +
(2) ÷ and −
(3) + and ×
(4) ÷ and +
= 1
~ As given, 588 ÷ 28 × 32 + 72 − 160 = 21 × 32 + 72 − 160 = 672 + 72 − 160 = 584, not 760. Interchange − and +: 588 ÷ 28 × 32 − 72 + 160 = 672 − 72 + 160 = 760, which is correct. The other swaps do not work; for example, interchanging ÷ and + gives 588 + 28 × 32 ÷ 72 − 160, which is not even a whole number.

Q 32 | eco | Planning, Growth & Budget
The consumption of fixed capital is also known as _________.
(1) depreciation
(2) net investment
(3) appreciation
(4) gross investment
= 1
~ Fixed capital such as machinery and buildings wears out through use and age; the value of this wear and tear during a year is called depreciation, which national income accounting (NCERT Macroeconomics) also calls consumption of fixed capital. It links gross and net measures: net investment = gross investment − depreciation, and NDP = GDP − depreciation. Appreciation is a rise in an asset's value, the opposite idea, while gross investment is the total addition to the capital stock before depreciation is deducted.

Q 34 | ih | Medieval India
In Chola administration, ________ was the assembly in the villages which were inhabited predominantly by the Brahmanas.
(1) Ur
(2) Khilya
(3) Nagaram
(4) Sabha
= 4
~ Under the Cholas, brahmadeya villages, granted to and settled mainly by Brahmanas, were run by an assembly called the sabha (or mahasabha) of prominent Brahmana landholders, which worked through committees for irrigation tanks, gardens, temples and justice; the Uttaramerur inscriptions of Parantaka I describe how committee members were chosen by drawing palm-leaf tickets from a pot. The ur was the assembly of ordinary peasant villages, and the nagaram was an association of traders that managed affairs in towns. "Khilya" is not a Chola assembly.

Q 35 | geo | Indian Geography

(1) Rajasthan
(2) Maharashtra
(3) Madhya Pradesh
(4) Gujarat
= 1
~ The Tapi (Tapti) rises at Multai in Betul district of Madhya Pradesh and flows west for about 724 km, between the Satpura range and the Satmala-Ajanta hills, to the Gulf of Khambhat near Surat. Q 36 | sci | Chemistry
Which of the following is used as a cooling medium for the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) and the superconducting magnets in MRI scanners and NMR spectrometers?
(1) Neon
(2) Chlorine
(3) Argon
(4) Helium
= 4
~ Liquid helium, which boils at about 4.2 K (−269 °C), is used to keep the superconducting magnets of MRI scanners and NMR spectrometers below the temperature at which they lose all electrical resistance. CERN's Large Hadron Collider cools its superconducting magnets to 1.9 K (−271.3 °C) with superfluid helium, colder than outer space. Helium is an inert noble gas that does not solidify at normal pressure. Neon and argon are also noble gases but liquefy at much higher temperatures (about 27 K and 87 K), and chlorine is a reactive, toxic halogen.

(2) Sexual parasitism
(3) Klepto parasitism
(4) Competitive parasitism
= 1

Q 48 | ih | Modern India
Ryotwari system of revenue collection in India, introduced by the British, was based on the _______.
(1) Smith’s theory of rent
(2) Ricardian theory of rent
(3) Malthusian theory of rent
(4) Marx’s theory of rent
= 2
~ The Ryotwari system, begun by Alexander Read and Thomas Munro in Baramahal (1792), extended over Madras Presidency from 1820 and later to Bombay, settled revenue directly with each cultivator (ryot) without intermediaries. Q 58 | qa | Geometry
