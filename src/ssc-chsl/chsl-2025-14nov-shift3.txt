Q 98 | sci | Biology
What is the primary function of an artificial kidney for humans?
(1) To produce urine
(2) To remove nitrogenous waste products from the blood
(3) To filter glucose from the blood
(4) To increase blood pressure
= 2
~ An artificial kidney (haemodialysis machine) removes nitrogenous wastes such as urea and creatinine from the blood of patients with kidney failure. Blood passes along a semi-permeable membrane bathed in dialysing fluid, and the wastes diffuse out. Useful substances like glucose are retained.
