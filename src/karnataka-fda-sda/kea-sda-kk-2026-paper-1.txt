Q 1 | sci | Biology
Which part of the Brain is called ‘location of consciousness’?
(1) Cerebellum
(2) Pons
(3) Cerebrum
(4) Medulla
= 3
~ The cerebrum, the largest part of the brain, is the seat of consciousness, intelligence, memory, thinking and voluntary actions. The cerebellum coordinates balance and posture, the pons relays signals and helps regulate breathing, and the medulla controls involuntary actions such as heartbeat and breathing.

Q 2 | sci | Biology
Select the incorrect match.
(1) Rhinovirus - Common cold
(2) Plasmodium - Pneumonia
(3) Entamoeba - Amoebiasis
(4) Trichophyton - Ringworm
= 2
~ Plasmodium, a protozoan spread by the female Anopheles mosquito, causes malaria, not pneumonia; pneumonia is caused mainly by bacteria such as Streptococcus pneumoniae and Haemophilus influenzae. The other pairs are correct: rhinovirus – common cold, Entamoeba histolytica – amoebiasis (amoebic dysentery), Trichophyton (a fungus) – ringworm.

Q 5 | sci | Biology
In the preparation of ‘Bio-ferfilizer’, which among the following microbes are used?

(2) Bacteria and Blue-green algae
(3) Protozoa and Blue-green algae
(4) Protozoa and Bacteria

Q 6 | sci | Chemistry
The gases mixtures present in tanks used by scuba divers are
(1) Helium, Oxygen, Nitrogen
(2) Oxygen, Nitrogen, Hydrogen
(3) Hydrogen, Oxygen, Helium
(4) Hydrogen, Helium, Nitrogen
= 1
~ Scuba tanks hold oxygen diluted with nitrogen (compressed air or nitrox), and for deeper dives helium is added (trimix = helium + oxygen + nitrogen). Helium is much less soluble in blood than nitrogen, so it reduces nitrogen narcosis and 'the bends'. Hydrogen is not used because it forms an explosive mixture with oxygen.

Q 8 | sci | Chemistry
Food storing cans are made from electroplating of other metals onto iron. The metal used for electroplating of food cans is
(1) Arsenic
(2) Copper
(3) Stannum
(4) Plumbum
= 3
~ Food cans are 'tin cans': iron is electroplated with tin (Latin stannum, symbol Sn), which is less reactive than iron and non-toxic, so it does not react with food. Arsenic and lead (plumbum, Pb) are poisonous, and copper reacts with food acids.

Q 9 | sci | Chemistry
A 35% (v/v) solution of ethylene glycol is used in cars. The correct reason for this is
(1) It is an antifreeze for cooling the engine
(2) As a fuel for combustion
(3) As a shining substance
(4) Helps to open airbag
= 1
~ A 35% (v/v) ethylene glycol solution in water is the radiator coolant: it lowers the freezing point of water (a colligative property) so the coolant does not freeze in cold weather, and also raises its boiling point. Hence it is used as an antifreeze for engine cooling.

Q 12 | sci | Chemistry
Pregnant women who have the habit of smoking have to face premature birth, spontaneous abortions and deformed babies. This is mainly due to increase in the level of
(1) S
(2) O₂
(3) N₂
(4) CO
= 4
~ Tobacco smoke contains carbon monoxide (CO). CO binds haemoglobin about 200 times more strongly than oxygen, forming carboxyhaemoglobin, so less oxygen reaches the foetus. A raised CO level in a smoking mother's blood can therefore cause premature birth, spontaneous abortion and deformed babies.

Q 13 | geo | Environment & Ecology
Read the following statements:
Statement I : Rain water normally has a pH of 5.6
Statement II : Nitrogen Oxides and sulphur dioxide cause acid rain.
Select the correct answer from the following choices.
(1) Statement I is correct and Statement II is incorrect
(2) Both the Statements I and II are correct
(3) Both the Statements I and II are incorrect
(4) Statement I is incorrect and Statement II is correct
= 2
~ Normal rain is slightly acidic (pH about 5.6) because atmospheric CO₂ dissolves in it to form weak carbonic acid. When oxides of nitrogen and sulphur dioxide from burning fossil fuels form nitric and sulphuric acids, the pH falls below 5.6 – this is acid rain. Both statements are correct.

Q 14 | sci | Chemistry
The mixture which can be used to join railway tracks is
(1) Copper oxide + Iron
(2) Carbon + Aluminium
(3) Iron (III) oxide + Aluminium
(4) Aluminium oxide + Iron
= 3
~ This is the thermite reaction: Fe₂O₃ + 2Al → Al₂O₃ + 2Fe + heat. Aluminium, being more reactive, displaces iron from iron(III) oxide; the reaction is so exothermic that the iron formed is molten and is used to weld (join) railway tracks and cracked machine parts.

Q 15 | sci | Physics
A person A lifts a 10 kg object and keeps it at a height of 1m in 3 seconds. Another person B lifts a 5 kg object and keeps it at a height of 2m in 4 seconds. Then:
(1) A has done more work than B
(2) B has done more work than A
(3) A has delivered more power than B
(4) B has delivered more power than A
= 3
~ Work = mgh. A: 10 × 10 × 1 = 100 J; B: 5 × 10 × 2 = 100 J, so both do equal work. Power = work/time: A = 100/3 ≈ 33.3 W, B = 100/4 = 25 W. A finishes the same work faster, so A delivers more power.

Q 16 | sci | Physics
A copper cube (A) and an aluminium cube (B) of same volume are immersed completely in water. Then:
(1) The buoyant force on cube (A) is greater
(2) The buoyant force on cube (B) is greater
(3) The buoyant force on both of them is same
(4) The cube (B) will become weightless in water
= 3
~ By Archimedes' principle, buoyant force = weight of liquid displaced = V × ρ(water) × g. It depends on the volume immersed and the liquid, not on the material of the body. Equal volumes fully immersed displace equal water, so the buoyant force is the same. Aluminium (density 2.7 g/cm³) is denser than water, so it does not become weightless.

Q 17 | sci | Physics
The minimum attainable temperature is
(1) 0°C
(2) -20°C
(3) 273°C
(4) -273.15°C
= 4
~ The lowest possible temperature is absolute zero, 0 K = -273.15°C, at which the thermal motion of particles is at its minimum. No temperature below it is possible (it can be approached but never quite reached). 0°C is only the freezing point of water.

Q 25 | apt | Arithmetic

Q 26 | apt | Arithmetic

Q 30 | apt | Reasoning
At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock will the hands of a clock be at a right angle?

(2) 16 3/4 mins past 4

= 3

Q 31 | apt | Arithmetic
An ordinary cube has four blank faces, one face marked 3 and another marked 4, then the probability of obtaining a sum of 15 in 5 throws is
(1) 7/2592
(2) 7/1944
(3) 7/7776
(4) 7/1296
= 1
~ A sum of 15 comes from five 3s (1 sequence) or one 3, three 4s and one blank (5!/3! = 20 sequences). KEA's key counts these 21 sequences over 6^5 = 7776: 21/7776 = 7/2592. Strictly, a blank has probability 4/6, so the exact value is (1 + 20×4)/7776 = 81/7776 = 1/96, which is not an option. (Official KEA key; some sources differ.)

= 2

= 2
