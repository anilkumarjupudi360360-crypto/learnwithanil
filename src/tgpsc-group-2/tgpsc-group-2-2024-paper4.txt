Q 1 | geo | Telangana Geography
Consider the following statements :
A. Manjira, a tributary of River Godavari meets Godavari at Nirmal.
B. Tungabhadra meets River Krishna at Alampur.
Which of the above statements is/are correct ?
(1) A only
(2) B only
(3) Both A and B
(4) Neither A nor B
= 2
~ The Manjira, a major tributary of the Godavari, joins the Godavari at Kandakurthi in Nizamabad district, not at Nirmal, so A is wrong. The Tungabhadra joins the Krishna near Alampur in Jogulamba Gadwal district, on the Telangana–Andhra Pradesh border, so B is correct.

Q 2 | tsh | Telangana Culture & Heritage
Match the following Jataras with the Gods they are associated with :
Jatara | God
A. Mannemkonda Jatara | I. Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy
B. Bejjanki Jatara | II. Lingamanthula Swamy
C. Peddagollagattu Jatara | III. Mallikarjuna Swamy
D. Komuravelli Jatara | IV. Venkateshwara Swamy
 | V. Ramalingeshwara Swamy
Choose the correct answer :
(1) A-III; B-II; C-IV; D-V
(2) A-V; B-I; C-III; D-IV
(3) A-IV; B-I; C-II; D-III
(4) A-IV; B-III; C-I; D-II
= 3
~ Mannemkonda in Mahabubnagar district is the hill shrine of Sri Venkateswara Swamy. The Bejjanki jatara is held for Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy, the Peddagollagattu (Peddagattu) jatara near Suryapet honours Lingamanthula Swamy, the deity of the Yadavas (Gollas), and Komuravelli in Siddipet district is the seat of Mallikarjuna Swamy. So A-IV, B-I, C-II, D-III.

Q 3 | tsm | Idea of Telangana (1948–1970)
During the time of which Nizam were the administrators brought from outside the state on a large-scale ?
(1) Sikander Jah
(2) Nasir-ud-Daula
(3) Afzal-ud-Daula
(4) Mir Mahboob Ali Khan
= 4
~ In the reign of Mir Mahboob Ali Khan (Asaf Jah VI, 1869–1911), the modern administration begun by Salar Jung I was expanded, and because few locals had modern education, officials were brought in on a large scale from North India and other regions. This influx first created the resentment of local people (Mulkis) against outsiders (non-Mulkis). Sikander Jah, Nasir-ud-Daula and Afzal-ud-Daula ruled before him.

Q 4 | tsh | Telangana History
Consider the following statements :
A. There were 5 subas under Nizam-ul-Mulk Asaf Jah.
B. Medak, Nizamabad, Mahabubnagar, Nalgonda and Athraf-Balda were under Warangal Suba.
Which of the above statements is/are correct ?
(1) A only
(2) B only
(3) Both A and B
(4) Neither A nor B
= 4
~ Nizam-ul-Mulk Asaf Jah I governed the six Mughal subas of the Deccan (Khandesh, Berar, Aurangabad, Bidar, Bijapur and Hyderabad), not five, so A is wrong. In the later Nizam administration, Medak, Nizamabad, Mahabubnagar, Nalgonda and Athraf-i-Balda formed the Medak (Gulshanabad) Suba, while the Warangal Suba covered Warangal, Karimnagar and Adilabad, so B is also wrong.

Q 5 | tsm | Idea of Telangana (1948–1970)
Whose reforms during the Nizam’s time are said to have laid the foundation for the conflict between Mulkis and non-Mulkis in the later times ?
(1) Mir Turab Ali Khan
(2) Maharaja Kishan Pershad
(3) Sir Akbar Hydari
(4) Mir Laiq Ali Khan
= 1
~ Mir Turab Ali Khan, better known as Salar Jung I, was Diwan of Hyderabad from 1853 to 1883 and modernised its revenue, judicial, police and education systems. Because few locals had modern training, he brought in educated officials from North India and elsewhere, and this first influx of outsiders into government jobs is said to have laid the foundation for the later Mulki vs non-Mulki conflict. Kishan Pershad, Akbar Hydari and Laiq Ali were later Prime Ministers of the Nizam.

Q 6 | tsm | Idea of Telangana (1948–1970)
In which year were the Hyderabad Civil Services Regulations promulgated based on a Farman issued by Mir Osman Ali Khan ?
(1) 1916
(2) 1917
(3) 1918
(4) 1919
= 4
~ The Mulki Rules were formally laid down in 1919, when the Hyderabad Civil Service Regulations were promulgated on the basis of a Farman of Mir Osman Ali Khan (Asaf Jah VII). They defined a Mulki as a person born in Hyderabad State or one who had lived there for at least 15 years and given up the intention of returning to his native place, and reserved government jobs for Mulkis.

Against whom was the popular song ‘Bandenaka Bandi Katti’ written by Yadagiri during the Telangana Armed Struggle ?
(1) Nizam Mir Osman Ali Khan
(2) Visnur Ramachandra Reddy
(3) Jenna Reddy Pratapa Reddy
(4) Kalluri Subba Rao
= 3
~ ‘Bandenaka Bandi Katti, Padahaaru Bandlu Katti’ is the famous song of Bandi Yadagiri from the Telangana Armed Struggle. TGPSC's key says it was written against Jenna Reddy Pratapa Reddy, a powerful landlord (deshmukh) of the time who oppressed the peasants. Because the song's refrain addresses the ‘Nizam Sarkar’, many books describe it as a song against the Nizam's rule. (Official key; some sources differ.)

Q 8 | tsh | Telangana Culture & Heritage
Consider the following statements :
A. Kati Papalu are an exclusive group who are connected with the final rites of a corpse.
B. Kati Papalu wear a long “angarakha” made out of black cloth.
C. They carry a begging bag containing rice and other food items.
D. They do magical tricks and invoke Goddess Mahankali.
Which of the above statements are correct ?
(1) A and B
(2) A and D
(3) A, B and C
(4) B, C and D
= 2
~ Kati Papalu (‘kati’ means the burial or cremation ground) are a small group traditionally connected with the last rites of the dead, so A is correct. Q 10 | tsm | Idea of Telangana (1948–1970)
Consider the following statements :
A. Between 1946 and 1948, the Hyderabad Government appointed about 50,000 Muslims from outside the state in Military, Police and Excise Departments.
B. Konda Venkata Ranga Reddy expressed his displeasure on the appointment of non-Mulkis though eligible Mulkis were available for the jobs in the state after police action.
C. After police action, those Non-Mulki officials who occupied higher positions in the state brought their relatives and friends and got them appointed in the jobs by using their authority.
D. All this led to “Non-Mulki Go Back” agitation.
Which of the above statements are correct ?
(1) A and B
(2) A, B and C
(3) B, C and D
(4) A, B, C and D
= 3
~ After the Police Action (September 1948), officials from Madras and other States held top posts in Hyderabad State and used their authority to get their relatives and friends appointed, while qualified locals were passed over; K.V. Ranga Reddy openly protested against this. The resentment led to the "Non-Mulki Go Back" (Idli-Sambar Go Back) agitation of 1952. So B, C and D are correct; TGPSC's key treats statement A (about 50,000 outside Muslims appointed in 1946–48) as incorrect.

Q 12 | tsm | Idea of Telangana (1948–1970)
Consider the following statements :
A. A person is called a Mulki if, by birth, he/she is a subject of the Hyderabad State.
B. A person is called a Mulki if his/her father completed 12 years in government service at the time of his/her birth.
C. A non-Mulki woman becomes Mulki if her husband was a Mulki.
D. A non-Mulki man can become a Mulki by marrying a Mulki woman.
Which of the above statements are correct ?
(1) A and B
(2) B and D
(3) A and C
(4) A, C and D
= 3
~ Under the Mulki Rules of 1919, a person was a Mulki if he or she was born a subject of Hyderabad State, or had lived in the State for at least 15 years and given up the intention of returning home; a woman married to a Mulki was also treated as a Mulki. So A and C are correct. The rules had no 12-year condition on the father's service (B), and a man did not become a Mulki by marrying a Mulki woman (D).

B. The Commission in its report absolved the police of their responsibility for the death of people in the police firing.
Which of the above statements is/are correct ?
(1) A only
(2) B only
(3) Both A and B
(4) Neither A nor B
= 2
~ During the 1952 Mulki ("Non-Mulki Go Back") agitation, police fired on agitating students in Hyderabad city on 3 and 4 September 1952, and the Justice Pingle Jagan Mohan Reddy Commission was appointed to inquire into these firings; so A, which gives 1 and 2 September, is wrong. In its report the Commission justified the firing and did not hold the police responsible for the deaths, so B is correct.

Q 16 | tsm | Idea of Telangana (1948–1970)
Consider the following statements :
A. Ambedkar suggested four parameters to measure the balance between the bigger and smaller States.
B. According to him, economic viability is dependent on two factors namely, natural resources and human resources.
C. He mentioned that there should not be any mixed State.
D. He stated that one State should have one language.
Which of the above statements are correct ?
(1) A and B
(2) B and C
(3) C and D
(4) A, B, C and D
= 3
~ In ‘Thoughts on Linguistic States’ (1955), written after the SRC report, B.R. Q 19 | tsm | Idea of Telangana (1948–1970)
Arrange the following in chronological order in relation to Visalandhra :
A. The discussion was started on Visalandhra in Hyderabad State Assembly.
B. It was passed by the Assembly.
C. Rajya Sabha accepted it.
D. The President of India signed the bill.
Choose the correct answer :
(1) D, B, C, A
(2) A, B, C, D
(3) A, C, B, D
(4) A, D, B, C
= 2

Q 21 | tsm | Idea of Telangana (1948–1970)

Which of the above statements are correct ?
(1) A and B
(2) B and C
(3) C and D

= 1
