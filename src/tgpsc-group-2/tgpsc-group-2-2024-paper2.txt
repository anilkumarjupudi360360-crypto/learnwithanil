Q 1 | ih | Ancient India
Consider the following statements :
A. Level of planning and size of a settlement in Indus Valley Civilization is not strictly co-related. Bigger sites weren’t always better planned.
B. The relatively small site of Kalibangan shows higher level of planning than Lothal, which was double in size.
Which of the above statements is/are correct ?
(1) A only
(2) B only
(3) Both A and B
(4) Neither A nor B
= 1
~ In the Harappan civilisation the level of planning was not strictly related to the size of a settlement, so A is correct. Standard texts give the example the other way round: the relatively small site of Lothal shows a higher level of planning than Kalibangan, which is about double its size. B reverses the two sites, so only A is correct.

Q 2 | ih | Ancient India
Which of the following statements regarding early Vedic culture are correct ?
A. Polygamy was practised, but polyandry was not.
B. In Rigveda, there is little trace of the rigid restrictions typical of caste system.
C. ‘Viṡ’ was a bigger socio-political structure than ‘Grāma’.
D. The foundation of socio-political structure in Rigvedic society was village.
Choose the correct answer :
(1) A and B only
(2) A, B and C only
(3) A and D only
(4) A, B, C and D
= 2
~ In the Rigvedic period polygamy is attested, especially among chiefs, while polyandry was not an accepted practice, and the varna divisions had not yet hardened into rigid caste restrictions, so A and B are correct. The social units rose from kula (family) to grama, viś (clan) and jana (tribe), so viś was bigger than grama (C correct). The basic unit of society was the family (kula), not the village, so D is wrong.

Q 3 | ih | Ancient India
Which of the following statements regarding Buddhist sects are correct ?
A. Sthaviravada believed in the concept of Nirvana.
B. Sarvastivadins viewed constituents of phenomenon as not wholly momentary but existing forever in latent form.
C. Sammitiyas propagated the doctrine of soullessness.
D. Sautrantikas maintained that knowledge of outside world is only a feasible inference.
Choose the correct answer :
(1) A and C only
(2) A, B and C only
(3) B and D only
(4) A, B and D only
= 4
~ The Sthaviravada (Theravada) school held Nirvana as the final goal, and the Sarvastivadins taught that the elements (dharmas) of phenomena exist in all three times, past, present and future, in latent form. The Sautrantikas held that the external world is not directly perceived but only inferred. The Sammitiyas (Pudgalavadins) instead taught the existence of a "person" (pudgala), departing from the doctrine of soullessness, so C is wrong.

Q 4 | ih | Ancient India
Match the following land types with their meaning :
Land types | Meaning
A. Aprahata | I. Desert
B. Shadvala | II. Fallow
C. Sharkavati | III. Fertile
D. Urvara | IV. Grassy
 | V. Sandy

(3) A-II; B-V; C-IV; D-I

Q 5 | ih | Art & Culture
Which option of the following sequences indicates the correct chronological order of the religious leaders ?
(1) Shankaracharya – Ramanuja – Chaitanya
(2) Ramanuja – Shankaracharya – Chaitanya
(3) Ramanuja – Chaitanya – Shankaracharya
(4) Shankaracharya – Chaitanya – Ramanuja
= 1
~ Adi Shankaracharya, the exponent of Advaita, lived in the 8th–9th century CE (traditionally 788–820). Ramanuja, who taught Vishishtadvaita, lived in the 11th–12th century (traditionally 1017–1137). Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, the Bengal Vaishnava saint, lived from 1486 to 1534. So the order is Shankaracharya – Ramanuja – Chaitanya.

Q 9 | ih | Ancient India

Q 11 | ih | Medieval India
Which of the following statements about Muhammad-Bin-Tughlaq are correct ?
A. He continued Khilji’s policy of reducing the village headmen, namely ‘khuts’ and ‘muqaddams’ to ordinary cultivators.
B. He created a separate department called ‘diwan-i-amir-i-kohi’.
C. His agrarian reforms had a long term impact, but failed disastrously during his reign.
D. He established new measurement of yard called ‘gazz-i-sikandari’.
Choose the correct answer :
(1) B and C only
(2) A, B and C only
(3) A, B, C and D
(4) A and D only
= 1
~ Muhammad bin Tughlaq set up a separate agriculture department, diwan-i-amir-i-kohi, which advanced loans to extend and improve cultivation; the experiment failed in his reign through famine and corruption, though his agrarian ideas had lasting importance, so B and C are correct. Reducing the khuts and muqaddams to ordinary cultivators was Alauddin Khalji's policy; Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq had already restored some of their privileges, so A is wrong. The gaz-i-Sikandari yard was introduced by Sikandar Lodi.

Which of the above statements is/are correct ?
(1) A only
(2) B only
(3) Both A and B
(4) Neither A nor B
= 2
~ As part of his market control, Alauddin Khalji set up three markets at Delhi, one for food grains, one for costly cloth and one for horses, slaves and cattle, so A (four markets) is wrong. Q 13 | ih | Medieval India
Which of the following statements are correct ?
A. The Firdausi order was a branch of the Suhrawardi.
B. The Shattari order was an orthodox order.
C. The Qureshi order of Sufism flourished in the Deccan.
D. Chishti Sufis belonged to three different branches of the Chishti order – Nagauriya, Sabiriya and Nizamiya.
Choose the correct answer :
(1) A, B and C
(2) B, C and D
(3) A, B and D
(4) A, C and D
= 3
~ The Firdausi order, a branch of the Suhrawardi silsila, flourished in Bihar under Sharafuddin Yahya Maneri, and the Shattari order, brought to India by Shah Abdullah Shattari in the 15th century, is counted among the orthodox (ba-shara) orders. The Chishtis divided into the Nagauri (Hamiduddin Nagauri), Sabiri (Alauddin Ali Ahmad Sabir) and Nizami (Nizamuddin Auliya) branches. There was no "Qureshi" Sufi order (the statement seems to confuse it with the Qadiri order, which was well established in the Deccan), so C is wrong.

Q 14 | ih | Art & Culture
Which of the following statements are correct ?

B. Ramananda preached in the language of the common people.
C. Vallabhacharya was the founder of Pushtimarga.
D. Jayadeva’s Gita Govinda was composed in Hindi.

Q 15 | ih | Medieval India
Consider the following statements about coinage during Akbar’s reign :

B. There was free and open minting system and mints were widely distributed.
Which of the above statements is/are correct ?
(1) A only
(2) B only
(3) Both A and B

= 3

Q 17 | ih | Medieval India

Q 27 | ih | Modern India & Freedom Struggle
The Famine Enquiry Commission of 1880 led by Sir Richard Strachey is associated with which policy shift ?
(1) Increased government responsibility for food relief
(2) Emphasis on free market mechanisms to address famines
(3) Introduction of a universal Public Distribution System
(4) Prioritization of infrastructure development in famine prone areas
= 1
~ The Famine Commission of 1880 under Richard Strachey, set up after the Great Famine of 1876–78, laid down that relief in famine was a duty of the State. It recommended famine codes and a Famine Relief and Insurance Fund, which led to the Famine Code of 1883, marking greater government responsibility for relief. A universal Public Distribution System came only after Independence.

Q 147 | tsh | Telangana History
‘Bhagela system’ that prevailed in Telangana refers to :
(1) share cropping in cultivation of crops.
(2) share paid to the artisan castes at the time of harvest of the crop.
(3) cheap or often compulsory free (unpaid) labour.
(4) a type of exchange of money or goods in agrarian economy.
= 3
~ Under the Bhagela system in Nizam-era Telangana, a labourer, usually from the lowest castes, who borrowed money from a landlord had to work for him as a bonded servant until the debt was repaid, often for life and even across generations. The Bhagela received little or no wage, so the system meant cheap or compulsory unpaid labour, like vetti (forced labour). It was not sharecropping or a harvest share paid to artisans.

Q 148 | soc | Social Exclusion & Rights
