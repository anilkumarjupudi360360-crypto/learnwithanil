# NCERT Class 12 Geography, India: People and Economy, Chapter 5: Mineral and Energy Resources
# Questions written by Learn with Anil from the chapter (current NCERT edition, Reprint 2026-27). November 2026.

Q 1 | geo | India: People and Economy
Consider the following statements about the characteristics of minerals:
1. They are unevenly distributed over space.
2. Good quality minerals are generally found in larger quantities than low quality minerals.
3. All minerals are exhaustible and take a long time to form geologically.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(1) 1 and 2 only
(2) 2 and 3 only
(3) 1 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 3
~ There is an inverse relationship between quality and quantity: good quality minerals are fewer than low quality ones. Since minerals do not have a "second crop", they must be conserved.

Q 2 | geo | India: People and Economy
More than 97 per cent of India's coal reserves occur in the valleys of:
(1) Damodar, Sone, Mahanadi and Godavari
(2) Ganga, Yamuna and Ghaghara
(3) Krishna, Kaveri and Pennar
(4) Narmada, Tapi and Mahi
= 1
~ Most of India's coal is Gondwana coal found in these river valleys.

Q 3 | geo | India: People and Economy
According to the chapter, most of India's major mineral resources occur to the east of a line linking:
(1) Mumbai and Kolkata
(2) Mangaluru and Kanpur
(3) Delhi and Chennai
(4) Ahmedabad and Bhubaneswar
= 2
~ Minerals are concentrated mainly in the north-eastern plateau, south-western plateau and north-western belts.

Q 4 | geo | India: People and Economy
The north-western mineral belt, which extends along the Aravalis in Rajasthan and part of Gujarat, is known mainly for:
(1) Coal and iron ore
(2) Copper, zinc, building stones such as sandstone, granite and marble, and gypsum
(3) Monazite and thorium
(4) Petroleum from the Assam valley
= 2
~ This belt is associated with the Dharwar system of rocks. Dolomite and limestone there supply the cement industry, and Gujarat and Rajasthan have rich salt sources.

Q 5 | geo | India: People and Economy
Which two main types of iron ore are found in India?
(1) Haematite and magnetite
(2) Bauxite and laterite
(3) Galena and sphalerite
(4) Monazite and ilmenite
= 1
~ India has the largest reserve of iron ore in Asia, and its ore is in great demand because of its superior quality.

C. Dalli and Rajhara — 3. Jharkhand

Q 7 | geo | India: People and Economy
Manganese is an important raw material for:
(1) Producing aluminium
(2) Making electric wires
(3) Smelting of iron ore and manufacturing ferro-alloys
(4) Generating nuclear power
= 3
~ Manganese deposits are mainly associated with the Dharwar system; Madhya Pradesh and Odisha are leading producers.

Q 8 | geo | India: People and Economy
Bauxite, the ore used for manufacturing aluminium, is found mainly in:
(1) Beach sands of Kerala
(2) Gondwana coal seams
(3) Tertiary deposits associated with laterite rocks
(4) Himalayan glaciers
= 3
~ Odisha is the largest producer of bauxite, with Kalahandi and Sambalpur as leading producers; Lohardaga in Jharkhand also has rich deposits.

Q 9 | geo | India: People and Economy
Copper deposits in India mainly occur in:
(1) Singhbhum (Jharkhand), Balaghat (Madhya Pradesh), and Jhunjhunu and Alwar (Rajasthan)
(2) Kalahandi and Koraput (Odisha)
(3) Nellore (Andhra Pradesh) only
(4) Digboi (Assam)
= 1
~ Copper is indispensable in the electrical industry for wires, motors, transformers and generators.

Q 10 | geo | India: People and Economy
Which district of Andhra Pradesh produces the best quality mica, according to the chapter?
(1) Guntur
(2) Nellore
(3) Kurnool
(4) Visakhapatnam
= 2
~ In Jharkhand, high quality mica comes from a belt about 150 km long and 22 km wide in the lower Hazaribagh plateau. In Rajasthan, the mica belt runs about 320 km from Jaipur to Bhilwara.

Q 11 | geo | India: People and Economy
About 80 per cent of India's coal deposits are of which type?
(1) Anthracite
(2) Peat
(3) Lignite
(4) Bituminous, non-coking grade
= 4
~ Coal occurs in rock sequences of mainly two geological ages: Gondwana and Tertiary.

Q 12 | geo | India: People and Economy
Which is the largest coalfield of India, according to the chapter?
(1) Raniganj
(2) Talcher
(3) Singrauli
(4) Jharia
= 4
~ Jharia is the largest, followed by Raniganj; both are in the Damodar valley in the Jharkhand–Bengal coal belt.

Q 13 | geo | India: People and Economy
Tertiary coals are found in which group of states?
(1) Jharkhand, West Bengal and Odisha
(2) Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya and Nagaland
(3) Maharashtra and Telangana
(4) Madhya Pradesh and Chhattisgarh
= 2
~ Lignite (brown coal) occurs in the coastal areas of Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Gujarat and in Jammu and Kashmir.

Q 14 | geo | India: People and Economy
Consider the following statements about petroleum in India:
1. Before the Oil and Natural Gas Commission was set up in 1956, Digboi in Assam was the only oil producing region.
2. Mumbai High, about 160 km off Mumbai, was discovered in 1973 and started production in 1976.
3. Barauni is an example of a field-based refinery, while Digboi is market-based.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(1) 1 and 2 only
(2) 2 and 3 only
(3) 1 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3

~ It is the other way round: Digboi is a field-based refinery and Barauni is market-based.

Q 16 | geo | India: People and Economy
Thorium is mainly obtained in India from:
(1) Salt pans of Gujarat
(2) Coal seams of the Damodar valley
(3) Iron ore of Bailadila
(4) Monazite and ilmenite in the beach sands of Kerala and Tamil Nadu

Q 17 | geo | India: People and Economy

(1) Indian Space Research Organisation

Q 20 | geo | India: People and Economy
Which of the following is NOT suggested in the chapter for the conservation of mineral resources?
(1) Developing alternative energy sources like solar, wind, wave and geothermal
(2) Using scrap metals for recycling, especially copper, lead and zinc
(3) Using substitutes for scarce metals
(4) Increasing the export of strategic and scarce minerals
= 4
~ The chapter recommends reducing the export of strategic and scarce minerals so that existing reserves last longer.
