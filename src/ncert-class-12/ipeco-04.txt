Q 1 | geo | India: People and Economy
India accounts for about 2.45 per cent of the world's surface area. What share of the world's water resources does it have, according to the chapter?
(1) About 1 per cent
(2) About 4 per cent
(3) About 17 per cent
(4) About 25 per cent
= 2
~ India has about 4 per cent of the world's water resources but more than 17 per cent of its population.

Q 3 | geo | India: People and Economy
Of the mean annual flow of India's rivers, about how much surface water can actually be utilised because of topographical, hydrological and other constraints?
(1) About 690 cubic km (32 per cent)
(2) About 1,869 cubic km (100 per cent)
(3) About 100 cubic km (5 per cent)

~ India has about 10,360 rivers and tributaries longer than 1.6 km, but only about 32 per cent of the surface water can be used.

Q 4 | geo | India: People and Economy

(1) About 10 per cent
(2) About 33 per cent
(3) About 60 per cent
(4) About 90 per cent
= 3
~ Precipitation is relatively high in their catchments. The flow of south Indian rivers like the Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri has been largely harnessed.

Q 5 | geo | India: People and Economy
Groundwater utilisation is very high in which of the following states?
(1) Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan and Tamil Nadu
(2) Chhattisgarh, Odisha and Kerala
(3) Bihar and Tripura only
(4) Arunachal Pradesh and Sikkim
= 1
~ Chhattisgarh, Odisha and Kerala use only a small part of their groundwater potential, while Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Tripura and Maharashtra use it at a moderate rate.

Q 6 | geo | India: People and Economy
Agriculture accounts for what share of surface water and groundwater utilisation in India?
(1) 50 per cent of surface water and 50 per cent of groundwater
(2) 89 per cent of surface water and 92 per cent of groundwater
(3) 9 per cent of surface water and 5 per cent of groundwater
(4) 2 per cent of surface water and 5 per cent of groundwater
= 2
~ The industrial sector uses 2 per cent of surface water and 5 per cent of groundwater; the domestic sector uses 9 per cent of surface water.

Q 12 | geo | India: People and Economy
"Haryali" is a watershed development project that is:
(1) A rainwater harvesting law of Tamil Nadu
(2) Run by a private company in Rajasthan
(3) Sponsored by the Central Government and executed by Gram Panchayats with people's participation

Q 15 | geo | India: People and Economy
In Rajasthan, covered underground tanks built near or in houses to store harvested rainwater are locally called:
(1) Johad
(2) Bugyal
(3) Kayal
(4) Kund or Tanka
= 4
~ Rainwater harvesting checks falling groundwater tables, dilutes contaminants like fluoride and nitrates, prevents soil erosion and arrests salt-water intrusion in coastal areas.

Q 19 | geo | India: People and Economy

Q 20 | geo | India: People and Economy
In urban areas, which of the following is suggested in the chapter as a way to recycle and reuse water?
(1) Using water from bathing and washing utensils for gardening
(2) Drinking untreated industrial water
(3) Pumping more groundwater
(4) Draining all used water into rivers
= 1
~ Using lower-quality water for gardening, industrial cooling and fire fighting conserves better quality water for drinking.
