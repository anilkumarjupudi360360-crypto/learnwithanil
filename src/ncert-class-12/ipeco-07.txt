Q 1 | geo | India: People and Economy
According to the chapter, roads carry about what share of India's passenger and freight traffic every year?
(1) 85 per cent of passenger and 70 per cent of freight traffic
(2) 50 per cent of passenger and 50 per cent of freight traffic
(3) 30 per cent of passenger and 90 per cent of freight traffic
(4) 10 per cent of passenger and 20 per cent of freight traffic
= 1
~ Road transport is relatively suitable for shorter distances.

Q 2 | geo | India: People and Economy
The Shahi (Royal) road built by Sher Shah Suri was renamed during the British period as the:
(1) Golden Quadrilateral
(2) Grand Trunk Road
(3) National Highway 7
(4) East-West Corridor
= 2
~ It connected Calcutta and Peshawar; today the Grand Trunk Road extends from Amritsar to Kolkata.

Q 3 | geo | India: People and Economy
Arrange the following road development milestones in chronological order:
1. Twenty-Year Road Plan
2. Nagpur Plan
3. NHAI operationalised
Select the correct answer:
(1) 1 – 2 – 3
(2) 2 – 1 – 3
(3) 2 – 3 – 1
(4) 3 – 1 – 2
= 2
~ The Nagpur Plan was drawn up in 1943, the Twenty-Year Road Plan came in 1961, and the National Highways Authority of India was operationalised in 1995.

Q 5 | geo | India: People and Economy
The Golden Quadrilateral, about 5,846 km long, connects which cities?
(1) Srinagar, Kanniyakumari, Silchar and Porbandar
(2) Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Chennai
(3) Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai and Kolkata
(4) Mumbai, Pune, Goa and Mangaluru
= 3
~ It is a 4/6-lane, high-density traffic corridor that reduces the time and cost of movement between India's metro cities.

Q 6 | geo | India: People and Economy

(1) Kolkata with Mumbai
(2) Srinagar with Kanniyakumari
(3) Silchar (Assam) with Porbandar (Gujarat)
(4) Delhi with Guwahati
= 3
~ The East-West Corridor is about 3,640 km long; the North-South Corridor (about 4,076 km) connects Srinagar with Kanniyakumari, including the Kochi–Salem spur.

Q 11 | geo | India: People and Economy

(2) 1.435 m
(3) 1.000 m
(4) 0.762 m

Q 15 | geo | India: People and Economy
About what share of India's foreign trade by volume moves through ocean routes?
(1) 25 per cent
(2) 50 per cent
(3) 70 per cent
(4) 95 per cent
= 4
~ About 95 per cent by volume and 70 per cent by value. India has about 12 major and 200 minor ports along a coastline of about 7,517 km.

Q 18 | geo | India: People and Economy
Arrange the following milestones of radio in India in chronological order:
1. Radio renamed Akashwani
2. Radio broadcasting started by the Radio Club of Bombay
3. Indian Broadcasting System renamed All India Radio
Select the correct answer:
(1) 2 – 1 – 3
(2) 1 – 2 – 3
(3) 3 – 2 – 1
(4) 2 – 3 – 1
= 4
~ Radio broadcasting started in 1923; it came under the Indian Broadcasting System in 1930, became All India Radio in 1936 and Akashwani in 1957.
