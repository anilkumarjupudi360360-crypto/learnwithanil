Q 1 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
Which of the following developments of the late 1980s and early 1990s is discussed in the chapter as having changed Indian politics?
1. The end of Congress dominance and the start of an era of coalitions
2. The implementation of the Mandal Commission recommendations
3. The adoption of new economic policies
(1) 1 only
(2) 1 and 2 only
(3) 2 and 3 only
(4) 1, 2 and 3
= 4
~ All three reshaped politics after 1989. The assassination of Rajiv Gandhi in 1991 also led to a change in the leadership of the Congress.

Q 2 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The National Front government formed in 1989 was headed by:
(1) Chandra Shekhar
(2) V.P. Singh
(3) H.D. Deve Gowda
(4) Atal Bihari Vajpayee
= 2
~ The National Front, led by the Janata Dal with regional parties, formed the government with outside support from the BJP and the Left.

Q 3 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The Mandal Commission, whose recommendations were implemented in 1990, dealt with:
(1) Reservations for Other Backward Classes (OBCs) in central government jobs and educational institutions
(2) Reorganisation of states on linguistic lines
(3) Centre–state relations
(4) Electoral reforms
= 1
~ The Commission, chaired by B.P. Mandal and set up by the Janata government, recommended 27 percent reservation for OBCs. Its implementation in 1990 led to protests as well as support.

Q 4 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
In which case did the Supreme Court uphold the decision to give reservations to OBCs in government jobs?
(1) Kesavananda Bharati case
(2) Golaknath case
(3) Indira Sawhney case
(4) S.R. Bommai case
= 3
~ In the Indira Sawhney case (1992) the Court upheld OBC reservations, with conditions such as excluding the "creamy layer" and a general ceiling of 50 percent on reservations.

Q 5 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
Rajiv Gandhi was assassinated in May 1991 during an election campaign. Who became Prime Minister after the 1991 elections?
(1) Manmohan Singh
(2) P.V. Narasimha Rao
(3) Chandra Shekhar
(4) I.K. Gujral
= 2
~ P.V. Narasimha Rao led a Congress government from 1991 to 1996, which began the new economic policy with Manmohan Singh as Finance Minister.

Q 6 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The new economic policy begun in 1991, mentioned in the chapter, is associated with:
(1) More controls and licences on industry
(2) Nationalisation of all industries
(3) Closing the economy to foreign trade
(4) Liberalisation, privatisation and globalisation, also called structural adjustment
= 4
~ The reforms reduced state controls, opened the economy to foreign investment and trade, and gave the private sector a larger role. Most parties later came to accept these policies.

Q 7 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP), which emerged as a party of Dalit voters, was founded in 1984 under the leadership of:
(1) B.R. Ambedkar
(2) Jagjivan Ram
(3) Kanshi Ram
(4) Karpoori Thakur
= 3
~ Kanshi Ram built the BSP from BAMCEF, an organisation of government employees from backward and Dalit communities. The party became a major force in Uttar Pradesh.

Q 8 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The United Front government of 1996, supported from outside by the Congress, was headed first by:
(1) H.D. Deve Gowda
(2) V.P. Singh
(3) Atal Bihari Vajpayee
(4) Chandra Shekhar
= 1
~ Deve Gowda was followed by I.K. Gujral in 1997. The United Front was a coalition of regional and left parties.

Q 9 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The National Democratic Alliance (NDA), which formed governments in 1998 and 1999, was led by:
(1) The Bharatiya Janata Party, with Atal Bihari Vajpayee as Prime Minister
(2) The Congress, with Manmohan Singh as Prime Minister
(3) The Janata Dal, with V.P. Singh as Prime Minister
(4) The CPI(M), with Jyoti Basu as Prime Minister
= 1
~ The BJP led a coalition of many national and regional parties. The NDA government under Vajpayee completed its term in 2004.

Q 10 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The United Progressive Alliance (UPA) government formed in 2004 was headed by:
(1) Sonia Gandhi
(2) Atal Bihari Vajpayee
(3) Manmohan Singh
(4) Pranab Mukherjee
= 3
~ Manmohan Singh led the Congress-led UPA from 2004 to 2014, with outside support from the Left parties in its first term.

Q 11 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
In the 2014 Lok Sabha election, the BJP-led NDA came to power under Narendra Modi. What was significant about the BJP's result?
(1) It was the first coalition government in India
(2) It was the first time a regional party formed the government
(3) No party got any seats
(4) It was the first time since 1984 that a single party won a majority of seats in the Lok Sabha on its own
= 4
~ After thirty years of coalition and minority governments, the BJP won a majority on its own in 2014, and again in 2019. Its government continued to include allies as part of the NDA.

Q 12 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The "era of coalitions" after 1989, according to the chapter, meant that:
(1) Elections stopped being held
(2) No single party usually won a majority, and governments were formed by alliances of national and regional parties
(3) The Congress won every election
(4) State governments were abolished
= 2
~ Between 1989 and 2014 no party won a majority on its own in the Lok Sabha. Regional parties gained an important role in forming and running governments at the Centre.

Q 13 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
Which of the following is part of the "growing consensus" among parties that the chapter identifies after 1989?
(1) Rejection of all reservation
(2) Agreement on new economic policies and on accepting the political and social claims of backward castes
(3) Abolition of the federal system
(4) Return to a one-party system
= 2
~ Most parties came to accept economic reforms, the claims of OBCs for representation, the role of state-level parties and pragmatic coalition-building.

Q 14 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The Mandal Commission (the second Backward Classes Commission) was appointed by which government?
(1) The Congress government under Indira Gandhi
(2) The National Front government under V.P. Singh
(3) The NDA government under Vajpayee
(4) The Janata government under Morarji Desai
= 4
~ The Janata government decided to set it up in 1978, and it began work in 1979. The report came in 1980, but it was implemented only in 1990 by the National Front government.

Q 15 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
Why did the political importance of regional parties increase after 1989, according to the chapter?
(1) Because coalition governments at the Centre depended on their support, and they could influence national policies
(2) Because national parties were banned
(3) Because Parliament was abolished
(4) Because the Constitution gave them reserved seats
= 1
~ Regional parties became partners in coalition governments and pressed for regional interests in national policy. This deepened federalism in practice.

Q 16 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The rise of OBC parties and leaders in states like Uttar Pradesh and Bihar after 1989, discussed in the chapter, reflects:
(1) The end of democracy
(2) The decline of all caste identities
(3) The growing political assertion of backward castes and their demand for a share in power
(4) The return of princely rule
= 3
~ Parties like the Samajwadi Party and the Rashtriya Janata Dal mobilised OBC voters. Their rise changed the social base of power in north India.

Q 17 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
Which statement about the coalition governments of the 1990s is correct?
(1) They were all led by the Congress
(2) They never included regional parties
(3) They brought together parties with different ideologies, so policies were often made through compromise and a common minimum programme
(4) They abolished the office of Prime Minister
= 3
~ Coalitions needed agreements like a common minimum programme. This encouraged moderation and pragmatic politics, though it also caused instability at times.

Q 18 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
Which Prime Minister headed the United Front government after H.D. Deve Gowda in 1997?
(1) I.K. Gujral
(2) Chandra Shekhar
(3) P.V. Narasimha Rao
(4) Charan Singh
= 1
~ I.K. Gujral became Prime Minister in April 1997. The United Front government fell when the Congress withdrew its support, leading to elections in 1998.

Q 19 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
Why did the implementation of the Mandal Commission report in 1990 lead to intense debate?
(1) Because it abolished all existing reservations
(2) Because it extended reservations to OBCs in central government jobs, which some groups supported strongly and others opposed in protests
(3) Because it reorganised the states
(4) Because it ended the Planning Commission
= 2
~ Supporters saw it as a step towards social justice for backward castes; opponents argued it was unfair. The issue shaped politics for years and was finally settled by the Supreme Court.

Q 20 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
According to the chapter, which of the following best describes politics after 1989?
(1) The return of one-party dominance by the Congress
(2) The abolition of regional parties
(3) A system with only two national parties
(4) More competitive politics, with coalition governments, the rise of regional and backward-caste parties, and a broad consensus on some major policies
= 4
~ Indian politics became more plural and competitive. The chapter notes both the challenges and the democratic deepening that came with these changes.
