Q 1 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
Who became Prime Minister after the death of Jawaharlal Nehru in 1964?
(1) Indira Gandhi
(2) Lal Bahadur Shastri
(3) Morarji Desai
(4) K. Kamaraj
= 2
~ Congress president K. Kamaraj consulted party leaders and MPs, and Lal Bahadur Shastri was chosen unanimously. The smooth succession was a sign of democratic maturity.

Q 2 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The slogan "Jai Jawan Jai Kisan" was given by:
(1) Jawaharlal Nehru
(2) Indira Gandhi
(3) Morarji Desai
(4) Lal Bahadur Shastri
= 4
~ Shastri coined the slogan during the 1965 war and the food crisis, honouring both soldiers and farmers.

Q 3 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
Where did Lal Bahadur Shastri die in January 1966?
(1) Tashkent
(2) New Delhi
(3) Moscow
(4) Shimla
= 1
~ Shastri died in Tashkent, then in the USSR (now in Uzbekistan), soon after signing the agreement with Pakistan's President Ayub Khan.

Q 4 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The results of the 1967 general election have been described as a "political earthquake" because:
(1) The elections were cancelled
(2) The Congress won every state
(3) The Congress lost its majority in many states and its majority in the Lok Sabha was much reduced
(4) No party contested the elections
= 3
~ The Congress lost power in several states, including Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Odisha, Madras and Kerala, and was left with a slim majority in the Lok Sabha.

Q 5 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The strategy of bringing together opposition parties of different ideologies to defeat the Congress, before the 1967 elections, was called:
(1) Garibi Hatao
(2) Grand alliance
(3) Non-Congressism
(4) Total revolution
= 3
~ Ram Manohar Lohia argued that Congress rule was undemocratic and against the interests of the poor, so all opposition parties should unite against it.

Q 6 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
After 1967, coalition governments of non-Congress parties in several states were called:
(1) National Front
(2) Samyukta Vidhayak Dal (SVD) governments
(3) United Progressive Alliance
(4) Janata governments
= 2
~ The SVD governments brought together parties of very different ideologies. In Punjab, for example, the government included the Akali Dal and the Jana Sangh along with other parties.

Q 7 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The phrase "Aya Ram, Gaya Ram" refers to:
(1) A famous election slogan
(2) A popular film
(3) A government scheme
(4) Frequent defections of legislators from one party to another, after an MLA in Haryana changed parties three times in one fortnight in 1967
= 4
~ Gaya Lal, an MLA in Haryana, changed his party three times in a fortnight. The phrase became a symbol of unprincipled defections, which the anti-defection law of 1985 later tried to stop.

Q 8 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The group of senior Congress leaders who controlled the party organisation in the 1960s was called:
(1) The Syndicate
(2) The Young Turks
(3) The Kitchen Cabinet
(4) The Grand Alliance
= 1
~ The Syndicate included K. Kamaraj, S.K. Patil, S. Nijalingappa, N. Sanjeeva Reddy and Atulya Ghosh. It had helped make Indira Gandhi Prime Minister but later clashed with her.

Q 9 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
In the 1969 presidential election, the official Congress candidate was defeated. Who won with Indira Gandhi's support?
(1) V.V. Giri
(2) N. Sanjeeva Reddy
(3) Zakir Hussain
(4) Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed
= 1
~ Indira Gandhi called for a "conscience vote", and V.V. Giri defeated N. Sanjeeva Reddy, the Syndicate-backed official candidate. This led to the split in the Congress.

Q 10 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
After the Congress split in 1969, the group led by the Syndicate came to be known as:
(1) Congress (R)
(2) Congress (I)
(3) Congress (O)
(4) Congress (S)
= 3
~ Congress (O) stood for "Organisation", led by the Syndicate. Indira Gandhi's group was called Congress (R), the "Requisitionists".

Q 11 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
Which of the following steps did Indira Gandhi take around 1969–71 to give the government a radical, pro-poor image?
(1) Privatising all banks
(2) Nationalising fourteen major private banks and abolishing the privy purses of former princes
(3) Abolishing the Planning Commission
(4) Joining a military alliance
= 2
~ Bank nationalisation in 1969 and the abolition of privy purses by the 26th Amendment in 1971 were part of her move towards a socialist programme.

Q 12 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The election slogan "Garibi Hatao" (remove poverty) was used by Indira Gandhi in the elections of:
(1) 1952
(2) 1962
(3) 1967
(4) 1971
= 4
~ In the 1971 Lok Sabha election, Congress (R) won 352 seats with the Garibi Hatao slogan, while the opposition's "grand alliance" campaigned on "Indira Hatao".

Q 13 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The "privy purses", abolished in 1971, were:
(1) Taxes paid by farmers
(2) Salaries of MPs
(3) Funds for political parties
(4) Payments and privileges guaranteed by the government to the former rulers of princely states
= 4
~ When the princely states were integrated, their rulers were promised these privileges. Indira Gandhi argued they were against the principle of equality, and the 26th Amendment abolished them.

Q 14 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The anti-Congress "grand alliance" in the 1971 Lok Sabha election was formed by:
(1) Non-communist, non-Congress parties, including the Congress (O), Jana Sangh, Swatantra Party and Samyukta Socialist Party
(2) The CPI and CPI(M) alone
(3) Regional parties of south India only
(4) Indira Gandhi's Congress (R) and the Syndicate together
= 1
~ The grand alliance had no clear programme beyond removing Indira Gandhi. Voters chose her positive slogan of Garibi Hatao, and the alliance won only a few seats.

Q 15 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
What was the "Kamaraj Plan" of 1963?
(1) A plan for heavy industries
(2) A proposal that senior Congress leaders resign from ministerial posts to work for the party organisation
(3) A plan to reorganise states
(4) An economic plan for Tamil Nadu
= 2
~ Under the plan, several Chief Ministers and Union ministers resigned to strengthen the party. Kamaraj himself resigned as Chief Minister of Madras.

Q 16 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
After the 1971 victory, the chapter says the Congress system was "restored", but in a different form. What was the main difference?
(1) The Congress became a coalition of foreign parties
(2) The Congress now had no leaders
(3) The new Congress depended more on the popularity of one leader and less on a strong organisation with factions
(4) The Congress gave up contesting elections
= 3
~ Indira Gandhi's Congress won on her personal appeal and a populist programme, with the party organisation weaker than before. The old system of internal factions and accommodation was less important.

Q 17 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
The 1971 victory of Indira Gandhi was strengthened further by which event of December 1971?
(1) The first nuclear test
(2) The Emergency
(3) India's victory in the war that led to the creation of Bangladesh
(4) The death of the Syndicate leaders
= 3
~ The liberation of Bangladesh made Indira Gandhi immensely popular, and the Congress also won most state assembly elections held in 1972.

Q 18 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
Which of the following was a reason for the decline of the Congress in the 1967 elections?
(1) The Congress had too many popular leaders
(2) Economic difficulties such as drought, price rise and food shortages, along with the devaluation of the rupee
(3) The abolition of elections
(4) The end of the Cold War
= 2
~ The mid-1960s saw failed monsoons, food shortages, rising prices and the devaluation of the rupee in 1966, causing discontent. The death of two Prime Ministers also hit the party.

Q 19 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
Who became Prime Minister after Lal Bahadur Shastri's death in 1966?
(1) Morarji Desai
(2) K. Kamaraj
(3) Gulzarilal Nanda
(4) Indira Gandhi
= 4
~ Indira Gandhi defeated Morarji Desai in a secret ballot among Congress MPs. Gulzarilal Nanda served briefly as acting Prime Minister in between.

Q 20 | pol | Politics in India since Independence
Why does the chapter say the coalition governments formed after 1967 were short-lived?
(1) Because they were made up of parties with very different ideologies, held together mainly by opposition to the Congress, and defections were common
(2) Because the Constitution banned coalitions
(3) Because the Congress dissolved them by law
(4) Because no elections were held
= 1
~ Parties like the Jana Sangh and the Communists had little in common. Their coalitions often broke down quickly, and defections made governments unstable.
