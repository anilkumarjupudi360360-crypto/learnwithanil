Q 4 | geo | India: People and Economy
What was the density of population of India as per the 2011 census?
(1) 117 persons per sq km
(2) 313 persons per sq km
(3) 382 persons per sq km
(4) 1,102 persons per sq km
= 3
~ Density rose from 117 persons per sq km in 1951 to 382 in 2011. It ranges from 17 in Arunachal Pradesh to 11,297 in the NCT of Delhi.

Q 6 | geo | India: People and Economy
Physiological density of population is calculated as:
(1) Total population ÷ total area
(2) Total population ÷ net cultivated area
(3) Agricultural population ÷ net cultivable area
(4) Urban population ÷ urban area
= 2
~ Agricultural density is the agricultural population (cultivators, agricultural labourers and their families) divided by net cultivable area.

Q 7 | geo | India: People and Economy
In which decade did India record a negative growth rate of population?
(1) 1901–1911
(2) 1911–1921
(3) 1941–1951
(4) 1971–1981
= 2
~ Population fell slightly (–0.31 per cent) between 1911 and 1921, during the stagnant phase of 1901–1921.

Q 8 | geo | India: People and Economy
Match the phases of population growth in India with their descriptions:
A. 1901–1921 — 1. Population explosion
B. 1921–1951 — 2. Stagnant or stationary phase
C. 1951–1981 — 3. Growth rate high but gradually slowing down
D. Post-1981 — 4. Steady growth
Select the correct answer:
(1) A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
(2) A-4, B-2, C-3, D-1
(3) A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
(4) A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3
= 1
~ The explosion phase saw an average annual growth of about 2.2 per cent. After 1981, falling birth rates have slowed growth.

Q 9 | geo | India: People and Economy
Which of the following is NOT given as a cause of the population explosion during 1951–1981?
(1) A rapid fall in the mortality rate
(2) A high fertility rate
(3) International migration of Tibetans, Bangladeshis, Nepalis and others
(4) A sharp increase in the mean age at marriage
= 4
~ A rising age at marriage and improved female education helped slow growth after 1981; they did not cause the explosion.

Q 15 | geo | India: People and Economy
A person who works for at least 183 days (six months) in a year is classified by the census as a:
(1) Marginal worker
(2) Seasonal migrant
(3) Non-worker
(4) Main worker
= 4
~ A marginal worker works for less than 183 days a year. The population is divided into main workers, marginal workers and non-workers.

Q 18 | geo | India: People and Economy
The share of workers in the primary sector (cultivators and agricultural labourers) declined from 58.2 per cent in 2001 to what in 2011?

(2) 54.6 per cent
(3) 66.8 per cent
(4) 30.0 per cent
= 2
~ In 2011, about 54.6 per cent of workers were in the primary sector, 3.8 per cent in the secondary sector and 41.6 per cent in the tertiary sector.
