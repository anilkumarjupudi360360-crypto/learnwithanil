Q 1 | ram | Characters
Who was Mandodari and what was her lineage ?
(1) Ravana's sister and daughter of Sage Vishrava
(2) Ravana's wife and daughter of the demon Mayasura
(3) Vibhishana's wife and daughter of the Ocean God
(4) Shurpanakha's daughter and queen of Lanka
= 2
~ Mandodari was Ravana's chief queen and the daughter of the asura architect Maya (Mayasura) and the apsara Hema (Uttara Kanda, Valmiki Ramayana). Sage Vishrava was Ravana's father, and Ravana's sister was Shurpanakha, not Mandodari. Vibhishana's wife was Sarama.

Q 2 | ram | Kandas & Places
Which place in the Ramayana is associated with the exile period and is believed to be where Sita was abducted by Ravana ?
(1) Panchavati
(2) Chitrakoot
(3) Dandakaranya
(4) Kishkindha
= 1
~ On sage Agastya's advice Rama, Sita and Lakshmana built their hut at Panchavati on the banks of the Godavari, and it was from there that Ravana carried Sita away (Aranya Kanda). Dandakaranya is the wider forest region in which Panchavati lies, so it is less exact. Chitrakoot was their earlier stay, and Kishkindha was the Vanara kingdom.

Q 3 | ram | Dynasties & Events
Who killed the demoness Tataka, and why ?
(1) Rama, because she attacked Vishwamitra's hermitage
(2) Lakshmana, because she insulted Rama
(3) Rama, under Vishwamitra's order, despite her being a woman
(4) Bharata, during the search for Rama
= 3
~ In the Bala Kanda, Vishwamitra took young Rama and Lakshmana to the Tataka forest. Rama hesitated to kill a woman, but Vishwamitra told him it was his duty to protect the people, so Rama killed Tataka on the sage's order. The demons who attacked Vishwamitra's yajna were her sons Maricha and Subahu, which is why option (1) is not the best answer.

Q 4 | ram | Characters
Who was Shabari ?
(1) A sage who gave Rama a bow
(2) Rama's devotee living in a forest
(3) A rakshasi who later became a devotee
(4) A queen who donated her kingdom to Rama
= 2
~ Shabari was an aged woman ascetic and disciple of sage Matanga who lived in the forest near Lake Pampa, waiting for Rama. When Rama came she welcomed him with fruits and directed him to Sugriva at Rishyamuka (Aranya Kanda). She was neither a rakshasi nor a queen.

Q 5 | ram | Kandas & Places
What is the name of the mountain to which Hanuman went to obtain the Sanjeevani herb ?
(1) Mahendragiri
(2) Kailasha
(3) Gandhamadana
(4) Dronagiri
= 4
~ In the Yuddha Kanda, when Lakshmana and the army lay struck down, Hanuman flew to the Himalayas and carried back the whole herb-bearing peak. Popular tradition and many retellings call this peak Dronagiri; Valmiki describes it as the herb mountain between the Rishabha and Kailasa peaks. Mahendragiri was the hill from which Hanuman leapt across the sea to Lanka.

Q 6 | ram | Dynasties & Events
Which dynasty did Lord Rama belong to ?
(1) Yadava Dynasty
(2) Maurya Dynasty
(3) Suryavansha (Surya Dynasty)
(4) Chandravansha (Chandra Dynasty)
= 3
~ Rama was born in the Ikshvaku line of the Suryavamsha (Solar dynasty), which traces its descent from Vivasvan (Surya) through Manu and Ikshvaku and ruled from Ayodhya. The Chandravamsha (Lunar dynasty) was the line of the Kurus and Yadavas, and Krishna was a Yadava. The Mauryas were a historical dynasty.

Q 7 | ram | Dynasties & Events
According to the Yuddha Kanda, which weapon did Rama use to kill Ravana ?
(1) Narayanastra
(2) Agneyastra
(3) Brahmastra
(4) Indrastra
= 3
~ In the Yuddha Kanda, Indra's charioteer Matali reminded Rama of the Brahmastra, which sage Agastya had given him. Rama invoked it with mantras, and it pierced Ravana's chest and killed him. The Narayanastra is associated with Ashwatthama in the Mahabharata war.

Q 8 | ram | Characters
Who has been called Daasharathi in 'Ramayana' ?
(1) Vali
(2) Hanuma
(3) Rama
(4) Dasharatha
= 3
~ "Daasharathi" means "son of Dasharatha" and is a well-known name of Rama. The Telugu Dasarathi Satakam of Kancherla Gopanna (Bhakta Ramadasu) addresses Rama by this name. Dasharatha is the father himself, not "son of Dasharatha".

Q 9 | fes | Hindu Festivals
Rama navami is celebrated in which Telugu month ?
(1) In Vaishakha
(2) In Chaitra
(3) In Phalguna
(4) In Kartika
= 2
~ Sri Rama Navami falls on Chaitra Shukla Navami, the ninth day of the bright fortnight of the month of Chaitra, and marks Rama's birth. In Andhra Pradesh and Telangana it is marked by the Sita Rama Kalyanam, most famously at Bhadrachalam. Phalguna is the month just before Chaitra.

Q 10 | ram | Kandas & Places
What is the Second Chapter (Kanda) of Ramayana ?
(1) Aranyakanda
(2) Balakanda
(3) Ayodhyakanda
(4) Sundarakanda
= 3
~ Valmiki's Ramayana has seven kandas in this order: Bala, Ayodhya, Aranya, Kishkindha, Sundara, Yuddha and Uttara. The Ayodhya Kanda is the second; it narrates Kaikeyi's two boons and Rama's exile. The Bala Kanda is the first.

Q 11 | bhg | Skandhas & Avatars
The incarnation of Rama is mentioned in which verse (Skandha) in the Bhagavatam ?
(1) In the Navamaskandha
(2) In the Prathamaskandha
(3) In the Saptamaskandha
(4) In the Dashamaskandha
= 1
~ The story of Rama is told in the Ninth Skandha (Navama Skandha) of the Srimad Bhagavatam (chapters 10–11), as part of the account of the Solar dynasty. The Tenth Skandha (Dashama) is devoted to Krishna. The First Skandha only lists the avatars briefly.

Q 12 | ram | Characters
What is the name of Lakshmana's wife ?
(1) Mandodari
(2) Uttara
(3) Uurmila
(4) Priyamvada
= 3
~ Lakshmana married Urmila, daughter of King Janaka and younger sister of Sita, at the same time as Rama's wedding (Bala Kanda). Mandodari was Ravana's wife. Uttara was King Virata's daughter and Abhimanyu's wife in the Mahabharata.

Q 13 | pur | Deities & Legends
Among these, Shukracharya is the teacher of whom ?
(1) For gods
(2) For humans
(3) For demons
(4) For yakhshas
= 3
~ Shukracharya (Ushanas, a descendant of sage Bhrigu) was the guru of the asuras (daityas and danavas) and knew the Mritasanjivani vidya for reviving the dead. Brihaspati was the guru of the devas (gods).

Q 14 | mbh | Bhagavad Gita & Events
What unique weapon did Arjuna receive from Lord Shiva ?
(1) Vajrayudha
(2) Sudarshana Chakra
(3) Brahmastra
(4) Pashupatastra
= 4
~ In the Vana Parva (Kirata episode), Arjuna did penance and fought Shiva, who came disguised as a hunter (Kirata). Pleased with his valour, Shiva gave him the Pashupatastra, his own weapon. The Vajra belongs to Indra and the Sudarshana Chakra to Vishnu (Krishna).

Q 15 | mbh | Bhagavad Gita & Events
Who killed Dronacharya in the Kurukshetra war ?
(1) Arjuna
(2) Bhima
(3) Dhrishtadyumna
(4) Yudhishthira
= 3
~ On the fifteenth day, Drona laid down his weapons after Yudhishthira said "Ashwatthama is dead" (meaning the elephant of that name). Dhrishtadyumna, who had been born from the sacrificial fire to kill Drona, then beheaded him (Drona Parva). Yudhishthira's half-truth only made Drona give up fighting.

Q 16 | mbh | Characters
Who was the only warrior who survived from the Kaurava side after the war ?
(1) Dushasana
(2) Shakuni
(3) Ashwatthama
(4) Karna
= 3
~ Among the options, only Ashwatthama, Drona's son, survived the war; he later led the night raid on the Pandava camp (Sauptika Parva). Strictly, Kripacharya and Kritavarma also survived with him. Dushasana was killed by Bhima, Shakuni by Sahadeva and Karna by Arjuna.

Q 17 | mbh | Bhagavad Gita & Events
What curse did Arjuna suffer when he rejected Urvashi's advances in Indra's court ?
(1) He would never be able to marry
(2) He would be born as a eunuch in his next life
(3) He would lose all interest in women
(4) He would live as a eunuch for one year
= 4
~ In Indra's heaven (Vana Parva), Arjuna refused Urvashi because he regarded her as a mother of the Kuru line, and she cursed him to become a eunuch. Indra limited the curse to one year, which Arjuna used during the year in disguise (ajnatavasa) as Brihannala, the dance teacher in King Virata's palace.

Q 18 | mbh | Bhagavad Gita & Events
Before the Kurukshetra war, who went to Hastinapura as a peace envoy on behalf of the Pandavas ?
(1) Vidura
(2) Krishna
(3) Yudhishthira
(4) Sanjaya
= 2
~ In the Udyoga Parva, Krishna went to Hastinapura as the Pandavas' peace envoy and asked for even five villages for them. Duryodhana refused and tried to seize him, and Krishna showed his cosmic form. Sanjaya was Dhritarashtra's envoy sent to the Pandavas.

Q 19 | mbh | Parvas & Places
How many Parvas are there in the Mahabharata ?
(1) 16
(2) 18
(3) 12
(4) 20
= 2
~ The Mahabharata is divided into 18 major parvas (books), from the Adi Parva to the Svargarohana Parva; the Harivamsha is a supplement (khila). The number 18 recurs in the epic: 18 days of war, 18 akshauhinis and 18 chapters of the Bhagavad Gita.

Q 20 | mbh | Bhagavad Gita & Events
On which day of the Kurukshetra war was Bhishma fatally wounded and forced to lie on a bed of arrows ?
(1) 5th day
(2) 10th day
(3) 12th day
(4) 15th day
= 2
~ Bhishma led the Kaurava army for the first ten days. On the tenth day Arjuna, keeping Shikhandi in front, pierced him with arrows, and Bhishma fell onto a bed of arrows (sharashayya), where he waited for Uttarayana before giving up his life (Bhishma Parva). Drona then became commander.

Q 21 | mbh | Bhagavad Gita & Events
In which chapter of the Bhagavad Gita does Lord Krishna describe the concept of the "Kshetra" and "Kshetrajna" ?
(1) Chapter 10
(2) Chapter 13
(3) Chapter 12
(4) Chapter 15
= 2
~ Chapter 13 of the Gita, Kshetra-Kshetrajna Vibhaga Yoga, explains the body as the field (kshetra) and the self that knows it as the knower of the field (kshetrajna). Chapter 10 is Vibhuti Yoga, Chapter 12 is Bhakti Yoga and Chapter 15 is Purushottama Yoga.

Q 22 | mbh | Bhagavad Gita & Events
According to Bhagavad Gita, which of the following is not one of the three gunas (qualities) of material nature ?
(1) Sattva
(2) Tamasu
(3) Rajasu
(4) Atma
= 4
~ The Gita (especially Chapter 14, Gunatraya Vibhaga Yoga) says that prakriti (material nature) has three gunas: sattva, rajas and tamas. The Atma (self) is not a guna; it is beyond the three gunas.

Q 23 | mbh | Bhagavad Gita & Events
According to the Bhagavad Gita, Krishna tells Arjuna that he has the right to perform his duty, but not to claim what ?
(1) Ask for help
(2) Expect fame
(3) Abandon action
(4) The fruits of his actions
= 4
~ Bhagavad Gita 2.47 says "karmanyevadhikaraste ma phaleshu kadachana": you have a right to action alone, never to its fruits. This is the basis of nishkama karma (action without desire for results). The same verse warns against attachment to inaction, so option (3) is the opposite of the teaching.

Q 24 | mbh | Bhagavad Gita & Events
As stated in the Bhagavad Gita, which power or discipline does Krishna describe as capable of burning all karmic bondage to ashes ?
(1) Meditation
(2) Fasting
(3) Selfless service
(4) True knowledge (Jnana)
= 4
~ Bhagavad Gita 4.37 says that just as a blazing fire turns firewood to ashes, the fire of knowledge (jnanagni) burns all karma to ashes. The verse is from Chapter 4, Jnana Karma Sanyasa Yoga. Meditation and selfless service are taught elsewhere, but this image is used only for jnana.

Q 25 | mbh | Characters
Who taught the Mahabharata to Janamejaya ?
(1) Vyasa
(2) Vaisampayana
(3) Shuka
(4) Asteeka
= 2
~ At Vyasa's command, his disciple Vaishampayana recited the Mahabharata to King Janamejaya, Parikshit's son, during the snake sacrifice (sarpa satra) (Adi Parva). Vyasa composed the epic but did not recite it himself on that occasion. Astika was the young sage who stopped the snake sacrifice.

Q 26 | bhg | Characters
Who narrated the Śrīmad Bhāgavatam to King Parīkṣit ?
(1) Veda Vyāsa
(2) Suka Maharshi
(3) Nārada Muni
(4) Sanat Kumāra
= 2
~ Shuka Maharshi, Vyasa's son, narrated the Bhagavatam to King Parikshit over seven days on the bank of the Ganga while the king awaited death from Takshaka's bite. Vyasa composed the Bhagavatam and taught it to Shuka, and Narada had inspired Vyasa to compose it.

Q 27 | bhg | Skandhas & Avatars
Which incarnation of Lord Vishnu appeared to return the Vedas that had been stolen by the demon Somakasurudu ?
(1) Kurma
(2) Matsya
(3) Varaha
(4) Narasimha
= 2
~ In the Matsya (fish) avatar, Vishnu killed the demon who had stolen the Vedas from Brahma and restored them; Telugu tradition names this demon Somakasura, while the Bhagavatam calls him Hayagriva. Kurma supported Mandara mountain during the churning of the ocean, and Varaha lifted the Earth from the ocean.

Q 28 | bhg | Skandhas & Avatars
Which incarnation of Lord Viṣhṇu lifted Govardhana Hill ?
(1) Kūrma
(2) Rāma
(3) Vāmana
(4) Sri Krishna
= 4
~ Krishna lifted Govardhana hill and held it up for seven days to shelter the people and cattle of Vraja from the heavy rain sent by Indra (Bhagavatam, Tenth Skandha). Kurma bore Mandara mountain, and Vamana measured the three worlds in three steps.

Q 29 | bhg | Skandhas & Avatars
Who was the demon killed by Lord Varāha in the Bhāgavatam ?
(1) Hiraṇyakaśipu
(2) Hiraṇyākṣa
(3) Rāvaṇa
(4) Baka
= 2
~ Varaha killed Hiranyaksha, who had taken the Earth (Bhudevi) down into the cosmic waters, and lifted the Earth up on his tusks (Bhagavatam, Third Skandha). Hiranyaksha's brother Hiranyakashipu was killed by Narasimha, Ravana by Rama and Bakasura by Krishna.

Q 30 | bhg | Places & Events
What was the curse given by the sage that led to King Parīkṣit's death ?
(1) He would be turned into a demon
(2) He would lose his kingdom
(3) He would be killed by a snake bite
(4) He would forget all scriptures
= 3
~ Parikshit placed a dead snake on the shoulder of sage Shamika, who was in meditation. The sage's son Shringi cursed that the king would die from the bite of the serpent Takshaka on the seventh day (Bhagavatam, First Skandha). Parikshit spent those seven days hearing the Bhagavatam from Shuka.

Q 31 | bhg | Characters
Who instructed the goddess of devotion to sing Kesavakirtanam in the Bhagavatam ?
(1) Narada
(2) Sanaka
(3) Vaishampayana
(4) Parashara
= 1
~ In the Bhagavata Mahatmya (from the Padma Purana), Narada meets Bhakti Devi grieving at Vrindavan over her aged sons Jnana and Vairagya. He consoles her and tells her to remember and sing the glory of Keshava (Krishna). Later the Sanaka brothers recite the Bhagavatam in a seven-day session that Narada arranges.

Q 32 | mbh | Characters
Why did Krishnadvaipayana Maharshi get the name Vyasa ?
(1) For writing the Puranas
(2) For dividing the Vedas
(3) For writing the Mahabharata
(4) For preaching the Brahma Sutras
= 2
~ "Vyasa" means one who divides or arranges. Krishna Dvaipayana divided the single Veda into four, the Rig, Yajur, Sama and Atharva, and taught them to Paila, Vaishampayana, Jaimini and Sumantu, so he is called Veda Vyasa. He also composed the Mahabharata and the Puranas, but his title comes from dividing the Vedas.

Q 33 | bhg | Characters
Who is the mother of Hiranyaksha ?
(1) Aditi
(2) Kadru
(3) Diti
(4) Suparni
= 3
~ Hiranyaksha and Hiranyakashipu were the twin sons of sage Kashyapa and Diti, which is why they are called Daityas (Bhagavatam, Third Skandha). Aditi, another wife of Kashyapa, was the mother of the Adityas (devas), and Kadru was the mother of the nagas (serpents).

Q 34 | bhg | Skandhas & Avatars
In which skandha of the Bhagavatam is the story of Ambareesh ?
(1) In the ninth verse
(2) In the sixth verse
(3) In the seventh verse
(4) In the eleventh verse
= 1
~ The story of King Ambarisha, a great devotee of Vishnu who kept the Ekadashi vow, and of sage Durvasa being chased by the Sudarshana Chakra, is told in the Ninth Skandha (chapters 4–5) of the Bhagavatam. In the options, "verse" stands for skandha (canto).

Q 35 | mbh | Characters
What is the real name of Bhishma ?
(1) Devadatta
(2) Devavrata
(3) Dhanunjaya
(4) Yudhishtira
= 2
~ Bhishma was born Devavrata, the son of King Shantanu and Ganga. He came to be called Bhishma ("one of the terrible vow") after he vowed lifelong celibacy and gave up the throne so that his father could marry Satyavati (Adi Parva). Dhananjaya is a name of Arjuna, and Devadatta is the name of Arjuna's conch.

Q 36 | ram | Dynasties & Events
Who brought Ganga to the earth ?
(1) Bhagiratha
(2) Dileepa
(3) Sagara
(4) Raghu
= 1
~ King Bhagiratha of the Ikshvaku line did severe penance to bring the heavenly Ganga down to earth, so that her waters could liberate his ancestors, the sixty thousand sons of Sagara burnt by sage Kapila; Shiva received her in his matted hair (Bala Kanda). Sagara could not find a way to bring her down, and Sagara's grandson Amshuman and Bhagiratha's father Dilipa failed despite long penance. Ganga is therefore called Bhagirathi.

Q 37 | bhg | Characters
Who cursed Parikshit ?
(1) Āstīka
(2) Jaratkaru
(3) Srungi
(4) Krusha
= 3
~ Shringi, the son of sage Shamika, cursed Parikshit to die from Takshaka's bite within seven days, because the king had placed a dead snake on his father's shoulders. Astika was the sage who stopped Janamejaya's snake sacrifice, and Jaratkaru was Astika's father. Krisha was Shringi's companion who told him of the insult (Adi Parva).

Q 38 | pur | Puranas
As per the Matsya Purana, who is saved by Lord Vishnu in His Matsya (fish) avatar during the great deluge ?
(1) Parikshit
(2) Manu
(3) Daksha
(4) Vyasa
= 2
~ In the Matsya Purana, Vishnu as a fish warns King Manu (Vaivasvata Manu) of the coming deluge and tows his boat, which carries the seeds of all life, to safety through the flood. The Purana itself is a dialogue between Matsya and Manu.

Q 39 | pur | Puranas
What critical preservation role does the Matsya avatar of Vishnu fulfill in the Matsya Purāṇa's flood narrative ?
(1) Destroys demons
(2) Rescues the Vedas and seeds for mankind
(3) Brings back the sun after eclipse
(4) Guides the gods in battle
= 2
~ In the flood story, the Matsya avatar's main task is to preserve what is needed for the next creation: Manu, the seeds of all beings, and the sacred knowledge (Vedas) for mankind. Killing demons is not the focus of the Matsya Purana's flood story, and the other options have no link to it.

Q 40 | pur | Puranas
According to the Brahma Purāṇa, how is Varaha (the boar incarnation of Vishnu) associated with the Pitrus (ancestors) ?
(1) He forgot them during cosmic dissolution
(2) He performed Shraddha rites for them after rescuing them from a curse
(3) He judged their sins in Yamaloka
(4) He sent them to Swarga based on merit
= 2
~ Puranic tradition links the Varaha avatar with the origin of shraddha: after lifting the Earth, the boar is said to have made the first pinda offerings to the Pitrs from the clods on his tusks (also told in the Mahabharata, Shanti Parva). Judging sins belongs to Yama, and going to Swarga by merit is the working of karma, not Varaha's role. The "rescue from a curse" detail is APPSC's framing. (Official final key; some sources differ.)

Q 41 | pur | Puranas
Which phrase best describes the Vishnu Purāṇa's theological stance, as reflected in the identification of Vishnu ?
(1) Vishnu as a limited deity among many
(2) Vishnu as the pervader of cosmic division
(3) Vishnu as the all-pervading Brahman
(4) Vishnu as one among the trimurti
= 3
~ The Vishnu Purana, narrated by Parashara to Maitreya, identifies Vishnu with the supreme, all-pervading Brahman, who is the cause of creation, preservation and dissolution; the name "Vishnu" itself is explained there from the root "vish", to enter or pervade. It treats Brahma and Shiva as his forms, so "one among the trimurti" understates its position.

Q 42 | pur | Puranas
According to the Garuda Purana, what is the primary purpose of human birth ?
(1) Accumulating wealth
(2) Seeking pleasures
(3) Attaining liberation (moksha)
(4) Gaining power over others
= 3
~ The Garuda Purana (especially its Preta Khanda) teaches that human birth is rare and should be used to attain liberation (moksha) through dharma, devotion and knowledge. Spending life on wealth, pleasure or power only leads to further rebirth.

Q 43 | pur | Puranas
What is the recommended practice in the Garuda Purana to aid a soul after death ?
(1) Building temples
(2) Performing Shraddha and charity
(3) Reciting mantras for wealth
(4) Constructing a tomb
= 2
~ The Garuda Purana prescribes the funeral and ancestral rites for the departed: pinda offerings in the days after death, shraddha ceremonies, and gifts (dana), such as the gift of a cow to help the soul cross the Vaitarani river. These help the soul on its onward journey. Building tombs is not part of these rites.

Q 44 | pur | Puranas
What was the main reason for Lord Vishnu's Matsya (Fish) avatar as per the Matsya Purana ?
(1) To defeat a demon in the ocean
(2) To recover the stolen Vedas
(3) To create the universe
(4) To teach Manu about warfare
= 2
~ The recovery of the Vedas stolen by a demon (Somakasura or Hayagriva) is the best-known purpose of the Matsya avatar in the Bhagavatam and in popular tradition. In the Matsya Purana itself, the stress is on saving Manu and the seeds of life from the deluge. (Official final key; some sources differ.)

Q 46 | pur | Puranas
What is the main significance of the Kurma avatar of Lord Vishnu as described in the Kurma Purana ?
(1) Saving the Vedas from demons
(2) Supporting the churning of the ocean (Samudra Manthan)
(3) Defeating the demon Hiranyakashipu
(4) Protecting King Manu from the flood
= 2
~ As the Kurma (tortoise), Vishnu bore the sinking Mandara mountain on his back so that the devas and asuras could churn the ocean of milk (Samudra Manthan) for amrita. The Kurma Purana is said to have been narrated by Vishnu in this tortoise form. Protecting Manu from the flood was Matsya's role, and killing Hiranyakashipu was Narasimha's.

Q 47 | pur | Puranas
The Skanda Purana is traditionally divided into how many sections or khandas ?
(1) 6
(2) 7
(3) 18
(4) 20
= 2
~ The common printed Skanda Purana is divided into seven khandas: Maheshvara, Vaishnava, Brahma, Kashi, Avantya, Nagara and Prabhasa. Another recension divides it into six samhitas, which makes option (1) tempting, but by khandas the count is seven.

Q 48 | pur | Tirthas & Kshetras
Which mountain, sacred in Hinduism and closely associated with Lord Shiva, is prominently mentioned in the Skanda Purana ?
(1) Vindhya
(2) Himalaya
(3) Kailash
(4) Aravalli
= 3
~ Mount Kailasa is the abode of Shiva and Parvati and the mountain most closely tied to Shiva. The Skanda Purana, which glorifies Shiva and his son Skanda (Kartikeya), refers to it often. The Himalaya is the whole range (Parvati's father Himavan), but Shiva's particular abode is Kailasa.

Q 49 | pur | Puranas
Which Purāṇa is the longest, with over 81,000 verses ?
(1) Padma Purana
(2) Skanda Purana
(3) Vishnu Purana
(4) Garuda Purana
= 2
~ The Skanda Purana is the largest of the eighteen Mahapuranas and is traditionally said to have about 81,100 shlokas. The Padma Purana comes second with about 55,000 shlokas, and the Vishnu and Garuda Puranas are much smaller (about 23,000 and 19,000).

Q 50 | pur | Tirthas & Kshetras
In which region did Sūta Mahāmuni narrate the Purāṇa stories to the great sages ?
(1) Dandakaranyam
(2) Naimisharanyam
(3) Chitrakootam
(4) Brundavanam
= 2
~ Suta (Ugrashravas, son of Romaharshana) narrated the Puranas and the Mahabharata to the sages led by Shaunaka during their long sacrifice (satra) in the Naimisharanya forest on the Gomati river. Dandakaranya and Chitrakoot are Ramayana forests, and Brindavan is linked with Krishna.

Q 51 | aga | Vaikhanasa & Pancharatra
Which of the following Agamic traditions is primarily followed in the worship practices at the Tirumala Venkateswara Temple in Tirupati ?
(1) Chattada Sri Vaishnavam
(2) Pancharatra Agama
