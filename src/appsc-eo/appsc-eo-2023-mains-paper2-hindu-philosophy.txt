# APPSC Executive Officer Grade-III Main Examination (Notification 24/2021), Paper II: Hindu Philosophy & Temple System, computer-based test held 17 February 2023.

Q 1 | ram | Characters
Who has been called Devarshi in ‘Ramayana’?
(1) Valmiki
(2) Vishwamitra
(3) Narada
(4) Vashishtha
= 3
~ Narada, the celestial sage who wanders the three worlds, is called Devarshi (divine sage). In the opening sarga of the Bala Kanda, Valmiki questions Narada about the ideal man and Narada narrates the story of Rama. Vishwamitra and Vashishtha are Brahmarshis, and Valmiki is called the Adi Kavi or Maharshi.

Q 2 | ved | Vedas & Upavedas
How many branches of ancient knowledge (Vidya) are there?
(1) 5
(2) 7
(3) 11
(4) 14
= 4
~ Tradition counts fourteen vidyas (chaturdasha vidya): the four Vedas, the six Vedangas, Purana, Nyaya, Mimamsa and Dharmashastra. If the four Upavedas are added, the count becomes eighteen (ashtadasha vidya), but eighteen is not an option here.

Q 3 | pur | Deities & Legends
Which ‘guna’ is predominant in Vishnu?
(1) Sattva
(2) Rajas
(3) Tamas
(4) Sattva with Tamas
= 1
~ Among the Trimurti, Brahma is linked with rajas (creation), Vishnu with sattva (preservation) and Rudra/Shiva with tamas (dissolution). So sattva guna is predominant in Vishnu.

Q 4 | pur | Puranas
Who is called ‘the fifth Veda’?
(1) Ramayana
(2) Upanishadas
(3) Agamas
(4) Puranas
= 4
~ The Chandogya Upanishad (7.1.2) calls Itihasa-Purana "the fifth Veda" (panchamo vedah), so the Puranas are traditionally known as the fifth Veda. The Ramayana is called the Adi Kavya, and the Upanishads are part of the Vedas themselves.

Q 5 | ved | Vedas & Upavedas
The Mimamsa Sutras were written by:
(1) Vedavyasa
(2) Gauthama
(3) Kapila
(4) Jaimini
= 4
~ Jaimini composed the Mimamsa Sutras, the basic text of Purva Mimamsa, which interprets Vedic ritual. Badarayana (identified with Vedavyasa) wrote the Brahma Sutras of Uttara Mimamsa/Vedanta, Gautama wrote the Nyaya Sutras, and Kapila founded Sankhya.

Q 6 | eod | Endowments Administration
Classification of the institutions in the Andhra Pradesh Charitable and Hindu Religious Institutions and Endowments Act, 1987 is based on which of the following?
(1) Annual income
(2) Daily income
(3) Monthly income
(4) Total number of Archakas
= 1
~ Section 6 of the A.P. Charitable and Hindu Religious Institutions and Endowments Act, 1987 (Act 30 of 1987) requires the Commissioner to prepare and publish lists of institutions according to their annual income, in fixed income slabs. Maths and Dharmadayams are listed separately whatever their income. Daily or monthly income and the number of archakas are not the basis.

Q 7 | fes | Hindu Festivals
‘Vinayaka chavaithi’ is celebrated in which Telugu month?
(1) Chaitra
(2) Ashwin
(3) Bhadrapada
(4) Magha
= 3
~ Vinayaka Chavithi is celebrated on Bhadrapada Shukla Chaturthi, the fourth day of the bright fortnight of the Telugu month Bhadrapada (August–September). Ashwayuja is the month of Dasara, and Magha is the month of Ratha Saptami.

Q 8 | mbh | Parvas & Places
The first chapter (Parva) of ‘Mahabharata’ is:
(1) Sabha Parva
(2) Adi Parva
(3) Shanti Parva
(4) Aswamedha Parva
= 2
~ Adi Parva is the first of the eighteen parvas of the Mahabharata. Sabha Parva is the second, Shanti Parva the twelfth and Ashvamedhika Parva the fourteenth.

Q 9 | fes | Hindu Festivals
Vinayaka Pooja is done with how many types of leaves?
(1) 11
(2) 16
(3) 21
(4) 28
= 3
~ On Vinayaka Chavithi, Ganesha is worshipped with twenty-one kinds of leaves (Ekavimshati Patra Puja), such as machi, bruhati, bilva, durva and others. The number 21 is also used for the modakas and durva offered to Ganesha.

Q 10 | pur | Tirthas & Kshetras
As per Hindu mythology, the total number of Shakti Peetha is:
(1) 36
(2) 40
(3) 48
(4) 51
= 4
~ The most widely followed tradition counts 51 Shakti Peethas, places where parts of Sati's body fell, often linked with the 51 letters of the Sanskrit alphabet. Other texts give 18 (Ashtadasha Shakti Peethas), 52 or 108.

Q 11 | pur | Puranas
Durga Sapthashathi is mentioned in which Purana?
(1) Markandeya Purana
(2) Devi Purana
(3) Bhagavatha Purana
(4) Skanda Purana
= 1
~ Durga Saptashati, also called Devi Mahatmyam or Chandi Path, has 700 verses and forms chapters 81 to 93 of the Markandeya Purana. It describes the Devi's victory over Madhu-Kaitabha, Mahishasura and Shumbha-Nishumbha.

Q 12 | ram | Dynasties & Events
What was the name of King Dasharatha's father?
(1) Raghu
(2) Nabhaga
(3) Dilipa
(4) Aja
= 4
~ Aja was the father of Dasharatha. The genealogy in Bala Kanda (Sarga 70) of the Ramayana says Dasharatha was born of Aja, and Kalidasa's Raghuvamsha makes Aja the son of Raghu. Raghu and Dilipa are earlier ancestors in the line.

Q 13 | eod | Endowments Administration
In 1987, the Andhra Pradesh government constituted the _______ to review and suggest measures for better management of institutions and endowments.
(1) Fazlali Commission
(2) Dhar Commission
(3) Justice Shri Challa Kondaiah Commission
(4) Reddy Commission
= 3
~ The Andhra Pradesh Government appointed a commission headed by Justice Challa Kondaiah to study the administration of Hindu religious and charitable institutions and endowments. Its recommendations, including abolition of hereditary rights in temples, shaped the A.P. Charitable and Hindu Religious Institutions and Endowments Act, 1987. The Fazl Ali and Dhar Commissions dealt with the reorganisation of States.

Q 14 | ved | Vedas & Upavedas
Vedas are also known as:
(1) Ihihasa
(2) Purana
(3) Shruthi
(4) Smrithi
= 3
~ The Vedas are called Shruti, "that which is heard", because they were revealed to the rishis. Smriti, "that which is remembered", covers later works such as the Dharmashastras, and Itihasa and Purana are separate classes of texts.

Q 15 | phi | Alwars & Nayanars
Which Alwar is also known as Godadevi?
(1) Nammalwar
(2) Andal
(3) Perialwar
(4) Poigai Alwar
= 2
~ Andal, also called Goda Devi or Kodai, is the only woman among the twelve Alwars and composed the Tiruppavai and Nachiyar Tirumozhi. Periyalwar was her foster father, not Goda Devi herself.

Q 16 | mbh | Parvas & Places
What was the name of city where the Pandavas spent their incognito period?
(1) Ekachakra Nagari
(2) Hastinapur
(3) Panchalnagar
(4) Viratanagar
= 4
~ The Pandavas spent their thirteenth year of exile in disguise (ajnatavasa) at the court of King Virata in Viratanagara, capital of the Matsya kingdom, as told in the Virata Parva. Ekachakra is where they lived after escaping the lac house.

Q 17 | inc | Allocation of Funds (Sec. 57)
According to the Andhra Pradesh Charitable and Hindu Religious Institutions and Endowments Act, 1987, the Common Good Fund shall vest in a committee constituted by the________________.
(1) Assistant commissioner
(2) Executive officer
(3) Chairman
(4) Government
= 4
~ The Common Good Fund is created under Section 70 of Act 30 of 1987, and Section 71 (Vesting of Common Good Fund) vests it in a committee constituted by the Government. An Assistant Commissioner, an Executive Officer or a temple chairman has no power to constitute this committee.

Q 19 | inc | Allocation of Funds (Sec. 57)
Surplus amounts, if any remains after repaying the amounts specified in Sub-section 3 Section 69 of the Andhra Pradesh Charitable and Hindu Religious Institutions and Endowments Act 1987, shall be transferred to:
(1) common good fund
(2) public fund
(3) Mathadhipathi
(4) trustees
= 1
~ Section 69 of Act 30 of 1987 establishes the Endowments Administration Fund, and any surplus left after the repayments specified in Section 69(3) is transferred to the Common Good Fund created under Section 70. The Common Good Fund is used for purposes such as renovating poor temples and promoting Hindu dharma. The surplus never goes to a Mathadhipathi or to trustees personally.

Q 20 | fes | Hindu Festivals
Which of the following states celebrates New Year’s day on Deepavali festival?
(1) Bihar
(2) Gujarat
(3) Punjab
(4) Kerala
= 2
~ In Gujarat the Hindu New Year (Bestu Varas) is celebrated as part of the Deepavali festival, on Kartika Shukla Pratipada, the day after Diwali. Punjab marks its new year with Vaisakhi, and Kerala with Vishu.

Q 21 | ved | Yajnas & Ashramas
How many Purusharthas of life were propounded by Manu?
(1) Two
(2) Three
(3) Four
(4) Five
= 3
~ The four Purusharthas, or goals of human life, are Dharma, Artha, Kama and Moksha. Manusmriti discusses dharma, artha and kama together as the trivarga (2.224), with moksha as the final goal. (Official final key; some sources differ.)

Q 22 | ram | Kandas & Places
On the advice of sage Agastya, at which place did Rama start living by building an ashram?
(1) Prayaga
(2) Kishkindha
(3) Panchavati
(4) Anjanadri
= 3
~ In the Aranya Kanda, Sage Agastya advised Rama to live at Panchavati on the banks of the Godavari, and Lakshmana built a hermitage there. Sita was abducted from Panchavati. Kishkindha was the vanara kingdom, and at Prayaga Rama visited Bharadwaja's ashram.

Q 23 | ved | Vedas & Upavedas
Rigveda is divided into ten:
(1) Anuvakas
(2) Mandalas
(3) Suktas
(4) Adhyayas
= 2
~ The Rigveda Samhita is divided into ten Mandalas (books), which contain about 1,028 suktas (hymns). Suktas and anuvakas are smaller units, and the other traditional division is into eight ashtakas of 64 adhyayas.

Q 24 | fes | Hindu Festivals
Which day is celebrated as Akshaya Tritiya?
(1) Phalguna Tritiya
(2) Jyeshtha Tritiya
(3) Chaitra Tritiya
(4) Vaisakha Tritiya
= 4
~ Akshaya Tritiya falls on Vaishakha Shukla Tritiya, the third day of the bright fortnight of Vaishakha (April–May). It is considered auspicious for new ventures, buying gold and charity.

Q 25 | lan | Endowment Lands (Secs. 75–86)

Q 26 | ram | Characters

In which state is ‘Vaishakhi’ or 'Baishaki' celebrated prominently?

(2) Himachal Pradesh
(3) Punjab
(4) Rajasthan

Q 28 | eod | Endowments Administration
Who must represent the Tirumala Tirupathi Devasthanams in the suits and other legal proceedings?
(1) Executive Officer
(2) Deputy Commissioner
(3) Assistant Commissioner

Deputy or Assistant Commissioners of the Endowments Department do not represent TTD.

Q 29 | ved | Vedas & Upavedas
The book that describes the words, tunes and connections used in Vedas is called:
(1) Shika
(2) Pratishakhya
(3) Mantra
(4) Vidhi
= 2
~ Pratishakhyas are phonetic manuals, one for each Vedic shakha, that explain the pronunciation, accents and sandhi (joining) of words in the Samhitas, e.g. the Rigveda Pratishakhya. Shiksha is the general Vedanga of phonetics, while the Pratishakhya applies it to a particular Veda.

Q 30 | fes | Hindu Festivals
The religious festival ‘Thai Poosam’ is celebrated in which Indian State?
(1) Tamil Nadu
(2) Karnataka
(3) Kerala
(4) Telangana
= 1
~ Thaipusam is a Tamil festival in honour of Lord Murugan (Subrahmanya). It falls in the Tamil month of Thai, on the day of the Pusam star, and is famous for kavadi processions at Palani and other Murugan temples in Tamil Nadu.

Q 31 | lan | Endowment Lands (Secs. 75–86)
The lands belonging to the Institutions or Endowments situated in scheduled areas can be purchased by which of the following authorities?
(1) District Collector
(2) Executive Officer
(3) Government
(4) Chairman
= 3
~ In Scheduled Areas, land transfers to non-tribals are restricted (A.P. Scheduled Areas Land Transfer Regulation, 1959). In line with this, the official key names the Government as the only authority that can purchase lands of institutions or endowments situated there. A District Collector, an Executive Officer or a chairman is not a purchaser of such lands.

Q 32 | phi | Advaita, Visishtadvaita & Dvaita

Q 33 | lan | Endowment Lands (Secs. 75–86)

Q 34 | ved | Vedas & Upavedas
‘Taittiriya Brahmana’ is related to which Veda?
(1) Rigveda
(2) Shukla Yajurveda
(3) Krishna Yajurveda

Q 35 | ram | Characters
Who said “Rama is dharma incarnate” (Rāmō vigrahavān dharmaḥ)?

Q 36 | fes | Hindu Festivals
Which festival is also celebrated as ‘Madanotsavam’?
(1) Holi

Q 39 | ved | Upanishads

Q 40 | ved | Vedas & Upavedas
How many system of treatment are explained by Ayurveda?
(1) 4
(2) 6
(3) 8
(4) 10
= 3
~ Ayurveda, one of the four Upavedas, is called Ashtanga Ayurveda because it has eight branches: kaya (general medicine), bala (paediatrics), graha (psychiatry), urdhvanga (ENT and eye), shalya (surgery), damshtra (toxicology), jara (rejuvenation) and vrisha (aphrodisiacs).

Q 41 | phi | Advaita, Visishtadvaita & Dvaita
Who was the founder of Vishishthadvaita philosophy?
(1) Shankaracharya
(2) Ramanujacharya

(4) Nimbarkacharya

~ Ramanujacharya (traditionally 1017–1137) systematised Visishtadvaita (qualified non-dualism) in his Sri Bhashya on the Brahma Sutras. Shankara taught Advaita, Vallabha Shuddhadvaita and Nimbarka Dvaitadvaita.

Q 42 | mbh | Characters
Who is known as Gangeya in ‘Mahabharata’?
(1) Krishna
(2) Arjuna
(3) Karna
(4) Bhishma

Q 43 | fam | Succession & Marriage
The marriage of Shakuntala and Dushyantha is a classic example of which kind of marriage?
(1) Daiva Vivaha
(2) Gandharva Vivaha
(3) Arsha Vivaha
(4) Brahma Vivaha
= 2
~ Shakuntala and Dushyanta married by mutual consent, without parents or rituals, which is a Gandharva vivaha, one of the eight forms of marriage listed in Manusmriti (3.21). The story is told in the Adi Parva of the Mahabharata. Brahma, Daiva and Arsha marriages are arranged by the bride's father.

Q 44 | fes | Hindu Festivals

Q 45 | phi | Veerasaiva & Other Cults
Which philosophy was propagated by Basaveswara?
(1) Advaita philosophy
(2) Chaitanya philosophy
(3) Veera Shaivam philosophy
(4) Dvaita philosophy

Q 52 | eod | Endowments Administration
An Executive Officer appointed under the Andhra Pradesh Charitable and Hindu Religious Institutions and Endowments Act 1987 to TTD shall be:
(1) the chief administrative officer
(2) a member
(3) a chairman
(4) a commissioner
= 1

Q 53 | eod | Endowments Administration

(1) Pujari
(2) Panda
(3) Executive Officer
(4) Archakatvam Mirandar
= 3
~ Section 2(1) of Act 30 of 1987 defines "archaka" to include a Pujari, Panda, Archakatvam Mirasidar or any other person who personally performs archana, puja or other rituals. An Executive Officer is an administrative officer under Section 29 and does not fall within this definition.

Q 54 | eod | Duties & Powers of EO (Sec. 29)
Section 29 of the Andhra Pradesh Charitable and Hindu Religious Institutions and Endowments Act 1987 makes provision for the _____________?
(1) punishment of office holders and servants

(3) removal of Mathadhipathi

= 4
~ Section 29 of Act 30 of 1987 deals with the appointment and duties of Executive Officers, who are appointed by the Government or the Commissioner depending on the institution's income. It also lists their duties, such as keeping records and accounts, collecting income and suing or being sued in the institution's name. Removal of a Mathadhipathi is dealt with in Section 51.

Q 56 | phi | Advaita, Visishtadvaita & Dvaita
Who wrote the ‘Bhamati’ commentary on Shankar Bhashya?
(1) Sureshwaracharya
(2) Kumarila Bhatt
