Q 24 | sci | Physics
The Rocket works on the principle of
= Newton’s 3rd law
~ A rocket throws hot exhaust gases backward at high speed (action), and the gases push the rocket forward with an equal and opposite force (reaction), which is Newton’s third law of motion. It can also be explained by conservation of momentum, which is why a rocket works even in the vacuum of space.

Q 26 | sci | Chemistry
The term ‘pH’ denotes
= Acidity and basicity of the Solution
~ pH (“potential of hydrogen”, introduced by S.P.L. Sørensen in 1909) is the negative logarithm of the hydrogen-ion concentration and shows how acidic or basic a solution is. At 25 °C, a pH below 7 is acidic, 7 is neutral and above 7 is basic.

Q 34 | geo | Indian Geography

Q 35 | geo | Indian Geography

Q 39 | geo | Disaster Management
The human factors which cause floods are:
= Deforestation
~ Deforestation removes the vegetation that slows runoff and lets rain soak into the soil, so water reaches rivers faster and silt raises riverbeds, leading to floods. Other human factors include encroachment on floodplains, unplanned urbanisation and poor drainage, while heavy rainfall and cyclones are natural causes.

Q 41 | men | Reasoning
Find the next number in the series 13, 13, 12, 14, 11, 15, ____
= 10
~ The terms in odd places (13, 12, 11, …) fall by 1 and those in even places (13, 14, 15) rise by 1. The next (7th) term is in an odd place, so it is 11 − 1 = 10.

Q 42 | men | Reasoning
The no. of times the hour hand and minute hand of a clock coincide in a day is
= 22
~ The two hands coincide once every 65 5/11 minutes, i.e. 11 times in 12 hours (only once between 11 and 1 o’clock, at 12). So in 24 hours they coincide 22 times, not 24.

Q 44 | men | Arithmetic
The sum of present ages of son and father is 60 years. Five years ago, fathers age is 4 times the age of the son. The present age of father is
= 45
~ Let the father be F and the son S. F + S = 60 and F − 5 = 4(S − 5), so F = 4S − 15; then 5S − 15 = 60, giving S = 15 and F = 45. Check: five years ago they were 40 and 10.

Q 45 | men | Reasoning
In a certain code language, CORONA = 60, COVAXIN = 81, then the value of COVISHIELD is
= 96
~ Each letter takes the value (its alphabet position − 1), i.e. the sum of the positions minus the number of letters. CORONA: 66 − 6 = 60; COVAXIN: 88 − 7 = 81; so COVISHIELD: 106 − 10 = 96.

Q 50 | men | Data Interpretation
The Mean of a data is 20. The Co-efficient of Variation is 30. The Variance of the data is
= 36
~ Coefficient of variation = (standard deviation ÷ mean) × 100, so 30 = (σ ÷ 20) × 100 and σ = 6. Variance = σ² = 36; 6 is the standard deviation, not the variance.

Q 126 | eod | Duties & Powers of EO (Sec. 29)
Which of the following is not a duty of the Executive officer under the provisions of the A.P. Endowments Act (Act 30/87)?
= Publication of institutions
~ Under Section 29 of Act 30 of 1987 the Executive Officer runs the administration of the institution: he manages its properties, collects its income, keeps accounts and carries out the lawful decisions of the trustees. Preparing and publishing the lists (categories) of institutions under Section 6 is the duty of the Commissioner, not of the EO.

Q 127 | eod | Endowments Administration
The power to constitute groups of institutions for the purpose of appointing a single Executive officer to each group is vested with
= Commissioner

Q 128 | eod | Endowments Administration

= Three grades
~ Apart from the Regional Joint Commissioner, Deputy Commissioner and Assistant Commissioner cadres, Executive Officer posts in the A.P. Q 129 | eod | Duties & Powers of EO (Sec. 29)

Q 130 | inc | Sources of Income

~ A temple's income comes from hundi offerings, fees for sevas and darshan, rents and leases of its lands and buildings, interest on deposits and donations. Government grants are not a regular source; on the contrary, temples contribute to funds such as the Endowments Administration Fund and the Common Good Fund.

Q 131 | eod | Endowments Administration
Who is the competent authority to sanction budget to the temples published under 6-C category?
= Assistant Commissioner
~ Section 57 of Act 30/1987 deals with the budgets of institutions, and a budget is sanctioned by the authority fixed for the institution's category. For 6(c) institutions, the lowest-income group, the sanctioning authority is the Assistant Commissioner; higher categories go to higher officers.

Q 132 | inc | Allocation of Funds (Sec. 57)
No provision is made under Section 57 of Act 30/87 for allocation of funds for one of the following purposes
= For giving donations for public purposes
~ Section 57 of Act 30/1987 deals with the annual budget of an institution. The budget must provide for the institution's own expenses and for the payments the Act requires, such as the amount payable to the Common Good Fund. Giving temple money away as donations for general public purposes is not one of the items it provides for.

Q 133 | lan | Endowment Lands (Secs. 75–86)

Q 141 | lan | Endowment Lands (Secs. 75–86)
