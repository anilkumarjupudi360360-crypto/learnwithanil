Q 1 | sci | Biology & Health
Which of the following statements is/are correct about branches of Life Sciences?
A.Astrobiology is the study of bacteria.
B.Anatomy, a field in the biological sciences concerned with the identification and description of the body structures of living things.
C.Biotechnology uses living cells to develop or manipulate products for specific purposes, such as genetically modified foods.
(1) Both A and B
(2) Both B and C
(3) Only A
(4) Only C
= 2
~ Astrobiology studies the origin, evolution and distribution of life in the universe; the study of bacteria is bacteriology, so A is wrong. Anatomy deals with identifying and describing the body structures of living things, and biotechnology uses living cells or organisms to make or modify products such as genetically modified foods, so B and C are correct.

Q 2 | sci | Biology & Health
The following item consist of two Statements: one labelled as the ‘Assertion (A)’ and the other as ‘Reason (R)’. You are to examine these two statements carefully and select the answers to these items using the codes given below:
Assertion (A): Spirogyra, Ulothrix, Cladophora and Chara are grouped under Thallophyta.
Reason (R): Spirogyra, Ulothrix, Cladophora and Chara lack roots, stem, leaves, and vascular system.
(1) Both A and R are individually true and R is the correct explanation of A
(2) Both A and R are individually true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A
(3) A is true but R is false
(4) A is false but R is true.
= 1
~ Spirogyra, Ulothrix, Cladophora and Chara are green algae, which are placed in the division Thallophyta. Thallophytes have a plant body (thallus) that is not differentiated into root, stem and leaves and has no vascular tissue, so the feature stated in R is exactly the basis of the grouping in A.

Q 3 | geo | Environment & Ecology
The following item consist of two Statements: one labelled as the ‘Assertion (A)’ and the other as ‘Reason (R)’. You are to examine these two statements carefully and select the answers to these items using the codes given below:
Assertion (A): Ozone depletion refers to the phenomenon of reductions in the amount of ozone in the stratosphere.
Reason (R): There are high levels of chlorine and bromine compounds in the stratosphere, the origins of these compounds are chlorofluorocarbons (CFC), used as cooling substances in air-conditioners and refrigerators, or as aerosol propellants, and bromo-fluorocarbons (halons), used in fire extinguishers.
(1) Both A and R are individually true and R is the correct explanation of A
(2) Both A and R are individually true but R is NOT the correct explanation of A
(3) A is true but R is false
(4) A is false but R is true.
= 1
~ Ozone depletion is the thinning of the ozone layer in the stratosphere. It is caused mainly by chlorine and bromine released there from chlorofluorocarbons (used in refrigerators, air-conditioners and aerosol sprays) and halons (used in fire extinguishers), so R correctly explains A.

Q 10 | sci | Chemistry
Which of the following statements is/are correct about the chemical process in baking a cake?

1.The Mahanadi, the Krishna, the Kaveri and the Godavari form deltas before entering into the Bay of Bengal.

Q 147 | men | Data Interpretation

= 1
~ For a moderately asymmetrical distribution, Mean – Mode = 3 (Mean – Median), i.e. Mode = 3 Median – 2 Mean (Karl Pearson's empirical relation). So 163.5 = 3 × 124.5 – 2 × Mean, giving Mean = (373.5 – 163.5)/2 = 105. Both are true and the Reason explains the Assertion.
