Q 1 | cur | Sports

~ Barcelona won the 2017–18 La Liga title under coach Ernesto Valverde, finishing 14 points ahead of Atlético Madrid, and also won the Copa del Rey that season.

~ Sindhu signed a four-year deal reported at about ₹50 crore with the Chinese sportswear brand Li Ning, one of the largest endorsement deals in badminton.

Q 3 | ih | Modern India
Who was the designer of the Indian National Flag?
= Pingali Venkayya
~ Pingali Venkayya, born near Machilipatnam in Krishna district, presented his flag design to Gandhi at the Bezawada (Vijayawada) Congress session in 1921. The present tricolour evolved from it.

Q 4 | cur | International
The 2018 Nobel Peace Prize winner Nadia Murad belongs to which country?
= Iraq
~ Nadia Murad is a Yazidi from northern Iraq who survived ISIS captivity. She shared the 2018 Peace Prize with Congolese surgeon Denis Mukwege for work against sexual violence in war.

Q 5 | cur | Sports
In which of the following years did the Indian National Blind Cricket Team win the World Cup?
= 2018
~ India beat Pakistan in the final of the Blind Cricket World Cup (ODI format) held in the UAE in January 2018, retaining the title it had won in 2014.

= Mithali Raj
~ Mithali Raj crossed 2,000 T20I runs in 2018 and was the first Indian cricketer, man or woman, to reach the mark.

Q 7 | cur | International
Which city is going to host the 2019 G20 Summit?

~ The 14th G20 summit was held in Osaka in June 2019, the first G20 summit hosted by Japan.

Q 11 | sci | General Science
What is the SI unit of inductance?
= Henry
~ The henry (H) is named after the American physicist Joseph Henry. One henry produces 1 volt when current changes at 1 ampere per second.

Q 15 | cur | Awards & Personalities

Q 16 | men | Data Interpretation & Statistics

Q 17 | sci | IT & Communication

Q 18 | sci | General Science

b. Thomas Alva Edison

Q 42 | men | Data Interpretation & Statistics

~ Karl Pearson's empirical rule is Mode = 3 Median – 2 Mean, which rearranges to Mean – Mode = 3(Mean – Median).
