Q 1 | ih | Modern India & Freedom Struggle
Who gave the famous slogan 'Educate, Organize and Agitate'
(1) Ambedkar
(2) Karl Marx
(3) Netaji Subhashchandra Bose
(4) Vallab Bhai Patel
= 1
~ B.R. Ambedkar gave the slogan "Educate, Agitate, Organise" to the depressed classes as their path to social and political emancipation; it is not associated with Marx, Bose or Patel.

Q 2 | pol | Local Government
Which one of the following is NOT a feature of 73rd Constitutional Amendment.
(1) Reservations to SC & ST in local bodies
(2) Reservations for women in local bodies
(3) Compulsory Elections for Local Bodies within 5 years
(4) Reservation for OBC, in Local Bodies
= 4
~ The 73rd Amendment (1992) makes reservation for SCs/STs, one-third reservation for women and elections every five years compulsory, but under Article 243D(6) reservation for backward classes is left to the discretion of the State legislature, so it is not a mandatory feature.

Q 5 | pol | Governance, Schemes & Social Issues
Right to Information contributes
(1) Transparent Governance
(2) Stable Governance
(3) Closed Governance
(4) Dictatorial Governance
= 1
~ The Right to Information Act, 2005 lets citizens obtain information held by public authorities, which makes government work open and accountable, i.e. transparent governance; closed or dictatorial governance is the opposite of its purpose.

Q 6 | pol | Governance, Schemes & Social Issues
The National Food Security Act, 2013 does not include
(1) Mid-day meals Scheme
(2) Integrated Child Development Services
(3) Public Distribution System
(4) Supply of food for all
= 4
~ The NFSA, 2013 covers subsidised grain through the Targeted Public Distribution System and nutrition support through mid-day meals and ICDS, but it covers only up to 75% of the rural and 50% of the urban population, not everyone.

Q 7 | pol | Governance, Schemes & Social Issues
The Consumer Protection Act envisages its machinery at
(1) The National level
(2) The National, State and District levels
(3) The National and State levels
(4) The State and District levels
= 2
~ The Consumer Protection Act, 1986 set up a three-tier redressal machinery: District Forums, State Commissions and the National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

Q 8 | pol | Constitution & Rights
The Indian Federalism is drawn from
(1) United States of America
(2) Britain
(3) Canada
(4) Germany
= 3
~ The Indian model of a federation with a strong Centre (residuary powers with the Centre, Governors appointed by the Centre) was borrowed from the Canadian Constitution, unlike the US model, which gives the states much more autonomy.

Q 9 | pol | Union & State Government
The disputes between Government of India and one or more states can be heard by Supreme Court under its
(1) Original Jurisdiction
(2) Advisory Jurisdiction
(3) Appellate Jurisdiction
(4) Nominal Jurisdiction
= 1
~ Under Article 131, the Supreme Court has exclusive original jurisdiction over disputes between the Government of India and one or more States, or between States; such cases start directly in the Supreme Court and do not come to it on appeal.

Q 42 | geo | World Geography
Which of the following earth quake waves are most destructive
(1) Surface Waves
(2) P-Waves
(3) R-Waves
(4) L-Waves
= 1
~ Surface waves travel along the earth's surface with the largest amplitudes and cause the most damage, while P-waves are the fastest but least destructive; L-waves (Love) and R-waves (Rayleigh) are the two kinds of surface waves, so "surface waves" is the complete answer.
