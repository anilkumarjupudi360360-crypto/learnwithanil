Q 1 | geo | Environment & Ecology

(1) Ethiopia
(2) Tanzania
(3) South Africa
(4) Kenya
= 2
~ Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa's highest peak, is a dormant volcano in north-eastern Tanzania, close to the Kenya border. Q 27 | geo | Indian & World Geography
What is the layer of the atmosphere directly above the troposphere called?
(1) Ionosphere
(2) Exosphere
(3) Stratosphere
(4) Mesosphere
= 3
~ The stratosphere lies directly above the troposphere, separated by the tropopause, and extends to about 50 km; it contains the ozone layer. Above it come the mesosphere, the thermosphere (which includes the ionosphere) and the exosphere.

Q 28 | geo | Indian & World Geography
The marginal areas submerged under the oceanic water are called
(1) continental shelf
(2) continental slope
(3) abyssal plain
(4) deep trench
= 1
~ The continental shelf is the gently sloping, submerged margin of a continent under shallow seas, usually up to about 200 m deep. Beyond it the continental slope drops steeply to the deep-sea abyssal plains and trenches.

Q 29 | geo | Indian & World Geography
Which of the following earthquake waves can travel through both solid and liquid?
(1) P waves
(2) S waves
(3) L waves
(4) M waves
= 1
~ P (primary) waves are longitudinal waves that pass through solids, liquids and gases. S (secondary) waves cannot travel through liquids – which is how the liquid outer core was inferred – and L (surface) waves move only along the surface.

Q 39 | pol | Indian Polity & Governance
There is no provision in the Indian Constitution for the impeachment of the
(1) Chief Justice of India
(2) Chief Justice of a High Court
(3) Vice President
(4) Governor of a State
= 4
~ The Governor holds office during the pleasure of the President (Article 156) and can be removed by the President at any time, so the Constitution has no impeachment-type procedure for a Governor. The President is impeached under Article 61, while judges (Articles 124(4) and 218) and the Vice President (Article 67(b)) are removed by a special procedure in Parliament.

Q 48 | pol | Indian Polity & Governance

Q 51 | pol | Indian Polity & Governance
Who is widely considered the pioneer or father of Policy Sciences?
(1) Harold D. Lasswell
(2) Woodrow Wilson
(3) Herbert Simon
(4) Thomas Dye
= 1
~ Harold D. Lasswell is regarded as the father of the policy sciences: in 'The Policy Orientation' (in The Policy Sciences, 1951, edited with Daniel Lerner) he called for a problem-oriented, multidisciplinary science of policy-making. Wilson pioneered public administration as a discipline, Simon is known for bounded rationality, and Dye defined public policy as whatever governments choose to do or not to do.

Q 52 | pol | Indian Polity & Governance
Who introduced the ‘systems theory’ to Political Science, viewing public policy as an output of the political system’s conversion process?
(1) Graham Allison
(2) Harold D. Lasswell
(3) David Easton
(4) Max Weber
= 3
~ David Easton applied systems theory to political science (The Political System, 1953; A Systems Analysis of Political Life, 1965). Inputs from the environment — demands and supports — are converted by the political system into outputs (authoritative decisions and policies), which feed back into the environment. Allison is known for his models of decision-making in the Cuban missile crisis.

Q 53 | pol | Indian Polity & Governance
Which of the following is the best example of a redistributive public policy?
(1) Building a new public highway
(2) Setting minimum environmental safety standards for factories
(3) Providing subsidies to local agricultural farmers
(4) Progressive income taxation and welfare transfers
= 4
~ In Theodore Lowi's typology, redistributive policies shift income or wealth from one group or class to another. Progressive income tax takes a larger share from higher incomes and welfare transfers pass resources to the poor — the textbook case. A public highway is distributive, safety standards are regulatory, and farm subsidies are usually classed as distributive.

Q 54 | pol | Indian Polity & Governance
During which part of public policy cycle does the government explore potential policy responses?
(1) Agenda Setting
(2) Policy Formulation
(3) Policy Implementation
(4) Decision Making
= 2
~ In the policy cycle (agenda setting → policy formulation → decision-making → implementation → evaluation), policy formulation is the stage where possible options for a problem already on the agenda are identified, explored and assessed. Decision-making then selects one option, and implementation puts it into effect.

Q 57 | eco | Indian Economy
Stage II of Demographic Transition implies a stage with
(1) high birthrate and high death rate
(2) high death rate and low birthrate
(3) low birthrate and low death rate
(4) low death rate and high birthrate
= 4
~ Demographic transition: Stage I — high birth and death rates, slow growth; Stage II — the death rate falls sharply (better food, health care, sanitation) while the birth rate stays high, causing rapid population growth; Stage III — the birth rate also declines; Stage IV — both rates are low.

Q 58 | eco | Indian Economy

Q 73 | geo | Environment & Ecology
