# APPSC Group-II Mains 2025 (Notification 11/2023) – Paper I, held 23 Feb 2025. Source: APPSC final key booklet (Series A, English version).

Q 1 | aph | Ancient Andhra
Which one of the following statements is incorrect with reference to megalithic burials in Andhra?
(1) Tirupati has the largest anthropomorphic burial site collection
(2) Many numbers of cyst burials were found in Visakhapatnam District
(3) Sarcophagus burials are more in number in Chittoor and Anantapuramu districts
(4) Krishna and Chittoor districts contained many numbers of dolmen burials
= 2
~ Cist burials are concentrated in the Rayalaseema and southern districts, not in Visakhapatnam district.

Q 2 | aph | Andhra Literature & Arts
Statement (A): "Gatha Saptasathi" is a collection of poems which predominantly focused on love and romantic themes.
Statement (B): Gunadya had written Brihatkatha in Sanskrit language.
(1) Both (A) and (B) are true
(2) Both (A) and (B) are false
(3) (A) is true, but (B) is false
(4) (A) is false, but (B) is true
= 3
~ Hala's Gathasaptasati is mostly love poetry in Maharashtri Prakrit. Gunadhya wrote the Brihatkatha in Paishachi Prakrit, not Sanskrit.

Q 3 | aph | Ancient Andhra
Which of the following statements is incorrect with reference to the Ikshvakus of Vijayapuri?
(1) Puranas mentioned Ikshvakus as 'Sri Parvathiyas'
(2) In their inscriptions, Ikshvakus of Vijayapuri had described themselves as the descendants of Ikshvakus of Ayodhya
(3) Ikshvakus of Vijayapuri had matrimonial ties with Sakas/Western Satraps of Ujjain
(4) Veerapurushadatta's reign is considered as the golden age of Buddhism in Andhra
= 2
~ The Puranas call them Sriparvatiyas, they married into the Western Kshatrapas of Ujjain, and Buddhism flourished under Virapurushadatta. Their own inscriptions do not claim descent from the Ikshvakus of Ayodhya.

Q 4 | aph | Ancient Andhra
'Janasraya' is the title of which of the following Vishnukundin rulers?
(1) Govindavarma
(2) Indrabhattarakavarma
(3) Vikramendravarma
(4) Madhavavarma
= 4
~ Madhava Varma II (Janasraya), the greatest Vishnukundin, performed many Vedic sacrifices. The work Janasrayi Chhandovichiti is linked with his title.

Q 5 | aph | Medieval Andhra

(4) Jayasimha Vallabha

~ Ayyana Mahadevi, queen of Kubja Vishnuvardhana, founder of the Eastern Chalukya line, built the Nedumba Vasati at Bezawada, as the Musinikonda grant records.

Q 6 | aph | Ancient Andhra

Q 17 | aph | Medieval Andhra
