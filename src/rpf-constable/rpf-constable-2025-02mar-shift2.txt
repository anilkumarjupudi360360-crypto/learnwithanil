Q 3 | qa | Number System & Simplification
Evaluate: 41 − [36 − {48 ÷ 2 − (6 − 12 ÷ 2) ÷ 2}]
(1) 29
(2) 30
(3) 31
(4) 28
= 1
~ Innermost bracket first: 6 − 12 ÷ 2 = 6 − 6 = 0, and 0 ÷ 2 = 0. Curly bracket: 48 ÷ 2 − 0 = 24. Square bracket: 36 − 24 = 12. Finally 41 − 12 = 29.

Q 13 | qa | Ratio, Average & Mixtures
Nirmal bought 52 books for Rs 1130 from one shop and 47 books for Rs 900 from another. What is the average price (in Rs) he paid per book ?
(1) 1010.81
(2) 1030.81
(3) 1020.81

Q 14 | qa | Mensuration & Geometry
Find the area (in cm²) of a rhombus whose diagonals are of lengths 47 cm and 48 cm.
(1) 1028
(2) 1100
(3) 1128
(4) 1116
= 3
~ The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles, so its area is half the product of the diagonals. Area = ½ × d₁ × d₂ = ½ × 47 × 48 = 47 × 24 = 1128 cm².

Q 15 | qa | Percentage & Profit-Loss
A merchant permits a 24% discount on his advertised price and then makes a profit of 20%. What is the advertised price on which he gains ₹76?
(1) ₹600
(2) ₹480
(3) ₹560
(4) ₹650
= 1
~ Let the advertised price be M. SP = 0.76M and CP = SP ÷ 1.2, so profit = SP − CP = SP/6. Profit ₹76 gives SP = ₹456, so M = 456 ÷ 0.76 = ₹600. Check: CP = 456 ÷ 1.2 = ₹380, profit = 456 − 380 = ₹76.

Q 16 | qa | Time, Work, Speed & Distance
A 275 m long train overtakes a man moving at a speed of 6 km/h (in same direction) in 45 seconds. How much time (in seconds) will it take this train to completely cross another 280 m long train, moving in the opposite direction at a speed of 26 km/h?
(1) 37
(2) 28
(3) 24
(4) 44
= 1
~ Relative speed with the man = 275 ÷ 45 m/s = 275/45 × 18/5 = 22 km/h, so the train's speed = 22 + 6 = 28 km/h. With the other train coming from the opposite direction, relative speed = 28 + 26 = 54 km/h = 15 m/s. Time = (275 + 280) ÷ 15 = 555 ÷ 15 = 37 seconds.

Q 17 | qa | Time, Work, Speed & Distance

(4) 4

Q 34 | qa | Number System & Simplification

Q 36 | rea | Syllogism & Statements
Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.
Statements:
All hens are mangoes.
All hens are shrimps.
All ducks are shrimps.
Conclusions:
(I): All ducks are mangoes.
(II): Some shrimps are mangoes.
(1) Both conclusions (I) and (II) follow.
(2) Only conclusion (II) follows.
(3) Neither conclusion (I) nor (II) follows.
(4) Only conclusion (I) follows.

Q 40 | rea | Blood Relations & Directions
In a certain code language,
'A : B' means ‘A is the wife of B’,
'A × B' means ‘A is the brother of B’,
'A < B' means ‘A is the father of B’ and
'A + B' means ‘A is the mother of B’.
How is B related to J if 'B : D < F + H × J’?
(1) Mother's daughter
(2) Mother's mother
(3) Mother's brother
(4) Mother's father
= 2
~ B : D means B is the wife of D; D < F means D is the father of F; F + H means F is the mother of H; H × J means H is the brother of J. So F is J's mother, D is F's father, and B, being D's wife, is F's mother. Hence B is J's mother's mother.

Q 44 | rea | Coding-Decoding

Q 46 | rea | Analogy & Classification

Q 47 | rea | Analogy & Classification
Based on the English alphabetical order, three of the following four letter-clusters are alike in a certain way and thus form a group. Which letter-cluster DOES NOT belong to that group?

Q 49 | rea | Blood Relations & Directions
Ajay starts from Point A and drives 6 km towards the east. He then takes a left turn, drives 7 km, turns left and drives 8 km. He then takes a left turn and drives 9 km. He takes a final left turn, drives 2 km and stops at Point P. How far (shortest distance) and towards which direction should he drive in order to reach Point A again? (All turns are 90 degree turns only unless specified.)
(1) 4 km to the south
(2) 3 km to the south
(3) 2 km to the north
(4) 1 km to the north
= 3
~ Take A as (0, 0). 6 km east gives (6, 0); a left turn faces north, 7 km gives (6, 7); left again faces west, 8 km gives (−2, 7); left faces south, 9 km gives (−2, −2); the final left faces east, 2 km gives P (0, −2). P is 2 km directly south of A, so he must drive 2 km to the north.

Q 50 | rea | Syllogism & Statements

Conclusions:

Q 74 | sci | Physics

(3) Total potential energy
(4) Total linear momentum
= 2
