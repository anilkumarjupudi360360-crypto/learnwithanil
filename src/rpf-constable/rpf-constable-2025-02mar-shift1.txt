Q 1 | qa | Simple & Compound Interest
Two banks, A and B, offered loans at 3.5% and 6% per annum, respectively. David borrowed an amount of ₹360000 from each bank. Find the positive difference between the amounts of simple interest paid to the two banks by David after 3 years.
(1) 28500
(2) 28000
(3) 27000
(4) 26500
= 3
~ SI paid to Bank A = 360000 × 3.5 × 3/100 = ₹37,800 and to Bank B = 360000 × 6 × 3/100 = ₹64,800. Difference = 64,800 − 37,800 = ₹27,000. Shortcut: 360000 × (6 − 3.5) × 3/100 = 27000.

Q 2 | qa | Time, Work, Speed & Distance
A 120 m long train crosses a man walking at a speed of 8.5 km/h in the opposite direction in 12 seconds. What is the speed (in km/h) of the train?
(1) 23.3
(2) 27.5
(3) 29.1
(4) 31.5
= 2
~ As the man walks in the opposite direction, relative speed = train speed + 8.5 km/h. Relative speed = 120/12 = 10 m/s = 10 × 18/5 = 36 km/h. Speed of the train = 36 − 8.5 = 27.5 km/h.

Q 3 | qa | Time, Work, Speed & Distance
Two pipes A and C can fill a tank in 10 hours and 15 hours respectively. If A is open all the time and C is open for 30 minutes, then find the time needed for the tank to be filled.
(1) 9 1/3 hours
(2) 8 4/3 hours
(3) 9 2/3 hours
(4) 8 2/3 hours
= 3
~ Together A and C fill 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6 of the tank per hour, so in the first 30 minutes they fill ½ × 1/6 = 1/12. The remaining 11/12 is filled by A alone in 11/12 × 10 = 55/6 = 9 1/6 hours. Total time = ½ + 9 1/6 = 9 2/3 hours.

Q 16 | qa | Percentage & Profit-Loss
Prem sells an article to Ria at a profit of 20%. Ria sells the article back to Prem at a loss of 20%. In this transaction:
(1) Ria makes a profit of 24%
(2) Ria incurs a loss of 12%
(3) Prem makes a profit of 25%
(4) Prem makes a profit of 12%
= 3
~ Let Prem's cost price be ₹100. He sells to Ria at ₹120, and Ria sells it back at a 20% loss, i.e. at 120 × 0.8 = ₹96. Prem gets the article back and gains ₹120 − ₹96 = ₹24, which is 24/96 × 100 = 25% on the ₹96 he pays to buy it back. Measured on his original cost of ₹100 the same gain would be 24%, which is not offered for Prem, so the keyed 25% is the gain on the buy-back price (₹96). Ria buys at ₹120 and sells at ₹96, a loss of 20%, so the options about Ria (24% profit, 12% loss) are wrong.

Q 22 | qa | Number System & Simplification
Which of the following statements is NOT correct?
(1) A number is divisible by 11 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 11.
(2) A number is divisible by 3 or 9 if the sum of digits is divisible by 3 or 9, respectively.
(3) A number is divisible by 6 if the sum of its digits is divisible by 3 and the digit at unit place is an even digit.
(4) A number is divisible by 5 if the digit at unit place is 5 or 0.
= 1
~ The statement about 11 is wrong: a number is divisible by 11 when the difference between the sums of its digits in odd and even places is 0 or a multiple of 11, not when its digit sum is divisible by 11. For example, 29 has digit sum 11 but is not divisible by 11. The rules given for 3 or 9, 6 and 5 are all correct.

(4) 113
= 3

Q 38 | rea | Syllogism & Statements
Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

All foams are candles.

Q 40 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles

(2) 3

(4) 4
= 3

It is used to develop crops with higher yield, better quality and tolerance to drought, salinity, pests and diseases; its developers, Emmanuelle Charpentier and Jennifer Doudna, won the 2020 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. Artificial pollination and hybrid seed technology are conventional breeding methods, and natural selection is not a breeding technique at all.

Q 105 | sci | Physics
When cotton and rubber are rubbed together, it will result in which of the following?
(1) Cotton will be positively charged and rubber negatively charged.
(2) Rubber will be positively charged and cotton negatively charged.
(3) Cotton and rubber will be positively charged.
(4) Cotton and rubber will be negatively charged.
= 1
~ When two materials are rubbed together, electrons pass from the one that holds them less tightly to the one that holds them more tightly. In the triboelectric series cotton lies on the positive side of rubber, so rubber gains electrons and becomes negatively charged, while cotton loses electrons and becomes positively charged. The two bodies always get equal and opposite charges, so they cannot both be positive or both be negative.

Q 114 | sci | Physics

(1) reversible isothermal expansion
(2) reversible adiabatic expansion
(3) reversible adiabatic compression
(4) reversible isothermal compression
= 4
~ A Carnot cycle has four reversible steps in this order: isothermal expansion at the source temperature, adiabatic expansion (the gas cools to the sink temperature), isothermal compression at the sink temperature (heat is rejected to the sink), and adiabatic compression back to the initial state. So the 3rd step is reversible isothermal compression.

Q 115 | sci | Physics

(2) a free electron would tend to move
(3) a hypothetical free south pole would tend to move
(4) a hypothetical free north pole would tend to move
= 4
~ A magnetic field line is the path along which a hypothetical free (isolated) north pole would tend to move, and the tangent at any point gives the direction of the field there. That is why, outside a magnet, field lines run from the north pole to the south pole. A free south pole would move the opposite way, and moving charges such as protons or electrons do not follow field lines, because the magnetic force on them acts perpendicular to the field.

= 3
