Q 1 | ect | Money, Banking & Inflation
A statement has been given below followed by two conclusions. Assuming given statement to be true which of the following options will be correct for given conclusions.
Statement: The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) increases the Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) to reduce excess liquidity in the economy.
Conclusions:
I. An increase in CRR leads to a decrease in the lending capacity of banks.
II. An increase in CRR results in higher money supply in the economy.
(1) Only Conclusion 1 follows
(2) Only Conclusion 2 follows
(3) Both 1 and 2 follow
(4) Neither 1 nor 2 follows
= 1
~ CRR is the share of deposits that banks must keep with the RBI, so raising it leaves banks less money to lend and Conclusion I follows. A higher CRR is a contractionary measure that reduces credit creation and the money supply, so Conclusion II states the opposite of what happens.

Q 13 | ect | National Income
In income method of national income accounting, which of the following is NOT included while calculating the national income of India?
(1) Compensation of employees
(2) Net interest earned by Indians
(3) Profit of corporates
(4) Salaries paid to Australian citizen working in Indian embassy in Australia
= 4
~ National income counts the factor incomes earned by the normal residents of India: compensation of employees, interest, rent and profits. An Indian embassy abroad is part of India's domestic territory, but Australian citizens hired there are normal residents of Australia, so their salaries are factor income paid to non-residents and are excluded from India's national income.

Q 14 | ect | Demand, Supply & Elasticity
Which of the following conditions must be prevailing in the market if market is NOT operating at equilibrium price determined?
(1) Aggregate demand = aggregate supply
(2) No shortage and no surplus
(3) Shortage in the market
(4) Demand = supply
= 3
~ At the equilibrium price, quantity demanded equals quantity supplied, so there is no shortage and no surplus; options 1, 2 and 4 all describe equilibrium. When the market is not at the equilibrium price, demand and supply do not match, and of the options given only a shortage (excess demand, which arises when price is below equilibrium) describes such a situation.

Q 15 | ect | Demand, Supply & Elasticity
Demand of good or service is said to be elastic if its elasticity satisfies which of the following conditions?
(1) Ed < 1
(2) Ed = 1
(3) Ed > 1
(4) Ed = 0
= 3
~ Demand is elastic when the percentage change in quantity demanded is greater than the percentage change in price, i.e. Ed > 1. Ed < 1 is inelastic demand, Ed = 1 is unitary elastic demand and Ed = 0 is perfectly inelastic demand.

Q 16 | ine | Balance of Payments & Exchange Rates
Match the following economic concepts with their correct descriptions:

Match the List – I with List – II and select the correct answer from the options given below.

Q 28 | ect | Demand, Supply & Elasticity
Given below are two statements, one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R).
Assertion (A): The demand of a tea is directly related to price of coffee.
Reason (R): The cross price elasticity of demand between two substitute goods is always positive.
In the context of the above two statements, which of the following is correct?
(1) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
(2) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)
(3) (A) is true, but (R) is false
(4) (A) is false, but (R) is true
= 1
~ Tea and coffee are substitutes: when the price of coffee rises, people switch to tea and the demand for tea rises, so A is true. For substitute goods the cross price elasticity of demand is positive, which is exactly why tea demand moves in the same direction as the price of coffee, so R explains A.

Q 31 | ine | Balance of Payments & Exchange Rates

Q 33 | ect | Money, Banking & Inflation
Which of the following statements is NOT true for Inflation rate?
(1) Inflation rate= nominal interest rate- real interest rate
(2) Inflation rate = (Price level in current period − price level in previous period)/price level in previous period × 100
(3) Inflation is monetary phenomena
(4) Inflation = nominal interest rate/real interest rate
= 4
~ By the Fisher relation, nominal interest rate ≈ real interest rate + inflation, so inflation = nominal rate − real rate (option 1 is true), and the inflation rate is the percentage change in the price level (option 2 is true). Friedman called inflation "always and everywhere a monetary phenomenon" (option 3). Inflation is the difference between the two rates, not their ratio, so option 4 is not true.

Q 34 | ect | Money, Banking & Inflation

(3) Nominal output or GDP will decrease

Q 37 | ect | Market Structures & Pricing
Given below are two statements, one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R):
Assertion (A): In a perfectly competitive market, equilibrium is reached when market supply equals market demand at a specific price and quantity.
Reason (R): The equilibrium price is determined by the interaction of supply and demand forces.
In the context of above two statements, which one of the following is correct?
(1) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.
(2) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(3) A is true, but R is false.
(4) A is false, but R is true.
= 1

Q 38 | ect | Market Structures & Pricing

~ A perfectly competitive market has a very large number of sellers of a homogeneous product, so each firm is too small to influence price and simply accepts the market-determined price as a price taker. Price makers and price discrimination belong to monopoly and other imperfect markets.

1. Mean = Mode =Median;
2. Odd order central moments are identically equal to zero;
3. Coefficient of skewness is zero.
Which of the following holds?
(1) 2 and 3 are true
(2) 1 and 3 are true
(3) 1 and 2 are true
(4) All three are true
= 4
~ The normal curve is perfectly symmetrical about its mean, so mean = median = mode, all odd-order central moments (μ1, μ3, μ5, …) are zero, and the coefficient of skewness β1 = μ3²/μ2³ is zero. All three statements are true.

Q 129 | dsp | Skewness & Kurtosis
On the basis of the following statements,
1. Karl Pearson’s measure of skewness lies between -∞ and ∞;
2. Bowley’s measure of skewness lies between -1 and 1;
3. Karl Pearson’s measure of skewness lies between -3 and 3;
Which of the following holds?
(1) 2 and 3 are true
(2) 1 and 3 are true
(3) 1 and 2 are true
(4) All three are true
= 1
~ Bowley's coefficient (Q3 + Q1 − 2Median)/(Q3 − Q1) always lies between −1 and +1, and Karl Pearson's coefficient in its median form 3(Mean − Median)/σ lies between −3 and +3; on this basis the key accepts statements 2 and 3 and rejects 1. However, Pearson's mode form (Mean − Mode)/σ has no theoretical limits, so some textbooks would also call statement 1 true, and in strict logic any value between −3 and +3 also lies between −∞ and ∞, which would make all three statements true. (Official key; some sources differ.)

Q 131 | mct | Mean, Median & Mode
For 6 bags of rice, the mean weight per bag is 119 kg. The weights of 5 bags are 115 kg, 109 kg, 129 kg, 117 kg and 114 kg. The weight (in kg) of the sixth bag is equal to:
(1) 120
(2) 125
(3) 140
(4) 130
= 4
~ Total weight of 6 bags = 6 × 119 = 714 kg. Weight of the 5 given bags = 115 + 109 + 129 + 117 + 114 = 584 kg. Sixth bag = 714 − 584 = 130 kg.

Q 132 | sdc | Schedules & Questionnaires
For collection of data through Schedules, which of the following does not hold?
(1) Trained enumerators are given schedules with relevant questions
(2) Schedules are filled by enumerators
(3) Occasional field checks are carried out to check on the work of enumerators
(4) Reliability of collected data does not depend upon capability of enumerator
= 4
~ In the schedule method, trained enumerators take the schedules to the respondents, ask the questions and fill in the answers themselves, and their work is checked in the field from time to time. Because the enumerator collects and records everything, the reliability of the data depends heavily on the enumerator's skill, training and honesty, so statement 4 is false.

Q 133 | cor | Karl Pearson's Correlation
If X ~ N(0, 1) and Y = X² then rXY is:
(1) 1
(2) -1
(3) 0.5
(4) 0
= 4
~ Cov(X, Y) = E(X·X²) − E(X)E(X²) = E(X³) − 0 × 1 = 0, because all odd moments of the standard normal distribution are zero. Hence rXY = 0, even though Y is completely determined by X: r measures only linear relationship, and the relation Y = X² is not linear.

Q 135 | cor | Karl Pearson's Correlation
Given that X, Y and Z = (X + 2Y) are variables such that Var(X) = 4, Var(Y) = 3 and Var(Z) = 4, then rXY is:
(1) 2/3
(2) 1/3
(3) −√3/2
(4) 2/√3
= 3
~ Var(Z) = Var(X) + 4Var(Y) + 2 × 2 × Cov(X, Y), so 4 = 4 + 12 + 4Cov(X, Y), giving Cov(X, Y) = −3. rXY = Cov(X, Y)/(σX σY) = −3/(√4 × √3) = −3/(2√3) = −√3/2. (2/√3 is greater than 1, so it can never be a correlation coefficient.)

Q 136 | cmp | Digital Logic & Number Systems
What will be the output of a two-input OR gate when both inputs are LOW (0)?
(1) 0
(2) 1
(3) Indeterminate
(4) 2
= 1
~ An OR gate gives output 1 if at least one input is 1; only when all inputs are 0 is the output 0 (0 + 0 = 0 in Boolean addition). The output is always a definite binary value, so "indeterminate" and "2" are impossible.

Q 137 | cmp | Digital Logic & Number Systems
A NOT gate is used in a digital circuit, where it takes the output of an OR gate as its input. If the inputs to the OR gate are A = 0 and B = 1, then what will the final output after passing through the NOT gate be?
(1) 0
(2) 1
(3) Indeterminate
(4) Same as the OR gate output
= 1
~ OR gate: A + B = 0 + 1 = 1. NOT gate inverts it: NOT 1 = 0. (An OR gate followed by a NOT gate is a NOR gate, and the final output is the opposite of the OR output, not the same.)

Q 138 | cmp | Digital Logic & Number Systems
Which of the following statements best describes the function of a NAND gate?
(1) A NAND gate outputs 1 only when both inputs are 1.
(2) A NAND gate performs logical addition and then inverts the output.
(3) A NAND gate outputs 0 only when both inputs are 1; otherwise, it outputs 1.
(4) A NAND gate is the same as an OR gate with an inverted output.
= 3
~ NAND = NOT AND: the AND output is inverted, so the output is 0 only when both inputs are 1 and is 1 in all other cases. Option 1 describes an AND gate, while options 2 and 4 (OR, i.e. logical addition, followed by inversion) describe a NOR gate.

Q 139 | cmp | Digital Logic & Number Systems
Read the given statement(s) and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statement(s) is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statement(s).
Statement: In 2’s complement representation, negative numbers are obtained by inverting all bits of the positive number and adding 1.
Conclusions:
I. A number with MSB = 1 in 2’s complement is always negative.
II. 2’s complement allows direct arithmetic operations without requiring separate subtraction circuits.
(1) Only conclusion I follows.
(2) Only conclusion II follows.
(3) Both conclusions I and II follow.
(4) Neither conclusion I nor II follow.
= 3
~ In 2's complement the most significant bit acts as the sign bit, so a number with MSB = 1 is negative (for example, 1111 = −1 in 4 bits). Because negatives are formed this way, subtraction A − B is done as A + (2's complement of B) on the same adder circuit, so no separate subtractor is needed. Both conclusions follow.

Q 140 | cmp | Digital Logic & Number Systems
Which of the following best describes the functionality of a NOR gate in digital logic circuits?
(1) It outputs HIGH only if both inputs are HIGH.
(2) It produces a LOW output only when both inputs are LOW.
(3) It is the inverse of an OR gate, producing a HIGH output only when both inputs are LOW.
(4) It performs the AND operation and then inverts the result.
= 3
~ NOR = NOT OR: the OR output is inverted, so the output is HIGH (1) only when both inputs are LOW (0), and LOW if any input is HIGH. Option 1 describes an AND gate, option 2 describes an OR gate (LOW only when both inputs are LOW), and option 4 describes a NAND gate.

Q 141 | cmp | MS Excel & Office

Q 143 | cmp | MS Excel & Office
Select the option that is correct regarding the following two statements labelled Assertion (A) and Reason (R).
Assertion (A): In MS-Excel 2019, inserting a new row in the middle of a dataset shifts the existing rows downward, preserving the relative cell references in formulas.
Reason (R): MS-Excel automatically updates absolute cell references when new rows or columns are inserted to ensure formulas remain accurate.
(1) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, and (R) correctly explains (A).
(2) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but (R) does not correctly explain (A).
(3) Assertion (A) is true, but Reason (R) is false.
(4) Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.
= 3
~ A is true: when a row is inserted, the rows below move down and formulas are adjusted so that they still point to the same data. The key treats R as false on the idea that absolute references (such as $A$5) are fixed and never change. In actual Excel behaviour, inserting rows or columns also adjusts absolute references (=$A$5 becomes =$A$6 when a row is inserted above row 5); the $ sign only stops a reference from changing when the formula is copied, so R can be argued to be true. (Official key; some sources differ.)

Q 144 | cmp | MS Excel & Office

(1) Select the cell and start typing directly.
(2) Click the cell and press F2 before editing.

Q 145 | cmp | MS Excel & Office
What will happen if a user selects multiple non-adjacent rows and attempts to delete them simultaneously, in MS Excel 2019?
(1) Excel will delete only the first selected row and ignore the others.
(2) Excel will delete all the selected rows at once.

= 2

Q 146 | cmp | Memory & Hardware
Which of the following best describes the role of cache memory in a computer system?
(1) It stores frequently accessed data to reduce access time for the processor.
(2) It serves as a backup for primary memory in case of failure.
(3) It provides long-term storage for operating system files.
(4) It replaces the need for RAM in modern computer systems.
= 1
~ Cache is a small, very fast memory (usually SRAM) placed between the CPU and main memory; it keeps copies of frequently used instructions and data so the processor can get them faster than from RAM. It is not a backup, it is volatile and not long-term storage (that is the job of secondary storage), and it works along with RAM rather than replacing it.

Q 147 | cmp | Memory & Hardware
Which of the following options is correct with reference to the given statements and conclusions?
Statement 1: Direct Memory Access (DMA) enhances system performance by allowing data transfers without continuous CPU intervention.
Statement 2: DMA can transfer data between memory and I/O devices without needing CPU control at any stage.
Conclusions:
I. DMA improves efficiency by freeing up CPU resources for other tasks.
II. The CPU is never involved in any data transfer when DMA is used.
(1) Only conclusion I follows.
(2) Only conclusion II follows.
(3) Both conclusions I and II follow.
(4) Neither conclusion I nor conclusion II follows.
= 1
~ Since DMA moves data between memory and I/O devices without continuous CPU intervention, the CPU is free for other work, so conclusion I follows. Conclusion II is too sweeping: the statements say nothing about every data transfer in the system, and in practice the CPU still sets up each DMA transfer (address, count, direction) and is informed by an interrupt when it ends, so "never involved in any data transfer" does not follow.

Q 148 | cmp | MS Excel & Office

Q 149 | cmp | MS Excel & Office
What happens when a user enters a number with leading zeros (e.g., 00789) into a cell, in MS Excel 2019, by default?
(1) Excel retains the leading zeros and stores the number as entered.
(2) Excel automatically removes the leading zeros and stores the number as 789.
(3) Excel stores it as a text value instead of a number, preserving the leading zeros.
(4) Excel converts it into a scientific notation format, such as 7.89E+02.
= 2
~ In the default General format Excel treats 00789 as the number 789 and drops the leading zeros. To keep them, the cell must be formatted as Text (or a custom format such as 00000 used), or an apostrophe typed first ('00789). Scientific notation is used only for very large numbers, not for a three-digit value.

Q 150 | cmp | Memory & Hardware
Which of the following statements about computer memory hierarchy is INCORRECT?
(1) Cache memory is faster than RAM but has lower storage capacity.
(2) Virtual memory is a section of the hard drive used to extend RAM.
(3) ROM can be written and modified multiple times during normal computer operation.
(4) SRAM (Static RAM) is used for cache memory because it is faster than DRAM.
= 3
~ ROM (Read Only Memory) can only be read during normal operation; its contents are written at manufacture or by special programming (as in EPROM/EEPROM flashing), not modified repeatedly while the computer runs. The other statements are correct: cache is faster but smaller than RAM, virtual memory uses disk space to extend RAM, and cache is built from SRAM because it is faster than DRAM.
