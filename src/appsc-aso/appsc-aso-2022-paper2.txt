Q 1 | ect | Production, Costs & Factor Pricing
“Production is the outcome of the combined activity of the four factors of production”.
With reference to the above statement, which of the following is/are NOT considered as factors of production?
(i) Land
(ii) Labour
(iii) Capital
(iv) Organisation
(v) Service
(1) (i) only
(2) (ii) only
(3) (i), (ii) and (iii) only
(4) (v) only
= 4
~ The four factors of production are land, labour, capital and organisation (entrepreneurship), earning rent, wages, interest and profit respectively. A service is an output of production, not a factor of production, so only (v) is not a factor.

Q 2 | ect | Demand, Supply & Elasticity
Two goods X and Y are said to be ______ if the price of good X is inversely related to the demand of good Y.
(1) substitutes
(2) complementary
(3) Giffen goods
(4) necessities
= 2
~ For complementary goods (e.g. car and petrol) a rise in the price of X reduces the demand for Y, so the cross elasticity of demand is negative. For substitutes the relation is direct (positive cross elasticity); Giffen goods and necessities describe a good's own price or income response, not a two-good relation.

Q 3 | ect | Demand, Supply & Elasticity
Which of the following statements are true/false?
(i) Inferior goods have positive income effect.
(ii) Inferior goods have negative income effect.
(1) Both statements (i) and (ii) are true
(2) Statement (i) is true and statement (ii) is false
(3) Both statements (i) and (ii) are false
(4) Statement (i) is false and statement (ii) is true
= 4
~ For an inferior good, demand falls when income rises (income elasticity < 0), so its income effect is negative. Hence (i) is false and (ii) is true; normal goods are the ones with a positive income effect.

Q 4 | ect | Demand, Supply & Elasticity
Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding degree of price elasticity of demand?
(1) Perfectly Elastic Demand: (Ed = 1)
(2) Perfectly Inelastic Demand: (Ed = 0)
(3) Less than Unit Elastic: (Ed < 1)
(4) More than Unit Elastic: (Ed > 1)
= 1
~ Perfectly elastic demand has Ed = ∞ (a horizontal demand curve); Ed = 1 is unitary elastic demand, so statement (1) is false. The other three are correctly stated: perfectly inelastic Ed = 0, relatively inelastic Ed < 1 and relatively elastic Ed > 1.

Q 5 | ect | Market Structures & Pricing
A ______ competition is a market situation, in which many firms compete with each other to sell their closely-related differentiated products.
(1) perfect
(2) monopoly
(3) monopolistic
(4) oligopoly
= 3
~ Monopolistic competition (Chamberlin) has many sellers selling close but differentiated products (brands), with free entry and some control over price. Perfect competition has homogeneous products, monopoly has a single seller and oligopoly has only a few sellers.

Q 6 | ect | National Income
If the nominal GDP = ₹18,000 crores and the real GDP = ₹12,000 crores, calculate the GDP deflator.
(1) 0.66 or 66%
(2) 1.5 or 150%
(3) 1 or 100%
(4) 0.60 or 60%
= 2
~ GDP deflator = Nominal GDP / Real GDP = 18,000 / 12,000 = 1.5, i.e. 1.5 × 100 = 150%. It shows that prices have risen by 50% over the base year.

Q 7 | ect | National Income
Which of the following is NOT a method of measurement of National Income?
(1) Value Added Method
(2) Income Method
(3) Expenditure Method
(4) Cost Accounting Method
= 4
~ National income is measured by three methods: the value added (product/output) method, the income method and the expenditure method. Cost accounting is a branch of accounting used by firms to find the cost of a product; it is not a method of measuring national income.

Q 8 | ect | National Income
If the disposable income is ₹100 and consumption expenditure is ₹80, calculate the Average Propensity to Save (APS)?
(1) 20%
(2) 30%
(3) 40%
(4) 10%
= 1
~ Saving S = Y − C = 100 − 80 = 20. APS = S / Y = 20 / 100 = 0.2 = 20% (equivalently APS = 1 − APC = 1 − 0.8).

Q 9 | ect | National Income
The part of investment (increase or decrease) which takes place due to a change in the level of national income is termed as ______.
(1) gross investment
(2) net investment
(3) induced investment
(4) autonomous investment
= 3
~ Induced investment depends on income: it rises or falls when national income (and hence demand) changes. Autonomous investment is independent of income, while gross and net investment differ only by whether depreciation is deducted.

Q 10 | ect | Money, Banking & Inflation
Which of the following statements are correct/incorrect regarding items excluded from money supply of a country?
(i) The cash held by commercial banks is included in money supply.
(ii) The stock of monetary gold held in reserves as a backing to paper currency is included in money supply.
(1) Both statements (i) and (ii) are incorrect.
(2) Both statements (i) and (ii) are correct.
(3) Statement (i) is correct and statement (ii) is incorrect.
(4) Statement (i) is incorrect and statement (ii) is correct.
= 4
~ Money supply counts only the money held by the public, so cash held by commercial banks (their vault cash and reserves) is excluded and statement (i) is incorrect. APPSC's key takes (ii) as correct, possibly because the monetary gold stock is counted among the assets behind reserve money (the monetary base). However, gold kept in reserve as backing for paper currency is not money in the hands of the public, and standard texts list it among the items excluded from money supply (as the question's own heading suggests), which would make both statements incorrect (option 1). (Official key; some sources differ.)

Q 13 | ect | Money, Banking & Inflation
Compute credit multiplier and total credit money created by the banking system, if the required reserve ratio is 10% for every ₹1,00,000 deposited in the banking system.

(2) 10 and ₹1,00,000

Q 17 | acc | Bank Reconciliation, Trial Balance & Final Accounts
Which of the following transactions will be added when the starting point is debit, i.e. favourable balance as per cash book.
(1) Cheques issued but not presented for payment
(2) Cheques deposited but not collected or credited by the bank
(3) Cheques deposited but dishonoured
(4) Cheques recorded but not deposited
= 1
~ Cheques issued are credited in the cash book at once, but the bank debits the account only when they are presented, so the pass book balance is higher and they are added to the cash book debit balance. Cheques deposited but not collected, cheques dishonoured and cheques recorded but not deposited all make the pass book balance lower, so they are deducted.

Q 22 | ine | Balance of Payments & Exchange Rates
Which of the following statements are true/false?
(i) The balance of payment of a country is a systematic record of all economic transactions between the residents of a country and rest of the world, during a year.
(ii) The current account records exports and imports of goods and services and unilateral transfers.
(1) Both statements (i) and (ii) are true
(2) Statement (i) is false and statement (ii) is true
(3) Statement (i) is true and statement (ii) is false
(4) Both statements (i) and (ii) are false

~ Both are standard definitions. The balance of payments records all economic transactions of residents with the rest of the world in a year, and its current account covers trade in goods (visible), services (invisible) and unilateral transfers such as remittances and gifts.

Q 23 | ine | Balance of Payments & Exchange Rates
The ______ account includes all international economic transactions with income or payment flows occurring within the current year and the ______ account is made up of transfers of financial assets and the acquisition and disposal of non-produced or non-financial assets.
(1) current; capital
(2) capital; capital
(3) current; current
(4) capital; current
= 1
~ The current account records transactions that create income or payment flows within the year (trade in goods and services, primary income and current transfers). The capital account records capital transfers and the acquisition or disposal of non-produced, non-financial assets such as land bought by a foreign embassy, trademarks, leases and licences.

Q 27 | ine | Trade & Protection
Which of the following statements are true/false regarding trade?
(i) Internal trade refers to buying and selling of goods and services within the boundaries of a nation.
(ii) The buying and selling of goods and services in large quantities for the purpose of resale or intermediate use is known as wholesale trade.

Q 29 | cmp | Digital Logic & Number Systems
Convert (0.25)₁₀ to binary.
(1) (0.01)₂
(2) (0.10)₂
(3) (1.01)₂
(4) (0.25)₁
= 1

Q 30 | ind | Poverty, Unemployment & Inequality
______ unemployment exists in an economy due to movement of people from one job to another and in the process, they remain unemployment for some times.
(1) Visible
(2) Seasonal
(3) Cyclical
(4) Frictional
= 4
~ Frictional unemployment is the temporary unemployment of people who are between jobs, i.e. while they search for or move to a new job. Seasonal unemployment follows the seasons (e.g. agriculture), cyclical unemployment follows business-cycle slumps, and visible (open) unemployment is the opposite of disguised unemployment.

Q 35 | cmp | Digital Logic & Number Systems

Q 36 | cmp | Memory & Hardware
Which of the following options is correct?
(1) A kilobyte = 2¹⁰ bytes, a megabyte = 2²⁰ bytes, and a gigabyte = 2⁴⁰ bytes
(2) A kilobyte = 2¹⁰ bytes, a megabyte = 2²⁰ bytes, and a gigabyte = 2³⁰ bytes
(3) A kilobyte = 2¹⁰ bytes, a megabyte = 2³⁰ bytes, and a gigabyte = 2⁴⁰ bytes
(4) A kilobyte = 2²⁰ bytes, a megabyte = 2³⁰ bytes, and a gigabyte = 2⁴⁰ bytes

Q 43 | cmp | MS Excel & Office
Which of the following statements are true/false?
(i) The IPMT financial function returns the interest payment for a given period based on period, constant payment and a constant interest rate.
(ii) The PPMT financial function returns the payment on the principal for a given period for an investment based on periodic, constant payments and a constant interest rate.
(1) Statement (i) is true and statement (ii) is false
(2) Both statements (i) and (ii) are true
(3) Statement (i) is false and statement (ii) is true
(4) Both statements (i) and (ii) are false
= 2
~ IPMT returns the interest part of a payment for a given period, and PPMT returns the principal part of the payment for that period, both assuming periodic constant payments and a constant interest rate. Together IPMT + PPMT equal the full instalment given by PMT, so both statements are true.

~ Maximum error E = z × σ/√n, so n = (zσ/E)². With z = 1.96 for 95% confidence: n = (1.96 × 5/0.5)² = 19.6² = 384.16, which is rounded up to 385 so that the error does not exceed 0.5.

Q 140 | sdc | Census, Sampling & Probability
An officer conducts an ethnographic investigation into the complications confronted by extremely-backwards employees. Which method of sampling will be the most appropriate?
(1) Random sampling
(2) Stratified sampling
(3) Cluster sampling
(4) Systematic sampling
= 3
~ An ethnographic study observes people in their natural groups and settings. Such employees are usually found together in particular work units or localities, so whole groups (clusters) are selected and studied in depth, which is cluster sampling; a list-based random, stratified or systematic sample would scatter the study and needs a complete sampling frame. Q 141 | sdc | Census, Sampling & Probability
Suppose bag A consists of 4 red and 5 white marbles and bag B consists of 6 red and 3 white marbles. A marble is selected at random from bag A and kept in bag B. Finally, a marble is selected at random from bag B. What is the probability that white marble was kept from bag A to bag B given that the marble chosen from bag B is red?
(1) 12/29
(2) 13/29
(3) 14/29
(4) 15/29
= 4
~ By Bayes' theorem. If a white marble is moved (probability 5/9), bag B has 6 red and 4 white, so P(red) = 6/10; if a red marble is moved (probability 4/9), bag B has 7 red and 3 white, so P(red) = 7/10. P(white moved | red drawn) = (5/9 × 6/10)/(5/9 × 6/10 + 4/9 × 7/10) = 30/(30 + 28) = 30/58 = 15/29.
