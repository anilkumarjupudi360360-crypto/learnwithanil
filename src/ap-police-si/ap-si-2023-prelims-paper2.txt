Q 1 | sci | Biology & Health
Match the following.
List-I    List-II
a) Calciferol    i) Pellagra
b) Niacin    ii) Rickets
c) Thiamin    iii) Xerophthalmia
d) Retinol    iv) Beri beri
The correct answer is :
(1) a-i, b-iv, c-iii, d-ii
(2) a-iv, b-iii, c-ii, d-i
(3) a-ii, b-i, c-iv, d-iii
(4) a-ii, b-iii, c-i, d-iv

~ Deficiency of calciferol (vitamin D) causes rickets (a-ii), niacin (B3) causes pellagra (b-i), thiamin (B1) causes beri beri (c-iv) and retinol (vitamin A) causes xerophthalmia (d-iii). Only option (3) has all four pairs right.

Q 2 | sci | Biology & Health
A colour blind man married a woman who is the daughter of a colour blind father and a homozygous mother with normal vision. What are the chances of their daughters being colour blind?
(1) 25%
(2) 50%
(3) 75%
(4) 100%
= 2
~ Colour blindness is X-linked recessive. The woman gets Xc from her colour blind father and X from her homozygous normal mother, so she is a carrier (XXc); her husband is XcY. Every daughter receives Xc from the father and either X or Xc from the mother, so half the daughters (XXc carriers) are normal and half (XcXc) are colour blind: 50%.

Q 3 | geo | Environment & Ecology
Study the following and identify the correct statements.
I) Water hyacinth is called terror of Bengal.
II) Scrubbers are used to control the heavy metal pollution.
III) UV-B rays cause snow blindness.
IV) Electrostatic precipitators are used to control harmful gases of automobile exhausts.
(1) I, III
(2) II, IV
(3) II, III
(4) III, IV
= 1
~ Water hyacinth is the invasive weed known as the "Terror of Bengal" because it chokes water bodies and depletes oxygen, and UV-B radiation causes snow blindness (photokeratitis). Scrubbers remove gases such as SO2 and some particulates, not heavy metals, and electrostatic precipitators remove particulate matter (fly ash), not the gases of automobile exhausts, so II and IV are wrong.

Q 4 | sci | Biology & Health
From the following, pick up the correct combinations :
S.No. | Endocrine gland | Hormone | Abnormality due to over secretion or under secretion
I | Thyroid gland | Calcitonin | Dwarfism
II | Parathyroid glands | Parathormone | Tetany
III | Adrenal glands | Glucocorticoids | Addison's disease
IV | Pancreas | Insulin | Diabetes insipidus
(1) I, II
(2) II, IV
(3) II, III
(4) I, III
= 3
~ Under-secretion of parathormone lowers blood calcium and causes tetany, and under-secretion of glucocorticoids causes Addison's disease, so II and III are correct. Calcitonin does not cause dwarfism (that comes from low growth hormone or thyroxine), and insulin deficiency causes diabetes mellitus, whereas diabetes insipidus is due to lack of ADH.

Q 6 | sci | Physics
The device used to accelerate charged particles is

(2) Cloud chamber
(3) Electroscope
(4) Cyclotron
= 4

Q 8 | sci | Physics
Due to which property of matter, soap bubbles are spherical in shape?
(1) Viscosity
(2) Young's modulus
(3) Surface tension
(4) Elasticity
= 3
~ Surface tension makes a liquid film contract to the smallest possible surface area for the enclosed air, and a sphere has the least surface area for a given volume, so soap bubbles are spherical.

Q 9 | sci | Physics
The change in the frequency of the sound heard by an observer due to the relative motion between the source of sound and the observer is known as
(1) Zeeman shift
(2) Raman shift
(3) Stark shift
(4) Doppler shift
= 4
~ This is the Doppler effect (Doppler shift): the apparent frequency rises when source and observer approach and falls when they move apart. Zeeman and Stark shifts are splittings of spectral lines in magnetic and electric fields, and the Raman shift is a change in scattered light's wavelength.

Q 10 | sci | Physics

(1) 72
(2) 12
(3) 18
(4) 36
= 4

Q 11 | sci | Physics
The de Broglie wavelength of an electron of mass 9.1 × 10^(-28) g travelling with a speed of 3.641 × 10^6 ms^(-1) approximately (in m) is
(1) 2.0 × 10^(-8)
(2) 2.0 × 10^(-10)
(3) 4.0 × 10^(-8)
(4) 4.0 × 10^(-10)
= 2
~ Using λ = h/(mv) with m = 9.1 × 10^(-31) kg, mv = 9.1 × 10^(-31) × 3.641 × 10^6 ≈ 3.31 × 10^(-24) kg m/s, so λ = 6.626 × 10^(-34) / 3.31 × 10^(-24) ≈ 2.0 × 10^(-10) m.

Q 12 | sci | Chemistry
According to molecular orbital theory the correct bond order values of species NO⁺, O2⁻ and CO⁺ respectively is
(1) 3.0, 2.5, 1.5
(2) 1.5, 3.0, 2.5
(3) 2.5, 1.5, 3.0
(4) 3.0, 1.5, 2.5
= 4
~ NO⁺ has 10 valence electrons (like N2): (8 − 2)/2 = 3.0. O2⁻ has 13 valence electrons (8 bonding, 5 antibonding): (8 − 5)/2 = 1.5. CO⁺ has 9 valence electrons (7 bonding, 2 antibonding): (7 − 2)/2 = 2.5.

Q 14 | sci | Chemistry

(2) Pig iron
(3) Cast iron
(4) Magnetite
= 1
~ Wrought iron is the purest commercial form of iron, with less than about 0.2% carbon, whereas pig iron and cast iron contain several percent carbon. Magnetite (Fe3O4) is an ore of iron, not a commercial form of the metal.

Q 16 | sci | Biology & Health
Assertion (A): Sorting out of the individuals with useful variations from a heterogenous populations by nature is called Natural Selection.
Reason (R): Useful variations appear in every generation and are inherited from one generation to other. These variations accumulate by number of generations. Thus, the variations are so prominent that individual change into a new species.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are correct; (R) is correct explanation for (A).
(2) Both (A) and (R) are correct; (R) is not correct explanation for (A).
(3) (A) is correct and (R) is not correct.
(4) (A) is not correct and (R) is correct.
= 1
~ Natural selection is nature's sorting of individuals carrying useful variations, and the reason describes how it works: useful variations appear in each generation, are inherited and accumulate over many generations until a new species arises. So (R) correctly explains (A).

Q 17 | sci | Biology & Health
Assertion (A): The amount of DNA present in somatic cell depends on the phase of the cell cycle.
Reason (R): Telophase and G1 phase nuclei contain only half (as 2c) the amount of DNA found in the prophase; G2 phase and metaphase cells represent 4c.
(1) Both (A) and (R) are correct; (R) is correct explanation for (A).
(2) Both (A) and (R) are correct; (R) is not correct explanation for (A).
(3) (A) is correct and (R) is not correct.
(4) (A) is not correct and (R) is correct.
= 1
~ DNA content changes through the cell cycle: it is 2c in G1 and telophase nuclei, doubles to 4c after the S phase, and stays 4c in G2, prophase and metaphase. The reason gives exactly this variation, so it explains the assertion.

Q 18 | geo | Environment & Ecology
Identify the exploitation efficiency in an ecosystem among the following.
(1) Production / Assimilation
(2) Production / Ingestion
(3) Consumer productivity / Prey production
(4) Ingestion of food / Prey production
= 4
~ Exploitation efficiency is the proportion of the prey's production that a consumer actually ingests, i.e. ingestion of food divided by prey production. Production/assimilation is production efficiency, so option (1) is a different measure.

Which of the following is NOT a feature of the Government of India Act, 1935?
(1) Dyarchy in the Provinces
(2) A Bi-cameral Legislature at the Provincial level
(3) Provincial Autonomy
(4) An All India Federation
= 1
~ The 1935 Act abolished dyarchy in the provinces (which came from the 1919 Act) and introduced provincial autonomy, with dyarchy shifted to the Centre. It also provided for bicameral legislatures in some provinces and an All-India Federation, which never came into being.

Q 97 | pol | Judiciary
The salaries and allowances of the Judges of High Court are charged on
(1) Consolidated Fund of India
(2) Consolidated Fund of the State
(3) Contingency Fund of India
(4) Contingency Fund of the State
= 2
~ Under Article 202(3) the salaries and allowances of High Court Judges are charged on the Consolidated Fund of the State (not subject to the vote of the Legislature), while their pensions are charged on the Consolidated Fund of India. Contingency Funds are only for unforeseen expenditure.

Q 98 | pol | Union & State Government
The State which has the largest number of seats reserved for Scheduled Tribes in Lok Sabha is
(1) Odisha
(2) Jharkhand
(3) Gujarat
(4) Madhya Pradesh
= 4
~ Of the 47 Lok Sabha seats reserved for Scheduled Tribes, Madhya Pradesh has the most (6). Odisha and Jharkhand have 5 each and Gujarat has 4.

Q 99 | pol | Constitution & Rights
The following Schedule of the Indian Constitution contains provisions relating to Anti-Defection Act
(1) Second Schedule
(2) Fifth Schedule
(3) Eighth Schedule
(4) Tenth Schedule
= 4
~ The Tenth Schedule, added by the 52nd Amendment Act (1985), contains the anti-defection provisions on disqualification of legislators for defection. The Second Schedule deals with salaries of constitutional office-holders, the Fifth with Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes, and the Eighth with languages.

Q 100 | pol | Union & State Government
Who among the following became the Prime Minister of India without being earlier the Chief Minister of a State?
(1) Chandra Shekhar
(2) Morarji Desai
(3) Charan Singh
(4) Vishwanath Pratap Singh
= 1
~ Chandra Shekhar (PM 1990-91) never headed a State government. Morarji Desai had been Chief Minister of Bombay State, and Charan Singh and V.P. Singh had both been Chief Ministers of Uttar Pradesh.
