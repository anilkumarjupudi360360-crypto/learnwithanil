Q 1 | qa | Time, Work, Speed & Distance
A train runs at a speed of 111 kmph to cover a distance of 222 km and then at a speed of 86 kmph to cover a distance of 258 km. Find the average speed of the train for the entire distance.
(1) 117 kmph
(2) 96 kmph
(3) 72 kmph

Q 3 | qa | Percentage & Profit-Loss
A vendor claims to sell wheat at a loss of 25%. But he cheats by using weights that weigh 55% less than what is mentioned on them. What is his profit percentage (rounded off to 2 decimal places)?

(3) 66.66

Q 6 | qa | Number System & Simplification
For a positive integer b > 1, if the product of two numbers 6344 and 42b8 is divisible by 12, then find the least value of b.
(1) 5
(2) 7
(3) 3
(4) 4
= 4
~ 12 = 4 × 3. 6344 is divisible by 4 (its last two digits are 44) and 42b8 is even, so the product is already divisible by 4. The digit sum of 6344 is 17, so 6344 is not divisible by 3; hence 42b8 must be, i.e. 4 + 2 + b + 8 = 14 + b must be a multiple of 3, giving b = 1, 4 or 7. Since b > 1, the least value is b = 4.

Q 36 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles

Q 37 | rea | Series
Refer to the following letter, symbol series and answer the question that follows. Counting to be done from left to right only.
(Left) F & L A V @ * K Z > N E $ B = M U & # T £ G € Z (Right)
How many such symbols are there each of which are immediately preceded by a letter and also immediately followed by another symbol?
(1) One
(2) Three
(3) Two
(4) Four
= 3
~ The required pattern is letter – symbol – symbol. It occurs at V @ * (@ has V before it and * after it) and at U & # (& has U before it and # after it). Q 91 | sci | Biology

Q 96 | geo | World & Physical Geography
Which of the following is the main characteristic of the convergent plate boundary?
(1) Plates slide past each other horizontally.
(2) Plates move in the same direction.
(3) Plates move away from each other.
(4) Plates come towards each other.
= 4
~ At a convergent (destructive) plate boundary, two lithospheric plates move towards each other, so one plate usually sinks (subducts) beneath the other or the crust is crumpled and pushed up. Such boundaries produce fold mountains like the Himalayas, deep ocean trenches and volcanic island arcs. Plates moving away from each other form a divergent boundary (e.g. the Mid-Atlantic Ridge), and plates sliding past each other horizontally form a transform boundary (e.g. the San Andreas Fault).

Q 97 | sci | Chemistry

(2) [HA]>[A⁻]

= 1
~ The Henderson-Hasselbalch equation for a weak acid buffer is pH = pKa + log([A⁻]/[HA]). If pH = pKa, then log([A⁻]/[HA]) = 0, so [A⁻]/[HA] = 1, i.e. [HA] = [A⁻]. This is the half-neutralisation point, where the acid is exactly half ionised and the buffer has its maximum buffering capacity.

Q 104 | sci | Physics
A UV light is passed from an optical fiber into air at an angle of 45° and the refractive index of the fiber is √2. The angle of refraction will be:
(1) 90°
(2) 30°
(3) 60°
(4) 0°
= 1
~ By Snell's law, n(fiber) × sin i = n(air) × sin r, so √2 × sin 45° = 1 × sin r. Since sin 45° = 1/√2, sin r = √2 × 1/√2 = 1, so r = 90°. Q 114 | sci | Physics
Earth’s magnetism is caused by the:
(1) Magnus effect
(2) solar effect
(3) Doppler effect
(4) dynamo effect
= 4
~ Earth's magnetic field is produced by the dynamo effect (geodynamo): convection currents of molten iron and nickel in the liquid outer core, twisted by Earth's rotation, generate electric currents, and these currents sustain the magnetic field. The Magnus effect is the curving of a spinning ball moving through air, and the Doppler effect is the apparent change in frequency of a wave due to relative motion of source and observer.
