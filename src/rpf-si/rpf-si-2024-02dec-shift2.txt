Q 1 | qa | Simple & Compound Interest
If at same rate of interest, in 2 years, the simple interest is ₹60 and compound interest is ₹66, then what is the principal (in ₹)?
(1) 143
(2) 150
(3) 145
(4) 154
= 2
~ Simple interest for 2 years is ₹60, so the interest for one year is ₹30. The extra ₹6 of compound interest is interest earned on the first year's ₹30, so the rate = 6/30 × 100 = 20%. Principal = 30 × 100/20 = ₹150.

Q 4 | qa | Time, Work, Speed & Distance
A man walks to a viewpoint and returns to the starting point by his car maintaining constant speed and thus takes a total time of 3 hours 30 minutes. He would have gained 3 hours by driving both ways. How long would it have taken for him to walk both ways with same walking speed?
(1) 6 hours 15 minutes
(2) 7 hours 30 minutes
(3) 6 hours 45 minutes
(4) 5 hours 15 minutes
= 1

Q 25 | qa | Simple & Compound Interest
A bank offers 8.8% compound interest per annum calculated on a half-yearly basis. A customer deposits ₹1924 each on 1 January and 1 July of a year. At the end of the year, the amount he would have gained by way of interest is ________. [Give your answer correct to 2 decimal places.]

(3) ₹257.69

Q 37 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles
I, J, K, L, P, Q and R are sitting around a square table facing the centre of the table. Only three people sit between P and J. Q sits to the immediate left of J. No one sits to the right of R. Only two people sit between R and Q. K sits to the immediate right of I. How many people sit between L and K?
(1) Four
(2) Two
(3) Three
(4) One
= 2
~ Seven people sit at a square table, and 'no one sits to the right of R' means the seat immediately to R's right is vacant, so take 8 seats with one empty. Facing the centre, left is clockwise; going clockwise from R the order is R, L, J, Q, K, I, P and then the empty seat (to R's right). Check: L and J sit between R and Q, Q, K and I sit between J and P, Q is immediately left of J and K is immediately right of I. Between L and K (the shorter way) sit J and Q, so the answer is two. Another seating, clockwise R, K, I, J, Q, L, P and the empty seat, also fits every clue and again puts two people (P and R) between L and K, so the answer does not change.

Q 38 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles
A, B, C, D, E, F and G sit around a circular table facing centre. F sits to the immediate left of G. E sits to the immediate left of C. C sits second to the left of A. B sits third to the left of G. How many people sit between F and E when counted from the left of E?
(1) 4
(2) 3
(3) 2
(4) 1
= 4
~ Facing the centre, left is clockwise, and the conditions give only one arrangement, clockwise from G: G, F, A, B, C, E, D. Q 40 | rea | Analogy & Classification
Based on the English alphabetical order, three of the following four letter-clusters are alike in a certain way and thus form a group. Which letter-cluster DOES NOT belong to that group?
(Note: The odd man out is not based on the number of consonants/vowels or their position in the letter-cluster.)
(1) BFL
(2) JNT
(3) FJP
(4) NRY
= 4
~ In the group, each cluster moves +4 and then +6 letters: B + 4 = F, F + 6 = L; J + 4 = N, N + 6 = T; F + 4 = J, J + 6 = P. In NRY, N + 4 = R but R + 7 = Y, so NRY does not belong to the group.

Q 41 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles
Seven boxes, A, B, C, D, E, F and G, are kept one over the other but not necessarily in the same order. Only three boxes are kept below D. Only two boxes are kept between D and G. Only C is kept immediately above B. A is kept at some place below F and at some place above E. How many boxes are kept above B?
(1) 2
(2) 3
(3) 1
(4) 4
= 3
~ Only three boxes are below D, so D is 4th from the bottom, and with two boxes between D and G, G is at the top or at the bottom. Reading 'only C is kept immediately above B' as C being the only box above B, C is at the top and B second from the top, so G is at the bottom, and E below A below F fills the rest: from the top C, B, F, D, A, E, G. Hence only 1 box (C) is above B. If the clue is read merely as 'C is immediately above B', other stacks also fit (e.g. G at the top, C second and B third from the top, giving 2 boxes above B), so the question is loosely worded. (RRB key; some sources differ.)

Q 113 | geo | Environment & Ecology

(2) Zebra
(3) Lion
(4) Elephant
= 4
~ A keystone species has an effect on its ecosystem far greater than its numbers suggest.
