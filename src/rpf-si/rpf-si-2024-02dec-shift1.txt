Q 1 | qa | Percentage & Profit-Loss
The price (per litre) of petrol increases by 75%. By what percent should its consumption be reduced such that the expenditure on it increases by 33% only?
(1) 70%
(2) 82%
(3) 76%
(4) 75%
= 3
~ Expenditure = price × consumption. New price = 1.75 times and new expenditure = 1.33 times, so new consumption = 1.33/1.75 = 0.76 times the old. The new consumption is therefore 76% of the original, which means consumption must actually be reduced by 100 − 76 = 24%. No option shows 24%; RRB marked 76%, which is the new consumption as a percentage of the old one, not the reduction. (RRB key; some sources differ.)

Q 2 | qa | Ratio, Average & Mixtures
Which of the following ratio is smallest?
(1) 11 : 60
(2) 29 : 42
(3) 17 : 33
(4) 19 : 36
= 1
~ Write each ratio as a decimal: 11/60 ≈ 0.18, 29/42 ≈ 0.69, 17/33 ≈ 0.52 and 19/36 ≈ 0.53. In 11 : 60 the first term is less than one-fifth of the second, while in all the others it is more than half. So 11 : 60 is the smallest ratio.

Q 3 | qa | Percentage & Profit-Loss
The selling price of 16 books is equal to the cost price of 12 books. Find the loss or gain percentage.
(1) 25 % loss
(2) 100/3 % loss
(3) 100/3 % gain
(4) 25 % gain
= 1
~ Let the cost price of 1 book be ₹1. SP of 16 books = CP of 12 books = ₹12, so SP of 1 book = 12/16 = ₹0.75. Since SP is less than CP, there is a loss of 1 − 0.75 = 0.25, i.e. 25% loss.

Q 36 | rea | Analogy & Classification

Q 47 | rea | Blood Relations & Directions
Rayan starts from Point A and drives 13 km towards East. He then takes a left turn, drives 5 km, turns left and drives 15 km. He then takes a left turn and drives 11 km. He takes a final left turn, drives 2 km and stops at Point P. How far (shortest distance) and towards which direction should he drive in order to reach Point A again? (All turns are 90 degree turns only unless specified.)
(1) 6 km to the South
(2) 5 km to the South
(3) 6 km to the North
(4) 5 km to the North
= 3
~ Take A as the origin. He goes 13 km East, then left (North) 5 km, then left (West) 15 km, then left (South) 11 km, then left (East) 2 km. East–West: 13 − 15 + 2 = 0; North–South: 5 − 11 = −6, so P is 6 km directly South of A. He must drive 6 km to the North to reach A.

~ Gregor Johann Mendel, an Austrian monk, first described the laws of inheritance from his experiments on garden pea plants (1856–1863), published in 1866. His laws of dominance, segregation and independent assortment were rediscovered in 1900 by de Vries, Correns and Tschermak, and he is called the father of genetics. Mendeleev made the periodic table, while Darwin and Wallace proposed the theory of natural selection.

Q 100 | sci | Physics
Which of the following is the most appropriate definition of Newton's second law?
(1) The force needed to extend or compress a spring by some distance is proportional to that distance.
(2) Whenever one object exerts a force on a second object, the second object exerts an equal and opposite force on the first.
(3) The acceleration of an object depends on the mass of the object and the amount of force applied.
(4) An object at rest remains at rest, and an object in motion remains in motion at constant speed and in a straight line unless acted on by an unbalanced force.
= 3
~ Newton's second law states that the rate of change of momentum is proportional to the applied force, which gives F = ma: the acceleration of a body depends on the net force applied and on its mass (a = F/m). The spring statement is Hooke's law, the equal-and-opposite statement is Newton's third law, and the 'remains at rest' statement is Newton's first law (law of inertia).

= 3
