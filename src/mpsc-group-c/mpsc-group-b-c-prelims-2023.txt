# MPSC Maharashtra Non-Gazetted Group-B and Group-C Services Combined Preliminary Examination 2023 (Advt. No. 001/2023), General Ability Test, held 30 April 2023, booklet set A; answers: MPSC's final answer key of 7 June 2023

Q 1 | his | Maharashtra History & Social Reformers
During the establishment of Labour Organisation ‘Bombay Mill Hand Association’ which of the following personalities supported Narayan Meghaji Lokhande ?
a. Keshavrao Bagde, Keshavrao Bole
b. Raghu Bhikaji, Genu Babaji
c. Narayan Surkaji, Vitthalrao Korgaonkar
d. Krishnaji Arjun Keluskar, Ramchandra Shinde, Narayan Pawar
(1) a and b only
(2) b and d only
(3) a, b and c only
(4) b, c and d only
= 4
~ Lokhande founded the Bombay Mill Hands Association in 1890, regarded as India's first organised labour association. His co-workers in this effort included Raghu Bhikaji, Genu Babaji, Narayan Surkaji, Vitthalrao Korgaonkar, Krishnaji Arjun Keluskar, Ramchandra Shinde and Narayan Pawar, so b, c and d are correct; the names in a are not counted among his associates in this effort.

Q 2 | his | Modern India & Freedom Struggle
In 1857 uprising ______ Governor General said that, had Shinde and Nijam participated, then uprising would have been successful.
(1) Lord Dalhousie
(2) Lord Ripon
(3) Lord Mountbatten
(4) Lord Canning
= 4
~ Lord Canning was Governor-General during the 1857 uprising (and became the first Viceroy in 1858). He admitted that if Shinde (Scindia) of Gwalior and the Nizam had joined the revolt, the British would have had to leave India. Dalhousie had left in 1856, Ripon came in 1880 and Mountbatten in 1947.

Q 3 | his | Maharashtra History & Social Reformers
Which of the following statements are true about Prarthana Samaj ?
a. Prarthana Samaj was established by Dadoba Tarkhadkar, Dr. Atmaram Pandurang, Vaman Abaji Modak, Bhau Mahajan, etc. on 31 March 1867 at Bombay.
b. Prarthana Samaj started ‘Subodh Patrika’.
c. Through Prarthana Samaj N.M. Joshi established Social Service League to improve the conditions of labourers.
d. Prarthana Samaj established colleges at Mumbai, Solapur, Kolhapur.
(1) a and b only
(2) b and c only
(3) a, b and c only
(4) b, c and d only
= 3
~ Prarthana Samaj was founded in Bombay on 31 March 1867 by Dr. Atmaram Pandurang with Dadoba Pandurang Tarkhadkar, Vaman Abaji Modak, Bhau Mahajan and others (a). ‘Subodh Patrika’ was its organ (b). N.M. Joshi, inspired by the Samaj, founded the Social Service League in 1911 for workers' welfare (c). The Samaj ran night schools, libraries and orphanages, not colleges at Mumbai, Solapur and Kolhapur, so d is wrong.

Q 4 | his | Modern India & Freedom Struggle
Which of the following statements are true about ‘Hindu Mahasabha’ ?
a) On 9 April 1915 Maharaja of Kasim Bazar, Sir Mahindra Chand Nandi announced the formation of Hindu Mahasabha at Haridwar.
b) B.S. Munje was the President of the Hindu Mahasabha.
c) Dr. Munje, Dr. Hedgewar, Dr. Paranjape started a branch of Hindu Mahasabha in Nagpur on 11 November 1923.
d) 28, 29, 30 December 1938 the Grand Session of the Hindu Mahasabha was held at Pune.
(1) a, b and c only
(2) b, c and d only
(3) a, c and d only
(4) All of the above are correct
= 1
~ The Hindu Mahasabha was formally launched at the Haridwar Kumbh in April 1915 under Maharaja Manindra Chandra Nandi of Kasimbazar (a); B.S. Moonje later served as its President (b); Moonje, Hedgewar and Paranjape opened its Nagpur branch in 1923 (c). The December 1938 session (presided by V.D. Q 5 | his | Modern India & Freedom Struggle
During the uprising of 1857 which British Superior Military Officers played an important role ?
a. Neill, Havelock
b. Outram, Lawrence Brothers
c. Campbell, Sir Hume Rose
d. William Digby, Colin Clark
(1) a and b only
(2) b and c only
(3) a, b and c only
(4) b, c and d only
= 3

Q 6 | his | Maharashtra History & Social Reformers
In which area did the peasant revolts of 1875 take place ?
a. Bhimadi, Shirur, Parner, Nagar
b. Indapur, Haveli, Baglan, Satara
c. Tasgaon, Shirala, Jath, Malkapur
d. Shrigonda, Karjat, Solapur, Ratnagiri
(1) a and b only
(2) b and c only
(3) a, b and d only
(4) c and d only
= 3
~ The Deccan Riots of 1875 against moneylenders began at Supe in Bhimthadi taluka of Pune and spread through Pune, Ahmednagar, Satara and Solapur districts (Shirur, Parner, Nagar, Indapur, Haveli, Shrigonda, Karjat etc.), so the official key accepts a, b and d. The places in c (Tasgaon, Shirala, Jath) were not centres of the 1875 riots.

Q 8 | his | Modern India & Freedom Struggle
In 1919 the British Government started the Constitutional Reforms because
a. Increased dissatisfaction among Indians due to the revolutionary movement
b. Alliance between Congress and Muslim League
c. Alliance between Extremist and Moderates
d. The political situation created by the First World War
(1) a and b only
(2) b and c only
(3) b, c and d only

= 4
~ The Montagu–Chelmsford Reforms (Government of India Act 1919) came against rising revolutionary activity and unrest, the Congress–League Lucknow Pact of 1916, the reunion of Moderates and Extremists at the 1916 Lucknow Congress, and the political situation of the First World War (Montagu's August 1917 declaration). All four are correct.

Q 12 | geo | Maharashtra Geography
The local rock used to construct houses in rural area on the Deccan Plateau of Maharashtra is
(1) Laterite rock
(2) Marble rock
(3) Granite rock
(4) Basalt rock
= 4
~ The Deccan Plateau of Maharashtra is made of the Deccan Trap — layers of basalt formed from lava flows. This hard black basalt is easily available locally and is widely used for houses, temples and forts. Laterite (jambha) is used mainly in the Konkan, not on the plateau.

Q 17 | geo | Maharashtra Geography

(2) 5
(3) 4
(4) 3
= 1

Q 21 | eco | Indian Economy
For 2011-12 planning commission had defined poverty line with the help of following criteria.
(1) ₹ 816 and ₹ 1,000 per capita income per month in rural areas and urban areas respectively
(2) ₹ 47 and ₹ 32 per capita income per day in rural areas and urban areas respectively
(3) ₹ 49.63 and ₹ 56.64 per capita income per day in rural areas and urban areas respectively
(4) None of these
= 1
~ Using the Tendulkar methodology, the Planning Commission (2013) fixed the 2011-12 poverty line at a monthly per capita expenditure of ₹ 816 in rural and ₹ 1,000 in urban areas (about ₹ 27 and ₹ 33 a day). The ₹ 32 (rural) and ₹ 47 (urban) per day figures came later from the Rangarajan Committee (2014), and option 2 even reverses them.

Q 23 | eco | Money, Banking & Budget
Which are the instruments of fiscal policy ?
a. Public spending and public debt
b. Democracy and federal structure
c. Taxation and budget
d. Parliament and administration
(1) a only
(2) b and c only
(3) d only
(4) a and c only
= 4
~ Fiscal policy is the government's use of its revenue and expenditure to influence the economy; its instruments are taxation, public expenditure, public debt (borrowing) and the budget (deficit financing). Democracy, federal structure, Parliament and administration are political institutions, not fiscal instruments, so a and c are correct.

Q 29 | eco | Money, Banking & Budget
During 2021-22, M3 included
a. Post Office Saving Bank Deposit
b. Total Post Office Deposits
c. Time Deposits with Banks
d. Demand Deposits with Banks
(1) a, b and c
(2) c and d
(3) a, b and d
(4) all of the above
= 2

Q 32 | eco | Money, Banking & Budget
Which of the following statement/s is/are incorrect ?
a. UTGST, is collected by Central Government and Union Territory Governments.
b. IGST, is collected by Central Government and State Governments.
(1) Only a is incorrect
(2) Only b is incorrect
(3) Both a and b are incorrect

= 3

(2) India, Asia, Europe
(3) Flowers, Fragrant, White
(4) Cup, Saucer, Tea
= 3
~ The figure shows a middle circle partly overlapping two circles that are drawn apart from each other. Flowers fit it best: some flowers are fragrant and some flowers are white. History, Geography, Civics and Cup, Saucer, Tea are three separate classes, and India lies wholly inside Asia, so options 1, 2 and 4 do not fit.
