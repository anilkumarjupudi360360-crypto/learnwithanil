Q 1 | gs | Indian Polity
In which circumstances the Council of Ministers has not to resigned from office ?
If Lok Sabha
i. Passed No-confidence motion
ii. Doesn’t pass money bill
iii. Doesn’t pass impeachment resolution
iv. Doesn’t pass Government bill
v. Doesn’t pass amendment bill
(1) i, iii
(2) iii, v
(3) ii, iv
(4) ii, v
= 2
~ Under collective responsibility to the Lok Sabha (Art. 75(3)), the ministry must resign if a no-confidence motion is passed or if a money bill or an important government bill is defeated, as these show loss of confidence. Failure of an impeachment resolution or of a constitutional amendment bill is not a vote on the government's confidence, so no resignation follows (iii, v).

Q 4 | gs | Economy
Which is the essential characteristics of Direct Tax ?
a. Impact of Tax
b. Incidence of Tax
c. Shifting of Tax
d. All the above
(1) a and b
(2) a, b and c
(3) d
(4) b and c
= 1
~ In a direct tax (e.g. income tax) the impact (who pays first) and the incidence (who finally bears the burden) fall on the same person, because the tax cannot be passed on. Shifting of tax is a feature of indirect taxes like GST, so only a and b apply.

Q 118 | hin | Grammar
निम्नलिखित में से किस शब्द में हलन्त लगेगा ?
(1) जगन्नाथ
(2) अधीन
(3) विधिवत
(4) सूचीपत्र
= 3
~ Vidhivat (duly) is vidhi + the suffix "-vat", which ends in a halant: विधिवत्. Jagannāth, adheen and soochipatra end in full (vowelled) letters.

Q 119 | hin | Grammar
‘मैं इसका वह अर्थ नहीं लगाता जो कि आप लगाते हैं ।’ इस वाक्य में किस प्रकार का दोष है ?
(1) कथित पदत्व दोष
(2) न्यून पदत्व दोष
(3) अधिक पदत्व दोष
(4) अक्रमत्व दोष
= 3
~ "Jo ki" — "ki" is superfluous after "jo"; using an unnecessary extra word is adhik padatva dosh (redundancy). Correct: "Main iska vah arth nahin lagata jo aap lagate hain."

Q 120 | hin | Vocabulary & Usage
‘बुद्धि भ्रष्ट होना’ वाक्य को व्यक्त करने वाला मुहावरा कौन-सा है?
(1) अक्ल पर पत्थर पड़ना
(2) चक्कर में पड़ना
(3) धोखा खाना
(4) उल्लू बनाना

~ Akl par patthar padna (a stone falling on one's wits) = to lose one's good sense. Chakkar mein padna = to get confused; dhokha khana = to be deceived; ullu banana = to make a fool of someone.

Q 121 | hin | Vocabulary & Usage
‘न कारण होगा, न कार्य होगा’ को व्यक्त करने वाली कहावत है
(1) विष की गाँठ खोद फेंकना
(2) वह ठूँठ काट देना जिस पर उल्लू बैठे
(3) न रहेगा बॉस, न बजेगी बॉसुरी
(4) न तीन बुलाओ, न तेरह आयें
= 3
~ Na rahega baans, na bajegi baansuri (no bamboo, no flute) = remove the cause and the effect cannot happen. The booklet misprints baans/baansuri as "बॉस/बॉसुरी".

Q 123 | hin | Vocabulary & Usage
निम्नलिखित में शुद्ध शब्द कौन-सा है ?
(1) अनेकों
(2) अलक्ष
(3) अधीन
(4) पौरुषत्व
= 3
~ Adheen (dependent) is correct. Anek already means many, so "anekon" is wrong; "alaksh" should be alakshya; "paurushatva" doubles the abstract suffix — correct is paurush or purushatva.

Q 124 | hin | Grammar
निम्नलिखित में कौन-सा शब्द संज्ञा, पुल्लिंग रूप है ?
(1) किसलय
(2) जातुज
(3) नीति
(4) उज्ज्वलन
= 1
~ Kisalay (a tender new leaf) is a masculine noun ("naya kisalay nikla"). Neeti (policy) is a feminine noun and jātuj (made of lac) is an adjective. Ujjvalan (burning, shining) is a rarely used Sanskrit noun, so D is arguable, but the key takes the common masculine noun kisalay.

Q 125 | hin | Vocabulary & Usage
‘मयूर’ का तद्भव रूप है
(1) मऊर
(2) मौर
(3) मउर
(4) मोर
= 4
~ Mayoor (peacock) is the tatsam (Sanskrit) form; its tadbhav (evolved everyday) form is mor. Maur (a bridegroom's crown) comes from mukut.

Q 126 | hin | Vocabulary & Usage
‘सरदार’ किस भाषा का शब्द है ?

Q 127 | hin | Vocabulary & Usage
नीचे चार शब्द दिये गये हैं। इनमें से एक शब्द बेमेल हैं यानी शेष तीन शब्द पर्यायवाची हैं। आपको उस शब्द का चयन करना है जो बेमेल हैं ।

Q 128 | hin | Grammar
निम्नांकित में कौन-सा युग्म पुल्लिंग-स्त्रीलिंग का नहीं है ?
(1) गन्ना, पहिया
(2) आटा, रोटी
(3) दही, नदी
(4) पान, बाँस
= 1
~ Ganna (sugarcane) and pahiya (wheel) are both masculine — pahiya stays masculine although -iya words like gudiya and chidiya are feminine — so this pair is not masculine–feminine; āta (m)–roti (f) and dahi (m)–nadi (f) are. Strictly, pān and bāns (D) are also both masculine, but the key gives A.

Q 129 | hin | Grammar
नीचे दिए गए शब्दों में कौन-सा शब्द एकवचन है ?

(3) गायें
(4) गौएँ

Q 131 | eng | Grammar
I take a ladder and climb to the roof to be ______ the house.
(1) over
(2) in
(3) on
(4) within
= 3
~ "On" shows position touching the top surface of something; a person standing on the roof is on the house. "Over" means above without contact, and "in/within" mean inside.

Q 132 | eng | Grammar
The cat jumped from the chair ______ the table.
(1) above
(2) onto
(3) in
(4) on
= 2
~ "Onto" shows movement to a position on a surface, so it suits a verb of motion: jumped from the chair onto the table. "On" shows only position, not movement.

Q 133 | eng | Grammar
How ______ ______ ______ in a cooker ?
(1) was cake bake
(2) is cake bake
(3) cake is baked
(4) is cake baked
= 4
~ A simple-present passive question follows: question word + is/are + subject + past participle → "How is cake baked in a cooker?" "Bake" is not a past participle, and "cake is baked" lacks question word order.

Q 134 | eng | Vocabulary
It is my ______ to vote.
(1) rite
(2) right
(3) write
(4) writ
= 2
~ "Right" = a legal or moral entitlement; voting is a right. The others sound similar but differ in meaning: rite (ceremony), write (to put words on paper), writ (a court order).

Q 135 | eng | Vocabulary
I am ______ for all that you have done for me.
(1) gratefull
(2) greatfull
(3) grateful
(4) greatful
= 3
~ The correct spelling is "grateful" (thankful): the suffix -ful has a single "l", and the word has no "great" in it (it comes from Latin gratus, pleasing).

Q 136 | eng | Grammar
Select the indirect speech for
Meeta asked me, “Are you fine ?”
(1) Meeta asked me that I was fine.
(2) Meeta told me whether I was fine.
(3) Meeta asked me if I was fine.
(4) Meeta asked me if I had fine.
= 3
~ A yes/no question is reported with "asked + if/whether"; the question becomes a statement, "you" becomes "I" and "are" shifts back to "was": Meeta asked me if I was fine.

Q 137 | eng | Vocabulary
______ assets cases are investigated by the CBI.
(1) Disproportionate
(2) Improportionate
(3) Misproportionate
(4) Unproportionate
= 1
~ "Disproportionate assets" (DA) is the legal term for assets of a public servant beyond his known sources of income — an offence under the Prevention of Corruption Act that the CBI investigates. The other words are not standard English.

Q 140 | eng | Vocabulary

Q 147 | eng | Grammar
She ______ that she would come back the next day.
(1) said
(2) asked
(3) enquired

= 1

Q 148 | eng | Grammar
The ______ ______ ______ monk gave me the advice.
(1) good old Spanish
(2) old good Spanish
(3) Spanish old good
(4) good Spanish old
= 1
~ Adjective order: opinion → size → age → shape → colour → origin → material → purpose. So good (opinion) + old (age) + Spanish (origin).

Q 149 | eng | Grammar
This is ______ I lost my purse.
(1) where
(2) what
(3) that
(4) which
= 1
~ The blank refers to a place, so the relative adverb "where" is used: This is (the place) where I lost my purse.

Q 150 | eng | Grammar
Roti ______ rice for lunch – its your choice. Please let me know what you prefer.
(1) but
(2) and
(3) or
(4) yet
= 3
~ "Or" joins alternatives when only one is to be chosen; "it's your choice … what you prefer" shows a choice between roti and rice.
