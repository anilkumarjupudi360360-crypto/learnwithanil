Q 1 | gs | Indian Polity
State Administration is found in which pattern of Government ?
i. Unitary Parliamentary System
ii. Unitary Presidential System
iii. Federal Parliamentary System
iv. Federal Presidential System
(1) i ii
(2) iii iv
(3) i iii
(4) ii iv
= 2
~ A separate tier of State (provincial) administration exists only where power is divided between the Centre and the units, i.e. in a federal system. A federation can be parliamentary (India, Canada) or presidential (USA), so State administration is found in both iii and iv; a unitary system has one supreme central government.

If the population of Gram Panchayat area is more than one thousand, what shall be the number of wards in that Gram Panchayat ?
(1) 10
(2) 20
(3) 30
(4) 40
= 2

Q 16 | gs | Economy
What do you mean by “Slow-down Economy” in India at present ?
(1) Recession
(2) Depression
(3) Revival
(4) Boom
= 1
~ A slowdown is the downswing of the business cycle: the economy still grows, but its growth rate keeps falling, as in 2019-20 when India's GDP growth dropped to about 4%. Of the options, this contraction phase is closest to recession (the Hindi paper calls it 'सुस्ती'); depression is a deep, prolonged collapse, while revival and boom are upswing phases.

Q 17 | gs | Economy
Which is the formula of Per Capita Income ?
(1) Gross National Income / Total Population
(2) Net National Income / Population
(3) Gross Domestic Product / Population
(4) Net Domestic Product / Population
= 2
~ Per capita income = National income ÷ population. National income is the Net National Income (NNP at factor cost), so per capita income = Net National Income / Population. Ratios based on GDP or NDP measure output per head within the country, not the income of nationals.

Q 19 | gs | Science & Technology
Microwave oven consumes less power due to
(1) Smallest frequency of radiation
(2) Shorter wavelength of radiation
(3) Large wavelength and large frequency of radiation
(4) Small wavelength and small frequency of radiation
= 2
~ An oven's microwaves (about 2.45 GHz, wavelength about 12 cm) have a short wavelength compared with radio waves, so they can be confined in the small metal cavity and are absorbed directly by water molecules in the food. Heat is produced inside the food itself, with little energy wasted on air or vessel, so less power is used. Wavelength and frequency are inversely related, so C and D are impossible.

Q 20 | gs | Science & Technology

Q 32 | gs | Indian Polity
What is correct ?
If a bill passed by State Legislature and sent to the Governor for assent, what Governor will do ?
i. He can sign on the bill.
ii. If he doesn’t sign on the bill, he can return the bill to the State Legislature with some suggestions.
iii. If the State Legislature again pass the bill, the Governor have to sign on the bill.
iv. If Governor doesn’t agree with the provisions of the bill, he can refer the bill to the President.
(1) (i)
(2) (i), (ii)
(3) (i), (ii), (iii)

Q 41 | gs | Science & Technology
Vitamin(s) responsible for the coagulation of blood is/are
(1) Vitamin A and D
(2) Vitamin K and C
(3) Vitamin K only
(4) Vitamin C only
= 3
~ Vitamin K is needed by the liver to make prothrombin and other clotting factors (II, VII, IX, X); its deficiency delays clotting and causes bleeding. Vitamin A is for vision, D for calcium absorption and C for collagen formation (its lack causes scurvy).

Q 42 | gs | Indian History & Culture

Q 51 | comp | Computer Fundamentals
Which one of the following is not a non-impact printer ?
(1) Daisy wheel
(2) Inkjet
(3) Laser
(4) Thermal
= 1
~ A daisy-wheel printer is an impact printer: a petal-shaped wheel carrying the characters strikes an inked ribbon against the paper. Inkjet (sprays ink), laser (toner fused by heat) and thermal (heat-sensitive paper) printers print without striking the paper, so they are non-impact printers.
