Q 1 | aph | Ancient Andhra
Which of the following centres was recognised by archaeologists as a microlithic implement centre?
= Giddaluru
~ Giddalur in Prakasam district is a well-known prehistoric site with microlithic and earlier stone tools.

Q 2 | aph | Ancient Andhra
The Buddhist work which refers to 'Andhra Patha' is the
= Bhimasena Jataka
~ The Bhimasena Jataka mentions Andhapatha (the Andhra route or region), one of the early literary references to Andhra.

Q 3 | aph | Ancient Andhra
According to inscriptional evidence, the ruler of Bhattiprolu in the 3rd century BC was
= Kubhiraka
~ The Bhattiprolu stupa casket inscriptions name Raja Kubhiraka, son of Khubha. The inscriptions also give an early variant of the Brahmi script.

Q 4 | aph | Ancient Andhra
The Satavahana queen who acted as regent during the minority of her son was
= Devi Naganika
~ Naganika, queen of Satakarni I, ruled as regent for her minor son. Her Naneghat inscription records Vedic sacrifices performed by the family.

Q 5 | aph | Ancient Andhra
Generally, the rate of interest on deposits in guilds during the Satavahana period was
= 12%
~ The Nasik inscriptions record endowments deposited with weavers' guilds at interest of about 1% a month (12% a year), with the interest used for monks' needs.

Q 6 | aph | Ancient Andhra
The 'Kolika' guild of the Satavahana period belonged to
= Weavers
~ Kolikas were weavers. Inscriptions mention guilds (srenis) of weavers, potters, oil-pressers, corn dealers and others.

Q 7 | aph | Andhra Literature & Arts

= Aradalam
~ The official key gives Aradalam as the base cream used by dancers of the Satavahana age.

Q 8 | aph | Ancient Andhra
Bhavaviveka was an acharya in the Buddhist vihara at
= Dhanyakataka
~ Bhavaviveka (Bhavya), a Madhyamaka philosopher, taught at Dhanyakataka (Dharanikota, near Amaravati).

Q 9 | aph | Andhra Literature & Arts
The Jain philosophical work 'Samayasara' was composed by
= Kundakundacharya
~ Acharya Kundakunda, linked with Konakondla in Anantapur district, wrote Samayasara, Pravachanasara and Niyamasara.

Q 10 | aph | Ancient Andhra

= Rudrapurushadatta
~ Rudrapurushadatta, son of Ehuvala Chantamula, was the last known Ikshvaku king of Vijayapuri, after whom the Pallavas took the region.

Q 11 | aph | Ancient Andhra

= N. Venkataramanayya
~ The historian N. Venkataramanayya identified the deity Sriparvata Swami, worshipped by the Vishnukundins, with the Buddha.

~ The upasika Bodhisiri built a chaitya and monasteries at Nagarjunakonda, as an inscription there records.
