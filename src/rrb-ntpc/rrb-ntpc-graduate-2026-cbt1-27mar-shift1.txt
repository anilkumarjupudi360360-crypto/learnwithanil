Q 9 | qa | Number System & Simplification
Three positive integers, A, B, and C, satisfy the following conditions: HCF(A,B) = 12, HCF(B,C) = 18, HCF(A,C) = 6 and LCM(A,B,C) = 1080. If A = 12x, B = 36y, and C = 18z, where x, y, and z are pairwise coprime positive integers, then the minimum possible value of A + B + C is:
(1) 222
(2) 216
(3) 224
(4) 220
= 1

Statements:
(I) V is shorter than N but taller than T. M is taller than T.
