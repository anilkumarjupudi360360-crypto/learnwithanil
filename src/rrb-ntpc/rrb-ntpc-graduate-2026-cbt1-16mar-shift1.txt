Q 1 | qa | Mensuration
Find the area of a quadrilateral ABCD whose area is twice the area of a triangle PQR. The sides of triangle PQR are in the ratio 4:5:6 and the perimeter of the triangle is 90 cm (use √7 = 2.6).
(1) 656 cm²
(2) 702 cm²
(3) 763 cm²
(4) 698 cm²
= 2
~ The sides are 4k, 5k and 6k with 15k = 90, so k = 6 and the sides are 24, 30 and 36 cm. Semi-perimeter s = 45. By Heron's formula, area = √(45 × 21 × 15 × 9) = √(15² × 9² × 7) = 135√7 = 135 × 2.6 = 351 cm². ABCD has twice this area: 2 × 351 = 702 cm².

Q 4 | sci | Physics
Working of hydraulic lift and hydraulic brakes is based on:
(1) Faraday's law
(2) Gay-Lussac’s law
(3) Pascal’s law
(4) Coulomb's law
= 3
~ Pascal's law: pressure applied to an enclosed liquid is transmitted equally in all directions. A small force on a small piston creates pressure that acts on a larger piston and produces a larger force, which is how hydraulic lifts and brakes work. Faraday's law is about electromagnetic induction, Gay-Lussac's law about gas pressure and temperature, and Coulomb's law about electric charges.

Q 5 | rea | Arrangement & Puzzles
P, Q, R, S, T, U and V sit around a circular table facing the centre. Only one person sits between U and P when counted from the right of U. Only one person sits between P and R. Only one person sits between U and Q. Only two people sit between V and R. Only one person sits between T and Q. How many people sit between T and V when counted from the left of T?
(1) 3
(2) 2
(3) 4
(4) 1
= 4
~ Going to the right from U: P is second (one person between). R is two seats beyond P (the other seat two away from P is U's). Q is two seats from U on the other side, i.e. second to U's left. V must have two people between it and R, which leaves the seat right after U. T is two seats from Q, which leaves the seat between P and R, and S takes the last seat. Order to the right from U: U, V, P, T, R, Q, S. Counting to the left of T we meet P and then V, so only one person (P) sits between them.

Q 20 | qa | Data Interpretation & Statistics

(1) 32.03
(2) 36.77
(3) 30.77
(4) 28.47

Q 26 | qa | Time, Work, Speed & Distance

Q 42 | sci | Biology
Consider a hypothetical population of rabbits experiencing logistic growth. If the carrying capacity of their environment is 1000 individuals and the current population is 500, what would you expect to happen to the population?
(1) The population will decline rapidly due to resource depletion.
(2) The population will continue to grow exponentially without any constraints.
