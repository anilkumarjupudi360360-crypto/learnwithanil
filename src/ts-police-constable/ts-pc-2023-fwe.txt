Q 1 | eng | Grammar
Fill in the blank with an appropriate form of the verb given in brackets to make it a meaningful sentence.
She ______ (hand) me a picture of sloppily drawn birthday cake last year.
(1) Have handed
(2) Had handed
(3) Handing
(4) Handed
= 4
~ The time marker "last year" refers to a completed action at a definite past time, so the simple past "handed" is needed. The present perfect "have handed" cannot be used with a definite past time expression, and "had handed" would need a later past action as its reference point.

Q 2 | eng | Grammar
Fill in the blank with an appropriate form of the verb given in brackets to make it a meaningful sentence.
I ______ (pull) out my calculator before I added up all the money.
(1) Had pulled
(2) Have pulled
(3) Pulled
(4) Had been pulled
= 1
~ Of two past actions, the one that happened earlier takes the past perfect: pulling out the calculator came before adding up the money, so "had pulled" is correct. "Had been pulled" is passive and does not fit the active subject "I".

Q 4 | eng | Grammar
Identify the correct passive form of the verb from the options given below to fill in the blank in the following sentence.
A nation ______ (hold) together by shared beliefs and shared attitudes.
(1) Is held
(2) Were held
(3) have been held
(4) to be hold
= 1
~ The sentence states a general truth about a singular subject ("a nation"), so the simple present passive "is held" (is + past participle) is needed. "Were held" and "have been held" do not agree with the singular subject, and "to be hold" is not a finite verb and uses the wrong form of "hold".

Q 5 | eng | Grammar
Fill in the blank with an appropriate verb that agrees with the subject in the following sentence.
One of the most important secrets to success ______, instead of being against something, be for something.
(1) Is
(2) Are
(3) Has
(4) Does
= 1
~ In "One of the ...", the real subject is "one", which is singular, so it takes the singular linking verb "is" (the plural "secrets" is only the object of the preposition "of"). "Is" also links the subject to the complement "be for something"; "has" or "does" make no sense here.

Q 7 | eng | Grammar
Identify the grammatically incorrect sentence in passive voice from the options given below.
(1) These windows in my room really need to be cleaned soon.
(2) The President is hoping to be elected for a second term.
(3) Sheela and Swathi might have been invited to the party.
(4) Raveendra has asked to make a presentation at the meeting by his team leader.
= 4
~ Since the agent is "by his team leader", the sentence must be passive: "Raveendra has been asked to make a presentation at the meeting by his team leader." "Has asked" is an active form, so sentence (4) is wrong, while the other three use correct passive forms.

Q 8 | eng | Grammar
Fill in the blank with correct preposition in the following sentence.
Great innovators believe that logic and imagination shake hands ______ each other.

(2) To

Q 10 | eng | Grammar
Identify the correct form of the following sentence to be converted to direct speech.
'They told us that they would start the admission process the following day.'
(1) "We will start the admission process tomorrow," they told us.
(2) We told them, "They will start the admission process tomorrow."
(3) "We would start the admission process the next day," they told us.
(4) We told them, "We had to start the admission process tomorrow."
= 1
~ The speakers are "they" and the listeners "us", so the reporting part is "they told us"; inside the quotation the speakers refer to themselves as "we", "would" goes back to "will" and "the following day" goes back to "tomorrow". Option (3) keeps the indirect-speech forms "would" and "the next day", and options (2) and (4) reverse who is speaking.

Q 15 | eng | Grammar

Unless you ______ (read) today, you cannot be a perfect person tomorrow.
(1) Are reading
(2) Read
(3) Had read
(4) Have read
= 2

Q 18 | eng | Grammar
Fill in the blank with correct preposition in the following sentence.
Steve Jobs said, "Innovation distinguishes ______ a leader and a follower."
(1) Between
(2) From
(3) On
(4) About
= 1

Q 28 | ari | Percentage, Profit & Loss
A Shopkeeper spent 20% of his profit in a particular month towards wages of his workers. From the remaining profit he spent 10% towards his shop rent and 40% towards his family expenses. He then saved the balance amount of Rs.50,000 in a bank. Then the total amount he spent towards wages and rent is (in Rupees) :
(1) 25,000
(2) 35,000
(3) 40,000
(4) 75,000
= 2

Q 30 | ari | Time, Speed & Distance
The distance between two stations A and B is 480 km. A car started from A. But its speed is reduced by 20 kmph than it's usual speed due to road traffic and the usual time of reaching the destination B is increased by 2 hours. Then the usual duration of the total journey is (in hours) :
(1) 6
(2) 5 1/2
(3) 8
(4) 7 1/2
= 1
~ If the usual speed is v km/h, then 480/(v − 20) − 480/v = 2, which simplifies to v(v − 20) = 4800, so v = 80 km/h. The usual time = 480/80 = 6 hours (at 60 km/h it takes 8 hours, 2 hours more).

Q 31 | rea | Calendar & Clocks
The day of the week on 5th September 1887 was :
(1) Sunday
(2) Monday
(3) Tuesday
(4) Saturday
= 2
~ 1600 years give 0 odd days and the next 286 years (69 leap + 217 ordinary) give 69 × 2 + 217 = 355 ≡ 5 odd days. From 1 January to 5 September 1887 (not a leap year) there are 248 days ≡ 3 odd days, so the total is 5 + 3 = 8 ≡ 1 odd day, which is Monday.

Q 41 | ari | Time & Work
It is given that, if two pipes of radii r1 and r2 can fill an empty tank in t1 minutes and t2 minutes respectively and the speed of the flow is same in both the pipes then t1r1² = t2r2². There are three taps of diameters 1 cm, 1 1/2 cm, and 2 cm respectively. The speed of the water flow through them is same. The widest tap can fill an empty tank in 45 minutes. If all the taps are opened simultaneously, then in how many hours the empty tank will be filled?
(1) 12/29
(2) 20/29
(3) 25
(4) 19 1/4
= 1
~ Since t × r² is constant, the 2 cm tap takes 45 min, the 1.5 cm tap 45 × (2/1.5)² = 80 min and the 1 cm tap 45 × 2² = 180 min. Together they fill 1/45 + 1/80 + 1/180 = (16 + 9 + 4)/720 = 29/720 of the tank per minute, so the time is 720/29 minutes = 12/29 hour.

~ 'good' = ba (statements 1 and 2), 'some' = ja (1 and 3) and 'men' = sa (2 and 3); so 'people' = na, 'many' = ya and 'bad' = ra. 'many bad people' is therefore ya, ra, na, i.e. option 4 (na ra ya); options with sa wrongly include 'men'.

Q 200 | rea | Series & Analogies
