PDFs

Previous papers are hard to practise

APPSC releases question papers and final keys as separate PDF files. Practising them topic by topic, and keeping track of mistakes, is slow manual work.

APPSC sets its papers in English and Telugu, so practice material has to work in both. Coaching is costly and mostly in big cities, which leaves many aspirants with scattered free material.
