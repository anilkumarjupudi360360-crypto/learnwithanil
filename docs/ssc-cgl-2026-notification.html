SSC CGL 2026: Tier 1 is over. Answer key, result and Tier 2

The SSC CGL 2026 Tier-I computer-based exam ran from 30 September to 30 October 2026. Next come the Tier-I answer key with objections, then the result, then Tier-II, which is tentatively in December 2026. Below: the status as of 4 November 2026, the vacancies, the pattern and the CGL previous papers.

Who could apply

  • A bachelor's degree; JSO needs 60% in Class 12 Maths or Statistics in the degree, and SI (Statistics) needs Statistics as a subject.
  • Age on 1 August 2026 by post: 18–27, 18–30, 20–30 or 18–32 years; relaxation OBC 3, SC/ST 5, PwBD 10 years.
  • Fee ₹100; women, SC, ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen paid nothing.

Quick answers (as of 4 November 2026)

Is the SSC CGL 2026 Tier 1 answer key out?

Not as of 4 November 2026 in the reports we found. SSC has not announced a date; the dates going around online are unofficial. It will be on ssc.gov.in with a short objection window.

Is the SSC CGL 2026 Tier 1 result out?

No. The result comes after the answer key and objections.

When is SSC CGL Tier 2 2026?

SSC has not given an exact date. It is tentatively in December 2026.

How many vacancies are there in SSC CGL 2026?

About 10,731 (tentative), revised from 12,256 at the notification.

Is there negative marking in SSC CGL?

Yes. Tier-I: 0.5 mark per wrong answer. Tier-II Paper I: 1 mark per wrong answer (each question carries 3).

Do Tier-I marks count in the final merit?

No. Tier-I only qualifies you for Tier-II; the final merit is on Tier-II.

What is the SSC CGL age limit?

It depends on the post: 18–27, 18–30, 20–30 or 18–32 years on 1 August 2026.

When will the SSC CGL 2027 notification come?

Not announced. It will appear in SSC's 2027-28 exam calendar, which is not out yet.
