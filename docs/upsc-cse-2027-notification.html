UPSC CSE 2027: Prelims on Sunday, 23 May 2027

UPSC's Calendar of Examinations 2027 schedules the Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination 2027 for Sunday, 23 May 2027. The notification is due on 13 January 2027 with applications until 2 February 2027, and the Mains begin on 20 August 2027. Vacancies come with the notification. Below: the dates, eligibility and attempts, the pattern, and the Prelims and Mains previous papers.

Who can apply (CSE 2026 rules; the 2027 notification will confirm)

  • A bachelor's degree from a recognised university. Final-year students may apply for the Prelims but must pass before the Mains application.
  • Age 21 to 32 years on 1 August of the exam year, with 5 more years for SC/ST, 3 for OBC (non-creamy layer) and 10 for persons with benchmark disabilities.
  • Attempts: 6 for General/EWS, 9 for OBC and PwBD, no limit for SC/ST up to the upper age limit.
  • Fee ₹100; women, SC, ST and PwBD candidates pay nothing.

Quick answers (as of 10 November 2026)

Is the UPSC CSE 2027 notification out?

No, not as of 10 November 2026. UPSC's Calendar 2027 schedules it for 13 January 2027. In 2026 the notification came about three weeks after its calendar date, so a short delay is possible.

When is UPSC Prelims 2027?

Sunday, 23 May 2027, as per UPSC's Calendar 2027.

Has the UPSC 2027 exam date changed?

No change has been announced as of 4 November 2026. UPSC says calendar dates can change if circumstances require, so check upsc.gov.in.

When does UPSC Mains 2027 start?

20 August 2027, over 5 days.

What is the last date to apply for UPSC CSE 2027?

2 February 2027 as per the calendar; the notification will give the final date.

How many vacancies are there in UPSC CSE 2027?

Not announced yet; they come with the notification. CSE 2026 had about 933 (CSE 2025: 979).

Is the UPSC Mains 2026 result out?

Yes. UPSC declared the CSE Mains 2026 result on 5 November 2026: 2,526 candidates qualified for the personality test (interview). UPSC will announce the interview schedule and e-summon letters on upsc.gov.in.

How many attempts are allowed in UPSC CSE?

6 for General and EWS, 9 for OBC and PwBD, and no limit for SC/ST up to the upper age limit (37).

Is there negative marking in CSAT?

Yes. One-third of the question's marks is deducted for a wrong answer in both GS Paper I and CSAT. CSAT is only qualifying (33%), but wrong answers can still make you fail it.
