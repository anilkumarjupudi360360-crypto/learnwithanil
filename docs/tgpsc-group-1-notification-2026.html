TGPSC Group-I notification 2026: latest status

As of 16 November 2026, the Telangana Public Service Commission (TGPSC) has not issued a new Group-I notification. The last recruitment, under Notification No. 02/2024 for 563 posts, is complete: 562 candidates were selected, and the courts upheld the result in 2026. Below: that timeline, the Prelims and Mains pattern, and the Group-I previous papers to start with while the next notification is awaited.

Quick answers (as of 16 November 2026)

Is there a TGPSC Group-I notification in 2026?

No. As of 16 November 2026 TGPSC has not issued a new Group-I notification. The last one was Notification No. 02/2024 (563 posts), whose selection list came in September 2025.

How many posts were there in the last TGPSC Group-I?

563 posts under Notification No. 02/2024; 562 candidates were selected and one post was withheld because of a court case.

Is there an interview in TGPSC Group-I?

No. Selection is on the Mains written marks (six papers of 150 marks); the Prelims only screens candidates for the Mains.

What is the TGPSC Group-I Prelims pattern?

One objective paper of General Studies & Mental Ability: 150 questions, 150 marks, 2½ hours.
