UPSC EPFO APFC 2026: Recruitment Test on Sunday, 20 December

UPSC's EPFO notification for 2026 is for Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner only: 80 posts under Special Advertisement No. 52/2026 (August 2026). Applications closed on 14 September 2026 and the pen-and-paper Recruitment Test is on Sunday, 20 December 2026. There is no EO/AO or PA advertisement this year. Below: the dates, the pattern, eligibility and the EPFO previous papers.

UPSC EPFO APFC 2026: 80 पद (विशेष विज्ञापन 52/2026), भर्ती परीक्षा रविवार, 20 दिसंबर 2026। इस वर्ष EO/AO या PA का कोई विज्ञापन नहीं।

Who could apply

  • A bachelor's degree from a recognised university (a diploma in Company Law, Labour Laws or Public Administration is desirable).
  • Maximum age 35 for UR/EWS, 38 for OBC and 40 for SC/ST, with further relaxations in the advertisement.
  • Fee ₹25; women, SC, ST and PwBD candidates paid nothing.

EO/AO and PA

  • Enforcement Officer / Accounts Officer: no advertisement in 2026. The last EO/AO recruitment was Advt. 52/2025 (test 30 November 2025, common with APFC).
  • Personal Assistant: the latest is still Advt. 51/2024 (323 posts, test 7 July 2024). UPSC published its answer key in September 2026.
  • The EO/AO and APFC papers test the same syllabus, so every EPFO paper on this site is useful practice for 20 December.

Quick answers (as of 4 November 2026)

Is the UPSC EPFO notification 2026 out?

Yes, but only for APFC: 80 posts under Special Advertisement No. 52/2026 (August 2026). Applications closed on 14 September 2026.

When is the UPSC EPFO APFC exam 2026?

Sunday, 20 December 2026, pen and paper.

Is the EPFO APFC admit card out?

Not as of 4 November 2026. UPSC puts the e-admit card on upsc.gov.in a little before the test, usually 1–3 weeks before.

Is the EPFO EO/AO notification 2026 out?

No. UPSC has not advertised EO/AO posts in 2026. Coaching sites report that EPFO withdrew its request for 311 EO/AO posts in June 2026; we have not seen an official notice on it.

Can I still apply for UPSC APFC 2026?

No. Applications closed on 14 September 2026 after an extension from 11 September.

Is there negative marking in the UPSC EPFO exam?

Yes. One-third of the question's marks is deducted for each wrong answer.

What is the UPSC EPFO APFC exam pattern?

120 objective questions for 300 marks in 2 hours, then a 100-mark interview; the final merit weighs the test 75% and the interview 25%.

Is the UPSC EPFO 2025 answer key out?

Not as of 25 October 2026. UPSC usually publishes keys only after the whole recruitment is over (the PA 2024 key came in September 2026).
