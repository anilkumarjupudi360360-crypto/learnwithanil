Subject-wise: by syllabus part

The same 270 questions sorted by the Notification 07/2026 syllabus, so you can see which parts APPSC asks most.

Paper II: History, Culture & Geography of India and Andhra Pradesh

Ancient India · 6 questions
Stone Age and the Indus Valley (Harappan) Civilization; Vedic age, Buddhism and Jainism; Satavahanas, Sangam age and Guptas; foreign travellers
Medieval India · 12 questions
South Indian kingdoms (Pallavas, Chalukyas, Cholas and others); Bhakti and Sufi movements; regional languages and arts; Mughals, Shivaji and the Marathas; Delhi Sultanate; Arrival of Europeans and the East India Company
British Rule and India's Freedom Struggle · 12 questions
British expansion and land revenue systems; Congress and the national movement (Gandhi, Bose, Patel, Ambedkar); Independence, Partition and nation-building after 1947; 1857 Revolt and social reformers; Peasant, tribal and workersʼ movements; communalism
Ancient and Medieval Andhra · 12 questions
Early Andhra kingdoms (Satavahanas to Vishnukundins); Kakatiyas, Reddis, Gajapatis and Vijayanagara; Eastern Chalukyas; origins of Telugu people and language
Modern Andhra and the Freedom Movement · 10 questions
Social awakening: reformers, library movement, press, women; Andhra in the freedom struggle (Justice Party, Alluri); Andhra Movement: Potti Sreeramulu to Andhra Pradesh (1956); Company rule in Andhra (Munro, C.P. Brown, Mackenzie)
United Andhra Pradesh and the 2014 Split · 8 questions
Economic effects of the 2014 split; AP Reorganisation Act 2014: assets, employees, water; Loss of Hyderabad and the new capital (Amaravati); United AP 1956–2014 and the agitations
Land, Climate, Rivers and Natural Resources · 6 questions
Landforms of India and Andhra Pradesh; Soils, rivers and dams; Climate and climate change
Farming, Industry and People · 12 questions
Farming, livestock, forests and fisheries; Population, migration and urbanisation; Tribes, castes, religions, languages and settlements; Industries, mining, transport and ports
Wildlife, Environment and Disasters · 12 questions
Wildlife, forests and biodiversity; Pollution, sustainable development and EIA; Cyclones, floods, droughts and disaster management

Paper III: Polity, Constitution, Governance, Law & Ethics

The Constitution and our rights · 6 questions
How the Constitution was made and its main features; Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles and how rights are protected; Emergency powers and changing (amending) the Constitution; Jobs and duties of the Union and State governments
Centre and States: sharing power and money · 9 questions
Federalism: a strong Centre, Centre–State friction and cooperation; The Governor's role and powers; Who makes which laws: Union, State and Concurrent Lists; Sharing money: Finance Commission and fiscal federalism
Panchayats and municipalities · 4 questions
73rd and 74th Amendments: what panchayats and municipalities get; Women and weaker sections in local bodies
Parliament and State Legislatures · 6 questions
State Legislatures, Legislative Councils and privileges; Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha: make-up and powers; How a Bill becomes law; motions and House business
Courts and judicial review · 5 questions
Judicial review: courts checking laws and orders; Public Interest Litigation (PIL) and judicial activism; How courts are organised; amendments affecting the courts
Running the government: administration, policies and civil services · 18 questions
What public administration is and its history (Kautilya to British rule); Government development policies and why implementation falls short; Civil services: role, reforms and integrity; Statutory, regulatory and quasi-judicial bodies (commissions, tribunals)
Good governance and the citizen's voice · 12 questions
Civil society and NGOs; Right to Information (RTI) and Public Service Act; Social audit; Good governance, e-governance and Citizen's Charter
Ethics for public servants · 24 questions
Ethics basics; integrity and accountability in public and private life; Human values: family, education, gender equality, great leaders; Public-service ethics: codes, leadership, corruption, Lokpal, international ethics; Attitude, persuasion and emotional intelligence
Everyday laws: civil, criminal, labour, cyber and tax · 6 questions
Labour laws; Cyber laws and cyber crime; Civil and criminal law and courts (new criminal laws, Nirbhaya Act); Tax laws: income tax, corporate tax, GST

Paper IV: Economy & Development of India and Andhra Pradesh

India's big economic problems · 7 questions
Why farming and factories grow slowly (and what is being done); Growth record, reforms and India's growth outlook; Trade gap, balance of payments and the falling rupee; Rising prices: inflation and oil prices
Where India's money comes from · 8 questions
Taxes and other government income; Government borrowing: public debt at home and abroad; Monetary policy (RBI) and fiscal policy tools; Foreign investment (FDI and FII)
The Union Budget and Centre–State money · 8 questions
Finance Commission and Centre–State sharing of funds; Deficits and bringing them down (fiscal consolidation); GST and indirect taxes; Budget basics: structure, types and kinds of spending
Andhra Pradesh's money and the bifurcation · 10 questions
AP's income, budget, deficits and debt; AP's minerals, forests and water disputes with neighbours; Bifurcation Act promises, central help and Centre–AP disputes; Foreign-aided (externally aided) projects in AP; State Finance Commission and money for local bodies
Growth for everyone (inclusive growth) · 12 questions
Poverty and job programmes; Food security and ration shops (PDS); Banking for all (financial inclusion), SHGs and DWCRA; Health, education, women's empowerment and welfare schemes; What inclusive growth means and why people get left out
Farming and allied sectors (India and AP) · 16 questions
Farm markets, prices and farm policy (MSP, APMC, e-NAM); Fisheries, dairy, horticulture and the Rainbow revolution; AP agriculture: share in SGDP, irrigation, crops and schemes; Role of farming, productivity and farm credit; From the green revolution to sustainable and organic farming
Industry and business (India and AP) · 15 questions
Role of industry and India's industrial policies (1948 to 1991 and after); Public sector companies, disinvestment and privatisation; AP's industrial policy, incentives, corridors, MSMEs and exports; Manufacturing push: Make in India, startups, PLI, MSMEs, SEZs and corridors
Infrastructure: roads, power, digital and cities · 14 questions
Paying for infrastructure: PPP and pricing of public utilities; AP's infrastructure: transport, energy, ICT, airports and projects; Cities: smart cities, urban services, waste and disasters; Digital India, IT and e-governance; Transport and energy infrastructure in India (incl. solar)

Paper I (General Essay) topics are in the Answer Writing Tool Kit. Paper V (Science, Technology & Environmental Issues) is not on the site yet.
