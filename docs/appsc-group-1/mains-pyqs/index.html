Paper II

History, Culture & Geography of India and Andhra Pradesh

  • Ancient India6 questionsStone Age and the Indus Valley (Harappan) Civilization; Vedic age, Buddhism and Jainism; Satavahanas, Sangam age and Guptas; foreign travellers
  • Medieval India12 questionsSouth Indian kingdoms (Pallavas, Chalukyas, Cholas and others); Bhakti and Sufi movements; regional languages and arts; Mughals, Shivaji and the Marathas; Delhi Sultanate; Arrival of Europeans and the East India Company
  • British Rule and India's Freedom Struggle12 questionsBritish expansion and land revenue systems; Congress and the national movement (Gandhi, Bose, Patel, Ambedkar); Independence, Partition and nation-building after 1947; 1857 Revolt and social reformers; Peasant, tribal and workersʼ movements; communalism
  • Ancient and Medieval Andhra12 questionsEarly Andhra kingdoms (Satavahanas to Vishnukundins); Kakatiyas, Reddis, Gajapatis and Vijayanagara; Eastern Chalukyas; origins of Telugu people and language
  • Modern Andhra and the Freedom Movement10 questionsSocial awakening: reformers, library movement, press, women; Andhra in the freedom struggle (Justice Party, Alluri); Andhra Movement: Potti Sreeramulu to Andhra Pradesh (1956); Company rule in Andhra (Munro, C.P. Brown, Mackenzie)
  • United Andhra Pradesh and the 2014 Split8 questionsEconomic effects of the 2014 split; AP Reorganisation Act 2014: assets, employees, water; Loss of Hyderabad and the new capital (Amaravati); United AP 1956–2014 and the agitations
  • Land, Climate, Rivers and Natural Resources6 questionsLandforms of India and Andhra Pradesh; Soils, rivers and dams; Climate and climate change
  • Farming, Industry and People12 questionsFarming, livestock, forests and fisheries; Population, migration and urbanisation; Tribes, castes, religions, languages and settlements; Industries, mining, transport and ports
  • Wildlife, Environment and Disasters12 questionsWildlife, forests and biodiversity; Pollution, sustainable development and EIA; Cyclones, floods, droughts and disaster management
Paper III

Polity, Constitution, Governance, Law & Ethics

  • The Constitution and our rights6 questionsHow the Constitution was made and its main features; Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles and how rights are protected; Emergency powers and changing (amending) the Constitution; Jobs and duties of the Union and State governments
  • Centre and States: sharing power and money9 questionsFederalism: a strong Centre, Centre–State friction and cooperation; The Governor's role and powers; Who makes which laws: Union, State and Concurrent Lists; Sharing money: Finance Commission and fiscal federalism
  • Panchayats and municipalities4 questions73rd and 74th Amendments: what panchayats and municipalities get; Women and weaker sections in local bodies
  • Parliament and State Legislatures6 questionsState Legislatures, Legislative Councils and privileges; Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha: make-up and powers; How a Bill becomes law; motions and House business
  • Courts and judicial review5 questionsJudicial review: courts checking laws and orders; Public Interest Litigation (PIL) and judicial activism; How courts are organised; amendments affecting the courts
  • Running the government: administration, policies and civil services18 questionsWhat public administration is and its history (Kautilya to British rule); Government development policies and why implementation falls short; Civil services: role, reforms and integrity; Statutory, regulatory and quasi-judicial bodies (commissions, tribunals)
  • Good governance and the citizen's voice12 questionsCivil society and NGOs; Right to Information (RTI) and Public Service Act; Social audit; Good governance, e-governance and Citizen's Charter
  • Ethics for public servants24 questionsEthics basics; integrity and accountability in public and private life; Human values: family, education, gender equality, great leaders; Public-service ethics: codes, leadership, corruption, Lokpal, international ethics; Attitude, persuasion and emotional intelligence
  • Everyday laws: civil, criminal, labour, cyber and tax6 questionsLabour laws; Cyber laws and cyber crime; Civil and criminal law and courts (new criminal laws, Nirbhaya Act); Tax laws: income tax, corporate tax, GST
Paper IV

Economy & Development of India and Andhra Pradesh

  • India's big economic problems7 questionsWhy farming and factories grow slowly (and what is being done); Growth record, reforms and India's growth outlook; Trade gap, balance of payments and the falling rupee; Rising prices: inflation and oil prices
  • Where India's money comes from8 questionsTaxes and other government income; Government borrowing: public debt at home and abroad; Monetary policy (RBI) and fiscal policy tools; Foreign investment (FDI and FII)
  • The Union Budget and Centre–State money8 questionsFinance Commission and Centre–State sharing of funds; Deficits and bringing them down (fiscal consolidation); GST and indirect taxes; Budget basics: structure, types and kinds of spending
  • Andhra Pradesh's money and the bifurcation10 questionsAP's income, budget, deficits and debt; AP's minerals, forests and water disputes with neighbours; Bifurcation Act promises, central help and Centre–AP disputes; Foreign-aided (externally aided) projects in AP; State Finance Commission and money for local bodies
  • Growth for everyone (inclusive growth)12 questionsPoverty and job programmes; Food security and ration shops (PDS); Banking for all (financial inclusion), SHGs and DWCRA; Health, education, women's empowerment and welfare schemes; What inclusive growth means and why people get left out
  • Farming and allied sectors (India and AP)16 questionsFarm markets, prices and farm policy (MSP, APMC, e-NAM); Fisheries, dairy, horticulture and the Rainbow revolution; AP agriculture: share in SGDP, irrigation, crops and schemes; Role of farming, productivity and farm credit; From the green revolution to sustainable and organic farming
  • Industry and business (India and AP)15 questionsRole of industry and India's industrial policies (1948 to 1991 and after); Public sector companies, disinvestment and privatisation; AP's industrial policy, incentives, corridors, MSMEs and exports; Manufacturing push: Make in India, startups, PLI, MSMEs, SEZs and corridors
  • Infrastructure: roads, power, digital and cities14 questionsPaying for infrastructure: PPP and pricing of public utilities; AP's infrastructure: transport, energy, ICT, airports and projects; Cities: smart cities, urban services, waste and disasters; Digital India, IT and e-governance; Transport and energy infrastructure in India (incl. solar)
