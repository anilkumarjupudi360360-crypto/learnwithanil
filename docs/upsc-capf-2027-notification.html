UPSC CAPF AC 2027: exam on Sunday, 4 July 2027

UPSC's Calendar 2027 schedules the Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination 2027 for Sunday, 4 July 2027, earlier than in 2026 (19 July). The notification is due on 17 February 2027, with applications until 9 March 2027. Below: the dates, eligibility, the pattern, the 2026 vacancies and the CAPF previous papers.

Who can apply (2026 rules; the 2027 notification will confirm)

  • A bachelor's degree from a recognised university; final-year students may apply.
  • Age 20 to 25 years on 1 August of the exam year; 5 more years for SC/ST, 3 for OBC, and relaxations for ex-servicemen and central government servants.
  • Men and women can apply. Physical standards (2026): men 165 cm, chest 81 cm with 5 cm expansion, 50 kg; women 157 cm, 46 kg.
  • Fee ₹200; women, SC and ST candidates pay nothing.

Quick answers (as of 11 November 2026)

Is the UPSC CAPF 2027 notification out?

No, not as of 11 November 2026. It is scheduled for 17 February 2027 in UPSC's Calendar 2027.

When is the UPSC CAPF AC 2027 exam?

Sunday, 4 July 2027.

Has the CAPF 2027 exam date changed?

No change has been announced as of 11 November 2026. Note it is two weeks earlier than the 2026 exam (19 July 2026).

What is the last date to apply for CAPF 2027?

9 March 2027 as per the calendar.

How many vacancies are there in CAPF 2027?

Not announced yet. CAPF 2026 had 349 (BSF 108, CRPF 106, CISF 70, SSB 53, ITBP 12).

What is the age limit for UPSC CAPF AC?

20 to 25 years on 1 August of the exam year, plus 5 years for SC/ST and 3 for OBC.

Is there negative marking in UPSC CAPF?

Yes, one-third of the question's marks in Paper I (objective). Paper II is descriptive.

Can women apply for UPSC CAPF AC?

Yes. Women are eligible and pay no fee.
