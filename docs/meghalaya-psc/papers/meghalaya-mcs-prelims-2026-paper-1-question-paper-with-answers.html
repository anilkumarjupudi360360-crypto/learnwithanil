  1. Q1 · Geography & Environment · Environment & Ecology

    As per a global weather body, the glaciers on Mount Kilimanjaro are projected to be wiped out by 2040. In which country is Mount Kilimanjaro located?

    1. Ethiopia
    2. Tanzania
    3. South Africa
    4. Kenya
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Tanzania

    Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa's highest peak, is a dormant volcano in north-eastern Tanzania, close to the Kenya border. The World Meteorological Organization (WMO) warned in its State of the Climate in Africa report that Africa's last glaciers – on Kilimanjaro, Mount Kenya and the Rwenzori Mountains – could disappear by the 2040s.

  5. Q5 · Current Affairs · National & International

    Which is the nodal ministry for the Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM)?

    1. The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare
    2. The Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change
    3. The Ministry of Rural Development
    4. The Ministry of Jal Shakti
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) The Ministry of Jal Shakti

    The Jal Jeevan Mission (launched 15 August 2019) aims to give every rural household a functional household tap connection ('Har Ghar Jal'). It is run by the Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation of the Ministry of Jal Shakti, which was formed in 2019 by merging the water resources and drinking water ministries.

  6. Q6 · Science & Technology · General Science

    What kind of disease is Crohn’s disease that was recently seen in news?

    1. A respiratory disorder
    2. An autoimmune neurological disorder
    3. A chronic inflammatory bowel disease
    4. A heart disease
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) A chronic inflammatory bowel disease

    Crohn's disease is a chronic inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) that can affect any part of the digestive tract, most often the end of the small intestine (ileum), causing abdominal pain, diarrhoea and weight loss. Ulcerative colitis is the other main type of IBD.
