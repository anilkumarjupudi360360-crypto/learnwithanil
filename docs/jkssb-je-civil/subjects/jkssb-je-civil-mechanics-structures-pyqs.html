  2. JE Civil 2023 · 19 November · Q4 · Mechanics & Structural Analysis · Engineering Mechanics

    A stone is thrown vertically upwards with a vertical velocity of 98 m/sec. It returns to the ground in

    1. 5 sec
    2. 10 sec
    3. 20 sec
    4. 98 sec
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 20 sec

    Time to reach the top: t = u/g = 98/9.8 = 10 s; it takes the same time to fall back. Total time of flight = 2u/g = 2 × 98/9.8 = 20 s.

  3. JE Civil 2023 · 19 November · Q5 · Mechanics & Structural Analysis · Engineering Mechanics

    Two horizontal force F1=80 N and F2 = 70 N acting on a 20 kg block kept on rough horizontal surface, having coefficient of friction = 0.4, shown in following figure. The magnitude of the net acceleration of the block

    1. 0 m / sec²
    2. 10 m / sec²
    3. 20 m / sec²
    4. 48 m / sec²
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) 0 m / sec²

    The forces oppose each other, so the net applied force is 80 − 70 = 10 N. Limiting friction = μmg = 0.4 × 20 × 9.81 ≈ 78.5 N, which is far more than 10 N, so static friction (10 N) balances the push and the block stays at rest: a = 0.

  4. JE Civil 2023 · 19 November · Q6 · Mechanics & Structural Analysis · Engineering Mechanics

    Which of the following option is true about the moment of inertia of a section? i. The unit of moment of an area does not depends upon the units of its area. ii. The moment of inertia of an area may be obtained by the methods of integration. iii. The Routh's rule is used in finding out the moment of a body which is unsymmetrical about three mutually perpendicular axes. The true option is

    1. Only (i)
    2. Only (ii)
    3. Only (iii)
    4. None of the above is correct
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Only (ii)

    (ii) is true: I = ∫y² dA. (i) is false: moment of inertia of an area has units of length⁴ (mm⁴, m⁴), so it depends on the units used. (iii) is false: Routh's rule applies to bodies that are symmetrical about three mutually perpendicular axes.

  17. JE Civil 2023 · 19 November · Q7 · Mechanics & Structural Analysis · Strength of Materials

    The state of pure shear stress is produced by

    1. Tension in one direction and equal compression in perpendicular direction
    2. Equal tension in two directions at right angles
    3. Equal compression in two directions at right angles
    4. Same magnitude of compressive stress in all three direction
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Tension in one direction and equal compression in perpendicular direction

    With principal stresses σ1 = +σ and σ2 = −σ, the normal stress on planes at 45° is (σ1 + σ2)/2 = 0 and the shear stress there is (σ1 − σ2)/2 = σ: a state of pure shear. Equal tensions or equal compressions in two directions (B, C) give zero in-plane shear on every plane, and equal all-round compression (D) gives no shear at all.

  18. JE Civil 2023 · 19 November · Q8 · Mechanics & Structural Analysis · Strength of Materials

    At the time of designing a 40 m length chimney with one end is fixed, effective length is considered as

    1. 20 m
    2. 40 m
    3. 80 m
    4. 120 m
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 80 m

    A chimney behaves as a vertical cantilever, fixed at the base and free at the top. For one end fixed and the other free, effective length = 2L = 2 × 40 = 80 m.
