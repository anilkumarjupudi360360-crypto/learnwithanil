  1. Q1 · Surveying · Surveying

    Theory of probability can be applied to

    1. Cumulative errors only
    2. Accidental errors only
    3. Both accidental and cumulative errors
    4. None of the above is correct
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Accidental errors only

    Accidental (random) errors are small, equally likely to be positive or negative and follow the normal law of probability, so probable error and least-squares adjustment apply to them. Cumulative (systematic) errors follow a definite law and are removed by calculated corrections, not by probability.

  2. Q2 · Surveying · Surveying

    The principle of "working from whole to part" is used in surveying because

    1. Plotting and mapping becomes easy
    2. Survey work can be completed quickly
    3. Accumulation of errors is prevented
    4. Lower manpower requirement
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Accumulation of errors is prevented

    A framework of main control points is first fixed with high precision and the details are then filled in by less precise methods. Errors in the minor work stay localised within the framework instead of accumulating and magnifying, as they would when working from part to whole.

  3. Q3 · Surveying · Surveying

    The relation between the radius (R) and degree (D) of a curve is

    1. R = D/1719
    2. R = 1719/D
    3. R/D = 1719
    4. D/R = 1719
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) R = 1719/D

    Degree of curve D is the angle subtended at the centre by a 30 m arc. So 30 = R × D × π/180, giving R = 5400/(πD) = 1718.9/D ≈ 1719/D (R in metres, D in degrees). A sharper curve (larger D) has a smaller radius.

  4. Q4 · Mechanics & Structural Analysis · Engineering Mechanics

    A stone is thrown vertically upwards with a vertical velocity of 98 m/sec. It returns to the ground in

    1. 5 sec
    2. 10 sec
    3. 20 sec
    4. 98 sec
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 20 sec

    Time to reach the top: t = u/g = 98/9.8 = 10 s; it takes the same time to fall back. Total time of flight = 2u/g = 2 × 98/9.8 = 20 s.

  5. Q5 · Mechanics & Structural Analysis · Engineering Mechanics

    Two horizontal force F1=80 N and F2 = 70 N acting on a 20 kg block kept on rough horizontal surface, having coefficient of friction = 0.4, shown in following figure. The magnitude of the net acceleration of the block

    1. 0 m / sec²
    2. 10 m / sec²
    3. 20 m / sec²
    4. 48 m / sec²
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) 0 m / sec²

    The forces oppose each other, so the net applied force is 80 − 70 = 10 N. Limiting friction = μmg = 0.4 × 20 × 9.81 ≈ 78.5 N, which is far more than 10 N, so static friction (10 N) balances the push and the block stays at rest: a = 0.

  7. Q7 · Mechanics & Structural Analysis · Strength of Materials

    The state of pure shear stress is produced by

    1. Tension in one direction and equal compression in perpendicular direction
    2. Equal tension in two directions at right angles
    3. Equal compression in two directions at right angles
    4. Same magnitude of compressive stress in all three direction
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Tension in one direction and equal compression in perpendicular direction

    With principal stresses σ1 = +σ and σ2 = −σ, the normal stress on planes at 45° is (σ1 + σ2)/2 = 0 and the shear stress there is (σ1 − σ2)/2 = σ: a state of pure shear. Equal tensions or equal compressions in two directions (B, C) give zero in-plane shear on every plane, and equal all-round compression (D) gives no shear at all.

  9. Q9 · Building Materials & Construction · Building Materials

    Presence of 0.20% sugar by weight of cement in the mixing water is likely to

    1. Retard the setting of cement
    2. Reduce the early strength of cement
    3. Accelerate the setting of cement
    4. Decrease the work ability
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Retard the setting of cement

    Sugar is a powerful retarder. About 0.05% by mass of cement delays setting by roughly 4 hours, and at about 0.2% hydration is retarded so much that the final set may not occur for 72 hours or more (Neville). So 0.20% sugar in the mixing water retards the setting of cement.
