  1. 1 of 10 · JKSSB JE Civil 2025 · Q1 · Surveying

    Which of the following set of terms does not relate to operation of a theodolite?

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Gauging and sounding

    Transiting (plunging) the telescope, face left/face right observations and swinging the telescope right or left are all theodolite operations. Gauging (measuring stream discharge) and sounding (measuring water depth) belong to hydrographic surveying, not to theodolite work.

  2. 2 of 10 · JKSSB JE Civil 2023 · Q4 · Mechanics & Structural Analysis

    A stone is thrown vertically upwards with a vertical velocity of 98 m/sec. It returns to the ground in

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 20 sec

    Time to reach the top: t = u/g = 98/9.8 = 10 s; it takes the same time to fall back. Total time of flight = 2u/g = 2 × 98/9.8 = 20 s.

  3. 3 of 10 · JKSSB JE Civil 2025 · Q2 · Surveying

    If the whole circle bearing is 315°20′, its quadrantal bearing would be

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) N 44°40′ W

    A WCB between 270° and 360° lies in the NW quadrant, so QB = N (360° − WCB) W = N (360° − 315°20′) W = N 44°40′ W.

  4. 4 of 10 · JKSSB JE Civil 2023 · Q9 · Building Materials & Construction

    Presence of 0.20% sugar by weight of cement in the mixing water is likely to

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Retard the setting of cement

    Sugar is a powerful retarder. About 0.05% by mass of cement delays setting by roughly 4 hours, and at about 0.2% hydration is retarded so much that the final set may not occur for 72 hours or more (Neville). So 0.20% sugar in the mixing water retards the setting of cement.

  5. 5 of 10 · JKSSB JE Civil 2025 · Q3 · Surveying

    Error due to inclination of line of collimation in levelling across a river can be eliminated by

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Reciprocal levelling

    Across a river the level cannot be set up midway between the two points, so reciprocal levelling is used: readings are taken with the level near each bank in turn and the mean of the two apparent differences of level cancels the collimation error (and curvature and refraction).

  6. 6 of 10 · JKSSB JE Civil 2023 · Q5 · Mechanics & Structural Analysis

    Two horizontal force F1=80 N and F2 = 70 N acting on a 20 kg block kept on rough horizontal surface, having coefficient of friction = 0.4, shown in following figure. The magnitude of the net acceleration of the block

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) 0 m / sec²

    The forces oppose each other, so the net applied force is 80 − 70 = 10 N. Limiting friction = μmg = 0.4 × 20 × 9.81 ≈ 78.5 N, which is far more than 10 N, so static friction (10 N) balances the push and the block stays at rest: a = 0.

  7. 7 of 10 · JKSSB JE Civil 2025 · Q4 · Surveying

    The length of a survey line when measured with a chain of 20 m nominal length was found to be 841.5m. If the chain used is 0.1 m too long, the correct length of the measured line is

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) 845.7 m

    True length = (L′/L) × measured length = (20.1/20) × 841.5 = 845.7 m. A chain that is too long gives a measured length that is too short, so the correction is positive.

  8. 8 of 10 · JKSSB JE Civil 2023 · Q10 · Building Materials & Construction

    If water-cement ratio is 0.6 then, water content per standard bag of cement is

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 30 kg

    A standard bag of cement weighs 50 kg. Water = w/c ratio × mass of cement = 0.6 × 50 = 30 kg (about 30 litres).

  9. 9 of 10 · JKSSB JE Civil 2025 · Q5 · Surveying

    In any closed traverse, if the survey work is error free, then I. The algebraic sum of all the latitudes should be equal to zero II. The algebraic sum of all the departures should be equal to zero III. The sum of the northings should be equal to the sum of the southings Which of the above statements are correct?

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) I, II and III

    For an error-free closed traverse the closing error is zero, so ΣL = 0 and ΣD = 0. ΣL = 0 simply means the sum of northings (+ latitudes) equals the sum of southings (− latitudes), so all three statements hold.

  10. 10 of 10 · JKSSB JE Civil 2023 · Q6 · Mechanics & Structural Analysis

    Which of the following option is true about the moment of inertia of a section? i. The unit of moment of an area does not depends upon the units of its area. ii. The moment of inertia of an area may be obtained by the methods of integration. iii. The Routh's rule is used in finding out the moment of a body which is unsymmetrical about three mutually perpendicular axes. The true option is

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Only (ii)

    (ii) is true: I = ∫y² dA. (i) is false: moment of inertia of an area has units of length⁴ (mm⁴, m⁴), so it depends on the units used. (iii) is false: Routh's rule applies to bodies that are symmetrical about three mutually perpendicular axes.
