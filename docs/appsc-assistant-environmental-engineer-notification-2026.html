APPSC Assistant Environmental Engineer Notification 2026: 41 posts in the AP Pollution Control Board

APPSC Notification No. 08/2026 (6 October 2026) invited online applications for 41 posts (one of them carried forward) of Assistant Environmental Engineer in the A.P. Pollution Control Board, for engineering graduates. Applications ran from 6 to 27 October 2026; the computer-based exam date is still to be announced. Below: dates, eligibility, the exam pattern and syllabus, and the 2025 papers to start practising.

Who can apply

A B.E./B.Tech. degree in Civil, Mechanical, Chemical or Environmental Engineering from a recognised university in India, or a pass in Sections A and B of the AMIE (India) Civil examination. Fee: ₹250 application processing fee plus ₹80 examination fee; SC, ST, BC, PwBD and ex-service candidates, white-card families and unemployed youth of AP are exempt from the ₹80 examination fee.

One-third of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer. Selection is on the 300 marks of the written examination, after the qualifying Computer Proficiency Test. Minimum qualifying marks: 40% for OC, sportspersons, ex-servicemen and EWS, 35% for BCs, 30% for SC, ST and PwBD. In the 2025 exam Paper II carried 300 marks; it is now 150.

Paper II syllabus: Common Subject

  • Ecosystems: structure and function, food chains, food webs and ecological pyramids, energy flow, biogeochemical cycles, bioaccumulation and biomagnification, ecosystem services and carrying capacity.
  • Natural resources: water (surface and ground water, floods, droughts, dams), mineral, land, forest and energy resources, renewable and non-renewable energy.
  • Biodiversity and biotic resources: genetic, species and ecosystem diversity, values of biodiversity, India as a mega-diversity nation, hotspots, threats, in-situ and ex-situ conservation, the Biological Diversity Act.
  • Engineering materials: structure and properties, phase diagrams, heat treatment, stress–strain diagrams.
  • Fluid mechanics and hydraulics: Newtonian and non-Newtonian fluids, fluid properties, manometers, stream function and velocity potential, continuity, Bernoulli's and impulse-momentum equations, laminar and turbulent pipe flow, Reynolds number, Hagen–Poiseuille and Darcy–Weisbach equations, losses in pipes, dimensional analysis and similarity, boundary layer.
  • Process calculations and thermodynamics: conservation of mass and energy, tie components, recycle, bypass and purge, degrees of freedom, first and second laws, equations of state and properties of real systems.
  • Air, water, soil and noise pollution and control technologies: primary and secondary air pollutants, particulate, SOx and NOx control, ambient air quality standards; drinking-water standards, primary, secondary and tertiary wastewater treatment (grit chambers, sedimentation, trickling filters, oxidation ponds, activated sludge, septic tanks, sludge disposal); soil pollution; noise standards.
  • Solid and industrial waste management: municipal solid waste collection, segregation, transport and processing, e-waste, bioremediation.
  • Global environmental problems, policies and laws: climate change, ozone depletion, deforestation and desertification, the Earth Summit and the Kyoto, Montreal and Paris agreements, the Environment (Protection), Air, Water, Forest and Wild Life Acts, and the solid waste, bio-medical waste and hazardous waste rules.
  • Environmental impact assessment and sustainable development: EIA structure and baseline data, risk assessment, environmental management plans, sustainable development, green buildings, ecological footprint, life cycle assessment, low-carbon lifestyle.

Paper I syllabus: General Studies & Mental Ability

Current events (international, national, Andhra Pradesh); general science and technology; history of India with a focus on AP and the national movement; geography of India and AP; Indian polity and governance; Indian economy and planning; sustainable development and environment; disaster management; logical reasoning; data analysis.

Quick answers (as of 8 November 2026)

Can I still apply for APPSC Assistant Environmental Engineer?

The application window was 6 to 27 October 2026 (11:59 pm). We had not seen an extension notice for Notification 08/2026 as of 8 November 2026; check psc.ap.gov.in.

When is the APPSC Assistant Environmental Engineer exam?

Not announced yet. It will be a computer-based test (CBRT); APPSC will publish the date in a web note and the hall ticket.

How many Assistant Environmental Engineer posts are there?

41, including 1 carried forward, in the A.P. Pollution Control Board, under Notification 08/2026 of 6 October 2026.

Who can apply for APPSC Assistant Environmental Engineer?

B.E./B.Tech. graduates in Civil, Mechanical, Chemical or Environmental Engineering (or AMIE Civil, Sections A and B), aged 18 to 44 on 1 July 2026 (with the usual relaxations).

Is there negative marking in the Assistant Environmental Engineer exam?

Yes. One-third of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer.

What is the Assistant Environmental Engineer salary?

Pay scale ₹57,100 – ₹1,47,760 a month.
