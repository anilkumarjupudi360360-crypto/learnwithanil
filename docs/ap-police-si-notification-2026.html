AP Police SI Notification 2026: 378 Sub-Inspector posts

The State Level Police Recruitment Board, Andhra Pradesh notified 378 Sub-Inspector posts on 16 September 2026: 253 SCT SI (Civil), 116 SCT Reserve SI (AR) and 9 SI in the Special Protection Force. SLPRB will announce the online application dates and the Preliminary Written Test date in a press note. Below: posts, eligibility, the exam pattern and syllabus, and the previous papers to start practising.

Who can apply

A degree from a university recognised in India, as on 1 July 2026; SC and ST candidates who passed Intermediate and studied a degree are also eligible. Age 21 to 27 years as on 1 July 2026 (born between 2 July 1999 and 1 July 2005), relaxed by up to 5 years for SC, ST, BC and EWS candidates and for AP government employees. Fee ₹600 (₹300 for SC and ST candidates of AP; non-local candidates pay ₹600 as open category).

Physical standards (PMT) and physical test (PET)

Height at least 167.6 cm and chest 86.3 cm with 5 cm expansion for men; height 152.5 cm and weight 40 kg for women (lower heights for Aboriginal ST candidates of the agency areas). PET: 1,600 m run in 8 minutes (women 10 min 30 s), 100 m in 15 seconds (women 18 s), long jump 3.80 m (women 2.75 m). SI (Civil) candidates must pass the 1,600 m run and one of the other two; RSI (AR) and SPF candidates must pass all three, and their PET carries 100 marks.

Each paper must be qualified: 40% for OC (and EWS), 35% for BC, 30% for SC and ST. SI (Civil) selection is on Papers III and IV of the Final Written Examination; for RSI (AR) and SPF the PET marks are added. No marks are deducted for wrong answers.

Syllabus

  • Arithmetic: number system, simple and compound interest, ratio and proportion, average, percentage, profit and loss, time and work, work and wages, time and distance, clocks and calendars, partnership, mensuration.
  • Test of Reasoning / Mental Ability: verbal and non-verbal reasoning, analogies, similarities and differences, spatial visualisation and orientation, problem solving, analysis, judgement, decision making, visual memory.
  • General Studies: general science and contemporary developments in science, technology and the environment; current events of national and international importance; history of India and the Indian National Movement; geography of India; Indian polity and economy, including rural development, planning and economic reforms.

Quick answers (as of 10 November 2026)

Has the AP Police SI 2026 online application started?

Not yet, as far as we could find on 10 November 2026. SLPRB says it will announce the application dates in a press note on slprb.ap.gov.in.

When is the AP Police SI exam 2026?

The Preliminary Written Test date has not been announced yet.

How many SI posts are there in AP Police 2026?

378: 253 SCT SI (Civil), 116 SCT Reserve SI (AR) and 9 SI in the Special Protection Force. SI (Communications) and SI (PTO) have a separate notification.

What is the age limit for AP SI 2026?

21 to 27 years on 1 July 2026 (born between 2 July 1999 and 1 July 2005), relaxed by up to 5 years for SC, ST, BC and EWS candidates.

Is there negative marking in the AP SI prelims?

No. No marks are deducted for wrong answers in the AP Police SI written tests.

What are the qualifying marks for AP SI?

40% for OC (and EWS), 35% for BC and 30% for SC and ST, in each paper.
