TGPSC AMVI notification: none yet in 2026

TGPSC has not issued an Assistant Motor Vehicle Inspector notification in 2026: none of the 11 notifications on its 2026 list is for AMVI (checked on 4 November 2026). The last one was TSPSC Notification 31/2022 for 113 posts, whose exam was held in June 2023. Its rules are below, as the best guide to the next one, with the TG AMVI previous papers.

Who could apply (31/2022)

  • A degree in Mechanical or Automobile Engineering, or a 3-year Diploma in Automobile Engineering.
  • A valid heavy motor vehicle (transport) driving licence; women with only an LMV licence had 2 years after joining to get the heavy endorsement.
  • Physical standards: men 165 cm and chest 86.3 cm (+5 cm); women 157.5 cm; lower for SC/ST. Eyesight 6/6.
  • Age 21 to 39 years on 1 July 2022, plus 5 years for SC, ST, BC and EWS. Fee ₹200 + ₹120 exam fee (exam fee waived for the unemployed).

Quick answers (as of 4 November 2026)

Is the TGPSC AMVI notification 2026 out?

No. TGPSC's list of 2026 notifications (checked 4 November 2026) has no AMVI post.

When was the last TG AMVI notification?

TSPSC Notification 31/2022, dated 31 December 2022, for 113 posts.

When was the last TG AMVI exam held?

28 June 2023, computer-based. Certificate verification followed in June 2024.

Can a diploma holder apply for TG AMVI?

Yes. Under 31/2022 a 3-year Diploma in Automobile Engineering qualified, as did a Mechanical or Automobile Engineering degree.

Is a heavy licence needed for TG AMVI?

Yes, a valid heavy motor vehicle (transport) licence.

What is the TG AMVI salary?

₹45,960 – ₹1,24,150 under 31/2022.

Is there negative marking in TG AMVI?

The 31/2022 notification does not mention negative marking.
