  1. Q1 · History · Themes in World History

    From 1603, real power in Japan was held by the shoguns of which family, ruling from Edo (present-day Tokyo)?

    1. The Meiji
    2. The Qing
    3. The Ming
    4. The Tokugawa
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) The Tokugawa

    The emperor stayed in Kyoto with little power. The country was divided among about 250 domains, each under a lord called a daimyo.

  2. Q2 · History · Themes in World History

    In Tokugawa Japan, the "samurai" were:

    1. The warrior class, who served the shogun and the daimyo
    2. Rich merchants
    3. Buddhist priests
    4. Peasant farmers
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) The warrior class, who served the shogun and the daimyo

    The samurai were the ruling elite. By the nineteenth century, many of them had become administrators rather than fighting men.

  3. Q3 · History · Themes in World History

    In 1853, the American Commodore Matthew Perry arrived in Japan and demanded that Japan:

    1. Give up its emperor
    2. Open its ports for trade and diplomatic relations
    3. Become a US colony
    4. Adopt Christianity
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Open its ports for trade and diplomatic relations

    Japan signed a treaty with the USA in 1854. The opening of the country set off a political crisis that led to the end of the shogunate.

  4. Q4 · History · Themes in World History

    The Meiji Restoration of 1868:

    1. Restored the shogun to full power
    2. Removed the shogun and brought the emperor to power in Edo, renamed Tokyo
    3. Made Japan a colony
    4. Ended Japan's monarchy
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Removed the shogun and brought the emperor to power in Edo, renamed Tokyo

    The new emperor's reign was called Meiji, meaning "enlightened government". The new government set out to build a modern state.

  5. Q5 · History · Themes in World History

    The Meiji slogan "fukoku kyohei" meant:

    1. Asia for the Asians
    2. Expel the barbarians
    3. Equality for all
    4. Rich country, strong army
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Rich country, strong army

    The government wanted to develop the economy and build a strong army, so that Japan would not be colonised by the West.

  6. Q6 · History · Themes in World History

    Which of the following was a Meiji reform? 1. Compulsory schooling for all children from 1870s 2. A conscription law, under which every young man had to serve in the army 3. Abolition of all taxes Select the correct answer using the code given below:

    1. 1 only
    2. 1 and 2 only
    3. 2 and 3 only
    4. 1, 2 and 3
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) 1 and 2 only

    The Meiji state also introduced a new land tax and built railways. Textbooks taught loyalty to the emperor and the nation.

  7. Q7 · History · Themes in World History

    The large business houses that dominated the Japanese economy, such as Mitsubishi and Sumitomo, were called:

    1. Zaibatsu
    2. Daimyo
    3. Shogunates
    4. Communes
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Zaibatsu

    The zaibatsu were family-controlled business groups. They grew with state support during Japan's industrialisation.

  8. Q8 · History · Themes in World History

    Fukuzawa Yukichi, a leading Meiji intellectual, argued that Japan should:

    1. Stay closed to the world
    2. Follow China's example
    3. "Expel Asia" and learn from the West
    4. Give up industry
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) "Expel Asia" and learn from the West

    He founded what became Keio University. Others, like Ueki Emori, pressed for freedom and people's rights.

  9. Q9 · History · Themes in World History

    Japan colonised Taiwan in 1895 and Korea in 1910. Which wars before these showed Japan's new military strength?

    1. Victory over China in 1894-95 and over Russia in 1904-05
    2. Defeat by China
    3. Victory over Britain
    4. War with the USA in 1900
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Victory over China in 1894-95 and over Russia in 1904-05

    The victory over Russia was the first time an Asian power defeated a European power in modern times.

  10. Q10 · History · Themes in World History

    After its defeat in the Second World War, Japan was occupied by the USA (1945-52). Japan's new constitution of 1947 is known for:

    1. Restoring the shogun
    2. Abolishing the emperor completely
    3. A "no war" clause, under which Japan renounced war
    4. Making Japan a part of the USA
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) A "no war" clause, under which Japan renounced war

    Article 9 renounces war as a means of settling disputes. Women also got the right to vote, and the zaibatsu were broken up.
