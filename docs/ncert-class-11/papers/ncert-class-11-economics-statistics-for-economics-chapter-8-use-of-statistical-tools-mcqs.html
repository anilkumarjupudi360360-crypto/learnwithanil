  1. Q1 · Economics · Statistics for Economics

    What is the first step in making a statistical project?

    1. Drawing graphs
    2. Identifying a problem or an area of study
    3. Writing the bibliography
    4. Calculating the mean
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Identifying a problem or an area of study

    A project begins by identifying the problem or area to be studied. This decides the target group, the data to be collected and the tools to be used.

  2. Q2 · Economics · Statistics for Economics

    Which is the correct sequence of steps in a project?

    1. Conclusion → collection of data → choice of target group → identifying the problem
    2. Collection of data → identifying the problem → bibliography → analysis
    3. Analysis → presentation → collection → choice of target group
    4. Identifying the problem → choice of target group → collection of data → organisation and presentation → analysis and interpretation → conclusion → bibliography
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Identifying the problem → choice of target group → collection of data → organisation and presentation → analysis and interpretation → conclusion → bibliography

    The steps follow a logical order: decide what to study and whom to study, collect and organise the data, present and analyse them, draw conclusions, and list the sources used.

  3. Q3 · Economics · Statistics for Economics

    In a project, the 'target group' refers to:

    1. The teachers who evaluate the project
    2. The investigators who collect the data
    3. The group of people or units the study focuses on, such as households in a study of consumer awareness
    4. The books used for the study
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) The group of people or units the study focuses on, such as households in a study of consumer awareness

    Once the problem is identified, the investigator decides whom the study is about. For a study of consumer awareness, the target group could be households.

  4. Q4 · Economics · Statistics for Economics

    The bibliography of a project is:

    1. The list of sources, such as books, reports and websites, that were consulted
    2. The questionnaire used in the survey
    3. The final conclusion
    4. The table of raw data
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) The list of sources, such as books, reports and websites, that were consulted

    The bibliography lists the secondary sources used, so that readers can check them and know where the information came from.

  5. Q5 · Economics · Statistics for Economics

    For a project on consumer awareness among households, which instrument is most suitable for collecting primary data?

    1. A questionnaire or interview schedule
    2. A published census report
    3. A stock market index
    4. A price index
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) A questionnaire or interview schedule

    To know what households themselves know and do, the investigator must ask them directly, using a carefully designed questionnaire or interview schedule.

  6. Q6 · Economics · Statistics for Economics

    In a consumer awareness survey, you want to show the percentage of respondents who know each of several quality marks. A respondent may know more than one mark. Which presentation suits best?

    1. A pie diagram
    2. An ogive
    3. A histogram
    4. A simple bar diagram of the percentages
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) A simple bar diagram of the percentages

    The percentages do not add up to a whole because respondents can know several marks, so a pie diagram is unsuitable. A bar for each mark compares them clearly.

  7. Q7 · Economics · Statistics for Economics

    A household's monthly expenditure is divided among food, rent, education and other items. Which diagram best shows the share of each item in the total?

    1. Histogram
    2. Pie diagram
    3. Ogive
    4. Scatter diagram
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Pie diagram

    A pie diagram shows how a total is divided into its components, each as a sector proportional to its share.

  8. Q8 · Economics · Statistics for Economics

    To study how the price of onions changed over twelve months, which presentation is most suitable?

    1. A pie diagram
    2. A frequency polygon
    3. An arithmetic line graph (time series graph)
    4. A bivariate table
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) An arithmetic line graph (time series graph)

    A line graph with months on the x-axis and prices on the y-axis shows the trend and fluctuations over time.

  9. Q9 · Economics · Statistics for Economics

    To find out whether family income and spending on children's education move together, which statistical tool would you use?

    1. Mode
    2. Pie diagram
    3. Index number
    4. A scatter diagram and the coefficient of correlation
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) A scatter diagram and the coefficient of correlation

    Correlation measures whether and how strongly two variables move together. A scatter diagram gives a first visual check.

  10. Q10 · Economics · Statistics for Economics

    You want the typical income of families in a locality where a few families are very rich. Which average should you use?

    1. Median
    2. Arithmetic mean
    3. Weighted mean
    4. Sum of incomes
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Median

    The few very high incomes would raise the mean far above what most families earn. The median is not affected by these extreme values.
