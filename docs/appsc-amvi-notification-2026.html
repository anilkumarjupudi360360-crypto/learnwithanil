APPSC AMVI Notification 2026: 50 posts

APPSC issued a brief notification (No. 36/2026, 13 October 2026) for 50 posts of Assistant Motor Vehicle Inspector in the A.P. Transport Service. Online applications run from 6 to 26 November 2026; the detailed notification gives the fee and relaxations. This is a new recruitment, separate from Notification 21/2025, whose exam was held in January 2026. Below: the dates, eligibility, the pattern and the AMVI previous papers.

Who can apply

  • A degree in Mechanical or Automobile Engineering, or a Diploma in Automobile Engineering.
  • A motor driving licence with at least 3 years' driving experience and a Heavy Transport Vehicle endorsement. Women holding only an LMV licence must get the HTV endorsement within 2 years of joining.

The January 2026 AMVI exam (Notification 21/2025)

  • Notification 21/2025 (24 September 2025) was a limited recruitment for one carried-forward post. Its papers were held on 27 and 28 January 2026; these are the 2026 papers on this site.
  • Final key and result: 23 April 2026. Selection: 20 May 2026. That recruitment is complete.

Quick answers (as of 4 November 2026)

Is the APPSC AMVI 2026 notification out?

Yes. Brief notification No. 36/2026 of 13 October 2026, 50 posts.

When can I apply for APPSC AMVI 2026?

From 6 November to 26 November 2026, 11:59 pm, on psc.ap.gov.in after OTPR.

Is the APPSC AMVI exam date out?

No. The brief notification gives no exam date; APPSC will announce it later.

Can diploma holders apply for APPSC AMVI?

Yes, with a Diploma in Automobile Engineering. A degree must be in Mechanical or Automobile Engineering.

Is a heavy licence compulsory for AMVI?

Yes: a driving licence with 3 years' experience and an HTV endorsement. Women with only an LMV licence get 2 years after joining to add the endorsement.

Is there negative marking in APPSC AMVI?

Yes, one-third of the question's marks for each wrong answer.

What was the January 2026 AMVI exam?

Notification 21/2025, for one carried-forward post. Its final key and result came on 23 April 2026 and the selection on 20 May 2026.

What is the AMVI salary in AP?

₹48,440 – ₹1,37,220 a month (pay scale).
