AP Police Constable Notification 2026: 1,156 posts

The State Level Police Recruitment Board, Andhra Pradesh notified 1,156 posts on 16 September 2026: 542 SCT Police Constables (Civil), 285 SCT Police Constables (AR), 158 Firemen, 150 Warders (Men) and 21 Warders (Women). SLPRB will announce the online application dates and the Preliminary Written Test date in a press note. Below: posts, eligibility, the exam pattern and syllabus, and the previous paper to start practising.

Who can apply

Intermediate or an equivalent examination passed as on 1 July 2026; SC and ST candidates who passed SSC and studied and appeared for both years of Intermediate are also eligible. Age for SCT PC (Civil) and (AR): 18 to 24 years as on 1 July 2026 (born between 2 July 2002 and 1 July 2008), with the usual relaxations (Home Guards up to 32). Fee ₹300 (half that for SC and ST candidates of AP; non-local candidates pay ₹300 as open category).

Physical standards (PMT) and physical test (PET)

Height at least 167.6 cm and chest 86.3 cm with 5 cm expansion for men; height 152.5 cm and weight 40 kg for women (lower heights for Aboriginal ST candidates of the agency areas). PET: 1,600 m run in 8 minutes (women 10 min 30 s), 100 m in 15 seconds (women 18 s), long jump 3.80 m (women 2.75 m). PC (Civil), Firemen and Warders must pass the 1,600 m run and one of the other two; PC (AR) candidates must pass all three, and their PET carries 100 marks.

Qualifying marks in each written test: 40% for OC (and EWS), 35% for BC, 30% for SC and ST. Preliminary marks do not count for selection. No marks are deducted for wrong answers.

Syllabus (Preliminary and Final Written Examination)

  • English (printed in English only, also in the Telugu and Urdu versions of the paper).
  • Arithmetic (SSC standard).
  • Test of Reasoning / Mental Ability.
  • General Science.
  • History of India, Indian culture and the Indian National Movement.
  • Indian geography, polity and economy.
  • Current events of national and international importance.

Quick answers (as of 10 November 2026)

Has the AP Police Constable 2026 application started?

Not yet, as far as we could find on 10 November 2026. SLPRB will announce the online application dates in a press note on slprb.ap.gov.in.

When is the AP Constable exam 2026?

The Preliminary Written Test date has not been announced yet.

How many AP Police Constable posts are there in 2026?

1,156 in this notification: 542 SCT PC (Civil), 285 SCT PC (AR), 158 Firemen, 150 Warders (Men) and 21 Warders (Women).

What is the age limit for AP Constable 2026?

18 to 24 years on 1 July 2026 for PC (Civil) and (AR), with the usual relaxations.

Is there negative marking in the AP Constable exam?

No. No marks are deducted for wrong answers.

Do the prelims marks count for selection?

No. The Preliminary Written Test is only qualifying (40% OC, 35% BC, 30% SC/ST). Selection is on the Final Written Examination.
