MPSC Group C notification 2026: 16,717 posts, prelims on 24 January 2027

The Maharashtra Public Service Commission (MPSC) advertised the Maharashtra Group-C Services Combined Preliminary Examination 2026 (Advt. No. 017/2026) on 25 June 2026 for 2,619 posts. Two corrigenda added cadres and posts, and the one of 6 November 2026 takes the total to 16,717 posts in 27 cadres, from Clerk-Typist and Talathi to Staff Nurse and Civil Engineering Assistant. Applications and cadre options are open again from 13 to 27 November 2026, and the prelims is now on 24 January 2027. Below: the key dates, the posts, who can apply, the exam pattern and the MPSC Group C previous papers to practise.

Candidates who applied earlier need not apply again; they can add the new cadres they qualify for as an option in "My Account" (no new fee). Posts received from the government until the prelims result is final are also counted for the prelims result. The cadre-wise reservation is in Annexure-1 of the 6 November corrigendum on mpsc.gov.in.

The prelims syllabus covers current affairs, history (especially modern India and Maharashtra), geography (India and Maharashtra), the Indian Constitution and polity, economy, general science, and intelligence test and arithmetic. The prelims only shortlists candidates for the main exam; the final selection is on the main exam. The 2026 prelims was first advertised as a computer-based test; MPSC's corrigendum of 16 July 2026 made it an offline OMR exam.

Who can apply

  • Indian citizen with a knowledge of Marathi. Most cadres need a degree from a recognised university; others need the qualification of their cadre (for example, Industry Inspector: an engineering or technology diploma or a science degree; AMVI: SSC and a 3-year diploma in automobile or mechanical engineering, with experience and driving licences as in the advertisement; Staff Nurse, Civil Engineering Assistant, Craft Instructor and Assistant Fisheries Development Officer: their professional qualification, listed in Annexure-3 of the 6 November corrigendum).
  • Clerk-Typist and Tax Assistant: typing speed of at least 30 words a minute in Marathi and 40 in English (government commercial certificate or the equivalent computer typing course); some reserved categories get two years after appointment to pass it.
  • Age as on 1 October 2026: at least 19 (18 for Tax Assistant); at most 38 years for the open category and 43 for backward classes, EWS and orphans, with more for sportspersons, ex-servicemen and persons with disabilities. A government resolution of 5 November 2026 relaxes the upper age limit by one more year.
  • Prelims fee (not refundable): Rs. 394 for the open category, Rs. 294 for backward classes, EWS and orphans, Rs. 44 for ex-servicemen; bank charges extra.

Quick answers (as of 16 November 2026)

What is the last date to apply for MPSC Group C 2026?

27 November 2026 (11.59 pm) on mpsconline.gov.in; the fee can be paid online till 30 November 2026.

When is the MPSC Group C prelims 2026 exam?

On 24 January 2027. It was first fixed for 25 October 2026, then for 3 January 2027; MPSC's corrigendum of 6 November 2026 moved it to 24 January 2027.

How many vacancies are there in MPSC Group C 2026?

16,717 posts in 27 cadres after the corrigendum of 6 November 2026 (the advertisement of 25 June 2026 had 2,619, and the corrigendum of 22 July 2026 raised it to 5,707).

Do I need to apply again if I applied in June–July 2026?

No. Candidates who applied earlier keep their application and can add the new cadres they qualify for as options from "My Account", without paying the fee again.

What is the MPSC Group C prelims exam pattern?

One objective paper, the General Ability Test: 100 questions, 100 marks, 1 hour, in Marathi and English, with a quarter mark deducted for each wrong answer.

What is the age limit for MPSC Group C 2026?

19 to 38 years for the open category and up to 43 for backward classes, EWS and orphans, as on 1 October 2026 (18 is the minimum for Tax Assistant), plus the one-year relaxation of 5 November 2026.

What is the MPSC Group C application fee?

Rs. 394 for the open category, Rs. 294 for backward classes, EWS and orphans, and Rs. 44 for ex-servicemen, plus bank charges.
