Who can apply

A bachelor's degree from a recognised university. Some posts need a particular degree: Assistant Section Officer (Finance) a degree in mathematics, economics or commerce; Assistant Section Officer (Law) a law degree; Senior and Junior Accountant a degree in commerce, economics or mathematics plus a computer qualification. Age limits, relaxations and the fee are given in the detailed notification.
