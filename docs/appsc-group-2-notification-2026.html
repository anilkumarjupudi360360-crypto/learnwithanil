APPSC Group 2 Notification 2026: 720 posts, apply by 11 December, screening test on 21 February 2027

APPSC issued the detailed Group-II notification (No. 37/2026) on 10 November 2026, raising the posts from 686 in the October brief notification to 720 (471 executive, 249 non-executive, now including 31 Junior Office Assistant posts). Online applications opened on 10 November and close on 11 December 2026 at 11:59 pm. The screening test is on 21 February 2027. Here are the posts, the dates, the exam pattern, and the Group 2 previous papers to practise in the next fifteen weeks.

Who can apply

A bachelor's degree from a recognised university. Some posts need a particular degree: Assistant Section Officer (Finance) a degree in mathematics, economics or commerce; Assistant Section Officer (Law) a law degree; Senior and Junior Accountant a degree in commerce, economics or mathematics plus a computer qualification. Check the age limit and the post-wise qualification in the detailed notification before you pay.

How to apply online (10 November to 11 December 2026)

  1. Register once for the One Time Profile Registration (OTPR) on psc.ap.gov.in, or update your old OTPR: your details, education, community and photo. Note your OTPR ID (AP followed by 10 digits).
  2. If you have never set a password, create one on the APPSC candidate login page with your OTPR ID and mobile number.
  3. Log in, choose Notification No. 37/2026 (Group-II Services) and fill in the form; pick your exam centre.
  4. Pay the fee online (net banking, debit or credit card, UPI), submit, and save the PDF of your application.
  5. Do not wait for 11 December: near the Group 1 last date in October the website slowed down and many candidates could not pay. Apply early.

Fee and age limit

  • News reports of the detailed notification (Sakshi, The Hans India) do not give the fee or the age limit, and we could not read APPSC's PDF yet, so check them on psc.ap.gov.in.
  • Coaching websites quoting the detailed notification give: fee ₹250 application processing + ₹80 examination fee (exam fee waived for SC, ST, BC, PwBD, ex-service, white-card and unemployed AP candidates); age 18 to 44 as on 1 July 2026 (Excise Sub-Inspector 18 to 30; Municipal Commissioner from 21, Sub-Registrar from 20), with 5 years' relaxation for SC, ST, BC and EWS and 10 for persons with disabilities. Not yet confirmed by us from APPSC.
  • For comparison, in 2023 (Notification 11/2023) the fee was ₹250 + ₹80 and the age limit 18 to 42; AP has since raised the upper limit for non-uniformed posts from 42 to 44 (until 30 September 2027).

Cut-off marks

APPSC has not published category-wise cut-off marks for the last Group 2 recruitment (Notification 11/2023). The screening test is only a filter: about 92,250 candidates qualified for the Mains from the screening test of 25 February 2024, for 905 posts, and the final selection was made on the Mains marks (final selection list on 28 January 2026). Figures you see online as "Group 2 cut-off" are estimates by coaching sites, not APPSC's.

Quick answers (as of 11 November 2026)

Is the APPSC Group 2 notification 2026 out?

Yes. The brief notification No. 37/2026 came on 13 October 2026 (686 posts) and the detailed notification on 10 November 2026, with 720 posts: 471 executive and 249 non-executive.

What is the last date for APPSC Group 2 2026?

11 December 2026, 11:59 pm. Applications opened on 10 November on psc.ap.gov.in; you need your One Time Profile Registration (OTPR) first.

Has the APPSC Group 2 last date been extended?

The brief notification said 30 November; the detailed notification of 10 November gives 11 December 2026 (11:59 pm) as the last date. No further change as of 11 November 2026.

When is the APPSC Group 2 screening test 2026?

Sunday 21 February 2027, offline on an OMR sheet. The Mains will be online; APPSC will announce its date separately.

How many Deputy Tahsildar posts are there in Group 2 2026?

203 Deputy Tahsildar posts and 176 Deputy MPDO posts, out of 720 in all.

Why did Group 2 posts go up from 686 to 720?

Departments reported more vacancies before the detailed notification: 31 Junior Office Assistant posts were added, and a few counts changed (Deputy Tahsildar 200 → 203, Senior Accountant 90 → 91, Junior Accountant 48 → 47).

Is the Group 2 exam pattern changed?

No. It is still a 150-mark screening test, then two 150-mark Mains papers, then a qualifying Computer Proficiency Test. Selection is on the Mains marks.

Is there negative marking in APPSC Group 2?

Yes. One-third of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer.

What is the APPSC Group 2 fee?

Coaching websites quoting the detailed notification give ₹250 application fee + ₹80 exam fee, with the exam fee waived for SC, ST, BC, PwBD, ex-service, white-card and unemployed AP candidates (the same as in 2023). Confirm on psc.ap.gov.in before paying.
