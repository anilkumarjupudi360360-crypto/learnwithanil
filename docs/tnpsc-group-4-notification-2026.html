TNPSC Group 4 notification 2026: 6,574 posts, exam on 10 January 2027

TNPSC issued Notification No. 11/2026 for the Combined Civil Services Examination-IV (Group-IV Services) on 6 October 2026: 6,574 vacancies, including 291 Village Administrative Officers. Applications closed on 8 November 2026 after an extension, and the written exam is on Sunday, 10 January 2027. Below: the key dates, the posts, who can apply, the exam pattern and the TNPSC Group 4 previous papers to practise.

Exam pattern (single paper, SSLC standard)

PartSubjectQuestions · marks
ATamil Eligibility-cum-Scoring Test (in Tamil only)100 · 150
BGeneral Studies (Tamil and English)75 · 150 (with Part C)
CAptitude and Mental Ability (Tamil and English)25
Total3 hours, OMR, objective200 · 300

Parts B and C are valued only if the candidate scores at least 40% (60 marks) in Part A; the marks of all three parts together decide the rank. The minimum qualifying mark is 90 out of 300 for all communities. There is no interview. Each question has a fifth option, (E) "Answer not known"; leaving a question blank costs 0.5 mark. Differently abled candidates who studied only English may take General English (SSLC standard) instead of Part A.

Who can apply

  • Minimum qualification: a pass in SSLC (10th standard); Typist, Steno-Typist and some other posts also need the technical qualifications listed in the notification.
  • Age as on 1 July 2026 (as amended on 4 November 2026 under G.O.(Ms) No.68 of 30 October 2026, which raised the upper limit by five years): for most posts 18 to 35 years for "Others", 37 for BC(OBCM), BCM and MBC/DC, and 40 for SC, SC(A) and ST; VAO from 21. For many posts there is no upper limit for BC, MBC, SC and ST candidates who hold a qualification higher than the minimum. See the amendment for each post and the concessions for persons with benchmark disability, ex-servicemen and destitute widows.
  • Fee: TNPSC One Time Registration fee of Rs. 150 (valid five years) and an exam fee of Rs. 100, unless exempted (see the notification for the categories).

Quick answers (as of 15 November 2026)

When is the TNPSC Group 4 exam 2027?

Sunday, 10 January 2027, from 9.30 am to 12.30 pm, as fixed in Notification No. 11/2026.

Was the TNPSC Group 4 last date extended?

Yes. Addendum 11B/2026 of 4 November 2026 extended the last date from 5 November to 8 November 2026, 11.59 pm, after the age limit was raised. Applications are now closed.

How many vacancies are there in TNPSC Group 4 2026?

6,574 in Notification No. 11/2026, including 291 VAO posts, about 3,743 Junior Assistant posts and 1,572 Typist posts.

Was the TNPSC Group 4 age limit increased?

Yes. G.O.(Ms) No.68 of 30 October 2026 raised the upper age limit by five years for all categories, and TNPSC amended the notification on 4 November 2026 (for most posts: 35 for "Others", 37 for BC/BCM/MBC, 40 for SC/ST, as on 1 July 2026).

What is the TNPSC Group 4 exam pattern?

One OMR paper of 200 questions for 300 marks in 3 hours: Part A Tamil (100 questions, 150 marks, at least 40% needed), Part B General Studies (75) and Part C Aptitude & Mental Ability (25) for 150 marks. No interview.

What is the minimum qualifying mark for TNPSC Group 4?

90 out of 300 for all communities, and at least 60 of 150 in Part A Tamil.

When will the TNPSC Group 4 hall ticket be released?

Not announced as of 15 November 2026. TNPSC releases it on tnpscexams.in before the exam and announces it in a press release; it is not sent by post.
