    Which of the following statements about Sikhism and Buddhism in India are true? i. Sikhism originated in the 15th century in Punjab, while Buddhism originated in India in the 6th century BCE. ii. The Golden Temple in Amritsar is a holy site for Sikhs, while Bodh Gaya is a holy site for Buddhists. iv. Sikhism and Buddhism have a significant following in the Indian states of Punjab and Sikkim, respectively.

    1. i and ii
    2. ii and iv
    3. ii and iii
    4. i, iii, and iv
