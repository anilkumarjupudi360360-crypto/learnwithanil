    Answer: (3) Figure 3

    సమాధానం: (3) Figure 3

    All 3-wheeler and 4-wheeler drivers are drivers of motor vehicles (big circle); 3-wheeler drivers and 4-wheeler drivers are separate groups (two non-overlapping circles), and a school van is a 4-wheeler, so school-van drivers form a small circle inside the 4-wheeler circle, which is exactly Figure 3.
