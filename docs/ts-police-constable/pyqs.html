  1. 1 of 10 · TS Police Constable Mains 2023 · Q1 · English

    Fill in the blank with an appropriate form of the verb given in brackets to make it a meaningful sentence. She ______ (hand) me a picture of sloppily drawn birthday cake last year.

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Handed

    The time marker "last year" refers to a completed action at a definite past time, so the simple past "handed" is needed. The present perfect "have handed" cannot be used with a definite past time expression, and "had handed" would need a later past action as its reference point.

  2. 2 of 10 · TS Police Constable Prelims 2022 · Q1 · English

    A pair of socks ______ been missing from my room.

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) has

    The subject is "A pair" (singular), not "socks", so the singular auxiliary "has" goes with "been missing". "have" and "are" wrongly agree with "socks", and "is been" is not a valid verb form.

  3. 3 of 10 · TS Police Constable Mains 2023 · Q2 · English

    Fill in the blank with an appropriate form of the verb given in brackets to make it a meaningful sentence. I ______ (pull) out my calculator before I added up all the money.

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Had pulled

    Of two past actions, the one that happened earlier takes the past perfect: pulling out the calculator came before adding up the money, so "had pulled" is correct. "Had been pulled" is passive and does not fit the active subject "I".

  4. 4 of 10 · TS Police Constable Prelims 2022 · Q2 · English

    I always listen to great speeches carefully so that I ______ implement them in every walk of my life.

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) can

    A purpose clause with "so that" takes a modal such as can, may or will before the base verb, so "so that I can implement them" is correct. "was", "am" and "had" cannot be followed directly by the base form "implement".

  5. 5 of 10 · TS Police Constable Mains 2023 · Q4 · English

    Identify the correct passive form of the verb from the options given below to fill in the blank in the following sentence. A nation ______ (hold) together by shared beliefs and shared attitudes.

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Is held

    The sentence states a general truth about a singular subject ("a nation"), so the simple present passive "is held" (is + past participle) is needed. "Were held" and "have been held" do not agree with the singular subject, and "to be hold" is not a finite verb and uses the wrong form of "hold".

  6. 6 of 10 · TS Police Constable Prelims 2022 · Q3 · English

    The teacher ______ completed this chapter.

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) has

    "Completed" here is a past participle forming the present perfect, which needs "has" with a third-person singular subject like "The teacher". "have" and "are" do not agree with a singular subject, and "is completed" would wrongly make the teacher the thing completed.

  7. 7 of 10 · TS Police Constable Mains 2023 · Q5 · English

    Fill in the blank with an appropriate verb that agrees with the subject in the following sentence. One of the most important secrets to success ______, instead of being against something, be for something.

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Is

    In "One of the ...", the real subject is "one", which is singular, so it takes the singular linking verb "is" (the plural "secrets" is only the object of the preposition "of"). "Is" also links the subject to the complement "be for something"; "has" or "does" make no sense here.

  8. 8 of 10 · TS Police Constable Prelims 2022 · Q4 · English

    My mother ______ the newspaper in the morning.

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) reads

    "In the morning" here describes a daily habit, and habits are expressed with the simple present: "My mother reads the newspaper in the morning." The continuous forms describe an action in progress at one particular time.

  9. 9 of 10 · TS Police Constable Mains 2023 · Q7 · English

    Identify the grammatically incorrect sentence in passive voice from the options given below.

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Raveendra has asked to make a presentation at the meeting by his team leader.

    Since the agent is "by his team leader", the sentence must be passive: "Raveendra has been asked to make a presentation at the meeting by his team leader." "Has asked" is an active form, so sentence (4) is wrong, while the other three use correct passive forms.

  10. 10 of 10 · TS Police Constable Prelims 2022 · Q5 · English

    India is ______ country. Every Indian is ______ icon in helping the poor people.

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) a, an

    The article depends on the first sound of the next word: "country" begins with a consonant sound, so "a country", and "icon" begins with a vowel sound, so "an icon".
