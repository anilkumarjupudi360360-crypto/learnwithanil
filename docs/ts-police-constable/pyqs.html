  1. 1 of 10 · TS Police Constable Mains 2023 · Q1 · English

    Fill in the blank with an appropriate form of the verb given in brackets to make it a meaningful sentence. She ______ (hand) me a picture of sloppily drawn birthday cake last year.

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Handed

    The time marker "last year" refers to a completed action at a definite past time, so the simple past "handed" is needed. The present perfect "have handed" cannot be used with a definite past time expression, and "had handed" would need a later past action as its reference point.

  2. 2 of 10 · TS Police Constable Prelims 2022 · Q1 · English

    A pair of socks ______ been missing from my room.

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) has

    The subject is "A pair" (singular), not "socks", so the singular auxiliary "has" goes with "been missing". "have" and "are" wrongly agree with "socks", and "is been" is not a valid verb form.

  3. 3 of 10 · TS Police Constable Mains 2019 · Q1 · English

    Which of the following statements is incorrect regarding the usage of the article?

    Show answer

    Answer (TSLPRB's preliminary key): (1) Eight-year-old Roxy Getter is the youngest girl who climbed the Mount Kilimanjaro in 2017.

    The names of single mountains take no article: we say "Mount Everest" and "Mount Kilimanjaro", so "climbed the Mount Kilimanjaro" is wrong and should be "climbed Mount Kilimanjaro". The other sentences are correct: "a cold" but "the flu" is the usual idiom for these illnesses, "What a + singular noun" is the normal exclamation, and a unique post after "elected" (President) takes no article.

  4. 4 of 10 · TS Police Constable Prelims 2018 · Q1 · English

    Choose the incorrectly spelt word from the following set of words :

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) maneouvre

    The correct spelling is "manoeuvre" (British) or "maneuver" (American); "maneouvre" puts the o and e in the wrong order. "Acquaintance", "erroneously" and "demurrage" (a charge for keeping a ship, wagon or container beyond the agreed time) are all spelt correctly.

  5. 5 of 10 · TS Police Constable Prelims 2016 · Q1 · English

    Choose the right matching of one word substitutes with their description.

    List IList II
    (i) Congestion(A) An accident in which two vehicles hit each other
    (ii) Collision(B) Situation in which roads are full of traffic
    (iii) Bottleneck(C) Occasion when a vehicle stops working
    (iv) Breakdown(D) Place in a road where the traffic cannot pass
    Show answer

    Answer (TSLPRB's preliminary key): (4) (i) – (B), (ii) – (A), (iii) – (D), (iv) – (C)

    "Congestion" is the state of roads full of traffic (B); a "collision" is an accident in which two vehicles hit each other (A); a "bottleneck" is a narrow or blocked place in a road where traffic cannot pass freely (D); and a "breakdown" is when a vehicle stops working (C). So the matching is (i)–(B), (ii)–(A), (iii)–(D), (iv)–(C).

  6. 6 of 10 · TS Police Constable Mains 2016 · Q1 · English

    What is the synonym of 'amenable' in 'Both parties were amenable to a peaceful settlement of the land dispute'.?

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Responsive

    "Amenable" means open and responsive to a suggestion, that is, willing to accept it or be persuaded. Here both parties were willing to accept a peaceful settlement, so "responsive" is the synonym. "Unwilling", "doubtful" and "disagreeable" all express reluctance or opposition, which is nearly the opposite of "amenable".

  7. 7 of 10 · TS Police Constable Mains 2023 · Q2 · English

    Fill in the blank with an appropriate form of the verb given in brackets to make it a meaningful sentence. I ______ (pull) out my calculator before I added up all the money.

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Had pulled

    Of two past actions, the one that happened earlier takes the past perfect: pulling out the calculator came before adding up the money, so "had pulled" is correct. "Had been pulled" is passive and does not fit the active subject "I".

  8. 8 of 10 · TS Police Constable Prelims 2022 · Q2 · English

    I always listen to great speeches carefully so that I ______ implement them in every walk of my life.

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) can

    A purpose clause with "so that" takes a modal such as can, may or will before the base verb, so "so that I can implement them" is correct. "was", "am" and "had" cannot be followed directly by the base form "implement".

  9. 9 of 10 · TS Police Constable Mains 2019 · Q2 · English

    Fill in the blanks using the correct preposition: Where did you come ______?

    Show answer

    Answer (TSLPRB's preliminary key): (2) from

    "Where did you come from?" asks about a person's place of origin or starting point, and "from" is the preposition that marks the starting point of movement. "For" asks about purpose ("What did you come for?"), not place, while "above" and "into" make no sense after "come" in this question.

  10. 10 of 10 · TS Police Constable Prelims 2018 · Q2 · English

    In the following question, four words are given, of which two words are nearly the same or opposite in meaning. Find the two words that are similar or opposite in meaning and indicate the number of the correct letter combination provided in the options. A. Possessive B. Dreadful C. Secretive D. Repugnant

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) B-D

    "Dreadful" (very bad, causing fear or disgust) and "repugnant" (extremely distasteful, offensive) are nearly the same in meaning, so the pair is B-D. "Possessive" (wanting to own or control) and "secretive" (inclined to hide things) are neither synonyms nor antonyms of each other or of the other two words.
