  3. PC Mains 2023 · Q198 · Reasoning & Mental Ability · Logical Reasoning

    Study the following information and answer the question given below. 'A*B' means 'A is greater than B' 'A#B' means 'A is not greater than B' 'A%B' means 'A is less than B' 'A$B' means 'A is not less than B' 'A@B' means 'A is neither greater than nor less than B'.     1. S $ Q
    2. R * P

    1. S $ Q
    2. R * P

    4. Q @ U
