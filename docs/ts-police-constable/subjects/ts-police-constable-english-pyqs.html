Grammar

  1. PC Prelims 2022 · Q1 · English · Grammar

    A pair of socks ______ been missing from my room.

    1. has
    2. have
    3. is
    4. are
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) has

    The subject is "A pair" (singular), not "socks", so the singular auxiliary "has" goes with "been missing". "have" and "are" wrongly agree with "socks", and "is been" is not a valid verb form.

  2. PC Prelims 2022 · Q2 · English · Grammar

    I always listen to great speeches carefully so that I ______ implement them in every walk of my life.

    1. had
    2. was
    3. am
    4. can
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) can

    A purpose clause with "so that" takes a modal such as can, may or will before the base verb, so "so that I can implement them" is correct. "was", "am" and "had" cannot be followed directly by the base form "implement".

  3. PC Prelims 2022 · Q3 · English · Grammar

    The teacher ______ completed this chapter.

    1. are
    2. is
    3. have
    4. has
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) has

    "Completed" here is a past participle forming the present perfect, which needs "has" with a third-person singular subject like "The teacher". "have" and "are" do not agree with a singular subject, and "is completed" would wrongly make the teacher the thing completed.

  4. PC Prelims 2022 · Q4 · English · Grammar

    My mother ______ the newspaper in the morning.

    1. is reading
    2. was reading
    3. reads
    4. will be reading
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) reads

    "In the morning" here describes a daily habit, and habits are expressed with the simple present: "My mother reads the newspaper in the morning." The continuous forms describe an action in progress at one particular time.

  5. PC Prelims 2022 · Q5 · English · Grammar

    India is ______ country. Every Indian is ______ icon in helping the poor people.

    1. a, an
    2. an, a
    3. a, the
    4. of, the
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) a, an

    The article depends on the first sound of the next word: "country" begins with a consonant sound, so "a country", and "icon" begins with a vowel sound, so "an icon".

  6. PC Prelims 2022 · Q7 · English · Grammar

    I ______ an appointment with the doctor soon.

    1. will make
    2. had made
    3. am making
    4. was making
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) will make

    "Soon" points to the future, so the simple future "will make" fits. The past forms "had made" and "was making" clash with "soon".

  7. PC Prelims 2022 · Q8 · English · Grammar

    People often ______ games for sports and sports for games.

    1. mistaken
    2. mistaking
    3. mistake
    4. mistook
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) mistake

    A general truth with a plural subject and the adverb "often" takes the simple present base form: "People often mistake games for sports" ("mistake X for Y" means wrongly take X to be Y). "Mistaken" and "mistaking" need an auxiliary, and "mistook" is past tense.

  8. PC Prelims 2022 · Q9 · English · Grammar

    Rahul and his friends ______ invited to the party by me.

    1. is
    2. have been
    3. was
    4. had
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) have been

    "Invited ... by me" is passive, and the compound subject "Rahul and his friends" is plural, so the plural passive "have been invited" is correct. "is" and "was" are singular, and "had invited" is active, which does not fit "by me".

  9. PC Prelims 2022 · Q11 · English · Grammar

    If you study history, you ______ that all stories of success are also stories of great failures.

    1. see
    2. think
    3. will find
    4. know
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) will find

    This is a first conditional: "If + simple present, ... will + base verb", so "If you study history, you will find ..." is correct. "Find" also suits the sense of discovering something through study.

  10. PC Prelims 2022 · Q12 · English · Grammar

    Back in my native place, we ______ good internet.

    1. does not have
    2. was not have
    3. did not have
    4. had not have
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) did not have

    "Back in my native place" refers to the past, and the past negative of "have" is "did not have", which works with every subject. "Does not have" is singular present, and "was not have" and "had not have" are ungrammatical.

  11. PC Prelims 2022 · Q13 · English · Grammar

    The police ______ arrested the thieves.

    1. has
    2. have
    3. is
    4. are
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) have

    "Police" is a collective noun that always takes a plural verb, so the present perfect is "The police have arrested the thieves". "Has" is singular, and "is/are arrested" would be passive forms that do not fit an object like "the thieves".

  12. PC Prelims 2022 · Q14 · English · Grammar

    Either of the foot paths ______ to Superintendent's house.

    1. lead
    2. leads
    3. have led
    4. is led
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) leads

    "Either of" + plural noun takes a singular verb, because "either" means one of the two: "Either of the foot paths leads to ...". "Lead" and "have led" are plural, and "is led" is a passive form that changes the meaning.

  13. PC Prelims 2022 · Q16 · English · Grammar

    I never feel that I am superior ______ anyone.

    1. than
    2. to
    3. for
    4. on
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) to

    Latin comparatives such as superior, inferior, senior, junior and prior are followed by "to", not "than", so the answer is "superior to anyone".

  14. PC Prelims 2022 · Q17 · English · Grammar

    Radha and Rima ______ business partners.

    1. are
    2. is
    3. was
    4. had been
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) are

    Two subjects joined by "and" make a plural subject, which takes a plural verb: "Radha and Rima are business partners". "Is" and "was" are singular.

  15. PC Prelims 2022 · Q18 · English · Grammar

    She ______ I cannot disturb her now.

    1. has been resting
    2. had been resting
    3. is resting
    4. was resting
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) is resting

    "I cannot disturb her now" shows the action is going on at this moment, so the present continuous "is resting" fits best. The past forms "had been resting" and "was resting" do not match "now".

  16. PC Prelims 2022 · Q19 · English · Grammar

    We ______ two daughters.

    1. will have
    2. has
    3. have
    4. having
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) have

    "Have" shows possession and agrees with the plural subject "We": "We have two daughters". "Has" is for third-person singular subjects, "having" cannot stand alone as a main verb, and "will have" does not suit a present fact.

  17. PC Prelims 2022 · Q20 · English · Grammar

    Either Maya or her friend Manju ______ responsible for the picnic plan.

    1. are
    2. was
    3. is
    4. were
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) is

    With "either ... or", the verb agrees with the nearer subject. "Her friend Manju" is singular, and the sentence states a present fact, so "is responsible" is correct. "Are" and "were" are plural.

Vocabulary

  1. PC Prelims 2022 · Q6 · English · Vocabulary

    The synonym of "hard" is

    1. beautiful
    2. firm
    3. soft
    4. weak
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) firm

    "Hard" (solid, not easily pressed or bent) means the same as "firm". "Soft" is its antonym, and "weak" and "beautiful" are unrelated in meaning.

  2. PC Prelims 2022 · Q10 · English · Vocabulary

    The antonym of "opaque" is

    1. Fragile
    2. Partial
    3. Transparent
    4. Broken
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Transparent

    "Opaque" means not allowing light to pass through, so its opposite is "transparent", which lets light pass clearly.

  3. PC Prelims 2022 · Q15 · English · Vocabulary

    I have done a ______ deal of work.

    1. big
    2. great
    3. huge
    4. enormous
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) great

    "A great deal of" is a fixed expression meaning "a large amount of". "Big", "huge" and "enormous" have similar meanings but are not used in this idiom.

Comprehension

  1. PC Prelims 2022 · Q21 · English · Comprehension

    Ms. Yanada recalls all too clearly that mid-summer day in 1945 when US warplanes dropped an atomic bomb in Hiroshima. A second atom bomb was dropped by the USA on the southwestern city of Nagasaki three days later, forcing Japan to surrender to the allied forces and bringing the Pacific war to an end. More than 1,40,000 people were killed instantly after the nuclear blasts and many more died in the months and years after. Victims continue to die each year from cancer and radiation-linked illnesses. Today Ms. Yanada is part of a group called Japan Confederation of A & H Bomb Sufferers, made up of survivors like her struggling for a fair deal in life. In Japan, a vast number of people are still lobbying the government to give them the status they have been denied for so long. Ms. Yanada's group makes frequent visits to the offices of the influential members of the district. The US dropped ______ on Hiroshima.

    1. one bomb
    2. two bombs
    3. three bombs
    4. four bombs
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) one bomb

    The passage says US warplanes "dropped an atomic bomb" on Hiroshima. The second bomb was dropped on Nagasaki, not on Hiroshima, so "two bombs" is a trap.
