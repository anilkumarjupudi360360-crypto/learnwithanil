  1. Q1 · English · Grammar

    A pair of socks ______ been missing from my room.

    1. has
    2. have
    3. is
    4. are
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) has

    The subject is "A pair" (singular), not "socks", so the singular auxiliary "has" goes with "been missing". "have" and "are" wrongly agree with "socks", and "is been" is not a valid verb form.

  2. Q2 · English · Grammar

    I always listen to great speeches carefully so that I ______ implement them in every walk of my life.

    1. had
    2. was
    3. am
    4. can
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) can

    A purpose clause with "so that" takes a modal such as can, may or will before the base verb, so "so that I can implement them" is correct. "was", "am" and "had" cannot be followed directly by the base form "implement".

  3. Q3 · English · Grammar

    The teacher ______ completed this chapter.

    1. are
    2. is
    3. have
    4. has
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) has

    "Completed" here is a past participle forming the present perfect, which needs "has" with a third-person singular subject like "The teacher". "have" and "are" do not agree with a singular subject, and "is completed" would wrongly make the teacher the thing completed.

  4. Q4 · English · Grammar

    My mother ______ the newspaper in the morning.

    1. is reading
    2. was reading
    3. reads
    4. will be reading
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) reads

    "In the morning" here describes a daily habit, and habits are expressed with the simple present: "My mother reads the newspaper in the morning." The continuous forms describe an action in progress at one particular time.

  5. Q5 · English · Grammar

    India is ______ country. Every Indian is ______ icon in helping the poor people.

  6. Q6 · English · Vocabulary

    The synonym of "hard" is

    1. beautiful
    2. firm
    3. soft
    4. weak
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) firm

    "Hard" (solid, not easily pressed or bent) means the same as "firm". "Soft" is its antonym, and "weak" and "beautiful" are unrelated in meaning.

  8. Q8 · English · Grammar

    People often ______ games for sports and sports for games.

    1. mistaken
    2. mistaking
    3. mistake
    4. mistook
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) mistake

    A general truth with a plural subject and the adverb "often" takes the simple present base form: "People often mistake games for sports" ("mistake X for Y" means wrongly take X to be Y). "Mistaken" and "mistaking" need an auxiliary, and "mistook" is past tense.

  9. Q9 · English · Grammar

    Rahul and his friends ______ invited to the party by me.

    1. is
    2. have been
    3. was
    4. had
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) have been

    "Invited ... by me" is passive, and the compound subject "Rahul and his friends" is plural, so the plural passive "have been invited" is correct. "is" and "was" are singular, and "had invited" is active, which does not fit "by me".
