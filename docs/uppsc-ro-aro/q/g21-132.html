    How many Squares and Triangles are there in the following figure?

    1. 2 Squares and 16 Triangles
    2. 2 Squares and 14 Triangles
    3. 3 Squares and 16 Triangles
    4. 3 Squares and 18 Triangles

    1. 2 वर्ग तथा 16 त्रिभुज
    2. 2 वर्ग तथा 14 त्रिभुज
    3. 3 वर्ग तथा 16 त्रिभुज
    4. 3 वर्ग तथा 18 त्रिभुज

    Answer: (4) 3 Squares and 18 Triangles
