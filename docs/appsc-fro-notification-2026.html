APPSC Forest Range Officer (FRO) Notification 2026: 5 posts

APPSC Notification No. 16/2026 (16 October 2026) invites online applications for 5 posts of Forest Range Officer in the A.P. Forest Service. Apply by 5 November 2026. Below: dates, eligibility and physical standards, the exam pattern and syllabus, and the previous papers to start practising.

Who can apply

A bachelor's degree in agriculture, botany, chemistry, computer applications or computer science, engineering (agriculture, chemical, civil, computer, electrical, electronics or mechanical), environmental science, forestry, geology, horticulture, mathematics, physics, statistics, veterinary science or zoology. Physical standards: men at least 163 cm tall, chest 79 cm with 5 cm expansion, able to walk 25 km in 4 hours; women at least 150 cm, chest 74 cm with 5 cm expansion, able to walk 16 km in 4 hours (lower heights for Scheduled Tribes and some other groups). Fee: ₹250 application processing fee plus ₹120 examination fee.

General Forestry I syllabus

  • Renewable and non-renewable natural resources: forest, water, mineral, food, energy and land resources.
  • Plant sciences: classification, morphology, tissues, wood anatomy, seeds, reproduction, propagation, plant physiology, inheritance and plant diseases.
  • Biodiversity and its conservation: levels and value of biodiversity, hotspots, biomes of India, population ecology.
  • Soil science and geology: rocks and minerals of India, soil formation and properties, soil conservation, reclamation of problem soils, organic matter, N and C cycles.
  • Water resource and watershed management: water harvesting, groundwater recharge, forest hydrology.
  • Silviculture, agroforestry, social forestry and community forest management: regeneration, nursery and planting, silviculture of 14 species grown in AP (teak, red sanders, sandalwood, neem, bamboo, eucalyptus, casuarina and others).

General Forestry II syllabus

  • Ecosystems and wildlife: ecosystem structure and function, GPP and NPP, types of ecosystems, wildlife and protected areas of India.
  • Forest protection: injuries, pests and diseases, fire, grazing, encroachment and other human impacts.
  • Forest utilization: logging and transport, seasoning and preservation, wood properties, MFP/NTFP species of AP, antidote plants.
  • Animal management and economic zoology: animal ailments, dentition, snakes and snake bite, nutrition, birds and migration, beneficial and harmful insects, fish and prawns.
  • Forest economics, legislation and administration: cost-benefit and investment methods, forest policies of 1894, 1952 and 1988, AP Forest Act 1967, Forest Conservation Act 1980, Wildlife Protection Act 1972, forest services.
  • Forest inventory, mensuration and remote sensing: sampling methods, tree and stand measurement, instruments, GIS, GPS and forest cover monitoring.
  • Environmental science: pollution, solid waste, sustainable development, climate change, environmental laws (EPA 1986, Air Act 1981, Water Act 1974, APWALTA 2004).
  • Forest survey and engineering, and first aid.

Paper I and Paper II

Paper I General Studies & Mental Ability follows the common APPSC syllabus (current events, science, history, geography, polity, economy, environment, disaster management, reasoning, data analysis). Paper II Mathematics is SSC standard: arithmetic, algebra, trigonometry, geometry, mensuration and statistics.

Quick answers (as of 4 November 2026)

Is the APPSC FRO last date extended?

The notification gives 5 November 2026 (11:59 pm). One coaching website (3 November) reports an extension to 12 November 2026, but we could not yet find APPSC's own notice on psc.ap.gov.in. To be safe, apply by 5 November.

How many Forest Range Officer posts are there in 2026?

5, including 1 carried forward (Notification 16/2026, 16 October 2026).

What is the age limit for APPSC FRO?

18 to 30 years on 1 July 2026, with the usual relaxations for reserved categories.

When is the APPSC FRO exam 2026?

Not announced yet. It will be computer-based (CBRT); APPSC will announce the date later.

Is there negative marking in APPSC FRO?

Yes. One-third of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer.

Is there a walking test for FRO?

Yes. Candidates picked in the ratio 1:3 after the written test take a walking test and a medical test, and a qualifying Computer Proficiency Test.
