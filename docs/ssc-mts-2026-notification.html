SSC MTS 2026 notification: not out yet

SSC's exam calendar for 2026-27 listed the Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff and Havaldar (CBIC & CBN) Examination 2026 notice for 30 June 2026. It was postponed, and SSC had not published it as of 15 November 2026. The last cycle, MTS 2025, was notified on 26 June 2025 and its computer-based exam was held in February 2026. Below: where things stand, the rules of the last notice (they rarely change), the exam pattern and the MTS previous papers.

Who could apply (last notice)

  • Class 10 (matriculation) or equivalent, passed on or before the cut-off date in the notice.
  • Age on 1 August of the notice year: 18–25 years for MTS, 18–27 years for Havaldar (CBIC & CBN) and some MTS posts; relaxation OBC 3, SC/ST 5, PwBD 10 years.
  • Fee ₹100; women, SC, ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen pay nothing.
  • The paper is set in English, Hindi and 13 regional languages; you choose the language when you apply.

Quick answers (as of 15 November 2026)

Is the SSC MTS 2026 notification out?

No, not as of 15 November 2026. SSC's calendar had it on 30 June 2026, but it was postponed and SSC has not given a new date.

When will the SSC MTS 2026 notification come?

SSC has not announced a date. Coaching sites have guessed several months (October, November); none of these is an SSC date. Watch ssc.gov.in.

What is the SSC MTS age limit?

In the last notice: 18–25 years for MTS and 18–27 years for Havaldar and some MTS posts, on 1 August of the notice year, with the usual relaxations (OBC 3, SC/ST 5, PwBD 10 years).

What qualification do I need for SSC MTS?

Class 10 (matriculation) or equivalent from a recognised board.

Is there negative marking in SSC MTS?

Only in Session II (General Awareness and English): 1 mark is cut for each wrong answer. Session I (Maths and Reasoning) has no negative marking. Every question carries 3 marks.

Is the SSC MTS 2025 result out?

The computer-based exam result for the Havaldar PET/PST came on 3 August 2026, and the PET/PST was held from 24 to 31 August 2026. Check ssc.gov.in for the final result of MTS and Havaldar.

Where can I practise SSC MTS previous papers?

On the SSC MTS page of this site: whole shifts of MTS 2024 with SSC's answer key and an explanation for every question, in English and Hindi, as timed mock tests or by subject.
