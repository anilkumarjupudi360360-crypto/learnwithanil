    The bar-graph given below presents the average monthly commission earned by salesmen A, B, C, and D over the years. In the year 2006, the commission earned by salesman D is what percentage more than the average commission earned by the salesman C during the given years? (Correct to 2 decimal places)

    1. 3.78%
    2. 4.84%
    3. 8.89%
    4. 9.62%

(2 దశాంశ స్థానాలకు సవరించండి)

    1. 3.78%
    2. 4.84%
    3. 8.89%
    4. 9.62%
