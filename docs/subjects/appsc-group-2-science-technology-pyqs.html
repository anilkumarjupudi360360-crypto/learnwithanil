General Science

  1. Screening 2019 · Q11 · Science & Technology · General Science

    What is the SI unit of inductance?

    Show answer

    Answer: Henry

    The henry (H) is named after the American physicist Joseph Henry. One henry produces 1 volt when current changes at 1 ampere per second.

  2. Screening 2019 · Q18 · Science & Technology · General Science

    Match List I (Scientist) with List II (Invention): a. Galileo Galilei b. Thomas Alva Edison c. Alexander Graham Bell d. Karl Ferdinand Braun I. Telephone II. Cathode Ray Tube III. Telescope IV. Electric Light Bulb

    Show answer

    Answer: a – III, b – IV, c – I, d – II

    Galileo improved the telescope (1609), Edison the incandescent bulb (1879), Bell patented the telephone (1876) and Braun built the first cathode ray tube (1897).

  3. Mains 2019 · P-I · Q35 · Science & Technology · General Science

    An automatic response to a stimulus is called a

    Show answer

    Answer: Reflex

    A reflex is an involuntary, rapid response controlled through the spinal cord via a reflex arc, like pulling a hand away from heat.

  4. Mains 2019 · P-I · Q58 · Science & Technology · General Science

    What is IQF in food preservation?

    Show answer

    Answer: Individually Quick Frozen

    IQF freezes each piece separately and quickly so that pieces do not stick together and ice crystals stay small, as in frozen shrimp or peas.

  1. Mains 2025 · P-II · Q87 · Science & Technology · Biotechnology & Health

    What is the primary environmental concern associated with Genetically Modified (GM) crops?

    1. Cross-pollination with non-GM crops
    2. Reduced pesticide use
    3. Increased yield
    4. Soil degradation
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Cross-pollination with non-GM crops

    Gene flow through pollen can move transgenes into conventional crops or wild relatives. That threatens biodiversity and the purity of organic produce.

  2. Mains 2025 · P-II · Q88 · Science & Technology · Biotechnology & Health

    3. Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)
    4. Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Genetic Engineering Appraisal Committee (GEAC)

    The GEAC, under the Ministry of Environment (Rules of 1989 under the EP Act, 1986), approves the environmental release of GM organisms such as Bt cotton and GM mustard DMH-11.

    Show answer

  1. Mains 2025 · P-II · Q114 · Science & Technology · IT & Communication

    What is Digital arrest?

    1. Arrest made by police when the accused is in foreign countries
    2. Arrest made by police when the accused cannot be arrested physically
    3. Arrest made by police when the accused is granted anticipatory bail by courts
    4. Term used by cyber criminals to scare victims
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Term used by cyber criminals to scare victims

    'Digital arrest' is a scam in which fraudsters pose as police, CBI or customs officers on video calls and 'detain' victims online to extort money. No such legal procedure exists in Indian law.
