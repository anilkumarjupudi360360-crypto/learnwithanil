  5. Screening 2024 · Q100 · Current Affairs · National

    Consider the following statements about MILAN 2024: I. The Harbour phase of MILAN 2024 is from 19th to 25th February 2024 and is aimed towards building upon the experience of multilateral forces operating together at sea. III. The Sea phase of MILAN 2024 is being conducted from 22nd to 27th February 2024 and provides a platform for participating navies to discuss maritime subjects of common interest and share solutions. Which among the above given statements are correct?

    1. I and II only
    2. II and III only
    3. I, II and III
    4. None of the above
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) None of the above

    MILAN 2024 was hosted at Visakhapatnam by the Eastern Naval Command. The harbour phase (19–23 Feb) was for professional exchanges and the sea phase (24–27 Feb) for operations at sea, so all three statements are wrong.

    Show answer

    Answer: Joseph Nye
