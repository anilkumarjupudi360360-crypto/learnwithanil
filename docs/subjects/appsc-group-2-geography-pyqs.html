Physical Geography

  1. Screening 2024 · Q32 · Geography · Physical Geography

    Identify the incorrect statement:

    1. At the focus of an earthquake, the energy is released.
    2. The point on the surface nearest to the focus is called epicentre.
    3. Epicentre is the first one to experience the waves.
    4. Epicentre is a point directly below the focus.
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Epicentre is a point directly below the focus.

    The epicentre is the point on the surface directly above the focus (hypocentre), not below it.

  2. Screening 2024 · Q33 · Geography · Physical Geography

    Granite is a/an:

    1. Extrusive Igneous rock
    2. Intrusive Igneous rock
    3. Mechanically formed Sedimentary rock
    4. Mechanically formed Metamorphic rock
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Intrusive Igneous rock

    Granite forms when magma cools slowly deep below the surface, which produces large crystals. Basalt is the typical extrusive igneous rock.

  3. Screening 2024 · Q34 · Geography · Physical Geography

    Match List I (Landform) with List II (Geomorphic agent): a. Delta I. Waves and Currents b. Cirque II. Winds c. Cliffs III. Running water d. Sand Dunes IV. Glaciers

    1. a-I, b-II, c-III, d-IV
    2. a-II, b-I, c-IV, d-III
    3. a-III, b-I, c-IV, d-II
    4. a-III, b-IV, c-I, d-II
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) a-III, b-IV, c-I, d-II

    Deltas are deposited by rivers, cirques are carved by glaciers, sea cliffs are cut by waves and currents, and sand dunes are built by wind.

  4. Screening 2024 · Q35 · Geography · Physical Geography

    In which of the following layers of the atmosphere do meteorites burn up when they enter from space?

    1. Mesosphere
    2. Thermosphere
    3. Ionosphere
    4. Exosphere
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Mesosphere

    Most meteors burn up in the mesosphere, about 50–85 km above the surface, because of friction with air molecules.

  5. Screening 2024 · Q36 · Geography · Physical Geography

    Match the following on heating and cooling of the atmosphere (Process – Mechanism): a. Advection I. Horizontal heating b. Convection II. Vertical heating c. Insolation III. Direct heating d. Conduction IV. Heating the lower layers

    1. a-I, b-II, c-III, d-IV
    2. a-II, b-I, c-IV, d-III
    3. a-III, b-I, c-IV, d-II
    4. a-III, b-IV, c-I, d-II
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) a-I, b-II, c-III, d-IV

    Advection moves heat horizontally, convection moves it vertically, insolation is the direct heating by solar radiation, and conduction warms the air layers in contact with the ground.

  6. Screening 2024 · Q38 · Geography · Physical Geography

    Match List I (Ocean Bottom Relief) with List II (Characteristic feature): a. Continental Shelf I. Presence of canyons and trenches b. Continental Slope II. Associated with active volcanoes and strong earthquakes c. Deep Sea Plain III. Presence of massive sedimentary deposits d. Oceanic Deeps IV. Presence of fine-grained sediments

    1. a-I, b-II, c-III, d-IV
    2. a-II, b-I, c-IV, d-III
    3. a-III, b-I, c-IV, d-II
    4. a-III, b-IV, c-I, d-II
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) a-III, b-I, c-IV, d-II

    Continental shelves hold thick sediment deposits, slopes are cut by canyons and trenches, abyssal plains are covered with fine sediments, and oceanic deeps lie along plate boundaries with volcanoes and earthquakes.

  7. Screening 2024 · Q40 · Geography · Physical Geography

    Which of the following mountains are young fold mountains?

    1. The Appalachians
    2. The Aravalis
    3. The Alps
    4. The Urals
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) The Alps

    The Alps, like the Himalayas, are young fold mountains of the Alpine (Tertiary) orogeny. The Appalachians, Aravallis and Urals are old, worn-down ranges.

  8. Screening 2019 · Q14 · Geography · Physical Geography

    A study of mapping the sea floor is known as

    Show answer

    Answer: Bathymetry

    Bathymetry measures water depth and maps the shape of the ocean or lake floor, much as topography maps land.

  9. Screening 2019 · Q28 · Geography · Physical Geography

    Where is the 'International Tsunami Information Centre' located?

    Show answer

    Answer: Honolulu

    The ITIC was set up in 1965 by UNESCO's Intergovernmental Oceanographic Commission and is based in Honolulu, Hawaii.

  10. Mains 2019 · P-I · Q84 · Geography · Physical Geography

    Petrology is the

    Show answer

    Answer: Science of rocks

    Petrology studies the origin, composition and structure of rocks: igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic.

Indian Geography

  1. Screening 2024 · Q37 · Geography · Indian Geography

    Consider the following statements: I. Kavaratti is the largest city in Lakshadweep Islands. II. Nine Degree Channel separates Minicoy Island of Lakshadweep from Maldives. Which of the above statements is/are correct?

    1. Statement I only
    2. Statement II only
    3. Both of them
    4. None of them
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) None of them

    Kavaratti is the capital of Lakshadweep, not its largest settlement. Minicoy is separated from the Maldives by the Eight Degree Channel; the Nine Degree Channel separates Minicoy from the main Lakshadweep group.

  2. Screening 2024 · Q42 · Geography · Indian Geography

    The part of the Himalayas between the Satluj and Kali rivers is called:

    1. Punjab Himalayas
    2. Kashmir Himalayas
    3. Himachal Himalayas
    4. Kumaon Himalayas
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Kumaon Himalayas

    By the river-valley division, the Punjab Himalayas lie between the Indus and Satluj, the Kumaon Himalayas between the Satluj and Kali, the Nepal Himalayas between the Kali and Tista, and the Assam Himalayas between the Tista and Dihang.

  3. Screening 2024 · Q43 · Geography · Indian Geography

    The Eastern Ghats stretch from:

    1. The Mahanadi valley to Seshachalam hills
    2. Mahendragiri to Seshachalam hills
    3. Mahendragiri to the Nilgiris
    4. The Mahanadi valley to the Nilgiris
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) The Mahanadi valley to the Nilgiris

    The Eastern Ghats run as a broken chain from the Mahanadi valley in Odisha to the Nilgiris in Tamil Nadu, where they meet the Western Ghats.

  4. Screening 2024 · Q44 · Geography · Indian Geography

    Which of the following rivers forms the Dhuandhar waterfalls?

    1. The Mahanadi
    2. The Narmada
    3. The Indus
    4. The Brahmaputra
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) The Narmada

    The Narmada forms the Dhuandhar ('smoke cascade') falls at Bhedaghat near Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh, where it flows through marble rocks.

  5. Screening 2024 · Q45 · Geography · Indian Geography

    Pre-monsoon 'Mango Showers' are common in the coastal areas of:

    1. Karnataka
    2. Tamil Nadu
    3. Gujarat
    4. Andhra Pradesh
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Karnataka

    Mango showers are pre-monsoon thunderstorms in Kerala and coastal Karnataka that help mangoes ripen early.

  6. Screening 2024 · Q46 · Geography · Indian Geography

    In India, very deep black soils are found in:

    1. North-Western part of the Deccan Plateau
    2. Eastern part of the Deccan Plateau
    3. Southern part of the Deccan Plateau
    4. South-Eastern part of the Deccan Plateau
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) North-Western part of the Deccan Plateau

    Black soils formed from Deccan basalt are deepest in the north-western Deccan Plateau, in Maharashtra and parts of Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh.

  7. Screening 2024 · Q47 · Geography · Indian Geography

    The most widespread forests in India are:

    1. Tropical Evergreen forests
    2. Semi Evergreen forests
    3. Tropical Deciduous forests
    4. Temperate Deciduous forests
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Tropical Deciduous forests

    Tropical deciduous (monsoon) forests, both moist and dry, are the most widespread forests in India.

  8. Screening 2024 · Q52 · Geography · Indian Geography

    Match List I (Functional classification of town) with List II (Example): a. Mining town I. Jamshedpur b. Industrial town II. Ankleshwar c. Administrative town III. Udhampur d. Garrison Cantonment town IV. Gandhinagar

    1. a-I, b-II, c-III, d-IV
    2. a-II, b-I, c-IV, d-III
    3. a-IV, b-III, c-II, d-I
    4. a-III, b-IV, c-I, d-II
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) a-II, b-I, c-IV, d-III

    The NCERT examples are Ankleshwar (oil) as a mining town, Jamshedpur as an industrial town, Gandhinagar as an administrative town and Udhampur as a garrison town.

  9. Screening 2024 · Q54 · Geography · Indian Geography

    In which region in India was tea plantation started in the first half of the 19th century?

    1. Brahmaputra valley
    2. Darjeeling region
    3. The Nilgiris
    4. Cardamom hills
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Brahmaputra valley / (2) Darjeeling region

    The British began tea plantations in the Brahmaputra valley of Assam in the 1830s, and planting in Darjeeling followed soon after. The final key accepted both options 1 and 2.

  10. Screening 2024 · Q58 · Geography · Indian Geography

    Bilaspur is the headquarters of which of the following Railway zones?

    1. South Eastern
    2. East Coastal
    3. East Central
    4. South East Central
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) South East Central

    Bilaspur (Chhattisgarh) is the headquarters of the South East Central Railway. The South Eastern Railway is headquartered at Kolkata, East Coast at Bhubaneswar and East Central at Hajipur.

  11. Screening 2019 · Q33 · Geography · Indian Geography

    _______, with an elevation of 8,586 metres, is the highest peak in India.

    Show answer

    Answer: Kanchenjunga

    Kanchenjunga, on the Sikkim–Nepal border, is the third-highest mountain in the world and the highest peak in Indian-administered territory.

  12. Screening 2019 · Q34 · Geography · Indian Geography

    'Arwah' caves, known for their limestone formations and fossils, are located in which State of India?

    Show answer

    Answer: Meghalaya

    Arwah cave is near Sohra (Cherrapunji) in Meghalaya. Its limestone passages contain fossils of marine life.

  13. Screening 2019 · Q35 · Geography · Indian Geography

    Which Indian river is known as 'Tsangpo'?

    Show answer

    Answer: Brahmaputra

    The Brahmaputra is called the Yarlung Tsangpo in Tibet, the Siang or Dihang in Arunachal Pradesh, and the Jamuna in Bangladesh.

  14. Screening 2019 · Q36 · Geography · Indian Geography

    Which of the following States in India is NOT crossed by the Tropic of Cancer?

    Show answer

    Answer: Odisha

    The Tropic of Cancer passes through eight states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram. Odisha lies entirely south of it.

World Geography

  1. Screening 2024 · Q39 · Geography · World Geography

    Identify the correct arrangement of the following water bodies as per their salinity in decreasing order:

    1. Lake Van > Dead Sea > Great Salt Lake
    2. Dead Sea > Great Salt Lake > Lake Van
    3. Great Salt Lake > Dead Sea > Lake Van
    4. Dead Sea > Lake Van > Great Salt Lake
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Lake Van > Dead Sea > Great Salt Lake

    The NCERT values are Lake Van in Turkey about 330‰, the Dead Sea about 238‰ and the Great Salt Lake about 220‰.

  2. Screening 2024 · Q41 · Geography · World Geography

    Prairies are the temperate grasslands present in:

    1. The United States and Mexico
    2. The United States only
    3. The United States and Canada
    4. Canada and Alaska
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) The United States and Mexico / (3) The United States and Canada

    The prairies cover the interior plains of the United States and southern Canada, extending into northern Mexico. APPSC's final key accepted both options 1 and 3.

AP Geography

  1. Screening 2024 · Q48 · Geography · AP Geography

    Match List I (River) with List II (Origin): a. Vamsadhara I. Nallamala hills b. Machkund II. Nandidurga hills c. Gundlakamma III. Jayapur hills d. Pennar IV. Mudugal hills

    1. a-I, b-II, c-III, d-IV
    2. a-II, b-I, c-IV, d-III
    3. a-IV, b-III, c-II, d-I
    4. a-III, b-IV, c-I, d-II
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) a-III, b-IV, c-I, d-II

    The Vamsadhara rises in the Jayapur hills of Odisha, the Machkund in the Mudugal hills, the Gundlakamma in the Nallamala hills, and the Pennar (Penna) at Nandidurga in Karnataka.

  2. Screening 2024 · Q49 · Geography · AP Geography

    Identify the incorrect statement:

    1. The geographical extent of laterite soils in Andhra Pradesh is very limited.
    2. Laterites are formed in semi-arid and low rainfall regions.
    3. Laterites are less fertile and appear red and yellowish red.
    4. Laterites can be used to grow mango and cashewnut orchards.
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Laterites are formed in semi-arid and low rainfall regions.

    Laterite soils form under high temperature and heavy rainfall with alternating wet and dry seasons, which leach away silica and lime.

  3. Screening 2024 · Q59 · Geography · AP Geography

    Match the following on mineral resources of Andhra Pradesh (Mineral – Place in erstwhile districts): a. Manganese I. Forest areas of Visakhapatnam and East Godavari b. Bauxite II. Garividi in Vizianagaram c. Mica III. Jammalamadugu and Mydukuru in Kadapa region d. Limestone IV. Nellore region

    1. a-I, b-II, c-III, d-IV
    2. a-II, b-I, c-IV, d-III
    3. a-IV, b-III, c-II, d-I
    4. a-III, b-IV, c-I, d-II

    Answer: (2) a-II, b-I, c-IV, d-III

    Manganese is mined around Garividi, bauxite in the Eastern Ghats forests of Visakhapatnam and East Godavari, mica in the Nellore belt and limestone around Jammalamadugu and Mydukuru.

  5. Mains 2025 · P-I · Q29 · Geography · AP Geography

    The Eastern Ghats in Srikakulam are called

    1. Papi Kondalu
    2. Palakondalu
    3. Nallamala Kondalu
    4. Mahendra Girulu

  6. Mains 2025 · P-I · Q72 · Geography · AP Geography

    Telugu Ganga canal water is supplied to which of the following states?

    1. Telangana
    2. Karnataka
    3. Uttar Pradesh
    4. Tamil Nadu

  7. Screening 2019 · Q69 · Geography · AP Geography

    Which is the oldest land mass in Andhra Pradesh?

    Show answer

    Answer: Cuddapah Basin

    The crescent-shaped Cuddapah Basin of Proterozoic rocks is among the oldest geological formations of the State, and it hosts limestone, barytes and uranium deposits.

  8. Mains 2019 · P-I · Q112 · Geography · AP Geography

    Which soils spread in parts of Andhra Pradesh are the most important from the point of view of agricultural production?

    Show answer

    Answer: Deltaic Alluvial

    The fertile deltaic alluvium of the Godavari and Krishna deltas makes them the State's rice bowl.
