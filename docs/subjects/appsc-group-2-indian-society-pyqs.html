Social Structure

  1. Screening 2024 · Q50 · Indian Society · Social Structure

    Match List I (Scheduled Tribe) with List II (State/Union Territory): a. Gaddis I. Sikkim b. Jarawas II. Jharkhand c. Santhals III. Andaman and Nicobar Islands d. Bhutias IV. Himachal Pradesh

    1. a-I, b-II, c-III, d-IV
    2. a-II, b-I, c-IV, d-III
    3. a-IV, b-III, c-II, d-I
    4. a-III, b-IV, c-I, d-II
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) a-IV, b-III, c-II, d-I

    The Gaddis are pastoralists of Himachal Pradesh, the Jarawas live in the Andaman Islands, the Santhals are concentrated in Jharkhand and the Bhutias in Sikkim.

  2. Screening 2024 · Q62 · Indian Society · Social Structure

    A circular structure used by Zoroastrians for excarnation, the disposal of human dead bodies through exposure to the natural elements and scavenger animals such as vultures, is called:

    1. Dakhma
    2. Aparigraha
    3. Sallekhana
    4. Shenshai
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Dakhma

    A dakhma, or Tower of Silence, is where Parsis expose their dead. Aparigraha and Sallekhana are Jain concepts, and Shenshai is a Parsi calendar.

  3. Screening 2024 · Q63 · Indian Society · Social Structure

    'Diku Andi' means:

    1. Marriage by exchange in Koya and Savara tribes
    2. Marriage by capture in Naga tribes
    3. Marriage by probation in Kuki tribe
    4. Marriage by negotiation in Ho tribes
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Marriage by negotiation in Ho tribes

    Among the Ho of Jharkhand, 'andi' is the negotiated marriage arranged between families. The Kukis are known for marriage by probation and the Nagas for marriage by capture.

  4. Screening 2024 · Q65 · Indian Society · Social Structure

    Consider the following statements: I. W.P. Scott defined divorce as 'an institutionalised arrangement for terminating the marriage relationship and allowing each partner the right to remarry'. II. The first legislation in respect of the dissolution of marriage in India was enacted in 1869, and is the 'Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act'. III. The irresponsible departure from the home on the part of the husband, leaving the family to fend for itself, is called 'the poor man's divorce'. Which of the above given statements are correct?

    1. I and II only
    2. II and III only
    3. I and III only
    4. All of the above
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) I and III only

    Statement II is wrong: the 1869 law was the Indian Divorce Act, which applies to Christians. Desertion by the husband is described in sociology as 'the poor man's divorce'.

  5. Screening 2024 · Q66 · Indian Society · Social Structure

    Ravindra and Seema are husband and wife. Krishna and Madhavi are children of Ravindra–Seema. Madhavi is married to Harish and Jyothi is the daughter of Madhavi–Harish. Then the consanguineous kinship is between:

    1. Ravindra and Seema
    2. Madhavi and Harish
    3. Krishna and Jyothi
    4. Krishna and Harish
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Krishna and Jyothi

    Consanguineous kin are related by blood. Krishna is Jyothi's maternal uncle. The other pairs are related through marriage (affinal kinship).

  6. Screening 2024 · Q67 · Indian Society · Social Structure

    As per the recommendations of the Sachar Committee, which among the following Muslim groups needs additional attention, similar to that of Hindu OBCs?

    1. Ajlaf
    2. Arzal
    3. Ashraf
    4. Shaikh and Syed
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Ajlaf

    The Sachar Committee (2006) found the Ajlaf comparable to Hindu OBCs and the Arzal comparable to Scheduled Castes. The Ashraf (including Syeds and Shaikhs) are the upper strata.

  7. Screening 2024 · Q82 · Indian Society · Social Structure

    According to whom are 'the existence of strong personal attachments and the fact of death, which of all human events is the most upsetting and disorganizing to man's calculations, perhaps the main sources of religious belief'?

    1. Talcott Parsons
    2. Emile Durkheim
    3. Bronislaw Malinowski
    4. Simone de Beauvoir
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Bronislaw Malinowski

    Malinowski explained religion as a response to anxiety and crisis, death above all, which human reason and technique cannot control.

  8. Screening 2024 · Q85 · Indian Society · Social Structure

    A consensual and informal relationship practised in accordance with the matrilineal laws of Kerala that prevailed among Namboothiris and Nairs is known as:

    1. Sambandham
    2. Pakulam Pathiravum
    3. Pranchiyettan
    4. Udakapoorvam
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Sambandham

    Sambandham was a loose marital alliance under the marumakkathayam (matrilineal) system, often between Namboothiri men and Nair women. The Madras Marumakkathayam Act, 1933 regulated it.

  9. Screening 2024 · Q88 · Indian Society · Social Structure

    Which of the following was the custom practised during the ancient period that allowed married women to cohabit with a designated male for the sole purpose of procreating a child if her husband was impotent or had died?

    1. Apastamba
    2. Sikata Nivavari
    3. Pratigya
    4. Niyoga
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Niyoga

    Niyoga let a widow or the wife of an impotent man bear a child with an appointed man, usually a brother-in-law. The Dharmashastras discuss it.

  10. Screening 2019 · Q116 · Indian Society · Social Structure

    About 95% of Himachal Pradesh's population was Hindu in Census 2011, the highest share among states.

  3. Screening 2024 · Q74 · Indian Society · Social Issues

    After a landmark judgement on Triple Talaq in 2017, which among the following methods of divorce practised in Muslim society was recently challenged in the Apex Court of India?

    1. Talaq-e-Hasan
    2. Talaq-i-Tafweez
    3. Talaq-e-Ahsan
    4. Lian
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Talaq-e-Hasan

    After Shayara Bano (2017) struck down instant triple talaq, petitions filed in 2022 challenged talaq-e-hasan, in which talaq is pronounced once a month over three months.

  4. Screening 2024 · Q75 · Indian Society · Social Issues

    Which among the following IT initiatives by the Ministry of Home Affairs facilitates dissemination of information about significant crimes, including human trafficking, across the country on a real-time basis and enables interstate coordination?

    1. Investigation Tracking System for Sexual Offences (ITSSO)
    2. National Database of Sexual Offenders (NDSO)
    3. Crime Multi Agency Centre (Cri-MAC)
    4. Special Police Unit for Women and Children (SPUWAC)
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Crime Multi Agency Centre (Cri-MAC)

    Cri-MAC, run by the NCRB and launched in 2020, alerts police across states in real time about major crimes, including human trafficking.

  5. Screening 2024 · Q76 · Indian Society · Social Issues

    Who defined 'Communalism as the political assertiveness of a community to maintain its identity in a plural society undergoing modernization'?

    1. Prof. Ram Ahuja
    2. Bipin Chandra
    3. Majumdar and Madan
    4. Robert Melson and Howard Wolpe
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Robert Melson and Howard Wolpe

    Melson and Wolpe linked communalism to competition between communal groups in plural societies undergoing modernisation, a view cited in Indian sociology textbooks.

  6. Screening 2024 · Q77 · Indian Society · Social Issues

    The Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961 enacted by the Government of India came into force from:

    1. 20th May, 1961
    2. 1st June, 1961
    3. 20th June, 1961
    4. 1st July, 1961
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) 1st July, 1961

    The Act was passed in May 1961 and came into force on 1 July 1961. It penalises giving, taking and demanding dowry.

  7. Screening 2024 · Q78 · Indian Society · Social Issues

    Consider the following statements: I. In the 15th Lok Sabha the percentage of elected women members in Lok Sabha crossed 10% of its total strength for the first time. II. The 17th Lok Sabha has a higher percentage of representation of elected women members than before and it is the highest ever since independence. III. The women members elected to the Legislative Assembly of Andhra Pradesh in General Elections 2019 was 11·74% of its total strength. IV. The Women's Reservation Bill, 2023 got the assent of the President on 28th September, 2023. Which among the above given statements are correct?

    1. I, II and III only
    2. I, III and IV only
    3. I, II and IV only
    4. II, III and IV only
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) I, II and IV only

    In 2009, 59 women (about 11%) were elected to the 15th Lok Sabha, and in 2019, 78 (about 14%) to the 17th, a record at the time. The Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam received assent on 28 September 2023. Only about 8% of AP MLAs elected in 2019 were women.

  8. Screening 2024 · Q86 · Indian Society · Social Issues

    Recently the Delhi High Court upheld the constitutional validity of a provision of the Hindu Marriage Act which 'prohibits marriage between people who are related to each other through ancestors from their parent's side unless their customs allow them to do so'. The said provision is in which Section of the Hindu Marriage Act?

    1. Section 4(a)
    2. Section 5(v)
    3. Section 12(1)(a)
    4. Section 13(ii)
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Section 5(v)

    Section 5(v) bars marriage between sapindas unless custom permits it. The Delhi High Court upheld the provision in 2024.

Social Justice & Welfare

  1. Screening 2024 · Q64 · Indian Society · Social Justice & Welfare

    The online portal developed by the Government of India for effective enforcement of the Child and Adolescent Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act, 1986 (amended in 2016) is:

    1. SPARC
    2. SRIJAN
    3. YUKTI
    4. PENCIL
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) PENCIL

    PENCIL (Platform for Effective Enforcement for No Child Labour), launched in 2017, links central and state governments and district project societies to enforce the Act.

  2. Screening 2024 · Q68 · Indian Society · Social Justice & Welfare

    In line with the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act 2013, which of the following robots can be used to end India's manual scavenging?

    1. Kempa
    2. Vyommitra
    3. Bandicoot
    4. ManavSudh
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Bandicoot

    Bandicoot, built by the Kerala start-up Genrobotics, cleans manholes and sewers so that people do not have to enter them. Vyommitra is ISRO's humanoid robot for Gaganyaan.

  3. Screening 2024 · Q69 · Indian Society · Social Justice & Welfare

    In which of the following cases is it alleged that the verdict of the Supreme Court of India diluted the stringent provisions of the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989?

    1. K.C. Vasanth Kumar & Another vs State of Karnataka
    2. Srish Kumar Choudhury vs State of Tripura
    3. Om Prakash Mahajan vs State of Madhya Pradesh
    4. Subhash Kashinath Mahajan vs State of Maharashtra
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Subhash Kashinath Mahajan vs State of Maharashtra

    In Subhash Kashinath Mahajan (2018) the Court allowed anticipatory bail and a preliminary inquiry before arrest. Parliament reversed this with the 2018 amendment, which added Section 18A.

    Show answer
