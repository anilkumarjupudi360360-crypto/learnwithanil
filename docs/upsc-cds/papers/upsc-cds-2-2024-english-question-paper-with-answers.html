  1. Q1 · English · Synonyms & Antonyms

    Directions: Select the option that is opposite in meaning to the underlined word. The leader's views were equivocal on the contentious issue. (underlined: equivocal)

    1. obscure
    2. ambiguous
    3. clear
    4. vague
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) clear

    "Equivocal" means open to more than one interpretation or deliberately unclear, so its opposite is "clear"; obscure, ambiguous and vague are all near-synonyms.

  2. Q2 · English · Synonyms & Antonyms

    Directions: Select the option that is opposite in meaning to the underlined word. The authorities interacted with a group of mutinous students. (underlined: mutinous)

    1. unruly
    2. contumacious
    3. seditious
    4. obedient
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) obedient

    "Mutinous" means rebellious or refusing to obey authority, so "obedient" is its opposite; unruly, contumacious (stubbornly disobedient) and seditious are synonyms.

  8. Q8 · English · Fill in the Blanks

    Directions: Select the most appropriate choice to fill in the blank. Those medicines, ________ are used to treat kidney infection, have been withdrawn from the market.

    1. what
    2. which
    3. when
    4. who
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) which

    A non-defining relative clause about things (medicines) is introduced by "which"; "who" is for people and "what" cannot follow a noun as a relative pronoun.

  9. Q9 · English · Fill in the Blanks

    Directions: Select the most appropriate choice to fill in the blank. ________ intake of sugar can cause health problems.

    1. Access
    2. Excess of
    3. Excessive
    4. Excessively
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Excessive

    An adjective is needed to qualify the noun "intake", so "Excessive intake"; "Excessively" is an adverb and "Excess of intake" is unidiomatic.
