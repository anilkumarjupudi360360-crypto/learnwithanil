  1. Q1 · General Science · Biology

    A sprinter feels cramps and pain in the thigh muscles after a run. This is due to accumulation of

    1. lactic acid
    2. CO₂
    3. pyruvic acid
    4. ethanol
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) lactic acid

    During intense exercise muscles run short of oxygen and respire anaerobically, converting pyruvic acid into lactic acid; its build-up causes muscle cramps and pain. Ethanol is formed in anaerobic respiration of yeast, not human muscle.

  2. Q2 · General Science · Biology

    A leaf was plucked from a plant on a sunny day and kept for 2 minutes in boiling water. It was subsequently immersed in boiling alcohol and treated with iodine solution. What will be the final colour of the leaf after the test?

    1. Colourless
    2. Green
    3. Blue
    4. White
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Blue

    A leaf from a sunlit plant has made starch by photosynthesis. Boiling alcohol removes the chlorophyll, and iodine then turns the starch blue-black.

  3. Q3 · General Science · Biology

    If the red blood cells (RBCs) of human blood are isolated and are diluted in normal saline (an isotonic solution to blood), what will happen to the RBCs?

    1. The RBCs will swell
    2. The RBCs will swell and burst
    3. The RBCs will shrink
    4. No change in the diameters of the RBCs
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) No change in the diameters of the RBCs

    In an isotonic solution there is no net movement of water into or out of the cell, so RBCs keep their size. They would swell and burst in a hypotonic solution and shrink in a hypertonic one.

  4. Q4 · General Science · Biology

    Which one of the following statements regarding bile secreted by liver is not correct?

    1. Bile contains enzymes for digestion of lipids.
    2. Bile facilitates emulsification of fats.
    3. Bile neutralizes the acidic pH of the food coming from stomach.
    4. Bile makes the pH of the food alkaline and facilitates action of pancreatic enzymes.
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Bile contains enzymes for digestion of lipids.

    Bile contains no digestive enzymes; its bile salts only emulsify fats, and its alkalinity neutralizes the acidic food from the stomach. Fats are digested by lipase from the pancreas.

  5. Q5 · General Science · Biology

    The blood pressure of a normal human being is found to be 120/80 mmHg. These 'numbers' represent the blood pressure at

    1. ventricular contraction and ventricular relaxation, respectively
    2. ventricular relaxation and ventricular contraction, respectively
    3. auricular contraction and auricular relaxation, respectively
    4. ventricular contraction and auricular contraction, respectively
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) ventricular contraction and ventricular relaxation, respectively

    120 mmHg is the systolic pressure, when the ventricles contract. 80 mmHg is the diastolic pressure, when the ventricles relax.

  6. Q6 · General Science · Physics

    A person burned a firecracker in front of a cliff and heard its echo 5 s after it burst. The distance of the cliff from the person, if the speed of the sound is 340 m/s, is close to

    1. 1700 m
    2. 170 m
    3. 85 m
    4. 850 m
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) 850 m

    The sound travels to the cliff and back, so 2d = 340 × 5 = 1700 m, which gives d = 850 m.

  9. Q9 · General Science · Physics

    The area under the velocity-time graph for a particle moving in a straight line with uniform acceleration gives

    1. its average velocity
    2. its net displacement
    3. the distance travelled by it
    4. its average speed
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) its net displacement

    The area under a velocity-time graph equals the displacement, with areas below the time axis counted as negative. Distance would need the area under the speed-time graph, with all parts counted as positive.
