  1. Q1 · Arithmetic · Number System

    What is the largest number which divides both 2³⁵ − 1 and 2⁹¹ − 1?

    1. 34
    2. 90
    3. 127
    4. 129
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 127

    gcd(2ᵐ − 1, 2ⁿ − 1) = 2^gcd(m, n) − 1. Here gcd(35, 91) = 7, so the HCF is 2⁷ − 1 = 127.

  2. Q2 · Arithmetic · Number System

    What is the largest power of 10 that divides the product 29 × 28 × 27 × . . . 2 × 1?

    1. 4
    2. 5
    3. 6
    4. 7
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 6

    The product is 29!, and the power of 10 is fixed by the number of 5s: ⌊29/5⌋ + ⌊29/25⌋ = 5 + 1 = 6. There are plenty of 2s, so 10⁶ is the largest power.

  4. Q4 · Algebra · Polynomials & Quadratics

    If the roots of the equation x² − bx + c = 5 differ by 5, then which one of the following is correct?

    1. b² = 4c + 5
    2. c² = 4b − 5
    3. b² + c² = 5
    4. b² − c² = 5
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) b² = 4c + 5

    The equation is x² − bx + (c − 5) = 0, so (difference of roots)² = b² − 4(c − 5) = 25. This gives b² − 4c + 20 = 25, i.e. b² = 4c + 5.

  5. Q5 · Algebra · Sets, Logarithms & Inequalities

    In a party of 150 persons, 75 persons take tea, 60 persons take coffee and 50 persons take milk. 15 of them take both tea and coffee, but no one taking milk takes tea. If each person in the party takes at least one drink, then what is the number of persons taking milk only?

    1. 50
    2. 40
    3. 30
    4. 20
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 30

    Since nobody takes both milk and tea, 150 = 75 + 60 + 50 − 15 − n(coffee ∩ milk), so n(coffee ∩ milk) = 20. Milk only = 50 − 20 = 30.

  6. Q6 · Arithmetic · Ratio, Time & Work

    A, B, C, D and E enter into a business. They invest money in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4 : 5 : 6. However, the time invested by them is in the ratio 6 : 5 : 4 : 3 : 2. If the profit distributed is directly proportional to time and money invested, then who receives the highest amount of profit?

    1. C
    2. Both B and D
    3. Both C and D
    4. All get equal profit
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) C

    Profit shares are money × time: 2×6 = 12, 3×5 = 15, 4×4 = 16, 5×3 = 15, 6×2 = 12. C has the largest product (16).

  7. Q7 · Arithmetic · Number System

    Consider the following numbers: 1. 437 2. 797 3. 1073 How many of the above numbers are prime?

    1. Only one
    2. Only two
    3. All three
    4. None
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Only one

    437 = 19 × 23 and 1073 = 29 × 37 are composite. 797 has no prime factor up to √797 ≈ 28.2, so it is prime. Only one number is prime.

  8. Q8 · Arithmetic · Ratio, Time & Work

    A can do a certain work at twice the speed of B. Further, B can do the same work at 1·5 times the speed of C. All of them together can finish the work in 12 days. In how many days can C alone finish the work?

    1. 36 days
    2. 45 days
    3. 60 days
    4. 66 days
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) 66 days

    If C's rate is c, then B's rate is 1·5c and A's is 3c, so together they do 5·5c = 1/12 of the work per day. That gives c = 1/66, so C alone takes 66 days.

  9. Q9 · Arithmetic · Number System

    The sum of digits of a 2-digit number is 12. When the digits are reversed, the number becomes greater by eighteen. What is the difference between the digits in the number?

    1. 1
    2. 2
    3. 3
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) 2

    For the number 10a + b, reversing gives 9(b − a) = 18, so b − a = 2. With a + b = 12 the number is 57, and the digits differ by 2.

  10. Q10 · Arithmetic · Speed & Distance

    The time taken by a train to cross a man travelling in another train is 10 seconds, when the other train is travelling in the opposite direction. However, it takes 20 seconds, if both the trains are travelling in the same direction. The length of the first train is 200 m and that of the second train is 150 m. What is the speed of the first train?

    1. 60 km/hr
    2. 56 km/hr
    3. 54 km/hr
    4. 52 km/hr
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 54 km/hr

    To pass a man, the first train covers only its own 200 m: u + v = 200/10 = 20 m/s and u − v = 200/20 = 10 m/s. So u = 15 m/s = 54 km/hr. The 150 m length of the second train plays no part.
