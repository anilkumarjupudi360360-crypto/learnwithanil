  1. Q1 · English · Synonyms & Antonyms

    Directions: In the following items, a pair of words is provided. You are required to select the option that most appropriately describes the meaning of both the words and mark your response on the Answer Sheet accordingly. Presumptive and Presumptuous

    1. Presumptive means something likely to be true, and Presumptuous means to be disrespectfully bold
    2. Presumptive means to be disrespectfully bold, and Presumptuous means something likely to be true
    3. Presumptive means to exist, and Presumptuous means something very important
    4. Presumptive means something very important, and Presumptuous means to exist
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Presumptive means something likely to be true, and Presumptuous means to be disrespectfully bold

    "Presumptive" means based on a reasonable assumption, i.e. probably true (e.g. heir presumptive); "presumptuous" means overstepping limits, impertinently bold.
