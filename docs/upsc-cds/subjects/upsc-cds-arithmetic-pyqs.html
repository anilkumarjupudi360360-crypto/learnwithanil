  2. CDS I 2025 · Maths · Q5 · Arithmetic · Number System

    Let p be the remainder when 7⁸⁴ is divided by 342 and q be the remainder when 7⁸⁴ is divided by 344. What is (p − q) equal to?

    1. 0
    2. 1
    3. 2
    4. 6
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) 0

    7³ = 343 = 342 + 1 = 344 − 1. So 7⁸⁴ = (343)²⁸ ≡ 1²⁸ = 1 (mod 342) and ≡ (−1)²⁸ = 1 (mod 344). Hence p = q = 1 and p − q = 0.
