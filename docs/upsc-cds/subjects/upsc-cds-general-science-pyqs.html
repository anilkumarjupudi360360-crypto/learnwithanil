  23. CDS II 2023 · GK · Q3 · General Science · Physics

    A concave mirror of radius of curvature 50 cm is used to form an image of an object kept at a distance of 25 cm from the mirror on its principal axis. What will be the position of the image from the mirror?

    1. At infinity
    2. At 50 cm
    3. At 25 cm
    4. At 75 cm
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) At infinity

    The focal length is f = R/2 = 25 cm, so the object is at the focus. The reflected rays come out parallel and the image forms at infinity (1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/25 − 1/25 = 0).
