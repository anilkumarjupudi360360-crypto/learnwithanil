RPF SI 2026: next recruitment through the SSC

There is no RPF Sub-Inspector notification for 2026 yet. From the next cycle the Staff Selection Commission (SSC), not the Railway Recruitment Boards, conducts the recruitment, under the Railway Protection Force (Amendment) Rules, 2025. Below: the status, what changes, the facts of the last RRB exam (CEN RPF 01/2024), its pattern, and its question papers with answers.

What changes with the SSC

  • The Railway Protection Force (Amendment) Rules, 2025 (G.S.R. 766(E), 17 October 2025) say direct recruitment of Sub-Inspectors and Constables (Executive) will follow the Staff Selection Commission's recruitment procedure for the Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs). Earlier the Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) held it.
  • Constable (Executive): above 18 and below 23 years on the date of the notification, and a pass in the High School (10th) examination or equivalent (new rule 48.3).
  • Physical standards for the executive cadre: height 170 cm, chest 80 cm unexpanded and 85 cm expanded; women and reserved categories as per the CAPF standards. The medical examination is done by CAPF medical officers or government doctors.
  • The SSC has not yet issued an RPF notification or announced the new exam pattern (as of 15 November 2026). Until it does, the RRB papers below are the best guide to the level and the topics.

Quick answers (as of 15 November 2026)

Is the RPF SI 2026 notification out?

No, not as of 15 November 2026. From the next cycle the SSC conducts RPF recruitment; neither the SSC nor the RPF has issued a notice yet.

Who will conduct the next RPF SI exam?

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC). The RPF (Amendment) Rules, 2025, notified on 17 October 2025, say selection of Sub-Inspectors and Constables (Executive) follows the SSC's procedure for the Central Armed Police Forces.

Will the RPF SI exam pattern change?

Probably, because the SSC will follow its CAPF procedure, but the SSC has not published the new scheme yet. The last RPF SI exam (RRB) had 120 questions in 90 minutes: General Awareness 50, Arithmetic 35 and Reasoning 35.

How many posts were there in the last RPF SI recruitment?

452 Sub-Inspector (Executive) posts under CEN RPF 01/2024. The final result came on 26 August 2025.

What was the RPF SI age limit?

20 to 28 years on 1 July 2024 under CEN RPF 01/2024, with the usual relaxations for reserved categories. The next notice will give the new dates.

What is the RPF SI salary?

Pay Level 6 of the 7th Pay Commission, initial basic pay ₹35,400 a month, plus allowances.

Is there negative marking in RPF SI?

Yes. In the RRB CBT one-third of a mark was cut for each wrong answer.
