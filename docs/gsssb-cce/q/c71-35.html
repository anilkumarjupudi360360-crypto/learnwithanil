    The line chart given below shows the number of cars sold by six companies. What are the ratio of car sold by B and A together to the car sold by E and F together?

    1. 4 : 3
    2. 3 : 4
    3. 1 : 2
    4. 2 : 1

    1. 4 : 3
    2. 3 : 4
    3. 1 : 2
    4. 2 : 1
