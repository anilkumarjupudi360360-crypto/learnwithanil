  1. Q1 · Physical & General Science · Life Science & Health

    Find the similarity in the following common cold, ringworm, conjunctivities, whooping cough.

    1. All of them are non-communicable diseases
    2. All of them do not respond to curative treatment
    3. All of them are contagious
    4. No preventive measure is available for all of them
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) All of them are contagious

    Common cold (virus), ringworm (fungus), conjunctivitis (virus/bacteria) and whooping cough (bacterium Bordetella pertussis) all spread from person to person by contact or droplets, so all are contagious. They are communicable, they can be treated, and whooping cough is prevented by the DPT vaccine.

  2. Q2 · Physical & General Science · Life Science & Health

    Thyroid gland is situated in our

    1. Liver
    2. Neck
    3. Arm-pits
    4. None of the above
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Neck

    The thyroid is a butterfly-shaped endocrine gland in the front of the neck, wrapped around the trachea just below the larynx. It secretes thyroxine, which controls the body's metabolic rate.

    Who discovered ‘Boson’ particle?

    1. Meghnad Saha
    2. Satyendra Nath Bose
    3. C. V. Raman
    4. J. C. Bose
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Satyendra Nath Bose

    Bosons are named after Satyendra Nath Bose, whose 1924 work on the statistics of photons (Bose–Einstein statistics) describes this class of particles with whole-number spin, such as photons and the Higgs boson.

  4. Q4 · Mental Ability & Arithmetic · Arithmetic

    If the length of a rectangle is increased by 20% and the breadth is reduced by 20%, what will be the effect on its area?

    1. 4% increase
    2. 6% increase
    3. 4% decrease
    4. None of the above
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 4% decrease

    New area = 1.2L × 0.8B = 0.96 LB, i.e. 4% less. Shortcut: net change = x − y − xy/100 = 20 − 20 − (20×20)/100 = −4%, a 4% decrease.

  9. Q9 · Mental Ability & Arithmetic · Arithmetic

    P and Q can do a work in 12 days, Q and R in 15 days, R and P in 20 days. Find in what time P will do it alone?

    1. 30 days
    2. 20 days
    3. 24 days
    4. 60 days
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) 30 days

    Adding the three pairs: 2(P + Q + R) = 1/12 + 1/15 + 1/20 = 12/60 = 1/5, so P + Q + R = 1/10 per day. P = 1/10 − (Q + R) = 1/10 − 1/15 = 1/30, so P alone takes 30 days.
