RRB NTPC 2026: 5,236 posts, applications closed, CBT date awaited

The Railway Recruitment Boards notified 5,236 NTPC posts in 2026: 3,548 for graduates (CEN 06/2026) and 1,688 for Class 12 pass candidates (CEN 07/2026). Both application windows have closed; the correction windows run in November 2026 and the CBT-1 date is still to be announced. Below: dates, posts, eligibility, pattern and the NTPC previous papers.

Who could apply

  • Graduate posts: a degree; age 18–33 years on 1 January 2027. Undergraduate posts: Class 12 (10+2); age 18–30 years on 1 January 2027.
  • Age relaxation as in earlier cycles: OBC (NCL) 3 years, SC/ST 5 years, PwBD 10 years and more, ex-servicemen by service.
  • Fee ₹500 (₹400 refunded after you sit CBT-1); SC, ST, ex-servicemen, PwBD, women, transgender, minority and EBC candidates pay ₹250 (fully refunded after CBT-1).

Quick answers (as of 15 November 2026)

Is the RRB NTPC 2026 last date extended?

For graduates (CEN 06/2026), the last date of 6 November 2026 was reported extended to 8 November 2026. The undergraduate window (CEN 07/2026) closed on 13 November 2026. Both are closed now; only the correction windows remain (graduate 9–18 November, undergraduate 16–25 November 2026).

When is the RRB NTPC 2026 exam date?

RRB has not announced the CBT-1 dates as of 15 November 2026. One report says the graduate CBT-1 is tentatively in the fourth week of December 2026, but RRB has not confirmed it. The exam city slip comes about 10 days before the exam.

How many vacancies are there in RRB NTPC 2026?

5,236: 3,548 graduate-level posts (CEN 06/2026) and 1,688 undergraduate posts (CEN 07/2026). Goods Train Manager has the most, 2,750.

What is the RRB NTPC age limit for 2026?

18–33 years for graduate posts and 18–30 years for undergraduate posts, on 1 January 2027, with the usual relaxations (OBC 3, SC/ST 5 years).

Is there negative marking in RRB NTPC?

Yes. One-third of a mark is cut for every wrong answer in both CBT-1 and CBT-2. Unanswered questions get zero.

Do CBT-1 marks count in the final merit?

No. CBT-1 only shortlists candidates for CBT-2; the merit list is drawn from CBT-2 marks (and the typing or aptitude test where a post needs it).

Can a Class 12 pass candidate apply for graduate posts?

No. Graduate posts (CEN 06/2026) need a degree. Class 12 pass candidates apply under CEN 07/2026 for Commercial cum Ticket Clerk, Accounts Clerk cum Typist, Junior Clerk cum Typist and Trains Clerk.
