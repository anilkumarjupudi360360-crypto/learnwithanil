GSSSB CCE 2.0 notification 2026: 1,237 posts, apply by 30 November

The Gujarat Subordinate Service Selection Board (GSSSB) has advertised its second Combined Competitive Examination under the new rules, "CCE 2.0" (Advt. No. 468/2026-27), for 1,237 Class-III posts: 243 Group-A posts in eight cadres and 994 Junior Clerk posts in Group-B. Online applications on ojas.gujarat.gov.in are open from 5 November (2 pm) to 30 November 2026 (11.59 pm). Below: the key dates, the posts, who can apply, the exam pattern and the GSSSB CCE previous papers to practise.

Exam pattern

StagePaperMarks
Preliminary (Group-A and Group-B together)150 objective questions in 120 minutes: Reasoning 60, Quantitative Aptitude 30, General Awareness and Current Affairs 30, English 15, Gujarati 15; 0.25 mark deducted for each wrong answer150
Main exam, Group-A (descriptive)Paper I Gujarati Language Skill (100), Paper II English Language Skill (100), Paper III General Studies (150), 3 hours each350
Main exam, Group-B (objective)120 minutes: Constitution 20, Economy 20, Public Administration 20, Ethics 10, Government Schemes 30, Current Affairs 30, History & Geography of Gujarat 20, Cultural Heritage of Gujarat 10, Gujarati & English 20, Science, Environment & IT 20200

The prelims is only a screening test: its marks are not counted for the final list. Group-A and Group-B have separate lists, and about seven times the posts in each category are called to the main exam. The minimum qualifying mark is 40% in each stage. GSSSB's earlier CCE prelims (Advt. 212/2023-24, held in April–May 2024) had 100 questions in 60 minutes; the papers on this site are from that exam.

Who can apply

  • Indian citizen with a graduate degree from a recognised university, a basic knowledge of computers as in the Gujarat Civil Services rules of 1967, and an adequate knowledge of Gujarati or Hindi or both.
  • Age as on 30 November 2026: 20 to 35 years. Relaxation: 5 years for women of the general category and for men of the reserved categories, 10 years for women of the reserved categories and for men with disabilities, up to an upper age of 45; ex-servicemen get their service period plus 3 years.
  • Prelims fee: Rs. 500 for the unreserved category; Rs. 400 for all women, SEBC, SC, ST, EWS, persons with disabilities and ex-servicemen. GSSSB refunds the fee to candidates who appear in the exam.

Quick answers (as of 16 November 2026)

What is the last date to apply for GSSSB CCE 2.0?

30 November 2026 (11.59 pm) on ojas.gujarat.gov.in; the fee can be paid online till 2 December 2026.

How many posts are there in GSSSB CCE 2.0?

1,237: 243 Group-A posts (Senior Clerk 51, Social Welfare Inspector 89, Assistant Tribal Development Officer 50 and others) and 994 Junior Clerk posts in Group-B.

What is the GSSSB CCE 2.0 prelims exam pattern?

150 objective questions for 150 marks in 120 minutes: Reasoning 60, Quantitative Aptitude 30, General Awareness and Current Affairs 30, English 15 and Gujarati 15, with 0.25 mark cut for each wrong answer.

What is the age limit for GSSSB CCE 2.0?

20 to 35 years as on 30 November 2026, with relaxations for women, reserved categories, persons with disabilities and ex-servicemen up to an upper age of 45.

What is the GSSSB CCE application fee?

Rs. 500 for the unreserved category and Rs. 400 for women, SEBC, SC, ST, EWS, persons with disabilities and ex-servicemen; it is refunded to those who appear in the exam.

When is the GSSSB CCE 2.0 exam date?

GSSSB has not announced the prelims date yet; it will be on gsssb.gujarat.gov.in.
