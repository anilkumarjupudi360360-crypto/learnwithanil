    Study the Bar Chart given below to answer the question. Sale of the products of three different brands in five (2001-2005) different years (in Lakh pieces) What is the ratio between increase of sales of LIKE from 2001 to 2002 and decrease of sales of BOB from 2003 to 2004?

    1. 1 : 2
    2. 2 : 1
    3. 2 : 3
    4. 3 : 2

    1. 1 : 2
    2. 2 : 1
    3. 2 : 3
    4. 3 : 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) 1 : 2
