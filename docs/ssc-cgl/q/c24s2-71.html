    The given chart shows the production of different types of cars in the year 2021. Study the given chart and answer the question that follows. If the total production in the year 2021 was 2,00,00,000, then what will be the central angle of the sector representing the production of type D cars in 2021?

    1. 50°
    2. 42°
    3. 36°
    4. 72°
