    The question consists of a set of three figures X, Y and Z showing a sequence of folding of a piece of paper. Figure Z shows the manner in which the folded paper has been cut. Choose a figure which would most closely resemble the unfolded form of figure Z.

    1. A
    2. C
    3. D
    4. B

    1. A
    2. C
    3. D
    4. B
