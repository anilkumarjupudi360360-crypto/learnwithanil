In the screening test one-third of a mark is cut for each wrong answer, and its marks only decide who goes to the Mains (about 50 candidates per post). Final merit is out of 825 (Mains 750 + interview 75). The numbers below are the questions APPSC asked from each part in the 2019, 2023 and 2024 screening tests on this site.

Paper I: General Studies

120 questions · 120 marks · 120 minutes · 347 previous questions on this site

A. History and Culture (30 marks)

89 PYQs: Ancient India 19 · Medieval India 27 · Modern India 31 · Art & Culture 7 · Andhra History 5 · Practise: History & Culture questions

  1. Ancient India: Indus Valley, Vedic age and Mahajanapadas, Jainism and Buddhism, the Mauryas, foreign invasions, the Kushans, Satavahanas, Sangam age, Sungas and Guptas
  2. Early medieval and South India: Kanauj, the Badami and Eastern Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas, Kalyani Chalukyas, Cholas, Hoysalas, Yadavas, Kakatiyas and Reddis
  3. Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara and the Mughals; Bhakti and Sufi movements; administration, society, literature, art and architecture
  4. European trading companies and their struggle for supremacy (Bengal, Bombay, Madras, Mysore, Andhra, the Nizam); Governors-General and Viceroys
  5. The revolt of 1857; 19th-century religious and social reform movements; the freedom movement and revolutionaries in India and abroad
  6. Gandhi, Patel, Subhas Bose and Ambedkar; post-independence consolidation and the reorganisation of states

B. Constitution, Polity, Social Justice and International Relations (30 marks)

86 PYQs: Constitution Basics & Preamble 8 · Fundamental Rights & Duties 8 · DPSP 3 · Union Executive 7 · Parliament 8 · State Government 6 · Judiciary 4 · Federalism & Centre-State Relations 8 · Local Government 4 · Constitutional & Statutory Bodies 18 · Amendments & Governance 5 · Social Justice & Welfare 3 · International Relations 4 · Practise: Polity, Social Justice & IR questions

  1. The Constitution: evolution, features, Preamble, Fundamental Rights and Duties, Directive Principles, amendments and the basic structure
  2. Union and states; Parliament and state legislatures; issues of the federal structure; devolution of powers and finances to local bodies
  3. Constitutional authorities; Panchayati Raj; public policy and governance
  4. Liberalisation, privatisation and globalisation and governance; statutory, regulatory and quasi-judicial bodies
  5. Rights issues: human rights, women, SC and ST, children
  6. India's foreign policy and international relations; important institutions and agencies; major central and state government schemes

C. Indian and Andhra Pradesh Economy and Planning (30 marks)

87 PYQs: Planning & Development 9 · National Income & Growth 10 · Money, Banking & Inflation 17 · Public Finance & Taxation 4 · Agriculture 5 · Industry & Infrastructure 9 · Poverty, Employment & Population 9 · External Sector 3 · AP Economy 21 · Practise: Indian & AP Economy questions

  1. Features of the Indian economy, NITI Aayog, human development, environment and sustainable development
  2. National income, demography, poverty and inequality, unemployment, employment and poverty schemes, rural and urban development
  3. Agriculture, irrigation, land reforms, agricultural credit and MSP, food security; industry and industrial policy, Make in India, SEZs and corridors; energy; trade, balance of payments and the WTO
  4. RBI and monetary policy, banking reforms and NPAs, markets and SEBI; taxes and GST; Centre-state finances and Finance Commissions; public debt, fiscal policy and the budget
  5. The Andhra Pradesh economy after 2014: urbanisation, skills and welfare; the AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 and the special-status question

D. Geography (30 marks)

85 PYQs: Physical Geography 24 · World Geography 23 · Indian Geography 27 · AP Geography 6 · Environment & Ecology 5 · Practise: Geography questions

  1. General geography: the solar system, the earth's motions and interior, the atmosphere, climate change and oceans
  2. Physical geography of the world, India and AP: earthquakes and landslides, drainage, monsoon, vegetation, parks and sanctuaries, soils, rocks and minerals
  3. Social geography: population, density, sex ratio, literacy, SC and ST population, urbanisation and migration
  4. Economic geography: sectors of the economy, basic industries, transport and trade

Paper II: General Aptitude

120 questions · 120 marks · 120 minutes · 339 previous questions on this site

A. General Mental Ability, Administrative and Psychological Abilities (60 marks)

164 PYQs: Numbers & Series 26 · Reasoning 43 · Arithmetic 70 · Data Interpretation & Sufficiency 7 · Administrative Abilities 16 · Psychological Abilities 2 · Practise: Mental Ability & Reasoning questions, Administrative & Psychological Abilities questions

  1. Reasoning: logical and analytical reasoning, number series, coding-decoding, relations, Venn diagrams, clocks, calendars and ages
  2. Arithmetic: number system, ratio and proportion, averages (mean, median, mode), powers and roots, HCF and LCM, percentages, interest, profit and loss, time and work, time and distance
  3. Mensuration and geometry; basic algebra and simplification; data interpretation, data sufficiency and probability
  4. Emotional and social intelligence, interpersonal skills, decision making, critical thinking, problem solving and personality

B. Science and Technology (30 marks)

87 PYQs: General Science 15 · Space Technology 15 · Defence Technology 7 · Biotechnology & Health 10 · IT & Communication 12 · Energy 11 · Environment & Ecology 16 · Agriculture & Food 1 · Practise: Science & Technology questions

  1. Science and technology policy, institutions and Indian scientists
  2. Information and communication technology, e-governance and cyber security
  3. Space and defence: ISRO and satellite programmes (communication, IRNSS, remote sensing), DRDO
  4. Energy: India's needs and resources, energy policy, solar, wind and nuclear power
  5. Environment: biodiversity, climate change, forest and wildlife law, pollution, disaster management; biotechnology, nanotechnology and genetic engineering; health and environment

C. Current Affairs (30 marks)

88 PYQs: National 34 · International 21 · AP Affairs 14 · Sports 4 · Awards & Personalities 8 · Reports & Indices 7 · Practise: Current Affairs questions

  1. Current events of regional, national and international importance

Mains syllabus at a glance

Seven descriptive papers of 150 marks and 3 hours each. Telugu and English (Class X standard) only have to be passed; Papers I to V count for the merit.

  • Telugu (qualifying)
  • English (qualifying)
  • Paper I: General Essay (three essays, one from each section) · 2025 questions with model answers
  • Paper II: History, Culture and Geography of India and Andhra Pradesh · 2025 questions with model answers
  • Paper III: Polity, Constitution, Governance, Law and Ethics · 2025 questions with model answers
  • Paper IV: Economy and Development of India and Andhra Pradesh · 2025 questions with model answers
  • Paper V: Science, Technology and Environmental Issues · 2025 questions with model answers

Every Group 1 Mains question of 2020, 2023 and 2025, paper by paper and topic by topic, with model answers

How to use this syllabus

  • Paper I sections carry 30 marks each, so no section can be skipped; start with the topics that have the most previous questions above.
  • Half of Paper II (60 marks) is mental ability and administrative and psychological abilities: practise it daily with a timer, it is the easiest place to gain marks.
  • Science and technology and current affairs move with the news: read the last 12 months of AP and national developments with the static topics.
  • Take the 2024, 2023 and 2019 papers as full timed tests (120 questions in 120 minutes) with the one-third negative mark in mind.
