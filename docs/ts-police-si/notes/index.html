Frequently asked questions

Where can I get TS Police SI previous year question papers with answers?

This page has 191 questions from the SI Preliminary Written Test held by TSLPRB on 7 August 2022 (booklet A), in English and Telugu as printed. Every answer is taken from TSLPRB's final key, released on 21 October 2022, and each question has a short explanation. You can read the paper with answers, practise it as a quiz or download the PDF, free.

What is the TS Police SI Preliminary Written Test pattern?

The SI Preliminary Written Test is one objective paper of 200 questions for 200 marks in 3 hours: Arithmetic and Test of Reasoning / Mental Ability, and General Studies. The qualifying marks are 30% for OC, 25% for BC and 20% for SC, ST and ex-servicemen. Qualified candidates take the physical tests (PMT/PET) and then the Final Written Examination.

When is the 2026 Preliminary Written Test?

TGPRB has tentatively scheduled the SI Preliminary Written Test for 29 November 2026 (forenoon). Dates can change, so confirm on tgprb.in. The 2022 paper on this page follows the same pattern.

Are the TS Police SI questions available in Telugu?

Yes. Every question that the booklet printed in Telugu is here exactly as printed. Tap తెలుగు at the top of the page to practise in Telugu.

Is there a free TS Police SI mock test?

Yes. Tap Create a quiz, choose subjects or topics, and pick Timed test: you get a free mock test made of real TSLPRB questions, with your score and a topic-wise analysis at the end. Practice mode works like practice bits, one question at a time with the answer and explanation.

Do I need to register?

Only once, and it is free. Before your first quiz or test you enter your name, phone, email and district one time; after that every quiz and test on every exam opens straight away on that phone or computer. There is no password.

Which questions are left out?

Questions that TSLPRB deleted in the final key, and questions for which the final key accepted three or four options, are left out. Where the key accepted two options, both are shown as correct, and where it also gave the mark for leaving a question blank, the explanation says so.
