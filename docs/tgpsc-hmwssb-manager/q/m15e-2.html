    The voltage across 5 A source in the given circuit is

    1. 17.5 volt
    2. 25 volt
    3. 15 volt
    4. 20 volt
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) 17.5 volt

    Take the bottom wire as reference and let V be the top-right node (the node shared by the shunt 5 Ω and the 5 A source). KCL at that node: (V − 10)/5 + V/5 = 5 → 2V − 10 = 25 → V = 17.5 V, which is the voltage across the 5 A source.
