Grammar

  1. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q121 · English · Grammar

    My mother is ______ English teacher at a University.

    1. an
    2. the
    3. a
    4. are
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) an

    "English" begins with a vowel sound, so the indefinite article "an" is used: "an English teacher". "A" is used only before consonant sounds, and "the" would wrongly suggest a teacher already identified.

  2. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q122 · English · Grammar

    Every day before sleeping, I turn off ______ lights in my room.

    1. an
    2. the
    3. a
    4. are
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) the

    The lights are specific ones (those in my room) and the noun is plural, so the definite article "the" is needed; "a" and "an" cannot go with a plural noun.

  3. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q123 · English · Grammar

    Moving to a foreign country is not ______ easy thing to do. You have to complete many formalities before that.

    1. an
    2. the
    3. a
    4. are
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) an

    The article comes before "easy", which begins with a vowel sound, so "an easy thing" is correct.

  4. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q124 · English · Grammar

    Though the ______ is relevant, we need to consider all possible aspects.

    1. information
    2. inform
    3. informations
    4. informing
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) information

    After "the" a noun is needed, and "information" is an uncountable noun with no plural form, so "informations" is wrong; "inform" and "informing" are verb forms.

  5. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q125 · English · Grammar

    Nowadays, a large number of ______ are getting stressed out easily.

    1. Person
    2. Peoples
    3. People
    4. Personing
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) People

    "A large number of" needs a plural noun, and the plural of "person" in this sense is "people"; "peoples" means different nations or races, which does not fit here.

  6. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q126 · English · Grammar

    We do not have a ______ of time, so let's discuss this quickly.

    1. lot
    2. much
    3. enough
    4. few
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) lot

    The fixed phrase is "a lot of time". "A much of" and "a enough of" are not English, and "few" is used only with countable nouns, so it cannot go with "time".

  7. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q127 · English · Grammar

    You must ______ Golconda. It is an interesting historical monument.

    1. visit
    2. be visiting
    3. is visiting
    4. visited
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) visit

    A modal verb such as "must" is followed by the base form of the verb, so "You must visit Golconda" is correct.

  8. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q128 · English · Grammar

    Raghav is not ______ the meeting today because he has a medical emergency at home.

    1. attend
    2. attended
    3. is attending
    4. attending
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) attending

    "Is not attending" is the present continuous used for a fixed plan; after the auxiliary "is" only the -ing form fits, and "is attending" would repeat "is".

  9. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q129 · English · Grammar

    You must visit the handicrafts shop at the end of this road. It ______ an excellent variety of gift items.

    1. had
    2. has
    3. was
    4. have
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) has

    The subject "It" (the shop) is third person singular and the sentence describes a present fact, so the simple present "has" is correct; "have" does not agree with "it" and "had" is past tense.

  10. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q130 · English · Grammar

    I ______ think the team will win the game tonight.

    1. don't
    2. doesn't
    3. are
    4. am
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) don't

    With the subject "I" the negative of the simple present is "do not (don't) + base verb": "I don't think ...". "Doesn't" is used only with he, she or it.

  11. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q131 · English · Grammar

    Gymnastics ______ a very difficult sport.

    1. am
    2. is
    3. were
    4. are
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) is

    Names of sports and subjects that end in -s, such as gymnastics, athletics and physics, are singular, so they take "is".

  12. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q132 · English · Grammar

    Eighty days ______ not enough to complete this project.

    1. are
    2. is
    3. were
    4. am
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) are

    The key treats "Eighty days" as a plural subject and so takes the plural verb "are". Standard grammar books (e.g. Wren & Martin) treat a plural noun that names a specific amount of time, money or distance considered as a whole as singular ("Ten miles is a long distance"), so many would mark "is" here. (Official final key; some sources differ.)

  13. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q133 · English · Grammar

    We must talk ______ what we can do as a community to reduce pollution in our locality.

    1. at
    2. about
    3. in
    4. for
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) about

    "Talk about" something means to discuss a topic, so "talk about what we can do" is correct.

  14. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q134 · English · Grammar

    To do this job, you don't have to be particularly skilled ______ anything.

    1. for
    2. in
    3. at
    4. an
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) at

    "At" is the usual preposition after "skilled" (as in "good at"), so the key gives "skilled at anything". Dictionaries such as Oxford also accept "skilled in something", so option (2) is arguably correct too. (Official final key; some sources differ.)

  15. Manager 2015 Paper 1 (GS) · Q135 · English · Grammar

    Most of the companies prefer to remain in touch with their clients ______ email.

    1. by
    2. in
    3. to
    4. for
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) by

    The means of communication takes "by": by email, by phone, by post.
