  1. Manager 2015 Paper 2 (Civil) · Q27 · Building Materials & Construction · Construction & Estimation

    Cornice and coping is measured in ______

    1. Running meter & Square meter
    2. Running meter & Running meter
    3. Square meter & Square meter
    4. Square meter & Running meter
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Running meter & Running meter

    Cornices and copings are linear features of a building, so in estimates both are measured in running metres, with their cross-section described in the item. Area units are used for items such as plastering and flooring.

  2. Manager 2015 Paper 2 (Civil) · Q30 · Building Materials & Construction · Construction & Estimation

    In earth work excavation, normally lead and lift is considered for preparation of road estimate

    1. 30 m and 1.5 m
    2. 20 m and 1.5 m
    3. 20 m and 2.0 m
    4. 30 m and 2.0 m
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) 30 m and 1.5 m

    The normal rate for earthwork in estimates includes an initial lead of 30 m (horizontal carriage) and a lift of 1.5 m (vertical raising). Extra lead or lift beyond these is paid for separately.
