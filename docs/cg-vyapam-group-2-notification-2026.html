CG Vyapam Group 2 notification 2026: 1,703 posts, apply by 27 November

The Chhattisgarh Karmchari Chayan Mandal (CGSSB, which has taken over from CG Vyapam) has advertised the written exam for the Group-2, Class III (postgraduate and graduate level) Sub-group-02 posts (exam code G20227) on 5 November 2026: 1,703 posts in all. Online applications are open on cgssb.cgstate.gov.in from 5 to 27 November 2026, and the exam is on 10 January 2027. Below: the key dates, the posts, who can apply, the exam pattern and the CG Vyapam previous papers to practise.

छत्तीसगढ़ कर्मचारी चयन मण्डल: समूह-2 तृतीय श्रेणी (स्नातकोत्तर एवं स्नातक स्तर) उप समूह-02 लिखित भर्ती परीक्षा (G20227) — कुल 1,703 पद (सहायक ग्रेड-3: 1,206); ऑनलाइन आवेदन 05 से 27 नवम्बर 2026 (सायं 5 बजे तक), त्रुटि सुधार 28 से 30 नवम्बर 2026; परीक्षा 10 जनवरी 2027 (रविवार), 10:00 से 12:15 बजे; 100 प्रश्न, 2 घंटे।

Minimum qualifying marks: 33% for unreserved and 23% for reserved categories and persons with disabilities. For the 7 Home and Police posts, 15 times the vacancies are called to a qualifying physical measurement test; the final allotment is on the written marks, the post preferences and the reservation rules.

Who can apply

  • A bachelor's degree with a one-year computer diploma, or 10+2 with the Modern Office Management (MOM) diploma of a Chhattisgarh polytechnic, plus a skill certificate from the Chhattisgarh Shorthand-Typing-Computer Skill Test Council: typing at 5,000 key depressions an hour (Hindi or English) for Assistant Grade-3, Registration Clerk and ASI; 8,000 in Hindi and English for Data Entry Operator; shorthand at 100 words a minute with typing at 10,000 for the stenographers; shorthand at 60 words a minute with typing at 5,000 for Steno Typist.
  • Age: at least 21 years on 1 January 2026; the upper age and its relaxations follow the state's rules, with no one over 45 after all relaxations. The police posts follow the Home (Police) Department's own age rules and physical standards.
  • Fee: Rs. 350 general, Rs. 250 OBC, Rs. 200 SC, ST and persons with disabilities. Candidates domiciled in Chhattisgarh who sit the exam get the fee back in the bank account it was paid from.

Quick answers (as of 16 November 2026)

What is the last date to apply for CG Vyapam Group 2 2026?

27 November 2026 (5 pm) on cgssb.cgstate.gov.in; corrections are allowed from 28 to 30 November 2026.

When is the CG Vyapam Group 2 exam?

On Sunday, 10 January 2027, from 10:00 am to 12:15 pm, at the five divisional headquarters; admit cards are expected on 4 January 2027.

How many posts are there?

1,703, of which 1,206 are Assistant Grade-3; the rest are ASI-A (319), Registration Clerk (50), stenographers and steno typists (103) and Data Entry Operator (25).

What is the exam pattern?

100 objective questions for 100 marks in 2 hours: Computer 20, General Studies 20, Chhattisgarh GK 20, Mental Ability 10, Maths 10, Hindi 10 and English 10, with a quarter mark cut for each wrong answer.

What is the application fee?

Rs. 350 general, Rs. 250 OBC and Rs. 200 SC, ST and persons with disabilities; refunded to Chhattisgarh domicile candidates who sit the exam.
