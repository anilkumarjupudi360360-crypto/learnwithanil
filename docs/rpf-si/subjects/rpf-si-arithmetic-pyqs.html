  1. SI CBT 2024 · 2 December S1 · Q1 · Arithmetic · Percentage & Profit-Loss

    The price (per litre) of petrol increases by 75%. By what percent should its consumption be reduced such that the expenditure on it increases by 33% only?

    1. 70%
    2. 82%
    3. 76%
    4. 75%
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 76%

    Expenditure = price × consumption. New price = 1.75 times and new expenditure = 1.33 times, so new consumption = 1.33/1.75 = 0.76 times the old.     4. 86 kmph
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) 96 kmph

    Time for the first part = 222/111 = 2 h and for the second part = 258/86 = 3 h. Total distance = 222 + 258 = 480 km and total time = 2 + 3 = 5 h. Average speed = 480/5 = 96 kmph.

    2. 1955
    3. 1966
    4. 1960

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) 1960
