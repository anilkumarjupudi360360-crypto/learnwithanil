TGPSC MPSO / ASO Notification 2026: 102 posts

TGPSC Notification 07/G/ES/2026 (28 October 2026) invites online applications for 102 posts of Mandal Planning and Statistical Officer / Assistant Statistical Officer in the Directorate of Economics & Statistics, Telangana. Apply from 30 October to 27 November 2026, 5 pm; the written exam is tentatively in January 2027. Below: dates, zone-wise posts, eligibility, the two-paper pattern and syllabus, the 2018 exam, and previous papers to practise.

Who can apply

  • A bachelor's degree with Statistics as one of the main subjects; or
  • A bachelor's degree with Mathematics, Economics, Commerce or Computer Science as a main subject, with Statistics as a paper in one, two or all three years.
  • Fee: ₹1,000 application processing fee (₹500 for SC, ST and PwD) plus ₹80 exam fee; unemployed applicants are exempt from the exam fee, employees pay it.

Objective type, bilingual (English and Telugu). Selection is on the written marks only; there is no interview. Qualifying marks: 40% OC, EWS and sports, 35% BC, 30% SC, ST and PH. The notification does not mention negative marking.

Paper I syllabus: General Studies & General Abilities

  • Current affairs (regional, national, international); international relations and events.
  • General science; India's achievements in science and technology; environmental issues and disaster management.
  • Economy of India and Telangana; geography of India with a focus on Telangana.
  • Indian Constitution and polity with a focus on local self-government; policies of the Telangana State.
  • Society, culture, heritage, arts and literature of Telangana; modern Indian history and the national movement; history of Telangana and the statehood movement.
  • Logical reasoning, analytical ability and data interpretation; basic English.

Paper II syllabus: Statistics (degree level)

  • Introduction: meaning and limits of statistics, primary and secondary data, sampling and census, schedules and questionnaires, frequency distributions, tables, diagrams and graphs.
  • Central tendency, dispersion, skewness and kurtosis: AM, GM, HM, median, mode, deciles, percentiles; range, QD, MD, SD, coefficient of variation; Karl Pearson's and Bowley's skewness.
  • Probability: sample space, events, theorems, conditional probability, independence, Bayes' theorem.
  • Random variables and distributions: Bernoulli, binomial, Poisson, normal; unbiasedness, consistency, efficiency, sufficiency; large and small sample tests; null and alternative hypotheses.
  • Correlation and regression: Karl Pearson's and Spearman's rank correlation, probable error, regression equations and coefficients.
  • Time series (trend, seasonal variation) and index numbers (price, quantity, tests of adequacy, deflating, cost of living).
  • Sampling techniques: complete enumeration vs sampling, sample size, simple, stratified and systematic random sampling.
  • Basics of computers: binary, octal and hexadecimal systems, codes, bits and bytes, memory, logical operators (AND, OR, XOR, NOT, shift), algorithms and flow charts, MS Office.

Quick answers (as of 8 November 2026)

What is the last date for TGPSC MPSO / ASO 2026?

27 November 2026, 5:00 pm. Applications opened on 30 October 2026.

How many MPSO / ASO posts are there?

102 zonal posts: Zone I 12, II 14, III 15, IV 17, V 7, VI 25 and VII 12.

When is the TGPSC MPSO / ASO exam?

Tentatively in January 2027. TGPSC will announce the exact date; hall tickets come 7 days before.

Can B.Sc Maths or B.Com students apply?

Yes, if Statistics was a paper in at least one year of the degree. Degrees with Statistics as a main subject, or Maths, Economics, Commerce or Computer Science with a Statistics paper, qualify.

What is the MPSO / ASO age limit?

18 to 44 years on 1 July 2026, with 5 more years for SC, ST, BC and EWS and 10 for PH.

What is the MPSO / ASO salary?

Pay scale ₹36,750 – ₹1,06,990.

Is there negative marking in MPSO / ASO?

The notification does not mention negative marking.

Is there an interview?

No. Selection is on the 300 marks of the written exam (Paper I and Paper II).

When was the last MPSO / ASO exam?

On 3 September 2018, under TSPSC Notification 12/2018 for 474 posts.

Where can I practise MPSO / ASO Statistics previous questions?

TSPSC has not published the 2018 question booklets online. The APPSC Assistant Statistical Officer Paper II papers have the same statistics topics (with economics, accountancy and computers), in English and Telugu with APPSC's final key: see the Paper II practice list on this page.

Practise Paper I with TGPSC's General Studies papers

Paper I (General Studies & General Abilities) follows the same syllabus as the Paper I of other TGPSC posts. These papers have every question with the official key and a short explanation in English and Telugu:

  • TGPSC AMVI 2023 Paper I: General Studies & General Abilities (150 questions)
  • TSPSC HMWSSB Manager 2015 Paper I: General Studies

Practise Paper II with APPSC's Statistics papers

TSPSC has not put the 2018 question booklets online. The APPSC Assistant Statistical Officer exam has a Paper II on the same statistics topics (collection of data, central tendency, dispersion and skewness, correlation, time series, index numbers, computers), with economics and accountancy too. Every question is in English and Telugu with APPSC's final key and a short explanation:

  • APPSC Assistant Statistical Officer 2025 Paper II: Statistics, Economics, Accountancy & Computers
  • APPSC Assistant Statistical Officer 2022 Paper II: Statistics, Economics, Accountancy & Computers
