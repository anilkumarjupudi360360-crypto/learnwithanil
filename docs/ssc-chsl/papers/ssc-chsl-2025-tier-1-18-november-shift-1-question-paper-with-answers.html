  1. Q1 · English Comprehension · Error Spotting & Sentence Improvement

    Identify the part of the sentence that contains a grammatical error. A. The group of students, B. along with the professor, C. were discussing the new findings D. in the research lab.

    1. The group of students
    2. along with the professor
    3. were discussing the new findings
    4. in the research lab.
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) were discussing the new findings

    The subject is "The group" (singular); a phrase added with "along with" does not change the number of the subject. So the verb must be singular: "The group of students, along with the professor, was discussing the new findings."

    1. for
    2. to
    3. with
    4. on
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) for

  4. Q4 · English Comprehension · Fill in the Blanks & Cloze Test

    Fill in the blank with the most appropriate verb: She ______ her position on the matter only after repeated questioning by the press.

    1. reiterates
    2. clarified
    3. deflects
    4. assumes
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) clarified

    "Clarified her position" means she made her stand clear: she explained where she stood only after the press questioned her repeatedly, a completed past event, so the past tense "clarified" fits. "Reiterates" (repeats), "deflects" (turns aside) and "assumes" (takes on) do not suit the sense, and all three are present-tense forms.
