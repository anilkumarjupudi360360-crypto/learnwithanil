    Identify the part of the sentence that contains a grammatical error. A. The final decision was made B. between the two countries C. after long negotiation D. and several meetings.

    1. The final decision was made
    2. between the two countries
    3. after long negotiation
    4. and several meetings
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) after long negotiation

    Part C is wrong: formal talks between countries are spoken of as a series, so the plural is needed: 'after long negotiations', which also matches the parallel plural 'several meetings'. Parts A, B and D are correct.

  2. Q2 · English Comprehension · Fill in the Blanks & Cloze Test

    Fill in the blank with the most appropriate preposition: The archaeologist stumbled ______ an ancient manuscript buried beneath the ruins.

    1. across
    2. into
    3. upon
    4. over
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) upon

    'Stumble upon' is a phrasal verb meaning to find something by chance, which is what happened to the archaeologist. 'Stumble into' means to get involved in something accidentally and 'stumble over' means to trip on something. ('Stumble across' has the same meaning as 'stumble upon', but SSC's key is 'upon'.) (SSC key; some sources differ.)
