  3. CHSL 2025 · 18 November S1 · Q4 · English Comprehension · Fill in the Blanks & Cloze Test

    Fill in the blank with the most appropriate verb: She ______ her position on the matter only after repeated questioning by the press.

    1. reiterates
    2. clarified
    3. deflects
    4. assumes
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) clarified
