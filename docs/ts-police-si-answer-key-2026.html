TS Police SI answer key 2026

The TS Police SI Preliminary Written Test is on Sunday, 29 November 2026 (tentative). TGPRB publishes a preliminary key on www.tgprb.in a few days after the exam, takes objections question by question in the candidate login, and releases the final key with the results. Work out your score below with the 0.2 negative mark and see whether you clear the qualifying mark. We will add the 2026 question paper with answers here once the paper is available.

Calculate your score

Count the questions you got right and wrong with the key (out of 200). Each right answer gets 1 mark, each wrong answer loses 0.2 marks, and an unanswered question gets 0.

When the key comes: what happened in 2022

Preliminary key12 August 2022, five days after the 7 August exam
Objections13 to 15 August 2022, question by question, in the candidate login on the board's website
Final key and results21 October 2022, with the qualified list; candidates could see their OMR sheet in the login
2026TGPRB will announce the dates; this page will be updated

TGPRB usually deletes a few questions (their marks are given to everyone) and accepts more than one answer for some, so your final score can differ slightly from the preliminary key.

Is there a cut-off?

Not in the prelims. Everyone who reaches the qualifying mark of their category goes on to the physical tests; the competition is decided in the Final Written Examination (Papers III and IV for SI Civil), where TGPRB later publishes the marks of the last selected candidate in each category.

Quick answers (as of 4 November 2026)

When will the TS SI answer key 2026 come?

After the exam on 29 November 2026. In 2022 the preliminary key came 5 days after the exam, so expect it in early December 2026 on www.tgprb.in.

How do I raise an objection to the TS SI key?

In your candidate login on www.tgprb.in, question by question, during the objection window TGPRB announces with the key (three days in 2022).

Is there a cut-off in the TS SI prelims?

No. Everyone who gets the qualifying mark of their category (60/50/40 out of 200) goes on to the physical tests.

How many marks are needed to pass the TS SI prelims?

60 for OC/EWS, 50 for BC, 40 for SC, ST and Ex-Servicemen, after the 0.2 negative mark.

What was the highest score in the 2022 TS SI prelims?

133 out of 200; the average was 47.25 and 46.8% of candidates qualified.
