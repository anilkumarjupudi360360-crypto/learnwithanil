Theory of Machines

  1. AMVI 2026 Paper 2 (Automobile) · Q8 · Engineering Mechanics & Machine Design · Theory of Machines

    The radial distance from the pitch circle to the root of the tooth is known as the

    1. Addendum
    2. Clearance
    3. Dedendum
    4. Whole depth
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Dedendum

    In gear terminology the dedendum is the radial distance from the pitch circle to the root (dedendum) circle, while the addendum is the radial distance from the pitch circle to the tip (addendum) circle. The whole depth is addendum plus dedendum. Clearance is the radial gap between the tip of a tooth and the root of the mating gear, equal to the dedendum minus the addendum.

  2. AMVI 2026 Paper 2 (Automobile) · Q9 · Engineering Mechanics & Machine Design · Theory of Machines

    What is the correct mathematical formula for the module of a gear ? (Where D is the pitch circle diameter and N is the number of teeth)

    1. D/N
    2. D/(2N)
    3. 2D/N
    4. 3D/N
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) D/N

    The module m is the pitch circle diameter divided by the number of teeth, m = D/N, usually expressed in millimetres. It is the reciprocal of the diametral pitch (N/D). The circular pitch is π times the module, p = πD/N = πm, and two gears can mesh only if they have the same module. For example, a gear with a pitch circle diameter of 100 mm and 50 teeth has a module of 2 mm.

  3. AMVI 2026 Paper 2 (Automobile) · Q10 · Engineering Mechanics & Machine Design · Theory of Machines

    In a simple gear train, how is the driven gear's motion related to the driver gear if there is an odd number of gears ?

    1. Opposite direction
    2. Same direction
    3. Does not depends on gear ratio
    4. Depends on gear ratio
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Same direction

    Each external mesh reverses the direction of rotation. With an odd number of gears in a simple train (for example driver, one idler and driven) there is an even number of meshes, so the driven gear turns in the same direction as the driver; with an even number of gears it turns the opposite way. Idlers change only the direction, not the speed ratio, which depends only on the teeth of the driver and the driven gear.

  10. AMVI 2023 Paper 2 (Automobile) · Q4 · Engineering Mechanics & Machine Design · Machine Design

    Read the given statement and conclusions carefully and select the correct option. Statement: Self-locking screw jack means when the rotational force on the screw is removed, it will remain motionless and will not rotate backwards. Conclusions: I. Self-locking screw jacks are safer compared to hydraulic jack which is not self-locking. II. Single start trapezoidal type screws are typically self-locking. III. All screw jacks with double start lifting screws are also considered self-locking.

    1. Statement and all the conclusions I, II and III are true.
    2. Statement is incorrect, but conclusions I and II are true.
    3. Statement is true, but conclusions I, II and III are false.
    4. Only statement and conclusions I and II are true.
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Only statement and conclusions I and II are true.

    A screw is self-locking when the friction angle is at least equal to the helix (lead) angle, so the load cannot turn it back once the effort is removed; the statement is true. Single-start square or trapezoidal screws have a small lead angle and are normally self-locking, which makes such jacks safe to leave under load (I and II). A double-start screw has twice the lead and a larger helix angle, so it is often not self-locking; III is false.
