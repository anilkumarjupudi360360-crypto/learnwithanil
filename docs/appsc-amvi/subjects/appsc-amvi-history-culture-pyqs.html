  4. AMVI 2019 Paper 1 (GS & MA) · Q9 · History & Culture · Modern India & Freedom Struggle

    Which of the following statements is/are correct regarding land revenue policy of British? 1. Land revenue policies made the land saleable, mortgageable and alienable. 2. Land revenue was periodically revised in ryotwari areas but fixed in Mahalwari System. 3. Areas covered by Mahalwari system included North Western Provinces and parts of Punjab. Select the correct answer using the code given below.

    Show answer

    Answer: 1 and 3 only

    British land revenue settlements turned land into a commodity that could be sold, mortgaged and alienated (statement 1). The Mahalwari system, introduced by Holt Mackenzie in 1822, covered the North-Western Provinces, parts of Central India and Punjab (statement 3). Statement 2 is wrong: revenue was revised periodically in both ryotwari and Mahalwari areas; it was fixed permanently only under the Permanent Settlement (zamindari) of Bengal, 1793.

  5. AMVI 2019 Paper 1 (GS & MA) · Q10 · History & Culture · Modern India & Freedom Struggle

    The Treaty of Salbai was signed between:

    Show answer

    Answer: British and Marathas

    The Treaty of Salbai (17 May 1782), negotiated through Mahadji Scindia, was signed between the English East India Company and the Marathas and ended the First Anglo-Maratha War (1775–82). The British kept Salsette, recognised Madhav Rao II as Peshwa and gave up the cause of Raghunath Rao, who was pensioned off. It secured peace between the two powers for about twenty years.
