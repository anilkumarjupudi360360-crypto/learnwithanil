    3. Uneven tread wear - the edges of the tyre wear out faster
    4. Smaller contact area with the road
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Smaller contact area with the road

    An under-inflated tyre flexes more and flattens out, so its contact patch with the road becomes larger, not smaller. The extra flexing raises rolling resistance and heat build-up, which increases fuel consumption, and the load is carried on the shoulders, so both edges of the tread wear faster than the centre. A smaller contact patch with centre wear is a symptom of over-inflation.
