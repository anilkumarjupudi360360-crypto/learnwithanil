  6. AMVI 2019 Paper 1 (GS & MA) · Q1 · General Science & Technology · Physics

    Who among the following Indian scientists is the first scientist to get Nobel Prize?

    Show answer

    Answer: C. V Raman

    Sir C. V. Raman won the 1930 Nobel Prize in Physics for discovering the Raman effect, the scattering of light announced on 28 February 1928 (now National Science Day). He was the first Indian and the first Asian to win a Nobel Prize in science; Rabindranath Tagore (Literature, 1913) was the first Indian Nobel laureate overall.
