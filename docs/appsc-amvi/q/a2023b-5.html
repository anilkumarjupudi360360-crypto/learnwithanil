  1. AMVI 2023 Paper 2 (Automobile) · Q5 · IC Engines, Fuels & Emissions · Engine Fundamentals & Cycles

    Match the following four internally reversible processes used in a four-stroke Otto cycle as shown in figure.

    List IList II
    (A) Isentropic compression(i) 2-3
    (B) Constant-volume heat addition(ii) 1-2
    (C) Isentropic expansion(iii) 4-1
    (D) Constant-volume heat rejection(iv) 3-4
    1. (A)-(i), (B)-(ii), (C)-(iii) and (D)-(iv)
    2. (A)-(iv), (B)-(iii), (C)-(ii) and (D)-(i)
    3. (A)-(ii), (B)-(i), (C)-(iv) and (D)-(iii)
    4. (A)-(ii), (B)-(iv), (C)-(i) and (D)-(iii)
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) (A)-(ii), (B)-(i), (C)-(iv) and (D)-(iii)

    On the P–V diagram of the air-standard Otto cycle, 1–2 is isentropic (constant-entropy) compression as the volume falls, 2–3 is heat addition at constant volume (vertical rise in pressure), 3–4 is isentropic expansion (power stroke), and 4–1 is heat rejection at constant volume (vertical drop in pressure). So A–(ii), B–(i), C–(iv) and D–(iii).
