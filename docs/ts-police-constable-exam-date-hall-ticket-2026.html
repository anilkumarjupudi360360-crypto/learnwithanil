TS Police Constable exam date 2026: Sunday, 20 December

TGPRB will hold the Preliminary Written Test for SCT Police Constable (Civil) and equivalent posts (Notification Rc.No.189/2026, 7,112 posts) on Sunday, 20 December 2026, in the forenoon, at 15 to 18 centres including the 10 erstwhile district headquarters. The date is marked tentative (press note of 17 September 2026); about 3.9 lakh applications were filed. Below: the hall ticket, exam-day rules, the pattern and qualifying marks, the posts, the salary and the physical tests.

How to download the hall ticket

  1. Go to www.tgprb.in and open the hall-ticket link (it appears only when TGPRB opens the download window).
  2. Log in to your candidate account with the details you used to apply (in 2022: your registered mobile number and password).
  3. Download the hall ticket and print it on A4 paper, preferably on both sides, so the ticket and the instructions fit on one sheet.
  4. Paste the passport photo you uploaded with the application in the box on page 1, using gum.
  5. Check your name, photo, exam centre and timing; visit the centre a day before the exam.

In 2022 the constable hall tickets could be downloaded from 8 am on 18 August to midnight on 26 August for the exam on 28 August: the window closed two days before the exam. The 2022 exam ran from 10 am to 1 pm; TGPRB has not announced the 2026 timing yet.

Exam-day rules (TGPRB's hall-ticket instructions)

  • Print the hall ticket on A4 paper (both sides if you can) and paste the same passport photo you uploaded with the application in the box on page 1, with gum, not pins or staples. A hall ticket without the photo is not accepted.
  • Reach early: candidates are let in one hour before the start, and no one is admitted even one minute after the exam begins.
  • Carry only the hall ticket and black or blue ballpoint pens.
  • Not allowed: mobile phones, smart watches and any wrist watch, calculators, Bluetooth devices, pen drives, wallets, purses, notes or loose papers. There is no cloakroom, so leave bags at home; women are advised not to wear jewellery.
  • A photo and fingerprints are taken in the hall: avoid mehendi or tattoos on the hands.
  • Write nothing on the OMR sheet except the answers and the details asked for: symbols, prayers or marks count as malpractice.
  • The question booklet is in English–Telugu or English–Urdu; where the two differ, the English version is final.
  • Keep the hall ticket safely until the whole recruitment is over.

Negative marking: 0.2 of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer; an unanswered question gets zero. The question booklet is in English–Telugu or English–Urdu. The prelims only qualifies you for the physical tests.

Salary

Pay scale ₹24,280 – ₹72,850 a month for every post in this notification. TGPRB puts the starting annual pay of a constable at about ₹4.21 lakh to ₹4.59 lakh including HRA (press note of 8 September 2026). SCT means Stipendiary Cadet Trainee: a stipend is paid during training.

After the prelims: physical tests and the final exam

  • Physical Measurement Test: height at least 167.6 cm for men and 152.5 cm for women (160 cm and 150 cm for ST candidates of the notified agency areas). There is no chest measurement.
  • Physical Efficiency Test: a 1,600 m run in 7 min 15 s for men (9 min 30 s for ex-servicemen) or 800 m in 5 min 20 s for women; the long jump (4 m men, 2.5 m women) and the shot put (6 m men, 4 m women) are qualifying. For AR, SAR CPL and SPF posts the run is scored.
  • Final Written Examination: one objective paper of 3 hours, 200 marks for Civil constable, Fire Fighter and Warder (100 marks for AR, SAR CPL and SPF, where run marks are added). You need 40% (OC, EWS), 35% (BC) or 30% (SC, ST, Ex-Servicemen).

Quick answers (as of 11 November 2026)

Is the TS Police Constable exam postponed?

No postponement had been announced as of 11 November 2026. The Preliminary Written Test stays on Sunday, 20 December 2026, forenoon (tentative).

When will the TS Constable hall ticket 2026 come?

Not announced yet. In 2022 the window opened 10 days before the exam, so expect it around 10 December 2026 on www.tgprb.in.

How many posts are there in TS Police Constable 2026?

7,112, including 3,697 PC (Civil), 1,052 PC (AR), 1,380 TGSP constables and 751 Fire Fighters.

Is there negative marking in the TS Constable exam?

Yes. 0.2 of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer.

What are the qualifying marks for TS Constable prelims?

60 out of 200 for OC/EWS, 50 for BC, 40 for SC, ST, Ex-Servicemen and Home Guards.

How many applied for TS Constable 2026?

About 3.9 lakh applications.
