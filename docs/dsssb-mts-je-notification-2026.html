DSSSB MTS & JE notification 2026: 1,260 posts, apply by 11 December

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has advertised 1,260 posts in its Combined Examinations, 2026 (Advertisement No. 06/2026, 6 November 2026): 1,075 Multi-Tasking Staff posts (Post Code 810/26) and 185 Junior Engineer (Electric/Electrical) posts (Post Code 809/26) in departments of the Government of NCT of Delhi and its bodies. Online applications are open on dsssbonline.nic.in from 12 November to 11 December 2026. Below: the dates, posts, eligibility, exam pattern and the DSSSB previous papers to practise.

Exam pattern

ExamWhat it hasQuestions · time
MTS (one tier)General Awareness, General Intelligence & Reasoning, Arithmetical & Numerical Ability, Hindi, English — 40 each200 (200 marks) · 2 hours
JE Tier-ISection A: the same five general parts, 20 each · Section B: 100 questions on Electrical Engineering200 (200 marks) · 2 hours
JE Tier-IIElectrical Engineering: Part I 100 one-mark questions, Part II 50 two-mark application questions150 (200 marks) · 2½ hours

Wrong answers cost 0.25 mark (0.50 for a two-mark question). Questions are in Hindi and English, except the language parts. For JE, Tier-I only shortlists candidates for Tier-II, and the final merit list is on Tier-II marks.

Who can apply

  • MTS: Matriculation (Class 10) pass; age 18 to 27 years.
  • JE: a degree or diploma in Electrical Engineering (some departments ask for 2 years' experience with the diploma); age 18 to 27 years (18 to 32 for the NDMC posts).
  • Age relaxation for reserved categories, persons with disabilities, ex-servicemen and Delhi government employees as given in the advertisement.
  • Fee: Rs. 100 through SBI e-pay; no fee for women, SC, ST, persons with disabilities and ex-servicemen.

Quick answers (as of 16 November 2026)

What is the last date to apply for DSSSB MTS and JE 2026?

11 December 2026 (till 11:59 pm) on dsssbonline.nic.in. Applications opened on 12 November 2026.

How many DSSSB MTS posts are there in 2026?

1,075 Multi-Tasking Staff posts (Post Code 810/26), most of them in the Directorate of Education (757 MTS General Duty and 239 MTS-Aaya).

How many DSSSB JE Electrical posts are there?

185 Junior Engineer (Electric/Electrical) posts (Post Code 809/26) in Delhi Transco, NDMC, MCD, DUSIB and the Delhi Agricultural Marketing Board.

What is the DSSSB MTS exam pattern?

One 2-hour test of 200 questions for 200 marks: General Awareness, Reasoning, Arithmetic, Hindi and English, 40 each, with 0.25 cut for each wrong answer.

What is the age limit for DSSSB MTS 2026?

18 to 27 years, with relaxation for reserved categories as per the advertisement.
