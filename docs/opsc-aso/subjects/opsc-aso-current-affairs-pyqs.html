    Which Union Ministry hosted the Purple Fest, 2025 at Rashtrapati Bhavan ?

    1. Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
    2. Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
    3. Ministry of Women and Child Development
    4. Ministry of Home Affairs
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment

    As per OPSC's key, the Purple Fest 2025 at Rashtrapati Bhavan was hosted by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. Purple Fest celebrates the abilities and inclusion of persons with disabilities, and disability affairs are handled by the Ministry's Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan).

  2. 2025 · Paper I General Awareness · Q8 · Current Affairs · National & International

    Mount Lewotobi Laki-Laki, an active volcano, is located in which country ?

    1. Philippines
    2. Indonesia
    3. Japan
    4. Malaysia
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Indonesia

    Mount Lewotobi Laki-Laki is an active volcano on Flores island in East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia; it is the twin of Lewotobi Perempuan. Its deadly eruptions in November 2024 and further eruptions in 2025 made news. Indonesia lies on the Pacific 'Ring of Fire'.
