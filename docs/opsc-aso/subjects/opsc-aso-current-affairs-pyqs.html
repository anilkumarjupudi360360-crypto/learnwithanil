  1. 2025 · Paper I General Awareness · Q7 · Current Affairs · National & International

    Which Union Ministry hosted the Purple Fest, 2025 at Rashtrapati Bhavan ?

    1. Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
    2. Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
    3. Ministry of Women and Child Development
    4. Ministry of Home Affairs
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment

    Purple Fest 2025 at Rashtrapati Bhavan was organised by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment through its Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (Divyangjan). Held in March 2025 in the presence of President Droupadi Murmu, it celebrated the talents of persons with disabilities through cultural performances, games, stalls and awareness activities, and promoted inclusion and accessibility. Purple is the colour linked worldwide with the disability-rights movement, which is why the festival carries that name. The other ministries are not the nodal agency for disability: Health and Family Welfare looks after public health and hospitals, Women and Child Development runs schemes such as Mission Shakti and Poshan, and Home Affairs deals with internal security and police. Related facts worth remembering: the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 recognises 21 types of disability, and the Accessible India Campaign (Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan) was launched in 2015 to make buildings, transport and websites barrier-free. The disability department was carved out as a separate Department in 2012. Memory tip: disability matters = Social Justice Ministry.

  2. 2025 · Paper I General Awareness · Q8 · Current Affairs · National & International

    Mount Lewotobi Laki-Laki, an active volcano, is located in which country ?

    1. Philippines
    2. Indonesia
    3. Japan
    4. Malaysia
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Indonesia

    Mount Lewotobi Laki-Laki is an active volcano on Flores Island in East Nusa Tenggara province, Indonesia. 'Laki-laki' means 'man' in Indonesian; it stands beside its twin peak, Lewotobi Perempuan ('woman'), so the pair is known locally as a husband-and-wife volcano. A deadly eruption in November 2024 killed several villagers and forced thousands to evacuate, and further powerful eruptions in 2025 threw ash columns kilometres high and disrupted flights to Bali. Indonesia has more active volcanoes than any other country because it lies on the Pacific 'Ring of Fire', where the Indo-Australian plate sinks beneath the Eurasian plate along the Sunda Arc. The other countries are wrong here even though some have famous volcanoes: the Philippines has Mayon and Taal, Japan has Mount Fuji and Sakurajima, and Malaysia has no active volcano at all. Trap: don't confuse Lewotobi with Merapi (Java) or Krakatoa (Sunda Strait), Indonesia's other well-known volcanoes. India's only active volcano is Barren Island in the Andaman Sea.

  3. 2025 · Paper I General Awareness · Q9 · Current Affairs · National & International

    BIMSTEC Youth Summit, 2025 was held in

    1. Myanmar
    2. Bangladesh
    3. Thailand
    4. India
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) India

    India hosted the BIMSTEC Youth Summit 2025, the first such summit of the grouping, at Gandhinagar, Gujarat, in February 2025. Organised by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports with the Ministry of External Affairs, it brought together youth delegates from all member countries under the theme 'Youth as a Bridge for Intra-BIMSTEC Exchange', with talks on entrepreneurship, digital connectivity and sustainable development. BIMSTEC (Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation) was formed by the Bangkok Declaration of 1997 and has seven members: five from South Asia (Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal, Sri Lanka) and two from Southeast Asia (Myanmar, Thailand). Its permanent secretariat is in Dhaka. Thailand hosted the 6th BIMSTEC leaders' Summit in Bangkok in April 2025, where the Bangkok Vision 2030 was adopted and Bangladesh took over the chair, so don't confuse that summit with the youth summit held in India. Myanmar and Bangladesh are members but did not host this event. For Odisha, BIMSTEC matters because the State lies on the Bay of Bengal and has old sea links with Southeast Asia, celebrated every year in Bali Jatra.

  20. 2022 · Paper I General Awareness · Q2 · Current Affairs · National & International

    Which State in India presented ‘Child Budget’ as part of the Annual State Budget ?

    1. Andhra Pradesh
    2. Gujarat
    3. Sikkim
    4. Madhya Pradesh
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Madhya Pradesh

    Madhya Pradesh presented a separate 'Child Budget' for the first time along with its annual budget for 2022-23. Finance Minister Jagdish Devda tabled the budget on 9 March 2022, and the child budget brought together in one statement the money set aside across many departments — such as school education, health, and women and child development — for schemes meant for children below 18. A child budget is not a separate fund; it tags existing spending so that the government and the public can track how much actually reaches children, much as a gender budget does for women. The key picks D because the question is based on the March 2022 news, when Madhya Pradesh's move was widely reported. Gujarat and Sikkim were not part of that news. Note, however, that Andhra Pradesh had already presented a child budget with its 2021-22 budget, so option A is not strictly wrong. Tip: in current-affairs questions, link the fact to the year of the news (here, 2022-23 and Madhya Pradesh). (Official key; some sources differ.)

  21. 2022 · Paper I General Awareness · Q3 · Current Affairs · National & International

    The Government of India announced it’s decision to convert 150 villages into ‘Villages of Excellence’ with technical assistance from ______.

    1. Japan
    2. Israel
    3. USA
    4. Netherlands
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Israel

    Israel is the country whose technical help India chose to turn 150 villages into 'Villages of Excellence'. The plan belongs to the Indo-Israel Agricultural Project, which the Union Agriculture Ministry runs with Israel's development agency MASHAV; India and Israel have had full diplomatic relations since January 1992. The villages were to be developed around the Indo-Israel Centres of Excellence (CoEs) for agriculture already working in several states. These CoEs demonstrate modern farming such as drip and micro-irrigation, fertigation, protected cultivation and quality nurseries for fruits and vegetables. A 'village of excellence' carries these methods from the centre to farmers' fields nearby, aiming at higher yields, higher incomes and better use of water. Why not the others: Japan, the USA and the Netherlands also cooperate with India in agriculture, but this programme was with Israel. Memory tip: Israel = water-saving farming (drip irrigation), the way a desert country learned to grow food.

  22. 2022 · Paper I General Awareness · Q4 · Current Affairs · National & International

    Which one of the following is not the objective of ‘Operation Sajag’ launched by Delhi Police ?

    1. To curb area domination exercise
    2. Keeping tap on criminals
    3. Intensive checking at pickets
    4. Special rewards to traffic-friendly drivers
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Special rewards to traffic-friendly drivers

    Giving special rewards to traffic-friendly drivers was not an aim of Operation Sajag, so D is the answer. Operation Sajag (sajag means 'alert') was launched by the North-West district of Delhi Police on 4 October 2021 to curb street crime. Its measures were all policing steps: area-domination exercises (a visible police presence in a locality), intensive checking at pickets, stepped-up patrolling and keeping a tab on known criminals. Option A's wording ('to curb area domination exercise') is clumsy — area domination was a tool of the drive, not something it curbed — but it clearly refers to part of the operation, while B and C are listed measures. Rewarding good drivers is a traffic-management idea with no link to this anti-crime drive. Note that Delhi Police reports to the Union Ministry of Home Affairs, not to the Delhi government. Tip: for 'which is not an objective' questions, look for the option that belongs to a different field — here, traffic.

  23. 2022 · Paper I General Awareness · Q5 · Current Affairs · National & International

    The GSI has approved the setting up of country’s first Geological Park at ______.

    1. Lamheta, Jabalpur
    2. Kadagu, Karnataka
    3. Idukki, Kerala
    4. Talala Gir, Gujarat
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Lamheta, Jabalpur

    Lamheta, near Jabalpur in Madhya Pradesh, was approved by the Geological Survey of India (GSI) as the site of the country's first geological park (geo-park). Lamheta lies on the bank of the Narmada, close to the marble rocks of Bhedaghat. The area is famous among geologists: the Lameta Formation, named after Lameta Ghat, is a Cretaceous rock layer that has yielded many dinosaur fossils, and the first dinosaur fossil found in India was collected from the Lameta bed near Jabalpur in 1828 by William Sleeman. The park aims to protect these rock formations and promote geo-tourism and science education. Why not the others: Kodagu (spelt 'Kadagu' in the question) in Karnataka is known for coffee and as the source of the Kaveri; Idukki in Kerala for its arch dam and hill country; and Talala in the Gir area of Gujarat for Gir forest and Kesar mangoes. Don't confuse it with the dinosaur fossil park at Raiyoli near Balasinor in Gujarat. GSI itself was founded in 1851 and has its headquarters in Kolkata.

  24. 2022 · Paper I General Awareness · Q7 · Current Affairs · National & International

    The Central Board of Secondary Education has introduced ‘Data Science Subject’ as a subject for it’s students of 8-12 class in collaboration with ______ company.

    1. Inter
    2. Dell
    3. Microsoft
    4. Infosys
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Microsoft

    Microsoft was the partner when the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) introduced Coding and Data Science as new subjects in 2021. Coding was offered for Classes 6 to 8 and Data Science for Classes 8 to 12 from the 2021-22 academic session, with the course content prepared together with Microsoft. The step followed the National Education Policy 2020, which wants schools to build computational thinking, coding and data skills from an early age. Intel (misprinted here as 'Inter'), Dell and Infosys all run their own education and skilling programmes in India, but none of them was the partner for this CBSE subject. For exam purposes, link each new school subject with its partner and the class range: Microsoft, Coding (6-8) and Data Science (8-12). CBSE is the national board under the Union Ministry of Education, with its headquarters in New Delhi. Trap to avoid: a familiar tech name in the options can pull you away from the partner actually named in the announcement, so learn such tie-ups as pairs. Memory tip: 'MS' for Microsoft and 'MS' for Maths and Science, the base of data science.

  25. 2022 · Paper I General Awareness · Q9 · Current Affairs · National & International

    Digital India ‘BHASHINI’ is ______. Which one of the above is not correct ?

    1. India’s Artificial Intelligence-led Language Translation Platform
    2. It gives access to global content in their own languages
    3. It is a step to realize digital photography
    4. It promotes Digital India Vision
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) It is a step to realize digital photography

    Calling BHASHINI 'a step to realise digital photography' is wrong; the platform has nothing to do with cameras or images. BHASHINI (BHASHa INterface for India) is India's Artificial Intelligence-led language translation platform, launched by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 4 July 2022 during Digital India Week at Gandhinagar. It is the platform of the National Language Translation Mission of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). Its aim is to let every citizen use the internet and digital services in his or her own language through speech-to-text, text-to-speech and machine translation tools for Indian languages, including Odia. So A (AI-led translation platform), B (access to global content in one's own language) and D (promotes the Digital India vision) all describe it correctly. Through 'Bhasha Daan', citizens can also contribute voice and text samples to build open language datasets. Trap to avoid: in a 'not correct' question, look for the statement that is off-topic; the word 'digital' appears in both C and D, but only D fits a language mission. Memory tip: Bhasha = language, so BHASHINI = language technology.

  5. 2022 · Paper I General Awareness · Q10 · Current Affairs · Sports, Awards & Misc.

    The theme of ‘Earth Day-2022 is ______.

    1. Nurture the Nature
    2. Go Green, Save Green
    3. Save the Earth, Save the Future
    4. Invest in our Planet
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Invest in our Planet

    'Invest in Our Planet' was the theme of Earth Day 2022, observed on 22 April. It asked businesses, governments and citizens to put money and effort into climate solutions and a green, prosperous future, instead of treating the environment as a cost; the same theme was kept for Earth Day 2023. Earth Day began in the United States in 1970, inspired by Senator Gaylord Nelson, and is now coordinated worldwide by EARTHDAY.ORG; the UN General Assembly named 22 April International Mother Earth Day in 2009. Options A, B and C are general green slogans that were never the official 2022 theme. Do not confuse Earth Day with World Environment Day (5 June, led by UNEP), whose 2022 theme was 'Only One Earth' with Sweden as host, or with Earth Hour, when lights are switched off for an hour in late March. For 2024 the Earth Day theme was 'Planet vs. Plastics'. Memory tip: Earth Day = 22 April = 'Invest in Our Planet' (2022); Environment Day = 5 June = 'Only One Earth' (2022).
