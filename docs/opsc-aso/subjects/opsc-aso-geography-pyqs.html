  1. 2025 · Paper I General Awareness · Q5 · Geography & Environment · Indian & World Geography

    Match the following : Composition of Atmosphere. a. Oxygen — 1. Is one of the most variable gaseous substance present in atmosphere, constituting between 0.2 to 4 percent. b. Nitrogen — 2. Constitutes only 21 percent of the total volume of atmosphere. c. Ozone — 3. Among gases accounts for 78 percent of total atmospheric volume. d. Water vapour — 4. It forms less than 0.00005 percent by volume of the atmosphere and is unevenly distributed.

    1. a-1, b-2, c-3, d-4
    2. a-2, b-3, c-1, d-4
    3. a-2, b-3, c-4, d-1
    4. a-4, b-1, c-3, d-2
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) a-2, b-3, c-4, d-1

    By volume, nitrogen is about 78% of dry air and oxygen about 21%. Ozone is a trace gas (less than 0.00005%), concentrated mainly in the stratosphere. Water vapour is the most variable gas: up to about 4% in warm, humid tropics and very little over deserts and polar regions. So a-2, b-3, c-4, d-1.

  1. 2025 · Paper I General Awareness · Q4 · Geography & Environment · Environment & Ecology

    What is edge species ?

    1. The species which occur abundantly in the ecotone
    2. Species on the verge of extinction
    3. Rare species
    4. Endemic species
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) The species which occur abundantly in the ecotone

    An ecotone is the transition zone between two ecosystems (e.g., forest and grassland). Species that occur mostly or abundantly in this junction zone are called edge species, and the greater variety and density of organisms found there is the 'edge effect'.
