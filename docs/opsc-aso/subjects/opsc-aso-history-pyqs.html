  1. 2025 · Paper I General Awareness · Q1 · History & National Movement · Modern India & National Movement

    What is the correct sequence of the following ? i. The Hartog Committee ii. The Hunter Education Commission iii. The Sadler University Commission iv. Kothari Education Commission

    1. i, ii, iii, iv
    2. iii, iv, ii, i
    3. ii, iii, iv, i
    4. ii, iii, i, iv
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) ii, iii, i, iv

    Chronological order: Hunter Education Commission (1882) → Sadler (Calcutta) University Commission (1917–19) → Hartog Committee (1929, auxiliary committee of the Simon Commission on education) → Kothari Education Commission (1964–66). Hence ii, iii, i, iv.

  2. 2025 · Paper I General Awareness · Q10 · History & National Movement · Modern India & National Movement

    Which one of the following is NOT correct ?

    1. First Governor-General of Fort William was Warren Hastings
    2. First Chief Justice of the Supreme Court in British India was Elijah Impey
    3. First Law Member of the Governor-General’s Council was Lord Amherst
    4. First Viceroy as well as the Governor-General of India was Lord Canning
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) First Law Member of the Governor-General’s Council was Lord Amherst

    The first Law Member of the Governor-General's Council was Lord (T. B.) Macaulay, appointed under the Charter Act of 1833; Lord Amherst was Governor-General (1823–28) during the First Anglo-Burmese War. The other statements are correct: Warren Hastings (Regulating Act, 1773), Elijah Impey (Supreme Court at Calcutta, 1774) and Canning (first Viceroy, 1858).

  2. 2022 · Paper I General Awareness · Q6 · History & National Movement · Art & Culture

    Match the following : (I) Meghalaya — (i) Hojagiri (II) Mizoram — (ii) Wangala Dance (III) Manipur — (iii) Bamboo Dance (IV) Tripura — (iv) Thang ta

    1. (I) – (ii), (II) – (iii), (III) – (iv), (IV) – (i)
    2. (I) – (iii), (II) – (i), (III) – (ii), (IV) – (iv)
    3. (I) – (ii), (II) – (i), (III) – (iii), (IV) – (iv)
    4. (I) – (iii), (II) – (iv), (III) – (i), (IV) – (iv)
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) (I) – (ii), (II) – (iii), (III) – (iv), (IV) – (i)

    Wangala is the harvest dance of the Garos of Meghalaya; the Bamboo Dance (Cheraw) belongs to Mizoram; Thang-ta is the traditional martial art of Manipur; Hojagiri is the dance of the Reang (Bru) community of Tripura. So (I)-(ii), (II)-(iii), (III)-(iv), (IV)-(i).
