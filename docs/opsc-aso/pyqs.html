  1. 1 of 10 · OPSC ASO 2025 Paper 1 General Awareness · Q1 · History & National Movement

    What is the correct sequence of the following ? i. The Hartog Committee ii. The Hunter Education Commission iii. The Sadler University Commission iv. Kothari Education Commission

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) ii, iii, i, iv

    Chronological order: Hunter Education Commission (1882) → Sadler (Calcutta) University Commission (1917–19) → Hartog Committee (1929, auxiliary committee of the Simon Commission on education) → Kothari Education Commission (1964–66). Hence ii, iii, i, iv.

  2. 2 of 10 · OPSC ASO 2025 Paper 2 Reasoning & Maths · Q1 · Reasoning & Mental Ability

    Choose the most appropriate option to complete the following series. V, R, N, ______, F, B

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) J

    Each letter is 4 places before the previous one: V(22) → R(18) → N(14) → J(10) → F(6) → B(2). So the missing letter is J.

  3. 3 of 10 · OPSC ASO 2025 Paper 3 English & Odia · Q1 · English

    Directions : Fill in the blanks in the following sentences by selecting the most appropriate option from the choices given below each. The intricate design ______ meticulous attention during manufacturing.

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) calls for

    "Call for" means to require or demand: an intricate design calls for meticulous attention. "Come to" means to reach or total, "get over" means to recover from, and "put off" means to postpone, so none of them fits.

  4. 4 of 10 · OPSC ASO 2022 Paper 1 General Awareness · Q1 · Indian Economy

    In the guidelines announced by Union Finance Minister in the 2022-2023 Budget, instead of using the term ‘Crypto Currency’, the term ______ is used.

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Virtual Digital Assets

    In the Union Budget 2022-23 the Finance Minister used the term 'Virtual Digital Assets' (VDAs), a category covering cryptocurrencies and NFTs. Income from transferring VDAs was made taxable at 30%, with 1% TDS on transfers.

  5. 5 of 10 · OPSC ASO 2022 Paper 2 Reasoning & Maths · Q1 · Reasoning & Mental Ability

    Select the correct option that indicates the arrangement of the given words in the order in which they appear in a telephone directory : (I) Krishanmurty (II) Krishnamurthy (III) Krishnmurthi (IV) Krishanmurthy (V) Krishnamurti

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) (IV), (I), (II), (V), (III)

    All begin 'Krish'. 'Krisha…' (I, IV) comes before 'Krishn…'; of these, Krishanmurt-h-y (IV) precedes Krishanmurt-y (I). Next, 'Krishna…' (II, V) precedes 'Krishnm…' (III), and Krishnamurt-h-y (II) precedes Krishnamurt-i (V). Order: IV, I, II, V, III.

  6. 6 of 10 · OPSC ASO 2022 Paper 3 English & Odia · Q1 · English

    Change the active voice into passive voice : I will clean the house every Saturday. The house ______ by me every Saturday.

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) will be cleaned

    The simple future passive is will + be + past participle. So "I will clean the house" becomes "The house will be cleaned by me". "Will cleaned" is ungrammatical (will needs a base form), and "cleaned" alone changes the tense to the past.

  7. 7 of 10 · OPSC ASO 2025 Paper 1 General Awareness · Q2 · Indian Polity

    Which of the following Articles are related to the Right to Religion ?

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Articles 25 to 28

    Articles 25–28 form the Right to Freedom of Religion: freedom of conscience and to profess, practise and propagate religion (25), to manage religious affairs (26), freedom from taxes for promoting a religion (27) and from religious instruction in certain institutions (28). Articles 14–18 cover equality, 19–22 freedom, and 29–30 cultural and educational rights.

  8. 8 of 10 · OPSC ASO 2025 Paper 2 Reasoning & Maths · Q2 · Reasoning & Mental Ability

    In a certain code language, STRAIGHT is written as TSARGITH. How will THURSDAY be written as in that language?

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) HTRUDSYA

    The word is split into pairs and the letters of each pair are swapped: ST-RA-IG-HT → TS-AR-GI-TH. Likewise TH-UR-SD-AY → HT-RU-DS-YA, i.e. HTRUDSYA.

  9. 9 of 10 · OPSC ASO 2025 Paper 3 English & Odia · Q2 · English

    Directions : Fill in the blanks in the following sentences by selecting the most appropriate option from the choices given below each. Each of the buildings ______ unique design.

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) has

    "Each" is singular, so it takes a singular verb even when followed by "of + plural noun": "Each of the buildings has ...". "Have", "are" and "were" are plural forms.

    Which State in India presented ‘Child Budget’ as part of the Annual State Budget ?

    Show answer

    Along with its 2022-23 annual budget (March 2022), Madhya Pradesh presented a separate 'Child Budget' for the first time, showing the funds earmarked across departments for schemes that benefit children.
