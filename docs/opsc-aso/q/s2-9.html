OPSC ASO 2022 Paper 2 Reasoning & Maths · Question 9

Question 9 of the OPSC ASO 2022 Paper II: Reasoning & Mental Ability and Mathematics Question Paper (27 August 2022). Try it first, then open the answer and explanation.

  1. 2022 · Paper II Reasoning & Maths · Q9 · Reasoning & Mental Ability · Non-verbal Reasoning

    Choose the box that is similar to the box formed from the given sheet of paper :

    1. 1, 2 and 3
    2. 1, 2 and 4
    3. 2 and 3
    4. 2, 3 and 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) 2, 3 and 4

    The four end-tabs fold in to close one face as a 2 × 2 block: half-disc and circle on top, cross and half-disc below. The bottom-right half-disc is the tab of the square-marked flap, so with this face in front the square is on the right face — box 3. Box 2 is the same box given a quarter-turn (square face hidden); box 4 shows three plain faces. Box 1 shows the square on top: impossible.
