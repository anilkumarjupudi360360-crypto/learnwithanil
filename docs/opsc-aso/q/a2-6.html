Question 6 of the OPSC ASO 2025 Paper II: Reasoning & Mental Ability and Mathematics Question Paper (9 November 2025). Try it first, then open the answer and explanation.

  1. 2025 · Paper II Reasoning & Maths · Q6 · Reasoning & Mental Ability · Non-verbal Reasoning

    Find the missing figure in the series from the given answer figures.

    1. 1
    2. 2
    3. 3
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 3

    Answer figure (3) completes the series. Track the three parts separately, using clock positions. (i) The inner square, with its shaded corner, turns 45° clockwise each time, which is why it appears alternately upright and as a diamond: the shaded corner goes top-right (1:30) → right (3) → bottom-right (4:30) → bottom (6), so next it is at the bottom-left (7:30), with the square upright again. (ii) The black dot moves 45° anticlockwise each time: left (9) → bottom-left (7:30) → bottom (6) → bottom-right (4:30), so next it is on the right (3). (iii) The white dot also moves 45° anticlockwise: right (3) → top-right (1:30) → top (12) → top-left (10:30), so next it is on the left (9). The fifth figure is therefore an upright square with the shaded corner at the bottom-left, the black dot on the right and the white dot on the left, which is figure (3). Figure (1) has the right corner but the dots swapped; (2) and (4) have the shaded corner at the top. Tip: when several parts move, follow each one on its own, noting its direction and step size.
