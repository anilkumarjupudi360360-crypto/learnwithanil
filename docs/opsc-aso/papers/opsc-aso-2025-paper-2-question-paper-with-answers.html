  1. Q1 · Reasoning & Mental Ability · Verbal Reasoning

    Choose the most appropriate option to complete the following series. V, R, N, ______, F, B

    1. K
    2. H
    3. J
    4. P
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) J

    Each letter is 4 places before the previous one: V(22) → R(18) → N(14) → J(10) → F(6) → B(2). So the missing letter is J.

  2. Q2 · Reasoning & Mental Ability · Verbal Reasoning

    In a certain code language, STRAIGHT is written as TSARGITH. How will THURSDAY be written as in that language?

    1. AYSDURTH
    2. HTRUDSYA
    3. UHTDRSYA
    4. HTRUDSAY
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) HTRUDSYA

    The word is split into pairs and the letters of each pair are swapped: ST-RA-IG-HT → TS-AR-GI-TH. Likewise TH-UR-SD-AY → HT-RU-DS-YA, i.e. HTRUDSYA.

  3. Q3 · Reasoning & Mental Ability · Verbal Reasoning

    A class of boys is standing in only one row. A boy is at thirty third position from both the ends in this sequence. How many boys are there in the class?

    1. 64
    2. 65
    3. 69
    4. 66
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) 65

    Total = (position from one end) + (position from the other end) − 1 = 33 + 33 − 1 = 65, because the boy himself is counted twice.

  4. Q4 · Reasoning & Mental Ability · Verbal Reasoning

    Suraj drove his bicycle towards the north. Then he turned to the right and covered a distance of 2 km. Then, he turned to the right and covered a distance of 4 km. He found himself 2 km east of his starting point. How far did he go by bicycle in the north direction?

    1. 2 km
    2. 4 km
    3. 6 km
    4. 8 km
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) 4 km

    After going north x km, he turns right (east) for 2 km and right again (south) for 4 km. Ending exactly 2 km east of the start means his north–south displacement is zero: x − 4 = 0, so x = 4 km.

  5. Q5 · Reasoning & Mental Ability · Verbal Reasoning

    At present, A is younger than B by 8 years. If 4 years ago, their ages were in the ratio 1 : 2, then what is the present age of B (in years)?

    1. 12
    2. 20
    3. 11
    4. 18
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) 20

    Let B = A + 8. Four years ago (A − 4) : (A + 4) = 1 : 2, so 2A − 8 = A + 4 and A = 12. Hence B = 12 + 8 = 20 years.

  6. Q6 · Reasoning & Mental Ability · Non-verbal Reasoning

    Find the missing figure in the series from the given answer figures.

    1. 1
    2. 2
    3. 3
    4. 4
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 3

    The shaded corner moves 45° clockwise at each step (top-right → right → bottom-right → bottom), while both dots move 45° anticlockwise (black: left → bottom-left → bottom → bottom-right; white: right → top-right → top → top-left). So next the shaded corner is at the bottom-left, the black dot on the right and the white dot on the left — answer figure (3).

  7. Q8 · Reasoning & Mental Ability · Non-verbal Reasoning

    Select the mirror image of the following figure :

    1. Figure A
    2. Figure B
    3. Figure C
    4. Figure D
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Figure A

    A vertical mirror (MN) swaps left and right but keeps top and bottom. From top to bottom, the zigzag in the given figure turns on the right, then the left, then the right again, so its mirror image must turn on the left, right, left. Only figure (A) shows this; (B) is tilted, and (C) and (D) are turned on their side.

  8. Q9 · Reasoning & Mental Ability · Non-verbal Reasoning

    Two different positions of the same dice are shown. Which number will be at the top if 6 is at the bottom?

    1. 4
    2. 3
    3. 5
    4. 2
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) 2

    1 is common to both views, so 5, 6, 3 and 2 all touch 1 and 4 is opposite 1. Reading each view clockwise from 1 gives 1-5-6 and 1-3-2, so the faces around 1 run 5, 6, 3, 2 in order; faces two apart are opposite: 5–3 and 6–2. With 6 at the bottom, 2 is on top.
