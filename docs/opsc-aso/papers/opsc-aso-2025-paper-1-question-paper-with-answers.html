  1. Q1 · History & National Movement · Modern India & National Movement

    What is the correct sequence of the following ? i. The Hartog Committee ii. The Hunter Education Commission iii. The Sadler University Commission iv. Kothari Education Commission

    1. i, ii, iii, iv
    2. iii, iv, ii, i
    3. ii, iii, iv, i
    4. ii, iii, i, iv
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) ii, iii, i, iv

    Chronological order: Hunter Education Commission (1882) → Sadler (Calcutta) University Commission (1917–19) → Hartog Committee (1929, auxiliary committee of the Simon Commission on education) → Kothari Education Commission (1964–66). Hence ii, iii, i, iv.

  2. Q2 · Indian Polity · Constitution & Polity

    Which of the following Articles are related to the Right to Religion ?

    1. Articles 14 to 18
    2. Articles 19 to 22
    3. Articles 25 to 28
    4. Articles 29 and 30
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Articles 25 to 28

    Articles 25–28 form the Right to Freedom of Religion: freedom of conscience and to profess, practise and propagate religion (25), to manage religious affairs (26), freedom from taxes for promoting a religion (27) and from religious instruction in certain institutions (28). Articles 14–18 cover equality, 19–22 freedom, and 29–30 cultural and educational rights.

  3. Q3 · Indian Polity · Constitution & Polity

    Which election method is used for Lok Sabha elections in India ?

    1. Proportional Representation
    2. First Past The Post (FPTP)
    3. Single Transferable Vote (STV)
    4. Mixed Electoral System
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) First Past The Post (FPTP)

    Lok Sabha members are elected from single-member territorial constituencies under the First Past The Post (simple plurality) system: the candidate with the most votes wins, even without a majority. Proportional representation by single transferable vote is used for Rajya Sabha and for Presidential and Vice-Presidential elections.

  4. Q4 · Geography & Environment · Environment & Ecology

    What is edge species ?

    1. The species which occur abundantly in the ecotone
    2. Species on the verge of extinction
    3. Rare species
    4. Endemic species
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) The species which occur abundantly in the ecotone

    An ecotone is the transition zone between two ecosystems (e.g., forest and grassland). Species that occur mostly or abundantly in this junction zone are called edge species, and the greater variety and density of organisms found there is the 'edge effect'.

  5. Q5 · Geography & Environment · Indian & World Geography

    Match the following : Composition of Atmosphere. a. Oxygen — 1. Is one of the most variable gaseous substance present in atmosphere, constituting between 0.2 to 4 percent. b. Nitrogen — 2. Constitutes only 21 percent of the total volume of atmosphere. c. Ozone — 3. Among gases accounts for 78 percent of total atmospheric volume. d. Water vapour — 4. It forms less than 0.00005 percent by volume of the atmosphere and is unevenly distributed.

    1. a-1, b-2, c-3, d-4
    2. a-2, b-3, c-1, d-4
    3. a-2, b-3, c-4, d-1
    4. a-4, b-1, c-3, d-2
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) a-2, b-3, c-4, d-1

    By volume, nitrogen is about 78% of dry air and oxygen about 21%. Ozone is a trace gas (less than 0.00005%), concentrated mainly in the stratosphere. Water vapour is the most variable gas: up to about 4% in warm, humid tropics and very little over deserts and polar regions. So a-2, b-3, c-4, d-1.
