    Convert from direct into indirect speech. Ayushi said to the group, "We have had enough of suffering today. It is time to march forward."

    2. Ayushi asked the group that they had had enough of suffering this day and it was time to march forward
    3. Ayushi told the group that they had enough of suffering and that it is time to march forward
    4. Ayushi told the group that we had had enough of suffering this day and it was time to march forward
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Ayushi told the group that they had had enough of suffering that day and it was time to march forward
