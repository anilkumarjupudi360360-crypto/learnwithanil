Fill in the Blanks

  1. NDA II 2023 · GAT · Q1 · English · Fill in the Blanks

    As the Southwest monsoon ________ through most of July, flash floods caused by the waters of the Narmada hit Jabalpur town.

    1. lashed
    2. flourished
    3. occupied
    4. dried
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) lashed

    Heavy rain is said to "lash" a place; a monsoon that lashed (struck violently) through July explains the flash floods. "Flourished", "occupied" and "dried" do not fit rain causing floods.

  2. NDA II 2023 · GAT · Q2 · English · Fill in the Blanks

    Given the scale of the challenges that ________ us today, our company proposes more viable plans for sustainability.

    1. supports
    2. conflict
    3. confronts
    4. convince
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) confronts

    Challenges "confront" (stand before and face) us, so "confronts" is the only word with the right meaning. "Supports" and "convince" make no sense here, and "conflict" would need "with". (Strictly, the plural "challenges" takes "confront", but the meaning is what is being tested.)

  3. NDA II 2023 · GAT · Q3 · English · Fill in the Blanks

    It is a really tough course, I am just worried that I won't be able to go the ________.

    1. way
    2. distance
    3. far
    4. route
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) distance

    "Go the distance" is an idiom meaning to complete something difficult to the end, which fits worry about finishing a tough course.

  4. NDA II 2023 · GAT · Q4 · English · Fill in the Blanks

    The ________ of the advancement made in agricultural productivity in India has been remarkable.

    1. extent
    2. context
    3. distance
    4. syntax
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) extent

    "Extent" means the degree or scale of something, so "the extent of the advancement" was remarkable; the other words do not describe how much progress was made.

    4. explores
    Show answer

    2. stabilise
    3. stratify
    4. establish
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) stabilise

  7. NDA II 2023 · GAT · Q7 · English · Fill in the Blanks

    2. position
    3. condition
    4. period
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) threshold

    A "threshold" is the point of entry or beginning of a new stage, so adolescence is the threshold through which a child enters adulthood; "wherein ... enters" suggests a doorway rather than a mere "period".
