How the mock test works

  • Questions appear in English and Telugu as printed. Switch language with the buttons at the top.
  • The timer gives you one minute per question. You can move between questions and change answers until you submit.
  • When you finish you get your score, accuracy and a topic-wise analysis, with the correct answer and an explanation for every question. Wrong answers go to your Mistakes notebook.
  • Previous papers as tests need the ₹45 PYQ Pass. Register once with your phone number and use the same number when you pay.
