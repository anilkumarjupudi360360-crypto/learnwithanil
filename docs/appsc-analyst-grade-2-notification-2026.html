APPSC Analyst Grade-II Notification 2026: 18 posts in the AP Pollution Control Board

APPSC Notification No. 21/2026 (13 October 2026) invites online applications for 18 posts of Analyst Grade-II in the A.P. Pollution Control Board, the laboratory analysts who test air, water and waste samples. Apply by 2 November 2026. Below: dates, eligibility, the exam pattern and syllabus, and the previous papers to start practising.

Who can apply

A bachelor's degree in Chemistry, Biochemistry, Biology or Environmental Sciences from a recognised university in India; first-class graduates are preferred. Fee: ₹250 application processing fee plus ₹80 examination fee; SC, ST, BC, PwBD and ex-service candidates, white-card families and unemployed youth of AP are exempt from the ₹80 examination fee.

Paper II syllabus: Common Paper

  • Chemistry: non-transition elements and their halides and oxides, polymorphism of carbon and sulphur, boranes and carboranes; photochemistry (laws, HCl and HBr reactions, fluorescence, phosphorescence, Jablonski diagram); spectroscopy and the Beer–Lambert law (Cr and Mn estimation); chromatography (TLC, gas-liquid, paper, column, HPLC); terpenoids; amino acids and proteins; solvent-free, ultrasound- and microwave-assisted green synthesis; furan and pyrrole.
  • Biochemistry: enzyme inhibition and mechanisms (chymotrypsin, trypsin, lysozyme); coenzymes; zymogen activation and multi-enzyme complexes; immunology, immunoglobulins, ELISA, vaccines and hypersensitivity; blood and lymph; autoimmunity; nervous and excretory systems; endocrinology; nutritional biochemistry and vitamins; clinical biochemistry (liver, renal, GTT, gastric and pancreatic function tests).
  • Biology: bacteria (structure of E. coli, nutrition, reproduction, economic importance); viruses (TMV, polio, HIV); plant and animal cells and organelles; mitosis and meiosis; Mendelian genetics, linkage, cytoplasmic inheritance, mutations; origin of life, Darwinism, Hardy–Weinberg law; nucleosome, DNA types and replication, DNA repair, RNA and protein synthesis.
  • Environmental Science: environment and ecology, ecosystems, energy flow, food chains, ecological pyramids and succession; forest, water, mineral and energy resources; air, water and soil pollution and waste management; greenhouse gases, global warming, the ozone hole, biogas; environmental assessment (plant, flora, animal and aquatic surveys); eco-planning, land use, urban and rural planning.

Paper I syllabus: General Studies & Mental Ability

Current events (international, national, Andhra Pradesh); general science and technology; history of India with a focus on AP and the national movement; geography of India and AP; Indian polity and governance; Indian economy and planning; sustainable development and environment; disaster management; logical reasoning; data analysis.

Quick answers (as of 10 November 2026)

Can I still apply for APPSC Analyst Grade-II?

The notification gave 13 October to 2 November 2026 (11:59 pm). A coaching website (Adda247) lists Analyst Grade-II among the APPSC posts whose last date was extended to 12 November 2026, but we could not confirm it from APPSC; check the candidate login on psc.ap.gov.in.

When is the APPSC Analyst Grade-II exam?

Not announced yet. It will be a computer-based test (CBRT); APPSC will publish the date in a web note and the hall ticket.

How many Analyst Grade-II posts are there?

18, in the A.P. Pollution Control Board, under Notification 21/2026 of 13 October 2026.

Who can apply for APPSC Analyst Grade-II?

Graduates in Chemistry, Biochemistry, Biology or Environmental Sciences, aged 18 to 44 on 1 July 2026 (with the usual relaxations).

Is there negative marking in the Analyst Grade-II exam?

Yes. One-third of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer.

What is the Analyst Grade-II salary?

Pay scale ₹48,440 – ₹1,37,220 a month.
