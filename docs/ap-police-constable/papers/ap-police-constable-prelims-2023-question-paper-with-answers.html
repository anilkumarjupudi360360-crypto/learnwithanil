  9. Q9 · English · Grammar

    Choose the sentence which is grammatically correct.

    1. The event be telecasted next week.
    2. The event will be telecast next week.
    3. The event is telecasted next week.
    4. The event will telecasts next week.
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) The event will be telecast next week.

    A future passive needs "will be" plus the past participle, and the standard past participle of telecast is "telecast". Option (1) lacks "will", (3) uses the present tense for a future event, and (4) puts "-s" after "will".
