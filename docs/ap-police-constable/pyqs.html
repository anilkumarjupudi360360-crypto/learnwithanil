  1. 1 of 10 · AP Police Constable Prelims 2023 · Q1 · English

    Choose the word which is similar in meaning to the underlined word in this sentence. The poor family has no visible means of "subsistence".

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) livelihood

    Subsistence means the bare means of staying alive, that is, a livelihood. "Employment" is a job, which is not the same as the minimum needed to survive.

  2. 2 of 10 · AP Police Constable Prelims 2023 · Q2 · English

    Choose the word that is nearest in meaning to the underlined word in the sentence. His Cartoons "lampooned" the leaders of his time.

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Ridiculed

    To lampoon is to mock or attack someone publicly through satire, so the nearest meaning is "ridiculed". "Praised" and "elevated" are the opposite in tone, and "exposed" means uncovered, not mocked.

  3. 3 of 10 · AP Police Constable Prelims 2023 · Q3 · English

    Choose the antonym of the underlined word in the sentence. Kiran's attendance at school was "sporadic".

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Regular

    Sporadic means happening only now and then, at irregular intervals, so its opposite is "regular". "Occasional" is a synonym, not an antonym.

  4. 4 of 10 · AP Police Constable Prelims 2023 · Q4 · English

    Choose the word that has the opposite meaning of the underlined word in the sentence. When she saw the stranger, her eyes "dilated" with fear.

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) contracted

    To dilate is to become wider or larger, so the opposite is "contracted" (became narrower). "Widened" is a synonym of dilated.

  5. 5 of 10 · AP Police Constable Prelims 2023 · Q5 · English

    Choose the correctly spelt word.

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Skilful

    The correct spellings are "playwright", "misspell" and "until", so options (1), (2) and (4) are wrong. "Skilful" is the correct (British) spelling; American English writes "skillful".

  6. 6 of 10 · AP Police Constable Prelims 2023 · Q6 · English

    Choose the correctly spelt word.

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) perseverance

    The word is spelt "perseverance" (persever + ance): it has "sev" in the middle, not "cev", and ends in "-ance", not "-ence" or "-anse".

  7. 7 of 10 · AP Police Constable Prelims 2023 · Q7 · English

    Choose the appropriate set of prepositions and fill in the blanks in the following sentence. The Sun is ______ the clouds, Vani drew that picture ______ a Crayon.

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) above, with

    The Sun is "above" the clouds (position higher than them), and a picture is drawn "with" a crayon (the instrument used). The other pairs do not fit both blanks.

  8. 8 of 10 · AP Police Constable Prelims 2023 · Q8 · English

    Choose the correct set of prepositions and fill in the blanks in the following sentence. Stirred ______ pride and envy Satan rebelled ______ God.

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) by, against

    "Stirred by pride and envy" shows the cause that moved Satan, and one rebels "against" someone. So the correct pair is "by, against".

  9. 9 of 10 · AP Police Constable Prelims 2023 · Q9 · English

    Choose the sentence which is grammatically correct.

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) The event will be telecast next week.

    A future passive needs "will be" plus the past participle, and the standard past participle of telecast is "telecast". Option (1) lacks "will", (3) uses the present tense for a future event, and (4) puts "-s" after "will".

  10. 10 of 10 · AP Police Constable Prelims 2023 · Q10 · English

    Choose the grammatically correct sentence.

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Of the two major political parties in Britain, the Conservatives and the Labour, the latter represents the interests of the working class.

    "The latter" refers to the second of two things just mentioned, which is what the sentence needs. "Later" (time), "letter" (a written message) and "latest" (most recent) do not fit the meaning.
