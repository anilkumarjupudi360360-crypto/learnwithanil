  1. 1 of 10 · APPSC Analyst Grade 2 2025 Paper 2 Biochemistry · Q1 · Biochemistry

    Reversible inhibitors:

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) slow down a chemical reaction but do not stop it completely

    Reversible inhibitors bind the enzyme through non-covalent bonds, so they only lower the rate of the reaction and the enzyme regains full activity when the inhibitor is removed or diluted out. They do not abolish the reaction altogether (that is typical of irreversible inhibitors), and they never speed it up.

  2. 2 of 10 · APPSC Analyst Grade 2 2025 Paper 2 Chemistry · Q1 · Chemistry

    A statement is given, followed by two conclusions numbered I and II. You have to consider the statement to be true, even if it seems to be at variance from commonly known facts. You are to decide which of the given conclusions can definitely be drawn from the given statement. Statement: Group 1 elements have lower ionization energies than Group 2 elements. Conclusions: I: Group 1 elements form +1 cations by losing one electron. II: Group 1 elements are more reactive than Group 2 elements.

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Both conclusion I and conclusion II are correct

    A low ionization energy means an atom gives up an electron easily. Group 1 atoms have a single outer s electron, so they readily lose it to form M+ ions (I), and the same ease of electron loss makes them more reactive than Group 2 metals (II). Both conclusions are therefore accepted.

  3. 3 of 10 · APPSC Analyst Grade 2 2025 Paper 2 Environmental Science · Q1 · Environmental Science

    The optical efficiency of a solar thermal collector is defined as:

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) ratio of solar radiation absorbed in the absorber to intercepted by the collector aperture

    Optical efficiency is the share of the radiation that falls on the collector aperture and is finally absorbed by the absorber: optical efficiency = radiation absorbed in the absorber / radiation intercepted by the aperture. It combines the mirror reflectance, the glass-cover transmittance and the absorber absorptance (Duffie and Beckman; Kalogirou). Options 1, 3 and 4 compare other pairs of quantities, such as only the glass transmittance.

  4. 4 of 10 · APPSC Analyst Grade 2 2025 Paper 2 Biology · Q1 · Biology

    Bacterial cell walls contain which of the following types of structural polysaccharides?

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Peptidoglycan

    Peptidoglycan (murein), made of alternating N-acetylglucosamine and N-acetylmuramic acid units cross-linked by short peptides, is the structural polysaccharide of bacterial cell walls. Chitin occurs in fungal walls and arthropod exoskeletons, cellulose in plant cell walls, and hyaluronic acid in animal connective tissue.

  5. 5 of 10 · APPSC Analyst Grade 2 2025 Paper 2 Biochemistry · Q2 · Biochemistry

    In a non-competitive inhibition, the Km value is ______ and Vmax is ________.

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) unchanged, lowered

    A non-competitive inhibitor binds a site away from the active site and binds the free enzyme and the enzyme-substrate complex equally well. It removes active enzyme molecules from the reaction, so Vmax falls, while substrate binding is not affected, so Km stays the same.

  6. 6 of 10 · APPSC Analyst Grade 2 2025 Paper 2 Chemistry · Q2 · Chemistry

    When aluminium carbide (Al4C3) reacts with water, the products formed are:

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) methane and aluminium hydroxide

    Aluminium carbide is a methanide containing C4- ions, so water protonates each carbon to give methane: Al4C3 + 12H2O → 4Al(OH)3 + 3CH4. The hydroxide (not the oxide) forms because the reaction takes place in excess water; ethane is not formed (calcium carbide, CaC2, is the one that gives ethyne).

  7. 7 of 10 · APPSC Analyst Grade 2 2025 Paper 2 Environmental Science · Q2 · Environmental Science

    Two statements are given, labelled as Assertion (A) and Reason (R). Select the correct option with reference to the statements. Assertion (A): Photovoltaic detectors normally use silicon solar cells measuring the short circuit current. Reason (R): Photovoltaic detectors generate a voltage proportional to the temperature difference between the hot and cold junctions.

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

    Photovoltaic detectors such as silicon solar cells are normally read by measuring the short-circuit current, which is proportional to the incident radiation, so A is true. APPSC's key is option 1, which treats R as a correct explanation. However, a voltage proportional to the temperature difference between hot and cold junctions is the working principle of a thermopile (Seebeck effect), not of a photovoltaic cell, so many sources would take R as false (option 3). (Official key; some sources differ.)

  8. 8 of 10 · APPSC Analyst Grade 2 2025 Paper 2 Biology · Q2 · Biology

    Select the option that is correct regarding the following two statements labelled Assertion (A) and Reason (R). Assertion (A): Some bacteria demonstrate chemolithotrophy, which allows them to oxidise inorganic substances for energy production. Reason (R): Bacteria lack mitochondria; hence they completely rely on glycolysis for ATP synthesis.

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) A is true, but R is false.

    The assertion is true: chemolithotrophs such as Nitrosomonas, Nitrobacter and Thiobacillus oxidise inorganic substances (NH3, NO2-, H2S, Fe2+) to obtain energy. The reason is false: bacteria do lack mitochondria, but they make ATP by respiration on the plasma membrane, by photophosphorylation or by fermentation, so they do not depend on glycolysis alone.

  9. 9 of 10 · APPSC Analyst Grade 2 2025 Paper 2 Biochemistry · Q3 · Biochemistry

    Certain molecules that resemble the substrate of the enzyme and compete for the active sites of enzyme, thereby reduce its efficiency. What are they?

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Competitive inhibitors

    Competitive inhibitors are structural analogues of the substrate and occupy the active site, so substrate and inhibitor compete for the same site. Uncompetitive inhibitors bind only the enzyme-substrate complex, and feedback inhibitors are end products that act at an allosteric site.

  10. 10 of 10 · APPSC Analyst Grade 2 2025 Paper 2 Chemistry · Q3 · Chemistry

    Which of the following statements is FALSE about non-transition elements?

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Non-transition elements typically show multiple oxidation states.

    In the keyed reading, non-transition (main-group) elements mostly show one characteristic oxidation state (+1 for Group 1, +2 for Group 2), and variable oxidation states are the hallmark of transition elements. Their compounds are usually colourless because they lack d-d transitions, and the group range in option 4 is correct. Note that "full outermost shell" in option 1 is true only of the noble gases, and p-block elements do show variable oxidation states, so this item is open to argument. (Official key; some sources differ.)
