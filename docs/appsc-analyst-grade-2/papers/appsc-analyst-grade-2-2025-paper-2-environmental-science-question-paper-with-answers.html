  1. Q1 · Environmental Science · Natural Resources

    The optical efficiency of a solar thermal collector is defined as:

    1. fraction of the rays reflected from the mirror to solar radiation absorbed in the absorber
    2. ratio of solar radiation absorbed in the absorber to intercepted by the collector aperture
    3. ratio of solar radiation transmitted from the absorber to intercepted by the collector aperture
    4. transmittance of glass envelope of absorber to solar radiation absorbed in the absorber
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) ratio of solar radiation absorbed in the absorber to intercepted by the collector aperture

    Optical efficiency is the share of the radiation that falls on the collector aperture and is finally absorbed by the absorber: optical efficiency = radiation absorbed in the absorber / radiation intercepted by the aperture. It combines the mirror reflectance, the glass-cover transmittance and the absorber absorptance (Duffie and Beckman; Kalogirou). Options 1, 3 and 4 compare other pairs of quantities, such as only the glass transmittance.

  2. Q2 · Environmental Science · Natural Resources

    Two statements are given, labelled as Assertion (A) and Reason (R). Select the correct option with reference to the statements. Assertion (A): Photovoltaic detectors normally use silicon solar cells measuring the short circuit current. Reason (R): Photovoltaic detectors generate a voltage proportional to the temperature difference between the hot and cold junctions.

    1. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    2. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A
    3. A is true but R is false
    4. A is false but R is true
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A

    Photovoltaic detectors such as silicon solar cells are normally read by measuring the short-circuit current, which is proportional to the incident radiation, so A is true. APPSC's key is option 1, which treats R as a correct explanation. However, a voltage proportional to the temperature difference between hot and cold junctions is the working principle of a thermopile (Seebeck effect), not of a photovoltaic cell, so many sources would take R as false (option 3). (Official key; some sources differ.)

  3. Q3 · Environmental Science · Natural Resources

    Floating Drum Type Biogas Plants are ________ Type Biogas Plants.

    1. Constant Volume
    2. Constant Pressure
    3. Constant Temperature
    4. Constant Enthalpy
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Constant Pressure

    In a floating-drum (KVIC) plant the gas holder drum rises and falls as gas is stored and used, so the gas is supplied at a constant pressure fixed by the weight of the drum while the gas volume changes. In a fixed-dome plant the gas space has a fixed volume and the pressure varies, so that one is the constant-volume type.

  4. Q4 · Environmental Science · Natural Resources

    In which biomass conversion technologies can pelletization, briquetting and the fuel extraction process be seen?

    1. Direct Combustion
    2. Physical Method
    3. Bio-chemical Method
    4. Thermo-chemical Method
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Physical Method

    Pelletisation and briquetting (densification) and the extraction of oil or fuel from seeds are mechanical, physical processes that change the form of the biomass but not its chemical nature, so they come under physical (mechanical) conversion. Direct combustion, gasification and pyrolysis are thermo-chemical routes, while anaerobic digestion and fermentation are bio-chemical routes.

  5. Q5 · Environmental Science · Natural Resources

    The part of solar radiation that reaches the earth's surface with no change in direction is called:

    1. beam radiation
    2. diffuse radiation
    3. arbitrary radiation
    4. thermal radiation
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) beam radiation

    Beam (direct) radiation is the part of sunlight that reaches the ground without being scattered by the atmosphere, so it keeps its direction from the sun's disc. The scattered part, which arrives from all directions of the sky, is diffuse radiation, and the two together make up global (total) radiation. "Arbitrary radiation" is not a standard term.

  6. Q6 · Environmental Science · Natural Resources

    In a solar air heating system, the flow rate through an air collector and storage loop is a compromise between ________ and ________.

    1. pressure drop; energy transfer to the air stream
    2. temperature drop; energy transfer to the air stream
    3. volume drop; energy transfer to the air stream
    4. temperature drop; energy transfer to the water stream
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) pressure drop; energy transfer to the air stream

    A high air flow improves heat transfer from the collector to the air, but the pressure drop across the collector and storage bed rises steeply with flow and needs more fan power. A low flow saves fan power but transfers less energy. The design flow rate is therefore a compromise between pressure drop and energy transfer to the air stream; option 4 wrongly refers to a water stream.

  7. Q7 · Environmental Science · Natural Resources

    ____________ is a solar collector using the absorption of solar radiation at the bottom of a 2 m to 3 m deep body of water to generate low-temperature heat.

    1. Non convecting solar pond
    2. Solar photovoltaic
    3. Solar desailation
    4. Solar distillation
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Non convecting solar pond

    A non-convecting (salt-gradient) solar pond is a pond a few metres deep in which a salt gradient stops convection, so heat absorbed at the dark bottom is trapped in the lower layer and can be drawn off as low-temperature heat (up to about 80-90 degrees C). Solar photovoltaic produces electricity, and solar desalination and distillation are water-purification processes, not pond-type heat collectors.
