  8. FRO Mains 2020 General Forestry I · Q4 · Forest Protection · Pests & Diseases

    Herbicides are used to kill:

    1. fungi
    2. insects
    3. undesirable plants or weeds
    4. bacteria
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) undesirable plants or weeds

    A herbicide (weedicide) is a chemical used to kill or suppress unwanted plants (weeds). Fungicides act on fungi, insecticides on insects and bactericides on bacteria.

  9. FRO Mains 2020 General Forestry II · Q8 · Forest Protection · Pests & Diseases

    ______ invades monoculture of Ailanthus excels causing defoliation.

    1. Ascotis imparta
    2. Tonica niviferana
    3. Atteva fabriciella
    4. Plecoptera reflexa
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Atteva fabriciella

    Atteva fabriciella (the ailanthus webworm moth) is the defoliator of Ailanthus excelsa; its larvae web and feed on the leaves, and outbreaks are common in pure plantations. Plecoptera reflexa, for example, is a known defoliator of Dalbergia sissoo, not Ailanthus.
