  4. FRO Mains 2023 General Forestry I · Q8 · Natural Resources · Forest & Water Resources

    All trees present outside the recorded forest area (RFA) irrespective of their number and patch size, are termed as:

    1. forest cover
    2. trees outside forest
    3. tree cover
    4. agroforestry
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) trees outside forest

    The India State of Forest Report defines Trees Outside Forest (TOF) as all trees growing outside the recorded forest area, whatever the patch size or number. Tree cover is only the part of TOF occurring in patches smaller than 1 ha, and forest cover refers to tree-covered land of more than 1 ha with canopy density above 10%.

  20. FRO Mains 2020 General Forestry I · Q10 · Natural Resources · Mineral, Food & Energy Resources

    How many agro-climatic zones are there in India?

    1. 10
    2. 13
    3. 15
    4. 20
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 15

    The Planning Commission divided India into 15 agro-climatic regions in 1989 on the basis of soil type, rainfall, temperature and water resources, for regional agricultural planning. The ICAR's NARP had earlier delineated 127 agro-climatic zones within the country, so 15 is the standard "regional" figure.
