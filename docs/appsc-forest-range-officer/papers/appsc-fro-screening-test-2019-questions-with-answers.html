  1. Q1 · Mental Ability & Data Interpretation · Data Interpretation

    The following pie diagram shows the monthly expenditure of Rakesh and Sohan. Rakesh earns Rs. 15,000 per month and Sohan Rs 18,000 per month. (The percentage shares from the diagram are given below.)

    ExpenditureRakeshSohan
    Children Education12%8%
    House19%27%
    Miscellaneous31%32%
    Food etc38%33%

    How much more or less does Sohan spend on children’s education than Rakesh?

    Show answer

    Answer: Rs. 360 Less

    Convert each share into rupees using each person's own income. Rakesh spends 12% of Rs 15,000 = Rs 1,800 on children's education; Sohan spends 8% of Rs 18,000 = Rs 1,440. So Sohan spends 1,800 − 1,440 = Rs 360 less than Rakesh.

    What is the measure of the angle used to represent the expenditure on ‘House’ by Rakesh in (approximately)?

    Show answer

    Answer: 68°

    In a pie diagram the full circle of 360° stands for 100%, so 1% = 3.6°. Rakesh's share for House is 19%, so its angle = 19 × 3.6° = 68.4°, which is about 68°. The angle depends only on the percentage share, not on the income.

  5. Q5 · General Science & Technology · Chemistry

    Arrange the following in order of decreasing magnetic moments: Fe²⁺, Mn²⁺, Cu²⁺, V³⁺

    Show answer

    Answer: Mn²⁺ > Fe²⁺ > V³⁺ > Cu²⁺

    The spin-only magnetic moment is μ = √[n(n + 2)] BM, where n is the number of unpaired d-electrons. Mn²⁺ (3d⁵) has 5 unpaired electrons (5.92 BM), Fe²⁺ (3d⁶) has 4 (4.90 BM), V³⁺ (3d²) has 2 (2.83 BM) and Cu²⁺ (3d⁹) has 1 (1.73 BM). Hence Mn²⁺ > Fe²⁺ > V³⁺ > Cu²⁺.

  6. Q6 · General Science & Technology · Chemistry

    Which of the following is/are correct about sigma bond? 1. Formed by axial overlapping 2. Weaker 3. Independent existence 4. Free rotation possible

    Show answer

    Answer: 1, 3 and 4

    A sigma bond forms by head-on (axial) overlap of orbitals along the internuclear axis, so it can exist on its own and the bonded atoms can rotate freely about it. Statement 2 is wrong: the greater overlap makes a sigma bond stronger than a pi bond, which forms by sideways overlap, exists only alongside a sigma bond and prevents free rotation.

  7. Q7 · General Science & Technology · Physics

    The temperature at which a conductor becomes a super conductor is called:

    Show answer

    Answer: Transition Temperature

    When certain materials are cooled, their electrical resistance suddenly falls to zero at a particular temperature called the transition (or critical) temperature, Tc. For mercury, in which Kamerlingh Onnes discovered superconductivity in 1911, Tc is about 4.2 K. Below Tc a superconductor also expels magnetic fields (Meissner effect).

  8. Q8 · Mental Ability & Data Interpretation · Arithmetic

    The principal amount which yields a Compound Interest of Rs. 208 in second year at 4% is:

    Show answer

    Answer: Rs. 5000

    Compound interest for the second year is interest on the first year's amount, i.e. P × r × (1 + r). So P × 0.04 × 1.04 = 208, giving 0.0416P = 208 and P = Rs 5,000. Check: first-year interest = 200, amount 5,200; second-year interest = 4% of 5,200 = 208.

  9. Q9 · Mental Ability & Data Interpretation · Arithmetic

    A person sold a box of pen at a gain of 15%. Had he bought it for 25% less and sold it for Rs. 60 less he would have made profit of 32%. The cost price of the box of pen was:

    Show answer

    Answer: Rs. 375

    Let the cost price be x, so the selling price is 1.15x. The new cost price is 0.75x and the new selling price, 1.15x − 60, must be 132% of 0.75x = 0.99x. So 1.15x − 60 = 0.99x, 0.16x = 60 and x = Rs 375. Check: new CP 281.25, new SP 371.25, profit 90 = 32%.

  10. Q10 · Mental Ability & Data Interpretation · Arithmetic

    There are 40 students in a hostel. If the number of students increases by 5 and the expenses of the mess increased by Rs. 45 per day while the average expenditure per head decreases by Rs. 1 then find the total original expenditure of the mess:

    Show answer

    Answer: Rs. 720

    Let the original average be Rs x per head, so the original expenditure is 40x. With 45 students the average is x − 1, so 45(x − 1) = 40x + 45. Then 45x − 45 = 40x + 45, 5x = 90 and x = 18. Original expenditure = 40 × 18 = Rs 720 (new: 45 × 17 = 765 = 720 + 45).
