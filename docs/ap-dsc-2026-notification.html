AP DSC 2026: 2,721 teacher posts

The Andhra Pradesh School Education Department issued brief notifications on 13 October 2026 for 2,721 teacher posts (DSC-2026): 2,111 School Assistant and SGT posts in government, ZP, MPP and municipal schools, 350 special-education posts, and 260 posts in residential societies and model schools. The detailed notification, with the application dates, fee, age limit and exam schedule, was issued on Wednesday 4 November 2026 on tet2dsc.apcfss.in, and online applications open on 11 November 2026 (Deccan Chronicle and Sakshi Education, 5 November 2026). The exam is a computer-based test (CBT).

Eligibility, fee and TET: the 2025 rules

  • Age (2025): 18 to 44 years, up to 49 for SC, ST, BC and EWS candidates and 54 for persons with disabilities. The reference date for 2026 will be in the detailed notification.
  • Fee (2025): ₹750 for each post applied for.
  • TET: SA (except Physical Education) and SGT candidates must have passed APTET or CTET; the TET score carries 20% of the final marks. PET, SA (Physical Education), PGT, PD and Principal posts need no TET.
  • SGT: Intermediate with 50% (45% for SC, ST, BC and PwBD) and a two-year D.Ed / D.El.Ed (or B.El.Ed). SA: a degree in the subject with 50% (45%) and B.Ed with that methodology. PGT: a post-graduate degree with 50% and B.Ed.
  • The Special TET of August 2026 (for in-service teachers) cannot be used to apply for DSC; the regular AP TET of June 2026 (results in September 2026) can.

These are the Mega DSC 2025 rules (G.O.Ms.No.15 and 16, 19 April 2025). The 2026 detailed notification may change them: check it before applying.
