SSC GD Constable 2027 notification: when it comes, and where GD 2026 stands

SSC's exam calendar listed the Constable (GD) 2027 notification for 30 September 2026, with the computer-based exam in January–March 2027. As of 15 November 2026 it has not been issued. The 2026 recruitment (25,487 posts) is at the physical-test stage: the CBE result came on 12 October 2026. Below: the status of both, the exam pattern, who can apply and the SSC GD previous papers to practise.

Who can apply (as in the 2026 notification)

  • Class 10 (matriculation) pass from a recognised board.
  • Age 18 to 23 years on the cut-off date (1 January 2026 for GD 2026); 3 years more for OBC and 5 for SC and ST.
  • Posts: Constable (GD) in the BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB and SSF, Rifleman (GD) in the Assam Rifles, Sepoy in the NCB. Pay Level 3: ₹21,700 – ₹69,100.
  • Fee ₹100; women, SC, ST and ex-servicemen pay nothing.

Quick answers (as of 15 November 2026)

Is the SSC GD 2027 notification out?

Not as of 15 November 2026. SSC's calendar listed it for 30 September 2026; coaching sites now expect it later in 2026.

When is the SSC GD 2027 exam?

Tentatively January to March 2027, as per SSC's calendar. The exact dates will come after the notification.

Is the SSC GD 2026 result out?

Yes. SSC declared the computer-based exam result on 12 October 2026, shortlisting 1,97,408 candidates for the PET/PST.

When is the SSC GD 2026 PET/PST?

News reports give 26 November to about 11 December 2026 (tentative). The CRPF issues the call letters on rect.crpf.gov.in; go by the date on yours.

Is there negative marking in SSC GD?

Yes. 0.25 mark is cut for each wrong answer; a right answer earns 2 marks.

What is the SSC GD age limit?

18 to 23 years on the cut-off date, with 3 more years for OBC and 5 for SC and ST.

What is the SSC GD Constable salary?

Pay Level 3: ₹21,700 – ₹69,100 a month in basic pay, plus allowances.
