TGPSC HMWSSB Manager 2026: 130 posts, exam in December

TGPSC notified 130 posts of Manager (Engineering) in the Hyderabad Metropolitan Water Supply & Sewerage Board on 18 September 2026 (Notification 04/OG/HM/2026). Applications closed on 17 October 2026. The written exam is tentatively in December 2026; the exact date has not been announced. Below: the dates, the posts, the pattern and the previous papers.

Selection is on the written marks only; there is no interview. Qualifying marks: 40% OC/EWS/sports, 35% BC, 30% SC/ST/PH. The notification does not mention negative marking. Fee: ₹1,000 (OC/BC) or ₹500 (SC/ST/PwD) plus ₹120 exam fee (unemployed exempt).

Quick answers (as of 4 November 2026)

Is the HMWSSB Manager exam date out?

No. The notification says the exam is tentatively in December 2026; TGPSC had not announced the exact date as of 4 November 2026.

Was the HMWSSB Manager last date extended?

No extension notice appeared on TGPSC's website. Applications closed on 17 October 2026 at 5 pm.

When will the HMWSSB Manager hall ticket come?

Not yet. As per the notification, hall tickets open 7 days before the exam.

Is there negative marking in the HMWSSB Manager exam?

The notification does not mention negative marking.

How many HMWSSB Manager posts are for Civil?

The notification gives shares only: 80% Civil, 15% Electrical/Mechanical, 5% CSE/IT/ECE of the 130 posts.

Is Paper 2 out of 150 marks?

No. Paper II has 150 questions carrying 300 marks; Paper I is 150 marks; the total is 450.

Is there an interview for HMWSSB Manager?

No. Selection is on the written exam marks only.

What is the HMWSSB Manager salary and age limit?

Pay ₹54,220 – ₹1,33,630; age 18 to 44 on 1 July 2026, with relaxations.
