  1. 1 of 10 · OLM PYQ Set 1 SHGs, NRLM, Mission Shakti & Livelihoods · Q1 · Livelihoods, SHGs & Mission Shakti

    [TS Police SI FWE 2023 Paper 4] What is the primary objective of a Self Help Group (SHG) In India ?

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) To Provide micro-finance to groups

    A Self Help Group is a small group of poor people, mostly women, who save regularly and lend to members, and through the NABARD-led SHG-Bank Linkage Programme get small bank loans, so its core purpose is micro-finance. Farm technology, seeds and education are not its primary aim.

  2. 2 of 10 · OLM PYQ Set 2 Rural Development, Panchayati Raj & Financial Inclusion · Q1 · Rural Development & Panchayati Raj

    [UPPSC RO/ARO Prelims 2025] Which one of the following is a correct match ?

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Etawah Pilot Project – Albert Myre

    The Etawah Pilot Project (1948, Mahewa, Etawah district, UP) was a rural development experiment led by the American town planner Albert Mayer; it became a model for the Community Development Programme of 1952. The Grow More Food Campaign began in 1943 under the British government, the co-operative movement began with the 1904 Act, and Sevagram is linked with Mahatma Gandhi.

  3. 3 of 10 · OLM PYQ Set 3 Odisha GK, Mission Shakti & Odisha Schemes · Q1 · Livelihoods, SHGs & Mission Shakti

    [OSSSC Forester, Forest Guard & Excise Constable 2026 (20 Apr, Shift 3)] Self-Help Groups (SHGs) play an important role in rural development. What is their main function in rural areas?

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Providing loans to members

    An SHG's core function is mobilising its members' small savings and lending among themselves (internal lending), and it later gets bank linkage for bigger loans. Supplying seeds or fertiliser, giving subsidies and buying at MSP are jobs of cooperatives, government departments and agencies, not SHGs' main role. Under DAY-NRLM and OLM, SHGs usually have 10–20 members and get a Revolving Fund and Community Investment Fund before bank linkage.

  4. 4 of 10 · OLM PYQ Set 1 SHGs, NRLM, Mission Shakti & Livelihoods · Q2 · Livelihoods, SHGs & Mission Shakti

    [UPSC CSE Prelims 2023] Consider the following statements: 1. The Self-Help Group (SHG) programme was originally initiated by the State Bank of India by providing microcredit to the financially deprived. 2. In an SHG, all members of a group take responsibility for a loan that an individual member takes. 3. The Regional Rural Banks and Scheduled Commercial Banks support SHGs. How many of the above statements are correct?

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Only two

    The SHG–Bank Linkage Programme was launched by NABARD in 1992, not by the State Bank of India, so statement 1 is wrong. SHG members share joint liability for loans taken by individual members, and Regional Rural Banks, commercial banks and cooperative banks lend to SHGs. Two: (b).

  5. 5 of 10 · OLM PYQ Set 2 Rural Development, Panchayati Raj & Financial Inclusion · Q2 · Rural Development & Panchayati Raj

    [UPPSC RO/ARO Prelims 2021] Consider the following events and arrange these in chronological order – (I) Launching of Community Development Programme (CDP) (II) Recommendation of Democratic Decentralization (III) Initiation of Key Village Scheme (KVS) (IV) Setting up of District Rural Development Agency (DRDA) at district level Select correct answer from the code given below -

    Show answer

    Answer: (3) (III), (I), (II), (IV)

    The Key Village Scheme for cattle improvement began in 1950 (First Plan); the Community Development Programme was launched on 2 October 1952; the Balwant Rai Mehta Committee recommended 'democratic decentralisation' (Panchayati Raj) in 1957; DRDAs were set up in the districts around 1980 to run IRDP. Order: III, I, II, IV.

  6. 6 of 10 · OLM PYQ Set 3 Odisha GK, Mission Shakti & Odisha Schemes · Q9 · Rural Development & Panchayati Raj

    [OSSSC Forester, Forest Guard & Excise Constable 2026 (2 May, Shift 1)] Which of the following indicators is NOT included in the National Multidimensional Poverty Index of India?

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Exchange rate

    NITI Aayog's National Multidimensional Poverty Index uses 12 indicators under three equal-weight dimensions. Health covers nutrition, child and adolescent mortality, and maternal health. Education covers years of schooling and school attendance. Standard of living covers cooking fuel, sanitation, drinking water, electricity, housing, assets and bank account. Electricity, nutrition and years of schooling are all indicators, but the exchange rate is a macroeconomic variable and is not part of the index. The bank-account indicator links the MPI directly to financial inclusion work under NRLM.

  7. 7 of 10 · OLM PYQ Set 1 SHGs, NRLM, Mission Shakti & Livelihoods · Q3 · Livelihoods, SHGs & Mission Shakti

    [TS Police Constable FWE 2016] Read the following statements: A) The DWCRA aimed at providing self-employment to women of BPL families. B) The NREP was launched in 1980. C) The NSS organisation gave three concepts of employment to measure its status-usual status, current weekly status and current daily status D) The PMRY aimed at providing wage employment in rural areas Which of the statements given above are correct?

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) A, B and C only

    DWCRA (1982) was a sub-scheme of IRDP giving self-employment to rural women of BPL families; the National Rural Employment Programme was launched in 1980 and merged into the Jawahar Rozgar Yojana in 1989; and the NSSO measures employment by usual, current weekly and current daily status. D is wrong: the Prime Minister's Rozgar Yojana (1993) gave self-employment to educated unemployed youth and was merged into PMEGP in 2008.

  8. 8 of 10 · OLM PYQ Set 2 Rural Development, Panchayati Raj & Financial Inclusion · Q3 · Rural Development & Panchayati Raj

    [APPSC Executive Officer 2026 Paper 1] Integrated Rural Development Program envisages

    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Assistance to poor rural families to come up above the poverty line

    The Integrated Rural Development Programme (IRDP), started in 1978 and extended across the country in 1980, aimed to help identified rural poor families rise above the poverty line. It provided productive assets through government subsidy and bank credit. Self-employment was the means it used, and in 1999 IRDP was merged into the Swarnajayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana.

  9. 9 of 10 · OLM PYQ Set 3 Odisha GK, Mission Shakti & Odisha Schemes · Q2 · Livelihoods, SHGs & Mission Shakti

    [OSSSC Forester, Forest Guard & Excise Constable 2026 (16 Apr, Shift 2)] In which year did the Government of Odisha launch the Mission Shakti scheme?

    Show answer

    Answer: (1) 2001

    The Government of Odisha launched Mission Shakti on 8 March 2001 (International Women's Day) to organise women into SHGs and empower them through savings, credit and livelihoods. 2005, 1999 and 2003 are wrong; 1999 is the Super Cyclone year and has nothing to do with this scheme. Mission Shakti became a full department in 2021, and today it works alongside OLM, which runs the NRLM-model SHG federations in rural Odisha.

  10. 10 of 10 · OLM PYQ Set 1 SHGs, NRLM, Mission Shakti & Livelihoods · Q4 · Livelihoods, SHGs & Mission Shakti

    [RPF Constable CBT 2025 (2 Mar, Shift 2)] Which of the following is a government programme meant to reduce poverty in India?

    Show answer

    Answer: (2) NRLM

    The National Rural Livelihoods Mission (NRLM, 'Aajeevika') was launched by the Ministry of Rural Development in June 2011, replacing the Swarnajayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana, to reduce rural poverty. It organises poor rural women into self-help groups and links them to credit and livelihoods; it was renamed Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana–NRLM (DAY-NRLM) in 2016. Make in India promotes manufacturing, Digital India builds digital infrastructure, and the Green Revolution of the 1960s raised food-grain output.
