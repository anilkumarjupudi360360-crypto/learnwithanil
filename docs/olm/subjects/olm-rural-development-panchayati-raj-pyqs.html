  1. Set 2 · Rural Development, Panchayati Raj & Financial Inclusion · Q1 · Rural Development & Panchayati Raj · Rural Development Programmes

    [UPPSC RO/ARO Prelims 2025] Which one of the following is a correct match ?

    1. Grow More Food Campaign – Jawaharlal Nehru
    2. Etawah Pilot Project – Albert Myre
    3. Co-operative Movement – Mahatma Gandhi
    4. Sevagram Project – Vinoba Bhave
    Show answer

    Answer: (2) Etawah Pilot Project – Albert Myre

    The Etawah Pilot Project (1948, Mahewa, Etawah district, UP) was a rural development experiment led by the American town planner Albert Mayer; it became a model for the Community Development Programme of 1952. The Grow More Food Campaign began in 1943 under the British government, the co-operative movement began with the 1904 Act, and Sevagram is linked with Mahatma Gandhi.

  2. Set 2 · Rural Development, Panchayati Raj & Financial Inclusion · Q2 · Rural Development & Panchayati Raj · Rural Development Programmes

    [UPPSC RO/ARO Prelims 2021] Consider the following events and arrange these in chronological order – (I) Launching of Community Development Programme (CDP) (II) Recommendation of Democratic Decentralization (III) Initiation of Key Village Scheme (KVS) (IV) Setting up of District Rural Development Agency (DRDA) at district level Select correct answer from the code given below -

    1. (I), (II), (III), (IV)
    2. (II), (I), (III), (IV)
    3. (III), (I), (II), (IV)
    4. (IV), (III), (II), (I)
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) (III), (I), (II), (IV)

    The Key Village Scheme for cattle improvement began in 1950 (First Plan); the Community Development Programme was launched on 2 October 1952; the Balwant Rai Mehta Committee recommended 'democratic decentralisation' (Panchayati Raj) in 1957; DRDAs were set up in the districts around 1980 to run IRDP. Order: III, I, II, IV.

  3. Set 2 · Rural Development, Panchayati Raj & Financial Inclusion · Q3 · Rural Development & Panchayati Raj · Rural Development Programmes

    [APPSC Executive Officer 2026 Paper 1] Integrated Rural Development Program envisages

    1. Self-employment programme for the rural poor
    2. To take over the functions of the erstwhile agricultural credit
    3. To help scheduled castes and tribes with adequate credit facility
    4. Assistance to poor rural families to come up above the poverty line
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Assistance to poor rural families to come up above the poverty line

    The Integrated Rural Development Programme (IRDP), started in 1978 and extended across the country in 1980, aimed to help identified rural poor families rise above the poverty line. It provided productive assets through government subsidy and bank credit. Self-employment was the means it used, and in 1999 IRDP was merged into the Swarnajayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana.

  6. Set 2 · Rural Development, Panchayati Raj & Financial Inclusion · Q6 · Rural Development & Panchayati Raj · Rural Development Programmes

    [SSC CGL 2024 Tier-I (9 Sep, Shift 3)] In Pradhan Mantri Gramin Awaas yojana, the cost of unit assistance is shared between Central and State Governments in the ratio ______ in plain areas.

    1. 30 : 70
    2. 90 : 10
    3. 60 : 40
    4. 40 : 60
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) 60 : 40

    Under the Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana – Gramin, the cost of unit assistance is shared between the Centre and the States in the ratio 60:40 in plain areas. The ratio is 90:10 for the North-Eastern States, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand and Jammu & Kashmir, and the Centre bears the full cost in other Union Territories. The unit assistance is ₹1.20 lakh in plains and ₹1.30 lakh in hilly states, difficult areas and IAP districts. PMAY-G replaced the Indira Awaas Yojana in 2016.

  7. Set 2 · Rural Development, Panchayati Raj & Financial Inclusion · Q7 · Rural Development & Panchayati Raj · Rural Development Programmes

    [APPSC FRO Mains 2023 Paper 1] What is the overall objective of ‘Mission Antyodaya’ scheme adopted in the Union Budget 2017-18 by the Indian government?

    1. Control of population growth
    2. Control of pollution in cities
    3. Rural development
    4. Control of crime rate in big cities
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) Rural development

    Mission Antyodaya, announced in the Union Budget 2017-18 and run by the Ministry of Rural Development, is a convergence framework for rural development. It aimed to bring one crore households out of poverty and make 50,000 gram panchayats poverty-free by pooling the resources of various schemes, with gram panchayats ranked on development gaps. It is not about population, urban pollution or crime.

    [APPSC FRO Screening Test 2025] Who is known as the Father of Community-led Watershed Development in India?

    1. Hermann Bacher
    2. Mridula Ramesh
    3. JVS Murty
    4. Marie Kurz
    Show answer

    Answer: (1) Hermann Bacher

    Fr. Hermann Bacher, a Swiss Jesuit priest who worked in Ahmednagar district of Maharashtra, pioneered participatory watershed development in which villagers plan and carry out the work. He helped launch the Indo-German Watershed Development Programme and co-founded the Watershed Organisation Trust (WOTR) in 1993, and is called the father of community-led watershed development in India.

  13. Set 3 · Odisha GK, Mission Shakti & Odisha Schemes · Q9 · Rural Development & Panchayati Raj · Poverty & Employment

    [OSSSC Forester, Forest Guard & Excise Constable 2026 (2 May, Shift 1)] Which of the following indicators is NOT included in the National Multidimensional Poverty Index of India?

    1. Electricity
    2. Nutrition
    3. Years of schooling
    4. Exchange rate
    Show answer

    Answer: (4) Exchange rate

    NITI Aayog's National Multidimensional Poverty Index uses 12 indicators under three equal-weight dimensions. Health covers nutrition, child and adolescent mortality, and maternal health. Education covers years of schooling and school attendance. Standard of living covers cooking fuel, sanitation, drinking water, electricity, housing, assets and bank account. Electricity, nutrition and years of schooling are all indicators, but the exchange rate is a macroeconomic variable and is not part of the index. The bank-account indicator links the MPI directly to financial inclusion work under NRLM.

    1. National Social Assistance Programme
    2. PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana
    3. National Food Security Mission
    4. Atal Pension Yojana
    Show answer

    Answer: (3) National Food Security Mission

    SDG 2 is 'Zero Hunger': end hunger, achieve food security and better nutrition, and promote sustainable agriculture. The National Food Security Mission, which raises production of rice, wheat, pulses and coarse cereals, maps directly to this goal. The National Social Assistance Programme (pensions) and Atal Pension Yojana relate to social protection under SDG 1 (No Poverty). PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana is an insurance scheme also linked to SDG 1. The National Food Security Act 2013 (the PDS entitlement) is another key SDG 2 instrument.
