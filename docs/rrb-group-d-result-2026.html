RRB Group D 2026: result out, PET next

The Railway Recruitment Boards declared the RRB Group D (CEN 09/2025) CBT result on 12 November 2026: each RRB published a PDF of roll numbers shortlisted for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) with zone-wise and category-wise cut-off marks. Scorecards open from 16 November 2026. Below: where things stand as of 15 November 2026, the notification facts, the exam pattern, PET standards and the previous papers to practise.

Who could apply

  • Class 10 pass, or ITI, or the National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) granted by NCVT (post-wise details in the notification).
  • Age 18 to 33 years on 1 January 2026; relaxation OBC-NCL 3 years, SC/ST 5 years, more for PwBD and ex-servicemen.
  • Fee ₹500 (₹400 refunded after the candidate takes the CBT). SC, ST, ex-servicemen, PwBD, women, transgender, minorities and Economically Backward Class paid a fee of ₹250, refunded in full after the CBT.
  • Pay: Level 1 of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix, starting basic pay ₹18,000 a month plus allowances.

Quick answers (as of 15 November 2026)

Is the RRB Group D result 2026 out?

Yes. The RRBs declared the CEN 09/2025 CBT result on 12 November 2026 as zone-wise PDFs of roll numbers shortlisted for the PET, with category-wise cut-off marks. Check your own RRB's website.

When will the RRB Group D scorecard 2026 come?

From 16 November 2026 (5 pm) on the RRB websites. Log in with your registration number and date of birth to see your raw and normalised marks.

When is the RRB Group D PET 2026?

Each zone's Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC) announces its own PET dates and issues PET admit cards. As of 15 November 2026 the dates were not out for all zones.

How many candidates qualified for PET in RRB Secunderabad?

1,705 roll numbers were shortlisted for PET against 1,012 vacancies of RRB Secunderabad (as reported by Testbook and qmaths). Check the official PDF on rrbsecunderabad.gov.in.

What is the RRB Group D cut off 2026?

Cut-offs are given zone-wise and category-wise in normalised marks in each RRB's result PDF. They differ from zone to zone, so compare your normalised score with your own RRB's cut-off.

How many posts are there in RRB Group D 2026?

22,195 Level-1 posts (CEN 09/2025): Track Maintainer Grade-IV, Pointsman-B, Assistants in various departments and other posts.

Is there negative marking in RRB Group D?

Yes. One-third of a mark is cut for each wrong answer. Each question carries 1 mark.

When will the next RRB Group D notification come?

Not announced as of 15 November 2026. The last two notices were CEN 08/2024 (32,438 posts) and CEN 09/2025 (22,195 posts).
